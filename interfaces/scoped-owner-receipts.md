# Scope-aware execution, receipts and accounting layers

`orchestration/scopes.py` supplies one governed Scope/ScopeRegistry for LEGAL_ENTITY, SUBGROUP and GROUP. Explicit stable IDs and legal-entity identifiers survive display-name changes, graph reorder and unrelated insertion. Duplicate/missing/self parents, cycles and illegal type transitions fail. A legal entity cannot silently become a Group. Hierarchy is structural metadata and creates neither accounting control/perimeter nor dependency edges. Metadata may remain absent at registration; execution requires material dimensions and a CURRENT effective Scope.

Single-entity and prior execution_scopes contexts normalize immediately into the same registry. There is no three-scope cap or legacy Group engine. Registry framework, jurisdiction, functional and presentation currencies remain distinct. No framework or currency conversion is performed.

Execution identity is a readable owner/Scope prefix plus SHA256 of a canonical tuple: owner, Scope ID/type, framework, jurisdiction, functional/presentation currency and bounded start/end dates. All workpapers and results use this key. Unique package/logical aliases support ordinary existing callers; repeated-owner aliases fail as ambiguous. Native diagnostic rechecks have a deterministic role suffix and retain the original qualified source. Native owner fingerprints and outputs remain behind production.assess_case; Node.execution_receipt provides a dimensional producing-node envelope without changing accounting authority.

Semantic facts and issue candidates preserve Scope. Unknown entities remain Scope Candidates with material questions, never approved registry/context/perimeter writes. Scoped context candidates remain PROPOSED. Reviewed bindings resolve one exact owner/Scope. Repeated owners require distinct scoped ReviewedInputPacks, exact binding populations and qualified_scope_sources matching the sealed inventory. Whole-document, text and population bindings cannot cross legal Scopes; explicit shared Group policy applicability can qualify contextual policy evidence. Group assembly remains independently reviewed and typed.

Existing Group receipts preserve their registered accounting semantics and actual upstream dependency/current-result checks. Their execution ledger now records exact source/target nodes, source/target Scopes, framework/currency dimensions, result/exact-case fingerprints, currentness and economic identity. Group local-revenue observations have an explicit LOCAL_RESULT_OBSERVATION contract; equal numbers never authorize substitution. The observation consumer is orchestration only, not a Consolidation owner or a conversion engine. Its Group context source must match actual scoped source inventory. Local results retain their native framework/currency, and conversion requirements are visible without a manufactured consolidated total.

`scoped_journals.py` qualifies source/posting Scope and accounting layer before wrapping the inherited CAO gross-line allocator. Exact node identity must match owner plus governed Scope context; journal currency and bounded period must match the registry. Event identity is qualified by posting Scope and period. Same-Scope event aliases and line reuse fail, while identical economics in distinct legitimate legal/Group/Subgroup layers remain distinct. This is a reviewed journal pack, not a posting API or intercompany network.

The Group source-assembly adapter retains complete reviewed keyed source populations, manifest-bound text assertions, acquisition activity-selection gates, specialist current-result receipts, evidence-only embedded implications and account-by-account journal replay. Generalizing Scope must not weaken those accounting controls.

Every public route still passes through public_record. Raw paragraphs, reviewer IDs, source/native fingerprints, routing keys and manifests remain internal; material conflicts, conversion requirements and other limits remain visible. Future Case/period lifecycle, selective invalidation, intercompany networks, conversion, persistence and authenticated approval belong to later workstreams.
