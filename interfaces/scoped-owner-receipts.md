# Bounded scoped owner receipts and source assembly

`orchestration/scopes.py` validates an optional explicit scope table of at most three contexts. A multi-scope Case has exactly one primary reporting Group and separate legal entities. Frameworks must agree and every source period must fit the Case. Without the table the original single-scope dimension gate remains unchanged. This is not an arbitrary entity graph.

A reviewed native input can contain `qualified_owner_results`. Each receipt names an actual upstream graph node, one registered semantic contract, its exact completed result, six source/consumer dimensions and supplied evidence. `result_bindings.py` rejects missing mandatory receipts, duplicate semantics, stale results, incorrect entity/currency/date populations and unsupported metric paths. Receipts are evidence-only: they neither manufacture certification nor copy journals into a second posting owner.

Acquisition-related translation requires a complete `activity_selection`: source/effective dates, disjoint complete records, entity/currency, source inventory, selected identities, included profit, gross revenue/expense accounts, full-year keyed balances, acquisition equity account and evidence. It rejects unsupported splits, gaps, overlaps, pre-acquisition inclusion, gross net-equal substitutions and contradictory closing or acquisition equity populations. The acquisition date is a specialist-result boundary, not a filename timestamp.

`ReviewedInputPack` supports complete keyed `PopulationBinding` values and reviewed `TextAssertion` rules. Assertions have one explicit capture, owner target and allowed normalization; absent/ambiguous text blocks execution. `source_semantic_controls` embeds the exact assertion/population manifest in native reviewed inputs. Inputs with `qualified_source_documents` cannot omit that manifest or downgrade to the legacy path. Full legal-extract coverage supplements typed dates/amounts/election/currency assertions in the controlled fixture. Patterns are review evidence, not accounting authority or automatic approvals.

`source_assembly_journals` is the bounded Consolidation assembly adapter. It specifies the actual qualified assembly owner, reporting Group, source journal evidence, existing economic ownership allocations and exact-payload independent review. It accounts for every specialist implication once as embedded source evidence, applies native Consolidation journals once through the inherited gross-line allocator, then reconciles every final account. Its execution ledger distinguishes source legal entity, target Group, currency, posting/evidence mode and Group level. A Group elimination cannot become a legal-book posting.

Semantic families added to the existing governed planner are acquisition, group structure, intercompany, foreign operation, impairment valuation and tax temporary difference. They route validated sourced issues through production metadata, never an objective keyword. Natural requests and raw company exports do not carry owner schemas or approve accounting.

All user-facing routes remain `public_record`-curated. Source paragraphs, reviewer identities, manifests, result/source fingerprints, confidence internals and routing keys remain internal. Material unresolved numeric discrepancies and accounting limitations remain visible.

Foreign nominal amounts preserve `amount_currency` separately from the legal workpaper currency. Source metadata must witness that denomination, and the reviewed native IC amount binds its original transaction currency. Reciprocal functional-book amounts reconcile to Parent source balances and the USD foreign-operation population before Group elimination. This adds denomination preservation, not general currency-conversion orchestration.
