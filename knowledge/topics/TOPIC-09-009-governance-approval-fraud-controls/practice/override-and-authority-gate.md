# Accounting authority and override gate

**Applicability.** Management designs approval and fraud controls under its own governance and reporting obligations. PCAOB AS 2401's journal, estimate-bias and unusual-transaction audit response is an audit anchor for applicable PCAOB engagements, not a universal company mandate. Scope the accounting impact and route investigations to authorized functions while preserving evidence.

**Matrix specification.** Row per decision type: policy election, estimate, nonroutine journal, master data, write-off, close reopening, unusual/related-party transaction and disclosure. Fields include initiating role, independent approver, competence, monetary and qualitative trigger, conflicted-party substitute, delegation validity, system role, emergency bypass, retrospective reviewer, required supporting memo and retention location. The workflow must prevent self approval or reveal an exception requiring independent after-the-fact review.

**Override monitoring.** Extract privileged/manual journals including direct consolidation entries and entries after close; reconcile counts to posted ledger, match user to access/delegation register, screen timing/unusual accounts, obtain support and independent disposition. Back-test significant estimates against outcomes, investigate consistent directional bias, and assess unusual transactions' business purpose and related-party disclosure. A CFO sign-off alone cannot neutralize a CFO override risk.

**Evidence and data.** Retain frozen delegation matrix, role exports, journal population and filters, approval/workflow logs, policy/estimate papers, exception challenge and disposition, emergency access logs and final disclosure tie-out. Identify immutable IDs across ERP, consolidation and reporting; record source timestamps and all manual transformations.

**Adverse case.** A $1 related-party side agreement affects disclosure and a CFO posts its journal with own approval. Threshold-only workflow would pass it; qualitative flag routes independent governance review. Freeze ledger evidence, assess disclosure and possible misstatement, inspect privileged history, escalate suspected fraud through authorized governance, and evaluate design/operating deficiencies under 09-007. Maintain confidentiality and legal routing as applicable.

**Handoffs.** Link journal controls to Domain 01 close, disclosure/policy and correction to Domain 02, auditor request and communication to Domain 10, ERP permissions to Domain 11, and decision memory to Domain 12. Do not imply investigative conclusions from an anomaly score.

References: [PCAOB AS 2401](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2401); [PCAOB AS 2201](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201); [SEC Release 33-8810](https://www.sec.gov/rule-release/33-8810).
