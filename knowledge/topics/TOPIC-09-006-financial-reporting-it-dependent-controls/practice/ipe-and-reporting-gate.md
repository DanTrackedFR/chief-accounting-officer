# IPE and final-reporting control gate

**Routing.** Record entity, filing regime, reporting basis, period, line/disclosure and assertion before selecting a control. SEC management ICFR guidance (Release 33-8810) and PCAOB AS 2201 apply to relevant US issuer assessments/audits; their scoping concepts are illustrative elsewhere. Reporting presentation rules require a separate framework/effective-date decision (including IFRS 18 from annual periods beginning 1 January 2027). Do not substitute an audit standard for management's accounting policy.

**Control specification.** For each final statement line and note, identify ledger/consolidation source, mapping version, approved adjustment, preparer, independent reviewer, expected variance threshold, review timing and retained final output. The reviewer traces a sample of mapped balances to the final trial balance and investigates unexpected movement at a threshold small enough to detect the stated assertion risk. A cross-cast alone cannot detect a missing disclosure or omitted entity.

**IPE acceptance.** Record report name/version, source tables, company/period/filter parameters, extraction timestamp, row count and control totals. Reconcile population to the authoritative ledger; inspect query logic/configuration and governed changes; validate spreadsheet joins/formulas and reject rows. Retain exact report and transformation versions used by the reviewer. If report logic changed mid-close, identify affected runs and repeat their dependent controls. For automated interfaces, compare source and destination counts/totals and resolve duplicates, rejects and reruns before certification.

**Evidence/data lineage.** `source transaction → subledger → GL → consolidation → reporting cube → statement/note` with identifiers, timestamps, mapping version and reconciling adjustments at each boundary. Preserve access/change logs for key logic, report-validation workpaper, reviewer annotations, exception disposition, disclosure tie-out and final approved version. An auditor can reproduce the population and see why each exception was accepted.

**Failure route.** Unreconciled feed, hidden filter, unsupported transformation or changed mapping blocks reliance on the dependent control. Quarantine the output, rerun from validated source, evaluate exposed periods/financial-statement effect and log control deficiency under TOPIC-09-007. Late release also invokes close escalation and statement revision controls; do not silently overwrite the evidence version.

**Challenge case.** A custom revenue report omits a new legal entity yet ties to itself: FAIL completeness. Reconcile entity population to consolidation scope and ledger before review; retest affected periods and disclosures. This control accepts only with independent population proof and retained report logic.

References: [SEC Release 33-8810](https://www.sec.gov/rule-release/33-8810); [PCAOB AS 2201](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201); [IFRS 18 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/). Authority must be rechecked for entity status and applicable period.
