# TOPIC-13-001 — claim-linked reporting/transaction case

Claims: [TOPIC-13-001-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-13-001-US-001], [TOPIC-13-001-UK-001], [TOPIC-13-001-AASB-001]. Existing workpaper preserved.

## Calculation and counterexample

At acquisition, consideration 1,000, NCI fair value 200, identifiable net assets at fair value after tax 700: goodwill 500. If an omitted customer intangible fair value 100 carries deferred tax 25, net identifiable assets rise 75 and goodwill falls to 425, subject to actual tax basis and measurement policy. Do not treat post-acquisition integration cost as purchase price. Compare NCI proportionate versus fair value option under IFRS, and US private-company goodwill alternatives and UK FRS 102 §19 separately.

## Controls and jurisdiction gate

Retain source population, contract/legal terms, computation, journal/version, reconciliation, disclosure tie-out and reviewer. Recompute under US ASC/SEC where relevant, UK entity regime and Australian tier/period rather than asserting IFRS equivalence. All model-derived claims need direct authoritative audit; IFRS core claims are limited to the specified 2026 issued routes.
