# Acquisition-method purchase-price allocation case

Parent purchases 80% of a business for cash 800; fair value of identifiable net assets is 700 and fair value of NCI is 180. Under an IFRS 3 fair-value NCI election where applicable, illustrative goodwill is 800+180−700=280. Under proportionate-share NCI measurement, NCI 20%×700=140 and goodwill 800+140−700=240. These are alternative permitted IFRS measurements for the assumed qualifying business combination, not interchangeable journal options after selection. US ASC 805 and UK FRS 102 Section 19 may differ materially in NCI/goodwill; do not import IFRS election by default. **Executed:** 280 versus 240 difference 40, driven by NCI measurement.
