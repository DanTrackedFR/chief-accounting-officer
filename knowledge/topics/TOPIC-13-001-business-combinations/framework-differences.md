# TOPIC-13-001 — Framework differences

Source checked: 2026-09-24. Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate.

## IFRS — IFRS 3
IFRS 3 applies the acquisition method to business combinations in scope. The CAO must identify the acquirer and acquisition date, recognise and measure identifiable acquired assets/liabilities and NCI under the applicable requirements/exceptions, and recognise goodwill or a bargain-purchase gain. The definition-of-a-business assessment is a gating decision because acquisition of assets that do not constitute a business does not receive business-combination accounting. IFRS 3 contains an optional concentration-test route within the business-definition guidance.

Current-status gate: the IASB's Business Combinations—Disclosures, Goodwill and Impairment project remains under redeliberation in 2026. Proposed/tentative disclosure changes are **not current IFRS requirements** and must not be treated as effective accounting.

Common-control combinations are outside IFRS 3's specified acquisition requirements; the IASB concluded its BCUCC project without issuing new requirements. Route those transactions to TOPIC-13-009 and applicable policy/jurisdiction analysis.

## US GAAP — ASC 805
ASC 805 is the principal US GAAP business-combinations topic and also uses an acquisition-method architecture, but detailed recognition, measurement, scope, private-company alternatives and subsequent-accounting differences mean an IFRS conclusion cannot simply be copied into US GAAP.

A specific current effective-date gate applies to **ASU 2025-03, Business Combinations (Topic 805) and Consolidation (Topic 810): Determining the Accounting Acquirer in the Acquisition of a Variable Interest Entity**. FASB issued it in May 2025 to require consideration of the same acquirer-identification factors for qualifying equity-exchange transactions involving a VIE business that are used for other acquisition transactions. The CAO must verify the entity's effective/early-adoption status before applying the amended route.

Public FASB material does not expose all current Codification paragraph bodies. Therefore paragraph-level US records remain PARTIAL unless checked against licensed/current Codification access; do not invent paragraph citations.

## UK GAAP — FRS 102 Section 19
FRS 102 Section 19 is not a mirror of current IFRS 3. The FRC states that Section 19 continues to be based on IFRS 3 (2004). It uses a **purchase method** architecture, permits merger accounting for qualifying group reconstructions, and has different treatment in areas including acquisition-related costs and contingent consideration. The 2024 edition is the key period-sensitive source for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2026 (subject to the standard's transition requirements).

Consequently, never route a UK GAAP acquisition through an IFRS 3 PPA mechanically. The CAO must use the FRS 102 version applicable to the reporting period and separately assess group reconstruction/PBE rules.

## AASB — AASB 3
AASB 3 is IFRS 3-aligned for for-profit entities, but Australian entity type, reporting tier and operative-period amendments remain explicit gates. The AASB site currently identifies the December 2022 compilation as applying to periods beginning on/after 1 January 2023 but before 1 July 2026, while listing subsequent/uncompiled amendments from 1 July 2026. For a transaction/reporting period after that date, the CAO must assemble the applicable AASB 3 requirements together with relevant uncompiled amendments rather than treating the older compilation as complete current text.

AASB materials state that for-profit entities complying with AASB 3 also comply with IFRS 3; NFP entities and Tier 2 disclosures can carry Australian-specific consequences. Entity type and tier are mandatory inputs.

## High-risk differences to resolve on every case
- definition of a business / asset-acquisition boundary;
- accounting acquirer, including VIE/reverse-acquisition fact patterns;
- NCI measurement alternatives and scope;
- acquisition-related costs;
- contingent consideration classification/measurement;
- identifiable intangible recognition and measurement;
- contingent liabilities/provisions;
- deferred tax and employee/award effects;
- common-control/group-reconstruction scope;
- measurement-period mechanics;
- goodwill subsequent accounting and any private-company alternatives;
- disclosure/reporting-tier requirements.

Do not create a single 'global GAAP PPA' and then adjust only goodwill. Framework selection changes the workplan upstream.
