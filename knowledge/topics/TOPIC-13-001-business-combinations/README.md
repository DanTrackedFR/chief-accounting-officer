# TOPIC-13-001 — Business Combination Accounting / Purchase Price Allocation Coordination

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**  
Primary capabilities: CAO-13-001, CAO-13-002  
Sensitivity: H — standards-heavy  
Last source check: 2026-09-24

## Objective
Enable the CAO to determine whether an acquisition is a business combination, establish the applicable framework and acquisition date, coordinate the acquisition-method workplan and PPA, challenge specialist inputs, produce acquisition accounting, disclosures and audit evidence, and preserve the resulting accounting positions in Company Accounting Memory.

## Boundary
This topic covers the overall business-combination and PPA operating model. Detailed acquisition-date accounting and contingent consideration route to TOPIC-13-002; acquisition costs/measurement-period adjustments to TOPIC-13-003; asset acquisitions/restructuring to TOPIC-13-005; common-control transactions to TOPIC-13-009; goodwill/impairment to TOPIC-04-006.

## CAO execution logic
1. Lock entity, reporting framework, reporting period, transaction date and reporting tier.
2. Obtain executed purchase agreement, amendments/side letters, closing statement, cap table, board papers, valuation materials and legal-entity chart.
3. Determine whether the acquired set is a **business** or an asset/group of assets. Do not assume an M&A legal label determines accounting.
4. Identify the accounting acquirer and acquisition date (control date), including reverse-acquisition/VIE analysis where relevant.
5. Identify consideration transferred, previously held interests, NCI and replacement awards; separate consideration from transactions that are not part of the combination.
6. Build the identifiable-asset/liability inventory. Explicitly search for previously unrecorded intangibles, contractual/customer relationships, technology, brands, favorable/unfavorable contracts, contingencies, tax effects and employee-related items.
7. Establish measurement bases and exceptions. Coordinate valuation specialists where fair-value expertise is required, but retain accounting ownership of scope, unit of account, useful lives, classification and linkage to source contracts/data.
8. Calculate provisional goodwill/bargain purchase only after the identifiable-net-assets bridge is complete and reviewed.
9. Build opening-balance-sheet entries and map them into consolidation/ERP reporting structures without overwriting legal-entity books unless the accounting design requires it.
10. Prepare acquisition disclosures, measurement-period tracker, audit evidence index and post-close ownership matrix.
11. Reconcile purchase agreement → funds flow/closing statement → valuation schedules → acquisition journal → consolidation → financial statements/disclosures.
12. Promote durable conclusions (business-v-asset conclusion, acquirer/date, valuation policies, NCI election where applicable, provisional items and owners) to Company Accounting Memory.

## Required inputs
- executed transaction agreements and closing evidence;
- legal/entity structure before and after closing;
- consideration/funds-flow schedule;
- target trial balance and accounting policies;
- target contracts and asset registers relevant to identification/valuation;
- tax basis/deferred-tax inputs where in scope;
- employee/award arrangements;
- valuation reports/models and underlying forecasts;
- materiality and reporting calendar.

## Output package
- scope/business-definition paper;
- acquirer and acquisition-date conclusion;
- PPA scope and responsibility matrix;
- identifiable-net-assets register;
- valuation input/challenge log;
- acquisition accounting workbook and journal package;
- goodwill/bargain-purchase bridge;
- disclosure support;
- measurement-period/open-items register;
- reconciliation and audit-evidence index;
- Company Accounting Memory candidates.

## Hard completion tests
A case is not complete merely because a valuation report exists. The CAO must be able to explain the accounting perimeter, prove completeness of the identifiable-net-assets population, trace material PPA amounts to evidence, distinguish accounting judgments from valuation assumptions, reconcile the acquisition journal to reporting, identify provisional items, and state the framework-specific differences.
