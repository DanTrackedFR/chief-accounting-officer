# TOPIC-13-011 — Hyperinflationary Accounting Assessment

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**  
Sensitivity: H  
Capability: CAO-13-021  
Source check: 2026-09-25

## Objective
Enable the CAO to identify a potentially hyperinflationary functional-currency environment, document the framework conclusion, execute/restage the required restatement mechanics, integrate FX translation and produce auditable reporting.

## CAO workflow
1. Confirm entity, functional currency, presentation currency, framework and reporting period.
2. Gather current and historical inflation/index data plus qualitative indicators.
3. Assess all relevant indicators; do not treat a single numerical indicator as an automatic bright-line conclusion.
4. Establish onset/cessation date and group consistency.
5. Classify monetary vs non-monetary items and identify historical/current-cost bases.
6. Select and evidence the general price index required by the applicable framework.
7. Restate applicable non-monetary items, equity components, income/expense and comparatives as required.
8. Calculate and reconcile the net monetary gain/loss.
9. Apply the relevant foreign-currency translation mechanics after inflation restatement where required.
10. Tie restated ledgers to consolidation/reporting and disclosures.
11. Document judgments, index source, mechanics, review and effective-date monitoring.

## Required evidence
Functional-currency memo; indicator assessment; index evidence; monetary/non-monetary mapping; restatement workbook/system output; net monetary result; FX bridge; consolidation bridge; disclosure support; review sign-off.

## Stop conditions
Do not finalize if functional currency, onset conclusion, price index, material classification questions or source balances remain unresolved.
