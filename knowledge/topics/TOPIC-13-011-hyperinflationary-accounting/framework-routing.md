# Framework routing — TOPIC-13-011

Source checked 2026-09-25.

IFRS: IAS 29 applies when functional currency is that of a hyperinflationary economy. Assess quantitative and qualitative indicators together. The July 2025 IFRIC agenda decision confirms that one indicator alone does not determine the conclusion and relevant other indicators may matter. Restatement uses a general price index and the net monetary result is reported in profit or loss. IFRIC 7 addresses first-year application. 2026 IASB project work is pipeline until effective.

US GAAP: route highly inflationary foreign-currency accounting through ASC 830. Do not import IAS 29 mechanics. Public authoritative Codification access remains insufficient for full paragraph mapping, so paragraph-level status is PARTIAL.

UK GAAP: route through FRS 102 Section 31 and applicable foreign-currency requirements. FRC materials identify UK-specific revaluation-reserve treatment driven by company law.

AASB: route through the operative AASB 129 compilation and Australian entity/reporting requirements. Verify the Australian version rather than assuming current IFRS text.

Core control: prove functional currency, onset/cessation assessment, index source, monetary/non-monetary classification, restatement calculation, net monetary result, FX/consolidation sequence and disclosures.
