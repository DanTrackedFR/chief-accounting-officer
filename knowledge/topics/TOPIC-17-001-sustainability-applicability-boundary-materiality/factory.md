# Sustainability applicability, boundary and materiality — substantive factory

Companion to prior README; capabilities CAO-17-001–003. Source checked 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for the decision framework, with local-law application conditional.

## Three independent gates

First determine **legal applicability** by entity, jurisdiction, securities/listing status, financial year, size, parent/subsidiary exemption and locally adopted standard. IFRS S1/S2 are issued standards effective for annual periods starting 1 January 2024, but issuance does not oblige every IFRS financial reporter to use them. [Australia's ASIC cohort guidance](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/sustainability-reporting/for-preparers-of-sustainability-reports/who-must-prepare-a-sustainability-report/) starts first cohort from 1 January 2025, second from 1 July 2026 and third from 1 July 2027; test the entity against all statutory size/NGER and financial-reporting criteria, not one headcount. [AASB S2](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-s2-sep-2024) is the Australian climate standard; AASB S1 is voluntary. [UK SRS S1/S2](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/uk-sustainability-reporting-standards) were published February 2026 for voluntary use, subject to future mandates. EU [Directive 2026/470](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2026/470/oj/eng) amended CSRD thresholds and transitions; check both turnover and employees and local transposition, including wave-one relief. The [SEC 2026 proposed rescission](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/05/s7-2026-19) is a proposal, not automatically the operative US requirement; stay/litigation and other reporting rules must be checked.

Second determine **reporting boundary** under the chosen regime. Begin with financial consolidated group; reconcile each subsidiary, associate, joint venture, operated site, leased site and value-chain relationship. GHG inventory consolidation may use equity share or control approaches under the [GHG Protocol](https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-standard); do not equate owned premises, leased premises or financial consolidation blindly. Record acquisitions/disposals, boundary date, exclusions and potential double counting. Third determine **materiality**: ISSB [IFRS S1](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/ifrs-sustainability-standards-navigator/ifrs-s1-general-requirements/) is oriented to information useful to primary users of general-purpose financial reports; ESRS under CSRD uses double materiality, considering impact and financial perspectives. Apply the specific regime's thresholds and disclosure relief, not the financial audit's performance-materiality number.

## Workpaper, controls and worked decision

Create an entity applicability matrix with legal citation, rule status, threshold inputs, period, group relief, assurance and filing date; a perimeter bridge from consolidated financial entities to sites and value chain; a topic/metric materiality record of evidence, stakeholders or financial effects as required, contrary evidence, scoring and reviewer. Separate legal counsel's conclusion from CAO data mapping. Control new entity/site completeness from legal-entity register, facilities, AP energy vendors and acquisition log. The audit trail must show why a site is inside or outside each boundary even if no emissions data exist yet.

Example: a UK subsidiary with 600 employees and 100m revenue is not made subject to UK SRS solely because the standards were issued. An Australian parent with 600 employees cannot be declared in-scope from headcount alone; apply ASIC's full tests. A 40%-owned operated facility may be outside financial subsidiary consolidation but relevant to a selected operational-control GHG boundary. Record a reasoned bridge rather than a single blanket entity list.

## Scenarios

S1: issuer applies IFRS S2 effective 2024 as a universal 2026 legal mandate: FAIL first gate. S2: EU company has 1,050 employees but 300m turnover: do not conclude in-scope under the revised core threshold without transition/local-law analysis. S3: acquisition omitted from site register: fail boundary completeness. S4: EU ESRS assessment uses investor-only materiality: fail double-materiality method. These tests establish routing; current local transposition and eligibility facts are residual case inputs. Dependencies 07-001 consolidation, 15-005 materiality, 16-001 regulation, 17-004 emissions.
