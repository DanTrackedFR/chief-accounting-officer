# TOPIC-04-005 — acquired intangible, life and disclosure test

Complements the existing canonical guide and retained `TOPIC-04-005-intangibles-amortization/README.md`. Capabilities CAO-04-013/014. Resolve [IAS 38](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-38-intangible-assets/), [AASB 138](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/), [FRS 102 Section 18](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) and [FASB ASC 350](https://asc.fasb.org/) for the reporting period, acquisition route and entity tier. UK Periodic Review 2024 is generally effective from 2026; current US detailed paragraphs remain unverified via the public portal.

The [AASB 138 December 2022 compilation](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-138-dec-2022) explicitly ends before periods beginning 1 July 2026. For a 2026–27 Australian period, identify subsequent operative amendments/edition on the AASB portal and the Tier 2 AASB 1060 version; a 2025 illustration must not be relabeled as a verified 2026–27 source. The FRC 2024 edition and its Periodic Review transition require an independent Section 18 selection for the same licence.

The [official AASB 138 version history](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/superseded-versions?pronouncement=274&standard_no=71) identifies AASB 2022-9 public-sector insurance amendments commencing 1 July 2026. Screen the licence holder's public-sector insurance status and check the assembled later-period edition. A commercial licence does not automatically change accounting simply because an older compilation ceases to cover its reporting date. The [FRC current-edition page](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) identifies September 2024 plus later amendments as the operative edition set; capture the actual adoption and tier before approving UK amortization.

## Purchase case and classification

An independently acquired, enforceable software licence costs 600,000, is available 1 July and has a supported six-year economic/legal life with zero residual. Assuming a calendar year and straight-line consumption, annual charge is 100,000; the first six months are 50,000; 31 December net carrying amount is 550,000 absent impairment. Dr licence 600,000 / Cr payable 600,000 at recognition; Dr amortization expense 50,000 / Cr accumulated amortization 50,000. Tie legal rights, purchase invoice, go-live acceptance, legal expiry/renewal evidence and register to the GL. If the contract is only remote access to supplier-hosted software, pause the entry and refer to 04-004's control/service assessment.

Under IFRS/AASB an indefinite life requires evidence of no foreseeable limit, not merely a renewable legal term; such an asset is not amortized but is subject to annual impairment and life reassessment. IFRS/AASB revaluation is restricted to a genuine active market, not a valuation spreadsheet for a unique brand. UK Section 18 is a separate finite-life/policy analysis; US finite/indefinite and impairment routes are separately governed. Internally generated customer lists are not turned into an IFRS/AASB asset because management estimates their value. Acquired identifiable assets in a business combination may have a different measurement starting point from the separate-purchase example; route to acquisition accounting and goodwill.

## Control and failure probes

Reviewer reconciles beginning gross cost + additions − disposals ± FX to ending gross cost, and opening accumulated amortization + charge − disposals ± FX to closing accumulated amortization, then ties gross, charge, accumulated balance and impairment to disclosures. Australian Tier 2 presentation/disclosure routes to AASB 1060 when applicable. Test: (1) one-day delayed go-live changes time-apportionment, not purchase date; (2) trademark claimed indefinite without renewal/market support fails; (3) unique-brand upward revaluation fails active-market evidence; (4) technology obsolescence triggers 04-006 even though scheduled amortization is current. The 50,000 calculation passes on stated assumptions; unusual legal and US cases require specific source review.
