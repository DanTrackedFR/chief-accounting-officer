# Intangible recognition, useful life, amortization and disclosure

Companion to both retained 04-005 guides. CAO-04-013/014. Claim IDs in `standards-claims.json` identify the provenance of the normative routes.

## Framework comparison

IAS 38/AASB 138 require an identifiable controlled nonmonetary resource, recognition criteria and an eligible acquisition or development cost basis. Separate acquisition differs from a business combination and internal generation. Finite life assets amortize from availability for use over the expected consumption period; indefinite life requires evidence that no foreseeable limit exists, annual reassessment and impairment rather than amortization. A revaluation policy needs an active market [04-005-IFRS-01; 04-005-AASB-01]. UK FRS 102 Section 18 treats intangible lives as finite, including a fallback life where reliable estimation fails under its operative edition; goodwill is separately Section 19. Do not adopt an IAS 38 indefinite life into UK books. The 2026 Periodic Review edition/transition and UK legal rights are gates [04-005-UK-01]. US ASC 350-30 distinguishes finite and indefinite life; finite assets amortize over supported useful life and follow the ASC 360 impairment route, while indefinite assets receive separate annual/triggered testing. Business-combination identifiable intangibles and goodwill require ASC 805/350 allocation; US ordinary intangibles lack a general IAS 38 upward-revaluation policy [04-005-US-01].

An acquired licence costs 600,000, becomes available 1 July, and has six-year supported remaining legal/economic life and nil residual. First calendar-year expense 600,000/6 × 6/12 = 50,000; net 550,000. Dr licence 600,000 / Cr payable 600,000; Dr amortization 50,000 / Cr accumulated amortization 50,000. A three-year nonrenewable legal right cannot use six years merely because the software remains useful technically. If renewal is enforceable or expected, document evidence and renewal costs before choosing a longer life [04-005-IFRS-01; 04-005-US-01].

Boundary alternatives: (1) remote supplier-hosted access without control routes to service/prepayment, not a 600,000 owned licence; (2) internally generated customer list is expensed under IAS 38/AASB 138; (3) an acquired unique brand cannot be revalued simply from an external appraisal absent an active market; (4) a 600,000 indefinite US trademark has zero routine amortization but requires ASC 350 impairment, whereas the same item needs an independently justified UK finite-life period; (5) shortening life after new obsolescence evidence changes future amortization and prompts impairment, not retroactive expense. These are distinct routes, not alternative answers for the stipulated six-year licence [04-005-IFRS-01; 04-005-UK-01; 04-005-US-01].

Workpaper captures acquisition contract, separability/right, controlled access, consideration allocation, go-live, renewal/legal expiration, consumption support, cost/life/method/residual, impairment indicators and disposal. Tie gross and accumulated rollforwards separately to GL and disclosures, including class, life/method, additions, impairment, research/development expense where applicable and material judgments. Apply AASB 1060 for eligible Tier 2 disclosures. Restrict register edits, review go-live and renewal overrides, and archive source version/effective date [04-005-AASB-01; 04-005-UK-01].
