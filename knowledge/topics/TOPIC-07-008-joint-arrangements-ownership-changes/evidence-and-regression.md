# TOPIC-07-008 — claim-linked group accounting case

Claims: [TOPIC-07-008-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-07-008-US-001], [TOPIC-07-008-UK-001], [TOPIC-07-008-AASB-001]. Retain existing factory.

## Calculation and adverse route

Two parties share 50/50 contractual rights to plant of 200 and obligations for debt of 120, with output rights: each joint operator records relevant share 100 asset and 60 liability plus own/share revenue/expenses under contract facts. If parties instead have rights only to net assets, a 50% joint venture is one investment accounted for using equity method under IAS 28, not proportionate line-by-line consolidation. Test legal form, contract, other facts and liabilities for output before classification. US ASC 810/323 and UK §15 may differ.

## Evidence and framework regression

Prepare signed rights/ownership memo, consolidated journal with entity and counterparty IDs, source rate or valuation, reserve/NCI rollforward, tax bridge and disclosure tie-out. Test the opposite classification under US ASC 810/323/805, UK FRS 102 and AASB before asserting convergence. Select effective period and reporting tier separately. These four claim IDs remain the source-audit queue; IFRS core route uses 2026 issued text.
