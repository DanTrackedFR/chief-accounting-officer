# Joint control and ownership change boundaries

Capabilities mapped to TOPIC-07-008 in canonical manifest. Existing README retained. Two investors each own 50% of a project, but the operating agreement requires unanimity for relevant activity decisions. Assess substantive veto and whether parties have rights to assets/obligations for liabilities (joint operation) versus rights to net assets (joint venture); 50/50 voting alone is not enough. A project vehicle does not automatically mean joint venture. Under IFRS 11/AASB 11, joint venture generally leads to IAS 28/AASB 128 equity method; joint operation recognizes the party's rights and obligations. ASC 810/323 and FRS 102 Section 15 require separate analysis.

If a parent owns 80% of a controlled subsidiary then sells 10% for 150 while retaining control, do not book a disposal gain merely from cash less proportionate subsidiary net assets. Under IFRS 10/AASB 10, calculate change in NCI and equity attributable to owners; suppose NCI carrying amount increases by 100, then the 50 difference between cash 150 and NCI adjustment 100 is owner equity (ignoring tax/costs). **Executed:** 150−100=50. If control is lost, derecognition and retained-interest remeasurement invoke a different model (07-009).
