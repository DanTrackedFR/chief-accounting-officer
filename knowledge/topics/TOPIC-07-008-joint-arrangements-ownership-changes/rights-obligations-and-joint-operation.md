# Joint operation versus joint venture: enforceable rights

**Facts.** Two investors each own 50% of a separate project vehicle. Decisions on relevant activities require both parties' consent. The vehicle's legal form ordinarily shields investors from its debts, but contracts require each party to take its proportional output and fund operating deficits; the vehicle has no independent external market. IFRS 11 assessment starts with enforceable joint control, then legal form, contractual terms and other facts determining whether parties have rights to assets and obligations for liabilities rather than merely rights to net assets. A 50/50 split or separate company alone is insufficient. Obtain joint agreement, sales/offtake, financing, guarantees and financial forecasts. If the arrangement is a joint operation, record the investor's rights to assets and obligations for liabilities, revenue and expenses under IFRS 11; if joint venture, route the interest to IAS 28 equity method. Do not mechanically put 50% of every legal-entity TB account into the group without rights analysis.

**Numerical decision.** If qualifying joint-operation rights establish 50% interest in output 100 units and obligations for project operating costs 60, a simple schedule may show 50 units of output and 30 cost as a starting trace, but revenue measurement needs actual customer contracts, price and control transfer. Conversely a joint venture with profit 40 would give an illustrative share of result 20 under equity method, adjusted for policy and basis differences; do not recognize half of its sales and cost. If a third party can unilaterally direct key production activities, retest joint control entirely. A party's guarantee may create additional obligation outside its 50% nominal share.

**Framework and QA.** US ASC 810/323 and joint venture guidance, UK FRS 102 Section 15 and AASB 11/128 require independent classification/reporting. IFRS 11 is [officially described](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-11-joint-arrangements/) as rights/obligations based, and IAS 28 governs joint venture equity method. Verify operative Australian compilation and FRS 102 period (2024 review principally 2026); do not apply a pending IAS 28 exposure draft. Reject classification based only on corporate form or 50/50 votes; preserve rights matrix, disclosure under IFRS 12 where applicable, group journal and reviewer conclusion.
