## Purpose
Maintain complete accounting-policy and estimate inventories and convert estimates into controlled, reviewable methodologies.

## Estimate inventory
Estimate name; FS line/assertion; framework; method/model; population; source data; assumptions; specialist input; uncertainty; sensitivity; owner/reviewer; frequency; JE/disclosure; controls; back-testing; change triggers.

## Methodology
Define measurement objective; identify data population; select method consistent with applicable guidance; evidence assumptions; calculate and sensitivity-test; compare prior estimates to outcomes; challenge bias; record overlays; reconcile output to GL/reporting; disclose uncertainty where required.

## Boundary
Estimate revisions from new information are not automatically errors. Policy, estimate and error classification is resolved before transition accounting.
