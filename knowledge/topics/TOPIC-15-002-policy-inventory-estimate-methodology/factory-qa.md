## Inventory completeness and framework map

Maintain separate policy and estimate registers linked by financial-statement assertion and transaction class. Policy inventory: entity/framework/period, product/transaction population, measurement and classification rules, elections, owner, standard/source version, exception, related systems/controls, disclosure and last review. Estimate inventory: account and unit of account, measurement objective, data population, method, inputs/assumptions, model version, uncertainty range, expert, owner, frequency, back-test, journal and disclosure. A policy can produce a monetary estimate; do not classify every method change as policy. [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/) and [AASB 108](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-108-mar-2021) distinguish input/technique estimate changes from measurement-basis policy changes or corrections of error; [FRS 102 Section 10](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) requires the operative edition. US [ASC 250](https://asc.fasb.org/) and topic-specific accounting guidance control; public paragraph-level access limitation remains. Tier 2 Australian disclosure exceptions are tested separately, not inferred from recognition alignment.

## Execution and worked estimate

For an AR loss allowance, start with a validated gross aging population, not the prior-period allowance. Example: current 0–30 days 4m at supported 1%; 31–90 days 1m at 5%; over 90 days 0.5m at 20%. Before forward-looking adjustment, illustrative allowance = 40k+50k+100k=190k. If a separately supported scenario overlay adds 25k without overlap, result 215k. Check the 5.5m gross receivables to AR subledger and GL, customer disputes/write-offs, historical loss calibration, macro forecast and overlay governance. A prior-year actual loss of 140k versus forecast 180k is a back-test signal, not proof of a 40k prior-period error. Document what information existed then and why the earlier estimate was reasonable; route omitted available data to TOPIC-15-006. Book illustrative adjustment only against existing allowance: if it is 170k, Dr ECL expense 45k / Cr allowance 45k, subject to actual accounting framework and taxes. The inventory links this model to credit-control data and reporting notes, not simply a monthly journal.

## Controls and adverse cases

Quarterly inventory completeness from GL, new product/contracts, acquisition and disclosure checklist; annual policy reelection/source review; estimate input snapshot and model access control; independent data/population tie-out, assumption challenge, sensitivity and hindsight-free back-test; approval of exception/change. S1: policy register omits a new lease class → fail completeness despite GL tie. S2: 5.5m aging but GL AR 5.7m → hold 215k conclusion until 0.2m explained. S3: 190k + 25k = 215k and allowance 170k → journal 45k; PASS arithmetic. S4: previous estimate differs from realized loss without evidence available at that earlier date → no automatic error. S5: management changes from cost to revaluation basis and calls it estimate → reject classification. Evidence: signed registers, source export, spreadsheet/code hash, population reconciliation, assumption memo, journals and disclosures.
