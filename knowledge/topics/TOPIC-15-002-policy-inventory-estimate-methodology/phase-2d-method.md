# Policy inventory, estimate method and changes — integrated canonical method

Canonical CAO-15-004–006. This incorporates the retained `TOPIC-15-002-accounting-estimates-changes` analysis without deleting it; CAO-15-007 chronology is mapped to 15-003 and error analysis to 15-006. `canonical-crosswalk.md` remains the path map. Claims are indexed in `standards-claims.json`.

## Classify before calculating

Maintain a policy register by entity/framework/period, transaction class, measurement basis/election, adoption history, disclosure, owner and change authority. Maintain a separate estimate register by unit/monetary amount, measurement objective, population, model/input version, source cut-off, uncertainty/range, approval and back-test. A technique or input change because of new information is generally an estimate change; changing a measurement basis may be a policy change. A failure to use reliable information available when prior statements were authorized routes to error evaluation, not a prospective-estimate shield. Back-test deviation alone is not proof of error [15-002-IFRS-01; 15-002-AASB-01; 15-002-UK-01; 15-002-US-01].

IFRS IAS 8/AASB 108 use prospective recognition for an estimate revision affecting current/future periods and disclosure of nature/amount where material and practicable. Policy change and prior error require their separate retrospective routes subject to transition/practicability. UK FRS 102 Section 10 classifies policy, estimate and error under its own wording and edition; the underlying estimate still follows Section 11/12, 17 or 27 as applicable. US ASC 250 similarly uses prospective accounting for estimates; a depreciation-method change may be an estimate effected by a principle change. US reporting entity status and topic-specific measurement exceptions matter, with disclosure of material changes. The IFRS 18/IAS 8 and AASB 108 2027 presentation/version gate and 2026 UK Periodic Review gate are recorded by reporting period [15-002-IFRS-01; 15-002-AASB-01; 15-002-UK-01; 15-002-US-01].

## ECL/CECL worked method

Existing aged AR population 4.0m at 1%, 1.0m at 5%, 0.5m at 20% produces 190,000; justified, nonduplicative forward overlay 25,000 produces allowance 215,000. If booked allowance is 170,000, Dr loss expense 45,000 / Cr allowance 45,000. Validate 5.5m gross AR to subledger/GL, customer cohorts, historical loss calibration, current conditions, reasonable supportable forecast, collections, disputes and write-offs. Reconcile allowance opening + charges − write-offs + recoveries/FX to closing and tie disclosure [15-002-IFRS-02; 15-002-US-02; 15-002-AASB-02].

IFRS 9/AASB 9 trade receivables can use the simplified lifetime-ECL route where eligible; prospective information is still required. US ASC 326 CECL uses its own expected-loss method, with ASU 2025-05 optional current ASC 606 receivable/contract-asset practical expedient and narrower non-public subsequent-collection election effective for periods beginning after 15 December 2025. Determine eligible population and election before using the example; do not transplant an IFRS provision matrix mechanically. UK FRS 102 financial-instrument impairment must be assessed under Sections 11/12 and the adopted version rather than called IFRS 9 ECL by default [15-002-IFRS-02; 15-002-US-02; 15-002-UK-02].

Adverse cases: gross GL 5.7m with only 5.5m aged fails completeness; a 25,000 overlay duplicates a cohort already raised to 20% fails double-count control; later 140,000 actual losses against prior 180,000 estimate alone do not prove a 40,000 error; deliberate omission of a known material customer default at prior authorization routes to 15-006. For an eligible nonpublic US company electing subsequent collections, document cut-off, election consistency and disclosures; a public company cannot silently take that narrower election [15-002-US-02].

## Workpaper and systems

For each material estimate preserve dated policy/standard version; objective; population extract and hash; GL reconciliation; method/code version; assumptions and evidence; specialist work; sensitivity and bias challenge; prior-outcome back-test; adjustment JE; note wording; owner/reviewer and board/audit committee escalation. Systems retain *known-at* dates separately from transaction dates, so later collections and forecasts cannot contaminate the prior decision. Independent reviewer challenges method changes and missing cohorts, not just formula arithmetic. Presentation/disclosure route is specific to the governing measurement topic and entity reporting tier. The duplicate folder remains read-only substantive evidence and the canonical output is this folder [15-002-IFRS-01; 15-002-US-02].
