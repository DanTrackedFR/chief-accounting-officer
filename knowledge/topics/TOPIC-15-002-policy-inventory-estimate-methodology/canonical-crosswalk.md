## Versioned source route

- [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/) addresses policies, estimates and errors; its 2021 amendments clarified input/technique changes, while IFRS 18 renames/restructures IAS 8 from periods beginning 1 January 2027 unless adopted early. A specific ECL measurement still requires IFRS 9; impairment needs IAS 36.
- [AASB 108 March 2021 compilation](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-108-mar-2021) applies to periods beginning 1 January 2023 but before 1 January 2027; later periods require the subsequent AASB edition/amendments. AASB 1060 governs Tier 2 disclosure where relevant. AASB 9 rather than AASB 108 measures the ECL itself.
- [FRC FRS 102 Section 10](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) governs UK policy/estimate/error classification, with 2026 Periodic Review edition and transition selection. The UK receivable impairment model follows applicable Sections 11/12; do not transplant the IFRS 9 ECL illustration.
- US ASC 250 is the classification/change route and ASC 326 the CECL measurement route. FASB's original [ASU 2016-13](https://storage.fasb.org/ASU_2016-13.pdf) shows the historic ASC 326 expected-loss framework, including 326-20-30-5–6. Exact current ASC 250/326 paragraph applicability remains unverified in Basic View; the historical ASU does not establish the current 2026 paragraph body. Do not approve a US production ECL or estimate-change conclusion from these citations alone.

The 2026 US trade-receivable gate includes FASB [ASU 2025-05](https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202025-05.pdf), effective for annual periods beginning after 15 December 2025 and interim periods within, with early adoption permitted and prospective application to estimates made after adoption. The ASU introduces a practical expedient for eligible current ASC 606 receivables/contract assets and a related subsequent-collection policy election only for entities other than public business entities (amended references 326-20-30-10A–10H; disclosure 326-20-50-12A–12B). It is optional and requires consistent application to the eligible population; the illustrative 215k cannot silently assume the election, treat all receivables as in scope or disregard current conditions. A public entity cannot use the nonpublic subsequent-collection election. This is a verified amendment source and effective gate, not a representation that Basic View's entire current paragraph body was inspected.

FASB's [June 2024 PCC/SBAC accounting-change memo](https://storage.fasb.org/PCC%20SBAC-Topic%205-20240625.pdf) reproduces and discusses ASC 250-10-45-17–19 as then applicable: prospective estimate revision and the combined change-in-estimate/change-in-principle route for depreciation methods. This is official FASB meeting material rather than a current Codification inspection. The US approval reviewer must check 250-10-45-17–19 and 250-10-50-4 in Basic View as of the case period, then determine whether the change affects the present period only or also future periods, whether the governing asset/receivable topic has an exception, and what material disclosures are required. Retain a version/date and paragraph-to-conclusion table without reproducing ASC text.
