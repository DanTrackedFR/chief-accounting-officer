# Receivable ECL vintage, forward information and US election scope

**IFRS simplified-model illustration.** Trade AR gross 1,000 in four customer cohorts: current 600, 1–30 days past due 200, 31–90 days 150 and over 90 days 50. Historical/forward-adjusted lifetime ECL rates are 1%, 3%, 10% and 40%, producing allowance 6+6+15+20=47. These assumed rates require support: remove recoveries/write-offs outside cohort, distinguish customer geography/product, adjust for forward economic conditions and backtest prior estimates. A major customer in current bucket with specific insolvency evidence cannot be protected by generic 1%; overlay case-specific scenario or segmentation without double counting. Compare opening 40, new impairment 12, approved write-offs 5 to expected closing 47 if no other changes (40+12−5=47); reconcile to GL and disclosed movement. IFRS 9/AASB 9 simplified lifetime approach for eligible trade receivables differs from a stage-based loan model.

**US scope gate.** ASC 326 CECL is distinct. FASB ASU 2025-05 is effective for annual periods beginning after 15 December 2025 and interim periods therein; for eligible Topic 606 trade receivables and contract assets, it allows a particular forecasting expedient, and qualifying nonpublic entities may elect consideration of eligible post-balance-sheet collections. It is not a blanket 47-to-zero waiver or an option for all financial assets. Document entity eligibility, period, election and receipt evidence; verify current Codification before paragraph claims. UK FRS 102 Section 11 basic-financial-instrument impairment is not an IFRS 9 matrix by default. Australian compilation/tier must match start date.

**QA.** Reperform 47 and 40+12−5=47; fail 1% applied to known insolvent customer without overlay, a US 2025-05 expedient extended to loans, or allowance netted against unrelated customer credit. Retain invoice-level vintage, customer credit evidence, macro assumptions, model version, write-off approvals, GL and disclosure.
