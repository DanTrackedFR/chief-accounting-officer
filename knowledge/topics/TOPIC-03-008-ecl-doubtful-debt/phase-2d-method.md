## IFRS and Australia

Trade receivables/contract assets without significant financing use lifetime expected credit losses under the simplified approach. [TOPIC-03-008-IFRS-R002] [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R002]
For qualifying balances with significant financing, the simplified lifetime approach is a consistently applied policy election. [TOPIC-03-008-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R003]
Otherwise the general approach uses12-month ECL initially and lifetime ECL after a significant increase in credit risk, with credit-impaired interest on the net carrying basis. [TOPIC-03-008-IFRS-R004] [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R004]
Expected loss is an unbiased probability-weighted discounted cash-shortfall estimate using reasonable supportable historical, current and forward-looking information. [TOPIC-03-008-IFRS-R005] [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R005]
Group shared-risk exposures appropriately and assess significant individual risks without counting an exposure twice in an individual overlay and collective matrix. [TOPIC-03-008-IFRS-R006] [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R006]
A purchased/originated credit-impaired asset instead tracks changes in lifetime losses using its credit-adjusted effective interest rate. [TOPIC-03-008-IFRS-R007] [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R007]
Write off the uncollectible portion when there is no reasonable expectation of recovery; pursuing enforcement does not necessarily prevent an accounting write-off. [TOPIC-03-008-IFRS-R008] [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R008]

## US and UK routes

US eligible amortized-cost trade receivables and contract assets use lifetime CECL; there is no IFRS-style12-month stage for ordinary assets in this population. [TOPIC-03-008-US-R009]
Historical experience is adjusted for current conditions and reasonable supportable forecasts, then a documented reversion for periods beyond forecast support. [TOPIC-03-008-US-R010]
Assess receivables collectively where shared risk characteristics exist and individually where they do not; remove individually measured exposures from the pool. [TOPIC-03-008-US-R011]
Under adopted ASU2025-05, eligible current606 receivables/assets may use the current-conditions practical expedient; non-PBEs may additionally elect subsequent collections only when applying that expedient. [TOPIC-03-008-US-R012]
ASU2025-05 applies prospectively for annual starts after15December2025, with early adoption permitted; neither election extends to all loans or eliminates remaining-exposure estimation. [TOPIC-03-008-US-R013]
UK Sections11/12 uses objective loss evidence, measuring recoverable contractual cash flows under the applicable amortized-cost loss requirements; do not book a forecast-only matrix solely because IFRS would. [TOPIC-03-008-UK-R014]
UK elected IFRS9 recognition/measurement instead uses the applicable IFRS9 ECL route, while required disclosures remain Sections11/12. [TOPIC-03-008-UK-R015]
Existing eligible IAS39 policies continue under their period rules; a new IAS39 choice after the2026 change is restricted to qualifying group-consistency circumstances. [TOPIC-03-008-UK-R016]
FRS101 uses adopted-IFRS measurement and eligible note exemptions; FRS105 Section9 uses its own objective-evidence route without the FRS102 IFRS9 election. [TOPIC-03-008-UK-R017]
UK objective-evidence impairment reversals require supported improvement and are capped at the amount without impairment. [TOPIC-03-008-UK-R018]

## Period, disclosure and implementation

IFRS9/AASB9 original2018 adoption uses the relevant opening-equity transition/comparator rules; Australian NFP/customer scope and disclosure tier remain independently selected. [TOPIC-03-008-IFRS-R019] [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R019]
US CECL original adoption is selected by public/SEC/smaller-reporting and nonpublic status, including eligible deferrals; current all-entity adoption does not erase historical comparative methods. [TOPIC-03-008-US-R020]
Changes in a supported loss estimate go through current loss expense/reversal; correcting a material previous error follows the separate error model. [TOPIC-03-008-IFRS-R021] [TOPIC-03-008-US-R021] [TOPIC-03-008-UK-R021] [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R021]
IFRS/AASB Tier1 notes include methods/assumptions, risk concentrations and loss-allowance movements with relevant stage and gross-balance information. [TOPIC-03-008-IFRS-R022] [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R022]
US notes identify portfolio risk, methodology/forecasts, allowance movements and elected applicable relief; UK disclosures follow the selected regime, not automatic IFRS7. [TOPIC-03-008-US-R023] [TOPIC-03-008-UK-R023]
Eligible Australian Tier2 uses AASB1060 financial-instrument disclosures rather than a private-entity label excusing all loss notes. [TOPIC-03-008-AASB-R024]
