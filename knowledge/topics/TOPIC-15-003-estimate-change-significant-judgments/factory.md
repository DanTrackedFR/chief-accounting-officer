# Estimate change and significant judgment identification — substantive factory

Case approval gate: establish which asset guidance governs the useful-life illustration, prove the revised forecast became available after prior authorization and check whether the earlier estimate was reasonable with then-available information. For US cases retrieve current ASC 250 and asset-specific ASC 360 text through authorized access. Later deterioration alone does not prove prior error.

Companion to existing README. CAO-15-007/008. Source checked 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate; actual accounting judgment depends on governing transaction standard.

## Distinct classification pathways

An accounting estimate is a monetary amount subject to measurement uncertainty; a significant judgment often decides whether an accounting criterion applies. A prior error concerns misuse or omission of information available/reasonably obtainable when the earlier financials were authorized. Freeze the chronology of fact, knowledge and evidence. [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/) and [AASB 108](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-108-mar-2021) guide prospective estimate change versus policy/error; operative [FRS 102 Section 10](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) has its own treatment, version gated for 2026; US ASC 250 guidance must be checked in current [FASB Codification](https://asc.fasb.org/). The specific measurement rule still belongs to the governing topic: IAS 16 useful lives, IFRS 9 ECL, IAS 36 impairment, ASC 360 or FRS 102. IFRS 18 early application/2027 effective date changes cross-references to IAS 8 basis-of-preparation content; record edition.

## CAO decision tree

1. Specify prior conclusion and proposed change, affected population/line, relevant periods and financial magnitude. 2. Determine whether the measurement basis or permitted accounting principle changes; if yes, route to policy. 3. Ask what reliable evidence existed at the prior reporting authorization date and whether it was used. If omitted or misapplied, investigate error. 4. If new information/developments legitimately change an input, measurement technique or useful life under same policy, apply the estimate change prospectively to current/future periods affected. 5. Separately identify material judgments such as control, lease term, principal/agent or CGU grouping; document alternative interpretation and disconfirming facts. 6. Recalculate entries and disclosures and send prior-period matters through materiality/restatement workflow. Avoid hindsight from subsequent outcome alone.

## Worked life revision and journal

An asset cost 1.2m, accumulated depreciation 0.3m, carrying 0.9m at revision date, zero residual. Original remaining three years yields 0.3m annual depreciation. New credible engineering evidence supports five remaining years; annual charge becomes 0.18m, 0.12m reduction each full year (before impairment/residual changes). Dr depreciation expense 0.18m / Cr accumulated depreciation 0.18m for the first full revised year. Do not restate prior 0.3m without evidence of error. If an old maintenance report showing five-year life was available and negligently ignored before earlier authorization, stop and assess prior error rather than automatic estimate change. Document engineering source, approval date, condition and usage, impairment indicators, accounting effect, model version and disclosure.

## Judgment register, controls and tests

Judgment register fields: criterion, facts, material alternative, evidence both ways, monetary effect, owner, external specialist and reassessment trigger. Estimate-change workpaper adds old/new inputs, chronology, prospective calculation, GL/reporting tie, sensitivity and communication. Controls: quarterly significant true-up review; changed-assumption alert; independent hindsight check; model access; approval of high-impact judgment; consistency across contracts; disclosure tie-out. S1: newer failure rates change warranty provision → estimate route. S2: prior formula omits known AR cohorts → potential error. S3: lease renewal term decision changes due to approved strategic plan → reassess judgment and lease remeasurement; do not treat as simple journal plug. S4: life revision arithmetic above → 0.12m annual difference PASS. Residual exact US ASC text must be verified for a production conclusion; no invented paragraph citation.
