# TOPIC-07-009 — claim-linked group accounting case

Claims: [TOPIC-07-009-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-07-009-US-001], [TOPIC-07-009-UK-001], [TOPIC-07-009-AASB-001]. Retain existing factory.

## Calculation and adverse route

At control loss, consolidated net assets including goodwill are 500, NCI carrying 100, sale proceeds 600 and retained 10% interest fair value 70. Before OCI/tax, gain is 600+100+70−500=270. Do not use the retained investment’s prior carrying amount as its initial post-control-loss value. A partial sale retaining control is instead an equity transaction. Reconcile acquisition-date control, purchase price, NCI, PPA and goodwill into disposal date; separately identify recycling or transfer of each OCI reserve under its own standard.

## Evidence and framework regression

Prepare signed rights/ownership memo, consolidated journal with entity and counterparty IDs, source rate or valuation, reserve/NCI rollforward, tax bridge and disclosure tie-out. Test the opposite classification under US ASC 810/323/805, UK FRS 102 and AASB before asserting convergence. Select effective period and reporting tier separately. These four claim IDs remain the source-audit queue; IFRS core route uses 2026 issued text.
