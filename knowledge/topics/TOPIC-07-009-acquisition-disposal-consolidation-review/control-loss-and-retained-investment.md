# Loss of control: complete group gain bridge

**Illustrative IFRS fact set.** At disposal date a consolidated subsidiary's group carrying net assets including any relevant goodwill are 500, NCI carrying amount is 100, sale consideration for parent's interest is 450, and parent retains a minority investment with fair value 120. Assume control is lost, no disposal costs/tax and no OCI reclassification in the simplified calculation. Gain is consideration 450 + retained-interest FV 120 + derecognized NCI 100 − derecognized net assets 500 = 170. Do not compare 450 solely with parent's share of 500 or leave retained interest at its old share of book value. Derecognize all subsidiary assets/liabilities and NCI, then recognize retained interest at the applicable initial basis. If accumulated foreign-currency translation balance or other OCI exists, resolve reclassification/reallocation under the governing standard and adjust the gain bridge; this 170 would then be incomplete.

**Boundary.** A 10% sale while control remains belongs to equity-transaction accounting rather than this loss-of-control calculation. A sale signed 30 September that closes 15 October may require control-date evidence; do not cut off consolidation solely on SPA signature. If residual stake confers significant influence or joint control, route subsequent measurement to IAS 28/IFRS 11; otherwise assess IFRS 9. Business sale may also invoke held-for-sale/discontinued-operation presentation (TOPIC-13-004) and tax/cash-flow effects, independent of gain calculation. For a newly acquired subsidiary, coordinate IFRS 3 acquisition date/PPA and avoid including pre-control results.

**Evidence/tests.** Independently tie 500 to group TB including acquisition basis and goodwill, 100 to NCI rollforward, 450 to SPA/bank and 120 to dated valuation; test 450+120+100−500=170. Fail retaining subsidiary balances, omitting NCI, using book value for retained stake without analysis, or assuming US ASC 810/FRS 102 Section 9/AASB 10 exactly identical.
