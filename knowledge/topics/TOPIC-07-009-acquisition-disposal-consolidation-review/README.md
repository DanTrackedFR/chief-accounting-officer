# TOPIC-07-009 — Entity Acquisition / Disposal Consolidation / Consolidation Reconciliation & Review

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**  
Capabilities: CAO-07-017, CAO-07-018  
Sensitivity: H

## Purpose
Operationalize entry to and exit from the consolidation perimeter and provide a final CAO review that proves the consolidated result is complete, mathematically coherent, technically routed and auditable.

This topic coordinates—not replaces—business-combination, control, disposal, discontinued-operation, FX and equity-method topics.

## Acquisition consolidation
1. Establish transaction/effective control date from executed facts, not invoice/payment date.
2. Resolve whether transaction is a business combination, asset acquisition, common-control transaction or other event under applicable framework.
3. Lock pre/post ownership and control conclusion.
4. Obtain opening acquired TB and map to group COA; align accounting policies.
5. Route purchase accounting/PPA, identifiable assets/liabilities, consideration, goodwill/bargain purchase, NCI and deferred-tax interactions to the appropriate complex-transaction knowledge.
6. Consolidate results only from the date control is obtained, subject to applicable requirements.
7. Eliminate investment/equity and intragroup balances; establish opening NCI/CTA/acquisition reserves.
8. Build acquisition-to-consolidated-opening reconciliation and disclosure dataset.

## Disposal consolidation
1. Determine disposal date and whether control is retained or lost.
2. If control remains, route to ownership-change accounting; do not use loss-of-control model.
3. If control is lost, derecognize the subsidiary group balances/NCI and account for consideration/retained interest under applicable framework.
4. Analyze each accumulated OCI component for reclassification/transfer treatment; no blanket rule.
5. Separate disposal accounting from discontinued-operations presentation assessment.
6. Reconcile disposal proceeds/noncash consideration, derecognized net assets, NCI, retained interest and gain/loss.
7. Update perimeter, consolidation rules, intercompany relationships and reporting-package requirements from effective date.

## Framework routing
IFRS: IFRS 10 for control/loss of control and consolidation, IFRS 3 for business combinations, IAS 28 for retained associate/JV, IAS 21 for foreign-operation currency effects, IFRS 5 where discontinued-operation/held-for-sale assessment is relevant. AASB equivalents require operative-version checks; AASB 2024-4 creates a specific future effective-date gate for sale/contribution amendments. UK GAAP routes through FRS 102 Sections 9/14/15/19/30 and applicable company law. US GAAP routes through ASC 810 plus ASC 805/323/830/205-20 as relevant; paragraph-level authority remains PARTIAL where public Codification text is unavailable.

## Consolidation reconciliation & CAO review
Mandatory bridges:
- entity signed TB aggregate → mapped/translated TB;
- mapped/translated TB + consolidation journals = consolidated TB;
- opening consolidated equity + current movements = closing equity;
- NCI opening + share of results/OCI + transactions/distributions = closing NCI;
- CTA/foreign-currency reserve rollforward;
- intercompany matched population → eliminated population → unresolved difference;
- acquisition/disposal reserves and goodwill rollforwards;
- consolidated TB → primary statements → notes/disclosures.

Review analytics: entity contribution to P&L/BS/cash flow; unexplained retained earnings; abnormal signs; new/unmapped accounts; elimination changes; top-side journal concentration; FX/CTA reasonableness; ownership/NCI movement; balance-sheet movement; cash-flow balance; disclosure cross-footing.

## Stop conditions
Do not certify consolidation if: perimeter/control is unresolved; material entity TB is uncertified; material mapping is incomplete; material intercompany breaks are plugged; unexplained top-side entries remain; opening equity does not bridge; acquisition/disposal effective date is unsupported; financial statements do not tie to consolidated TB.

## Controls / evidence
Legal-close/effective-date evidence; perimeter approval; acquired/disposed TB; PPA or transaction paper; mapping/policy alignment; ownership/NCI schedule; journal approvals; intercompany elimination; FX/CTA schedule; final consolidation reconciliation; financial-statement tie-out; disclosure checklist; reviewer certification and unresolved-item log.

## Scenario tests
- Deal legally signs 28 June but control transfers 3 July: use control date, not signing date, for consolidation entry. **PASS**.
- Sale leaves 60% and control: route to TOPIC-07-008 within-equity model rather than loss-of-control disposal. **PASS**.
- Sale loses control and retains 25% with significant influence: route retained interest to equity-method architecture after loss-of-control accounting. **PASS**.
- Consolidated TB balances but opening equity bridge fails: certification blocked; balancing is necessary, not sufficient. **PASS**.
- Material intercompany difference remains: no plug; root-cause workflow. **PASS**.
