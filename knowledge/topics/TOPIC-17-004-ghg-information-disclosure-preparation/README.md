# TOPIC-17-004 — GHG information dependencies and sustainability disclosure

Status: DRAFT for independent Phase 2D QA; canonical capabilities: CAO-17-008, CAO-17-009. Prepared 2026-09-27. This topic contains independently authored decision and execution guidance; restricted standards and regulations are reference-only.

## Principles and scope

GHG quantities and sustainability disclosure assertions have specific boundary, unit and methodology dependencies. An accounting GL is only one evidence input. Identify the legal entity, jurisdiction, report date, group perimeter and applicable framework before evaluating requirements. This is a regulatory overlay, not a replacement for financial-statement recognition and measurement under IFRS, US GAAP, FRS 102 or AASB.

## Authoritative source and effective-period gates

- ISSB: [IFRS S1](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/ifrs-sustainability-standards-navigator/ifrs-s1-general-requirements/) and [IFRS S2](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/ifrs-sustainability-standards-navigator/ifrs-s2-climate-related-disclosures/) effective from periods beginning 1 January 2024, subject to jurisdiction adoption and transitions.
- Australia: [AASB S2](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-s2-sep-2024) effective from periods beginning 1 January 2025; [ASIC cohort test](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/sustainability-reporting/for-preparers-of-sustainability-reports/who-must-prepare-a-sustainability-report/) distinguishes statutory start dates and entity eligibility. AASB S1 is voluntary unless separately adopted.
- UK: [government UK SRS status](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/uk-sustainability-reporting-standards): S1/S2 issued February2026 for voluntary use. Separately, [FCA PS26/19](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps26-19-aligning-listed-issuers-sustainability-disclosures-international-standards), published30September2026, finalises in-scope listed-issuer comply-or-explain rules for periods beginning1January2027, with specified Scope3/climate-first relief; identify actual listing category and prior-period route. Issuance alone does not make all UK entities mandatory reporters.
- EU: [Commission CSRD and amendments timeline](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/financial-markets/company-reporting-and-auditing/company-reporting/corporate-sustainability-reporting_en), ESRS and local transposition; Directive (EU) 2026/470 is in force and narrows core scope to undertakings exceeding both EUR 450 million net turnover and 1,000 average employees, with group, issuer, transition and Member State options to verify from the [Official Journal text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2026/470/oj/eng). Check local transposition and the relevant financial year; historical wave-one obligations may differ.
- US: [SEC climate rule defense decision](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2025-58) and [2026 proposed rescission](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/05/s7-2026-19). A proposal is not a final rule; check current litigation/stay and other disclosure obligations.
Source check: 2026-09-27, official publisher pages. Exact paragraph and form instructions require verification for the specific entity and period; FASB Codification paragraph bodies were not relied upon. Treat amended laws, pending rules, adoption elections and transition relief separately. Do not infer a mandatory obligation solely from a standard's publication date.

## CAO decision and execution

Identify Scope 1 direct sources, Scope 2 purchased energy and relevant Scope 3 value-chain categories under the adopted standard; check permitted transition relief and disclosure timing. Gather fuel, electricity, supplier, travel and activity data by site and period; select applicable emission factors and global warming potentials; calculate activity × factor with unit conversion; disclose methods, exclusions, estimates, uncertainty and category coverage. Link governance, strategy, risk management, metrics and targets to the same controlled risk/metric register.

Workpaper minimum: entity and period; governing rule and URL/version; applicability facts; scope/boundary; inputs and provenance; calculation/mapping; alternatives and judgments; review/sign-off; reporting output; exceptions, remediation and change log. Control owner reconciles source population to accounting or operational systems, and a second reviewer checks the conclusion against the effective rule. Preserve submission/assurance evidence and the signed version.

## Worked application and boundaries

100,000 kWh × 0.4 kg CO2e/kWh = 40,000 kg = 40 tCO2e. Confirm factor geography, year, Scope 2 method and meter population before publishing; the arithmetic alone cannot validate the disclosed Scope 2 total.

For each workpaper consider materiality, disclosure, data quality, presentation, applicable assurance and whether a discovered difference changes an accounting entry. Route underlying accounting recognition/measurement to the governing financial topic. Do not post a journal solely to make a regulatory return or sustainability metric reconcile; document the rule-specific bridge.

## Executed desk scenarios (decision-route QA)

Scope 3 is omitted without checking applicable relief: FAIL; factor units changed without validating both activity and output conversion: FAIL (a correctly converted t/MWh factor has the same numerical value in kg/kWh and passes); unresolved category excluded with evidenced materiality assessment: conditional PASS. These checks exercise decision routing only; they do not certify a live entity's filing or a paragraph-level legal conclusion. A production case must collect actual evidence and rerun current-rule verification.
