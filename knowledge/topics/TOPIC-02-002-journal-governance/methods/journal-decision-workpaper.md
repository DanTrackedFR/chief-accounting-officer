# Journal preparation, approval and exception workpaper

Extends existing README/factory/knowledge pack for TOPIC-02-002. Capabilities CAO-02-004–006. PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE; 2026-09-27.

## Minimum case record

Identify event, entity/book/currency, reporting period, accounting policy and source. Reconcile source population to calculation, then calculation to balanced entry and posting batch. Classify entry as routine recurring, estimate, correction, consolidation, reclass or exceptional. Capture preparer, qualified independent reviewer, approval before posting, date, posting ID, reversal and subsequent true-up. If segregation cannot be achieved, document a timely compensating review by someone who can challenge substance and access; self-approval by a privileged user is not cured by retrospective signature.

Illustration: December services received 75,000; 50,000 invoices have posted, 25,000 remains unbilled. If the 75,000 source is complete and contracts/support confirm service receipt, post only debit expense 25,000 / credit accrued liability 25,000. An accrual of 75,000 would double count the posted 50,000. On January invoice arrival, reverse/settle with traceable identifiers and investigate variance, not delete the December workpaper. A 2,000 difference between accrued and actual gets its own period/materiality assessment. If the December receipt is not evidenced, the case remains open; an arbitrary reserve is not an alternative.

## Control and system execution

Preparer access cannot approve its own entry; approval workflow checks closed period, valid entity/account/dimension, balanced amount, duplicate source ID and unusual-user/time/rule indicators. Reviewer checks substance, estimate reasonableness, support completeness, calculation and period. Batch completeness reconciles approved to posted and rejected journals, with repair owner. Maintain immutable version of source extract, calculation, approval and post/reversal IDs; include manual top-side entries outside subledgers in the GL integrity review.

**Negative test:** controller posts the 25,000 in a locked period using emergency rights and approves it personally. Result FAIL even if amount is right; log privileged action, require independent timely review, consider control deficiency and examine whether other entries used same pathway. Underlying expense/obligation recognition follows the applicable topic rather than a universal journal standard. [PCAOB AS 2401](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2401) informs US auditor management-override procedures; this is not a universal company accounting obligation.
