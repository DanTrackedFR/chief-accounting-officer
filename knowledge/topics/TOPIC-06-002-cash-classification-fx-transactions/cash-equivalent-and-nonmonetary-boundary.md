# Cash equivalent, prepayment and settlement boundary

**Instrument classification.** Treasury buys a six-month deposit with four months remaining at 31 December. A residual maturity of four months does not determine whether it was a cash equivalent; assess short-term highly liquid nature, original acquisition purpose/maturity, known cash amount, insignificant value-change risk and withdrawal restrictions under IAS 7/AASB 107. A three-month original deposit used for short-term cash commitments may qualify if all criteria hold, whereas a three-month residual term on a long-duration investment need not. Under US ASC 230 and FRS 102 Section 7, apply framework-specific definitions and policy; record consistent classification and disclose relevant restrictions. Reconcile bank confirmation, instrument schedule, treasury purpose and cash-flow definition. An overnight overdraft may have different cash-flow and balance-sheet treatment depending on contractual demand and integral cash-management facts.

**FX boundary.** EUR-functional company prepays USD 1,000 for a service when EUR/USD is 1.25; assume payment creates a non-refundable, non-monetary right to receive services and no foreign-currency refund right. Initial prepayment is EUR 800. At year end rate 1.00, do not mechanically remeasure that historical-cost non-monetary prepayment to EUR 1,000; retain EUR 800 before service consumption/impairment. In contrast an unpaid USD 1,000 monetary payable initially EUR 800 would be remeasured to EUR 1,000, with 200 loss. If contract permits cash refund in USD, reassess monetary classification. IAS 21/IFRIC 22 considerations on advance consideration may affect transaction date for later recognition; avoid blanket analogies.

**Controls.** Classify each balance by contractual right (cash versus service), rate date and source; reconcile cash receipts/payments to bank and monetary FX to ledger. Fail a USD prepayment blindly remeasured, a long-term investment relabeled cash solely from residual life, or restricted account omitted from disclosure. Official [IAS 7](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows/), [IAS 21](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/); current ASC and AASB compilation needed for production.
