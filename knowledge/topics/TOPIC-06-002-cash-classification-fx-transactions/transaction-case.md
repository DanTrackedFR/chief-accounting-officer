# Foreign-currency transaction and cash classification case

Capabilities CAO-06-004/005. Existing standards, method and tests retained in both duplicate paths. First establish functional currency (TOPIC-06-004) and classification of the underlying item; this illustration assumes EUR functional currency and USD monetary trade payable.

An entity purchases services for USD 1,000 when EUR/USD is 1.25 USD per EUR: initial expense/payable EUR 800. At period end EUR/USD is 1.20: unpaid USD 1,000 liability EUR 833.33, so Dr FX loss 33.33 / Cr payable 33.33. On settlement at EUR/USD 1.00: cash EUR 1,000; Dr payable 833.33, Dr FX loss 166.67, Cr cash 1,000. Total expense plus FX cost EUR 1,000. **Executed:** 1,000/1.25=800; 1,000/1.20=833.33 rounded; 1,000−833.33=166.67; total FX loss 200. PASS subject to rate direction/precision. A historical-cost non-monetary prepayment does not follow the same closing-rate remeasurement; route underlying service timing separately.

For a three-month readily convertible investment, assess cash-equivalent purpose, acquisition maturity and insignificant value risk rather than blindly treating a three-month instrument as cash. Demand deposit restrictions, overdrafts and cash-flow classification require entity terms and framework-specific assessment. Source families IAS 7/IAS 21, ASC 230/830, FRS 102 Sections 7/30 and AASB 107/121; exact current paragraph map for US/UK/AASB and instrument terms remain PARTIAL. Preserve contract/invoice, rates and rate-source timestamps, settlement bank, monetary classification, FX journal and cash-equivalent eligibility review.
