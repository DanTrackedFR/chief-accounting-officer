# TOPIC-16-002 — Annual & Interim Filing Accounting Support
Capabilities: CAO-16-004, CAO-16-005
Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Knowledge type: JURISDICTION / PRACTICE

## Objective
Operate accounting support for annual and interim public/regulatory filings so reported numbers, accounting judgments, disclosures and certifications remain consistent with the books and applicable filing requirements.

## Filing operating model
Create one filing plan linking close, consolidation, technical memos, disclosure drafts, audit/review, legal review, board/audit committee, certifications, structured-data production and submission. Assign a single accountable owner for every financial statement, note and accounting-dependent non-financial disclosure.

## Annual filing workflow
1. Confirm jurisdiction, form, reporting framework, reporting period and filing deadline.
2. Freeze scope and disclosure checklist from current authoritative requirements.
3. Reconcile trial balance/consolidation to primary statements and note tables.
4. Inventory significant judgments, estimates, unusual transactions, new standards, restatements/error corrections and subsequent events.
5. Link every material accounting conclusion to an approved memo/workpaper.
6. Run disclosure tie-out across statements, notes, MD&A/management report and other filing sections.
7. Clear audit and legal comments through controlled issue logs.
8. Complete management/committee approvals and certifications.
9. Validate structured-data output and final rendered document against approved source.
10. Submit, retain acceptance evidence and perform post-filing lessons learned.

## Interim workflow
Do not treat interim reporting as a shortened annual close. Identify the applicable interim framework and regulator rules; update material estimates and judgments; assess seasonality, impairment indicators, acquisitions/disposals, tax rate, contingencies, subsequent events and going concern; reconcile year-to-date and comparative information; and track annual-disclosure items that require interim update.

## US example
Domestic SEC reporting companies generally use Forms 10-K and 10-Q, while foreign private issuers follow different forms and periodic reporting. Filing requirements, certifications, review requirements and deadlines depend on issuer/filer status. Confirm current SEC form instructions and rules for the actual registrant.

## UK example
For in-scope issuers, FCA DTR 4 governs periodic financial reporting, including annual-report content and timing; applicable interim requirements must be checked against the issuer's status and DTR provisions.

## Controls
Disclosure checklist version/effective date; GL-to-filing reconciliation; controlled disclosure change log; preparer/reviewer evidence; cross-reference/tie-out matrix; audit/legal issue tracker; certification support package; final-document checksum/version lock; submission dual control and accepted receipt.

## Failure modes
Copying last year's filing without current-rule check; disclosure numbers maintained outside controlled sources; late technical conclusions; inconsistent adjusted/non-GAAP metrics; untracked post-audit changes; XBRL/tagging changes after accounting sign-off; assuming annual policies automatically answer interim recognition/measurement questions.
