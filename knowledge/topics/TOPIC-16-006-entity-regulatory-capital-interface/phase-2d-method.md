# Entity regulatory capital interface — completion method

Canonical CAO-16-012/013. This integrates `factory.md` and `sector-methods.md`. Regulatory claims live in `standards-claims.json`; financial-statement recognition remains with the relevant accounting topic.

## Applicability tree and framework boundary

Start with legal entity, licence/permission, supervisor, solo versus consolidated perimeter, report date, reporting form and operative prudential rule. An accounting equity balance is a source input, not regulatory eligible capital; risk exposure, actuarial liabilities, own-funds quality, deductions, buffers, restrictions and waivers are independently calculated. IFRS/US/UK/AASB financial statements retain their own recognition/measurement; a regulator-only filter usually belongs in the return bridge rather than a GL journal. If a source accounting error is discovered, correct it through the accounting topic and regenerate the return [16-006-REG-01; 16-006-REG-02].

Australian ADI: establish APS 110 applicability and Level 1/Level 2 perimeter, APS 111 eligible capital and deductions, risk-weighted assets from operative risk standards, institution-specific PCR/buffers and return. Existing arithmetic: illustrative eligible CET1 20m − 3m qualifying intangible deduction − 1m evidenced adjustment = 16m; RWA 160m gives 10%. Against an **assumed** 11% combined requirement, required 17.6m and deficit 1.6m. Actual PCR and buffers must replace the hypothetical threshold [16-006-REG-01].

Australian general insurer: GPS 110/112 determine capital base and prescribed capital amount; reconcile insurance/asset/concentration/operational risk components, reinsurance, tax and supervisory adjustment. Existing 30m base / 24m **assumed** PCR = 125%, 6m headroom; omitted 8m catastrophe charge changes PCR to 32m, coverage 93.75%, deficit 2m. General insurer returns and assurance depend on the operative GPS/GRS version and actual licence [16-006-REG-02]. Australian life insurer: LPS 110/112 test statutory funds and company-level requirements; Fund A 12m/10m surplus 2m, Fund B 7m/9m deficit 2m. A zero aggregate headroom does not clear Fund B; check transfer restrictions and actual directions. LPS 112's July 2026 edition is a specific effective-date gate [16-006-REG-03].

UK authorised payment institution: Payment Services Regulations 2017 regulation 22 and Schedule 3/FCA method or direction determine ongoing own funds; compare applicable initial-capital floor and method-based requirement. An **assumed** 125k floor, 180k calculated requirement and 210k eligible funds yield 30k headroom; a valid 40k deduction makes 170k eligible and a 10k shortfall. Verify service categories and safeguarding separately: safeguarded client money is not freely available own funds. EU payment entity follows PSD2 Articles 7–9 and domestic implementation/current successor rules rather than UK calculations [16-006-REG-04].

## Close workpaper and adverse tests

Store licence number, jurisdiction, entity/group map, period, rule instrument/version/effective date, return cell, accounting source, regulatory adjustment with provision and sign, instrument eligibility, denominator/risk-engine source, FX, waiver, model hash, owner, reviewer, escalation, filed form and receipt. Reconcile TB/equity to capital tiers, technical liabilities and RWA/PCR separately, then to every return cell. Independent reviewer checks perimeter and threshold, version, signs and new acquisitions; negative headroom triggers immediate compliance/CFO escalation rather than waiting for month end. Controls include role separation between accounting source preparation and regulatory interpretation, source population totals, version freeze, supervisor correspondence and post-filing reconciliation [16-006-REG-01; 16-006-REG-02; 16-006-REG-03; 16-006-REG-04].

Fail: consolidated bank capital with solo RWA; insurance risk charge taken from an outdated standard; Fund B deficit hidden by Fund A; payment safeguarding treated as own funds; an acquisition raises goodwill but intangible deduction not refreshed; a regulator waiver inferred from precedent rather than an operative direction. Presentation/filing and public disclosures follow the actual licence/return, while any accounting impairment, debt classification or note change is handled under its financial standard. This is a reusable conditional method, not a certification of an unknown regulated entity [16-006-REG-01; 16-006-REG-04].
