# Entity-level regulatory reporting and capital interface — substantive factory

Companion to original README. Capabilities CAO-16-012/013. Source checked 2026-09-27. Proposed PARTIAL: no universal capital rule applies across banking, insurance, payments and other licences; the factory method is developed but exact regime requires a licensed entity.

## Regulatory versus financial capital

Begin by identifying regulator, licence, solo or consolidated perimeter, prescribed return and date, accounting basis and any transitional arrangements. Accounting equity is an input, not a synonym for eligible own funds/regulatory capital. A bank may have prudential filters, capital tiers, risk-weighted assets and buffers; an insurer may use solvency capital, technical provisions and admissibility rules; a payments institution may use safeguarded assets or its own-funds formula. Do not mix return instructions across sectors or countries. Illustrative primary routing: [APRA prudential and reporting standards](https://www.apra.gov.au/cross-industry/prudential-and-reporting-standards), [EBA single rulebook](https://www.eba.europa.eu/regulation-and-policy/single-rulebook), [US Federal Reserve regulations](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg.htm), [UK PRA rulebook](https://www.prarulebook.co.uk/). An actual entity's rule, regulator guidance and effective-period transitional provisions must be retrieved before any threshold or capital conclusion.

## Bridge method

The EBA interactive rulebook is a navigation aid rather than the authentic legal instrument; obtain the operative EUR-Lex regulation and national implementing law before a European legal conclusion. Likewise, distinguish APRA website guidance from the authorized Federal Register instrument and proposals from current rules.

Create a signed balance-sheet-to-return bridge with legal entity and currency, opening/closing period, source account and report IDs, regulatory adjustment line, authoritative rule citation, reason, sign, reviewer and evidence. Reconcile accounting equity to eligible capital through separately supported deductions/additions. Reconcile the capital denominator (for example risk-weighted assets or solvency requirement) independently. Verify intra-group assets and liabilities, intangible/deferred tax treatment, subordinated instruments, distribution restrictions and transitional filters only under the actual regime. Compare reported capital with minimum, buffers and management triggers; escalation of potential breach belongs immediately with regulatory compliance and the CFO, not only in the month-end accounting queue.

Illustrative arithmetic, **not** a prudential determination: statutory equity 20.0m, identified intangible deduction 3.0m and another independently authorized adjustment of 1.0m → illustrative eligible capital 16.0m. If the actual prescribed requirement were 14.0m, arithmetic headroom would be 2.0m. Neither 16.0m nor 2.0m is a compliance conclusion without defining eligible instruments, deductions, denominator, scope and regulator form. A material acquisition may increase statutory equity while increasing deductions and risk exposures; a one-direction assumption is unsafe.

## Controls and test cases

Control over entity/perimeter mapping; signed GL extract completeness; dual review of adjustment citations and signs; independent numerator/denominator tie-out; regulatory team approval; submission receipt; post-filing variance analysis. Inputs: signed statements/TB, entity ownership, instrument terms, prudential classification memo, regulator form and model, approvals. S1: parent consolidated equity used for a solo licence → fail. S2: acquired software intangible changes and capital bridge is unchanged → investigate; do not assume deduction if sector rule differs. S3: negative headroom with no escalation → fail immediately. S4: valid bridge but return denominator from prior quarter → fail. Dependencies 07-001 scope, 04-005 intangibles, 06-005 debt and 16-007 sector change. Residual blocker precisely the unidentified jurisdiction/licence/rule: retain PARTIAL for standards treatment until a sector-specific pack is approved.
