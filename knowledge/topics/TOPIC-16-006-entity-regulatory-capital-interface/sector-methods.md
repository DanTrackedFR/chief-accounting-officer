# TOPIC-16-006 — sector-specific capital routes and challenge cases

This complements the retained ADI example in `factory.md`. Capabilities CAO-16-012 entity reporting perimeter and CAO-16-013 financial-to-regulatory capital bridge. Source checked 2026-09-28. The reusable output is a rule-to-return mapping, not an invented universal regulatory ratio. Each route begins with licence, legal entity, solo/group perimeter, period, operative rule, supervisor adjustment, prescribed form, return deadline and management escalation. Obtain current legal instrument and local permission/waiver before a filing.

## Australian ADI

[APS 110](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/aps-110) defines the capital adequacy framework and [APS 111](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/aps-111) the composition/adjustments of capital. Distinguish Level 1 ADI and Level 2 group, CET1, Tier 1 and total capital, risk-weighted assets, leverage, applicable buffers and institution-specific PCR. Accounting equity 20m less an illustrative 3m intangible deduction and a separate evidenced 1m adjustment gives 16m only if each item qualifies under the operative APS 111 version; the input bridge alone does not determine RWA. At 160m independently calculated RWA, 16m/160m = 10%. A hypothetical 11% combined requirement requires 17.6m, leaving 1.6m short; obtain the actual PCR and buffers before calling this a breach. Challenge a purported 10% ratio that uses consolidated capital with solo RWA: fail.

## Australian general insurer

[GPS 110](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/gps-110) applies to general insurers and Level 2 insurance groups; [GPS 112](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/gps-112) governs the capital-base size and composition. Construct prescribed capital amount from operative standard-method risk charges, any supervisory adjustment and relevant floor; reconcile asset, insurance, concentration and operational risk inputs to the return. Capital base is independently bridged from accounting equity with instrument eligibility and deductions, not inferred from net assets. Illustrative base 30m and **assumed, independently evidenced** PCR 24m give 6m arithmetic headroom and 125% coverage. If an omitted catastrophe concentration charge would increase the PCR by 8m, the corrected 32m PCR gives 2m shortfall and 93.75% coverage; fail the earlier green sign-off and escalate. The assumed amounts are not APRA-prescribed percentages. Test policy liability valuation, recoverable reinsurance and tax adjustments separately; accounting profit does not substitute for eligible capital. [GPS 310](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/gps-310) identifies assurance requirements for specified regulatory returns, so retain auditor-ready source lineage and assessment of the applicable version.

## Australian life insurer

[LPS 110](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/lps-110) determines the prescribed capital amount by fund and at life-company level; [LPS 112](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/lps-112) addresses measurement of capital. The LPS 112 version effective 1 July 2026 requires an explicit reporting-period gate. Build a statutory-fund-by-fund capital-base/PCR bridge and test restrictions on transferring surplus between funds before aggregating. For illustration, Fund A base 12m versus PCR 10m and Fund B base 7m versus PCR 9m yield company totals 19m/19m but Fund B has a 2m shortfall; a zero aggregate headroom does not prove each fund meets the relevant test. Check an actual company's floor, group position and APRA directions before reporting. Countertest: a life book mapped to the general-insurer GPS 110 charge schedule fails the licence gate.

## UK payment institution and EU route

For a UK authorised payment institution, [Payment Services Regulations 2017 regulation 22](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/752/regulation/22) compares ongoing own funds with the applicable initial-capital and calculated amount; the operative Schedule 3 method and FCA directions/waivers govern the actual case. Illustrative initial-capital floor 125k and calculated own-funds requirement 180k produce a higher requirement of 180k. If eligible own funds are 210k, arithmetic headroom is 30k; a 40k ineligible intangible deduction would reduce eligible funds to 170k and turn it into a 10k shortfall. All four figures are assumptions, not statutory thresholds. Payment-service category, authorized method, safeguarding obligation and issuer permissions must be checked independently; customer money is not free regulatory capital. For an EU payment institution use [Directive (EU) 2015/2366 Articles 7–9](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/2366/oj/eng), current consolidated amendments and the relevant Member State implementation/supervisor method. [EBA's Method B clarification](https://www.eba.europa.eu/single-rule-book-qa/qna/view/publicId/2021_6241) concerns transaction payment volume, not safeguarded balances. A UK regulatory calculation is not transplanted into an EU subsidiary.

## Workpaper and controls common to all routes

Required columns: entity ID, licence and supervisor, legal/reporting perimeter, period and effective instrument, accounting source account, prudential adjustment and paragraph, eligible capital tier, risk input/denominator, return cell, currency/FX date, source document, model version, preparer, challenge, approver and filing receipt. Reconcile each input to the signed TB, instrument register, actuarial/risk model or payment transaction population, then reconcile cells to submitted return. Run prior-period movement and stress challenge; retain supervisory correspondence and waived rules. A second reviewer checks sign/eligibility, period alignment, floor/adjustment, numerator/denominator and headroom, with immediate escalation when negative. No real-entity regulatory compliance conclusion follows solely from the illustrative cases.
