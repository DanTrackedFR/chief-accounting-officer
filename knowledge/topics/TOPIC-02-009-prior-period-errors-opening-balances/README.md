# TOPIC-02-009 — Prior-Period Errors & Opening-Balance Integrity

Status: REVIEWED
Built: 2026-09-22
Capabilities: CAO-02-019, CAO-02-020
Knowledge types: PRINCIPLES, STANDARDS, PRACTICE
Framework sensitivity: MEDIUM

## Objective
Distinguish errors from current-period estimates/policy changes, correct them using the applicable framework and prove opening balances/rollforwards remain continuous.

## IFRS/AASB core
IAS 8 / AASB 108 govern errors, accounting policies and estimates. Material prior-period errors are generally corrected retrospectively by restating comparatives and, where relevant, opening balances of the earliest period presented, subject to impracticability provisions. Changes in accounting estimates are prospective; policy changes generally follow specific transition or retrospective treatment. Materiality applies individually and collectively.

IFRS 18 becomes effective from 1 January 2027 and moves some basis-of-preparation requirements into IAS 8, so source routing must be period-aware.

## US GAAP / UK GAAP
Route US conclusions to the applicable Codification error/accounting-change guidance and UK conclusions to current FRS 102 requirements. Do not assume IFRS mechanics solely because the economic issue is similar. Jurisdictional filing/reissuance requirements are separate overlays.

## CAO workflow
1. Establish what was known/available when prior statements were prepared.
2. Classify issue: error, estimate change, policy change, new transaction/fact, or presentation/reclassification.
3. Quantify by period and statement/disclosure, including tax only through tax-accounting interface.
4. Assess materiality individually/cumulatively and qualitative factors.
5. Determine correction method under framework and filing status.
6. Rebuild opening balance and affected rollforwards.
7. Prepare entries/restatement tables/disclosures and audit support.
8. Identify control/process root cause and remediation.

## Opening-balance proof
Prior closing balance + approved retrospective/restatement/migration adjustments = current opening balance. Differences require item-level provenance. Acquisition, FX, system migration and chart-of-accounts remapping must not become unexplained plugs.

## Controls
Prior-to-current TB bridge; opening-balance lock; approved adjustment register; comparative tie-out; error log; cumulative misstatement tracking; root-cause remediation; migration conversion reconciliation.

## Tests
New information about a condition arising this year is not automatically a prior-period error. Mathematical mistake using information available last year can be an error. A system migration opening difference with no provenance fails even if current TB balances. An estimate revised because new information emerged routes to estimate-change guidance, not error correction.

## Artifacts
Issue classification memo; materiality analysis; restatement bridge; opening-balance reconciliation; comparative tie-out; correction entries; disclosure support; remediation plan.
