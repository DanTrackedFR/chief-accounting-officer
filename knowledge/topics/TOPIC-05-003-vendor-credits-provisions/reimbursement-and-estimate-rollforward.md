# Provision versus recoverable supplier credit

**Two independent claims.** Vendor debit 30 from overpayment: test enforceable refund or credit right, recovery evidence, legal set-off and expected use; an asset may exist but cannot simply be netted with a separate warranty liability. Warranty provision 50 in retained case derives from a probability distribution for a sufficiently large population, assuming present obligation, probable outflow and reliable estimate under IAS 37. If estimated warranty payments in next year are 20 and new current sales add expected obligations 15, a simplified provision movement is opening 50−cash claims 20+new expense 15=45, before unwind, remeasurement or FX. A vendor indemnity to reimburse 10 of warranty claims requires its own recoverability threshold; do not reduce the gross provision automatically to 35 or net against AP. Document whether recovery is virtually certain under IAS 37 before recognizing separate reimbursement asset, with its cap and presentation/disclosure analysis.

**Counterfactual.** A single lawsuit with outcome 0 or 100 at 20% probability is not automatically recognized at 20 just because portfolio warranty expected value is 50; assess present obligation, probable outflow, measurement and contingent liability disclosure separately. Post-period settlement evidence may corroborate period-end condition but must be dated and evaluated under subsequent-event standard. Discount future payments if time value is material and avoid adding a risk premium twice. US ASC 450 probability/measurement and FRS 102 Section 21/AASB 137 should not inherit IAS 37 wording without verification.

**QA and audit.** Reperform 50−20+15=45; fail netting 30 vendor debit and 50 warranty to a single payable 20, or recognizing 10 recovery solely from an informal promise. Retain contracts, claim history/actuarial estimate, product cohort, payments, supplier confirmation, legal correspondence, separate rollforwards and reviewer memo. Official [IAS 37](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-37-provisions-contingent-liabilities-and-contingent-assets/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). UK 2026 edition and US paragraph bodies need operative review.
