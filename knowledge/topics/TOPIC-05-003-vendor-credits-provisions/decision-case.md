# Vendor debit balance versus provision: recognition and measurement case

Capabilities CAO-05-005/006. Existing factory retained. These two questions must not be collapsed into “clean up AP.”

Vendor Y's AP account has a 30 debit after overpayment. Obtain contractual refund right, credit memo, vendor confirmation and post-period settlement. If enforceable and recoverable, present an appropriate asset rather than offsetting against unrelated suppliers; if disputed, assess impairment/recovery. Separately, a product warranty portfolio has a present obligation from past sales and a supportable distribution of cash outflows: 70% probability of 0, 20% of 100 and 10% of 300. Illustrative expected value is 0+20+30=50 for a large population; evaluate discounting if time value material and obligation-specific risks without double counting. Do not treat a single claim's most likely outcome automatically as a portfolio expected value. Dr warranty expense 50 / Cr provision 50 if IAS 37/AASB 137 recognition tests and assumptions are met. A merely possible lawsuit may require disclosure, not a booked 50; a remote case follows its own disclosure rule.
