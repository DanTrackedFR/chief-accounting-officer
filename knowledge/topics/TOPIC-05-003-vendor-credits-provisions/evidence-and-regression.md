## Executed method and boundary case

Legal claim outcomes: 60% chance of 100 and 40% of nil. For a single obligation, the best estimate is not mechanically 60; weigh most likely result, risks and evidence. A virtually certain insurer recovery of 40 is a separate asset, subject to the cap and facts, never netted blindly against the provision. Vendor debit 25 from duplicate payment is investigated as a refund receivable or prepayment; it is not a negative provision. US ASC 450 probability and range convention can yield a different amount than IAS 37, so document both calculations and disclosure exposure.
