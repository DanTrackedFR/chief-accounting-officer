# TOPIC-03-002 — Variable Consideration / Transaction Price Allocation

Status: REVIEWED / US SOURCE-DEPTH PARTIAL
Capabilities: CAO-03-004, CAO-03-005
Sensitivity: M (standards-heavy in execution)
Source checked: 2026-09-22

## Objective
Determine variable consideration, constrain it appropriately, determine stand-alone selling prices (SSP), and allocate transaction price to performance obligations in a way consistent with the applicable revenue model.

## Authoritative source map
IFRS: IFRS 15, principally transaction-price requirements around paragraphs 47–72 and allocation around 73–90; IFRS Foundation official standard page and IFRIC agenda material.
US GAAP: ASC 606-10-32 architecture; official FASB Revenue Recognition Implementation Q&As/TRG material. Full current Codification paragraph-body verification remains a source-depth limitation.
UK GAAP: revised FRS 102 Section 23 for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2026, subject to transition/early adoption; use current FRC edition and Factsheet 10. Do not assume old Section 23 or full IFRS identity.
AASB: AASB 15 paragraphs 46–90, including Australian NFP paragraphs where applicable; current AASB portal version.

## PRINCIPLES / CAO logic
1. Resolve framework, reporting period, customer/contract and performance obligations.
2. Build consideration population: fixed; discounts; rebates; refunds; credits; concessions; incentives; bonuses; penalties; returns; usage/volume amounts; non-cash consideration; customer payments; financing effects.
3. Identify what is genuinely variable versus a contract modification, price concession, refund obligation or separate purchase.
4. Select the estimation method that best predicts entitlement: expected-value approach for ranges/populations where appropriate, or most-likely-amount approach for binary/narrow outcomes.
5. Apply the framework's variable-consideration constraint. Do not default variable consideration to zero merely because uncertainty exists.
6. Reassess estimates at each reporting date and route changes to the correct performance obligation(s).
7. Establish SSP at contract inception. Prefer observable separate-sale evidence; otherwise use a supportable estimation approach permitted by the framework.
8. Allocate on relative SSP unless the specific discount or variable-consideration allocation criteria support allocation to one or more specific obligations/distinct goods/services.
9. Preserve contract-inception allocation basis; do not reallocate simply because SSP changes later.
10. Record journal, contract-balance, disclosure and estimate-control consequences.

## IFRS / AASB core
IFRS 15 and AASB 15 use the converged five-step revenue model. Transaction price can include fixed and variable amounts. Variable consideration is estimated and then constrained. Transaction price is ordinarily allocated on relative SSP; specific rules can allocate a discount or variable amount to a narrower part of the contract when criteria are met. AASB 15 paragraph 126 requires disclosure of methods/inputs/assumptions used for transaction price, constraint and allocation judgments. Australian NFP-specific requirements must be checked when relevant.

## US GAAP
Topic 606 is substantially converged with IFRS 15 on this core architecture. Official FASB implementation Q&As confirm expected-value versus most-likely-amount analysis and that the constraint does not mechanically force the estimate to a single possible outcome or zero. Differences can arise from subsequent amendments, terminology, effective guidance and application details; therefore cross-framework answers must retrieve the current ASC record rather than assert perfect identity.

## UK GAAP
For 2026-onward reporting, revised FRS 102 Section 23 is based on the IFRS 15 revenue model but is proportionately adapted. The CAO must route by period and use the revised Section 23 transaction-price/allocation requirements rather than importing IFRS 15 paragraphs. Earlier periods use the applicable prior FRS 102 model.

## Significant judgments
Historical experience relevance; susceptibility to external factors; time until uncertainty resolves; range/number of possible outcomes; price concessions versus impairment/credit risk; SSP comparability; residual/adjusted-market/cost approaches where used; allocation of discounts/variable amounts to specific obligations; consideration payable to customer.

## Example
A service contract has fixed consideration of 1,000 plus a 200 performance bonus. If evidence supports 160 as the expected-value estimate before constraint, the CAO does not automatically book 160. It separately assesses reversal risk under the applicable framework. Only the amount surviving the constraint enters transaction price. If two performance obligations have supported SSPs of 600 and 400 and no specific-allocation exception applies, a 1,000 transaction price allocates 600/400. Production calculations use actual facts and framework rules, not these illustrative assumptions.

## Accounting / journals
Revenue recognized follows the amount allocated to satisfied performance obligations. Changes in constrained variable consideration can create catch-up revenue/reduction and contract asset/liability movements depending on satisfaction status and allocation. Refund/rebate liabilities and consideration payable to customers require separate balance-sheet mapping where applicable.

## Controls / audit evidence
Executed contract and amendments; pricing/discount schedules; historical outcome data; approved forecast inputs; constraint memo; SSP evidence; allocation workbook/model; review of concessions/rebates; quarterly estimate reassessment; contract-to-billing/revenue reconciliation; disclosure support; model/change controls.

## Systems/data
Contract ID; obligation ID; consideration component/type; estimation method; probability/outcome inputs; constrained/unconstrained amount; reason; SSP source/method/date; allocation percentage/amount; reassessment date; actual settlement; contract balance and GL mapping. Preserve lineage from CRM/contract/billing to revenue engine/ERP.

## Artifacts
Variable-consideration memo; SSP study; transaction-price model; allocation schedule; rebate/refund rollforward; JE pack; disclosure support; estimate-change log.

## Scenarios
1. SaaS uptime bonus with strong history: estimate + constraint; do not default nil.
2. New product rebate highly susceptible to market factors: challenge constraint and evidence.
3. Discount demonstrably relates to one bundle: test specific discount allocation rather than blanket relative SSP.
4. Variable usage fee relates specifically to a series: test specific variable allocation criteria.
5. Customer incentive payment: determine distinct good/service versus reduction of transaction price.
6. 2026 UK reporter: route revised Section 23; do not copy IFRS paragraph references.
7. Australian NFP contract: check NFP overlay before allocation conclusion.
8. US contract: use ASC 606 record; flag source-depth limitation if paragraph-level conclusion exceeds verified public FASB material.
