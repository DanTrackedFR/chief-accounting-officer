## Objective
Determine variable consideration, constrain it appropriately, determine stand-alone selling prices (SSP), and allocate transaction price to performance obligations in a way consistent with the applicable revenue model. ## IFRS / AASB core
IFRS 15 and AASB 15 use the converged five-step revenue model. Transaction price can include fixed and variable amounts. [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R001] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R001] Variable consideration is estimated and then constrained. [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R003] [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R001] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R001] Transaction price is ordinarily allocated on relative SSP; specific rules can allocate a discount or variable amount to a narrower part of the contract when criteria are met. [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R009] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R009] [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R006] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R006] [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R001] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R001] AASB 15 paragraph 126 requires disclosure of methods/inputs/assumptions used for transaction price, constraint and allocation judgments. [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R003] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R015] Australian NFP-specific requirements must be checked when relevant. [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R009] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R009]

## US GAAP
Topic 606 is substantially converged with IFRS 15 on this core architecture. Official FASB implementation Q&As confirm expected-value versus most-likely-amount analysis and that the constraint does not mechanically force the estimate to a single possible outcome or zero. [TOPIC-03-002-US-R002] [TOPIC-03-002-US-R003] Differences can arise from subsequent amendments, terminology, effective guidance and application details; therefore cross-framework answers must retrieve the current ASC record rather than assert perfect identity. ## UK GAAP
For 2026-onward reporting, revised FRS 102 Section 23 is based on the IFRS 15 revenue model but is proportionately adapted. [TOPIC-03-002-UK-R013] The CAO must route by period and use the revised Section 23 transaction-price/allocation requirements rather than importing IFRS 15 paragraphs. [TOPIC-03-002-UK-R013] Earlier periods use the applicable prior FRS 102 model. [TOPIC-03-002-UK-R012] [TOPIC-03-002-UK-R013]

## Example
A service contract has fixed consideration of 1,000 plus a 200 performance bonus. If evidence supports 160 as the expected-value estimate before constraint, the CAO does not automatically book 160. [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-002-US-R003] [TOPIC-03-002-UK-R004] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R003] Only the amount surviving the constraint enters transaction price. [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-002-US-R003] [TOPIC-03-002-UK-R004] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R003] If two performance obligations have supported SSPs of 600 and 400 and no specific-allocation exception applies, a 1,000 transaction price allocates 600/400. ## Accounting / journals
Revenue recognized follows the amount allocated to satisfied performance obligations. Changes in constrained variable consideration can create catch-up revenue/reduction and contract asset/liability movements depending on satisfaction status and allocation.
