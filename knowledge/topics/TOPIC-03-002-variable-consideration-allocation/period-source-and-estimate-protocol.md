This is additive to the retained `factory.md`, `reassessment-and-specific-allocation.md` and example. For IFRS, the [2025 issued IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2025/issued/ifrs15.html) routes estimates through paragraphs 50–59, discount and variable-allocation exceptions through 81–86, and changes in transaction price through 87–90. For Australia, [AASB 15](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022) is the corresponding 2023-to-pre-July-2026 compilation; select the later operative version for periods starting 1 July 2026 onward. For UK FRS 102, document old versus Periodic Review 2024 Section 23 (principal commencement 1 January 2026) and reperform the decision under the actual UK edition. For US ASC 606, verify exact current Basic View paragraphs and amendment state before publishing paragraph citations; topic-level mapping alone is not such evidence. Do not treat inability to reproduce ASC wording as a blocker.

The preparer must retain (a) the contractual upside/downside range, (b) probability-weighted or most-likely estimate with rationale, (c) reversal constraint evidence with sensitivity to customer approval and external factors, (d) SSP observations, and (e) why the bonus is specific to one obligation *and* why specific allocation depicts the consideration expected for that obligation. In the retained example, 100 constrained bonus produces 540/460 (A/B) under the qualifying B-specific case. If the 60 change occurs with B 50% complete, record the 30 cumulative catch-up and 30 remaining allocation only after verifying a faithful progress measure. If the target fails, reverse the estimate and update the constraint; a 160 estimate must not survive contrary final settlement. A payment after the reporting date may provide evidence about the reporting-date estimate, but new post-period performance is not automatically an adjusting event.

At close, compare estimate snapshots to contract modifications and settlement population; backtest actual bonus by customer cohort; investigate bias and post unsupported manual overrides to the reviewer queue.
