## Framework and period decisions

Pre-2026 UK Section 23 measures consideration at fair value, considers discounts/rebates and identifiable components, and recognizes service revenue by reliable completion rather than silently applying the revised allocation model. [TOPIC-03-002-UK-R012]
Revised UK adoption uses full retrospective or cumulative opening equity for uncompleted contracts without comparator restatement; record variable-consideration and modification expedients and transition-impact disclosure. [TOPIC-03-002-UK-R013]
IFRS and AASB retain their adoption route, and US retains public/private/full/modified adoption and subsequent amendment elections; a later effective date is not a reason to change historical estimates without transition authority. [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R014] [TOPIC-03-002-US-R014] [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R014]
For AASB post-June 2026 periods, apply the base model with effective amendments and NFP enforceable/sufficiently-specific scope, and use AASB 1060 disclosures for eligible Tier 2. [TOPIC-03-002-AASB-R015]
IFRS notes explain price, constraint and SSP judgments and remaining obligations; US nonpublic reliefs are evaluated separately, and UK Section 1A relief is not applied to an ineligible entity. [TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R016] [TOPIC-03-002-US-R016] [TOPIC-03-002-UK-R016]

## Calculations and executable outputs

Retained general allocation: fixed 1,000, SSP 600/400, initially constrained bonus zero → 600/400. Later unconditional bonus 200 without exception → 120 A catch-up, 80 B deferred. Specific-allocation adverse branch: contract awards 200 only for B's measurable uptime, B's normal price is 400 plus that bonus, and independent price evidence supports the objective. A remains 600; B becomes 600. If B is unperformed, Dr AR 200 / Cr contract liability B 200, with no A catch-up. When B transfers, Dr liability B 600 / Cr revenue B 600. The alternative 120 revenue catch-up would misstate this branch. Failure of the evidence reverts to relative allocation. The four frameworks are separately selected in the scenario file, including the UK alternative-allocation objective.

Workpaper VC-002 contains all outcomes/probabilities, external dependency and resolution horizon, method/constraint conclusion, price-change source, customer payment/noncash/financing schedule, inception SSP sample, discount/bonus exception memo and revenue/balance-note bridge. Reviewer rechecks the downside outcome and changes the route when the specific-allocation support fails.

Control: reconcile approved bonus/SLA settlements and credit notes to the estimate population each close; independent reviewer challenges optimism and stale SSP samples. Data: price-component ID, probability/constraint snapshot, entitlement date, exception flag and basis, immutable inception SSP, obligation IDs, satisfied percentage, catch-up/deferral event and disclosure tag. The executed general versus specific branches reconcile to 1,200, but produce different A revenue and B liabilities; that accounting difference is the regression target.
