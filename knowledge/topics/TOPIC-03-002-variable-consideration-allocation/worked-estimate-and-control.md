# Variable consideration and allocation — estimate challenge

Capabilities CAO-03-004/005. Existing factory and README retained. This record separates estimation, constraint and allocation in one reproducible case.

Two services A/B have observed standalone selling prices 600 and 400. The fixed price is 900 plus a binary 200 performance bonus. At inception, documented probability of meeting target is 70%. With one binary outcome, the most-likely estimate could be 200; a probability-weighted expected value would be 140. Select the method that best predicts entitlement in the circumstances, not the one producing preferred revenue. The estimate must then pass the applicable constraint: suppose persuasive past evidence supports inclusion of only 100 of the 200 pending resolution. This is an *assumed supported conclusion* for arithmetic, not a universal 50% haircut. Transaction price is 1,000. Absent a valid specific-allocation exception, allocate A 600 and B 400 on 60/40 SSP weights. If the bonus specifically relates to B and narrowly allocates under the applicable criteria, rerun the allocation rather than automatically applying 60/40 to all consideration.

**Executed:** fixed 900 + constrained 100 = 1,000; 1,000×0.6=600; 1,000×0.4=400. If the target fails after A is delivered, determine the amount of price change allocable to satisfied versus unsatisfied obligations and any catch-up, rather than reversing all revenue mechanically. PASS arithmetic; constraint and exception tests require contract and historical outcome evidence.

Control: versioned probability dataset, exclusion of canceled/unrepresentative contracts, contractual bonus metric, external uncertainty, reversal-risk memo, independent SSP evidence, formula check, monthly reassessment, actual-to-estimate backtest and revenue-engine version linkage.
