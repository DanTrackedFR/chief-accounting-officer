# Variable consideration: reassessment and specific-allocation gate

**Two promises.** Standalone selling prices A 600 and B 400; fixed total 900, bonus up to 200 only for achieving B's service-level target. Prior retained case assumes supported constrained inclusion of 100 and otherwise 60/40 allocation, producing A 600/B 400 from transaction price 1,000. That is a *default* illustration. If the variable amount relates specifically to B and allocating it entirely to B is consistent with the allocation objective under IFRS 15, evaluate the specific-allocation exception: allocate fixed 900 on SSP ratio (A 540, B 360) and bonus 100 to B, yielding A 540/B 460. The alternative sums to 1,000 but is not automatically permitted merely because the metric mentions B; inspect service obligations, pricing, contract negotiation and expected consideration. UK revised FRS 102 Section 23 and US ASC 606/AASB 15 require their own exact rules/period.

**Subsequent change.** At next reporting date, new evidence supports constrained bonus 160 rather than 100. Price increases 60. Assess which obligation(s) receive the change under the original allocation basis and whether any related obligation is already satisfied. Under the illustrative permitted B-specific allocation, A remains 540 and B increases from 460 to 520. If B is 50% satisfied, the appropriate cumulative revenue effect for B may be 30, with 30 allocated to future service, assuming a valid over-time progress measure and no other complication. If the constraint instead resolves after B is fully satisfied, a catch-up may arise; do not automatically defer all 60. Review whether original estimate was reasonable or a prior error before treating change prospectively.

**Control.** Retain probability and reversal evidence, SSP observations, variable metric and allocation rationale, performance/progress, prior revenue by obligation, journal and disclosure. Test 540+460=1,000 and 540+520=1,060; fail wholesale assignment of 200 bonus without constraint, allocation to B from its label alone, or 60 posted entirely to cash. Official [IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). US current paragraphs and exact UK simplifications still need authorized review.
