# Materiality assessment and auditor performance-materiality support

Companion to existing README. CAO-15-011/012. Source checked 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for management materiality method, not for setting an auditor's performance materiality.

## Framework, scope and boundary

Financial-reporting materiality is entity/user/context specific, not a universal percentage. IFRS [Practice Statement 2](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/materiality-practice-statement/) supplies nonmandatory guidance on making materiality judgments; IAS 1/IFRS 18 and each transaction's Standard provide binding presentation/disclosure requirements. UK [FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) uses its own materiality architecture, [AASB 101/108](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-108-mar-2021) and disclosure tiers require Australian evaluation, and US [FASB Codification](https://asc.fasb.org/) plus [SEC Staff Accounting Bulletin 99/108](https://www.sec.gov/interps/account/sab99.htm) inform registrant analysis subject to current status. Auditor performance materiality is part of audit planning under [IAASB ISA 320](https://www.iaasb.org/consultations-projects/materiality-planning-and-performing-audit-and-evaluation-misstatements-identified-during-audit-isa); management supplies complete data and its own assessment, not a purported audit threshold.

## Decision and aggregation model

Build a misstatement register with origin, entity, statement/note, amount/sign, affected period, corrected/uncorrected, factual/judgmental/projected classification where relevant, reversal, tax, EPS, covenant and qualitative attributes. Evaluate individually and in aggregate across same assertion, financial statements and group; consider gross and net as appropriate, with risk of offset masking different lines. Relevant qualitative factors include movement across profit/loss, covenant or regulatory capital threshold, compensation metrics, trends, related parties, intentional misstatement and control environment. A fixed “5% of profit” screen may identify work for analysis but cannot conclude for all users. For disclosure, omission or obscuring information can be material even when no journal exists.

## Worked cases

Company reports 20m pretax profit. An uncorrected 100k liability understatement is 0.5% of profit, but moves a measured covenant ratio from 1.99 to 2.01 against a contractual minimum 2.00. The qualitative covenant consequence is potentially decisive; confirm definition, bank waiver and actual ratio, then recommend correction/disclosure as applicable. Separately eight errors of 20k each create 160k gross exposure if same direction; reviewing each as immaterial individually is invalid. A 50k deliberate related-party misclassification may be significant by nature despite smaller magnitude. Do not assume all errors can be netted or that performance materiality equals the company's booking threshold.

## Controls and tests

Maintain complete audit-difference and management-detected populations, quarterly aggregation across entities, independent qualitative factor check, approved correction/reopening governance, and disclosure tie-out. Provide auditors granular ledgers/transaction populations and calculation support; ask their intended scope where appropriate but do not record their confidential planning threshold as management's conclusion. S1: eight 20k same-direction errors cleared individually → FAIL; aggregate 160k. S2: 100k covenant crossing cleared solely by 0.5% profit → FAIL; qualitative analysis required. S3: misstatement reverses next period → still evaluate original and current periods. S4: no accounting amount, but related-party disclosure omitted → assess qualitative materiality. Source-depth limitations: current US ASC paragraph text and exact auditor rules require authorized access/context; no blanket numeric percentage. Dependencies 08-004 comparative, 09-007 deficiency, 10-004 audit differences, 15-006 error correction.
