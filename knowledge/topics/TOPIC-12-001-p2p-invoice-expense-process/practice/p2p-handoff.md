# P2P handoff

## Operating decision and reconciled data
PO/receipt/invoice triple match is an operational authorization; service-period accrual and capitalization remain accounting decisions. Register unmatched receipts, unbilled services, duplicate invoice IDs, credit memos and post-close invoices by entity/period. Reconcile AP subledger, GRNI and expense extracts to GL with source IDs and batch totals.

## Adverse case and workpaper
A January invoice for December services passes three-way match. Do not book solely in January; route cutoff/accrual determination to Domain 01 close and relevant expense/asset technical topic. Preserve PO, receipt, service evidence, calculation, reversing entry and January clearing.

## Ownership and technical handoff
AP owner resolves vendor status; accounting owner approves recognition and journal; procurement confirms receipt. ERP feed design/control routes to 11-002/003 and failed reconciliation to 09-005.

## Acceptance and authority
Retain a period/entity scoped population, source and destination totals, immutable transaction IDs, exception disposition, approved entries and reviewer sign-off tied to the final GL version. Test both an ordinary transaction and the adverse case above; an SOP or performance metric without a reconciled accounting output does not pass. The parent README and method provide the broader process design. Technical accounting treatment must be decided under the applicable framework and period in the named technical topic; these operational runbooks do not replace it. Applicable US issuer ICFR concepts: [SEC Release 33-8810](https://www.sec.gov/rule-release/33-8810); otherwise route legal/control duties locally.
