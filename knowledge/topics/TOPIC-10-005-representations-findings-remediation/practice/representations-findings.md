# Representation and finding trace

## Engagement route and decision
Management representation support is a substantiation process: inventory every representation, accountable fact owner, underlying evidence, period, known exceptions and approval. Auditor drafts/obtains representations under PCAOB AS 2805 or relevant ISA 580/local regime. A letter cannot replace contradictory evidence or cure a failed control.

## Control, evidence and data
Findings register links request/observation to affected assertion/control, root cause, period, potential misstatement, auditor communication, management response, corrective action, interim mitigation, retest and governance status. Restrict privileged drafts; preserve final signed versions and timestamps. Route deficiencies to 09-007 and corrections to Domain 02.

## Adverse case and escalation
A representation says all related parties were disclosed, yet the legal register includes a new affiliate. Stop signature, reconcile registers and transactions, update disclosure and communication, investigate control failure; do not use a broad qualifier to hide the unresolved discrepancy.

## Handoff and authority
Link the evidence packet to the request register and final statement version; route accounting errors to Domain 02, control failures to Domain 09 and systems/data gaps to Domain 11. Reassess scope and effective dates at each new engagement. References: [PCAOB auditing standards](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards), [IAASB standards and pronouncements](https://www.iaasb.org/standards-pronouncements), [IAASB 2025 Handbook](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/2025-handbook-international-quality-management-auditing-review-other-assurance-and-related-services). These references describe auditor responsibilities; management owns books, support and correction decisions.
