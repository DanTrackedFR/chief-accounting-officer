# ERP, chart of accounts and dimensions — acceptance case

Extends substantive README. Capabilities CAO-11-001–003. PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE; 2026-09-27.

Design grain first: legal entity/book, natural account, counterparty, product/project, cost center and transaction date/period. An account should encode accounting nature; dimensions should distinguish management analysis without erasing statutory/statement requirements. Maintain allowed combinations, ownership and effective dates, with historical mapping and documented crosswalk to each reporting basis. Test source transaction → subledger → GL → consolidated line/disclosure and reverse trace, including null/invalid dimensions. Change approval must compare prior and new mappings on a fixed transaction set.

Example: proposed COA combines 300,000 customer deposits with 500,000 deferred subscription revenue in one 800,000 liability account. If obligations and disclosure/reporting treatment differ, separate them by account or governed dimension with complete and reversible mapping; the aggregated 800,000 is not sufficient proof. On a 1 July COA change, retain old account, mapped new account, entity and original transaction IDs; bridge 300,000+500,000=800,000 with no loss of meaning. **Negative test:** one deposit lacks dimension and falls into revenue bucket. Expected FAIL, quarantine/reclassify and assess prior outputs. Framework classification routes to the underlying accounting topic; system design does not decide revenue. Evidence: COA policy, mapping versions, sample tests, migration bridge and approval.
