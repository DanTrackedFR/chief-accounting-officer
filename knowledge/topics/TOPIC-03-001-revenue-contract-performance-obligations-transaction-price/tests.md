# TOPIC-03-001 — Scenario Tests & QA

## Scenarios
1. SaaS MSA + order form + implementation: inventory all promises; do not assume implementation is separate; evaluate integration/customization and customer benefit.
2. Reseller arrangement: establish whether counterparty is customer for scoped activity before five-step analysis; principal-agent routes later.
3. Contract signed but either party can terminate without compensation before performance: test enforceable rights before treating as accounting contract.
4. Customer has weak credit: apply framework-specific collectibility criterion and price-concession facts; do not mechanically book billed revenue.
5. Hardware + support + material customization: determine distinctness in contract context rather than using SKU/billing lines.
6. Bonus/penalty pricing: include in transaction-price inventory and route to variable-consideration constraint analysis.
7. Customer receives vendor shares as purchase incentive under US GAAP: flag 2025 Topic 606 amendment/effective-date analysis.
8. UK entity with period beginning 1 January 2026: route to revised FRS 102 Section 23, not legacy Section 23.
9. Australian entity with period beginning August 2026: inspect AASB 2022-9 uncompiled amendment and entity type; do not rely solely on December 2022 compilation.
10. Insurance company service arrangement: determine whether revenue is in AASB/IFRS 17 or revenue-standard scope before applying five-step model.

## Pass criteria
Framework and effective period resolved; contract scope established; complete promise inventory; distinctness judgment supported; transaction-price population reconciles contractual economics; no invented terms; downstream topics invoked; controls/evidence/artifacts produced.

## QA result
PASS for topic-level REVIEWED status. Deep issue records for variable consideration, allocation, timing, modifications, principal-agent, contract balances/costs and disclosures remain owned by TOPIC-03-002 onward and must not be inferred from this record.
