# TOPIC-08-006 — Related Party Disclosure / Segment Reporting Support

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate

Primary capabilities: CAO-08-011, CAO-08-012.

This topic routes related-party reporting to IAS 24, ASC 850, FRS 102 Section 33 and AASB 124 as applicable, and segment reporting to IFRS 8, ASC 280 and AASB 8 where applicable. Resolve framework, jurisdiction, entity type and reporting period before applying requirements.

CAO workflow: establish the complete related-party population from group structure, ownership, governance and management records; reconcile transactions, balances and commitments to accounting systems; establish CODM evidence from internal reporting; identify operating/reportable segments and aggregation judgments; reconcile segment measures; prepare disclosures; retain review evidence; promote durable facts and judgments to Company Accounting Memory.

Controls include annual and event-driven related-party declarations, master-data flags, governance-to-accounting change feeds, segment reassessment triggers and disclosure tie-outs. Recurring reconciliation across ERP, AP/AR, legal-entity or governance data and disclosure workbooks is an automation candidate; TrackedFR is relevant only when that recurring cross-system reconciliation/manipulation problem exists.
