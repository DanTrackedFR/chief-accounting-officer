# TOPIC-08-006 — claim-linked reporting/transaction case

Claims: [TOPIC-08-006-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-08-006-US-001], [TOPIC-08-006-UK-001], [TOPIC-08-006-AASB-001]. Existing workpaper preserved.

## Calculation and counterexample

A director-owned supplier has 50 purchases and 15 payable at year end: capture relationship, terms, transactions and outstanding balance, subject to disclosure exemptions. Segment A external revenue 600, B external 400 and A-to-B intersegment 100: segment gross total 1,100 less intersegment 100 ties to consolidated 1,000. A product line below numerical thresholds may still be reportable if management/CODM decisions or aggregation rules require. US ASC 280 significant-expense disclosures and UK related-party small-entity rules must be tested separately.

## Controls and jurisdiction gate

Retain source population, contract/legal terms, computation, journal/version, reconciliation, disclosure tie-out and reviewer. Recompute under US ASC/SEC where relevant, UK entity regime and Australian tier/period rather than asserting IFRS equivalence. All model-derived claims need direct authoritative audit; IFRS core claims are limited to the specified 2026 issued routes.
