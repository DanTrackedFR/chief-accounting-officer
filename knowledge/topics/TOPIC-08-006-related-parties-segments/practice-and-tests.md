# Related parties and segments — close execution and challenge cases

Capabilities: CAO-08-011/012. This practice record extends the existing overview. It separates the related-party population test from the segment identification test; neither can be inferred solely from the ERP chart of accounts.

## Related-party population and reconciliation

At planning, obtain current and comparative group ownership, director/key-management lists, controlled entities, governance declarations and changes during the period. Compare them with vendor/customer masters, intercompany counterparties, equity registers and legal agreements. Preserve the source and effective dates of each relationship. A declaration is not proof of completeness; investigate matching addresses, names and bank details, and ask legal/HR for ownership changes.

For each identified counterparty, reconcile transactions, outstanding balances, commitments and terms to the relevant ledger and supporting agreement. Reconcile entity and currency before eliminating group balances. Group eliminations do not automatically remove disclosures required in each separate entity's accounts. Challenge assertions that a transaction was at arm's length unless substantiated under the applicable framework.

**Worked exception:** the ledger reports 210 of management-entity consulting invoices, but the AP extract has 225 and 15 sits in an accrued-invoice clearing account. The draft disclosure at 210 is incomplete pending assessment of the accrual and period cut-off. 225 − 210 = 15; trace service period and agreement before finalizing. Control result: exception found, disposition pending evidence.

## Segment decision workflow

Obtain the actual periodic package used by the chief operating decision maker (CODM), identify components with discrete financial information, document how performance is reviewed and resources allocated, then test aggregation and quantitative reportability under the applicable framework. Tie reported segment measures to CODM packages and explain reconciliations to consolidated totals, including eliminations and unallocated items. Reassess when leadership, reporting packages or acquisitions change. A legal entity or product label alone is not a segment conclusion.

**Worked reconciliation:** segment measures A 480 and B 320 sum to 800. Unallocated central costs of 90 and elimination of intersegment profit of 20 produce consolidated measure 690, if these are the actual reconciling items: 800 − 90 − 20 = 690. Compare the definition of the measure in the CODM package with the disclosure and retain review of the 110 bridge. PASS arithmetic; reportable-segment and disclosure conclusions remain fact-dependent.

## Framework and effective-period checks

- IFRS: IAS 24 for related parties and IFRS 8 for operating segments; confirm IFRS 8 applies to the reporting entity before building a segment note.
- US GAAP: ASC 850 and ASC 280; assess applicable public-entity scope and current segment disclosure requirements for the particular annual/interim period using authoritative current Codification and amendments. Paragraph-body verification remains PARTIAL where access is unavailable.
- UK GAAP: FRS 102 Section 33 for related parties; assess any applicable UK segment-reporting obligation and entity scope separately. Do not transplant IFRS 8 wholesale into FRS 102.
- AASB: AASB 124 and AASB 8 where in scope; confirm entity type and Australian disclosure overlays.

These are source-family pointers, not a completed paragraph map. Exact effective-date and transition checks for any amended segment disclosure requirements remain an independent QA gate. No restricted standards body text is reproduced.

## Scenario tests and evidence

1. Director-linked supplier omitted from the ERP related-party flag: join governance declaration to vendor master, investigate ownership and period, then recompute note population. Expected: disclosure review even if invoices were correctly posted. PASS routing.
2. Group receivable eliminated in consolidation but present in subsidiary statutory accounts: evaluate separate-entity disclosure. PASS routing.
3. Monthly product dashboard exists but CODM uses regional profitability for resource allocation: inspect actual CODM evidence before naming segments. PASS routing.
4. Acquisition changes internal reporting midyear: document change date, comparative implications and required explanation under the applicable framework. PASS trigger; framework-specific conclusion pending facts.
