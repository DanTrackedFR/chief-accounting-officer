# Segment and relationship disclosure: joint completeness review

**Two independent populations.** Related parties start from legal ownership, KMP, governance declarations and counterparty/control relationships; operating segments start from the components and discrete financial information regularly reviewed by the chief operating decision maker (CODM). A legal-entity list cannot substitute for either. Obtain board packs and recurring CODM dashboards, not a management slide made solely for the annual report. Resolve public-market scope before applying IFRS 8/AASB 8; private entities under FRS 102 do not acquire a universal IFRS 8 obligation merely by internal segment reporting. US ASC 280 has its own scope and disclosure amendments; validate public-entity applicability and effective period. Related-party disclosure separately routes IAS 24/AASB 124, ASC 850 and FRS 102 Section 33.

**Segment worked bridge.** CODM pack revenue for three components is A 600, B 250 and C 200, including A-to-B sales 40. Consolidated external revenue is 1,010: 600+250+200−40. Management proposes A and B aggregation. Require evidence of similar long-term economic characteristics and the relevant aggregation criteria, not merely same executive owner. If C is omitted because management calls it “non-core,” assess quantitative thresholds, combined external-revenue coverage and material information; a management label alone cannot remove a reportable component. Reconcile CODM measure (e.g. segment profit 180+40+20=240) to consolidated profit before tax after specified corporate costs and eliminations, retaining definitions, segment asset/liability measure if regularly provided, and comparative change log. The 1,010 arithmetic check proves reconciliation, not segment identification.

**Relationship worked bridge.** Related-party ledger shows sales to Parent 70, purchases from KMP-controlled entity 15, ending receivable 12, payable 4 and guarantee commitment 50. Tie each amount and terms to ERP and contract; compare related-party master against declarations, share register and legal events. A new KMP appointment on 1 July changes the relationship timeline, not necessarily all January–June transactions. Determine in-scope transactions and balances for each reporting entity, eliminate intragroup transactions at consolidated level, and test disclosure of commitments and ultimate parent. Under FRS 102 Section 1A, entity-size and 2026 amendment gates affect disclosure; AASB tier and NFP overlays require their own check. Do not assert arm's-length equivalence without support.

**QA.** Fail a 1,050 segment revenue total with no 40 elimination, a segment definition chosen from the legal chart rather than CODM evidence, a related-party note populated solely from AP flags, and a 2025 UK small-entity checklist copied into a 2026 period. Reviewer retains both populations, reconciliations, judgment memo and note tie-out.
