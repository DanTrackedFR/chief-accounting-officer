# TOPIC-16-001 — Regulatory Reporting Applicability, Filing Calendar & Public-Company Readiness
Capabilities: CAO-16-001, CAO-16-002, CAO-16-003
Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Knowledge type: JURISDICTION / PRINCIPLES / PRACTICE

## Objective
Determine which securities, corporate, prudential and other financial-reporting regimes apply to each reporting entity; convert them into an evidence-backed filing calendar; and assess whether accounting can execute the obligations reliably.

## Required context
Legal-entity and security listing map; incorporation and tax residence; exchange/market; filer/issuer classification; public-interest status; debt/equity instruments; group structure; reporting framework; fiscal year; auditor; regulated licences; prior filings; acquisitions/disposals; registration statements; covenant and statutory deadlines; material subsidiaries; filing systems and credentials.

## Decision logic
1. Establish the legal entity and security population before selecting rules.
2. Identify regulator/exchange and authoritative rule set for each entity/security.
3. Determine filer/issuer classification and exemptions using current rules, not precedent.
4. Separate financial-statement framework from securities filing, corporate-law, prudential and exchange obligations.
5. Map annual, interim, current/event-driven and structured-data obligations with trigger, due-date rule, owner, reviewer, submission channel and evidence.
6. Calculate dates from the rule and preserve the calculation; do not copy last year's date.
7. Add dependencies: audit/review, board/audit committee, certifications, legal disclosure, XBRL/ESEF, translation, publication and submission credentials.
8. Assess readiness across people, policy, close, audit, controls, disclosure production, systems/data, governance and filing mechanics.
9. Reassess applicability after listing, financing, acquisition, restructuring, filer-status or regulatory changes.

## Jurisdiction routing examples
**US SEC:** Exchange Act reporting companies generally file annual Form 10-K and quarterly Form 10-Q; foreign private issuers use different forms/cadence. CEO/CFO certifications and event-driven reports can create additional dependencies. Confirm current filer status, form, due date and scaled disclosure eligibility directly with SEC rules/forms.

**UK listed/regulated-market issuers:** route through FCA DTR/UKLR and Companies Act obligations as applicable. DTR 4 includes annual-report content/timing and structured electronic format requirements for in-scope issuers.

**EU regulated markets:** assess Transparency Directive/local implementation and ESEF requirements through the relevant national competent authority and ESMA materials.

**Australia:** determine Corporations Act/ASIC, ASX Listing Rules and sector-regulator requirements as applicable; never infer an Australian deadline from US/UK practice.

## Readiness diagnostic
Score evidence, not confidence: reporting basis; historical audited periods; close predictability; technical-accounting capacity; disclosure ownership; audit readiness; ICFR/control maturity; consolidation; equity/cap table; tax; related parties; segment information; EPS; subsequent events; legal contingencies; systems/data; structured reporting; board calendar; certifications; filing credentials. Each red/amber item needs owner, dependency, target date and acceptance evidence.

## Controls and evidence
Maintain an applicability memo with source, rule/version, conclusion and reviewer. Calendar changes require rule-based rationale and review. Use dual control for submission. Archive accepted filing receipt, final filed document and approval package. Event-driven obligations need trigger monitoring.
