# TOPIC-02-007 — Accrual Accounting & Prepaid Expenses

Status: REVIEWED
Built: 2026-09-22
Capabilities: CAO-02-015, CAO-02-016
Knowledge types: PRINCIPLES, STANDARDS, PRACTICE
Framework sensitivity: MEDIUM

## Objective
Recognize expenses/assets in the periods in which economic resources or services are consumed or rights remain, rather than mechanically following invoice or cash timing.

## Accrual logic
1. Identify the underlying transaction and applicable recognition topic.
2. Establish whether goods/services have been received or an obligation otherwise exists by period end.
3. Determine amount from contract, receipt, usage, milestone or supportable estimate.
4. Separate incurred-but-unbilled items from future commitments.
5. Record expense/asset and liability as appropriate.
6. Reverse or true-up using a controlled method that avoids double recognition when invoice posts.
7. Back-test estimates and refine methodology where bias/error recurs.

## Prepayment logic
A payment creates a prepaid asset only to the extent a future economic resource/right remains at reporting date. Build a service/coverage schedule, expense as benefit is consumed, reassess recoverability/classification and reconcile schedule to GL. A cash payment alone does not prove an asset.

## Framework notes
Accrual basis is foundational across the active frameworks, but specific recognition is governed by the relevant standard/topic. IFRS basis-of-preparation architecture moves with IFRS 18/IAS 8 from 2027; the accounting consequence for a specific item still depends on its underlying Standard. US GAAP authority comes from the relevant Codification topic, not the non-authoritative FASB Concepts Statements. FRS 102 and AASB similarly require topic-specific recognition.

## Methodology hierarchy
Prefer direct transaction evidence; then systematic operational data; then statistically/supportably estimated accruals. Avoid unsupported percentage-of-budget plugs. Material recurring estimates require methodology, data lineage, assumptions, back-testing and review.

## Controls
Uninvoiced receipt/service population completeness; PO/receipt-to-AP matching; contract register; recurring accrual recertification; reversal/double-post prevention; prepayment schedule-to-GL reconciliation; expired-service review; estimate back-testing; aged debit/credit balance review.

## Artifacts
Accrual methodology; accrual rollforward; unbilled-service population; estimate back-test; prepaid schedule; amortization schedule; reconciliation; journal pack.

## Tests
Annual insurance paid upfront: asset only for unexpired coverage, then systematic expense. December consulting delivered but invoiced January: accrue when obligation/expense recognition criteria are met. Purchase order for undelivered future service: commitment is not automatically an accrual. Repeated exact reversal/rebook without invoice matching: investigate process failure.

## TrackedFR fit
Strong for recurring AP/PO/receipt/GL data triangulation, accrual completeness and prepaid schedule-to-GL reconciliation across systems and Excel.
