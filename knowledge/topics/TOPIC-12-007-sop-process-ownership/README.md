# TOPIC-12-007 — Accounting SOP Design / Accounting Process Ownership

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Primary capabilities: CAO-12-017, CAO-12-018

## Objective
Turn accounting policy and process design into maintainable procedures with explicit ownership, review and escalation.

## SOP design standard
Each SOP states purpose, scope, effective date/version, owner, backup, prerequisites/access, inputs, systems, step sequence, decision points, accounting dependencies, expected outputs, controls/evidence, exceptions/escalations, reconciliation, close deadline, retention location and change history. Use screenshots only as supporting evidence; text and field/rule descriptions must survive UI changes.

Instructions should identify what success looks like and what to do when expected conditions fail. Do not encode unsupported accounting conclusions into an SOP; link to the approved policy/technical record.

## Ownership model
One accountable process owner maintains design and performance. Step owners execute; control owners evidence controls; system owners maintain technical configuration; accounting policy owners approve accounting logic. Define backups and handoffs. Ownership follows the end-to-end accounting result, not organizational convenience.

## Lifecycle
Draft from observed process; walkthrough with operator; compare to policy/system reality; remediate gaps; approve; train; publish; monitor exceptions; review after material process/system/policy changes and periodically; supersede old versions without destroying history.

## Maturity
Reactive: tribal knowledge. Controlled: documented critical steps. Standardized: common templates/RACI/versioning. Scaled: process/control/system maps and metrics. World-class: event-driven updates, evidence lineage, exception-led operation and durable knowledge integrated with CAO memory.

## Controls and metrics
Owner certification; overdue SOP reviews; process exceptions; key-person dependencies; unowned controls; steps performed outside approved systems; stale screenshots/field references; training completion where relevant.

## Artifacts
SOP template; process inventory; owner/RACI matrix; review calendar; change log; walkthrough record; exception/escalation matrix.

## Scenario tests
Operator uses undocumented workaround: capture, assess accounting/control impact, either approve into design or eliminate. Process owner leaves: backup and durable SOP prevent knowledge loss. ERP field changes: system change impact assessment triggers SOP/control update.

## Completion criteria
CAO can create an executable SOP, distinguish policy from procedure, establish accountable ownership and keep process knowledge current through change.
