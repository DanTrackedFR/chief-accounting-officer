# Payroll accrual and benefit classification case

Capabilities CAO-05-009/010. Existing factory retained. The payroll close must separate earned short-term pay, employer obligations, defined-contribution payable and defined-benefit actuarial accounting.

Ten employees earn 5 days of unpaid September salary after payroll cut-off at 200 per day each: 10×5×200=10,000 gross accrued salary. Employer contribution is 8% of that gross amount under assumed plan rules, or 800. If earned and payable, illustrative postings are Dr salary expense 10,000 / Cr accrued salary 10,000; Dr contribution expense 800 / Cr contribution payable 800. Employee withholding is not an additional employer expense; map it when the gross payroll is disbursed. True up to actual payroll, by person/pay-code/period, and avoid counting those five days again in the next payroll journal.

A defined-benefit plan instead requires plan-specific measurement of obligation and assets, actuarial assumptions and presentation; it cannot be accrued as a flat 8% contribution. Determine plan type, population, amendments/curtailments, discount rate, mortality and salary growth; obtain an actuary's report and reconcile census/payments to HR/payroll. IFRS IAS 19/AASB 119 and US ASC 715 differ in presentation of remeasurements and accounting mechanics; FRS 102 Section 28 must be assessed separately. Do not book actuarial gains to ordinary salary expense by default.

**Executed:** 10,000+800=10,800 employer-period expense under assumed DC arrangement; PASS arithmetic. Classification of plan and service period is evidence-dependent. Official [IAS 19 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-19-employee-benefits/) and [2013 revision history](https://www.ifrs.org/projects/completed-projects/2011/employee-benefits-2011-revision-of-ias-19/) checked 2026-09-27. Exact ASC 715, FRS 102 and AASB 119 current paragraph map, and scheme-specific disclosures, remain PARTIAL. Preserve census, payroll, scheme rules, actuarial valuation, contribution ledger, reconciliation and reviewer challenge.
