# TOPIC-05-005 — Payroll Accruals & Employee Benefits

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Built: 2026-09-23
Capabilities: CAO-05-009, CAO-05-010

## Primary source families
IAS 19 Employee Benefits; US GAAP compensation/pension Topics including 710/712/715 as applicable; FRS 102 Section 28; AASB 119. Share-based payment is excluded here and routes to TOPIC-05-007/008.

## IFRS — IAS 19
IAS 19 covers employee benefits other than share-based payment. Core principle: recognize liability when service has created future employee-benefit obligation and expense when the related service benefit is consumed unless another Standard requires/permitted capitalization. Short-term benefits expected to settle wholly within 12 months after period end are generally undiscounted. Post-employment plans are classified as defined contribution or defined benefit. Defined contribution expense/liability follows service/contribution obligation. Defined benefit accounting uses actuarial measurement, discounting, plan assets, net deficit/surplus and allocation between P&L/OCI under IAS 19. Other long-term benefits use a defined-benefit-like measurement model but different presentation. Termination benefits route to TOPIC-05-009.

## US GAAP
US GAAP is not one IAS 19-equivalent Topic. Compensation, deferred compensation, compensated absences, pensions and other postretirement benefits are distributed across Topics. The CAO first classifies benefit type, then retrieves applicable US literature. Never infer IAS 19 OCI/remeasurement mechanics for US plans. Public FASB material identifies Topic 710 Compensation—General and Topic 718 boundary issues; pension/postretirement accounting routes to Topic 715.

## UK GAAP
FRS 102 Section 28 covers short-term, post-employment, other long-term and termination benefits; share-based payment is Section 26. General recognition in 28.3 recognizes benefit cost as liability net of payments and expense unless another section capitalizes it. Periodic Review 2024 removes the undue-cost-or-effort simplified defined-benefit valuation exemption and includes multi-employer plan requirements; use reporting-period-appropriate text.

## AASB
AASB 119 follows IAS 19 core for for-profit Tier 1 entities, with Australian entity/tier overlays. AASB's standards portal lists AASB 119 operative for 2025/26 periods; version routing is mandatory around amendments effective from July 2026. Tier 2 disclosure conclusions require Australian simplified-disclosure context.

## Payroll accrual methodology
Accrue earned but unpaid salary/wages, employer taxes/contributions and other compensation based on service through reporting date. Use actual payroll calendar/day basis or another supportable method reflecting entitlement. Separate estimation uncertainty from payroll-processing timing. Reverse/true-up systematically and investigate recurring forecast error.

## Defined-benefit CAO workflow
Obtain plan terms, population, actuary report, asset data and funding arrangements; classify plan; validate measurement date and assumptions; reconcile opening-to-closing obligation/assets; assess curtailments/settlements/amendments; determine framework-specific P&L/OCI/equity effects; test asset ceiling/minimum funding where applicable; prepare disclosures and audit support.

## Controls / evidence
Approved benefit plan documents; payroll calendar; employee census reconciliation; actuarial specialist competence/objectivity; assumption governance; payroll accrual back-test; contribution/payable reconciliation; plan-asset statements; GL rollforward; disclosure tie-out. Specialist use does not outsource CAO responsibility for accounting conclusion.

## Scenarios
Three payroll days earned after last payroll: accrue service and employer burden. Defined-benefit valuation changes discount rate: route remeasurement by framework, not generic payroll accrual. Multi-employer plan lacks sufficient information: framework-specific accounting/disclosure required. Cash bonus settled after year-end: classify short-term vs other long-term based on expected settlement and plan terms.
