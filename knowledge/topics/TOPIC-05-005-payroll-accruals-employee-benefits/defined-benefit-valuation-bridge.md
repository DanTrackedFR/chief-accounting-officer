# Defined-benefit obligation, plan assets and payroll separation

**Simplified actuarial rollforward.** Opening defined-benefit obligation 1,000; current service cost 60, interest cost 40, benefit payments 50 and remeasurement loss 30 yield closing obligation 1,080. Plan assets open 800, interest income 32, employer contributions 70, benefit payments 50 and investment remeasurement gain 18 yield closing assets 870. Simplified net deficit rises from 200 to 210. Under IAS 19, current service/net interest and remeasurements have different P&L/OCI treatment; do not book the 30 actuarial loss as ordinary wages or deduct contributions 70 from service cost. Verify asset-ceiling and other plan details if applicable. This example assumes no amendments, curtailments, settlements or FX and a specified 4% opening discount/return basis; actual actuarial valuation must support assumptions.

**Systems bridge.** Employee census and salary/service history drive obligation; custodian confirms plan assets and benefit payments; payroll gives contribution remittances and short-term wage accruals. Link each data source to signed actuary report and GL rollforward. If payroll already expensed employer contributions 70 in an IAS 19 defined-benefit arrangement, reconcile and correct classification rather than treating the contribution as a separate defined-contribution cost. US ASC 715, FRS 102 Section 28 and AASB 119 presentation/remeasurement and disclosures require independent case analysis.

**Tests.** 1,000+60+40−50+30=1,080; 800+32+70−50+18=870; net deficit 210. Fail netting benefit payments in only one side or calling contribution 70 current service expense. Preserve plan document, census, discount curve, mortality/salary assumptions, asset statements, actuary sensitivity, journal and reviewer challenge. [IAS 19](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-19-employee-benefits/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/); US paragraph depth limited.
