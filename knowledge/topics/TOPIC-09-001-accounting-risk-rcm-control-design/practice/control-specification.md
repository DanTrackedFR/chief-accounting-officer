# CAO control specification — risk assessment and RCM

This operational specification supplements the README and worked case for CAO-09-001–003. It is not a four-framework accounting rule.

**Decision and workpaper.** Scope entity/book/period, significant accounts and disclosures, assertions, transaction streams, fraud and changes. A risk statement must identify a possible misstatement, causal point and financial consequence; “no review” is a missing control, not a risk. For each risk record magnitude/likelihood rationale, control objective, key control, owner, frequency, population, data/report lineage, detection precision, performance evidence, exception path, IT dependency and residual gap. The controller checks the risk universe against material accounts, recent transactions, errors and audit findings; an RCM copied from last year does not prove completeness.

**Example and evidence.** Delivery has 1,000 items, billing 970, unbilled accounting 25, five unresolved. Record risk of incomplete revenue/contract balances; evidence is independent delivery extract, complete billing and unbilled schedules, signed/absolute reconciliation, five IDs and decisions. A reviewer tests recognition on underlying contract/performance under Domain 03. The RCM design fails if it has only invoice approval; control operation fails if five remain unexplained after sign-off. A recurring failure triggers TOPIC-09-007 deficiency evaluation and TOPIC-11-003 lineage review.

**Jurisdiction/source.** IFRS, US GAAP, FRS 102 and AASB determine affected account assertions through the substantive transaction, not a common RCM law. Formal ICFR evaluation for an in-scope US issuer can use [SEC Release 33-8810](https://www.sec.gov/rule-release/33-8810); US auditor testing is under [PCAOB AS 2201](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201). For other entities resolve local governance/audit obligations, but retain risk-based control discipline. Both official sources checked 2026-09-27. Output: approved risk assessment, RCM version, open gaps, remediation owner and review date; no unconditional clean control claim.
