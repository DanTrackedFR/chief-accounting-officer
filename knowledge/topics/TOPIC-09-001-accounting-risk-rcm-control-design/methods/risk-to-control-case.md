# Risk-to-control matrix — complete design case

Extends existing substantive README. Capabilities CAO-09-001–003. PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE; 2026-09-27.

Risk statement: December services delivered but not billed are omitted from revenue and contract assets/liabilities because billing, delivery and GL populations do not reconcile. A mere “invoice approval” control addresses a different assertion. Map revenue and unbilled balances, completeness/cutoff, legal entities, interfaces and policy. Design: at close, a qualified reviewer compares independently controlled delivery/contract populations to billed, deferred and unbilled GL schedules, investigates every material difference and approves the final journal/reporting bridge. Specify source report IDs, filter dates, threshold by account/entity, reviewer expectations, evidence and correction route. An automated count control can support but not replace technical recognition judgments.

Illustration: delivery population 1,000 items; billed 970; unbilled schedule 25; five unresolved. The control fails completeness until those five are traced or supported as outside scope. Counts alone do not prove amounts: reconcile currency/amount and inspect contracts. Workpaper records 1,000=970+25+5, exception IDs, cutoff, owner and effect on statements. If a control owner signs with five unresolved and no assessment, design/operation fails. Reverse-map every significant account/disclosure to risk and key controls, including IT/data dependencies; document uncovered risks and redundant controls.

Source boundary: [SEC 33-8810](https://www.sec.gov/rule-release/33-8810) describes a top-down, risk-based ICFR evaluation for applicable US issuers; checked 2026-09-27. It is not a requirement of every IFRS/UK/AASB entity. Underlying revenue standard and effective period route to Domain 03. Evidence: risk assessment version, RCM, lineage, control design, dated execution and issue resolution. Result PASS for documented decision route, subject to independent control review.
