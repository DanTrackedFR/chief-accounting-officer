# Audit independence and communication boundary

## Engagement route and decision
Map auditor firm, engagement, covered entities, independence regime and non-audit services approvals. PCAOB independence requirements and AS 1301 govern applicable issuer audit interactions; other engagements route to applicable ethics code and local governance rules. CAO supplies complete service/relationship information and timely financial-reporting matters; the audit firm makes its independence determination.

## Control, evidence and data
Status register tracks open audit issues, materiality-sensitive matters, differences, control findings, owners, dates, evidence packet/version, escalation and governance meeting decisions. Preserve auditor communication and approved minutes. Legal/confidential matters follow controlled access; no undocumented side-channel resolution of accounting conclusions.

## Adverse case and escalation
The company asks its external auditor to design the key close control it will audit. Freeze engagement approval, route independence review to audit firm and governance before work begins, engage another adviser if needed, and document management ownership of design. Escalate an unresolved independence concern.

## Handoff and authority
Link the evidence packet to the request register and final statement version; route accounting errors to Domain 02, control failures to Domain 09 and systems/data gaps to Domain 11. Reassess scope and effective dates at each new engagement. References: [PCAOB auditing standards](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards), [IAASB standards and pronouncements](https://www.iaasb.org/standards-pronouncements), [IAASB 2025 Handbook](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/2025-handbook-international-quality-management-auditing-review-other-assurance-and-related-services). These references describe auditor responsibilities; management owns books, support and correction decisions.
