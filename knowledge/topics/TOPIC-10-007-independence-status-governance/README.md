# TOPIC-10-007 — Auditor Independence Information Support / Audit Status & Issue Governance

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Capabilities: CAO-10-017, CAO-10-018
Sensitivity: L with jurisdiction overlay

## Scope boundary
The CAO supports the company's side of auditor-independence information gathering and audit governance. The auditor is responsible for its independence conclusion under the applicable professional/regulatory regime. The CAO must not present a company process as determining auditor independence.

## Independence information support
Maintain a controlled register of auditor/audit-firm services and company-side facts needed for governance review: engagement/service description; entity; period; fee category/value; approval route; contracting party; related systems/accounting involvement; management responsibility retained; start/end dates; governance approval evidence; known affiliate/entity changes that may affect the information set.

Route actual permissibility and independence conclusions to the applicable auditor/professional/regulatory framework. Company policy may be stricter than the minimum rule and should be stored as Company Context.

## Audit status governance
Maintain one issue log spanning PBC critical path, technical issues, proposed adjustments, control findings, legal/specialist dependencies, disclosure issues and governance decisions. Each item has owner, status, due date, impact, decision needed, evidence, auditor position if known, management position, escalation level and closure criterion.

## Meeting cadence
Working-level status: requests/blockers. Controller/CAO review: accounting conclusions, adjustments, findings and timetable. CFO/executive escalation: material judgments, delays and unresolved differences. Audit committee/board route: only matters required by applicable governance/regulation or company governance policy, supported by concise decision-ready summaries.

For PCAOB engagements, AS 1301 requires specified two-way auditor/audit-committee communications, including audit strategy/timing, significant risks and timely significant observations; required communications should occur before report issuance. These are auditor requirements. Company-side governance should facilitate accurate information and timely escalation without taking over the auditor's obligation.

## Controls
Service/fee register completeness; preapproval evidence where company policy/regime requires it; entity-change trigger; issue-log owner/reviewer; status aging; decision log; meeting minutes/actions; final unresolved-item reconciliation before financial statement authorization/issuance process.

## Scenario tests
- Audit firm proposes non-audit service: CAO captures facts and governance approvals, routes permissibility to applicable independence regime/auditor — PASS.
- New subsidiary acquired midyear: trigger independence-information and audit-scope update — PASS.
- Material technical issue remains open near signing: escalate with facts/options/impact rather than marking green for timetable — PASS.
- Auditor-management disagreement resolves after new facts: preserve decision history and final conclusion — PASS.

## Sources
PCAOB AS 1301, Communications with Audit Committees (official, checked 2026-09-23). Other independence regimes require jurisdiction-specific source resolution before use.
