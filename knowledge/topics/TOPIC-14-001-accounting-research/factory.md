# Accounting research, guidance identification and hierarchy — applied factory

Retains original substantive README. CAO-14-001–003. Source check 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for method; no claim that any unresearched transaction is concluded.

## Reproducible research record

Freeze the exact fact question, entity, framework, jurisdiction, reporting period, transaction date, comparative dates and materiality. Search by rights/obligations and economic event, not management's label. Build an authority tree: directly applicable standard/codification scope and definitions → relevant recognition and measurement → presentation and disclosure → standard-specific transition → official interpretations/agenda decisions or regulator overlay at their actual authority → professional interpretation only for challenge. Where no direct provision applies, use the framework's formal policy hierarchy: [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/) for IFRS, current [FASB Codification](https://asc.fasb.org/) for US GAAP, [FRC FRS 102 Section 10](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) for UK GAAP and [AASB 108](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-108-mar-2021) for Australian reporting. FRS 102 Periodic Review 2024 is generally effective from 2026; IFRS 18 from 2027 may change IAS 8 title/cross-references. FASB ASUs communicate Codification changes but the current Codification is the relevant US source. SEC guidance applies to registrants, not every US GAAP preparer.

Log `source_id`, publisher URL, standard/section, *verified* paragraph if accessible, version, effective periods, checked date, rights/reference-only status and what proposition the source supports. The public FASB portal does not expose all current paragraphs; mark paragraph verification PARTIAL rather than fabricate. Never copy restricted standard text or substitute extensive paraphrase. If source is superseded or a proposal, record it as history/future, not current authority.

## Worked research tree

A cloud ERP implementation is called a “software asset.” Questions: who controls the code? Is there a software licence or only access service? What does configuration create? Which costs and service periods? Under IFRS first test IAS 38 and relevant IFRIC agenda decision; under US route hosting versus internal-use software/ASC 350-40, period including ASU 2025-06 effective-date status; under UK FRS 102 Section 18/current Section 20/23 as relevant; under AASB 138/other current compilation. Outcome may differ even with identical vendor invoices. A cost schedule of 200k containing 80k licence, 70k configuration, 30k training and 20k data migration is an **input population**, not a 200k capitalization answer. Require contract, vendor invoice, SOW, milestones and control analysis before categorizing each amount. A conclusion records alternatives, assumptions, journal/disclosure/control impact and unresolved items.

## Controls and scenarios

Research reviewer independently rechecks scope, effective date, alternative/exception, authoritative source and fact-to-conclusion trace; citation audit samples a key claim back to linked primary literature. S1: secondary blog conflicts with standard → primary source prevails. S2: current US conclusion cites only a 2005 pre-Codification statement → source-depth FAIL. S3: 2025 UK period uses 2026 rule without early adoption → period FAIL. S4: four framework conclusions share same wording but divergent fact assumptions → require frozen facts. S5: source is inaccessible at paragraph body → record PARTIAL and escalate before final paragraph-level claim. Artifacts: scoped research plan, issue tree, source register, alternatives table, signed memo and follow-on topic links (04-004 software, 15-001 policy, 14-008 citation QA).
