# TOPIC-14-001 — Accounting Research / Applicable Guidance Identification / Literature Hierarchy

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**
Primary capabilities: CAO-14-001, CAO-14-002, CAO-14-003
Sensitivity: M
Source checked: 2026-09-24

## Purpose
Give the CAO a repeatable method for finding the accounting question, identifying authoritative literature, separating requirements from interpretation and practice, and producing a traceable research conclusion.

## Research workflow
1. Frame the accounting question in facts, transaction, entity, period, framework and jurisdiction.
2. Identify scope and definitions before recognition or measurement.
3. Build a keyword and synonym map from the facts rather than searching only management's label.
4. Search primary authority first and record standard/topic, paragraph pointers, effective period and source URL.
5. Check amendments, transition, agenda decisions or other official interpretive material relevant to the period.
6. Identify jurisdictional overlays separately from the accounting framework.
7. Use reputable secondary interpretation to test completeness, never to silently replace available primary authority.
8. Build alternatives and exceptions, then apply the facts.
9. Record unresolved evidence, assumptions and judgments.
10. Produce conclusion, accounting consequences, disclosures, controls, documentation and follow-on skill routing.
11. Save citations and provenance; promote durable policy conclusions to Company Accounting Memory only after appropriate review.

## Literature hierarchy
Primary authoritative requirements outrank implementation commentary. Official interpretations, agenda decisions and regulator material are classified according to their actual authority and jurisdiction. Professional-firm material is secondary interpretation. CAO-authored methods and world-class practice are PRACTICE, not GAAP.

Where the applicable framework has a formal policy hierarchy for issues not directly addressed, follow that hierarchy rather than borrowing another framework automatically.

## Four-framework behavior
IFRS research resolves the applicable IFRS Accounting Standard, effective amendments and relevant IFRIC material. US GAAP research routes to the FASB Accounting Standards Codification and applicable SEC guidance for SEC registrants; public Codification access limitations must remain visible in source status. UK GAAP research routes to the operative FRS 102 version plus company-law/FRC overlays. AASB research independently verifies the operative Australian standard and entity/tier overlays rather than assuming IFRS equivalence.

## Evidence standard
Every material standards-sensitive conclusion records framework, period, source pointer, source status, facts relied on, judgment, alternatives considered and confidence. Quotation is not required; independently authored explanation plus precise authoritative pointer is preferred under repository rights rules.

## Controls and QA
Reviewer re-performs scope/effective-date check; verifies at least one primary-authority route for material requirements; checks exceptions and transition; confirms jurisdiction is not confused with framework; checks that pipeline proposals are not presented as current GAAP; validates citations and links; confirms conclusion-to-journal/disclosure traceability.

## Failure modes
Searching from a desired answer; starting with secondary commentary; ignoring scope; stale standard version; missing transition; treating agenda proposals as effective; importing IFRS into US GAAP or vice versa; confusing regulator rules with GAAP; unsupported paragraph precision; copied copyrighted standard text; conclusion without fact linkage.

## Scenario QA
1. Ambiguous contract label — PASS: facts and scope precede label.
2. IFRS issue with no direct requirement — PASS: framework policy hierarchy.
3. SEC registrant — PASS: Codification plus SEC overlay.
4. UK period spanning major FRS 102 amendments — PASS: effective-period gate.
5. AASB topic aligned with IFRS — PASS: independent Australian verification.
6. FASB paragraph body inaccessible publicly — PASS: PARTIAL source depth, no invented citation.
7. Proposed amendment found — PASS: pipeline status, not current GAAP.
8. Secondary source conflicts with primary source — PASS: primary authority wins.
9. Material judgment — PASS: alternatives and evidence documented.
10. Durable policy result — PASS: reviewed memory promotion.

## Completion criteria
Research is complete when the issue is correctly framed, applicable authority and period are established, material alternatives/exceptions are addressed, facts are applied, provenance is traceable, and accounting/reporting/control consequences are explicit.
