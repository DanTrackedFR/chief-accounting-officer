# Industry regulatory returns and change impact — substantive factory

Companion to original README. Capabilities CAO-16-014/015. Source checked 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for change-control method; any sector-specific return remains PARTIAL absent an identified rule.

## Applicability and scope gate

Map each licence to issuing authority, regulated activity, jurisdiction, legal entity, relevant product, reporting period and return name. A financial service company may submit a prudential capital return, conduct return, safeguarding report or transaction-volume return with different definitions. A health or energy business may face another regulator entirely. The accounting framework supplies source entries and recognition conclusions but is not a substitute for the return's rulebook. Select the official regulator publication, technical specification, FAQs (at their actual authority level), filing portal and effective date. Do not infer regulatory interpretation from one industry's analogous field. Possible primary portals include [APRA](https://www.apra.gov.au/cross-industry/prudential-and-reporting-standards), [UK FCA handbook](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/), [EBA](https://www.eba.europa.eu/regulation-and-policy/single-rulebook), and [US Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg.htm); which one applies is a case fact.

## Data and change-control factory

An EBA interactive rulebook/FAQ helps locate requirements but has no independent legal effect; verify the authentic EU instrument in the Official Journal and national rule where required. Classify supervisory guidance and informal FAQ at their actual authority level.

For every return line record rule citation, accounting source or operational event population, scope/filter, unit, period basis, transformation, manual overlay, evidence location and named compliance owner. Reconcile source total to GL, subledger or audited operational ledger, then document departures required by the return. Change bulletin triage: identify rule status (proposal/adopted/effective), transition, affected legal entities, mapping fields, source systems, controls and reporting periods. Compare old/new requirements line-by-line, estimate historic/backfill and model impacts, build parallel run and independent reviewer challenge, approve cutover and archive prior rule and output. A legal interpretation conflict goes to counsel/regulatory owner, while CAO owns accuracy of supplied accounting data and bridges.

## Worked example

A payments group reports 42m customer funds under a sector return. ERP bank accounts total 47m, but 5m belongs to corporate operating cash; 47m − 5m = 42m only if account ownership, cut-off, currency and outstanding settlement timing support the split. Separately reconcile the 42m to corresponding customer liabilities and investigate any timing/foreign-exchange differences. A revised rule includes a new category of pending settlement balances: do not simply add it to both assets and liabilities; dry-run the amended instructions, test duplication and recompute comparative information if required.

## Controls and scenarios

S1: rule is only proposed, system mapping changed for current return → fail effective-date gate. S2: FAQ appears to conflict with binding rule → flag hierarchy and obtain regulatory interpretation before changing ledger mapping. S3: new data field has no source-to-return lineage → fail parallel-run approval. S4: valid source reconciliation but wrong licensed entity included → fail perimeter control. Preserve change log, official source/period, mapping diff, parallel-run comparison, exception sign-off and filing receipt. Dependencies 06-001 cash, 11-003 interfaces, 16-006 capital, 16-004 disclosure controls. Sector-specific obligations are not generalized; this is a tested factory for executing them.
