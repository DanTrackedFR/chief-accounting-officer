# Assertion-level tie-out and late-change protocol

**Population.** Start with the exact authorized consolidation/TB version, all primary statement lines, each numerical note, accounting policy/judgment assertions, management narrative and comparative column. Assign a stable assertion ID to each published number or material statement. Map assertion → source owner → source version → transformation/formula → final output location. A cash note total that agrees to the balance sheet may still omit a restricted account; cross-footing tests accuracy, whereas source-population and classification tests completeness and presentation.

**Example.** Draft balance sheet cash 120 comprises banks 108 and petty cash 12. Cash flow closing cash equivalents show 110 because 10 restricted cash is excluded, while an additional 2 of bank overdrafts are included in a different cash-pool convention. The observed 10 gap does not resolve itself by adding a footnote: establish item-by-item classification under the applicable cash-flow and balance-sheet rules, account for any overdraft as applicable, then produce a signed bridge from 120 to the selected cash-flow measure with source bank confirmations. If the restricted amount is actually 15 after bank confirmation, invalidate both earlier numbers and the affected note/cash-flow certifications. Avoid arbitrary offsetting to make 120−10=110 when an overdraft of 2 also exists; the correct bridge depends on its cash-management facts.

**Control procedure.** Freeze document build and source hashes; rerun mathematical relationships, note rollforwards and duplicate disclosures; compare narrative claims to debt covenants, related-party register, litigation letters, going-concern forecast and subsequent-event log. Log every discrepancy with assertion ID, amount/qualitative risk, source owner, resolution and reviewer. A late journal affecting revenue, AR and tax triggers those mappings, cash flows, EPS/APM, comparative and relevant disclosures, not just the edited P&L cell. Certify only after rerun and closure; retain old/new versions and reasons. Select the correct checklist by IFRS/ASC and SEC if applicable, UK FRS 102/company law, or AASB entity/tier and current period; IFRS 18 generally becomes mandatory from 2027 and can change presentation/checklist coverage.

**Negative tests.** A note that ties internally to 110 but conflicts with a bank confirmation fails. A textual “no covenant breach” assertion contradicted by TOPIC-06-006 fails even if amounts tie. A reviewer signature predating a late journal is invalid for affected assertions. The procedure is practice-led; standards control the content and classification, not a universal tie-out format. Official anchors [IAS 7](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows/), [IFRS 18](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). US current authoritative detailed requirements require source access.
