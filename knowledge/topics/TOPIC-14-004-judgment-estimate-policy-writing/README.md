# TOPIC-14-004 — Judgment, estimate methodology and accounting policy writing

Capabilities: CAO-14-010, CAO-14-011, CAO-14-012. Status: DRAFT for independent QA; existing `tests.md` preserved as substantive 10-scenario routing evidence. Prepared 2026-09-27.

## Principles and source routing

A document must state the decision management actually made and enable a reviewer to reproduce it. Separate standard-backed accounting requirements, interpretation of facts, numerical estimates and optional operational policy. Confirm entity, framework, jurisdiction and reporting period before citing. IFRS: [IAS 8 official overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/) and transaction-specific Standard; US: [FASB Codification](https://asc.fasb.org/) ASC 250 and specific topic; UK: [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) Section 10 (2024 periodic review generally effective from periods beginning 1 January 2026); Australia: [AASB 108](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-108-mar-2021) and entity/tier overlays. Sources checked 2026-09-27. FASB paragraph bodies not independently verified in open access; do not fabricate paragraph-level support.

## Distinct artifacts

**Judgment paper:** decision question, locked facts and date, relevant criteria, strongest alternative, evidence for/against, conclusion and consequence, changes that would reverse it, GL/note/controls, preparer/reviewer. Example: a supplier has substitution rights in a hosted asset contract; document practical ability and economic benefit, not merely the contract's “lease” heading.

**Estimate methodology:** balance/assertion, measurement objective and unit, data population/source/cut-off, assumptions and uncertainty, model/version/calculation, validation and back-test, sensitivity and bias challenge, specialist input, prospective change/error analysis, ledger/disclosure reconciliation. Example: a 2% default assumption on 5m receivables produces 100k before recoveries/other adjustments; show cohort, evidence and alternative rate sensitivity rather than present 100k as an unsupported plug.

**Accounting policy:** authoritative requirement and entity-specific elections; applicable transaction classes/entities; recognition, measurement, presentation, disclosure and effective date; transition from old method; exceptions and approval; implementation in ERP, reconciliations and training; annual/change-trigger review. Example: a 2,000 capitalization threshold is a materiality/process policy, not a permission to recognize nonqualifying spend as PPE.

## Evidence and controls

Maintain claim-to-authority and calculation-to-source indexes, cited source version/date, source access limits, document owner, approval, supersession and review cadence. An independent reviewer checks fact chronology, alternatives, math, current effective literature, classification of policy/estimate/error, entries and disclosure before release.
