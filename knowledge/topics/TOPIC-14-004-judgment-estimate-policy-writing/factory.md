# Judgment, estimate methodology and policy papers — applied factory

Retains existing README and 10-scenario `tests.md`. CAO-14-010–012. Source checked 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for documentation method, not a blanket conclusion on substantive accounting standards.

## Three separate paper types

Judgment paper: criterion, entity/period, frozen facts, verified authority, evidence supporting and contradicting each alternative, conclusion, monetary impact, conditions for revisit. Estimate methodology: measurement objective, population, data and cut-off, model, input evidence, sensitivity, uncertainty, bias challenge, back-test, approvals and GL/note reconciliation. Policy paper: scope, mandatory principle versus permitted elections, recognition, measurement, presentation, disclosure, transition, consistency class, implementation in ERP/SOP and review trigger. Integrate, but do not merge, these records: policy specifies method, judgment applies threshold to facts, estimate supplies uncertain amount. IFRS [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/), US [ASC 250](https://asc.fasb.org/), [FRS 102 Section 10](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) and [AASB 108](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-108-mar-2021) distinguish policy, estimate and error; period/version and transaction-specific guidance still govern. IFRS 18's 2027 effective date can change IAS 8 references; don't cite a 2026 edition for a 2027 paper without checking.

## Miniature estimate methodology

For a receivables allowance, define aging data at reporting date 5.5m: 4m current ×1%=40k; 1m overdue ×5%=50k; 0.5m severely overdue ×20%=100k; base 190k. A separately supported forward-looking overlay 25k results in 215k, not a management plug. Paper must explain historical cohort calibration, default/write-off definitions, forecast scenario, source completeness, overlapping overlay risk, model validation, prior outcome back-test and entry from existing allowance. With book allowance 170k, proposed expense increment 45k. If 0.2m AR subledger-to-GL difference exists, withhold the estimate until population reconciliation is resolved. Distinguish input update based on new experience from a prior spreadsheet omission using available information. Judgment paper may separately address whether a receivable is a contract asset or financial asset; policy paper defines consistent ECL approach under framework.

## Controls and executed scenarios

Claim-to-source matrix, locked spreadsheet/model version, independent arithmetic and source sample, effective-date verification, alternative challenge, approved reviewer, ERP and disclosure tie. S1: 190+25=215 and 215−170=45 (thousands), arithmetic PASS. S2: model omits known 0.2m balance → reject final estimate. S3: useful-life changed due new evidence but paper calls it retrospective policy change → reject classification. S4: paper reproduces standards text instead of company facts → fail rights/entity application. S5: 2027 IFRS paper cites old IAS 1/IAS 8 location without IFRS 18 gate → fail. Original tests.md still carries ten broader desk scenarios; all must be rerun when sources or paper design change. Dependencies 15-001–004, 03-008 ECL, 14-003 memo and 14-008 QA.
