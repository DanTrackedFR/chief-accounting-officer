# Impairment and goodwill — framework-specific completion method

Companion to `applied-qa.md` and its independently calculated IFRS and US cases. CAO-04-015/016.

## Scope, sequence and measurement

IFRS IAS 36/AASB 136: indicator review for finite-lived assets; annual test for goodwill, indefinite-lived and not-yet-ready intangibles. Test the smallest independent cash-inflow unit when the asset alone cannot be tested. Align carrying amount and recoverable amount perimeter. Recoverable amount is higher of value in use and fair value less costs of disposal. Test relevant individual assets before the goodwill-bearing CGU, allocate CGU loss first to goodwill, then other assets subject to individual floors. Never reverse goodwill impairment; other asset reversal has a no-impairment ceiling [04-006-IFRS-01; 04-006-AASB-01]. UK FRS 102 Section 27 has its own recoverable-amount and allocation model; goodwill is normally amortized under Section 19 before relevant impairment review. Do not use an IFRS no-amortization opening goodwill balance [04-006-UK-01].

US held-and-used long-lived assets under ASC 360: identify indicators and lowest largely independent cash-flow group, compare carrying value with undiscounted use-and-disposal cash flows, and if unrecoverable measure loss by carrying value less fair value. Held-for-sale applies a separate classification/measurement and depreciation route. Test finite-lived intangibles in the applicable long-lived group. Indefinite-lived intangibles and goodwill use distinct ASC 350 tests; goodwill is tested at a reporting unit after other applicable asset impairment work, with qualitative-screen election where applicable. Eligible private entities may elect goodwill amortization/alternative testing, so entity status is a necessary input [04-006-US-01; 04-006-US-02].

## Numerical reconciliation and boundary

IFRS CGU 12.0m including 1.5m goodwill, VIU 10.8m, FVLCD 11.0m: recoverable amount 11.0m; loss 1.0m assigned to goodwill, remaining goodwill 0.5m. Dr loss 1.0m / Cr goodwill 1.0m. A later 13.0m recoverable amount does not reverse this goodwill loss. US *different* asset group 12.0m with 11.5m undiscounted cash and 10.8m fair value fails recoverability and gives 1.2m loss, subject to allocation; with 12.1m undiscounted cash it passes even when fair value is 10.8m. Do not imply the two groups, standards or valuations are identical [04-006-IFRS-01; 04-006-US-01].

A separate US reporting unit has 8.0m carrying amount including 2.0m goodwill and 7.4m supported fair value after other applicable impairments. Assuming no special election, illustrative goodwill impairment is 0.6m, capped by the 2.0m goodwill; Dr goodwill impairment 0.6m / Cr goodwill 0.6m. If the 8.0m includes a 1.2m ASC 360 impairment that has not yet been posted, recalculate the reporting-unit carrying and valuation basis before goodwill: blindly recording both 1.2m and 0.6m fails the sequencing test [04-006-US-02].

## Evidence and disclosure

Freeze CGU/reporting-unit/asset-group maps, opening carrying values and prior impairments, acquisition goodwill allocation, indicator log, forecast version, cash-flow perimeter, discount/terminal assumptions, market-participant support, fair-value hierarchy, climate/technology sensitivities, tax consistency and headroom. A second reviewer challenges budget back-test, independence of cash inflows, model access, discounting only where the chosen method uses it, valuation reconciliation and note sensitivities. Link impairments and reversals to income statement and asset-class rollforwards; disclose material events, amounts, segments/units and estimation uncertainty under the applicable standard. AASB 1060 Tier 2 disclosure and the operative 2026 AASB 136 compilation are separate gates [04-006-AASB-01; 04-006-UK-01].

Adverse tests: US undiscounted screen replaced with discounted VIU; goodwill loss reversed; UK goodwill left unamortized by importing IFRS; unsupported CGU aggregation conceals loss; a sale classification retains held-and-used depreciation. Each fails and forces a revised workpaper [04-006-US-01; 04-006-UK-01].
