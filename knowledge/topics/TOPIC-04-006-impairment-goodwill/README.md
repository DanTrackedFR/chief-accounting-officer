# TOPIC-04-006 — Asset Impairment & Goodwill Impairment Support

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate; US detailed model routing retained as framework-specific
Built: 2026-09-23
Capabilities: CAO-04-015, CAO-04-016

## Source map
IFRS: IAS 36 Impairment of Assets; IFRS 13 where fair value is used; IFRS 3 for goodwill origin. AASB: AASB 136 current compilation is period-sensitive; January 2026 compilation incorporates AASB 2026-1 illustrative uncertainty examples for specified periods. UK GAAP: FRS 102 Section 27 plus Section 19 goodwill/business combinations. US GAAP: ASC 360 long-lived assets and ASC 350 goodwill/intangibles; do not use IAS 36 mechanics by analogy.

## IFRS / AASB model
An asset cannot be carried above recoverable amount. Recoverable amount is the higher of fair value less costs of disposal and value in use. If individual cash inflows are not sufficiently independent, test at the cash-generating-unit level. Goodwill, indefinite-lived intangibles and intangibles not yet available for use require annual testing in addition to indicator-triggered testing. Other in-scope assets are tested when indicators exist.

For a CGU impairment, allocate loss first to goodwill then generally pro rata to other assets subject to framework floors. Goodwill impairment is never reversed. Other qualifying impairment losses are reversed when required by changed estimates, capped so carrying amount does not exceed the amount that would have existed absent impairment.

## Goodwill execution
1. Reconcile goodwill by acquisition and currency.
2. Confirm allocation to CGU/group of CGUs at the level required by framework and consistent with monitoring.
3. Test individual assets/other impairment issues in correct sequence.
4. Build CGU carrying amount consistently with recoverable-amount model.
5. Determine FVLCD and/or VIU; only one is needed if demonstrably above carrying amount.
6. Challenge forecast period, revenue/margin assumptions, capex/working capital, terminal growth, discount rate, tax consistency and climate/market/technology assumptions.
7. Run sensitivity/headroom analysis and disclosures.

## UK GAAP
Use FRS 102 Section 27 rather than IAS 36 automatically. Goodwill under FRS 102 is amortized, unlike IFRS/US GAAP ordinary goodwill, and impairment interacts with that finite-life model. Section 19/27 requirements and reporting-period version must be applied. This is a major framework difference.

## US GAAP
Route asset type first. Long-lived assets held and used under ASC 360 use US-specific recoverability/measurement mechanics; goodwill under ASC 350 has a separate reporting-unit model. Private-company accounting alternatives/elections can materially change goodwill amortization/impairment. Never use IFRS CGU/recoverable-amount mechanics as a substitute for ASC 350/360.

## AASB temporal overlay
AASB 136 compilation selection depends on reporting-period dates. AASB 2026-1 added illustrative examples concerning uncertainty in recoverable-amount assumptions for specified 2025/26 periods. Illustrative examples inform disclosure application but do not replace the standard's recognition/measurement requirements.

## CAO evidence
Board-approved forecast and actual-to-budget history; CGU/reporting-unit map; acquisition/goodwill allocation; carrying-value bridge; valuation model; discount-rate support; market multiples; terminal growth; sensitivity; impairment indicators; strategic/closure decisions; climate/technology assumptions; external valuation if used; disclosure draft.

## Controls
Quarterly indicator review; annual-test calendar; model version control; forecast approval; carrying-value completeness; goodwill allocation governance; independent discount-rate/valuation review; sensitivity threshold; disclosure tie-out; audit evidence archive.

## Scenarios
1. IFRS CGU has 5% headroom but reasonably possible margin change eliminates it: assess sensitivity disclosure, not only pass/fail.
2. Goodwill impairment recognized under IFRS then business recovers: no reversal.
3. UK FRS 102 goodwill: include amortization model; do not treat as indefinite-lived IFRS goodwill.
4. US long-lived asset has weak forecast: route through ASC 360 model, not IAS 36 VIU.
5. Australian 2025/26 entity: resolve exact AASB 136 compilation and consider new uncertainty examples.

## QA
PASS for cross-framework orchestration. US GAAP detailed numerical impairment testing must retrieve current ASC 350/360 records before final calculation; this is a deliberate framework-specific dependency, not a blocker.
