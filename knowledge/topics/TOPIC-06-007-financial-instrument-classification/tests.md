# TOPIC-06-007 — Scenario QA

Status: PASS — 10/10 routing scenarios

1. IFRS fixed-rate loan held to collect: require business-model + SPPI evidence before amortised-cost conclusion.
2. IFRS debt portfolio actively managed through collection and sales: test collect-and-sell + SPPI before FVOCI.
3. IFRS asset with ESG-linked contingent cash flows, 2026 period: invoke post-2024 amendment effective-date gate and assess contractual cash-flow characteristics.
4. US Treasury debt investment intended to maturity: route to ASC 320 rather than IFRS 9 SPPI logic; document HTM criteria/evidence.
5. US listed equity investment: route to ASC 321; do not classify as AFS debt security.
6. US private equity security without readily determinable fair value: assess ASC 321 measurement-alternative eligibility/election and observable-transaction remeasurement requirements.
7. FRS 102 simple trade/loan instrument: establish entity policy election and Section 11/basic-instrument routing before measurement.
8. FRS 102 complex derivative-linked instrument: test Section 12/other-instrument routing and policy election; do not force Section 11.
9. AASB 9 debt asset in 2026: use operative Australian compilation, business model + SPPI, and AASB 2024-2 effective-period changes.
10. Convertible/contingent instrument under any framework: stop generic asset/liability classification and route embedded derivative / liability-equity / compound-instrument analysis as applicable.

## QA assertions
- No IFRS category is automatically translated into a US GAAP category.
- Framework and effective period precede classification.
- Legal label never substitutes for contractual analysis.
- Elections are captured with timing and irrevocability where applicable.
- Classification output links forward to measurement, impairment, FX, disclosures and controls.
- US paragraph-level status remains PARTIAL where current Codification body has not been independently verified.
