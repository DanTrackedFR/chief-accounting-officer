# Debt-asset classification: evidence independent of legal label

**Two gates.** Portfolio Alpha holds bonds with contractual principal and a fixed market-rate return; management measures performance on contractual collections, collects cash and sells only rarely for documented credit reasons. Under the effective IFRS 9 business-model and SPPI analysis, amortized cost may be appropriate if contractual clauses actually satisfy SPPI and exceptions/elections do not alter outcome. Portfolio Beta holds otherwise identical instruments but managers are remunerated on fair-value performance and regularly sell to realize changes; a “hold-to-collect” assertion based solely on contractual maturity fails. Assess fair-value-through-profit-or-loss or other applicable category. Gather portfolio-level risk/compensation reports, actual sales by reason, investment policy and contract clauses; a single transaction can be nonrepresentative, but undocumented frequent turnover undermines the stated model.

**Contingent return fork.** Debt acquired for 980 pays annual coupon 40 and principal 1,000, but final principal rises 300 if an unrelated commodity index exceeds a threshold. Analyze contractual cash flows under operative IFRS 9, including May 2024 amendments effective for annual periods from 1 January 2026. A low probability of trigger does not, by itself, make the clause irrelevant. Model possible payoff 1,300 and 1,000, instrument cash-flow characteristics and benchmark comparison as required; do not build an amortized-cost EIR before classification. If instead returns vary with a borrower-specific sustainability target, assess the amended contingent-feature rules on their own terms rather than equating every ESG clause with commodity exposure. Document transition and comparative implications for actual adoption date. IFRS 9 asset categories do not determine issuer debt/equity classification; send convertible/warrant terms to TOPIC-13-007.

**Framework tests.** AASB 9 incorporates Australian 2026 amendment versions and entity/tier scope; FRS 102 Sections 11/12 and any permitted IFRS 9 policy election require explicit policy record; US ASC 320/321/825 and other topics use distinct classification. Fail Beta as amortized cost merely from maturity, a commodity-linked 300 ignored due to probability, and an IFRS conclusion copied into US ledger.
