# TOPIC-06-007 — evidence and adverse regression

Claims: [TOPIC-06-007-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-06-007-US-001], [TOPIC-06-007-UK-001], [TOPIC-06-007-AASB-001]. Existing work retained.

## Worked decision and failed branch

Portfolio A is held to collect, but a note pays 5% plus a contingent amount indexed to the borrower’s revenue. The business-model limb alone cannot secure amortised cost; assess the contingent SPPI terms under 2026 IFRS 9 amendments. A plain fixed-rate collect-only note may qualify at amortised cost, subject to credit-loss model. An irrevocable FVOCI election for an eligible equity instrument is instrument-specific and not a means to recycle gains. US ASC 320/321/825 classifications differ materially; FRS 102 §§11/12 and AASB 9 must be routed by edition and entity type.

## Review gate

Document contract/rate inputs, date/version, journal, reconciling item, alternatives, independent review and disclosure tie-out. The IFRS claim uses the specified issued edition. For US, UK and Australia, repeat scope, recognition, measurement, presentation, disclosure, election and transition tests under the actual reporting-period text. Current ASC operative paragraph has not been inspected. Preserve unresolved differences in the audit queue.
