# Linked contract and related-party ledger-to-note case

**Fact base.** A parent controls a supplier that sells machinery to its reporting subsidiary for 120 and also provides a three-year service contract for 30 a year. A side letter promises the supplier 15 if a future production milestone is reached. At year-end, the subsidiary owes 40 on machinery invoices, 7 accrued services and a 15 disputed milestone amount; the parent guarantees an unrelated 50 bank loan. The underlying standard for each component determines recognition and measurement. Related-party status alone does not make the machinery worth 120, force it to fair value or validate “arm's-length terms.” Assess control/relationships, contract combination, equipment acquisition, service timing, contingent consideration/obligation, guarantee and consolidation eliminations under the appropriate topic. Obtain executed side letter, guarantee, ownership register, approvals and source invoices before selecting journals.

**Workpaper.** Build distinct rows: equipment gross contract 120 and asset recognition basis; service contract 3×30=90 nominal commitments before discount/timing assessment; AP 40; accrued services 7; milestone 15 with whether a present obligation exists; guarantee maximum exposure 50. Do not add 40+7+15+50 into one recognized balance. Reconcile the 40 and 7 to subledger and bank settlements; test the 15 against evidence at reporting date and the 50 against the separate guarantee model and disclosure requirements. IAS 24 note preparation considers the relationship, transaction amounts, outstanding balances/commitments and terms, subject to group/reporting-entity exemptions and materiality; eliminate intragroup balances in consolidated statements but retain necessary related-party consideration in subsidiary statements. Any assertion that terms are arm's length needs corroboration, not a related-party register checkbox.

**Adverse tests.** If the 15 milestone was met after year-end, do not backdate a new obligation solely due to hindsight; assess existing conditions and subsequent events. If the supplier is controlled by the parent's KMP rather than the parent, update the relationship decision and required disclosures. If a side letter turns the machine price into financing, route financial-instrument and asset-cost effects separately. Independent preparer and reviewer sign the relationship graph, contract version completeness, note-to-GL tie, elimination bridge, guarantees register and conflict approval.

This is a disclosure/control illustration and does not determine the unprovided fair values or thresholds.
