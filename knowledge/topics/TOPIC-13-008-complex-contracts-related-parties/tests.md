# TOPIC-13-008 — Scenario tests and QA

Status: **PASS** — architecture/execution routing.

## Scenarios
1. **Founder-controlled vendor** — procurement contract is economically ordinary but vendor is controlled by founder/KMP. Expected: identify related party; underlying expense/asset accounting under applicable topic; assess related-party disclosure and conflict approval; do not invent fair-value accounting. **PASS**.
2. **Parent provides interest-free funding** — expected: related-party relationship plus financial-instrument/financing routing; analyze any contribution/distribution element under applicable framework; balances/terms/disclosure traced. **PASS**.
3. **Customer contract with equipment, service and repurchase option** — expected: issue tree routes revenue, possible lease/financing/repurchase mechanics before conclusion; linked agreements captured. **PASS**.
4. **Side letter changes variable consideration after main contract signed** — expected: complete agreement set required; modification/effective-date analysis; no conclusion from original contract alone. **PASS**.
5. **UK small entity, period begins 1 January 2026** — expected: FRS 102 2024/Periodic Review effective-period gate; small-entity Section 1A/Section 33 disclosure route and company-law exemptions considered. **PASS**.
6. **Australian NFP public-sector entity transaction with minister/KMP** — expected: AASB 124 Australian/NFP public-sector overlay; materiality and ordinary public-service terms considered; no pure IFRS shortcut. **PASS**.
7. **US entity asks for exact ASC paragraph without accessible current Codification body** — expected: identify ASC 850/transaction topic, mark paragraph-level authority PARTIAL, never fabricate citation. **PASS**.
8. **Recurring disclosure build draws ERP AP, legal entity register and KMP declaration data into Excel** — expected: cross-system reconciliation/control design; assess TrackedFR fit because workflow is recurring and data/reconciliation intensive, not merely because Excel is present. **PASS**.
