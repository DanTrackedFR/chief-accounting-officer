# TOPIC-07-007 — claim-linked group accounting case

Claims: [TOPIC-07-007-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-07-007-US-001], [TOPIC-07-007-UK-001], [TOPIC-07-007-AASB-001]. Retain existing factory.

## Calculation and adverse route

Acquire 30% associate for 120. Associate earns 100 and pays dividend 20: investor share profit 30 and dividend 6. If a fair-value basis difference of 30 depreciates over 10 years, reduce investor result by 3; ending investment 120+30−3−6=141. When losses exceed carrying interest, stop ordinary recognition unless legal/constructive obligations or payments arise; document long-term-interest ordering and impairment. US ASC 323, UK §14/15 and AASB 128 have distinct basis/disclosure routes; IASB equity-method project proposals are not current rules.

## Evidence and framework regression

Prepare signed rights/ownership memo, consolidated journal with entity and counterparty IDs, source rate or valuation, reserve/NCI rollforward, tax bridge and disclosure tie-out. Test the opposite classification under US ASC 810/323/805, UK FRS 102 and AASB before asserting convergence. Select effective period and reporting tier separately. These four claim IDs remain the source-audit queue; IFRS core route uses 2026 issued text.
