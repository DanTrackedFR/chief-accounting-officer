# Associate basis difference, share of results and loss boundary

**Scope.** Parent owns 30% with documented significant influence through board participation and operating-policy input but neither control nor unanimous joint control. IFRS/AASB route to IAS 28/AASB 128 equity method, subject to exceptions; US ASC 323 and UK FRS 102 Section 14 have distinct mechanics/elections. At acquisition price 300, investor's 30% share of identifiable net assets' carrying value is 270; suppose a fair-value uplift on investee equipment attributable to investor is 24 with remaining life four years. The residual 6 is included within the investment's basis analysis under the applicable method, not booked as a separate consolidated goodwill asset in this simple illustration. Each full year's additional depreciation of investor's basis difference is 24/4=6. If investee post-acquisition profit is 100 and dividends total 20, investment before other effects is 300+30−6−6=318; if there is also a justified unrealized upstream profit elimination of 6 as in the retained case, it becomes 312. The retained case's 318 included such an elimination but **no** basis amortization; the identical number under this alternate scenario must not conceal different drivers. Reconcile components independently.

**Loss boundary.** If investor's interest including applicable long-term interests is exhausted by losses, stop routine negative carrying amounts except to the extent of legal/constructive obligations or payments under the operative guidance; assess IFRS 9 exposure on relevant long-term instruments and impairment. Do not use a zero investment as proof guarantees have no obligation. Subsequent profit may need to cover unrecognized losses before further income recognition. Use investee audited/interim financials and adjust reporting dates/policies; a fiscal-year lag does not permit ignoring material transactions between dates.

**Tests/evidence.** Verify 24/4=6; show separate line for 6 basis amortization and 6 upstream elimination; fail investment 324 from treating dividends as income or a 12 “plug” without provenance. Retain acquisition valuation, investee TB, dividends, transaction-level unrealized profit, board rights, impairment and obligation register, share/OCI rollforward and note disclosures. Direct [IAS 28](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-28-investments-in-associates-and-joint-ventures/) confirms equity-method scope; [AASB 128](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-128-dec-2022), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). IASB equity-method proposals as at September 2026 are not current standard. US detailed source pending.
