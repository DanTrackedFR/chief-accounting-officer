# Accounting elections supplement — CAO-15-014

The existing TOPIC-15-006 README substantially covers CAO-15-013 error/estimate/policy classification. This supplement closes the distinct election-assessment capability without rewriting it. Prepared 2026-09-27; proposed PARTIAL pending independent source review.

An election exists only if the applicable standard or regulator permits it. Record framework, entity, reporting period, eligible class/population, scope restriction, election deadline, transition, whether revocable, consistency requirement and required disclosure. Distinguish a policy choice (subject to change guidance), a one-time transition expedient, a transaction-specific practical expedient and an operational setting with no accounting-policy status. Identify the counterfactual treatment and quantify recognition, measurement, presentation, disclosure, tax/covenant and system effects. Require formal approver and retain the original election and affected population in a register; a user preference or ERP default cannot create a valid accounting election.

Example: an eligible lessee considers a short-term lease recognition exemption under IFRS 16/AASB 16 or an applicable US ASC 842 policy election. Check precise framework criteria, term/options, election level and leases in the population; compare schedule, expense and disclosures. Do not make an isolated contract choice contrary to the required class-level or other consistency basis. Source: [IFRS 16 official overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-16-leases/), [AASB 16](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-16-dec-2021), [FASB Codification portal](https://asc.fasb.org/); period and exact US text require case verification.

Desk tests: (1) a non-eligible lease is claimed exempt because immaterial: FAIL until materiality analysis and precise election criteria separated; (2) choice introduced after comparatives are issued: PASS only after policy-change/transition assessment; (3) eligible population and disclosure tracked with documented framework authority: PASS. Linked TOPIC-04-007 through 04-010 and TOPIC-15-001. Public US paragraph bodies unverified.
