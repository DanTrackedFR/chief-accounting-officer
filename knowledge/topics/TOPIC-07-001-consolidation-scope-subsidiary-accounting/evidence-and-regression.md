# TOPIC-07-001 — claim-linked boundary case

Claims: [TOPIC-07-001-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-07-001-US-001], [TOPIC-07-001-UK-001], [TOPIC-07-001-AASB-001]. Existing topic factory retained.

## Executed case

Investor has 45% votes and remaining holders are dispersed. Do not infer control from percentage alone: inspect meeting attendance, voting patterns, substantive currently exercisable rights and who directs budgets/products. If control starts 1 July, include subsidiary results from that date, not full-year revenue, and route acquisition-date accounting to IFRS 3. An investee with contractual decision maker/variable fee needs principal-agent and US VIE analysis independently. Preserve legal rights matrix, board minutes and date-stamped ownership register.

## Review controls and period gates

Record input/source data, alternative decision branches, calculation, journals, disclosure tie-out and reviewer. The IFRS claim uses the 2026 issued route. US ASC, UK FRS 102 and Australian AASB need separate operative-period and entity-type audits; do not infer identical treatment. The four claims are tested by the cases above.
