# Consolidation perimeter and subsidiary onboarding case

Capabilities CAO-07-001/002. Existing README and tests retained. Parent holds 48% of voting shares; remaining 52% is widely dispersed, but another investor has substantive contractual rights to direct the activities most affecting returns. Under IFRS 10/AASB 10, 48% alone neither proves nor disproves control: identify relevant activities, substantive rights, exposure to variable returns and ability to use power to affect those returns. Under US ASC 810, evaluate VIE model as well as voting-interest route; FRS 102 Section 9 has its own scope/exemptions. No consolidation entry is posted until rights and entity scope are resolved.

Once subsidiary inclusion is established, obtain acquisition/control date, reporting period, local trial balance, accounting-policy and currency conversions, intercompany pairs and NCI. A subsidiary recognized halfway through the year does not contribute full-year pre-control income to group results. A local statutory exemption from preparing consolidated accounts is not by itself a group-level conclusion under another reporting framework.

**Scenario test:** 48% ownership + substantive third-party direction right → reject automatic consolidation from shareholding; request enforceable agreement and decision-making evidence.
