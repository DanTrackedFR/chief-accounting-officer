# Control decision and acquisition-date cutover

**Decision matrix.** An investor owns 48% of ordinary votes, appoints three of five directors, while a lender can veto extraordinary financing and liquidation. Establish which activities most affect investee returns and who currently directs them. Board appointment may confer power if substantive and relevant; lender veto may be protective. Assess variable returns (dividends, synergies, fees, guarantees) and the ability to use power to affect them. Verify board election mechanics, other holders' coordination, options and contractual amendments. A 48% shareholding neither proves nor disproves IFRS 10 control. If delegated decision-maker acts as agent, test principal-agent factors. An investment entity exemption or parent exemption requires its own scope conditions. US ASC 810 starts with variable-interest-entity and voting-interest routes; the IFRS conclusion cannot be copied. FRS 102 Section 9/AASB 10 require period/entity routing.

**Cutover.** Assume control becomes substantive on 1 July X1 and subsidiary yearly external revenue is 240 earned evenly. Illustrative group inclusion for July–December is 120, subject to actual transaction cut-off; January–June is not group revenue just because the year-end TB contains 240. Obtain control-date TB, fair-value PPA, pre/post-acquisition performance split, intercompany transactions, policy alignment, FX and NCI. If board rights actually take effect 1 October, the illustrative period inclusion falls to 60 and the earlier 120 schedule fails. A legal completion certificate alone does not prove when power transferred; analyze conditions and enforceability. Reconcile opening acquired net assets and consolidated revenue to original legal-entity ledgers with separate pre/post periods.

**Release.** Evidence: ownership graph, rights contracts, board minutes, financing side agreements, relevant-activities memo, control-date chronology, legal and accounting approval, subsidiary TB, NCI/PPA, source-to-group data lineage and IFRS 12/other applicable disclosures. Tests: protective veto alone does not grant lender control; 1 July case includes 120; 1 October revised rights requires 60; unverified VIE conclusion blocks US release. IFRS 10/AASB 10 authority: [IFRS 10](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-10-consolidated-financial-statements/), [AASB 10](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-10-dec-2022); [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) Section 9. US paragraph-level ASC 810 needs authorized text.
