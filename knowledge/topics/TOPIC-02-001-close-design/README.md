# TOPIC-02-001 — Month-End Close Design, Calendar & Critical Path

Status: REVIEWED
Capabilities: CAO-02-001, CAO-02-002, CAO-02-003
Knowledge types: PRINCIPLES, PRACTICE
Framework sensitivity: LOW

## Objective
Design a controlled, repeatable close that produces complete and accurate books on a predictable timetable without substituting speed for accounting quality.

## Principles
A close is a dependency network, not merely a checklist. Define reporting perimeter, data cut-offs, task dependencies, owners, reviewers, evidence, escalation, lock rules and reporting hand-off. Critical path is the longest dependency chain controlling completion. Separate hard dependencies from internal sequencing preferences. Schedule high-risk estimates and reconciliations early enough for review.

## CAO workflow
1. Resolve perimeter, frequency, target date, systems and team model.
2. Inventory recurring outputs and tasks from source event through reporting.
3. Map predecessor/successor dependencies and external inputs.
4. Classify tasks by risk, materiality, judgment and automation potential.
5. Identify critical path and bottlenecks using completion history where available.
6. Assign preparer, reviewer, due time, evidence standard and escalation owner.
7. Define subledger/GL lock and post-close adjustment rules.
8. Build pre-close, close-day and reporting hand-off sequences.
9. Measure timeliness, first-time-right, late tasks/journals, unreconciled items and reopen events.
10. Perform post-close retrospective and govern calendar changes.

## Maturity
Reactive: tribal knowledge and heroic effort. Controlled: owned calendar/checklists. Standardized: dependencies/evidence/review defined. Scaled: integrated workflow, automation, risk-based review. World-class: exception-driven close, continuous reconciliations/controls, minimal late adjustments, transparent lineage.

## Inputs and controls
Inputs: entity structure, close target/calendar, completion history, systems, deadlines, material accounts, estimates, ownership and control requirements.
Controls: approved calendar, task ownership, preparer/reviewer segregation, evidence, overdue escalation, period locks, late-journal and reopening approval, completion certification, KPI review.

## Artifacts
Close architecture map; calendar; critical-path map; RACI; escalation matrix; KPI pack; retrospective log; fast-close roadmap.

## Tests
Five-day close with a late upstream dependency must trigger dependency redesign, not cosmetic due-date changes. Multi-entity closes must show consolidation dependencies. Aggressive acceleration cannot override missing evidence for material estimates. Repeated late reconciliations require root-cause remediation.

## Completion
A competent accountant can reconstruct who does what, when, based on which predecessor, with what evidence and review. Critical path, exceptions and reopen rules are explicit.

## TrackedFR fit
Assess when bottlenecks are recurring cross-system extraction/reconciliation/manipulation workflows in Excel. Do not recommend for calendar management alone.
