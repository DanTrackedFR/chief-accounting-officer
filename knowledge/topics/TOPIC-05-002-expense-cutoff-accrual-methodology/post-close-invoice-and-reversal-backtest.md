# Service-period accrual, invoice matching and backtest

**Population.** A vendor provides December services under contract at 10 per week for four weeks. Only two weeks were accrued because approval workflow did not receive the service-owner acceptance until January. If all four December weeks were performed and the contract supports the fee, year-end accrual should be 40; recorded 20 is short 20. A January invoice of 45 contains 40 December service and 5 January service; its issue date does not move the December expense. Correct December by 20, reverse the cumulative 40 as the invoice is booked in January and recognize January 5 appropriately, without duplicating 40. Verify VAT/tax, legal entitlement, rate changes and asset capitalization eligibility rather than mechanically expensing from vendor name.

**Estimate backtest.** For 100 recurring service accruals, compare prior estimate to subsequent invoices by service period, not invoice-posting month. Reconcile true-ups by reason: quantity, rate, missing source, disputed delivery and FX; detect directional bias. If a charge was for an onerous contract, bonus or share award, route to its specialized model. Late-posted invoice population after close is a completeness control; unmatched purchase orders and goods received are separately tested. Link accrual IDs to reversal journal and invoice; a broad reversal of entire AP accrual account can hide duplicates.

**QA/evidence.** Fail 20 accrued merely because approval late, fail 45 December expense, and fail January 45 expense plus unreversed December 40. Retain contract, service acceptance, dated delivery/timesheets, invoice, AP accrual subledger, journal and reviewer backtest. IFRS recognition follows transaction-specific standard and accrual accounting; FRS 102 effective edition/US ASC/AASB asset or liability rules determine specialized items. No artificial single “accrual standard” is asserted. Source [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/), [IFRS Conceptual Framework](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/conceptual-framework/) where no specific rule controls.
