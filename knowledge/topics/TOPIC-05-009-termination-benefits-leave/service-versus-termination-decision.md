# Severance and leave service-condition test

**Separating components.** On 1 December X1 entity communicates closure to 12 employees. Each will receive 15,000 upon involuntary termination on 31 January X2, whether or not remaining service is rendered; assume legal terms prevent withdrawal of the offer. Candidate termination obligation is 12×15,000=180,000 at the appropriate recognition date under IAS 19, subject to detailed facts, discount if material and interaction with IAS 37 restructuring timing. A second 5,000 per employee is paid only if each works through the January handover. That 60,000 is compensation for future service, not automatically immediate termination benefit; recognize over required service period under applicable employee-benefit rules. If workers leave December and forfeit the 5,000, an upfront 240,000 severance provision would be wrong. A draft board plan communicated only to directors may not satisfy trigger.

**Leave.** Retained leave illustration 20 employees×4 earned unused days×250×1.10=22,000 assumes accumulating entitlement and employer on-costs. For non-accumulating leave that lapses without unused benefit, classify differently. Reconcile HR leave census, forfeiture/use trend, country law, award agreement and pay-rate change; do not double count leave paid in January and payroll short-term accrual. US ASC 420/710 and FRS 102 Section 28, AASB 119 require separate framework-specific trigger and benefit nature.

**QA.** Test 180,000 plus conditional 60,000 future-service component and 22,000 leave candidate, but do not sum into one recognized December liability without the independent tests. Fail provision 240,000 at December solely from “closure” label and a leave balance without employee-days source. Retain signed/communicated plan, irrevocability evidence, employee acknowledgment, service conditions, HR roster, payroll and settlement schedule, reviewer memo.
