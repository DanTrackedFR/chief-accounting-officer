# TOPIC-05-009 — evidence and adverse regression

Claims: [TOPIC-05-009-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-05-009-US-001], [TOPIC-05-009-UK-001], [TOPIC-05-009-AASB-001]. Existing methods and duplicate materials remain intact.

## Workpaper and boundary

50 earned leave days at expected pay 200/day implies 10,000 before forfeiture and on-cost adjustment; Dr expense 10,000 / Cr leave liability 10,000 if fully vested and unpaid. A communicated severance offer of 100,000 with no withdrawal right is distinguished from 30,000 conditional on six months future service. Recognize the latter over its service period, not all as termination expense. Test employee acceptance windows, law, new-hire/leaver rollforwards and settlement term. US ASC 420/712/710 triggers can differ from IAS 19, as can FRS 102 §28 and AASB 119.

## Framework challenge and control

The IFRS proposition is tied to the identified issued edition. US public/private choices and operative ASC, UK FRS 102 edition, and AASB commencement/tier must each be checked for the same fact pattern. Reperform numerical entries and opposite branches. Retain source population, legal/contract terms, date-stamped valuation or bank/rate feeds, journal lineage, disclosure support and reviewer. The detailed non-IFRS claims remain in the audit queue; they do not prevent use of the substantive method with a disclosed source gate.
