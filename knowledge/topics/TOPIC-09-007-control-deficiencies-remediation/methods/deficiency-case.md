# Deficiency evaluation and remediation — case paper

Extends substantive README. Capabilities CAO-09-017–018. PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE with US issuer overlay; 2026-09-27.

Case file identifies failed objective, accounts/assertions/periods/population, root cause, how long control did not operate, maximum possible exposure, likely misstatement, actual errors, compensating controls at sufficient precision, aggregation with similar failures, communication and remediation owner/date. Severity is not “no error found, therefore no deficiency.” For US integrated audits, [PCAOB AS 2201.62–.69](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201) addresses the auditor's severity evaluation; checked 2026-09-27. Management's and auditor's obligations differ, and non-US classifications require their own regime.

Illustration: monthly cash reconciliation was not reviewed in two months; exposed GL activity is 4m and materiality for the entity is 500,000. A 20,000 discovered error is not the maximum plausible magnitude. Determine whether missing review could reasonably fail to detect a larger material error, whether bank-feed controls independently cover the full period, whether similar deficiencies combine and whether qualitative fraud factors alter judgment. Do **not** mechanically classify on 20,000/500,000. Document an interim complete review of both months, correction, reviewer redesign, training/access and sustained re-performance testing; a future SOP alone does not remediate operating ineffectiveness.

Negative test: ticket marked closed upon hiring replacement, without proof controls ran. Expected FAIL; retest new operation over the relevant period and assess historical exposure. Evidence: original failed log, populations, materiality memo, compensating-control proof, remediation change approval, test samples and governance communication. Dependencies 09-001/008, 10-005, 11-006. Result PASS for required severity and remediation route, not a final legal classification for an unidentified entity.
