# Deficiency severity and closure case

**Applicability.** Begin with entity status and statutory reporting regime. For applicable US ICFR, use SEC management guidance and PCAOB AS 2201's reasonable-possibility/potential-magnitude analysis and its aggregation and compensating-control considerations. A non-US private entity can use the diagnostic method, but must route statutory labels and communication under its own governance and assurance obligations.

**Exposure model.** Record failed control, assertion, account/disclosure, affected entity/period, full transaction population, maximum plausible misstatement (including qualitative sensitivity), design versus operation, bypass access and actual errors. Do not infer zero risk from a clean sample. For a monthly review absent in Q3, inspect all Q3 entries and adjacent periods, not only the missed month's final balance. Assess correlated failures and aggregate by shared risk/root cause.

**Compensation test.** A proposed downstream review qualifies only if it independently covers the same assertion and period, has the necessary underlying population, a sufficiently low investigation threshold, competent owner and contemporaneous evidence of investigated outliers. CFO P&L review at a high threshold does not compensate for an unreviewed balance-sheet reconciliation. Retain the review's source data and investigation trail.

**Remediation workpaper.** Record root cause, interim mitigation, owner, design change, implementation timestamp, IT/configuration change approval, training, effective operating dates, population of runs, retest procedure and independent closure signature. For a new automated approval rule, test both allowed and blocked transactions, privileged bypass and change log, then obtain sufficient operation during the relevant assessment period. Deployment is not operating effectiveness.

**Example.** A reconciliation over a $20m suspense account was unsigned for three months. Two subsequent $30k items were cleared, but the account could conceal larger offsetting errors. Bound the exposed entries, investigate aged items, evaluate potential rather than recorded error, test any claimed compensating ledger review and aggregate with other close failures. Escalate classification under the governing regime; do not choose a universal dollar cutoff.

**Evidence and handoff.** Deficiency case file, population extract with lineage, quantitative/qualitative severity memo, aggregation matrix, compensating-control evidence, audit/governance communication log and retest results go to control testing (09-008) and audit communication (10). If final statements may be wrong, route to correction and disclosure topics in Domain 02. Preserve superseded conclusions and dates.

References: [PCAOB AS 2201](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201); [SEC Release 33-8810](https://www.sec.gov/rule-release/33-8810). These US sources do not establish universal classifications.
