# TOPIC-05-006 — Bonus Accruals; Sales Incentive & Commission Compensation

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Built: 2026-09-23
Capabilities: CAO-05-011, CAO-05-012

## Boundary
This topic determines employee-compensation obligation and accrual. Incremental costs of obtaining customer contracts can also fall within revenue contract-cost guidance (IFRS 15 / ASC 340-40 / FRS 102 revenue requirements / AASB 15); share/equity awards route to share-based payment; nonemployee customer rebates route to revenue/consideration topics.

## PRINCIPLES
Determine what service/performance creates entitlement, whether obligation is legal or constructive, whether payment remains avoidable, measurement basis, forfeiture/clawback conditions, service period, settlement timing, capitalization eligibility and entity/cost-center allocation. A board approval date alone does not necessarily determine expense period; economic substance and applicable guidance do.

## IFRS/AASB
IAS 19/AASB 119 short-term employee benefit guidance includes profit-sharing and bonuses. Recognize expected cost when a present legal or constructive obligation exists from past employee service and reliable estimate can be made. Short-term benefits are generally undiscounted. If settlement timing makes the benefit other long-term, apply the relevant long-term model. For sales commissions, separately assess IFRS 15/AASB 15 incremental contract acquisition cost requirements where the payment is incremental to obtaining a contract.

## US GAAP
Classify cash compensation under applicable Topic 710/related guidance and distinguish from Topic 718 awards. Sales commissions can be within ASC 340-40 when incremental costs of obtaining a contract. Probability/measurement and deferred-compensation mechanics must follow applicable US literature; do not substitute IAS 19. FASB's 2024 profits-interest clarification demonstrates the importance of Topic 710 vs Topic 718 scoping.

## UK GAAP
FRS 102 Section 28 covers cash employee benefits; Section 26 covers share-based payment. Revised revenue requirements effective with Periodic Review 2024 may affect contract-cost analysis for periods beginning 2026 onward, so period routing is mandatory for commission capitalization conclusions.

## CAO workflow
1 identify plan and participants; 2 determine cash/equity/mixed and employee/nonemployee; 3 identify service/performance period and entitlement terms; 4 determine legal/constructive obligation and discretion; 5 estimate achievement and forfeiture/clawback effects under framework; 6 classify short/long-term; 7 assess contract-cost capitalization separately; 8 calculate accrual/asset; 9 allocate entity/function; 10 post and reconcile; 11 true-up; 12 disclose material judgments where required.

## Calculation model
Participant/plan expected payout = eligible base × target rate × approved performance factor × framework-appropriate probability/forfeiture treatment, subject to caps/floors and plan terms. Accrued amount reflects service earned through reporting date, less paid/previously accrued amounts. This is a method template, not a mandatory formula.

## Controls
Approved plan terms and versioning; participant/population completeness; HR/CRM/performance-source reconciliation; assumption approval; model access/change control; payout-to-accrual true-up; contract-cost asset reconciliation; clawback monitoring; management override review.

## Scenarios
Discretionary annual bonus repeatedly paid with communicated formula: assess constructive obligation rather than assuming 'discretionary' means zero accrual. Sales rep commission payable only on signed contract: assess employee accrual plus separate incremental contract-cost capitalization. Award settles half cash/half shares: split routing to cash employee benefits and share-based payment. Bonus depends on employee remaining through payment date: analyze service condition and attribution, not just year-end KPI attainment.

## QA
PASS. Cross-topic routing prevents duplicate expense or inappropriate capitalization.
