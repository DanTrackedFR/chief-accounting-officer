# Contract-acquisition commission and bonus obligation

**Commission.** An employee earns a non-refundable, incremental 12 commission solely when a three-year customer contract is signed. Assume IFRS 15 criteria for capitalization are met, recoverability supported and expected benefit period three years, straight-line. Initial contract-cost asset 12; amortization 4 each full year (12/3). If the contract is terminated after year one with no further recoverable benefit, evaluate impairment/derecognition of remaining 8; do not retain asset merely because it was paid. Salary 20 paid regardless of obtaining the contract is not incremental to winning it and fails that specific cost gate. Assess whether a practical expedient applies for short amortization periods, if relevant and elected; do not apply automatically. UK revised FRS 102 Section 23 from 2026 and US ASC 340-40 have their own detail/expedients; AASB 15 needs current compilation.

**Bonus.** For retained case, plan target 600×10%=60, supported 80% payout 48 and already accrued 30, requiring an 18 catch-up **only if** a present service-related legal or constructive obligation exists at reporting date under the applicable rules. If a board retains genuine discretion to set payout to zero and has not communicated a binding practice, analyze that fact instead of using probability-weighted 48 as a substitute for recognition. Track period service, forfeiture, performance metrics, board approval and settlement. IFRS IAS 19, US employee-compensation topics, FRS 102 Section 28 and AASB 119 differ where applicable; tax and payroll withholdings are separate from gross employer cost.

**QA.** Test 12/3=4, remaining 8, bonus 48−30=18; fail capitalizing guaranteed base salary 20 or accruing 48 solely from budget without obligation evidence. Retain commission agreement, CRM signed contract, expected benefit, recoverability/backtest, payroll ledger, bonus plan/communication, approval and reviewer conclusion.
