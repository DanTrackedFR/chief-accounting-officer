# Carve-out perimeter and fresh-start decision case

The canonical topic path is `TOPIC-13-010-carve-out-reorganization`; the sibling `TOPIC-13-010-carve-out-fresh-start-reorganization` holds useful source, framework and test material for the **same** topic ID. Preserve both for QA canonicalization; this file cross-references the sibling rather than duplicating or deleting it.

**Perimeter.** Parent prepares historical standalone statements for Division A ahead of disposal. Parent trial balance has direct Division A revenue 500, direct costs 310 and centralized shared costs 100. A documented cost-driver analysis using occupied floor area attributes 30% of shared facilities cost to A, or 30. A separate finance/HR pool 60 is allocated using verified employee counts: A 40 of 200 employees, or 12. Division A illustrative pre-tax result is 500−310−30−12=148 before additional stand-alone costs, interest, tax, intercompany eliminations or capital transactions. Do not book 42 of allocated cost merely from management budget percentages; tie each source pool 100/60 to parent GL, approved driver, source-period numerator/denominator and recalculated 30/12. Document how the service would operate independently, assess whether allocation is reasonable for each historical period, and disclose basis and limitations where required. Cash flows and funding may not follow income allocation; maintain a separate parent-cash sweep and working-capital bridge.

**Control totals.** Build a ledger mapping for directly assigned assets and liabilities and a separate shared-asset/lease/employee/guarantee decision table. Reconcile 500/310/100/60 at source, verify that 30+70=100 and 12+48=60 across Division A and residual group, then bridge elimination and carve-out entries to consolidated financial statements. Include audit trail of allocation version, owner, approvals and override. A December disposal closing statement does not authorize a change in January–November historical attribution without evidence.

**Fresh-start fork.** A legal reorganization or bankruptcy filing does not automatically permit or require a new accounting basis. For a US entity, obtain confirmed plan/effective-date evidence and assess current ASC 852 qualification criteria and predecessor/successor presentation under authorized Codification access and relevant SEC filing rules. If criteria are not met, retain applicable continuation accounting and separately analyze modifications, impairment and debt settlements. IFRS/AASB have no blanket ASC 852-style reset; UK FRS 102 group reconstruction also is not a universal fresh-start model. Never revalue the above carve-out assets merely because the business is marketed or changes legal form.

IFRS 18 presentation generally from annual periods beginning 1 January 2027 unless early applied; FRS 102 Periodic Review 2024 chiefly from 1 January 2026. Reviewer tests perimeter completeness, allocation driver, source-to-carve-out TB, comparative consistency, related-party note, reorganization chronology and evidence index. Negative tests: allocation 42 without source drivers and fresh-start booked solely on a restructuring announcement fail.
