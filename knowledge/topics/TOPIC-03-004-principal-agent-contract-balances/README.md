## Objective

## Principal-agent decision logic
1. Identify each specified good or service promised to the customer before evaluating indicators. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R003]
2. Identify the party or parties that provide that specified item. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R005]
3. Ask whether the reporting entity obtains control of that specified item before transfer to the customer. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R003]
4. Evaluate evidence relevant to control, including primary responsibility, inventory risk and pricing discretion. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R003] Indicators support the control assessment; none is automatically determinative. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R003]
5. Consider whether the entity integrates third-party inputs into a combined output, obtains a right to a service that it directs another party to perform, or obtains control of a good before transfer. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R003]
6. Conclude principal or agent separately for different specified goods/services when necessary. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R005] [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R004] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R004] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R004] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R004]
7. Principal generally reports the consideration to which it expects to be entitled for the controlled good/service; agent reports the fee/commission for arranging provision. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R003] [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R004] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R004] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R004] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R004] Route variable consideration and consideration payable to customer to their own revenue topics.
8. Document gross-versus-net P&L, tax/commission/KPI/data consequences without allowing KPI preference to drive the accounting conclusion. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R004] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R004] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R004] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R004]

## Contract-balance decision logic
After performance/payment activity, determine whether the entity has: (a) an unconditional right to consideration — receivable; (b) a right conditioned on something other than passage of time — contract asset; or (c) an obligation to transfer goods/services for consideration already received or due — contract liability. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R006] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R006] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R006] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R006] Evaluate at contract level as required by the applicable framework and preserve linkage to performance obligations and billing.
