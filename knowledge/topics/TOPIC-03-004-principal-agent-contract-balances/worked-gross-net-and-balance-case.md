# Gross/net decision and contract-balance worked case

Capabilities CAO-03-008/009. Preserve the existing substantive README. Classification requires control of each *specified* good/service before transfer; billing or commercial KPI labels are not determinative.

A marketplace charges the customer 100 and owes a provider 85. If evidence establishes the marketplace controls the promised service before transfer, an illustrative presentation is revenue 100 and supplier cost 85 (gross margin 15), subject to underlying supplier-cost recognition. If it only arranges the provider's service, revenue is its 15 fee; receipt of 100 creates a settlement obligation of 85, not 100 revenue. Both yield 15 before other costs, but gross revenue and liabilities differ. Test primary responsibility, substitution/directing right, inventory/fulfilment risk and pricing discretion in context. A service platform may be principal for one distinct promise and agent for another. The [IFRS Interpretations Committee May 2022 reseller decision](https://www.ifrs.org/projects/completed-projects/2022/principal-versus-agent-software-reseller-ifrs-15/) is a control-analysis reference, not an automatic result for marketplaces.

Separate fact pattern: 300 of service has transferred under a 500 contract; 250 has been billed. If the 50 excess performance right is still conditional on substantive future acceptance, analyze contract asset 50; if only passage of time remains, classify as receivable. If 350 was billed instead, analyze a 50 contract liability for undelivered performance, subject to contract-level presentation and billing specifics. **Executed:** 300−250=50 asset-side candidate; 350−300=50 liability-side candidate; 100−85=15 agent fee. PASS arithmetic; accounting classification conditional on enforceable rights, specific promises and control.

Evidence: customer/provider contracts, service and acceptance logs, supplier selection/substitution rights, price approval, cash settlement, billing and obligation schedules. Reconcile gross cash to recognized fee and provider payable or gross revenue/cost, with reviewer sign-off. Framework pointers: [IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/), ASC 606, revised FRS 102 Section 23 and AASB 15. UK principal effective 1 January 2026 confirmed by [FRC](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). Current ASC paragraphs and exact UK transitional detail remain PARTIAL; do not imply universal gross/net equivalence.
