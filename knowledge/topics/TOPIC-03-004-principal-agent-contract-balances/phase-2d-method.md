## Balance recognition, measurement and notes

Present a right conditional on future performance as a contract asset and an unconditional right separately as AR, even if invoicing is delayed by administration. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R006] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R006] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R006] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R006]
An enforceable billed or received amount before transfer creates a contract liability; a refundable deposit or agent settlement may instead have a separately identified obligation. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R007] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R007] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R007] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R007]
Net performance/payment position within the applicable accounting contract, retain receivables separately and do not net different contracts solely because the customer is common. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R008] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R008] [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R008] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R008]

## Framework/period routes

Legacy UK Section 23 excludes amounts collected as agent from revenue and reports commission, but applies the old risks/rewards/service and accrued/deferred-income model rather than importing the revised contract-balance terminology as a recognition rule. [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R011]
Revised UK Section 23 applies for January-2026 starts or permitted early adoption, with full retrospective or uncompleted-contract cumulative-equity transition and disclosed expedients. [TOPIC-03-004-UK-R012]
IFRS/AASB and US retain their historical full/modified adoption routes and US public/private dates; current recognition amendments do not rewrite old comparative balances without an applicable transition provision. [TOPIC-03-004-IFRS-R013] [TOPIC-03-004-US-R013] [TOPIC-03-004-AASB-R013]

## Mixed-output executed case

In a contract collected for 140, assume the platform controls its own implementation service priced 40 but merely arranges an independent provider's 100 service, keeping a 15 arrangement fee. On complete transfer: Dr cash 140 / Cr implementation revenue 40 / Cr commission revenue 15 / Cr provider payable 85; remit Dr payable 85 / Cr cash 85. Net revenue 55 and retained cash 55. Calling the whole platform a principal would overstate revenue by 85. If the provider service is instead an input integrated into a platform-controlled combined output, the separate principal branch has gross revenue 140 and supplier cost 85; both branches have margin 55 but different presentation.

Workpaper PA-004 contains customer and provider promises, integration responsibility, substitution/pricing/risk rights, settlement bridge, legal unconditional-right dates and contract-level rollforward. Independent reviewer challenges each specified item and confirms the opposite branch would change revenue, not merely the memo label. Control matches provider settlements to cash and reviews gross-to-net analytics on channel changes. Data requires item-level principal/agent flags, legal party roles, gross collection, own-service fee, commission and settlement IDs, balance-condition codes and separately traceable receivables. The scenario file executes both presentation outcomes and rejects pricing-discretion-only conclusions.
