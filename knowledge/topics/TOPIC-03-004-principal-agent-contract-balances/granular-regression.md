## Marketplace arrangement: net, not gross

Customer pays 100; platform must remit 85 to the independent provider. Assume evidence shows provider, not platform, controls and fulfills the specified service, while platform only arranges it. Agent branch: Dr cash 100 / Cr provider payable 85 / Cr commission revenue 15; on remittance Dr provider payable 85 / Cr cash 85. Cash retained 15; revenue 15; no 85 cost of sales. A gross entry of 100 revenue and 85 cost is an adverse fail under these control facts (001–003). Pricing discretion alone does not reverse this conclusion; if platform instead controls a combined specified service before transfer, test principal and document gross consideration and supplier cost under the relevant guidance.

Separate contract: an earned fee of 50 for delivered service A cannot be billed until **distinct** service B is completed; A has transferred but the right remains conditioned on B. Dr contract asset 50 / Cr A revenue 50; when B condition is fulfilled Dr receivable 50 / Cr contract asset 50; collection Dr cash 50 / Cr receivable 50. If only invoice administration or passage of time remains after A, initial Dr receivable 50 is the correct adverse branch. If the purported customer acceptance is substantive to whether A transferred, do not record A revenue or a contract asset before acceptance (004–006). These paths must not be collapsed into an undifferentiated “unbilled” bucket.
