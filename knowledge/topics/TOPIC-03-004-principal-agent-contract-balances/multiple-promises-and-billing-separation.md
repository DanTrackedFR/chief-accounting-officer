# Principal for one promise, agent for another

**Bundle.** Platform contracts with customer for a platform subscription 30 and third-party delivery service 100, owes courier 85. Platform controls its hosted subscription and provides it directly, but for delivery it merely arranges an independent courier, subject to verified rights, pricing and responsibility. Illustrative revenue is subscription 30 plus agency fee 15 =45; courier remittance payable 85 when platform collects delivery gross 100. Cash collected 130 does not make revenue 130. If evidence instead shows platform controls specified delivery before transfer, delivery gross revenue 100 and supplier expense 85 may be appropriate, with total revenue 130. Analyze each specified good/service; never label whole company “agent.” Test customer/courier contracts, substitution rights, who remedies failures, inventory/fulfillment risk and pricing control in context, not by isolated indicator count.

**Balance fork.** Customer prepays 130 before either service transfers. At receipt, analyze liabilities for undelivered subscription and amounts collected on behalf of courier; do not automatically post one 130 contract liability under the agency scenario. At subscription delivery, release the earned 30; at completed courier service, recognize agent fee 15 and settle 85, subject to exact contractual cash/settlement rights. If customer can cancel and get refund, reassess refund liability and transaction price. A receivable arises for unconditional entitlement; a conditional right from performance remains contract asset. The gross/net revenue decision does not eliminate financial asset impairment on actual receivables.

**QA.** Reperform 30+100=130 gross cash, agency 30+15=45 revenue and 85 settlement; fail platform-wide single classification and netting supplier payable against unrelated receivable. Preserve event ledger, contracts, remittance, provider invoices, gross-to-net bridge and disclosure disaggregation.
