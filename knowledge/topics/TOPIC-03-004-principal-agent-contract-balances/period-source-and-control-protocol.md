# Specified promise, control and balance classification protocol

Before comparing principal/agent indicators, enumerate every distinct specified good, service or right transferred to the customer. The [2025 issued IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2025/issued/ifrs15.html) anchors are paragraphs B34–B38 for principal/agent, 22–30 for distinct promises, and 105–109 for contract balances/receivables; indicators inform but do not replace control before transfer. [AASB 15](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022) corresponds for annual starts in its stated 2023-to-before-July-2026 window, with subsequent compilation selection required thereafter. The FRC [FRS 102 register](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) governs edition choice; revised Section 23 principally applies from January 2026. Verify exact ASC 606 Basic View paragraphs separately rather than asserting inferred identifiers.

Take a marketplace sale priced at 120 to the end customer with 90 remitted to a supplier. If the marketplace controls the specified product before transfer, the illustrative gross sale is 120 with 90 related cost; if it only arranges supplier delivery, illustrative net revenue is 30. Neither settlement flow nor who issues an invoice resolves the conclusion. Inspect title/risk, inventory exposure, primary fulfillment responsibility, price discretion, supplier substitution, refunds, and whether the specified promise is the product or an arrangement service. Record evidence *per promise*: a bundle can contain a principal product and an agent service. A separately billed 30 commission due only after customer acceptance may be a conditional contract asset until the right becomes unconditional; a billed/unconditional amount is a receivable, and a customer prepayment before performance is a contract liability. Do not net unrelated contract assets/liabilities across contracts merely to match a gateway settlement report.

The control owner ties gross customer transactions, supplier liabilities, refunds, taxes and processor fees to a unique order and obligation ID; the reviewer re-performs both gross/net paths and confirms consistency with the balance rollforward. Adverse case: supplier ships directly but marketplace controls inventory before transfer; direct shipment alone must not force net revenue. Route reversals to TOPIC-03-007 and credit losses to 03-008. This remains PARTIAL pending the current US and later-period AASB authority check.
