# TOPIC-08-001 — reporting evidence and adverse route

Claims: [TOPIC-08-001-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-08-001-US-001], [TOPIC-08-001-UK-001], [TOPIC-08-001-AASB-001]. Retain existing reporting pack.

## Worked case

Debt of 500 has a covenant tested at 31 December. If breached at reporting date with no qualifying waiver obtained by then, test current presentation even if a January waiver is obtained before authorization; separate subsequent event and going-concern consequences. If the covenant is tested only after reporting date, evaluate the actual right at reporting date and required covenant disclosures instead of assuming immediate current classification. IFRS 18 begins annual periods from 1 January 2027 unless early adopted; build comparative mapping rather than applying it silently to 2026.

## Cross-framework and control gate

Select reporting period, entity filing tier and applicable edition before conclusion. US public/private disclosures and SEC route, UK small entity/FRS 102 period, and AASB Tier 1/Tier 2 and NFP must be tested independently. Reconcile source GL, consolidation, note tables, comparatives and approval version with named reviewer. An inaccessible IAS 1 operative paragraph is model-derived, not source-verified. Every unverified claim remains in the audit queue.
