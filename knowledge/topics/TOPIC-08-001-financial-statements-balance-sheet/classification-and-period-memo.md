# Balance-sheet classification, offsetting and period decision

**2026 IFRS reporter.** Ledger presents debt 200 as noncurrent. A covenant tested at 31 December X1 requires coverage ≥1.2; actual ratio is 1.1. A waiver is obtained on 15 January X2. Analyze contractual rights existing at reporting date under the effective IAS 1 liability-classification amendments, including whether the entity had a substantive right to defer settlement at 31 December, covenant test date and effect of a post-period waiver. Do not leave 200 noncurrent merely because refinancing is expected or a January waiver was signed; preserve signed debt terms, covenant calculation, lender response and date chronology. A covenant to be complied with only after reporting date can produce a different classification with note disclosures even where management expects difficulty; identify it separately. IFRS 18 replaces IAS 1 presentation requirements for annual periods from 1 January 2027 unless early adopted, so a 2026 presentation checklist must not silently apply IFRS 18.

**Illustrative reconciliation.** Approved TB has assets 1,000, liabilities 600 and equity 400. A valid 200 current/noncurrent debt reclassification changes neither assets, total liabilities nor equity: new total remains 1,000=600+400. Tie current portion to debt schedule, maturities and covenant assessment; ensure the 200 appears exactly once and related maturity and liquidity-risk notes agree. A supplier-finance program recorded as AP might require separate characterization and disclosures depending on substance; do not rename liabilities from presentation preference. A 30 customer receivable and 30 vendor payable with the same group counterparty cannot be offset on the balance sheet without the applicable enforceable-right and settlement conditions. Assess IAS 32 or applicable ASC/FRS 102/AASB rules and legal rights rather than netting equal numbers.

**Framework differentiation.** US ASC 210/470 and SEC requirements have their own refinancing/waiver rules; FRS 102 Sections 4/11 and UK company-law format can differ; AASB 101/132 require Australian operative versions and tier overlays. Management must maintain separate framework workpapers where group IFRS and local statutory accounts differ. Classification and disclosure changes may not be mirror-image recognition changes. Source anchors: [IAS 1](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-1-presentation-of-financial-statements/), [IFRS 18](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). Verify precise covenant paragraphs/transition in operative text and current US Codification under authorized access.

**QA.** Fail waiver dated after period treated as a period-end right without analysis, 200 counted in both classifications, unconditional netting of 30/30, or IFRS 18 mandatory in 2026. Pass only with source-to-line mapping, dual framework bridge where applicable, note tie, legal evidence and reviewer sign-off.
