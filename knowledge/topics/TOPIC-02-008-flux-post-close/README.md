# TOPIC-02-008 — Close Flux Review & Post-Close Adjustment Governance

Status: REVIEWED
Capabilities: CAO-02-017, CAO-02-018
Knowledge: PRINCIPLES + PRACTICE
Framework sensitivity: LOW

## Objective
Use analytical review to detect incomplete, inaccurate or unusual accounting before certification, and govern any adjustment after the normal close boundary.

## Flux method
Define comparison appropriate to account economics: prior month, prior year, forecast only as a reasonableness input, run-rate, unit volume, headcount or balance rollforward. Set thresholds using both amount and percentage plus qualitative risk. Decompose change into business driver, accounting event, FX, timing, reclassification, estimate change and unexplained residual. Explanations require evidence; “timing” is not a root cause.

## CAO workflow
1. Lock comparison population and version.
2. Rank accounts by materiality, volatility and risk.
3. Generate exceptions using absolute, relative and account-specific thresholds.
4. Trace material movement to transactions/subledgers and accounting treatment.
5. Separate expected business movement from accounting anomaly.
6. Resolve unexplained residual or document immaterial conclusion.
7. Determine whether adjustment is required before certification.
8. Preserve analysis and evidence.

## Post-close governance
Define close boundary and permitted reopen cases. Every post-close JE records reason, discovery date, financial-statement effect, root cause, approver and whether it indicates control/process failure. Reopen only when required by reporting obligations/materiality and governance; otherwise route through controlled late-adjustment policy. Never use a later period to conceal a known material error in an issued/prior period.

## Controls
Automated flux population; reviewer thresholds; evidence links; unexplained-item aging; late-JE approval; period-lock access; reopen authorization; recurring-adjustment root-cause review; certification after final adjustment set.

## Artifacts
Flux pack; exception register; late-JE log; reopen memo; root-cause analysis; remediation backlog.

## Tests
Large revenue increase with matching volume evidence can clear; flat payroll despite headcount increase triggers investigation. A late $20k entry may be qualitatively significant despite amount. A material prior-period error routes to TOPIC-02-009 rather than being quietly booked current period.

## TrackedFR fit
Strong where flux review repeatedly combines GL, warehouse, billing, payroll or operational datasets in Excel.
