# Flux review and post-close adjustment — decision paper

Extends existing README/factory; alternate `close-variance-post-close` README is retained. Capabilities CAO-02-017–018. PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE; 2026-09-27.

## Reproducible flux

Freeze the compared GL/reporting snapshots, currency basis, account/entity/dimension mapping and time periods. Quantify variance, then bridge volume, price/rate, FX, mix, timing, new contracts and corrections using source-supported figures. A commentary sentence such as “timing” is not a bridge. Rank by absolute and relative magnitude plus unusual trend and fraud/judgment sensitivity; materiality and thresholds depend on the entity. Challenge offsets within a consolidated total and compare to cash/subledger/nonfinancial activity.

Example: revenue rises from 1,000,000 to 1,350,000 (+350,000). Validated new customer invoices contribute +200,000; contract price change +100,000; FX +20,000. Residual +30,000 is an open exception, not automatically ordinary fluctuation. If 15,000 of the new customer invoices are January service mistakenly dated December, the supported December increase becomes +185,000 and unexplained/correction amounts change. Preserve each source/contract and the calculation; route revenue recognition to Domain 03.

## After lock

Record discovery time, entity/period, amount, evidence, whether statements are drafted/issued, materiality (including qualitative), affected accounts/tax/disclosures, proposed entry and alternatives. An authorized reviewer decides reopen, current-period correction if permitted, prior-period error route or no entry with documented reason. Closed-period permission must be restricted; preserve original and revised TB/reporting snapshots, journal IDs and why the action was taken. A lower-level manager cannot decide to defer a material misstatement for schedule convenience.

**Negative test:** 30,000 unexplained residual is called “timing” and close is locked. Expected FAIL until underlying transactions bridge. If the issue is a material prior-period error, route to TOPIC-02-009, not a routine post-close adjustment. Result PASS for escalation. Standards-sensitive correction follows IAS 8/AASB 108, FRS 102 Section 10 or ASC 250 as applicable; this operational topic does not invent common mechanics. Official [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/) and [FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) pages checked 2026-09-27.
