# Debt fee eligibility and exact effective-interest backtest

**Exact schedule.** Retained case: face 1,000, eligible fees 30, net initial liability 970, annual cash coupon 50 for two years, redemption 1,000. Solve 970=50/(1+r)+1,050/(1+r)^2. Exact annual effective rate approximately 6.6512589%; year-one interest 64.5172, payment 50, liability 984.5172. Year-two effective interest about 65.4828, coupon 50, pre-redemption carrying amount 1,000 (rounding aside). Thus total interest expense over two years is 130 while coupons total 100 and fees accrete 30. A model using rounded 6.65% might leave a rounding residual; keep full precision and post documented final true-up, not an unexplained new financing cost.

**Cost population.** Lender fee 20, directly attributable external legal cost 10 and general treasury salaries 4: assume the first 30 qualify on the fact-specific IFRS 9 initial measurement analysis, while general salaries 4 do not automatically qualify. Reconcile lender funding 1,000 minus 20 lender fee and 10 external fee to cash net 970, adjusting funds-flow if legal invoice was settled separately. Undrawn revolver facility fees, debt at FVTPL, debt modifications and commitment arrangements require independent classification and expense/capitalization logic. Under US GAAP presentation of recognized debt issuance costs is generally a direct deduction from debt following ASU 2015-03, but revolver arrangements and current ASC body need separate review; FRS 102 Sections 11/12 and AASB 9 own period routes. No assumption that all costs named “deal fees” are EIR inputs.

**QA/evidence.** Reperform PV equation and 970+64.5172−50=984.5172; test actual coupon dates, day count, variable interest and lender cash settlement. Fail capitalizing 4 general salary, an EIR schedule that does not reconcile redemption, or mistaking debt cash proceeds for face amount. Retain facility and fee contracts, approvals, cash/bank, GL debt register, EIR version and note/covenant bridge.
