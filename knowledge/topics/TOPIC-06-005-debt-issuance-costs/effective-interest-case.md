# Debt issuance costs and effective-interest control

Capabilities CAO-06-010/011. Existing debt factory retained. Assume a two-year bullet loan with principal 1,000, cash proceeds 970 after eligible directly attributable fees of 30, annual coupon 5% of face (50), and repayment of 1,000 at maturity. Initial amortized-cost carrying amount is 970 if fees qualify under the governing model. Solve effective annual interest rate r from 970=50/(1+r)+1,050/(1+r)^2; r≈6.65%. Using r=6.65% rounded, year-one interest expense ≈64.51, cash coupon 50 and debt accretion ≈14.51; closing carrying amount ≈984.51. Year-two rate must be solved with unrounded precision to reach 1,000 before redemption; do not leave a spurious residual from rounded r.
