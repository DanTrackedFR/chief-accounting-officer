# AR/revenue independent source completeness and precision

Retained example has AR GL and subledger both 740 while independent delivery events show one missing billable 40 and one duplicate 40; net-zero error is invisible to ledger tie. Join contract, performance event, invoice and cash by stable IDs. For missing event, decide control transfer and entitlement under TOPIC-03-003/005 before posting revenue/contract asset/AR. For duplicate invoice, reverse the invoice and associated tax/AR/revenue or billing-only entry as facts dictate; customer statement and credit note must follow. The two 40 items need not have equal revenue effects and cannot be netted to clear control. Test all delivery events against invoice/subledger plus all invoice lines against delivery/contract, including canceled and retried jobs.

**Precision.** A reviewer threshold of 50 aggregate net difference would pass this zero exception despite 80 gross misstatement candidates. Use population-level completeness/accuracy tests, gross unmatched amounts, event aging, high-risk manual journals and cutoff windows. Reconcile opening AR 500+invoice 900−cash 600−credits 50−write-offs 20+FX 10=740 and independently test each component to external/system evidence. Identify responsible entity and customer; don't offset items from two customers. Once corrected, rerun revenue disaggregation, contract balance, ECL and financial-statement notes. For multi-system recurring data, automate one-to-one/one-to-many matching with explicit exception queue and immutable run/version IDs.

**QA.** Fail “740=740 therefore pass,” suppression of duplicate/missing pair, or auto-booked 40 without performance evidence. Pass when every exception has owner, decision, journal, source trace, reviewer and rerun proof. This is practice-led; IFRS 15/ASC 606/FRS 102 Section 23/AASB 15 govern underlying revenue, IFRS 9/ASC 326/etc govern impairment. No fabricated framework-specific reconciliation requirement. Archive event extract, invoice, contract, GL, bank, exception log, mappings and approval.
