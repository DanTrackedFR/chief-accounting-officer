# TOPIC-13-011 — Hyperinflationary Accounting Assessment

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Primary capability: CAO-13-021
Sensitivity: H
Source check: 2026-09-24

## Objective
Enable the CAO to identify a potentially hyperinflationary functional-currency environment, route the entity to the correct framework, perform/restage the accounting workplan and connect restatement to FX translation and consolidation.

## IFRS
IAS 29 applies when an entity's functional currency is that of a hyperinflationary economy. Hyperinflation is assessed using multiple characteristics; cumulative inflation around 100% or more over three years is an indicator, not an automatic standalone bright line. Financial statements and comparatives are expressed in the measuring unit current at reporting date using a general price index; the net monetary gain/loss is recognized in profit or loss and disclosed. IFRIC 7 addresses first-time application of the restatement approach.

The July 2025 IFRIC agenda decision reinforces that the assessment considers all relevant indicators, may include indicators beyond IAS 29.3, and uses judgment; entities preparing on the same basis should not reach inconsistent group-level conclusions without support.

IAS 21 amendments on translation into a hyperinflationary presentation currency are effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, with earlier application permitted. Effective-period routing is mandatory.

## AASB
AASB 129 is the Australian counterpart and must be checked independently. Australian reduced-disclosure/Tier 2 routing can affect disclosures; AASB 1060 includes specific hyperinflation disclosures. Australian interpretation material also addresses first identification/application.

## UK GAAP
Do not assume IAS 29 applies to an FRS 102 preparer. Route to the current FRS 102 foreign-currency/hyperinflation requirements and the reporting period; Periodic Review 2024 amendments are generally effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026. Current FRC edition/effective-date gate is required.

## US GAAP
Route independently to current ASC 830 foreign-currency literature and applicable SEC guidance. Do not translate the IFRS IAS 29 restatement model into US GAAP. Current public Codification access does not support APPROVED paragraph-level mapping for all subissues, so detailed US paragraph claims remain PARTIAL pending licensed/authoritative access.

## CAO workflow
1. Resolve entity, functional/presentation currencies, framework and period.
2. Gather inflation series, index evidence, currency behavior, pricing/wage/indexation evidence and group conclusions.
3. Assess indicators collectively; document judgment and consistency across group.
4. Identify monetary/non-monetary populations and historical dates/bases.
5. Select/validate general price index where framework requires restatement.
6. Build restatement schedule and net monetary result.
7. Route FX translation/consolidation after local restatement in correct sequence.
8. Prepare disclosures, reconciliations and audit evidence.
9. Reassess cessation/entry each reporting period and monitor effective-date changes.

## Controls
Quarterly country watch; approved data source; group-consistency review; index governance; historical-date completeness; restatement-to-GL reconciliation; translation sequence control; disclosure tie-out; change monitoring.

## Documentation
Indicator assessment; functional-currency conclusion; index memo; population completeness; calculation workbook; FX/consolidation bridge; disclosure checklist; review/sign-off.

## Common failures
Treating 100% as a single automatic trigger; using presentation currency instead of functional currency; inconsistent group conclusions; restating monetary items incorrectly; translating before required restatement; stale index data; applying IFRS mechanics under US GAAP; ignoring 2027 IAS 21 effective-date change.

## Completion
CAO can identify the issue, gather evidence, make/document the indicator assessment, route the correct framework, calculate or supervise restatement mechanics, connect translation/consolidation, propose entries, disclosures and controls, and preserve uncertainty rather than asserting unsupported country status.
