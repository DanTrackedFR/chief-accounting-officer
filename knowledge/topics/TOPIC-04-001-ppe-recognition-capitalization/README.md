# TOPIC-04-001 — Fixed Asset Recognition, Capitalization & Additions

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Primary domain: 04 Assets & Capitalization
Capabilities: CAO-04-001, CAO-04-002, CAO-04-003
Checked: 2026-09-22

## Objective
Enable the CAO to decide whether tangible spend is PPE, expense, inventory, investment property or another asset; determine initial cost; establish the in-service/available-for-use date; and create an auditable addition record.

## Core CAO case graph
1. Resolve framework, entity, reporting period, asset purpose and ownership/control facts.
2. Determine applicable accounting scope and unit of account/component structure.
3. Test recognition criteria and future-benefit/service-potential basis as applicable.
4. Separate purchase/construction price from directly attributable and excluded costs.
5. Include qualifying dismantling/restoration obligation where applicable; route borrowing costs to separate topic when material.
6. Identify when asset reaches location/condition necessary for intended operation; stop capitalizing ordinary operating/start-up costs at that point.
7. Determine class, useful-life owner, depreciation method, residual value and impairment/revaluation policy dependencies.
8. Create asset-register record, GL entry, evidence package and reconciliation lineage.

## Framework anchors
IFRS: IAS 16. AASB: AASB 116, closely IFRS-aligned with Australian overlays. UK GAAP: FRS 102 Section 17. US GAAP: ASC 360 and other cost-specific Codification topics; US GAAP does not use one single PPE recognition standard identical to IAS 16, so source routing is fact-dependent.

## Critical practice distinction
A management capitalization threshold is an operational/materiality policy, not a substitute for the underlying asset-recognition model. CAO must distinguish accounting eligibility from the practical threshold used to expense individually immaterial qualifying items.
