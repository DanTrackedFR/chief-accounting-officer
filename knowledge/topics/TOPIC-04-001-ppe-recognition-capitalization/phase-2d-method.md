# PPE recognition and capitalization — completion method

Companion to the existing recognition pack and 550,000/10,000 case. Canonical capabilities CAO-04-001–003. Claims below are indexed in `standards-claims.json`; their evidence tier is independent of this method's substantive readiness.

## Decision and workpaper

Classify the transaction before using a capex approval as evidence. Compare contract/physical asset and economic purpose to inventory, ROU, software, business combination and investment-property routes. Select component and cost pool. Match every cost line to invoice, shipment, installation acceptance, time sheet or engineering evidence; record rebates and recoverable taxes separately. Capture commissioning date, residual, life, asset ID and restoration memo. Finance signs a gross-cost bridge from AP/project ledger to register and GL; a second reviewer checks exclusions and class notes. ERP controls must prevent duplicate invoices, transfers without asset IDs and backdated in-service dates. A threshold applied consistently to immaterial items is a materiality expedient, never proof that an otherwise ineligible cost becomes an asset [04-001-IFRS-02; 04-001-US-01].

For the existing case, 500,000 machine + 20,000 freight + 30,000 installation = 550,000 PPE; 10,000 training is expense; Dr PPE 550,000, Dr training 10,000, Cr payable 560,000. A separately measured present restoration obligation of **20,000** would instead add Dr PPE 20,000 / Cr retirement provision 20,000, subject to each framework's liability rules, and later unwinding/estimate changes are separate. No provision is invented if the obligation does not exist [04-001-IFRS-02; 04-001-US-02].

Boundary tests: (1) production sold during testing: sales and related production cost are accounted for separately under amended IAS 16, not netted into PPE; (2) 30,000 installation performed after the machine was already capable of intended operation: remove it unless it creates a distinct subsequent improvement; (3) a 100,000 replacement component with an old part still in the register: derecognize the old component, then test new recognition; (4) a short-term resale machine: inventory rather than operating PPE; (5) US fair-value uplift with no qualifying transaction: fail. These tests require invoice dates and scope, not only arithmetic [04-001-IFRS-03; 04-001-US-01].

Source/audit queue: inspect operative ASC 360 and cost-specific ASC 835-20/410-20 provisions, including presentation/disclosure, before asserting paragraph-level authority; check actual AASB edition, FRS 102 tier and election for a live case. No source-access gap changes the above routing into a universal conclusion for an unknown transaction.
