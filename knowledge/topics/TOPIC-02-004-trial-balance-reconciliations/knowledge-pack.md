# TOPIC-02-004 — Trial Balance Review & Balance Sheet Reconciliations

Status: REVIEWED
Capabilities: CAO-02-009, CAO-02-010
Knowledge types: PRINCIPLES, PRACTICE
Framework sensitivity: LOW; balance-specific conclusions inherit applicable accounting framework.

## Objective
Demonstrate that material balance-sheet balances are supported, complete, accurately valued/classified and free of unexplained reconciling items; use the TB as an analytical map of the ledger rather than a report to tick off.

## Reconciliation standard
A reconciliation contains: account/entity/period; ledger balance; independent source or rollforward; reconciling items; age; cause; owner; resolution date; accounting conclusion; supporting evidence; preparer/reviewer; sign-off. Merely agreeing one internally generated ledger report to another is not independent substantiation.

## Reconciliation types
Subledger-to-GL; external statement-to-GL; rollforward; schedule-to-GL; system-to-system; transactional population; valuation/model reconciliation; intercompany; clearing account; zero-balance expectation.

Select the method that proves the assertion. Bank statements support cash existence/completeness; fixed-asset rollforwards support movements; provisions need methodology/evidence, not only arithmetic agreement.

## CAO method
1. Obtain final/controlled TB and COA.
2. Scope accounts by quantitative materiality plus qualitative/risk criteria.
3. Assign appropriate reconciliation method/frequency.
4. Tie GL balance to controlled source.
5. Reperform rollforward/calculation.
6. Identify every difference rather than netting unrelated items.
7. Age and classify reconciling items: timing, known adjustment, data/interface, classification, unexplained.
8. Resolve or book valid adjustment; assess cut-off/error implications.
9. Reviewer challenges stale, recurring and unsupported items.
10. Certify only when completion criteria met.

## TB review analytics
Period-on-period and budget/expectation flux; unexpected signs; zero/dormant accounts; new accounts; large/manual/post-close entries; unusual account combinations; retained earnings; control-account mismatches; entity/dimension anomalies; balances inconsistent with business activity.

## Reconciling-item policy
Set risk-based aging/escalation. Never allow an aging threshold to legitimize an unexplained material item. Recurring reconciling items indicate process/system defects and feed remediation backlog.

## Controls / audit
TB version control; reconciliation completeness; preparer/reviewer; evidence; aging/escalation; certification; adjustment tracking; source access. Auditor should be able to trace GL → reconciliation → independent evidence and reperform key logic.

## Systems / TrackedFR
This is a primary TrackedFR use case when recurring reconciliations require ERP/subledger/warehouse/bank or operational datasets in Excel. Automation should preserve source lineage, matching rules, exceptions and reviewer evidence.

## Artifacts
Account inventory; risk-scoping matrix; reconciliation template; reconciling-item register; TB analytical review; certification dashboard; remediation log.

## Scenarios
1. AP GL agrees to ERP AP report generated from same ledger table: insufficient independent reconciliation if it does not test subledger completeness/integrity.
2. $20k unexplained item in low-balance account with fraud sensitivity: qualitative risk can require investigation despite numeric threshold.
3. Reconciliation has same $100k timing item for six months: reclassify as process defect; do not roll forward explanation.
