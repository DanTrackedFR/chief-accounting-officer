## Adverse execution and control

Retained service modification: after six months, 600 earned; remaining 600 plus discounted 300 amendment over twelve equal future months → 75/month, liability 825 after the first new month. Integrated-service opposite case: old 1,200×400/800=600, new 1,400×400/1,000=560 → reverse revenue 40. The new executable payment-right case has custom asset price 1,000, costs 300, expected total 600 and only cancellation reimbursement of 100. With no customer-created-asset/simultaneous-consumption route, no over-time revenue arises despite 50% cost progress; accumulated costs follow the appropriate inventory/asset model. When accepted later, revenue 1,000 and cost 600 are recognized. If a valid right to cost plus reasonable margin instead exists, the independent over-time branch earns 500 at the same progress. These outcomes exercise legal facts, not just multiplication.
