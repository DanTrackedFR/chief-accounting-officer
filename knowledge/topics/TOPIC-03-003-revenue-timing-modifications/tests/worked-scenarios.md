# Revenue timing and modification calculations

Capabilities: CAO-03-006/007. All amounts illustrative. The parent topic governs the classification tests. These calculations assume identified obligations, enforceable rights and a supported measure of progress; they cannot establish those facts themselves.

## Continuous service

An annual stand-ready service begins 1 January for 1,200. Assume an even service pattern. At 31 March, 3/12 × 1,200 = 300 cumulative revenue and 900 remains to be recognized. Advance billing does not change the performance measure. Compare to TOPIC-03-005 for contract-liability entries.

**Executed:** 300 + 900 = 1,200. PASS under even-performance assumption.

## Integrated service change

An integrated service is one partially satisfied obligation with original price 1,000 and approved revised total price 1,200. Assume a valid input measure of progress of 40% at modification date and previously recognized revenue of 350. Under a cumulative catch-up treatment, revised cumulative revenue is 1,200 × 40% = 480 and the current catch-up is 130. Verify that the modification affects the single undelivered/partially delivered obligation and that costs used in the denominator faithfully represent transfer; otherwise this computation cannot be posted.

**Executed:** 480 − 350 = 130; remaining revised price 720. PASS arithmetic, classification conditional.

## Distinct remaining service

Original remaining allocation is 300. Customer adds a distinct future service for 150, but the added price does not meet separate-contract criteria on the assumed facts. If the remaining promises are distinct from services already transferred, allocate the 450 remaining consideration to the remaining obligations under the applicable model and recognize prospectively. Do not apply the integrated-service catch-up of the prior example to completed performance.

**Executed:** 300 + 150 = 450 remaining consideration; zero automatic catch-up. PASS routing, allocation requires SSP evidence.

## Negative test and evidence

A custom asset has no alternative use but the termination clause offers only nominal compensation. Obtain legal analysis of enforceable right to payment before using an over-time method; otherwise test point-in-time transfer. Keep approved amendment, enforceability analysis, obligation/SSP map, original and revised consideration, progress inputs, prior revenue journal IDs, catch-up calculation and reviewer approval. IFRS 15/AASB 15 paragraph 35 and modification provisions are source-family pointers; ASC 606 and FRS 102 revised Section 23 require current period-specific verification. US paragraph-level source depth remains PARTIAL.
