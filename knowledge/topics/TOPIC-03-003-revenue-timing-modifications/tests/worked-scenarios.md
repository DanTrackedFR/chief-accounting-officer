# Revenue timing and modification calculations

Capabilities: CAO-03-006/007. All amounts illustrative. The parent topic governs the classification tests. These calculations assume identified obligations, enforceable rights and a supported measure of progress; they cannot establish those facts themselves.

## Continuous service

An annual stand-ready service begins 1 January for 1,200. Assume an even service pattern. At 31 March, 3/12 × 1,200 = 300 cumulative revenue and 900 remains to be recognized. Advance billing does not change the performance measure. Compare to TOPIC-03-005 for contract-liability entries.

**Executed:** 300 + 900 = 1,200. PASS under even-performance assumption.
