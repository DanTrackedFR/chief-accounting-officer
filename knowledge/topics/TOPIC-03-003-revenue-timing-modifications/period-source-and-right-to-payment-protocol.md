# Control over time: enforceable payment, progress and modification protocol

The retained right-to-payment counterexample is not cured by a deposit or a termination penalty. Verify legal rights throughout construction: a right only to recover a resale shortfall can fail to compensate performance to date. The [IFRS Foundation's IFRS 15 issued text and embedded IFRIC agenda decision](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2025/issued/ifrs15.html) locate the tests at paragraphs 35–37 and B6–B13, the progress-method requirements at 39–43 and B14–B19, point-in-time indicators at 38, and modification accounting at 18–21. [AASB 15](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022) follows corresponding paragraphs for its 2023-to-pre-July-2026 compilation; use a later operative compilation for periods from July 2026. Revised FRS 102 Section 23 principally begins 1 January 2026; use the actual edition and transition record. US 606 exact current paragraphs remain to be checked in ASC Basic View, not inferred from convergence.

For a 1,000 fixed-price build with 400 input costs incurred and 800 total expected eligible costs, a 50% cost-to-cost measure would imply 500 cumulative revenue **only after** an over-time criterion and a faithful cost input population are established. If 100 of the 400 is uninstalled material that does not depict progress, segregate it and apply the applicable method/adjustment; do not mechanically report 50%. If counsel confirms only a right to a 50 cancellation fee while performance to date has a selling price of 500, the 35(c) payment limb may fail notwithstanding the spreadsheet's 50% figure; assess 35(a) and (b), then point-in-time treatment if all fail. Capture the termination clause, local-law opinion, practical alternative-use evidence, project budget versions, excluded inputs, forecast-to-complete sign-off and customer acceptance. A change order must have enforceable scope/price approval before a modification is booked; test separate-contract, prospective and cumulative-catch-up paths using remaining distinctness and pricing.

Controls must independently reconcile contract-line versions to project costs, flag negative gross margin and substantial cost revisions, and prohibit revenue based only on invoices. Route cost assets to TOPIC-03-006 and unbilled position to 03-005. Test the adverse case of a late signed change order entered with a prior effective date; reject a retroactive amount unsupported at period end. This protocol remains PARTIAL until US Basic View and operative UK/AASB edition checks are signed.
