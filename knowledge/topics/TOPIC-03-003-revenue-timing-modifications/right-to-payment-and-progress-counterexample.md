# Over-time right to payment and progress input challenge

**Custom asset.** Customer receives a specialized asset at completion; supplier has no alternative use, but contract allows cancellation at any time for only a nominal fee. No-alternative-use alone does not establish over-time recognition under IFRS 15's relevant criterion: obtain legal opinion on enforceable right to payment for performance completed to date, including profit margin and jurisdictional enforceability. If that right does not exist and no other over-time criterion holds, assess point-in-time control transfer despite internal cost-to-cost system showing 40% progress. If enforceable payment exists, test whether input measure depicts transfer and excludes abnormal waste or uninstalled goods effects under operative rule.

**Progress sensitivity.** Contract price 1,200; estimated total eligible costs 800; actual eligible costs 320 gives 40% progress and illustrative cumulative revenue 480, before any other constraint. Of actual 320, suppose 80 is abnormal rework that does not depict progress. If excluded, supported costs 240/800=30% and revenue 360; do not keep 480 solely to meet margin budget. This is a simplified sensitivity; whether denominator also changes depends on forecast composition and contract facts. Prior recognized revenue 350 would imply current catch-up 10 under the 360 result, rather than 130 in retained modification example using 480. Do not splice different original/modified price and cost versions into one calculation. Modifications that add distinct services at standalone price may be separate contracts; other modifications may be prospective or cumulative catch-up based on remaining promises.

**QA/evidence.** Test 240/800×1,200=360 and 360−350=10, subject to consistent original facts; fail over-time from cost-incurrence alone, 80 abnormal rework counted as progress without assessment and negative change hidden in billing. Retain enforceable termination clause/opinion, schedule of work, waste logs, forecast revisions, modification approval, SSP and revenue engine version.
