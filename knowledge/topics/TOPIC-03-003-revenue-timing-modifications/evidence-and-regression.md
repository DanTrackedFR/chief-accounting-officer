# Timing and modifications — evidence and regression

Claims: [TOPIC-03-003-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-03-003-AASB-001], [TOPIC-03-003-UK-001], [TOPIC-03-003-US-001]. Use the retained right-to-payment protocol with counsel's jurisdiction-specific enforceability memo.

Take a 1,000 custom build, 400 incurred eligible cost, 800 forecast total. If no alternative use and a right to compensation for work to date are both established, 50% progress yields 500 cumulative revenue and Dr contract asset 500 / Cr revenue 500 before billing. If termination provides only 50 and no other over-time route applies, the 500 entry fails; hold inventory/work-in-progress under its own standard until point-in-time control. For a 200 approved change adding a distinct, separately priced deliverable, create a new contract; a price-only change to partially completed integrated work requires a revised cumulative calculation rather than deferring all change to future periods.

Boundary tests: invoice issued before customer acceptance cannot substitute for control; a retroactive change-order date unsupported at reporting date cannot create period revenue. IFRS/AASB use the over-time and modification paragraph routes, with Australian operative version selected by start date. Revised UK Section 23 must be tested for actual 2026 transition; earlier periods retain prior policy. US Topic 606 has its own legal-enforceability and transition/disclosure path. Cost inputs, exclusion flags, legal opinion, amendment approval timestamp and contract-asset movement must reconcile. Claims require US/UK direct paragraph audit and later AASB edition audit.
