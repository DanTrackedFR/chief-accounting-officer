# Standards and framework differences — TOPIC-13-010

Source check: 2026-09-25. Public-repo posture is REFERENCE_ONLY; no copyrighted standards body is reproduced.

## IFRS
IFRS Accounting Standards do not provide a general-purpose “fresh-start accounting” model equivalent to US reorganization accounting. Route the reporting entity and basis questions through the applicable presentation/consolidation/separate-financial-statement standards and transaction-specific literature. IFRS 10 defines consolidated financial statements around a parent and controlled subsidiaries; IAS 27 governs separate financial statements. IAS 1 remains the presentation anchor before IFRS 18 becomes mandatory for annual periods beginning 1 January 2027 (earlier application permitted). A carve-out label does not override full IFRS recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure requirements.

Authoritative anchors: IFRS 10; IAS 27; IAS 1 / IFRS 18 effective-date gate; transaction-specific standards.

## US GAAP
Carve-out financial statements are a reporting construction, not an automatic new accounting basis. Fresh-start accounting is a separate question routed to ASC 852 Reorganizations and requires the qualifying reorganization facts and criteria to be established. Public FASB materials do not expose all current Codification body text; paragraph-level US mapping therefore remains **PARTIAL** pending licensed/current Codification access. Do not infer fresh-start qualification from bankruptcy/restructuring language alone.

## UK GAAP
FRS 102 reporting must be routed through the entity/reporting perimeter and applicable Sections. Group reconstructions have a specific UK route under Section 19; that route must not be generalized into a carve-out/fresh-start rule. Periodic Review 2024 amendments are generally effective for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2026; 2026 FRC adapted-format amendments are effective from 1 January 2027 for entities choosing those formats.

## AASB
AASB reporting follows Australian-adopted standards plus entity-type/tier requirements. Do not infer Australian compliance solely from IFRS equivalence; verify the operative AASB compilation and Australian overlays for the period. There is no general AASB fresh-start model to import from ASC 852.

## Material differences
- US GAAP contains a specific reorganization/fresh-start accounting route; IFRS/AASB do not have a general equivalent.
- UK group-reconstruction accounting is a specific FRS 102 mechanism, not a universal fresh-start analogue.
- Carve-out perimeter/allocation judgments are driven heavily by facts, jurisdiction/filing requirements and faithful historical attribution rather than a single accounting standard.
- Effective-period routing remains mandatory, particularly IFRS 18/AASB 18 and FRS 102 changes.
