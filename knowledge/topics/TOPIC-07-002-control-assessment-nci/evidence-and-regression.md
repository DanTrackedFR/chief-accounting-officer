# TOPIC-07-002 — claim-linked boundary case

Claims: [TOPIC-07-002-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-07-002-US-001], [TOPIC-07-002-UK-001], [TOPIC-07-002-AASB-001]. Existing topic factory retained.

## Executed case

Parent owns 80% of subsidiary; subsidiary earns 100: allocate 80 controlling and 20 NCI before group-specific adjustments. Parent acquires another 10% for 50 while control remains; compare consideration to 10% of NCI carrying value and record difference in parent equity, not new goodwill or P&L under IFRS. If subsidiary loss exceeds NCI opening balance, do not clamp NCI at zero automatically. US ASC 810/805 and UK §9/19 have their own acquisition/NCI options and disclosure routes; AASB 10/3 period and tier matter.

## Review controls and period gates

Record input/source data, alternative decision branches, calculation, journals, disclosure tie-out and reviewer. The IFRS claim uses the 2026 issued route. US ASC, UK FRS 102 and Australian AASB need separate operative-period and entity-type audits; do not infer identical treatment. The four claims are tested by the cases above.
