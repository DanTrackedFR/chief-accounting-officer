# Technical consultation, memo and position paper — applied factory

Canonical capability map: CAO-14-007 technical accounting consultation records the question, stakeholders and advice; CAO-14-008 accounting memo writing produces the fact/source/analysis/journal document; CAO-14-009 position paper records a defensible selected treatment, alternatives, dissent and approval. These are three outputs of this one canonical topic, each tested by the worked sale-and-leaseback consultation below.

Retains original concise README. CAO-14-007–009. Source check 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for drafting/execution method.

## Choosing the artifact

Use a consultation note for a narrow answer with limited financial effect; a technical memo for a material accounting judgment; a position paper for complex, cross-functional, audit committee or regulator scrutiny. The depth changes, not the minimum provenance. Each artifact identifies decision owner, entity/framework/period, exact issue, signed factual record, source/paragraph if verified, current versus proposed guidance, credible alternatives, worked accounting impact, journal/presentation/disclosure, controls/evidence, conditions for reversal and approvals. Use source hierarchy under TOPIC-14-001; IFRS [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/), UK [FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/), [AASB](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/) and US [FASB Codification](https://asc.fasb.org/) are source families, not interchangeable generic authority. A legal or valuation specialist can establish facts and assumptions but does not make the CAO accounting conclusion.

## Position-paper case

Seller transfers a machine with carrying amount 600k for 1m and leases it back. Instead of writing “gain 400k,” paper asks if the transfer satisfies sale criteria, identifies side options and off-market terms, and measures retained right. Under a simplified IFRS qualifying-sale case with PV of leaseback payments 400k, retained fraction 40%, initial ROU 240k and gain on transferred 60% rights 240k. Entry Dr cash 1m; Dr ROU 240k; Cr original PPE 600k; Cr liability 400k; Cr gain 240k. Debit and credit each total 1.24m. Contrast failed-sale alternative: no derecognition/gain; financing analysis. US ASC 842 and 2026 UK FRS 102 Section 20 require independent calculations and sources. The memo would attach asset/lease contract, fair-value report, rate workpaper, schedule and modification/variable-payment analysis; if sale criterion cannot be established, conclude conditionally rather than book the illustration.

## Challenge and quality gate

Require a strongest-contrary-treatment section, not merely arguments for management's desired answer. Quantify alternatives where possible; indicate assumptions not yet known, effect on close/filing deadlines and what decision could change the conclusion. Reviewer recalculates key numbers, verifies source authority/effective period and checks every proposed entry against ledger mapping. S1: claimed 400k entire gain in simplified IFRS case → FAIL retained-right analysis. S2: US answer copied from IFRS paper → FAIL framework. S3: journal balances but contracts contain a repurchase option not analyzed → FAIL completeness. S4: rate unavailable → paper states conditional method and evidence request. S5: external valuation used without management challenge → FAIL. Outputs include memo version history, source citations, fact/evidence index, calculations, alternative bridge and signed implementation actions.
