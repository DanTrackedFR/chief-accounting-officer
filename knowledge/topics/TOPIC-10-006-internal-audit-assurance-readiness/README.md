# TOPIC-10-006 — Internal Audit Accounting Support / Assurance Readiness Assessment

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Capabilities: CAO-10-015, CAO-10-016
Sensitivity: M

## Scope
Support internal audit and other assurance activity over accounting/financial reporting while preserving role boundaries. The CAO can supply process, accounting, control and evidence context; internal audit/assurance providers retain their own scope, independence/objectivity and conclusions.

## Readiness assessment dimensions
- scope and criteria clearly defined;
- accounting framework/jurisdiction resolved;
- process/system boundaries current;
- risk/control matrix current where relevant;
- populations reproducible and reconciled;
- evidence retained and accessible;
- policies/SOPs match practice;
- material judgments documented;
- prior findings tracked;
- owners/reviewers available;
- data lineage/IPE controls documented;
- issue escalation and governance route defined.

## CAO workflow
1. Resolve engagement purpose, criteria, scope, period, entities and assurance provider.
2. Map scope to CAO topics/processes/accounts/disclosures/controls.
3. Run evidence-gap assessment before fieldwork.
4. Remediate factual documentation gaps without retroactively manufacturing evidence of control operation.
5. Coordinate requests through a controlled register.
6. Assess findings for accounting, control, system and disclosure consequences.
7. Route remediation through Domain 09 and operating-process owners.
8. Retain final report, responses and durable lessons in Company Accounting Memory.

## Maturity model
Reactive: evidence assembled ad hoc. Controlled: owners/register/basic tie-outs. Standardized: reusable evidence packs and current narratives. Scaled: system-generated lineage, integrated issue tracking, automated reconciliations. World-class: exception-driven assurance readiness with continuous evidence and traceability.

## Controls
Scope approval; evidence owner/reviewer; no backdating; version retention; confidential-data access; finding reconciliation between provider report and management register; closure validation.

## Scenario tests
- Internal audit asks for a control screenshot after period end where none was retained: disclose evidence gap; do not recreate historical evidence — PASS.
- Assurance scope includes sustainability metric linked to FS assumption: route Domain 17 plus accounting connectivity — PASS.
- Internal audit recommendation conflicts with accounting standard: CAO preserves required accounting and works on control/process alternative — PASS.
- SOC/service-organization report affects accounting process: route systems/control dependency assessment — PASS.

## Knowledge classification
Predominantly PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE. Specific assurance standards are invoked only after engagement/jurisdiction resolution; do not universalize one audit regime.
