# Internal audit assurance boundary

## Engagement route and decision
Specify whether the work is management control monitoring, internal audit assurance or external audit reliance. Internal audit's risk-based independent mandate follows the applicable charter; IIA Global Internal Audit Standards became effective 9 January 2025. Management retains financial reporting/control decisions. External auditor independently determines whether and how to use internal audit work under its engagement rules.

## Control, evidence and data
For each internal audit review, preserve approved scope, independence/conflict declaration, risk, population and test method, evidence lineage, exceptions, rating rationale, action owner and retest. Map findings to accounting RCM and close calendar. Avoid self-review when the same person designed the control and purports to independently assure it.

## Adverse case and escalation
Internal audit reviews an ERP role migration but tested only pre-migration users. Classify conclusion as limited, test post-migration access and privileged roles, bridge period exposure, escalate deficiencies; do not certify annual control effectiveness from stale scope.

## Handoff and authority
Link the evidence packet to the request register and final statement version; route accounting errors to Domain 02, control failures to Domain 09 and systems/data gaps to Domain 11. Reassess scope and effective dates at each new engagement. References: [PCAOB auditing standards](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards), [IAASB standards and pronouncements](https://www.iaasb.org/standards-pronouncements), [IAASB 2025 Handbook](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/2025-handbook-international-quality-management-auditing-review-other-assurance-and-related-services). These references describe auditor responsibilities; management owns books, support and correction decisions.
