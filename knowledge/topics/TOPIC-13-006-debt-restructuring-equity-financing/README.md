# TOPIC-13-006 — Debt Restructuring Accounting / Equity Financing Accounting

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Primary capabilities: CAO-13-011, CAO-13-012
Sensitivity: H
Source check: 2026-09-24

## Purpose
Enable the CAO to own accounting for amendments, exchanges, extinguishments and debt-for-equity transactions, and to build the accounting workplan for equity financing. The CAO determines classification, derecognition/modification, measurement, fees/costs, entries, presentation, disclosure, controls and evidence; legal, valuation, tax and treasury inputs support rather than replace the accounting conclusion.

## Required intake
Entity; framework; reporting period; transaction/closing/modification dates; existing debt agreements and carrying-value/EIR schedules; amendments/exchange documents; lender identity and related-party status; old/new contractual cash flows; fees by payer/payee; accrued interest; collateral/covenants; waivers; financial-difficulty facts; equity term sheet and executed documents; instrument rights; shares/options/warrants/convertibles issued; fair values; issuance costs; cap table; approvals; settlement evidence; and subsequent events.

## Principles
1. Reconcile legal form to accounting substance before posting. A refinancing, exchange, waiver or amendment is not automatically a new instrument or continuation.
2. For debt, decide derecognition/extinguishment versus modification before calculating the accounting consequence.
3. Preserve lender/counterparty identity, chronology and all contractual changes. Quantitative tests do not eliminate qualitative analysis where the applicable framework requires it.
4. Separate lender fees, third-party fees, accrued interest, premiums/discounts and transaction costs; destination depends on framework and conclusion.
5. A debt-for-equity settlement invokes both liability derecognition and equity measurement/classification. Do not post the difference directly to equity unless the applicable literature supports it.
6. For equity financing, classify every issued instrument before allocating proceeds/costs. Ordinary shares, preferred shares, warrants and convertibles may not all be equity.
7. Reconcile accounting proceeds to cash, legal closing statements, cap table and GL; investigate unexplained differences.
8. Current standard-setting projects are not current GAAP. Pipeline proposals are tracked separately from effective requirements.

## Framework routing
### IFRS
Route debt through IFRS 9 derecognition/modification requirements. An extinguished liability is removed; an exchange or substantial modification can result in derecognition and recognition of a new liability. IFRS 9 application guidance contains the quantitative 10% test for substantially different liability terms and specifies which borrower/lender fees enter that calculation. For a modification that does not derecognize the liability, apply the current IFRS 9 modification mechanics using the original effective interest rate and account for qualifying fees/costs consistently with the continuation conclusion. IFRIC 19 applies when equity instruments are issued to a creditor to extinguish all or part of a financial liability; measure the equity issued at fair value when reliably measurable, otherwise use the liability fair value, and recognize the difference from the derecognized liability in profit or loss subject to the interpretation's scope.

For equity financing, first apply IAS 32 liability/equity classification and compound-instrument requirements, then IFRS 9 where a financial liability/derivative component exists. Equity transaction costs that are directly attributable to the equity transaction are accounted for under the applicable equity guidance; mixed transactions require a supportable allocation. Route share-based consideration for goods/services to IFRS 2 where applicable rather than treating every share issuance as financing.

Effective-date gate: amendments to IFRS 9/IFRS 7 on classification and measurement are effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026. Separately, the IASB's 2026 amortised-cost project is still standard-setting work: its tentative proposals concerning substantial modification must not be applied as current requirements until finalized and effective.

### AASB
Apply current AASB 9 independently. Current text states that substantially different debt terms or a substantial modification result in extinguishment/new liability, and application guidance includes the 10% present-value test and fee rules. Route equity classification through current AASB 132 and debt-for-equity transactions through the Australian adoption of applicable IFRS interpretation requirements. Confirm the current Australian compilation and transaction/reporting date rather than assuming IFRS references alone establish compliance.

### US GAAP
Route debt modification/extinguishment through current Codification, principally the applicable debt modification/extinguishment and liability extinguishment guidance, with separate analysis for troubled/debtor-creditor facts and specialized instruments as applicable. Public FASB material confirms current GAAP requires determining whether an existing debt modification/exchange is continuation or issuance of a new obligation/extinguishment. FASB's 2025 debt-exchange proposal was paused in March 2026; it is not current GAAP. Because public FASB pages do not expose all current Codification paragraph bodies, transaction-level paragraph mapping remains PARTIAL until checked against a licensed/current Codification source.

Equity financing requires independent US classification under the relevant equity/liability/derivative/convertible/warrant guidance. Do not infer US equity classification from IAS 32. Analyze issuance costs and allocation based on the actual instruments and current Codification.

### UK GAAP / FRS 102
Apply current FRS 102 financial-instrument and equity/liability requirements, with mandatory reporting-period gating for Periodic Review 2024 changes generally effective for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2026. Determine whether debt is derecognized, modified or continued under the current Section 11/12 architecture applicable to the period; independently assess compound instruments and equity classification. Do not import IFRS 9's detailed mechanics or US rules without a current FRS 102 source check. Paragraph-level conclusions remain PARTIAL where the public source path has not been independently mapped in the case file.

## Material framework differences
- Liability/equity classification differs materially between IFRS/AASB and US GAAP; legal labels are insufficient.
- Debt modification/extinguishment tests, qualitative factors, fee treatment and distressed-debt rules require framework-specific routing.
- IFRS/AASB have an explicit 10% liability test in application guidance; never assume the same mechanics are the complete US/UK answer.
- Debt-for-equity measurement and gain/loss mechanics require specific framework routing.
- Equity issuance cost accounting and allocation across mixed instruments can differ.
- Current projects/proposals (including FASB's paused debt-exchange project and IASB's 2026 amortised-cost work) must be tagged PIPELINE, not silently applied.

## CAO execution workflow
1. Lock entity, framework, period, transaction date, materiality and legal counterparties.
2. Reconcile pre-transaction debt carrying amount to GL and lender statement, including principal, accrued interest, unamortized fees/discounts and hedging relationships.
3. Build old-versus-new terms matrix: principal, currency, coupon, maturity, payment dates, options, collateral, covenants, guarantees, conversion/equity features and counterparties.
4. Determine whether the transaction is repayment/new borrowing, exchange, amendment, waiver, partial extinguishment, debt-for-equity, or combination.
5. Apply framework-specific derecognition/modification analysis. Perform quantitative test where required and document qualitative factors separately.
6. Inventory every fee/cost by amount, payer, recipient, purpose and evidence; determine treatment only after the accounting conclusion.
7. For extinguishment, calculate carrying amount derecognized, consideration/new-instrument measurement and gain/loss; for continuation/modification, calculate revised carrying amount/EIR mechanics under the applicable framework.
8. Identify any embedded derivative, conversion, warrant, preferred-share or other feature requiring separate classification/measurement.
9. For debt-for-equity, determine scope, equity fair value/liability fair value evidence and gain/loss.
10. For equity financing, reconcile gross proceeds to closing statement/cash; classify each instrument; allocate proceeds and directly attributable costs using framework-specific requirements; update equity rollforward/cap table.
11. Assess covenant/going-concern/current-v-noncurrent and subsequent-event consequences with reporting topics.
12. Prepare JEs, rollforwards, disclosures, technical memo and evidence index.
13. Reconcile post-transaction debt/equity schedules to GL, cash, legal records and cap table.
14. Promote durable financing classification policies, elections and recurring fee/cost methodology to Company Accounting Memory.

## Calculation architecture
**Debt bridge:** opening carrying amount + accrued items/unamortized costs → old contractual cash flows → new contractual cash flows → framework quantitative test (where applicable) → conclusion → consideration/new liability measurement → modification/extinguishment gain/loss → fee/cost treatment → closing carrying amount/new EIR → JE.

**Equity bridge:** legal gross proceeds → cash/receivable settlement → instrument population → classification → proceeds allocation → directly attributable issuance costs and allocation → par/stated capital + additional paid-in capital/share premium/other components → closing cap-table-to-GL reconciliation.

Every material input carries source, date, owner, framework reference, judgment, calculation cell/record and reviewer.

## Documentation package
Executed old/new debt and financing documents; chronology; lender/counterparty matrix; debt carrying-value reconciliation; old/new cash-flow model; modification/extinguishment memo; quantitative/qualitative test; fee/cost register; fair-value support; debt-for-equity analysis; instrument-classification memo; proceeds/allocation workpaper; cap-table/legal-capital reconciliation; JE package; covenant/presentation/disclosure analysis; evidence index; open-items log.

## Controls and audit readiness
Minimum controls: executed-document completeness; independent old/new terms review; pre-transaction GL tie-out; model input/recalculation review; fee population and payer/payee validation; classification approval for complex instruments; fair-value source review; cash/closing statement reconciliation; cap-table-to-GL reconciliation; covenant/current-noncurrent review; disclosure tie-out; access/change control over models; subsequent modification trigger.

Audit evidence must allow a reviewer to reproduce why debt was modified or extinguished, every material cash-flow/fee input, gain/loss, new carrying amount/EIR, instrument classification, proceeds/cost allocation and financial-statement effect.

## Systems and data
Use ERP/GL and debt subledger for carrying values; treasury/debt systems for contractual schedules (accounting remains CAO-owned); legal repository for executed terms; bank data for settlement; cap-table/equity platform for issuances; valuation support for fair values; consolidation/reporting system for presentation/disclosure. Maintain stable instrument IDs across legal, treasury, cap table and GL.

**TrackedFR applicability:** assess when recurring cross-system debt/equity schedules, bank settlements, cap-table data and GL balances require repeatable reconciliation/manipulation. A one-off refinancing memo is not itself a TrackedFR use case.

## Scenario QA
1. Same lender, immaterial term changes and no derecognition under applicable framework: route to modification/continuation accounting, not automatic extinguishment.
2. IFRS/AASB modified cash flows meet substantial-difference conclusion: derecognize old liability, recognize new liability, calculate gain/loss and fee treatment.
3. IFRS quantitative result below 10% but major qualitative contractual change exists: do not stop at arithmetic; complete applicable qualitative analysis.
4. Equity issued to creditor to settle debt under IFRIC 19 scope: derecognize settled liability, measure equity under interpretation and recognize resulting P&L difference.
5. FASB 2025 proposed debt-exchange model appears economically relevant: tag PIPELINE; do not apply because project was paused and proposal is not current GAAP.
6. Financing includes ordinary shares plus warrants: classify components before allocating proceeds/costs; invoke warrant topic.
7. Convertible debt issuance: invoke TOPIC-13-007 plus financial-instrument topics; do not treat all proceeds as ordinary debt/equity by label.
8. Refinancing occurs near reporting date and affects current/noncurrent classification or going concern: invoke reporting topics and preserve chronology.
9. Debt fees paid to lender and third parties are mixed: separate by payer/recipient/purpose before applying framework treatment.
10. Closing statement/cash/cap table/GL do not reconcile: completion blocked until resolved or documented immaterial exception.
