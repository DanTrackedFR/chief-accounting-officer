# Debt restructuring and financing decision workpaper

**Sequence.** Determine legal extinguishment, lender identity, instrument and existing carrying value first. For an IFRS financial liability with amortized-cost carrying amount 980 immediately before a same-lender exchange, assume the present value of modified cash flows discounted using the *original* effective interest rate, incorporating qualifying lender fees under the applicable test, is 880. The absolute change is 100/980 = 10.204%, exceeding the IFRS 9 substantial-modification quantitative threshold. This example therefore follows derecognition, subject to checking the full contractual terms, fee population and qualitative changes. If the fair value of the new liability is 900 and there are no other settlement amounts or costs in this simplified fact pattern, derecognize 980, recognize the new liability 900 and recognize 80 gain. The 880 is the ten-percent-test present value, **not** necessarily the new liability's initial fair value. Reconcile old debt schedule and accrued interest to 980, new contractual cash flows to the 880 test and independent valuation to 900. A spreadsheet that books a 100 gain from the test amount fails.

**Counterexample.** If revised present value were 910, the numerical change would be 70/980=7.143%; the reviewer must still investigate qualitative changes (currency, embedded conversion, security, covenant package, lender change) and apply the relevant non-substantial modification mechanics if appropriate. Do not assume every below-ten-percent exchange leaves carrying amount untouched; recalculate the modification gain/loss and revised effective interest under the operative IFRS 9 guidance. For a different lender or contractual extinguishment, separate the legal/discharge analysis from a same-lender quantitative shortcut. US ASC 470-50 and troubled-debt restructuring/creditor models, FRS 102 Sections 11/12 and AASB 9 require their own assessment; a FASB proposal is never booked as current GAAP.

**Equity financing fork.** A separate ordinary-share issue raises 200 gross proceeds and incurs 8 directly attributable incremental issue costs, with no warrant or other component. Subject to the applicable tax presentation, the simplified IFRS equity increase is 192. If warrants, conversion or a mandatory cash settlement term are introduced, stop this arithmetic: classify each component first under IAS 32/IFRS 9, then allocate proceeds and costs under applicable rules; route detailed convertibles to TOPIC-13-007 and reporting presentation to Domain 08. Do not blend the 8 share issue cost into the debt extinguishment gain. Retain signed debt modification, lender fee invoices, rate calculation, valuation, equity issuance documents, cap table, board approvals and dated GL/reconciliation and disclosure workpapers.

**Tests.** Assert 100/980 >10%, gain 980−900=80, and 200−8=192 only in the stated plain-share case. Re-run if fee eligibility or accrued interest changes; negative scenario with a warrant must fail plain-share treatment.
