# TOPIC-13-009 — Common-Control Transactions / Spin-Offs / Demergers

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**

## Purpose
Executable CAO workplan for reorganizations and transfers under common control, spin-offs and demergers. Do not apply ordinary acquisition accounting until scope, reporting entity, framework, legal form, substance and periods presented are resolved.

## Required context
Framework/period; reporting entity and statement type; group structure before/after; ultimate controller; legal steps/dates; business/assets/liabilities transferred; consideration/equity; predecessor balances; comparatives; distribution mechanics; existing policy; materiality.

## CAO workflow
1. Freeze reporting entity, framework, period and transaction steps.
2. Map control before and after each step.
3. Determine whether a business, entity or asset group moved.
4. Identify whose statements are being prepared.
5. Test business-combination scope and reconstruction rules.
6. Identify and challenge the existing accounting policy/election.
7. Determine measurement basis, comparatives, equity/goodwill treatment, costs and NCI effects.
8. For spin-offs/demergers separately analyze distribution, derecognition, discontinued operations and retained interests.
9. Build opening balances and an equity bridge from predecessor records and reconcile to legal completion and GL.
10. Document alternatives, judgments, entries, disclosures, controls and durable memory candidates.

## Standards routing
### IFRS
IFRS 3 excludes business combinations involving entities or businesses under common control. IFRS has no comprehensive accounting model for those transactions. The IASB discontinued its BCUCC project after deciding in November 2023 not to develop requirements; its April 2024 project summary is not GAAP. Apply the IFRS policy hierarchy to the actual facts and do not present discussion-paper proposals as current requirements.

### US GAAP
Use independent Codification routing for common-control transfers and spin-offs, including applicable common-control, consolidation/derecognition and presentation guidance. Do not import IFRS policy-choice reasoning. Public FASB material does not expose every current Codification paragraph body, so paragraph-level authority remains **PARTIAL** unless independently verified.

### UK GAAP
FRS 102 Section 19 permits merger accounting for qualifying group reconstructions and contains the relevant mechanics. This materially differs from IFRS's standards gap. Apply the reporting-period gate, including Periodic Review 2024 requirements effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026, plus company-law/entity overlays.

### AASB
AASB 3 excludes combinations of entities or businesses under common control. Appendix B describes ultimate control by the same party/parties before and after with control not transitory. This exclusion does not itself create a complete measurement model. Apply Australian policy-selection and entity/tier requirements independently.

## Framework gates
- IFRS/AASB: common-control combinations are outside IFRS 3/AASB 3; no universal acquisition-method answer follows.
- UK: explicit FRS 102 group-reconstruction route.
- US: specific Codification routing; IFRS's standards gap is not a US policy election.
- Separate, consolidated and newly formed entity statements can differ.
- Spin-off/demerger is not synonymous with common-control business combination.

## Workpaper / documentation
Show predecessor carrying values, policy adjustments, consideration/equity/distribution, correct equity/goodwill/other routing, NCI/retained interests, costs, opening-balance bridge, comparative basis and group reconciliation. Minimum evidence: legal-step diagram; before/after control chart; business-v-asset assessment; scope/policy memo; predecessor TB; opening-balance/equity bridge; entries; comparative and distribution conclusions; approvals; disclosure checklist.

## Controls / systems
Control legal-step completeness, common-control validation, policy approval, predecessor-balance extraction, entity/COA mapping, opening balances, equity/distribution approval, eliminations, comparatives and disclosure tie-out. TrackedFR is relevant only where migration or recurring work genuinely requires multi-system extraction/reconciliation/manipulation.

## Stop conditions
Do not finalize while material uncertainty remains over reporting entity, control, business-v-asset status, transaction sequence/date, framework/version, policy/election, predecessor balances or distribution mechanics.

## Scenario tests
1. IFRS sister-company business transfer: outside IFRS 3; policy analysis required. **PASS**
2. IFRS reliance on IASB discussion-paper proposal: reject as current GAAP. **PASS**
3. UK qualifying group reconstruction: route to Section 19 merger-accounting criteria. **PASS**
4. Australian common-control combination: AASB 3 exclusion plus Australian policy analysis. **PASS**
5. US transfer: require ASC-specific routing. **PASS**
6. Spin-off with retained interest: separate distribution/derecognition/retained-interest/presentation. **PASS**
7. New holding company: resolve reporting entity/legal steps first. **PASS**
8. Missing predecessor TB: stop finalization. **PASS**
9. Asset group that is not a business: route away from business-combination conclusion. **PASS**
10. Multi-ERP reconstruction: require opening-balance mapping/reconciliation. **PASS**
