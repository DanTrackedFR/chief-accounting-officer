# Policy selection, change and governance — targeted factory completion

The existing 75-line README is substantive and retained. This companion adds reproducible transition arithmetic, source/version gates and an adverse-case test. CAO-15-001–003. Source checked 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate, conditional on independent authority review.

## Framework authority and transition gates

[IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/) covers IFRS policy selection, voluntary change and error/estimate classification. Apply standard-specific transition first; a voluntary change requires the appropriate qualitative test and is generally retrospective unless impracticable. [IFRS 18](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/) becomes effective periods beginning 1 January 2027, with early application possible, and changes IAS 8's title/related presentation architecture; do not cite an old-period paragraph map without versioning. US ASC 250 is the accounting-change route and SEC registrants may need preferability/reporting documentation; the [FASB Codification portal](https://asc.fasb.org/) does not provide a verified full current paragraph body here, so precise US citations stay source-depth PARTIAL. UK [FRS 102 Section 10](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) is period-gated by Periodic Review 2024 (generally effective from 1 January 2026). [AASB 108](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-108-mar-2021) follows the Australian compilation and Tier 2 disclosure overlay (AASB 1060), rather than silently borrowing IAS 8 notes. Neither an accounting election nor management's chosen materiality threshold can override a mandatory measurement rule.

## Quantified change workpaper

Suppose a permitted policy change, with proven basis under the relevant framework, would increase an opening asset by 120,000 and opening liability by 20,000 at the earliest comparative date, excluding tax. The illustrative net opening equity adjustment is 100,000 (120,000−20,000). If the comparative period result rises by 30,000, current opening equity is 130,000 higher before tax, and comparative statements/notes and EPS (if applicable) must be recalculated. Tax effects require a separate applicable deferred/current tax calculation; do not book 100,000 as a balancing plug. Contrast a new useful-life estimate with the same 30,000 prospective current-year effect: no automatic opening-equity adjustment. Record the old and new policy, requirement permitting change, facts, election level, preferred/more reliable-relevant rationale, transition method, affected line items/periods, journal/disclosure entries and implementation in ERP.

## Governance and tests

Policy register must record scope, owner, effective period, approved version, alternative, framework citation, audit consultation, system rule and review trigger. Controls test complete product/transaction population, consistent election across the required class, authoritative amendment monitoring, separate approval for policy exceptions, transition reconciliation and retired-version preservation. S1: a new Standard specifies modified transition but finance uses generic retrospective restatement → FAIL. S2: manager chooses a permitted alternative solely to improve a KPI → fail IFRS qualitative/US preferability gate. S3: above opening balances with net equity 100k and comparative +30k → arithmetic PASS; tax/disclosure remain case-specific. S4: 2026 UK reporting uses pre-2026 FRS 102 edition without early/adoption justification → FAIL. S5: policy is amended but billing or ERP rule unchanged → implementation control fails. Evidence: approved memo, versioned sources, bridge/journals, GL tie-out, board/auditor communications, updated SOP and policy inventory. Dependencies 14-001 research, 14-003 memo, 15-006 classification, 08-004 comparative and 11-004 ERP.
