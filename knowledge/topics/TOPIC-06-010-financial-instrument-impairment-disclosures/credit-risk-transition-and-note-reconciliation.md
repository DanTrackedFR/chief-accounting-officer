# Credit-risk deterioration, allowance and disclosure reconciliation

**IFRS 9 scenario.** A loan originally recognized with low credit risk has gross carrying amount 1,000. At reporting date, supportable lifetime ECL is 80 and 12-month ECL 20. Do not choose 20 simply because actual default is not expected within twelve months. Assess significant increase in credit risk since initial recognition using changes in lifetime default risk, forward-looking information, grades, days-past-due and rebuttable presumptions as applicable; if it has significantly increased, lifetime 80 is relevant. The 12-month measure is the portion of lifetime losses arising from defaults possible within the next twelve months, not merely cash shortfalls collected in twelve months. If the loan becomes credit-impaired, route interest recognition and measurement accordingly. The 80/20 are assumed model outputs, not derived from supplied probabilities; retain model, scenarios, weighting, discounting and backtest.

**Allowance and note.** In retained portfolio case allowance should move 70 opening +35 expense −20 write-offs +5 recoveries =90, but GL says 95. The 5 is an unresolved exception. Trace new assets, currency translation, acquisition, modification, interest unwind and recoveries by asset ID before reporting net loan 1,905 or 1,910. IFRS 7 disclosure may need reconciliation by credit-risk stage, credit concentration, collateral, liquidity maturity and market-risk sensitivities; do not present an internally consistent 90 note against 95 GL. Tie stage movement to ECL model and journal source, separate contractual write-off from cash application. US ASC 326 CECL lacks identical stage 1/2; FASB ASU 2025-05 offers scoped trade-AR/contract-asset elections from periods after 15 December 2025 and is **not** a broad loan-stage exemption. FRS 102 Section 11 impairment and AASB 9/7 tier overlays require independent versions.

**Tests/evidence.** Fail 20 ECL on a significant-deterioration case without rebuttal, a 90 disclosure versus 95 GL, an IFRS staged allowance copied into US CECL and a FASB expedient applied to ordinary loans outside its scope. Retain origin risk, score history, forward macro scenarios, model governance, exception investigation, credit committee approvals, allowance rollforward and note tie.
