# Instrument impairment and disclosure population case

Capabilities CAO-06-020/021. The existing topic governs scope and framework differences. A loan portfolio at amortized cost has gross carrying amount 2,000, opening allowance 70, current impairment charge 35, approved write-offs 20 and recoveries credited to allowance 5 under the chosen policy. Expected closing allowance 70+35−20+5=90; net carrying amount 1,910. If GL allowance is 95, the unexplained 5 must be traced, not forced to charge. **Executed:** 90 expected, 95 GL, 5 unresolved; FAIL disclosure and balance-sheet tie-out.

Under IFRS 9/AASB 9, select 12-month versus lifetime ECL for eligible assets based on significant increase in credit risk, except special models; credit-impaired and purchased/originated credit-impaired require own treatment. Do not apply the TOPIC-03-008 short-term trade-AR matrix to this loan by default. US ASC 326 CECL has no identical IFRS staging; UK FRS 102 Section 11 impairment differs. Disclosure pack under IFRS 7/AASB 7 addresses credit, liquidity and market risks and allowance movements, but entity-specific tier/period scope matters. [IFRS 7 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-7-financial-instruments-disclosures/) and [IFRS 9 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/) checked 2026-09-27. ASC 326/825, FRS 102 Section 11/12 and AASB 7/9 paragraph/effective-date mapping remains PARTIAL. Preserve asset population, risk grades, cash forecasts, ECL/staging model, write-off approvals, GL bridge and disclosure tie-out.
