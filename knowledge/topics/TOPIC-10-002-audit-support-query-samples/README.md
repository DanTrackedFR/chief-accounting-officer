# TOPIC-10-002 — Audit Support Paper Preparation / Audit Query Response / Audit Sample Support

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Capabilities: CAO-10-004, CAO-10-005, CAO-10-006
Sensitivity: L

## Purpose
Provide a repeatable management-side method for support papers, audit queries and sample support while preserving evidence lineage and accounting ownership.

## Core practice
Answer the question asked and support it with controlled evidence. Identify missing facts rather than assuming them. Preserve sample identifiers and population context. Management owns its accounting and evidence; the auditor owns independent selection, procedures and conclusions. A response that changes an earlier accounting fact, population or conclusion triggers impact assessment across related workpapers.

## Support-paper standard
Material papers state objective, entity/period, account/assertion, source data, facts, method/calculation, accounting basis where relevant, result/conclusion, preparer/reviewer, date and evidence links. Technical matters link to the authoritative accounting memo rather than creating inconsistent duplicate analysis.

## Query workflow
1. Log exact query, requestor and date.
2. Classify factual, data/population, accounting, judgment/estimate, control, specialist, or proposed-adjustment question.
3. Identify the controlled company source and accountable owner.
4. Draft a concise answer distinguishing fact from judgment.
5. Reconcile quantitative answers to controlled records.
6. Review consistency with prior submissions and financial statements.
7. Submit and retain the exact response/evidence version.
8. Track follow-up and closure.

## Sample workflow
Preserve auditor sample IDs and selection list; map each item to original population and ledger/subledger key; provide requested source documents, approvals, entry and relevant settlement evidence; record exceptions; determine whether an exception indicates accounting error, control issue, population issue or evidence gap; assess whether exceptions affect other populations.

## Confirmation boundary
Current PCAOB AS 2310, The Auditor's Use of Confirmation, applies for audits of fiscal years ending on or after 15 June 2025. Confirmation remains an auditor-controlled procedure. The CAO can provide accurate account/contact data, reconcile balances and investigate differences while preserving auditor control of the procedure.

## Controls
Query register; response reviewer; evidence access control; sample-to-population key preservation; response version history; cross-response consistency check; exception escalation; closure sign-off.

## Scenario tests
- Selected invoice evidence is unavailable: report gap and investigate rather than changing the selection — PASS.
- AR confirmation difference arises from cash in transit: reconcile timing and evidence while leaving the audit conclusion to the auditor — PASS.
- Query conflicts with prior revenue memo: reopen facts/conclusion impact before release — PASS.
- Manually transformed spreadsheet requested: provide source lineage and transformation/reconciliation support — PASS.
