# Query and sample chain of custody

## Engagement route and decision
Register each query/sample by request ID, audit assertion, legal entity, period, exact selection ID, owner, due date and response status. Auditor determines sample and procedure; management validates requested population and provides records without silently substituting a different item. Confirmation requests remain auditor controlled under PCAOB AS 2310 where applicable, or the relevant ISA/local standard elsewhere.

## Control, evidence and data
Trace sample ID from auditor population to source transaction, posting, subledger/GL and supporting contract/invoice; preserve extract filters/counts, status of voids/duplicates and redaction. Mark missing originals and alternative evidence explicitly. Independent reviewer compares response to the question and reconciles any roll-forward.

## Adverse case and escalation
Auditor selects a cancelled invoice in an exported sales population. Do not replace it with a live invoice. Explain cancellation and provide original, credit and ledger reversal; correct population logic if erroneous, resubmit population and escalate sampling consequence.

## Handoff and authority
Link the evidence packet to the request register and final statement version; route accounting errors to Domain 02, control failures to Domain 09 and systems/data gaps to Domain 11. Reassess scope and effective dates at each new engagement. References: [PCAOB auditing standards](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards), [IAASB standards and pronouncements](https://www.iaasb.org/standards-pronouncements), [IAASB 2025 Handbook](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/2025-handbook-international-quality-management-auditing-review-other-assurance-and-related-services). These references describe auditor responsibilities; management owns books, support and correction decisions.
