# Hyperinflation index and translation workpaper

This is the canonical assessment path for TOPIC-13-011. Sibling folders `TOPIC-13-011-hyperinflation`, `TOPIC-13-011-hyperinflationary-accounting` and bare `TOPIC-13-011` contain useful earlier material or stubs; preserve for independent canonicalization and count this ID once.

**Economy gate.** Local subsidiary has functional currency L. Three-year cumulative inflation near or above 100% is one IAS 29 indicator, not a mechanical universal trigger. Compile index series, wage/price indexing, preference for stable currency, credit pricing and other available evidence; conclude the first applicable reporting period consistently across group entities. Separately document US ASC 830 highly inflationary assessment, which has a different functional-currency/remeasurement model. AASB 129/Interpretation 7 follows its own operative compilation; FRS 102 Sections 30/31 require version-specific analysis.

**Illustrative IAS 29 restatement.** At 31 December X1 general price index is 250; a non-monetary machine recognized when the index was 200 has historical cost 100 L and no depreciation for this isolated illustration. Restated gross cost is 100×250/200=125 L, an index adjustment of 25. A 40 L cash balance at reporting date remains 40 L and is **not** multiplied by 250/200. A purchase of inventory 50 L when index was 225 yields restated cost 50×250/225=55.56 L before subsequent inventory measurement/expense. This isolated asset math is not a balanced set of IAS 29 financial statements: reconstruct depreciation, equity dates, income/expense flows, tax and the gain/loss on net monetary position, then reconcile all opening and closing balances. A generic journal “debit machine 25, credit inflation gain 25” is unsubstantiated until the complete monetary-position/equity calculation is performed. Carry comparatives in units current at the reporting date as applicable.

**Translation fork.** Only after the subsidiary's IAS 29 financials are correctly restated in L does the group analyze IAS 21 translation into presentation currency. Do not apply one exchange-rate shortcut to an un-restated local TB. The IASB **issued** Translation to a Hyperinflationary Presentation Currency in November 2025, effective annual periods from 1 January 2027 with early adoption allowed. Its specific fact pattern translates *into* a hyperinflationary presentation currency; do not confuse it with the ordinary IAS 29 local-restatement case or label the issued amendment a 2026 pipeline proposal. Verify AASB 121 amendment version/effective period independently.

**Evidence and QA.** Retain licensed/official index provenance and revisions, acquisition dates, item-level monetary status, impairment/tax adjustments, full income/restatement bridge, monetary gain/loss, exchange-rate source, consolidation journal and disclosure of index/basis. Test: machine 125, inventory 55.56, cash 40; fail inflation threshold treated as sole criterion, cash indexed, P&L credited 25 without full reconciliation, IAS 21 amendment called unissued.
