# TOPIC-13-011 — Hyperinflationary Accounting Assessment
Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Primary capability: CAO-13-021
Sensitivity: H

## Objective
Enable the CAO to identify, document and execute accounting when an entity's functional-currency economy becomes, remains or ceases to be hyperinflationary, while separating current requirements from pipeline standard-setting.

## Principles
1. Determine entity, functional currency, reporting period and framework before accounting.
2. Hyperinflation is a judgment based on the economic evidence; a single quantitative indicator is not a universal automatic trigger under IFRS/AASB.
3. Align conclusions across a group where the same reporting basis and economy apply; investigate inconsistent conclusions.
4. Separate local-entity restatement from group translation/consolidation.
5. Preserve source index, dates, monetary/non-monetary classification, historical acquisition/revaluation dates and opening-equity lineage.
6. Treat entry into and exit from hyperinflation as controlled accounting events with documented effective-period conclusions.

## Standards routing
- IFRS: IAS 29 plus IAS 21 interaction. The July 2025 IFRIC agenda decision confirms all relevant indicators are assessed, other relevant indicators may be considered, and judgment is required. Current 2026 IASB translation work is PIPELINE until issued/effective.
- US GAAP: route independently through ASC 830 highly-inflationary-economy requirements; do not infer US treatment from IAS 29. Public FASB paragraph-body access is insufficient for APPROVED paragraph-level mapping, so US authority status remains PARTIAL.
- UK GAAP: route through current FRS 102 foreign-currency/high-inflation requirements after resolving reporting period and edition; Periodic Review 2024 is effective for periods beginning 1 Jan 2026.
- AASB: AASB 129/AASB 121 architecture, with Australian current-version/effective-date gate. Australian Interpretation 7 provides first-period application context.

## CAO workplan
1. Resolve framework, period, entity, functional/presentation currency.
2. Assemble inflation and qualitative economic evidence.
3. Document hyperinflation conclusion and effective period.
4. Inventory monetary/non-monetary items and equity.
5. Validate general price index source and controls.
6. Restate applicable non-monetary items, equity, income and expenses under the applicable framework.
7. Determine monetary gain/loss mechanics.
8. Route deferred tax, impairment and other measurement interactions.
9. Translate foreign operations only after local hyperinflation accounting is correct.
10. Reconcile restated ledgers to consolidation and disclosures.
11. Document entry/continuing/exit conclusion and subsequent-period monitoring.
12. Promote durable functional-currency, index and policy conclusions to Company Accounting Memory.

## Evidence and controls
Minimum evidence: economy assessment, index provenance, functional-currency memo, item classification, historical-date population, calculation/reperformance, tax interaction, translation bridge, consolidation reconciliation, disclosure tie-out, reviewer approval.
Key controls: quarterly economy watch; index approval; completeness of historical dates; monetary/non-monetary classification review; model change control; local-to-group reconciliation; disclosure consistency.

## Framework differences
Never describe IAS 29 and US highly-inflationary accounting as equivalent. The CAO must independently route recognition, measurement, functional-currency and translation consequences under each framework.

## Current-source note — 24 Sep 2026
The IFRS Foundation's final July 2025 agenda decision confirms that stakeholders do not assess hyperinflation from one IAS 29 indicator alone, consider other relevant indicators, and use judgment. IASB projects concerning translation in hyperinflationary presentation currencies remain non-effective pipeline unless and until issued. AASB Interpretation 7 confirms retrospective-style application on first identifying hyperinflation for AASB 129 purposes. FRC current-edition/effective-date routing remains mandatory.

## Completion criteria
A case is not complete until the economy conclusion, functional currency, effective period, index, item population, restatement/translation mechanics, reconciliations, disclosures, evidence and reviewer conclusions are explicit.
