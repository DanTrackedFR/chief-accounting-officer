## Authority and period

- IFRS: IFRS 16 sale-and-leaseback provisions, including the September 2022 amendment on subsequent measurement of the seller-lessee liability, effective annual periods beginning 1 January 2024, early application permitted. Official [IFRS 16 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-16-leases/) and [amendment project](https://www.ifrs.org/projects/completed-projects/2022/lease-liability-in-a-sale-and-leaseback/), checked 2026-09-27. Verify transaction-specific paragraphs and retrospective transition against authorized text before approval.
- AASB: AASB 16 and [AASB 2022-5](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/related-documents?pronouncement=3655), periods beginning 1 January 2024. Independently check Australian entity/tier and current compilation.
- US GAAP: ASC 842 sale-and-leaseback, including the underlying ASC 606 transfer-of-control analysis and rules for seller-lessee and buyer-lessor. FASB [Codification portal](https://asc.fasb.org/); current detailed paragraph bodies not publicly verified here. Do not transplant IFRS retained-right gain mechanics.
- UK GAAP: [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) Sections 20 and 23 in the operative edition; Periodic Review 2024 generally effective periods beginning 1 January 2026. A 2025 period and a 2026 period may require different sale and leaseback routes/transition. Paragraph-level UK treatment remains to verify for a specific case.

## Sale versus financing decision

Gather purchase/sale agreement, leaseback, side letters, options, fair value support, initial asset carrying amount, carrying liability, cash paid, lease payments and dates. Test transfer of control under the relevant revenue/sale model before derecognizing the asset. A failed sale leaves the asset on the seller-lessee's books and generally recognizes a financing liability; buyer-lessor accounts for a financial asset rather than an acquired leased asset, subject to framework details. For a qualifying IFRS/AASB sale, identify the retained right of use separately from the rights transferred; recognize gain attributable to the transferred rights, not the retained right. Test off-market terms and the amendment's subsequent-measurement constraints. Under US/UK requirements separately determine gain, leaseback classification, off-market adjustments and transition. An option to repurchase can change sale qualification; do not simply book proceeds as revenue.

## Worked IFRS illustration (simplified)

A seller transfers an asset at supported fair value 1,000,000; previous carrying amount 600,000. The present value of qualifying leaseback payments is 400,000; no off-market adjustment or transaction costs. Retained proportion = 400,000 / 1,000,000 = 40%; initial ROU = 600,000 × 40% = 240,000. Total economic gain before leaseback = 400,000; gain on transferred 60% rights = 240,000. Illustrative seller entry: Dr cash 1,000,000; Dr ROU asset 240,000; Cr original PPE 600,000; Cr lease liability 400,000; Cr gain 240,000. This is a decision-route illustration, not a generic template for other frameworks or variable payments; verify the initial and subsequent model against the actual contract.

## Reconciliation, controls and disclosures

Liability bridge: opening + new leases + effective interest + increases from modification/reassessment + FX - payments - decreases/terminations = closing. ROU bridge separately tracks additions, depreciation, impairment, remeasurement, disposals and FX. Agree opening to audited prior-year ledger, additions to contract population, cash to AP/bank, interest to rate schedule and closing to GL; investigate reconciling items. Tie gain and asset derecognition to disposal ledger and note, lease maturities and variable payment disclosures to signed schedules. Prevent duplicate treatment of ordinary disposal gain, VAT/taxes and leaseback interest. Preserve management sale memo, revenue transfer conclusion, fair-value report, rate evidence, schedule version, journal and independent review.

1. Transfer fails sale test: PASS only if financing route and no PPE derecognition/gain.
2. Qualifying IFRS sale at fair value: PASS if gain limited to transferred right, calculated as above.
3. Off-market consideration: PASS only if adjustment and additional financing/prepayment analyzed.
4. 2026 UK case: PASS only if Periodic Review edition/transition checked independently.
5. Multiple ledgers disagree on liability: FAIL until contract-to-schedule-to-GL-to-note bridge explained.
