## Worked decision and failed branch

A EUR-functional subsidiary has USD 100 receivable booked at EUR 90. Closing rate makes it EUR 95: EUR 5 transaction FX in its earnings. Its translated net assets of EUR 1,000 at group rate 1.10 USD/EUR are USD 1,100. Do not post the EUR 5 again to translation reserve; calculate CTA from opening equity, current earnings, capital movements and rate history. A long-term intragroup loan requires evidence that settlement is neither planned nor likely before net-investment OCI treatment in consolidated statements. Test partial disposal and NCI attribution separately.
