# Revenue disclosure pack — population, bridge and control challenge

Capabilities CAO-03-024/025. Supplements the existing framework and control architecture. No universal four-framework checklist is implied; resolve entity, tier and period first.

## Illustrative disaggregation and tie-out

The 2026 consolidated GL records revenue of 1,500. Validated contract-revenue subledger shows subscription 900, usage 450 and implementation 100, total 1,450. A separately approved 50 manual true-up for a migration cohort explains the difference: 900+450+100+50=1,500. Allocate the 50 to an appropriate disaggregation category only after tracing contract and performance evidence; do not present an unexplained fourth bucket called “manual adjustments.” Compare disaggregation with external segment narrative and economic risk drivers.

**Executed:** revenue subledger 1,450; GL 1,500; approved bridge 50; unmatched zero if cohort evidence passes. If the true-up is unsupported, disclosure completeness and valuation FAIL despite mathematical tie.

## Contract-balance rollforward

Opening contract liability 400 + advance billings 900 − performance releases 700 − valid refunds 50 = 550 closing. GL is 570. The 20 difference is not silently added to disclosures; identify whether it is an acquisition, FX, reclassification, missed credit or error. Contract-asset rollforward must separately track performance creation, conversion to unconditional AR, impairment, modifications and FX. **Executed:** 400+900−700−50=550; 570−550=20 exception. FAIL until supported resolution.

## Disclosure control design

Preparer obtains the population of all reporting entities and revenue streams, documented exclusions, relevant contract-balance movements, contract-cost assets, remaining obligations, significant judgments and management-approved policy decisions. Reviewer checks completeness against GL account map, legal-entity consolidation, acquired contracts, disclosure checklist and narrative consistency. A review threshold such as the lesser of a risk-calibrated amount and a percentage of total revenue must be set in an entity-specific RCM; no arbitrary universal threshold is prescribed. Retain data extracts, query versions, reconciliation, true-up memo, contract evidence, disclosure draft/final diff, reviewer questions and sign-off.
