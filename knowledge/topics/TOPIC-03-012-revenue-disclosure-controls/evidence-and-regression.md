# TOPIC-03-012 — evidence and adverse regression

Claims: [TOPIC-03-012-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-03-012-UK-001], [TOPIC-03-012-US-001], [TOPIC-03-012-AASB-001]. Existing substantive pack is retained. Capability mappings: CAO-03-024, CAO-03-025.

## Executed method and boundary case

A 1,200 service contract has 300 revenue recognized and 900 unsatisfied at year-end. Reconcile 900 to the obligation ledger before testing RPO practical expedients; a 900 invoice balance alone does not prove RPO. If 200 is an acquired contract omitted from legacy CRM, a GL tie-out can pass while the RPO disclosure is incomplete. Independently compare acquisition deal schedules, billing population and performance ledger. UK small-entity relief and US nonpublic exemptions must be assessed by framework rather than copied from IFRS.

## Source and period gate

The cited IFRS paragraph range is from the identified issued edition; the source claim is limited to that route. UK current FRS 102 edition and 2026 amendments, US operative ASC and public/private distinctions, and AASB operative compilation and entity tier must each be independently checked. Preserve contracts, assumptions, calculation version, journal support, disclosure ties and sign-off; the four framework claims are linked to this adverse test. Source audit is separate from substantive method review.
