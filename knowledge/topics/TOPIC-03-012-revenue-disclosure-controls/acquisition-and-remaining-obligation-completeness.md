# Revenue disclosure: acquired contracts and remaining obligations

**Population check.** Consolidated revenue 1,500 includes manual true-up 50 atop contract subledger 1,450 in retained case. During acquisition, 120 of current revenue comes from acquired contracts held in a separate billing system. If the 1,450 subledger already includes that 120, do not add it again; prove by contract IDs and acquisition-date cutover. If it omits the 120, the presented 1,500 cannot be reconciled by simply appending 120: investigate whether the 50 manual true-up, other subledger items or consolidated revenue are misstated. Rebuild gross-to-net population and segment/financial statement bridge from contract facts. Distinguish pre-acquisition target revenue excluded from group results; legal invoice dates alone do not establish group revenue.

**Remaining performance obligations.** For an illustrative fixed-price three-year service contract with 900 total consideration, 300 recognized in year one, unrecognized nominal allocation 600. Before disclosing 600, assess variable consideration constraint, qualifying practical expedients, cancellations, renewals, contract duration and expected timing buckets under the applicable IFRS 15/ASC 606/AASB 15/FRS 102 requirements. A backlog sales dashboard is not automatically the RPO disclosure population. Reconcile opening contract liabilities, billings, releases and closing balances to note, and judgment/estimate descriptions to technical workpapers. UK FRS 102 revised Section 23, principally 2026, has proportionate disclosure rules; an IFRS checklist cannot be copied wholesale. AASB Tier 2 disclosure route separately.

**Control/evidence.** Fail “manual adjustment” as a permanent disaggregation bucket, acquired contract population omitted despite 1,500 tie, or 600 RPO posted without cancellation analysis. Retain acquired billing exports, migration map, contract IDs, standalone prices/allocation, recognized schedule, segment external reporting, disclosure checklist by framework/version and independent reviewer. [IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/), FASB ASC 606 and AASB 15 need operative paragraph/tier verification before release.
