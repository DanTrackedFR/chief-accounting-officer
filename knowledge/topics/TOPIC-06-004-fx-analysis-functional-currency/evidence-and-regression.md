## Worked decision and failed branch

USD sales 70% and EUR payroll/cost 80% are mixed signals. Document who determines prices, competitive environment, local autonomy and where operating cash is retained before weighing USD versus EUR; no vote-counting. If the business genuinely shifts to USD pricing and USD costs on 1 July, change functional currency prospectively from that date after evidence review, not from 1 January to smooth FX. Reconcile each GL FX line to opening exposure, transactions, settlements, closing exposure and source rates; unexplained residual remains exception.

## Review gate

Document contract/rate inputs, date/version, journal, reconciling item, alternatives, independent review and disclosure tie-out. The IFRS claim uses the specified issued edition. For US, UK and Australia, repeat scope, recognition, measurement, presentation, disclosure, election and transition tests under the actual reporting-period text. Current ASC operative paragraph has not been inspected. Preserve unresolved differences in the audit queue.
