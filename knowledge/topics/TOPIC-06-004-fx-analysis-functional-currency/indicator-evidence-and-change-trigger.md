# Mixed functional-currency indicators and prospective change

**Evidence weights.** An entity based in the Netherlands sells a commodity globally with USD quoted prices set by a USD market, pays 70% of wages/inputs in EUR, draws USD debt from parent and retains surplus cash in EUR. IAS 21 focuses first on currency mainly influencing sales prices and currency mainly influencing labour/material costs. The USD sales and EUR cost primary indicators conflict; funding and retained-cash indicators support but do not automatically override primary factors. Obtain multi-year contract currencies, price-setting mechanism, competitive market evidence, major supplier/payroll data, funding terms, cash flows and management forecasts. Record explicit reasoning and alternative EUR/USD outcome; jurisdiction, invoice denomination and parent's desired reporting currency do not determine it. UK FRS 102 Section 30, AASB 121 and US ASC 830 must be evaluated independently if those bases apply.

**Change event.** On 1 July X2 entity moves production and sales-price setting to a EUR market, with new contracts and labour inputs in EUR. Reassess whether underlying transactions/events actually changed and, if so, determine date and prospective translation of items into the newly selected functional currency under operative framework. Do not retrospectively switch X1 ledgers to improve EBITDA or treat a simple parent reporting-currency change as a subsidiary functional-currency change. Document interim cutover and rates, policy approved by CAO, comparative presentation and consolidation CTA implications. If only treasury refinancing switches from USD to EUR while operations remain unchanged, a functional-currency change is not automatically justified.

**Analytics.** Earlier case forecasts FX loss 20 but GL shows 35, difference 15. Decompose by opening monetary items, new invoices, settlements, rate changes, bank balances, consolidation journals and rounding, with unique IDs; escalate if none explains 15. Fail a balancing plug or a functional-currency switch based on this variance alone. Evidence: factor scoring with source data and rebuttals, rate feed control, change-date memo, prospectively remeasured balances, group approval. Official [IAS 21](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/); AASB 121 current compilation and ASC 830 detailed text are entity/source gated.
