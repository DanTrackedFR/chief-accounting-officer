# Suspense, clearing and cut-off — decision workpaper

Extends existing README/factory/knowledge packs and preserves alternate `suspense-cutoff` folder. Capabilities CAO-02-013–014. PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE with underlying framework routing; 2026-09-27.

## Cut-off test

Select populations spanning period end from invoices, receipts, shipments, service logs, bank feeds, payroll, subledgers and GL. Compare economic event/contractual rights and obligations to document date, approval date, system create date and posting date; none of these dates alone determines recognition across all transaction types. For an unbilled item, identify the correct transaction-specific framework and measure from reliable evidence. Assess magnitude both individually and in aggregate; route prior-period discoveries to TOPIC-02-009.

**Illustration:** warehouse confirms goods received 29 December for 25,000; invoice received 3 January; the 31 December GL lacks the receipt/accrual. Assuming control of goods and terms support recognition, debit inventory 25,000 / credit accrued payable 25,000 at 31 December, then clear against AP invoice with no duplicate expense. But if goods were merely in transit under seller-controlled terms, recognition may differ; obtain contract and shipping facts before entry. Substantive inventory terms belong to the asset topic; this case tests the operating cutoff workflow, not a universal IFRS/US/UK/AASB conclusion.

## Clearing register

Maintain gross item ID, source/destination, entity, account, currency, expected settlement, opened date, age, owner, evidence, subsequent events and permitted action. Reconcile every source posting to a target posting and explain timing. A 25,000 item aged 90 days is not routinely “timing”; trace whether duplicate, missing destination, wrong mapping or unsupported balance. A clearing account is not a reserve for uncertainty. Escalate unresolved balances under the account-certification risk policy and prohibit unsupported write-offs or netting.

**Failure injection:** source and GL totals match after +25,000 and −25,000 unmatched items offset. Expected FAIL; gross transaction IDs and aging reveal both, and the account cannot be certified clean. Result PASS for route. Evidence includes receipt/contract, source extract, journal IDs, clearing rollforward, reviewer and follow-up. IFRS 18 presentation changes effective from 2027 may affect later reporting, but the specific recognition conclusion remains in the underlying standard. Source: [IFRS 18 official overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/), checked 2026-09-27.
