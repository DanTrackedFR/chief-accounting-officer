# Cutoff and suspense authority gate

Map the transaction to actual recognition guidance (inventory, revenue, expense/liability or asset) for the entity framework and period. Suspense classification is a temporary processing state, never authority to defer recognition. Capture event/service date, delivery/acceptance evidence, invoice date, ledger date, source contract, entity and currency. Reconcile source events through rejected interface items to GL and reverse/correct at the right period with approved journal IDs.

**Worked route.** Goods received 29 December for 12,000; invoice 5 January. If control and obligation passed in December under the relevant purchase terms, a December inventory/expense and accrued payable may be needed, with January invoice clearing, subject to the technical analysis. If goods remained supplier controlled, that entry may be wrong. Prove contract terms and receipt evidence before choosing; a GRNI clearing balance by itself cannot decide. A 12,000 suspense debit with no counterparty becomes an aged exception, not an approved asset.

**Failure injection.** A late invoice was swept to current-month expense despite December delivery evidence. Reconcile December events and January receipts, quantify prior period and materiality, route possible issued-statement error to TOPIC-02-009 and failed cutoff control to 09-007. Retain original and corrected GL versions, approvals and evidence. IFRS, US GAAP, FRS 102 and Australian rules must be selected by transaction and effective period; this operating example does not claim identical recognition rules.
