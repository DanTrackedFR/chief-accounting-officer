# Equity award grant-to-ledger case

Capabilities CAO-05-013/014. Existing substantive README retained. This example assumes an equity-settled award, no market condition, no modification, and a three-year service vesting period; real terms can change classification and measurement.

On grant date, 100 employees each receive 100 options. Supported grant-date fair value is 12 per option. At end of year one, the best-supported estimate is 90 employees ultimately vest. Cumulative compensation cost is 100 options×90 employees×12×1/3 = 36,000. At end of year two, revised expected vesting is 80 employees. Cumulative required cost is 100×80×12×2/3 = 64,000, so year-two expense is 28,000, assuming year-one booked 36,000. At year three, 82 employees actually vest: total cost 98,400 and year-three expense 34,400. Dr share-based compensation expense / Cr equity for each period under the assumed IFRS equity-settled treatment, subject to the grant terms and applicable framework. **Executed:** 36,000+28,000+34,400=98,400; 82×100×12=98,400. PASS arithmetic.

Do not replace grant-date fair value with reporting-date share price for this equity-settled fact pattern. Cash-settled awards and employer/employee settlement choice require a different classification, liability remeasurement and potentially different treatment. IFRS 2/AASB 2, ASC 718 and FRS 102 Section 26 can diverge on forfeiture policy, measurement, modification and tax-withholding details; record the actual award terms before comparison.
