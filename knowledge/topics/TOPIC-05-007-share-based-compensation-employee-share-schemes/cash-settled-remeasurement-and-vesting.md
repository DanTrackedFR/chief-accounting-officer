# Cash-settled share award versus grant-date equity award

**Classify before valuing.** Cash-settled award promises 100 employees cash equal to the value of 100 shares each after two years of service. Assume all ultimately vest and liability fair value at end of year one is 8 per right; at end of year two fair value is 12. Under IFRS 2 cash-settled model, illustrative year-one cumulative service cost/liability is 100×100×8×1/2=40,000. At vesting, liability 100×100×12=120,000, giving year-two expense 80,000 before settlement. Cash settlement 120,000 clears liability, not equity. This differs from retained equity-settled grant-date-fair-value example; do not mix 40,000 with the 36,000 equity-award opening case or treat a reporting-date rise as equity OCI. Actual valuation must incorporate vesting/non-vesting conditions and fair-value terms under operative standard.

**Fork.** If employer can choose cash or equity, read exact contractual and constructive obligations; a net-settlement withholding feature can trigger specific IFRS 2 classification guidance rather than a naive all-cash answer. An entity receiving employee services while another group entity settles also requires group arrangement analysis. US ASC 718 and FRS 102 Section 26 may differ in award classification/forfeiture and UK period; AASB 2 uses its operative version. US ASU 2024-01 scope clarification for profits-interest awards applies for relevant nonpublic fiscal years beginning after 15 December 2025; do not apply it to every ordinary option.

**QA/evidence.** Test 40,000+80,000=120,000 and settlement liability zero. Fail equity credit for cash-only promise, failure to remeasure at year two, or using US/UK framework as identical. Keep board award/employee agreements, vesting census, fair-value models/input history, payroll tax, liability rollforward, cap-table interaction and reviewer. Official [IFRS 2](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-2-share-based-payment/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/), FASB ASC 718/ASU 2024-01 subject to current source verification.
