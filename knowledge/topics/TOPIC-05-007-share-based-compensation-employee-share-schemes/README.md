# TOPIC-05-007 — Share-Based Compensation & Employee Share Schemes

Status: REVIEWED / US+UK SOURCE-DEPTH PARTIAL
Capabilities: CAO-05-013, CAO-05-014
Knowledge: STANDARDS + PRACTICE + DIFFERENCES
Framework sensitivity: HIGH
Source check: 2026-09-23

## Objective
Classify, measure and recognize share-based compensation and employee share schemes over the appropriate service/performance period, with controlled treatment of vesting conditions, forfeitures, modifications, settlement and group arrangements.

## Official source map
IFRS: IFRS 2 Share-based Payment. IFRS Foundation confirms it covers transactions settled in cash, other assets or equity instruments and requires their effects, including employee option expense, in the financial statements.
US GAAP: ASC Topic 718 Compensation—Stock Compensation. Current FASB updates include ASU 2024-01 on profits-interest/similar awards; for nonpublic entities that amendment is effective for annual periods beginning after 15 December 2025. Current-period scope analysis must therefore include the clarified illustrative guidance.
UK GAAP: FRS 102 Section 26 Share-based Payment, using the current Periodic Review 2024 edition/effective-period routing. Detailed paragraph-level verification remains PARTIAL in this record.
AASB: AASB 2 Share-based Payment. AASB's current-version register shows the December 2021 compilation applies to periods beginning on/after 1 January 2022 and before 1 January 2027; AASB 18 consequential amendments apply later depending on entity type.

## CAO workflow
1. Resolve framework, entity, reporting period and award population.
2. Obtain plan rules, grant agreements, board approvals, cap table and employee/participant data.
3. Determine whether arrangement is within share-based-payment scope; distinguish employee/nonemployee, owner distribution, profit-sharing/cash bonus, customer consideration and business-combination consideration.
4. Determine settlement classification: equity, cash/liability or compound/choice arrangement under applicable framework.
5. Establish grant/measurement date and valuation basis.
6. Identify service, market, performance and non-vesting conditions and how each affects valuation versus vesting recognition.
7. Determine requisite/service period and tranche attribution method required/permitted by framework.
8. Build award-level expense schedule, forfeiture treatment and equity/liability rollforward.
9. Reassess liability-classified awards at required dates; preserve grant-date model for equity awards subject to modification/cancellation rules.
10. Route modifications/cancellations/settlements to TOPIC-05-008.
11. Reconcile HR/equity platform, valuation model, payroll/tax-withholding data, cap table and GL.
12. Prepare disclosures and judgment/valuation support.

## IFRS / AASB core
Equity-settled employee awards are generally measured using grant-date fair value of equity instruments and recognized as services are received, with condition-specific treatment. Cash-settled awards create a liability remeasured to fair value through settlement. IFRS 2's 2016 amendments clarified vesting/non-vesting effects for cash-settled awards, net settlement for withholding tax, and cash-to-equity modification; effective from 1 January 2018. AASB 2 follows the IFRS architecture, subject to Australian version/tier/entity checks.

## US GAAP core / differences
ASC 718 has a similar broad objective but important differences exist in classification, attribution, forfeiture policy, tax effects, modification accounting and private-company alternatives. Do not port IFRS 2 calculations into US GAAP. Profits interests and similar awards require current scope analysis under ASU 2024-01. Full current Codification paragraph-body verification remains a recorded programme limitation.

## UK GAAP
FRS 102 Section 26 provides the UK GAAP share-based-payment model. It is not assumed identical to either IFRS 2 or ASC 718. Current Section 26, entity eligibility and Periodic Review effective date must be checked for material conclusions.

## Measurement inputs
Share price; exercise price; expected term; volatility; risk-free rate; expected dividends; vesting/service/performance conditions; employee turnover/forfeiture evidence; award quantity; grant/approval dates; settlement features; tax-withholding terms. Complex option valuation may require valuation-specialist input, but CAO owns accounting scope, assumptions challenge, model-to-GL reconciliation and documentation.

## Journal patterns
Equity award during service: Dr compensation expense (or qualifying asset where another standard requires capitalization) / Cr equity.
Cash-settled award: Dr compensation expense / Cr liability, with remeasurement through settlement.
Exact entries depend on framework, award, capitalization and tax facts.

## Controls / audit
Complete grant population; board approval-to-system reconciliation; award master-data validation; valuation-model governance; assumption approval; service/performance-condition evidence; leaver/forfeiture completeness; modification trigger; payroll/cap-table/GL reconciliation; privileged-access control; disclosure tie-out.

## Systems/data
Award ID, participant, entity, grant/approval date, instrument, units, exercise price, vesting tranches, conditions, classification, valuation model/inputs, service period, forfeiture status, modification history, settlement, payroll/tax data, GL/equity mapping and evidence link.

## Artifacts
SBC accounting memo; award inventory; valuation instruction/review; expense schedule; equity/liability rollforward; modification register; reconciliation; disclosure pack.

## Scenarios
1. Employee options vest over four years: determine grant date, conditions, valuation and attribution under framework.
2. Cash-settled phantom shares: liability model and recurring remeasurement.
3. Profits-interest award at US private company in 2026: apply current Topic 718 scope guidance including ASU 2024-01.
4. Award contains market target plus service condition: separate valuation effect from service recognition logic.
5. Net share settlement for statutory withholding under IFRS: apply IFRS 2 net-settlement guidance rather than automatically splitting cash/equity.
6. Parent grants parent shares to subsidiary employees: route group share-based-payment requirements and intercompany accounting.
7. Australian Tier 2 entity: use current AASB 2 plus applicable AASB 1060 disclosure regime.
8. UK reporter: use current Section 26; do not infer treatment from IFRS 2 merely because concepts are similar.
