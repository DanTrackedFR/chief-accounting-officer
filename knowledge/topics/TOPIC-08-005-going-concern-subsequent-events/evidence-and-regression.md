# TOPIC-08-005 — reporting evidence and adverse route

Claims: [TOPIC-08-005-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-08-005-US-001], [TOPIC-08-005-UK-001], [TOPIC-08-005-AASB-001]. Retain existing reporting pack.

## Worked case

A major customer insolvency in January may confirm impairment of a 31 December receivable if distress existed then; a new January fire is ordinarily non-adjusting but may need disclosure. If a post-period loss of financing makes liquidation unavoidable before accounts authorization, reassess going-concern basis rather than treating it as a routine non-adjusting note. Separate covenant classification at 31 December from liquidity forecasts, mitigation, material uncertainty and authorization-date evidence. US ASC 855/205-40 has a different evaluation horizon and issuer facts, while UK §32 and AASB 110 require separate routes.

## Cross-framework and control gate

Select reporting period, entity filing tier and applicable edition before conclusion. US public/private disclosures and SEC route, UK small entity/FRS 102 period, and AASB Tier 1/Tier 2 and NFP must be tested independently. Reconcile source GL, consolidation, note tables, comparatives and approval version with named reviewer. An inaccessible IAS 1 operative paragraph is model-derived, not source-verified. Every unverified claim remains in the audit queue.
