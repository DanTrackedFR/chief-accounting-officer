# Going-concern and subsequent-event financing case

Capabilities per manifest CAO-08-009/010. Existing Leases-depth-like vertical slice retained, including standards/differences/workflow/tests. This case adds a cross-topic debt covenant challenge rather than replacing that work.

At 31 December an entity breaches a covenant, and lender grants waiver 15 February before 31 March authorization. Obtain agreement, waiver's effective scope/date and legal rights at reporting date. Under applicable presentation guidance, a later waiver does not automatically make the December liability non-current. For going concern, however, the waiver and forecasts may be relevant evidence available before authorization. Distinguish classification at reporting date, subsequent-event adjustment/disclosure, and going-concern basis/material uncertainty—three different decisions. If breach arose only from a January event, analyze whether non-adjusting with disclosure rather than rewriting year-end numbers. **Scenario outcome:** no automatic classification cure; separate liquidity forecast stress test and note approval. PASS routing, actual outcome conditional.

Review 13-week cash and 12-month-plus forecast, downside assumptions, facility headroom, refinancing evidence and parent support enforceability; do not replace stress testing with one management assertion.
