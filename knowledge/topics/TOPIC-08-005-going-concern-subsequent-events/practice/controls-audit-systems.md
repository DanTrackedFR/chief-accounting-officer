# Practice — Controls, Audit, Systems & Documentation

## Required vs world-class
Required accounting determines basis, recognition/adjustment and disclosures. A formal event register, covenant dashboard, board certification and automated data feeds are CAO practice/control design rather than standards-prescribed mechanics unless local regulation requires them.

## Core controls
- Formal subsequent-event cut-off and owner through authorization.
- Legal, treasury, sales, HR, tax and operations certifications for material post-period events.
- Board/minutes and legal-claims review.
- Post-close journal scan and unusual-transaction review.
- Covenant/refinancing status review tied to reporting-date classification and going concern.
- Forecast-to-actual back-testing and sensitivity review.
- Management-plan evidence and approval.
- Disclosure checklist/tie-out and authorization-date control.
- Reassessment immediately before authorization.

## Audit evidence
Approved forecasts; cash model; financing agreements; lender letters; covenant calculations; support letters and enforceability evidence where relevant; board minutes; legal letters; post-period bank statements/management accounts; event register; sensitivity outputs; disclosure drafts; management representation support.

## Systems/data
Event register should retain event ID, entity, reporting period, occurrence/discovery dates, source system/document, affected accounts, condition-origin date, framework conclusion, amount/range, disclosure status, going-concern impact, owner/reviewer and evidence links.

## Failure modes
Treating every post-period event as non-adjusting because it occurred later; using later favorable financing to rewrite a period-end condition improperly; failing to update disclosure for better evidence; stopping event review at audit fieldwork rather than authorization; using an optimistic forecast without executable funding; applying IFRS wording to ASC 205-40; overlooking local filing/entity requirements.

## TrackedFR fit
Potential fit when recurring subsequent-event or going-concern support requires reconciliation/manipulation across ERP, bank, debt/covenant, billing/AR and forecast datasets in Excel. Not a fit merely because management prepares a memo in Excel/Word.
