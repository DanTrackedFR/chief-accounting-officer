# Subsequent-Event Log Method

Status: REVIEWED
Checked: 2026-09-22

## Objective
Create a complete, reviewable population of events from reporting date through the applicable authorisation/issuance cut-off and drive each item to an accounting/disclosure conclusion.

## Required fields
Event ID; entity; reporting date; event date; date learned; source; owner; description; amount/range; affected accounts/disclosures; evidence of reporting-date condition; evidence of later-arising condition; framework; adjusting/recognized conclusion; journal required; disclosure required; going-concern impact; materiality; related technical topic; evidence links; preparer; reviewer; status.

## Population procedures
Use multiple independent feeds rather than relying on management recollection: board/committee minutes; legal claims; covenant/refinancing correspondence; bank statements and large cash movements; material customer/vendor failures; credit notes and returns; insurance events; payroll/restructuring actions; M&A/equity/debt activity; asset sales/purchases; tax/regulatory notices; cyber/operational incidents; post-close journals; forecast/liquidity changes.

## Decision sequence
1. Is event inside framework-specific evaluation window?
2. What condition is relevant to the accounting balance/disclosure?
3. Did that condition exist at reporting date? Separate later evidence from later causation.
4. If yes, determine which period-end estimate/recognition/disclosure is updated.
5. If no, determine material non-adjusting/nonrecognized disclosure.
6. Independently assess whether event changes going-concern basis or uncertainty disclosure.
7. Route to specialist topic where event changes another accounting conclusion (ECL, impairment, provisions, debt, combinations, restructuring, tax, etc.).
8. Close only when entry/disclosure/evidence/review is complete.

## CAO challenge prompts
- What exactly existed at reporting date?
- What new fact did the later event reveal rather than create?
- Would a market participant / management estimate at reporting date have incorporated the underlying condition?
- Is the event being labelled non-adjusting merely to avoid reopening numbers?
- Conversely, are hindsight outcomes being incorrectly pushed back into a period-end estimate?
- Does aggregation make individually small events material?
- Does this event contradict the going-concern forecast or covenant assumptions?

## Control design
Event-log owner sends structured certification to legal, treasury, commercial, HR, tax and corporate-development owners; controller performs completeness analytics; CAO/reviewer approves material conclusions; financial statements cannot be authorised until open material items are resolved or explicitly escalated.
