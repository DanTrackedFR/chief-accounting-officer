# TOPIC-08-005 — Going Concern & Subsequent Events

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Primary domain: 08 Financial Reporting
Capabilities: CAO-08-009, CAO-08-010
Checked: 2026-09-22

## Objective
Enable the CAO to determine the financial-reporting consequences of events and conditions affecting going concern and events occurring after period end through authorization/issuance.

## Core case graph
1. Resolve framework, entity, reporting period, authorization/issuance date and applicable jurisdiction.
2. Establish going-concern assessment horizon required by framework/regulation and management's plans.
3. Identify adverse conditions, liquidity/funding dependencies, covenant/refinancing facts and post-balance-sheet evidence.
4. Determine whether going-concern basis remains appropriate.
5. Determine whether material uncertainty/substantial doubt and related disclosures are required under the applicable framework.
6. Inventory post-period events through the authorization/issuance cut-off.
7. Classify each event as adjusting/recognized or non-adjusting/nonrecognized under the applicable framework.
8. Update measurements/disclosures where the event evidences period-end conditions; otherwise assess material disclosure.
9. Revisit going concern regardless of ordinary adjusting/non-adjusting classification where post-period events make the basis inappropriate.
10. Produce assessment paper, event log, disclosure support, controls and audit evidence.
