# Framework Differences — Going Concern & Subsequent Events

| Issue | IFRS | US GAAP | FRS 102 | AASB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Event terminology | Adjusting / non-adjusting | Recognized / nonrecognized | Adjusting / non-adjusting | Adjusting / non-adjusting |
| Event window | Reporting date to authorization for issue | Balance-sheet date to issuance or availability-to-be-issued depending entity | Period end to authorization under UK framework/governance | Reporting date to authorization for issue |
| Going-concern trigger language | Going-concern basis inappropriate if liquidation/cessation intended or no realistic alternative; material-uncertainty disclosure model | ASC 205-40 substantial-doubt model, including evaluation of management plans | FRS 102 + UK/FRC going-concern requirements | IAS-aligned core plus Australian requirements |
| Later evidence about period-end condition | Adjust measurement/disclosure | Recognized subsequent event | Adjust | Adjust |
| New condition after period end | No measurement adjustment; material disclosure | Nonrecognized; material disclosure | No adjustment; material disclosure | No adjustment; material disclosure |
| Going concern becomes inappropriate after period end | Fundamental basis change, not ordinary non-adjusting treatment | Apply ASC 205-40 and applicable basis/liquidation literature | Section 32 UK-specific going-concern provisions + Section 3 | AASB 110 requires basis change |
| Jurisdiction overlay | Listing/local law can add filing requirements | SEC/public-company overlay can be significant | Companies Act/entity regime significant | Corporations Act/ASIC/entity tier significant |

## CAO rule
Never translate terminology mechanically. First determine the economic fact and timing, then apply the framework's recognition, basis-of-preparation and disclosure model. A refinancing, covenant waiver, insolvency event or major customer loss can have different accounting consequences depending on what condition existed at period end and whether it changes the going-concern conclusion.
