# TOPIC-07-004 — claim-linked group accounting case

Claims: [TOPIC-07-004-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-07-004-US-001], [TOPIC-07-004-UK-001], [TOPIC-07-004-AASB-001]. Retain existing factory.

## Calculation and adverse route

Parent sells inventory to subsidiary for 100 at parent cost 80; subsidiary retains 40% at year end. Eliminate intragroup revenue/purchases 100, and unrealised group profit 8 (=20×40%) from closing inventory and group profit. If the transfer is upstream, allocate profit elimination/NCI in line with ownership; if downstream, do not charge subsidiary NCI mechanically. For depreciable fixed assets, reverse unrealised gain and restate future depreciation, with temporary differences assessed. Match both counterparties, currency and period before consolidation journal.

## Evidence and framework regression

Prepare signed rights/ownership memo, consolidated journal with entity and counterparty IDs, source rate or valuation, reserve/NCI rollforward, tax bridge and disclosure tie-out. Test the opposite classification under US ASC 810/323/805, UK FRS 102 and AASB before asserting convergence. Select effective period and reporting tier separately. These four claim IDs remain the source-audit queue; IFRS core route uses 2026 issued text.
