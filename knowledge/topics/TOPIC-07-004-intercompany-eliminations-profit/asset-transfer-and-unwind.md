# Fixed-asset transfer: group basis and later depreciation

**Assumptions.** Parent transfers equipment to an 80%-owned subsidiary for 150; parent's carrying amount was 100 and remaining useful life five years, straight-line, no residual. Subsidiary separately records 150 and annual depreciation 30. Group cancels the intragroup sale/gain 50 and restores equipment group basis to 100 at transfer date; group annual depreciation should be 20, so the consolidation process reverses excess 10 depreciation for each complete year while the asset remains held. After one complete year, subsidiary carrying amount 120 and group carrying amount 80: difference 40 is unamortized unrealized gain, agreeing 50−10. A one-time elimination 50 with no later unwind overstates expense and understates group carrying amount in subsequent years. Partial-year service requires actual in-service date. Disposal to an outsider accelerates release of remaining eliminated profit under applicable group rules.

**Direction and tax.** This is downstream parent-to-subsidiary; if upstream, the unrealized-profit effect can change attribution to NCI. Independently assess deferred tax from differing entity/group bases and tax rates; this no-tax example cannot be posted as complete real-world consolidation. If transferred asset is inventory, use remaining units rather than useful life; if impairment occurs, evaluate group basis and impairment separately. Intercompany receivable/payable and cash must also be reconciled, avoiding double entries from asset and transaction eliminations.

**QA/control.** Transfer price 150, pre-sale carrying 100, gain 50, separate books depreciation 30, group depreciation 20, first-year difference 40: reperform with asset ID, historical cost/depreciation, intercompany invoice, disposal status and group elimination schedule. Fail full 50 profit recognized in group, continuing 30 depreciation, or NCI/tax automatically ignored in a production pack. IFRS 10/AASB 10, ASC 810 and FRS 102 Section 9 route group accounting by applicable period; [IFRS 10 official](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-10-consolidated-financial-statements/) and [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/).
