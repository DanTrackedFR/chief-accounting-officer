# Unrealized group-profit elimination case

Capabilities CAO-07-007/008. Existing topic retained. Parent sells inventory to subsidiary for 150, original cost 100. At year end 40% remains unsold to outside customers. Intragroup sale and purchase are eliminated in full, and the unrealized profit in remaining inventory is (150−100)×40%=20. Group inventory should be reduced by 20; group profit reduced 20, with tax/NCI effects separately assessed under the framework and direction of sale. A blanket elimination of the full 50 profit would be wrong because 60% was sold externally. **Executed:** 50×40%=20; realized portion 30. PASS under stated tracing.

When the subsidiary sells upstream to the parent, consider NCI allocation of elimination under applicable consolidation rules; downstream and upstream need not have identical NCI consequences. Fixed-asset transfers require reversal of unrealized gain and adjustment of later depreciation over remaining life, not a one-period inventory entry. Intercompany receivables/payables, dividends and financing interest are separate eliminations; prevent double entry. Retain transaction IDs, cost, external sales evidence, inventory batch, ownership percentage, elimination journal, deferred tax assessment and next-period unwind. [IFRS 10](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-10-consolidated-financial-statements/) official overview checked 2026-09-27; ASC 810, FRS 102 Section 9 and AASB 10 specific NCI/tax mapping remains PARTIAL.
