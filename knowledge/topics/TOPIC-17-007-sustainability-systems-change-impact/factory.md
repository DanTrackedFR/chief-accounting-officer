# Sustainability systems requirements and change-impact assessment — substantive factory

Companion to prior README; capabilities CAO-17-014/015. Source checked 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for data/system architecture and change-control method.

## Functional and non-functional requirements

Translate the governing disclosure taxonomy into data requirements rather than buying a generic ESG dashboard. Ingest legal entities and sites, operational-control/equity boundary attributes, meter/fuel/travel/supplier activity, units, source dates, factor versions, categories, targets, narratives and financial accounting references. Systems must preserve time-specific boundary history, original source documents, transformations and adjustments, reviewer identity, effective versus learned dates, and immutable reporting snapshots. Require access controls, segregation between factor/master-data changes and publication approval, repeatable calculations, exception queues, audit export and controlled API/spreadsheet interfaces. [IFRS S1/S2](https://www.ifrs.org/sustainability/knowledge-hub/introduction-to-issb-and-ifrs-sustainability-disclosure-standards/) provide a reporting architecture, while [AASB S2](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-s2-sep-2024), [EU CSRD/ESRS](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/financial-markets/company-reporting-and-auditing/company-reporting/corporate-sustainability-reporting_en) and [UK SRS](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/uk-sustainability-reporting-standards) require independent applicability/format mapping. No single field represents all jurisdictional definitions.

## Event and lineage model

Key tables: `entity`, `facility`, `ownership/control interval`, `activity event`, `invoice/meter source`, `factor_version`, `calculation_run`, `exception`, `disclosure_version`. Each activity retains quantity/unit and event period; factors retain original publisher, geography, validity period, GWP/method and approval; calculation runs retain code/config hash and input snapshot. A late meter, acquired facility or restated factor creates a new run and explains current versus previously published amounts, never overwriting the historic run. Link reconciliation of site population to financial fixed-asset/lease/AP systems but permit justified differences. The entity's reporting year and local transition relief are parameters, not permanently hard-coded global flags.

## Worked migration and test design

A facility acquired on 1 April records Q1 consumption in the seller's meter feed, while ERP creates the entity in April. A financial-consolidation, operational-control and GHG boundary decision determines whether Q1 belongs in the acquiring group's metric. The system should represent the control interval and exclude/include Q1 under an explicit rule with traceability; an ERP-only query silently dropping Q1 is not a valid conclusion. A new directive changes scope: preserve old and new rules in parallel, calculate coverage and totals for a representative period, reconcile population, quantify impact, update disclosures and approve cutover.

## Controls and scenarios

Specifications are reviewed by sustainability, accounting, IT/security and assurance stakeholders. Test completeness, mapping uniqueness, factor/units, effective dates, organization changes, duplicate feeds, estimates, restatements, spreadsheet overrides, final published-number reproduction and disaster recovery. S1: factor overwritten in place → FAIL historical reproducibility. S2: acquired facility appears twice through vendor and meter feeds → FAIL deduplication. S3: revised rule deployed before effective period → FAIL rule version. S4: parallel run reconciles entity/site population and metric differences with signed approval → PASS. S5: GL agrees but missing landlord-paid energy → FAIL completeness. Outputs: data dictionary, source-to-disclosure map, test suite, parallel-run bridge, change approvals and signed production release. Dependencies 11-002/003, 17-001/003/006. Residual: requirements must be reparameterized for the entity's adopted regime; architecture is not a claim of legal applicability.
