# PBC intake and evidence contract

## Engagement route and decision
Request register fields: auditor request ID, assertion, entity, period, due date, owner, ledger/report source, version, reviewer and confidentiality level. Resolve accounting basis and engagement standard before mapping requests. A PCAOB engagement may invoke AS 1105/1215; non-PCAOB engagements route to the adopted ISA/local auditing standards and auditor instructions. Management owns underlying records and conclusions; auditor owns procedures and sufficiency judgments.

## Control, evidence and data
Reconcile requested account population to signed TB; retain extract query/parameters, source counts, cutoff and transformations. Review each packet for internal consistency and prior-year roll-forward. A late opening balance adjustment needs a revised TB and new packet version, with superseded version withdrawn by logged notice.

## Adverse case and escalation
A PBC upload that has only a screenshot of a trial balance while the supporting ledger was refreshed later fails version integrity. Freeze the exact TB and ledger extract, bridge differences, notify audit lead and log adjusted due date.

## Handoff and authority
Link the evidence packet to the request register and final statement version; route accounting errors to Domain 02, control failures to Domain 09 and systems/data gaps to Domain 11. Reassess scope and effective dates at each new engagement. References: [PCAOB auditing standards](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards), [IAASB standards and pronouncements](https://www.iaasb.org/standards-pronouncements), [IAASB 2025 Handbook](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/2025-handbook-international-quality-management-auditing-review-other-assurance-and-related-services). These references describe auditor responsibilities; management owns books, support and correction decisions.
