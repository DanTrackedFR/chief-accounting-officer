# TOPIC-02-005 — Reconciliation Governance & Account Certification

Status: REVIEWED
Capabilities: CAO-02-011, CAO-02-012
Knowledge types: PRINCIPLES, PRACTICE
Framework sensitivity: LOW

## Objective
Govern the complete population of balance-sheet reconciliations so certification means a defined accounting assertion has actually been evidenced and reviewed.

## Governance model
Maintain a complete account inventory sourced from the active COA/TB, not a manually curated list. Each in-scope account has entity/book, owner, reviewer, frequency, risk tier, method, due date, materiality/escalation criteria and certification status. New accounts enter governance automatically; closed/dormant accounts are explicitly retired.

Certification is an assertion by the responsible accountant/reviewer that the reconciliation uses appropriate support, material items are explained, required corrections are recorded or tracked, and unresolved exceptions are transparently reported. Certification must not erase open items.

## Risk tiering
Consider balance and activity; complexity; judgment/estimate; fraud exposure; volume; system interfaces; prior issues; manual journals; age of reconciling items; external evidence availability; financial-statement sensitivity. Set frequency and review depth accordingly.

## CAO workflow
1. Reconcile account inventory to COA/TB.
2. Assign risk tier, method, preparer/reviewer and frequency.
3. Define evidence and aging standards by reconciliation type.
4. Monitor preparation/review timeliness and rejected certifications.
5. Aggregate reconciling items across accounts to detect systemic issues.
6. Escalate stale/material/unexplained items and missing reconciliations.
7. Certify close only with transparent exceptions.
8. Trend quality metrics and remediate recurring root causes.

## Metrics
Completion on time; review on time; first-pass acceptance; unreconciled value/count; aging buckets; stale items; unsupported items; post-certification adjustments; recurring reconciling items; accounts without owner; reconciliations using weak support.

## Controls
Automated completeness check from COA/TB; certification lock after approval; reviewer independence; evidence retention; aging/escalation thresholds; late/rejected rec governance; account-owner changes; periodic risk-tier refresh; close-level exception certification.

## Artifacts
Reconciliation policy; account inventory; risk-tier methodology; certification statement; escalation matrix; aging dashboard; exception register; monthly certification pack.

## Tests
A completed reconciliation with unresolved material item is certified only with explicit exception/escalation, not marked clean. A newly created account must enter inventory without waiting for annual refresh. An immaterial balance with high transaction volume can remain high risk. Reviewer sign-off without opening support is not valid evidence of review.

## TrackedFR fit
Strong when governance is supported by recurring reconciliation data from multiple systems and Excel; less relevant when the need is only workflow sign-off.
