# Forecast cash-flow hedge and investment classification dependencies

**Hedge start.** Treasury forecasts a USD 1,000 inventory purchase six months out and takes a forward. Before hedge accounting, demonstrate the eligible highly probable transaction, specific hedged FX risk, contemporaneous designation, risk objective, hedge ratio and effectiveness approach under operative IFRS 9/AASB 9. A signed derivative contract alone is an economic hedge. If valid and fair-value gain 30 with supported effective hedge portion 28 and ineffectiveness 2, illustrative OCI cash-flow hedge reserve increases 28 and P&L gain 2 before tax, with derivative asset 30. If forecast acquisition of a non-financial item occurs, assess basis adjustment/reclassification from the reserve under the actual framework and cost recognition pattern. If forecast transaction is no longer expected, stop hedge accounting and assess immediate reserve release under the relevant rules; if still expected but no longer highly probable, treatment may differ. Do not continue designation merely to avoid P&L volatility.

**Investment scope.** A 30% stake bought for 300 cannot be treated as a trading debt instrument from a treasury tag. Test control (IFRS 10), joint control (IFRS 11), significant influence (IAS 28), then IFRS 9 if outside those scopes. Investee profit 100 gives provisional share 30 only after basis, dividends, OCI, policy, unrealized-profit and impairment adjustments. Hedged risk of the investment itself is another specific designation question, not a shortcut from 30% ownership.

**Framework/QA.** US ASC 815 hedge criteria and ASC 323 investment route are not assumed to match IFRS 9; FRS 102 Sections 12/14 and AASB 9/128 need period-specific conclusion. Negative tests: post-dated designation, OCI 30 when 2 supported ineffectiveness, reserve retained after a transaction is no longer expected without analysis, and 30% equity stake posted as an ordinary debt asset. Evidence: approved forecast, sales/purchase history, hedge designation and effectiveness model, derivative fair value, reserve rollforward, purchase invoice, investment rights/financials and note tie. Official [IFRS 9](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/), [IAS 28](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-28-investments-in-associates-and-joint-ventures/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/).
