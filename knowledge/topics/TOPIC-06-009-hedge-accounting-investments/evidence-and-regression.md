## Executed case

At close derivative gain 12 and hedged cash-flow change 10: effective 10 to cash-flow hedge reserve and excess 2 to P&L under the applicable model. If forecast purchase is no longer expected, remove the reserve to P&L under the applicable discontinuation route; if still expected but hedge relationship ceases, retain until the transaction affects earnings. Do not assume an associate investment qualifies for the chosen risk designation; test the exact eligible risk and group entity.
