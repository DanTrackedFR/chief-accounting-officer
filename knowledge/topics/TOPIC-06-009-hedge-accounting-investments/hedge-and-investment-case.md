# Hedge designation versus economic hedge; investment scope

Capabilities CAO-06-018/019. Both existing 06-009 paths retained. Treasury buys a derivative to hedge highly probable forecast purchases. A derivative gain of 30 and forecast cash-flow adverse change of 28 do not themselves prove hedge accounting eligibility. Obtain contemporaneous designation, eligible hedged item/risk, risk-management objective, effectiveness method and ongoing assessment under the selected IFRS 9/AASB 9, ASC 815 or FRS 102 Section 12 framework. If the qualifying IFRS cash-flow hedge assessment and measurement establish effective portion 28 and ineffectiveness 2, the illustrative current change could allocate 28 to the cash-flow hedge reserve and 2 to P&L, subject to detailed rules and timing. Without designation, route the 30 derivative change under default derivative accounting; do not retrospectively create a hedge memo.

**Executed:** 30−28=2; PASS arithmetic, hedge qualification explicitly unresolved. On the forecast purchase, document basis adjustment/reclassification treatment under the applicable framework rather than releasing the reserve mechanically. A 30% voting investment with substantive influence is not simply an IFRS 9 traded security; assess IAS 28/ASC 323/FRS 102 Section 14/AASB 128, and control/joint arrangement first. Cost 300 and 30% share of post-acquisition profit 100 gives illustrative equity-method increase 30 before dividends, impairments, basis differences and upstream profit elimination. **Executed:** 100×30%=30. PASS arithmetic, significant influence fact-dependent.
