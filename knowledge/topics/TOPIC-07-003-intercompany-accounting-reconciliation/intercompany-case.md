# Intercompany two-sided close case

Capabilities CAO-07-005/006. Existing topic retained. Entity A records 100 receivable from B; B records 95 payable to A. Settlement initiated by B for 5 on 31 December arrives A on 2 January. Do not eliminate 100 against 95 and leave 5 unexplained. Pair contract/invoice/payment IDs, legal entities and currencies, then determine ownership and cut-off: if B validly reduced its payable before A recognized cash in transit, align the correct bilateral positions by evidence; otherwise correct premature settlement entry. **Executed:** 100−95=5 exception. FAIL reconciliation until bank and contractual evidence resolve it.

At consolidation, convert both sides to common presentation currency with consistent rates and deal separately with transaction FX; an unmatched FX amount is not necessarily an intercompany principal difference. Govern recharge agreements, transfer pricing/tax interfaces, invoice/counterparty master, period lock, auto-netting rules and aging. Retain A/B ledger extracts, intercompany confirmations, remittance/bank and clearing journal. Framework overlay IAS 21/AASB 121, ASC 830, FRS 102 Section 30 for monetary items and translation; elimination itself is group process. [IFRS 10 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-10-consolidated-financial-statements/) and [IAS 21](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates/) checked 2026-09-27.
