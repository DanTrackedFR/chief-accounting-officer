# Intercompany FX and in-transit resolution

The retained case identifies a 5 difference between Entity A receivable 100 and B payable 95 at period end. Before an elimination, record invoices, currency and remittance IDs, B payment execution date, bank debit date, A receipt date and contractual discharge terms. If B legally discharged 5 on 31 December but A cash was credited in January, analyze cash in transit and the appropriate reporting-date receivable/payable positions in each entity. If B merely initiated a cancellable payment, a premature payable reduction may require reversal. Do not post a group-only balancing 5 and leave local books wrong.

**Separate currency effect.** Assume an independent USD 100 intercompany receivable/payable pair, with A's functional currency USD and B's EUR. At 31 December closing EUR/USD quote is 0.90 EUR per USD. A records USD 100; B's EUR liability is EUR 90 before its IAS 21 remeasurement effects. Group consolidation currency translates both using applicable methods; compare in one common currency rather than asserting 100−90=10 principal difference. Analyze where exchange differences remain in group profit or OCI based on currency, monetary item and any net-investment designation; elimination of principal is not automatic elimination of all FX effects. Verify rate source, rounding, book-date versus settlement-date chronology and net-investment documentation.

**Controls and negative tests.** Pair two sides by stable intercompany ID, entity, document currency and period; confirm unmatched items with both controllers; age and escalate beyond materiality; approve cut-off adjustment locally before group elimination; reconcile bank to clearing. Fail an exception auto-cleared solely because amounts offset with another pair, a USD/EUR unconverted comparison, or a payment-in-transit booked as revenue. IAS 21/AASB 121, FRS 102 Section 30, ASC 830 and IFRS 10/ASC 810 consolidation routes require framework-specific assessment. Official [IAS 21](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates/), [IFRS 10](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-10-consolidated-financial-statements/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/).
