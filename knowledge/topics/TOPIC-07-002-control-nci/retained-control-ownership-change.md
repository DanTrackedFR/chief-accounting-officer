# Purchase of NCI while control is retained

**IFRS example.** Parent owns 80% of a consolidated subsidiary and purchases another 10% for cash 75 while retaining control. NCI carrying amount immediately before purchase is 150 for 20%; assume identical economic rights and no other component. Carrying amount transferred from NCI is 150×10/20=75. Reduce NCI by 75, reduce group cash 75; no difference in owners' equity on these narrow assumptions, no new goodwill and no gain or loss. If cash consideration is 85 instead, reduce NCI 75 and owners' equity 10, not profit. Reconcile group total equity change to cash and the ownership register. Do not assume a 10/20 proportional allocation where preference, put or asymmetric loss-sharing rights change entitlement; establish the legal waterfall and attribution schedule first.

**Control loss fork.** If the same transaction causes loss of control instead of retained control, the above equity-only model is invalid. Resolve derecognition of subsidiary assets/liabilities/NCI, fair value of retained interest, consideration, recycled OCI and gain/loss under the applicable framework. Coordinate with TOPIC-07-009, IFRS 10, IFRS 3 acquisition and IFRS 12 disclosures. US ASC 810, FRS 102 Section 9 and AASB 10 must be checked independently; the IFRS illustration is not a universal cross-framework journal.

**Evidence.** Signed share purchase, ownership/rights before and after, effective date, NCI component rollforward (profit, OCI, dividends, FX), cash bank statement, consolidation JE and equity note. Negative tests: goodwill of 10 on retained control, P&L loss 10, NCI reduction 150 rather than 75, or routing an actual control-loss case through this entry.
