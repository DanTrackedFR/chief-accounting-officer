# NCI rollforward after control assessment

Capabilities CAO-07-003/004. Both existing 07-002 paths retained. Assume valid control and parent ownership of 80% of a subsidiary. Beginning NCI is 100; subsidiary post-acquisition profit attributable to ordinary holders is 200; subsidiary pays dividends of 50 to all holders. Before other movements, NCI share of profit 40 and NCI share of dividends 10, so closing NCI is 130. Eliminate intragroup dividends to parent separately; do not treat the parent's 80% dividend as group revenue. **Executed:** 100+200×20%−50×20%=130. PASS under simple equity rights.

If the parent purchases another 10% while retaining control, analyze equity transaction treatment under IFRS 10/AASB 10; do not automatically recognize a new acquisition gain or goodwill. Under US ASC 810 and FRS 102 Section 9, assess specific current requirements. Preference shares or loss-allocation arrangements can make simple 20% allocation wrong. Reconcile NCI by subsidiary from acquisition opening, profit/OCI, dividends, ownership changes, FX and disposals.
