# TOPIC-03-001 — Revenue Contract Accounting Assessment

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Capabilities: CAO-03-001, CAO-03-002
Checked: 2026-09-22

## Objective
Determine whether an arrangement is in the revenue model, identify the enforceable contract and customer, establish contract combination/portfolio boundaries, and identify the promises/performance obligations that feed the remaining revenue topics.

## Factory scope
PRINCIPLES: contract population, enforceability, customer definition, scope exclusions, combination, collectibility/contract criteria, promises, distinct goods/services, series/bundles, implicit promises.
STANDARDS: IFRS 15; ASC 606; revised FRS 102 Section 23 for 2026+ periods; AASB 15.
PRACTICE: contract intake, legal-to-accounting abstraction, product catalog mapping, controls, audit evidence, systems/data.
DIFFERENCES: convergence is high between IFRS 15/ASC 606/AASB 15 at core-model level, but wording/application differences and UK simplifications/versioning matter.

## Dependencies
Feeds TOPIC-03-002 onward. Interacts with leases, insurance, financial instruments, principal/agent, contract modifications and industry overlays.
