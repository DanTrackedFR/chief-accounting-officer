# Framework Knowledge — Revenue Contract Assessment

Checked: 2026-09-22
Status: REVIEWED

## IFRS 15
IFRS 15 is effective from 1 January 2018. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R019] Its core principle is revenue reflecting transfer of promised goods/services for expected consideration, implemented through the five-step model. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R043] For this topic, the CAO first confirms the arrangement is a contract with a customer in IFRS 15 scope, then identifies performance obligations/promises. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R001] Scope must be separated from leases, insurance, financial instruments and other specifically scoped literature where applicable. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R001]

Contract assessment requires enforceable rights/obligations, commercial substance and the framework's contract criteria, including collectibility assessment. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R002] Contract combination and modification rules prevent artificial contract splitting. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R006] Promises can be explicit or arise from customary business practices/published policies/specific statements. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R007] A promised good/service is separately accounted for when distinct under the standard's two-part distinct analysis; otherwise combine until a distinct bundle is identified. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R006] [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R008] Series guidance can aggregate qualifying repeated distinct services into one performance obligation. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R008] [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R009]

The 2024 IASB PIR concluded IFRS 15 is working as intended, while flagging principal-agent, consideration payable to customers and interactions with other standards for possible future consideration. These are update-monitoring items, not permission to change current accounting.

## US GAAP — ASC 606
Topic 606 was developed jointly with IFRS 15 and shares the core five-step revenue model. Scope applies to contracts with customers except arrangements governed by other Codification topics such as leases/insurance/financial instruments. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R001] The CAO must resolve US-specific scope, collectibility and enforceability using current Codification and applicable legal context. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R002] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R001]

FASB's 2024 PIR concluded the revenue standard is broadly achieving its objective. Post-issuance amendments continue: for example, 2025 guidance addressed share-based consideration payable to a customer. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R015] Therefore source/effective-date monitoring remains mandatory.

Public FASB materials confirm Topic 606's architecture, but full current Codification paragraph-by-paragraph approval remains REFERENCE_ONLY where the public route does not expose the complete text.

## UK GAAP — revised FRS 102 Section 23
The Periodic Review 2024 rewrote Section 23 Revenue from Contracts with Customers, generally effective for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2026. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R017] FRC Factsheet 10 confirms a single comprehensive five-step model.

Verified official FRC mapping:
- 23.4: five-step model.
- 23.5–23.6: consistent application / portfolio approach when results would not differ materially.
- 23.7: contract criteria before revenue-model accounting. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R002]
- 23.8: reassess only when significant facts/circumstances change after inception criteria are met.
- 23.9–23.10: consideration received before contract criteria are met remains a liability until criteria/revenue conditions are satisfied. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R002] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R043]
- 23.12: combine contracts entered at/near same time with same/related customer when package/single-objective, cross-dependent pricing/performance, or single-performance-obligation conditions apply. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R006]
- 23.17–23.25: performance obligations and distinct assessment. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R008]
- 23.26–23.35: warranties, upfront fees and customer options/material rights. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R010]

FRC explicitly notes revised Section 23 is based on IFRS 15 principles but includes simplifications and policy choices; it is designed to allow, not require, some policies that would meet IFRS 15. Do not treat it as an IFRS clone.

## AASB 15
AASB 15 incorporates IFRS 15 and retains the five-step model. The current AASB material maps contract identification to paragraphs 9–21, performance obligations to 22–30, transaction price to 46–72, allocation to 73–86 and satisfaction to 31–45. [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R042] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R011] AASB includes Australian not-for-profit application material; entity type is therefore required context before blindly inheriting a for-profit IFRS conclusion. [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R022]
