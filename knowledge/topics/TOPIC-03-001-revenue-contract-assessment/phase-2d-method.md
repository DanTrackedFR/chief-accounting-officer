# Revenue contract assessment — completed reusable method

Scope: CAO-03-001/002/003. This canonical method incorporates the useful promise and transaction-price material in all three retry folders. The retry folders remain supporting records, not additional topics. Practice recommendations below define an accounting work product; they do not authorize journal posting.

## Scope and accounting contract

Apply the customer revenue model only to ordinary-output exchanges with customers; separate lease, insurance and financial-instrument components under their governing literature before pricing the revenue portion. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R001] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R001] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R001] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R001]
Approval and commitment, identifiable enforceable rights and payment terms, commercial substance and collectible consideration establish the contract gate. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R002] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R002] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R002] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R002]
The US meaning of probable collectibility is more demanding than IFRS's more-likely-than-not interpretation; do not substitute a numeric credit score for either legal/accounting test. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R003] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R003]
Collectibility assesses entitlement after supported concessions, so reduced willingness to pay a disputed price and inability to pay an otherwise valid debt follow different routes. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R004] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R004] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R004] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R004]
If contract criteria fail, retain deposits as liabilities and reassess until criteria or the framework's termination/nonrefundability conditions permit recognition; receipt alone is insufficient. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R005] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R005] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R005] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R005]
Combine near-contemporaneous same/related-customer contracts only when package economics, dependent consideration or one combined obligation establishes the combination test. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R006] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R006] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R006] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R006]

## Promises and price

Identify explicit and customary promises that create customer expectations; activities that do not transfer a service, such as administrative setup, are not themselves separate obligations. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R007] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R007] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R007] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R007]
A distinct obligation requires customer benefit with available resources and separability in the contract context; significant integration, customization or interdependence can require one combined output. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R008] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R008] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R008] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R008]
Use the series unit for substantially similar distinct services with the same qualifying transfer pattern. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R009] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R009] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R009] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R009]
Separate service warranties and incremental material-right options from assurance warranties and ordinary marketing offers. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R010] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R010] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R010] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R010]
Build price from fixed and constrained variable consideration, financing, noncash consideration and payments to customers; amounts collected for another party are excluded. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R011] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R011] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R011] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R011]
A payment to a customer reduces price unless it purchases a distinct item at supportable value; an excess over that value reduces revenue. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R012] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R012] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R012] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R012]
US sales-tax exclusion and post-control shipping/handling policy elections must be documented separately; neither election proves the IFRS treatment. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R013]
The one-year financing expedient depends on expected payment-to-transfer interval, and financing arising for a substantive non-financing reason is assessed separately. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R014] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R014] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R014] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R014]
Noncash value and customer share incentives require their applicable valuation and amendment route, including US share-based-customer guidance, rather than replacing price with invoice face value. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R015] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R015] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R015] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R015]

## Entity, period and transition

For pre-2026 UK periods without early adoption, use the previous Section 23: assess sale risks/rewards and reliable benefit/cost measurement, services by reliable stage of completion, and identifiable components by substance; the new performance-obligation model is not applied retrospectively merely for convenience. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R016]
For UK periods starting from 1 January 2026, revised Section 23 applies unless an authorized earlier adoption already established the transition date. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R017]
UK transition permits full retrospective application or a cumulative opening-equity method for uncompleted contracts without restating comparatives; document completed-contract, variable-price and pre-adoption-modification expedients and the current-period impact disclosures. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R018]
IFRS 15 adoption from 2018 permits full or cumulative-effect retrospective routes with applicable expedients; AASB 15 adoption and Australian entity overlays are independently selected. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R019] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R019]
US original public/private adoption dates and ASU 2020-05 deferrals are historical branches; current adopters retain the selected full/modified retrospective route and assess later amendments independently. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R020]
For an Australian July 2026 start, use the base AASB 15 requirements plus effective uncompiled amendments, including AASB 2022-9's public-sector insurance scope change; no new general for-profit five-step model is assumed. [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R021]
Australian NFP revenue first tests an enforceable agreement and sufficiently specific transfer promises; a broad spending restriction alone does not establish a revenue obligation. [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R022]
An NFP capital transfer to construct its own controlled asset follows AASB 1058's construction obligation, while other nonexchange receipts follow 1058 after recognizing applicable liabilities; statutory public-sector licences have separate AASB 15 guidance. [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R023]
US NFP contributions require the ASC 958 exchange/contribution and condition test rather than forcing them into ASC 606; UK public-benefit nonexchange resources route to Section 34. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R024] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R024]

## Presentation and disclosure

After valid transfer, distinguish conditional contract assets, unconditional receivables and preperformance contract liabilities; principal/agent and refund liabilities are assessed separately. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R025] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R025] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R025] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R025]
IFRS revenue notes cover disaggregation, obligation terms, balances, significant judgments and remaining obligations, with valid short-term/right-to-invoice exemptions documented. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R026]
US public entities apply the full Topic 606 note suite; nonpublic entities may take specified disclosure reliefs, including remaining-obligation and certain judgment/balance details, while retaining required qualitative and quantitative information. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R027]
UK disclosure uses revised Section 23 or old Section 23 by period and Section 1A for eligible small entities, with additional information for true-and-fair presentation when needed. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R028]
Australian Tier 1 applies AASB 15 disclosures; eligible Tier 2 uses AASB 1060's simplified notes while retaining applicable recognition and measurement. [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R029]

## Execution, evidence and system outcome

Workpaper RC-001 records entity/framework/tier, annual start and adoption date, customer versus contributor, signed MSA/orders/side letters, governing-law enforceability, credit/concession evidence, each combination limb, promise list, distinctness/series/warranty/option conclusions and price waterfall. Include evidence that contradicts the preferred answer and route allocation, timing, costs and disclosure to 03-002/003/006/012.

Reviewer control: reconcile the CRM order population to CLM, billing and ERP; certify side letters; require accounting sign-off before a new SKU or promise rule is activated. Sample missing orders as well as posted revenue. Data: stable contract and obligation IDs, amendment lineage, promise type, customer/contributor flag, tier and version dates, price and SSP versions, control event, legal evidence link, reviewer and downstream rule.

Executed illustrations retain the 180 combined-equipment/service/option allocation and the opposite independent-contract/no-right branch. The scenario file additionally tests failed collectibility, NFP funding scope and old/new UK opening balances. Every journal balances; the review verifies the selected accounting route and note consequence as well as arithmetic. Reporting-period authority remains audit-required in the separate claim register; live contractual facts are implementation inputs, not a missing reusable method.

## US customer-share amendment branches

ASU 2025-04 applies for annual periods beginning after 15 December 2026, with early adoption allowed. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R030]
Customer-purchase targets enter its performance-condition definition. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R031]
Customer awards with service conditions require estimated forfeitures. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R032]
Their vesting assessment follows Topic 718, without Topic 606's variable-consideration constraint. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R033]
Select modified retrospective opening equity without recast, or full retrospective comparative recast using the amendment's outcome rules. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R034]

ASU 2025-07 keeps customer share consideration in Topic 606 until the receipt/retention right becomes unconditional; thereafter apply the financial-instrument literature. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R035]
It applies for annual starts after 15 December 2026; early adoption of this share-consideration change also requires the related derivatives change. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R036]

RC-001 records payable versus receivable customer shares separately, amendment adoption and transition population. Do not load either 2027 default into an unadopted 2026 rule version.

## Financing and population elections

Under revised UK Section 23, payment deferred beyond normal terms or financed off-market requires a time-value adjustment and separate interest income. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R038]
For advance payments, the UK entity may elect to adjust for financing; leaving the advance unadjusted is permitted. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R039]
The UK 12-month transfer/payment expedient is independently available. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R040]
A portfolio expedient requires reasonable expectation that its result does not materially differ from individual contract accounting. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R041] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R041] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R041] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R041]

For a substantive two-year advance of1,000 at10%, the financing-adjusted liability grows1,000 ->1,100 ->1,210 before transfer; interest expense totals210 and transfer revenue1,210. Under the UK unadjusted advance election, liability and transfer revenue stay1,000, with no interest expense. RC-001 retains the reason for financing, supported rate and elected policy, and distinguishes short payment intervals and deposits held for substantive performance protection. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R039]

Relative inception SSP allocates price across obligations unless a valid framework-specific allocation exception changes the result. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R042] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R042] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R042] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R042]
Recognize each allocated amount when or as control of its promised output transfers; a signature or invoice is not a substitute for transfer. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R043] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R043] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R043] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R043]

## Separate warranty and option rules

A service warranty is a separate promised service when it provides coverage beyond assuring specification compliance. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R045] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R045] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R045] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R045]
An assurance warranty routes to the applicable provision/contingency model rather than receiving a service allocation. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R046] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R046] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R046] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R046]
A customer option is a material right only for incremental benefit unavailable without the originating contract. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R047] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R047] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R047] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R047]

## UK regime selection before contract assessment

FRS101 qualifying individual statements use adopted-IFRS recognition/measurement with FRS101's eligible disclosure exemptions; FRS102 Section1A is a different regime. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R052]
An eligible micro-entity choosing the micro regime applies FRS105, rather than merely reducing FRS102 disclosures. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R053]
Revised FRS105 Section18 uses a customer-contract transfer model from annual starts1January2026, with early adoption requiring simultaneous application of the amendments. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R054]
Its revenue transition is prospective for contracts beginning after adoption; contracts already in progress retain their prior accounting policy. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R055]
Its contract gate tests approval, rights, terms, substance and probable payment. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R056]
Its promises include distinct transfers and valid customary expectations, excluding nontransfer administration. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R057]
Its disclosure route is the micro-entity minimum regime, not the FRS102 revenue-note suite. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R058]

RC-001 records UK regime and eligibility before selecting any rule. Maintain two micro-entity cohorts after adoption: old in-progress contracts and new contracts; a bulk opening-equity conversion of all contracts is rejected. FRS101 notes require an exemption-by-exemption eligibility workpaper with required group-equivalent disclosures, not an unchecked private-company checkbox.

## Failed-contract exit conditions

For IFRS/AASB, recognize a failed-gate receipt only when no transfer obligations remain and substantially all promised consideration received is nonrefundable, or the contract terminates and the receipt is nonrefundable. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R059] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R059]
UK revised FRS102 uses the no-remaining-obligations/substantially-all-receipts or terminated/nonrefundable exit, independently from later collection-only reassessment. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R060]
US additionally permits its qualifying transfer-complete/ceased-transfer nonrefundable-receipt route; document actual goods/services transferred and the absence of further obligations rather than treating all cash as earned. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R061]

## Tax and shipping election execution

US may elect to exclude qualifying government-imposed transaction taxes collected from customers; gross-receipts and inventory-procurement taxes are outside that election. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R062]
US may treat post-control shipping/handling as fulfilment rather than a separate promise, accruing related costs when associated revenue is recognized. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R063]
IFRS/AASB post-control shipping that transfers a promised service is evaluated as a separate obligation without the US shipping election. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R064] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R064]
UK promises follow the selected Section23 or Section18 model; a US fulfilment election is not a UK policy option merely because it simplifies billing. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R065]

The elected US fulfilment branch earns120 at product transfer and accrues supported shipping cost8. Review both the delivery terms determining product control and the election; changing the shipping flag alone cannot establish control.
