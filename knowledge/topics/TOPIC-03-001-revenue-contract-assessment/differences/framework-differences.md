# Revenue Contract Assessment — Framework Differences

| Area | IFRS 15 | ASC 606 | FRS 102 revised Section 23 | AASB 15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core model | Five-step | Jointly developed five-step | Five-step derived from IFRS 15, simplified | IFRS 15-based five-step |
| Effective routing | Current since 2018 | Topic 606 current, amendments/effective dates monitored | Major gate: revised Section 23 generally periods beginning >=1 Jan 2026 | Current AASB compilation required |
| Contract/performance obligation architecture | Full IFRS 15 | Highly converged but US-specific wording/amendments | FRC-specific paragraphs/simplifications | Core IFRS alignment + Australian material |
| NFP/public-sector | IFRS scope/application | US NFP application under US literature | UK entity regime | Explicit Australian NFP application material |
| Contract costs | IFRS 15 requirements | Topic 340-40 interaction | FRS 102 contains policy simplification/choice for certain obtain costs | IFRS-aligned core |
| Ongoing amendments | IASB PIR future agenda items | FASB post-issuance ASUs, including 2025 customer share-based consideration | Periodic Review 2024 + transition | AASB amendments track Australian adoption |

The CAO treats convergence as a retrieval efficiency, not permission to assert identical requirements. Framework, reporting period and entity type remain mandatory.
