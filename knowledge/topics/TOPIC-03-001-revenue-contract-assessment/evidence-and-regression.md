Claims: [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-001], [TOPIC-03-001-UK-001], [TOPIC-03-001-US-001]. Existing contract and allocation workpapers remain the operative method; this file controls source attribution.

## Execution and entry
For each order, collect approved MSA, SOW, pricing, side letters and customary offers. Create separate rows for promised goods, contract criteria and combination tests. The existing 180 allocation is 110.38 equipment + 59.43 service + 10.19 option. At a valid equipment transfer: Dr contract asset/receivable 110.38, Cr revenue 110.38, subject to the billing right; prepaid service/option consideration remains contract liability until earned. A single invoice must not force the whole 180 into revenue.

## Four-framework challenge
IFRS/AASB: an incremental renewal discount gives rise to the separately assessed option; AASB NFP funding and licence overlay is a separate boundary. US: run Topic 606 scope and the legally enforceable contract test independently; original ASU and IFRS parallel wording do not verify current ASC. UK: for a 2025 commencement use the earlier Section 23, while a 2026 commencement invokes revised Section 23 unless a transition exception applies. Record the transition election and opening contract population before comparing results.

## Adverse tests and control
Same customer/same day without linked economics must not trigger combination. A generally available discount must fail the material-right test. Reperform the 180 tie-out, then compare the opposite branch (no right) to its revised obligation allocation. The contract intake owner compares CRM and legal amendment populations to billing orders; reviewer checks missing side letters and countersigns the option exercise evidence. Retain line-level contract IDs, version dates, SSP history and reviewer. These tests exercise all four listed claims, with UK and US detailed source audits still open.
