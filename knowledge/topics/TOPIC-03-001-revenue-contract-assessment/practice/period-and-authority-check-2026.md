## Decision and calculation workpaper

First confirm that the equipment and service documents have the same customer/counterparty and were entered at or near the same time. Preserve negotiation emails, side letters, price concessions and billing data. Test separately whether they were negotiated as a package with one commercial objective, whether consideration in one depends on the other, or whether promised goods/services constitute one performance obligation. If any qualifying combination route is met, document the single accounting contract; otherwise retain separate contract IDs but still assess promises and the option in each. A common customer and signature date alone are not sufficient.

For the illustrative combined consideration of 180, suppose equipment SSP is 130 and first-year service SSP is 70. The option offers a third year for 40 when a comparable customer without this contract would pay 60. Assume the incremental 20 discount is a material right, 60% expected exercise, no other discount and a supportable option SSP of 12 (=20 × 60%). The total SSP is 212, so allocate 180 × 130/212 = 110.38 to equipment, 180 × 70/212 = 59.43 to service, and 180 × 12/212 = 10.19 to the option (rounding to two decimals totals 180). Recognize the equipment allocation only when control passes, the service allocation as service is transferred, and the option allocation when the underlying option is exercised or expires under the applicable model. The option's 40 future exercise price is *not* in the original 180 transaction price merely because exercise is forecast. Recalculate for the actual option terms, relative discount, likelihood of exercise and stand-alone evidence; a generally available promotion could eliminate the separate right.

At each reporting date reconcile the 180 consideration to receivables, contract assets and contract liabilities rather than posting the full invoice to revenue. Link each obligation to delivery and service event IDs, enforce a review of contract modifications, and retain the pricing population and exercise-history extract as audit evidence. A reversed conclusion (documents are separate and option not incremental) must generate a different allocation and disclosure narrative; the reviewer should sign both routes, not just confirm that the numbers sum. Interface to TOPIC-03-002 for variable price, 03-005 for billed/unbilled cutover, 03-006 for costs, 03-008 for credit impairment and 03-012 for remaining-performance-obligation disclosure.

**Adverse tests:** (1) same-day contracts with unrelated objectives must not be combined solely by date; (2) a 20 discount available to every customer is not automatically an option performance obligation; (3) the above 10.19 allocation must not be booked immediately as revenue; (4) if the period begins 1 July 2026, the linked December 2022 AASB compilation must not be treated as the operative text. Source reviewer must complete live ASC Basic View citation and later AASB compilation selection before proposing independent REVIEWED status.
