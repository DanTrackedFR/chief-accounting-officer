# Linked contracts and material right: canonical revenue intake

Canonical TOPIC-03-001 `revenue-contract-assessment` is complemented by duplicate/retry topic folders with more detailed performance-obligation and transaction-price material. Preserve them for QA canonicalization; this workpaper does not count them as extra topics. **Facts:** customer signs a 120 equipment contract and same-day 60 service contract negotiated together, with a 20 option discount available only if the customer buys a third year. Evaluate contract-combination criteria and whether discount option creates a material right separate from ordinary marketing discounts under IFRS 15/ASC 606/revised FRS 102 Section 23/AASB 15 as applicable. Signed contract labels do not predetermine one versus two accounting contracts or the performance obligations. Obtain standalone prices and option exercise evidence before allocating the transaction price; 120+60=180 billed consideration is not automatically 120 equipment revenue on signature plus 60 future service, and the conditional 20 is not simply immediate revenue reduction.

**Decision tree.** Establish enforceable rights/payment terms, commercial substance, probable collection and contract scope; identify distinct goods/services, warranty, option, financing and variable consideration; estimate transaction price subject to framework constraints; allocate to obligations using relative standalone selling prices and allocate qualifying discounts/variable amounts if specific rules apply; recognize on control transfer over time or at point in time; measure contract asset/liability/receivable based on performance and unconditional rights. If the service is essential to equipment functionality and customer cannot benefit separately, distinctness conclusion may differ. If the optional third year is available at the same discount to all customers, material-right conclusion may fail. Route credit loss to 03-008, fulfillment cost to 03-006, billing to 03-005 and disclosure to 03-012.

**Framework gate/test.** Revised UK FRS 102 Section 23 applies principally to periods beginning 1 January 2026 and is proportionately aligned, not identical to IFRS 15. Pre-2026 UK periods require old edition, and transition must be documented. US Topic 606 current paragraph body and FASB amendments require authorized confirmation; AASB 15 operative compilation/tier overlay is separate. Fail recognizing all 180 at signing, treating 20 option automatically as expense, or applying revised Section 23 to an ordinary December 2025-start period. Retain linked contracts/side letters, pricing, option history, delivery/acceptance, credit assessment, allocation calculation, revenue schedule, GL/subledger and reviewer approval. [IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/).
