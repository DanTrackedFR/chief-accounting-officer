# TOPIC-02-001 — Month-End Close Architecture

Status: REVIEWED — Phase 2D
Primary capabilities: CAO-02-001, CAO-02-002, CAO-02-003
Knowledge type: PRINCIPLES + PRACTICE, with standards-sensitive cut-off/accounting dependencies delegated to the relevant accounting topic.

## Objective
Design and operate a controlled, evidence-backed close that converts source-system activity and accounting judgments into a complete, accurate, timely period-end ledger and reporting package.

## CAO operating model
1. Define reporting perimeter, period, materiality and required outputs.
2. Build a close inventory by process/account/entity/system.
3. Identify hard dependencies and critical path.
4. Separate pre-close, close-window and post-close activities.
5. Assign preparer, reviewer, due time, evidence and escalation path.
6. Use risk/materiality to sequence work rather than treating every task equally.
7. Require subledger/interface completion before dependent reconciliations and reporting.
8. Track late inputs, exceptions, post-close journals and reopened periods explicitly.
9. Certify completion using evidence, not task-box status alone.
10. Feed recurring exceptions into transformation backlog.

## Required context
Reporting calendar; entities; ERP/subledgers; COA; close checklist; account ownership; materiality; reporting deadlines; upstream/downstream dependencies; recurring journals; reconciliations; consolidation/reporting tools; audit/control requirements.

## Outputs
Close calendar; dependency graph; RACI; critical-path list; close dashboard; exception log; evidence/sign-off pack; improvement backlog.

## Boundaries
This topic governs the close operating system. Recognition/measurement conclusions belong to their technical topics. Financial reporting presentation belongs to Domain 08. Control design is linked to Domain 09.

## World-class practice
Exception-driven close; materiality/risk tiering; automated data readiness checks; objective completion criteria; early estimates with true-up governance where supportable; continuous reconciliation; measurable bottleneck reduction; preserved evidence lineage.
