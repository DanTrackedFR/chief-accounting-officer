# Contract asset, receivable and refund are distinct

**Acceptance chronology.** A customer owes 500 for a March milestone, but right to payment requires substantive acceptance on 5 April. If control of service transferred 28 March independent of acceptance, March contract asset 500 and April reclassification to receivable 500 are plausible under IFRS 15; no second April revenue. If acceptance itself is necessary for customer control, March revenue/asset fail and performance recognition waits. The legal certificate and operational delivery logs determine which fork, not billing date. If March customer advance 200 relates to a separate undelivered obligation, retain its contract liability by contract; do not net against a 500 contract asset from a different contract absent standard-compliant presentation rules.

**Refund alternative.** Suppose customer has a valid right to a 50 refund of consideration for the March milestone due to a documented service-level concession. Assess variable consideration and refund liability 50 separately from credit-loss allowance on an unconditional 500 receivable. A customer inability to pay 50 is a credit-risk issue under IFRS 9/ASC 326/AASB 9/FRS 102 Section 11, not automatically a revenue-price concession. In practice both may coexist, requiring event-by-event evidence and no double reduction of revenue and AR. If 200 advance is refundable in cash on demand and no performance has occurred, classify the obligation based on applicable revenue/financial-liability guidance and terms.

**Control/test.** Verify March/April event chronology, contract ID and right, 500 reclass once, refund 50 source, 200 advance per contract, and separate credit-risk model. Fail net 500−200=300 across unrelated contracts, all April 500 as new revenue, or booking credit loss as discount without support. Retain agreement/acceptance, invoices, cash receipts, refund approvals, ECL and rollforward by contract. Official [IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/), [IFRS 9](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). UK 2026 transition and US text are gated.
