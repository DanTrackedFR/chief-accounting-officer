# Deferred and unbilled cut-off — evidence and regression

Claims: [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-001], [TOPIC-03-005-UK-001], [TOPIC-03-005-US-001]. Preserve the existing acceptance timeline and billing-to-revenue rollforward.

A 120 annual subscription paid 1 January and evenly provided for 12 months starts Dr cash 120 / Cr contract liability 120. After three months, Dr contract liability 30 / Cr revenue 30; closing liability 90. If an accepted 200 service is performed and 150 remains conditional on a substantive future certification, Dr contract asset 150 rather than receivable; the 50 advance and other rights must be presented at the appropriate contract level. When the certification condition lapses, Dr receivable / Cr contract asset 150. Assess impairment separately and trace a concession to variable price, not credit loss, when contract/pricing evidence supports it.

Adverse tests: invoice before substantive acceptance cannot force revenue or receivable; an invoice delayed solely by clerical processing may leave an unconditional receivable; a stale unbilled balance with no billing trigger fails close sign-off. IFRS/AASB mapping supports the right classification in the defined AASB period; Australian tier/NFP and July 2026 amendments need a separate gate. US 606 and revised UK Section 23 require their own edition, public/private or small-entity and transition review. The reconciliation owner extracts performance, invoice, acceptance, credit and cash events independently, tests negative balances, and obtains legal resolution of disputed acceptance. Claims remain UK/US source-auditable without blocking the accounting method.
