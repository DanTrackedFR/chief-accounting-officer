## A. Annual subscription billed in advance

Service runs 1 January–31 December. Price is 1,200, invoiced and paid on 1 January. [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-R002] [TOPIC-03-005-US-R002] [TOPIC-03-005-UK-R002] [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-R002] Assume one stand-ready obligation satisfied evenly and no other promises. On receipt: Dr cash 1,200, Cr contract liability 1,200. [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-US-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-UK-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-R001] At 31 March, three months of service: Dr contract liability 300, Cr revenue 300. [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-US-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-UK-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-R001] Closing liability is 900; twelve monthly releases of 100 exhaust the 1,200. [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-US-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-UK-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-R001] Billing on day one is not revenue evidence for the remaining nine months. **Executed arithmetic:** 1,200 opening additions − 300 performance release = 900 closing liability; 300 recognized revenue + 900 remaining liability = 1,200 consideration. [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-US-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-UK-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-R001] PASS.

## C. Completeness and exception check

For a March population with opening liability 900, new advance billings 200, performance releases 350 and customer refunds reducing that liability by 50, expected closing liability is 700. [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-R006] [TOPIC-03-005-US-R006] [TOPIC-03-005-UK-R006] [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-R006] [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-R002] [TOPIC-03-005-US-R002] [TOPIC-03-005-UK-R002] [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-R002] [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-US-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-UK-R001] [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-R001] A subledger balance of 730 leaves a 30 unexplained difference. Do not force a revenue journal: trace contract IDs, credit notes, payment status and GL mapping. [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-R012] [TOPIC-03-005-US-R013] [TOPIC-03-005-UK-R013] [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-R013] An old unbilled balance with no billing event requires a contract and recoverability review. [TOPIC-03-005-IFRS-R002] [TOPIC-03-005-US-R002] [TOPIC-03-005-UK-R002] [TOPIC-03-005-AASB-R002]

**Executed arithmetic:** 900 + 200 − 350 − 50 = 700; 730 − 700 = 30 exception. PASS detection, accounting disposition pending evidence.
