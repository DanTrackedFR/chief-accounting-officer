# Contract balance workpaper and executed scenarios

Scope: CAO-03-010/011. Illustrations use a single contract in a functional currency and ignore tax, financing, foreign exchange and expected credit losses. These are independently authored arithmetic checks, not quotations from standards. Apply the framework and reporting period in the parent README before posting.

## A. Annual subscription billed in advance

Service runs 1 January–31 December. Price is 1,200, invoiced and paid on 1 January. Assume one stand-ready obligation satisfied evenly and no other promises. On receipt: Dr cash 1,200, Cr contract liability 1,200. At 31 March, three months of service: Dr contract liability 300, Cr revenue 300. Closing liability is 900; twelve monthly releases of 100 exhaust the 1,200. Billing on day one is not revenue evidence for the remaining nine months.

**Executed arithmetic:** 1,200 opening additions − 300 performance release = 900 closing liability; 300 recognized revenue + 900 remaining liability = 1,200 consideration. PASS.

## B. Performance before an unconditional right

A 500 allocated milestone is completed on 28 March, but substantive acceptance remains pending under the assumed contract. Subject to evidence that control transferred despite that clause, the right to payment is still conditional: Dr contract asset 500, Cr revenue 500. Acceptance on 5 April removes the remaining condition: Dr receivable 500, Cr contract asset 500. Invoice issuance may occur later. If acceptance actually gates control, the March revenue conclusion fails and both entries must wait; retain the legal and delivery evidence. A credit loss assessment is a separate question.

**Executed arithmetic:** March contract asset 500; after April reclassification contract asset zero and receivable 500; no second revenue posting. PASS conditional on control-transfer conclusion.

## C. Completeness and exception check

For a March population with opening liability 900, new advance billings 200, performance releases 350 and customer refunds reducing that liability by 50, expected closing liability is 700. A subledger balance of 730 leaves a 30 unexplained difference. Do not force a revenue journal: trace contract IDs, credit notes, payment status and GL mapping. An old unbilled balance with no billing event requires a contract and recoverability review.

**Executed arithmetic:** 900 + 200 − 350 − 50 = 700; 730 − 700 = 30 exception. PASS detection, accounting disposition pending evidence.

## Evidence and review

Archive signed contract and amendments, allocation memo, performance event, acceptance record, invoice and cash IDs, rollforward by contract, journal IDs, exception disposition and reviewer sign-off. Cross-check TOPIC-03-004 (contract asset/liability classification), TOPIC-03-008 (credit loss), TOPIC-03-011 (subledger/GL).
