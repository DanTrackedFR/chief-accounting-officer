# Acceptance, right to consideration and credit-risk handoff

The retained acceptance scenario must distinguish a substantive customer acceptance clause from a routine specification check. The [2025 issued IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2025/issued/ifrs15.html) maps control to 31–38, customer acceptance to B83–B86, contract-asset versus receivable presentation to 105–109, and returns/refunds to B20–B27. Use [AASB 15](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022) for annual starts in its stated 2023-to-before-July-2026 window and select a later compilation afterward. UK FRS 102 Section 23 edition/transition depends on annual commencement, principally January 2026 for Periodic Review 2024. US ASC 606 paragraph exactness must be validated in live Basic View; Topic 606 alone is insufficient provenance.

For a 200 deliverable with 50 paid in advance, suppose contractual acceptance is substantive and may be refused for a nonconforming custom product. Before acceptance, do not create 200 revenue merely from shipment and an invoice; identify the actual control-transfer evidence and retain the 50 as an obligation to perform or refund as appropriate. If acceptance is only a formality because objective specifications demonstrably passed and control otherwise transferred, an accounting conclusion at delivery may differ. When 200 performance is valid but only 150 is conditional on a future certification unrelated to passage of time, distinguish a 150 contract asset from an unconditional 150 receivable; the 50 advance offsets the position within the same contract under the applicable presentation rules. Do not post both 200 receivable and 150 contract asset. Treat a genuine price concession as variable consideration, a refund as a refund liability where applicable, and subsequent counterparty default as credit impairment—not a retrospective revenue concession without pricing evidence.

Prepare an order-level timeline of dispatch, objective test, acceptance, invoicing, certification, payment and credit event; retain clause redlines and legal resolution of disputed acceptance. Reconcile subledger conditional rights and GL aging; require reviewer sign-off on boundary cases and route price changes to TOPIC-03-002, returns to 03-007, expected credit losses to 03-008. The adverse test is an invoice dated before acceptance with no objective proof of control: it cannot alone turn a conditional right into recognized revenue. Remains PARTIAL pending US and subsequent-period AASB checks.
