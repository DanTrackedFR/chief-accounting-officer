# TOPIC-17-003 — Sustainability controls and metric reconciliation

Status: DRAFT for independent Phase 2D QA; canonical capabilities: CAO-17-006, CAO-17-007. Prepared 2026-09-27. This topic contains independently authored decision and execution guidance; restricted standards and regulations are reference-only.

## Principles and scope

Metrics require the same core discipline as accounting evidence: a defined population, method, change control, reconciliations, and independent review, adapted to operational data. Identify the legal entity, jurisdiction, report date, group perimeter and applicable framework before evaluating requirements. This is a regulatory overlay, not a replacement for financial-statement recognition and measurement under IFRS, US GAAP, FRS 102 or AASB.

- Australia: [AASB S2](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-s2-sep-2024) effective from periods beginning 1 January 2025; [ASIC cohort test](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/sustainability-reporting/for-preparers-of-sustainability-reports/who-must-prepare-a-sustainability-report/) distinguishes statutory start dates and entity eligibility. AASB S1 is voluntary unless separately adopted.
- UK: [government UK SRS status](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/uk-sustainability-reporting-standards): S1/S2 issued February 2026 for voluntary use; specific mandates require a separate current-rule check.
- EU: [Commission CSRD and amendments timeline](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/financial-markets/company-reporting-and-auditing/company-reporting/corporate-sustainability-reporting_en), ESRS and local transposition; Directive (EU) 2026/470 is in force and narrows core scope to undertakings exceeding both EUR 450 million net turnover and 1,000 average employees, with group, issuer, transition and Member State options to verify from the [Official Journal text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2026/470/oj/eng). Check local transposition and the relevant financial year; historical wave-one obligations may differ.
- US: [SEC climate rule defense decision](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2025-58) and [2026 proposed rescission](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/05/s7-2026-19). A proposal is not a final rule; check current litigation/stay and other disclosure obligations.

## CAO decision and execution

Define metric specification (unit, boundary, source, factor version, gross/net basis, location/market treatment where applicable). Reconcile facilities and purchased energy population to leases/PPE/AP and meters, investigate missing sites and estimation. Test completeness, accuracy and cut-off of activity data; recalculate samples, approve manual adjustments and retain exceptions. Reconcile published metrics to signed schedules and explain changes in method or boundaries.

Workpaper minimum: entity and period; governing rule and URL/version; applicability facts; scope/boundary; inputs and provenance; calculation/mapping; alternatives and judgments; review/sign-off; reporting output; exceptions, remediation and change log. Control owner reconciles source population to accounting or operational systems, and a second reviewer checks the conclusion against the effective rule. Preserve submission/assurance evidence and the signed version.

## Worked application and boundaries

Three sites use 1,200, 900 and 700 MWh. If the ERP utility vendor population covers only 1,200 and 900, the 700 MWh site is a completeness exception even if the published sum of covered invoices agrees.

For each workpaper consider materiality, disclosure, data quality, presentation, applicable assurance and whether a discovered difference changes an accounting entry. Route underlying accounting recognition/measurement to the governing financial topic. Do not post a journal solely to make a regulatory return or sustainability metric reconcile; document the rule-specific bridge.

## Executed desk scenarios (decision-route QA)

A conversion factor is changed retrospectively without version evidence: FAIL; disclosure reconciles to a schedule but schedule excludes new sites: FAIL. These checks exercise decision routing only; they do not certify a live entity's filing or a paragraph-level legal conclusion. A production case must collect actual evidence and rerun current-rule verification.
