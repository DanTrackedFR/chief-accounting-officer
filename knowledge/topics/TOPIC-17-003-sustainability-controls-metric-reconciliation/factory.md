# Sustainability controls and metric reconciliation — substantive factory

Companion to prior README; CAO-17-006/007. Source checked 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for the control and reconciliation method.

## Assertions and data route

Begin with disclosure requirement and metric specification, not a dashboard field. Define numerator, denominator, units, organizational/operational boundary, time period, rounding, net versus gross, factor method and exclusions. The [IFRS S1/S2 disclosure architecture](https://www.ifrs.org/sustainability/knowledge-hub/introduction-to-issb-and-ifrs-sustainability-disclosure-standards/) identifies governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics/targets; [AASB S2](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-s2-sep-2024), EU ESRS and UK SRS can impose different local conditions. A control over an emissions total must first prove all relevant entities/sites/activities were included; agreement of a subtotal to an incomplete spreadsheet is insufficient.

Population tie: legal-entity and facility register ↔ site/asset and lease register ↔ meter IDs and utility contracts ↔ invoices/activity feed. Value tie: raw activity units and dates ↔ accepted estimates ↔ factors/conversions ↔ category/subtotals ↔ published metric. Assign exceptions by site, value, cause, owner and resolution. Separately test factor version and location/market-based electricity method where the applicable reporting rule requires it. Reconcile comparative restatements and acquisition/disposal boundaries to a signed bridge, not a plug. The [GHG Protocol Corporate Standard](https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-standard) is methodological authority where adopted; financial GL data help population checks but do not replace activity data.

## Precision and worked control

Three sites record 1,200, 900 and 700 MWh from independent meters. Expected total = 2,800 MWh. ERP utility invoices cover only 1,200 + 900 = 2,100 MWh; the 700 MWh gap is 25% of the complete population, not an acceptable reconciliation merely because ERP and spreadsheet both show 2,100. Investigate site acquisition, landlord-paid energy, unbilled period and meter mapping. A reviewer signs only after confirming the third site's 700 MWh is included or documented as out-of-boundary under the governing method. If an emission factor is 0.4 tCO2e/MWh for each site in an illustrative common geography, total would be 1,120 tCO2e (2,800×0.4), before method-specific treatment; do not apply one factor across geographies without evidence.

## Control design, evidence and tests

Preventive: approved site/meter master and factor library, required source IDs, unit validation, change permissions. Detective: independent population reconciliation, completeness gap threshold, reasonableness trends, sample recalculation, exception approval and publication tie-out. Test IPE completeness/accuracy, service-provider feeds and spreadsheet change logs; preserve raw snapshots and formula/model version. An independent reviewer redoes selected energy-to-emissions calculations and challenges counterintuitive trends. S1: factor overwritten without version → FAIL. S2: schedule agrees published number but omits acquired facility → FAIL. S3: 2,800 MWh supported and 1,120 tCO2e calculated with validated homogeneous factor → arithmetic PASS, source/geography still conditional. S4: utility invoice dated January for December consumption → period cut-off review required. Dependencies TOPIC-09-003, 11-003, 17-001, 17-004 and 17-006. Residual: applicable factor and site facts must be sourced for production.
