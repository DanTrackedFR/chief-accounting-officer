# Owners' equity and note population bridge

**Illustrative group rollforward.** Opening equity attributable to owners 400; current-year profit attributable to owners 80, OCI loss 10, ordinary-share issue proceeds 50 and dividends recognized 20. Ending owners' equity is 400+80−10+50−20=500. NCI opens 60, receives 15 of current-year profit, bears 3 of OCI and receives 5 dividends: ending NCI 67. Total closing equity is 567. Reconcile 500+67 to consolidated TB, 80/15 to consolidated profit allocation, 10/3 to OCI components, share issue 50 to cap table/bank and dividends to declaration/cash chronology. A parent purchase of additional NCI while retaining control must be routed under consolidation guidance rather than assumed to be an expense or new acquisition goodwill; this simple schedule excludes ownership changes.

**Disclosure population.** Build each note from its own authoritative subledger and technical topic: equity classes and capital terms, accumulated OCI by component, share awards and convertibles, debt and covenants, cash restrictions, related parties, segments, commitments, contingencies and subsequent events. Note completeness is not proven by reconciling 567. Track all disclosure-only items with evidence and owners. Preserve comparative rollforward and reconciliation from prior signed accounts to opening balances; prior-period errors require the framework-specific correction approach and disclosure, not an unexplained current-year equity plug. Share issue proceeds and costs, dividends and NCI allocation require applicable IAS 32/IFRS 10 or corresponding ASC/FRS 102/AASB treatment.

**Framework gate.** IFRS IAS 1 equity presentation and IAS 8 policy/error requirements apply in a 2026 non-early-adopter; IFRS 18 generally becomes mandatory 2027 and replaces IAS 1 presentation. US ASC 505 and SEC presentation may differ; FRS 102 Sections 6/22 and company law govern UK statutory equity; AASB 101/132 and tier overlays require operative version. Do not assume every IFRS note belongs in a UK small-entity or AASB Tier 2 set, nor infer that permitted disclosure relief removes recognition/measurement requirements.

**QA.** Fail any closing equity other than 567 on these assumptions, share issue 50 booked to revenue, dividends double counted in profit and equity, omitted NCI OCI 3, or a note total that agrees internally but lacks population evidence. Reviewer signs cap-table, board minutes, OCI movement, consolidation journal, note checklist and source-to-statement map.
