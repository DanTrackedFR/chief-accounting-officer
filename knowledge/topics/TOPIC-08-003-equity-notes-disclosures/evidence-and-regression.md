# TOPIC-08-003 — reporting evidence and adverse route

Claims: [TOPIC-08-003-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-08-003-US-001], [TOPIC-08-003-UK-001], [TOPIC-08-003-AASB-001]. Retain existing reporting pack.

## Worked case

Opening equity 100 + parent profit 40 + OCI 5 − dividends 10 = closing 135, assuming no other movements. A 20% NCI share of a subsidiary’s 30 profit is 6 to NCI, not automatically parent retained earnings. A 10% interest purchase without control loss adjusts parent equity and NCI rather than P&L. Tie authorized dividends, OCI reserves, capital transactions and each material note to GL/consolidation; challenge a late manual journal that updates the face but not the note.

## Cross-framework and control gate

Select reporting period, entity filing tier and applicable edition before conclusion. US public/private disclosures and SEC route, UK small entity/FRS 102 period, and AASB Tier 1/Tier 2 and NFP must be tested independently. Reconcile source GL, consolidation, note tables, comparatives and approval version with named reviewer. An inaccessible IAS 1 operative paragraph is model-derived, not source-verified. Every unverified claim remains in the audit queue.
