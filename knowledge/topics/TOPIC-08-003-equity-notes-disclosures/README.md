# TOPIC-08-003 — Statement of Changes in Equity / Notes & Disclosure Preparation

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**  
Primary capabilities: CAO-08-005, CAO-08-006  
Sensitivity: H

## Objective
Enable the CAO to prepare a complete equity roll-forward and a controlled notes/disclosure package that is consistent with primary statements, technical conclusions and the applicable reporting framework.

## Inputs
Final TB/consolidation; prior issued statements; equity register/cap table; dividends/distributions; share issuances/repurchases; SBC; OCI reserves; NCI; acquisitions/disposals; accounting-policy/estimate changes and errors; disclosure checklist; technical memos; materiality; framework/period/tier.

## Workflow
1. Resolve framework, period, entity/tier and reporting perimeter.
2. Reconcile every opening equity component to prior issued closing balances.
3. Build movements by owner transaction, comprehensive income, reserve transfer, NCI/ownership change, retrospective adjustment and other supported category.
4. Tie profit and OCI to performance statements and relevant notes.
5. Tie share capital/APIC or equivalent to legal/cap-table evidence and SBC schedules.
6. Tie dividends/distributions to approvals, legal evidence and cash/payable balances.
7. Build a disclosure inventory from applicable standards, entity facts and material transactions; do not treat a generic checklist as proof of applicability.
8. Link each disclosure to a source owner, evidence, primary-statement line and technical conclusion where relevant.
9. Perform consistency review across accounting policies, estimates/judgments, commitments, contingencies, related parties, segments, instruments, revenue, leases, taxes and subsequent events.
10. Clear exceptions and retain reviewer/certification evidence.

## Framework routing
IFRS reporting must use the applicable IAS 1-era presentation requirements until IFRS 18 becomes mandatory for annual periods beginning on/after 1 January 2027 (unless early adopted); IFRS 18 then governs overall presentation/disclosure and introduces enhanced aggregation/disaggregation and management-defined performance-measure requirements. US GAAP routes through applicable ASC presentation and topic disclosure requirements. UK GAAP routes through current FRS 102 plus statutory/company-law disclosures and reporting-period gates. AASB reporters require AASB framework, entity type and Tier 1/Tier 2 routing; AASB 18 dates differ for for-profit versus NFP/specified superannuation entities.

## Practice / controls
Required: opening-equity tie; movement support; primary-to-note ties; disclosure applicability assessment; source/evidence owner; reviewer evidence; comparative consistency.

Recommended: disclosure matrix containing requirement/pointer, applicability rationale, note, source, owner, status, evidence and reviewer; automated note-to-primary-statement ties; change log versus prior period; explicit 'not applicable' rationale for high-risk disclosures.

World-class: disclosure requirements are generated from entity facts and technical cases, not merely rolled forward; every material note number has data lineage; narrative claims are checked against numbers and Company Accounting Memory.

## Artifacts
Equity roll-forward; equity evidence pack; disclosure matrix; note drafting pack; cross-reference map; tie-out report; disclosure change log; certification sheet.

## Scenario tests
1. Prior-period error restates opening equity: CAO separates retrospective adjustment from current-period performance.
2. Subsidiary ownership changes without loss of control: equity/NCI movement must reconcile to consolidation conclusion.
3. Disclosure checklist says 'yes' but source schedule absent: disclosure remains incomplete.
4. 2027 IFRS adopter uses management-defined performance measure: CAO routes to IFRS 18 disclosure requirements.
5. AASB Tier 2 entity: CAO does not assume full Tier 1 disclosure package.

## Completion assessment
PASS at REVIEWED / production-candidate level. Full execution, framework routing, controls, evidence and scenario logic are present; detailed standard-by-standard disclosure paragraph inventory remains an ongoing record-level maintenance function rather than a reason to fabricate completeness.
