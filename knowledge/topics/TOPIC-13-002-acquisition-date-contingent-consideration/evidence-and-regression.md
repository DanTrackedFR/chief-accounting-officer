# TOPIC-13-002 — claim-linked reporting/transaction case

Claims: [TOPIC-13-002-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-13-002-US-001], [TOPIC-13-002-UK-001], [TOPIC-13-002-AASB-001]. Existing workpaper preserved.

## Calculation and counterexample

Earn-out fair value 100 included at acquisition; liability remeasured to 130 after target performance: Dr P&L loss 30 / Cr liability 30 unless new information qualifies as a measurement-period adjustment of acquisition-date facts. If sellers forfeit payment on employment termination, assess whether 100 is post-combination service compensation instead of purchase consideration. An equity-classified earn-out is not remeasured like liability. US ASC 805 and UK FRS 102 §19 contingent consideration may take materially different routes.

## Controls and jurisdiction gate

Retain source population, contract/legal terms, computation, journal/version, reconciliation, disclosure tie-out and reviewer. Recompute under US ASC/SEC where relevant, UK entity regime and Australian tier/period rather than asserting IFRS equivalence. All model-derived claims need direct authoritative audit; IFRS core claims are limited to the specified 2026 issued routes.
