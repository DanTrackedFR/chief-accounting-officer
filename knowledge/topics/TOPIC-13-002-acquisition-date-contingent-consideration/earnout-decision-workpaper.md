# Earn-out at acquisition: recognition, subsequent measurement and evidence

This independently authored IFRS/AASB illustration supplements the retained routing and source tests. It is not a model for FRS 102 or US GAAP without reperforming the applicable framework analysis.

**Facts.** On 1 April X1, buyer pays cash 300 for a business and promises a seller 100 if X2 revenue exceeds a threshold. Acquisition-date probability-weighted, discounted fair value of that cash-settled promise is 60. Another 20 is payable only if the selling founder remains employed through X2. The founder loses it on termination. An independent acquisition-date valuation memo supports 60; the 20 is separately evaluated as post-combination service remuneration, not automatically included in consideration. Confirm all contract terms, forfeiture mechanics and local employment law; do not decide from the word “earn-out.”

Under IFRS 3/AASB 3 acquisition-method routing, consideration transferred for the business is 360 (cash 300 + contingent liability 60), before other acquisition components. The debit to identifiable net assets and goodwill is determined by the separate PPA. Record a contingent consideration liability 60 on acquisition. At 31 December X1, if new X1 trading performance changes its fair value to 75 and does **not** reveal a condition existing at acquisition, recognize a subsequent 15 change under the applicable liability classification/measurement guidance, generally through profit or loss; do not increase goodwill to 75. At settlement for 80, account for the additional 5 after the last valuation, then extinguish the 80 liability against cash. The service-linked 20 is expensed over the requisite service period if the service conditions are met, with an accrual based on the relevant employee-benefit/award model; it is outside this 360 PPA illustration.

**Adversarial fork.** A valuation file received in August X1 identifies an omitted customer contract legally in force on 1 April X1. Preserve contract date, original provisional PPA, valuation inputs and evidence of when information became available; decide whether the resulting change is a valid measurement-period adjustment to acquisition-date facts. A contract first signed in May X1 is post-acquisition performance and fails that gate even though both arrive within one year. Record the affected asset, related tax and goodwill entries and consequential depreciation/amortization; reconcile original and adjusted PPA. The measurement period is not an elective hindsight window.

**Cross-framework release gate.** IFRS 3 paragraphs 37–40, 45–50 and 58 and the matching effective AASB 3 compilation anchor acquisition/subsequent routing. FRS 102 Section 19's purchase method and contingent-consideration rules differ: derive entries from the applicable edition, not the above fair-value-through-profit-or-loss trajectory. For US GAAP, check ASC 805 and current Codification text under authorized access; public ASUs do not establish a complete paragraph body. Control owner signs the employment-versus-consideration memo, valuation, GL liability rollforward 60+15+5−80=0, disclosure tie-out and period/version register. Negative tests: treating the founder payment as purchase price or all later fair-value changes as goodwill fails.
