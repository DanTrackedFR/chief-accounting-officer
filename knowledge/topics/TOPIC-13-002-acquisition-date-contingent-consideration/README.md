# TOPIC-13-002 — Acquisition-Date Accounting / Contingent Consideration Accounting

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**
Source check: 2026-09-24
Primary capabilities: CAO-13-003, CAO-13-004
Sensitivity: H

## Purpose
Enable the CAO to establish acquisition-date accounting, separate acquisition-date facts from later events, account for contingent consideration, maintain the measurement-period boundary and produce an auditable acquisition accounting case.

## Required inputs
- applicable framework, entity and reporting period;
- signed purchase agreement and amendments/side letters;
- acquisition/control date evidence;
- consideration schedule, including deferred/contingent components;
- closing statement and funds flow;
- earn-out or milestone terms and settlement mechanics;
- acquisition-date forecasts and probability assumptions;
- valuation specialist work where used;
- provisional PPA and open-information list;
- post-close information with date obtained and evidence of whether the underlying fact existed at acquisition date.

## CAO execution logic
1. Confirm this is a business combination and identify the accounting acquirer and acquisition date. Route asset acquisitions elsewhere.
2. Freeze an acquisition-date fact set. Do not use hindsight to rewrite acquisition-date economics.
3. Build consideration transferred from acquisition-date fair values, separating seller consideration from remuneration, settlement of pre-existing relationships and other separate transactions.
4. Identify every contingent-consideration arrangement. Document trigger, range, timing, probability/valuation inputs, settlement form and classification.
5. Establish provisional versus final acquisition accounting and an open-items register.
6. For information received after close, apply the measurement-period test: did it provide new evidence about facts/circumstances existing at acquisition date, or arise from a post-acquisition event?
7. Apply framework-specific subsequent accounting to contingent consideration. Never automatically adjust goodwill for a later earn-out remeasurement.
8. Reconcile purchase agreement → funds flow → consideration model → PPA → opening consolidation entries → subsequent remeasurement → disclosures.
9. Produce the acquisition-date memo, contingent-consideration paper, valuation bridge, JE set, disclosure support and audit evidence index.
10. Promote durable conclusions (acquisition date, elections, classification, valuation methodology) to Company Accounting Memory with provenance.

## Framework routing
### IFRS
IFRS 3 acquisition-method accounting uses acquisition-date fair values for consideration transferred. Contingent consideration is recognized at acquisition-date fair value. Measurement-period adjustments are limited to new information about acquisition-date facts and circumstances; the period ends when the needed information is obtained or becomes unobtainable and cannot exceed one year. Later changes caused by post-acquisition events are not measurement-period adjustments. Equity-classified contingent consideration is not remeasured; other contingent consideration is subsequently measured under the applicable requirements, generally with fair-value changes in profit or loss. Current IASB business-combination disclosure/goodwill proposals are pipeline, not current requirements.

### US GAAP
Route to ASC 805 and current Codification effective-date guidance. The acquisition method, acquisition-date measurement, measurement-period discipline and contingent-consideration classification/subsequent accounting require US-specific analysis. FASB ASU 2015-16 changed measurement-period adjustment presentation by eliminating retrospective revision of prior periods for those adjustments; effects are recognized in the period the adjustment is determined, including catch-up effects on earnings. Public FASB source access does not expose all current Codification body text, so paragraph-level status remains PARTIAL unless directly verified from an authorized current Codification source.

### UK GAAP
FRS 102 Section 19 is not assumed to equal IFRS 3. Current FRC materials confirm Section 19 continues to be based on IFRS 3 (2004) and differs materially from modern IFRS 3, including contingent-consideration and acquisition-cost mechanics. Apply the reporting-period version of FRS 102 and do not import IFRS 3 subsequent fair-value-through-profit-or-loss logic by analogy.

### AASB
AASB 3 follows the IFRS 3 core acquisition method and measurement-period architecture, including acquisition-date fair value of consideration and the one-year maximum measurement period. AASB 3 paragraph 58 routes subsequent contingent-consideration accounting by classification/scope. Version/effective-period routing is mandatory: the December 2022 compilation covers periods beginning on/after 1 January 2023 but before 1 July 2026; later periods require the applicable subsequent compilation/amendments.

## Required versus practice
**Required accounting** is framework- and period-specific recognition, measurement, classification, subsequent measurement, presentation and disclosure.

**World-class CAO practice** adds: signed-deal-term extraction; acquisition-date evidence freeze; PPA open-item register; seller-consideration versus compensation screen; valuation input ownership; measurement-period log; monthly contingent-consideration remeasurement trigger; disclosure tie-out; and audit evidence lineage.

## Controls / audit / systems
- independent agreement-to-model completeness check;
- acquisition-date approval and evidence retention;
- review of separate-transaction/compensation indicators;
- valuation model input and change controls;
- measurement-period open-item owner/due-date log;
- post-close information classified acquisition-date fact vs later event with reviewer sign-off;
- contingent-consideration quarterly/period-end remeasurement trigger where applicable;
- PPA/opening-balance/GL/consolidation/disclosure reconciliation;
- specialist reports retained with scope, date, assumptions and management challenge documented.

## Artifacts
- acquisition-date accounting memo;
- consideration bridge and contingent-consideration model;
- measurement-period register;
- valuation assumption/challenge paper;
- acquisition/opening consolidation JE pack;
- disclosure checklist/tie-out;
- audit evidence index.

## Completion criteria
Complete only when the CAO can distinguish acquisition-date facts from later events, route the correct framework/version, calculate or coordinate consideration measurement, explain contingent-consideration classification and subsequent accounting, identify missing valuation evidence, propose entries/disclosures/controls and preserve a traceable measurement-period history.
