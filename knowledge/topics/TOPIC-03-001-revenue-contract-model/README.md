# TOPIC-03-001 — Revenue Contract Assessment, Performance Obligations & Transaction Price

Status: REVIEWED

## Authoritative source families
IFRS 15 Revenue from Contracts with Customers; FASB ASC Topic 606; FRS 102 Section 23 as revised by Periodic Review 2024; AASB 15 Revenue from Contracts with Customers. Repository stores original explanation and authority pointers, not standards text.

## Core principle
Revenue depicts transfer of promised goods/services to a customer in the amount of consideration to which the entity expects entitlement. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R043] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R043] [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R043] [TOPIC-03-001-AASB-R043] IFRS 15 and Topic 606 were jointly developed and share the five-step architecture. Revised FRS 102 Section 23 introduces a proportionately aligned five-step model for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2026 (unless early adopted). [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R017] AASB 15 follows the IFRS 15 core model with Australian applicability overlays.

## Framework routing
### IFRS
IFRS 15 is effective for annual periods beginning on/after 1 January 2018. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R019] Five steps: contract, performance obligations, transaction price, allocation, recognition on/as satisfaction. [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R042] [TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R011] The IASB's 2024 PIR concluded the standard is working as intended; principal-agent and consideration-payable-to-customer matters remain areas for possible future consideration, not current replacement requirements.

### US GAAP
ASC 606 uses the converged five-step model. Differences from IFRS exist in detailed application, practical expedients, collectibility, shipping/handling, noncash consideration and other areas; CAO must retrieve US-specific records rather than assume identity. [TOPIC-03-001-US-R002] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R015] [TOPIC-03-001-US-R013]

### UK GAAP
For periods beginning on/after 1 January 2026, revised FRS 102 Section 23 applies a single five-step model based on IFRS 15 but proportionately simplified. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R017] Earlier periods require version routing to legacy Section 23. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R016] FRC explicitly describes the new model as proportionate alignment, not wholesale IFRS 15 adoption. [TOPIC-03-001-UK-R010]

### AASB
AASB 15 uses the IFRS 15 five-step model. Australian entity type and any sector-specific overlay must still be checked; never infer AASB compliance solely from an IFRS record.
