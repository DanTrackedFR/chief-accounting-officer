# Entity filing matrix and statutory bridge control

**Per-entity intake.** For each legal entity retain registration number, legal form, jurisdiction, accounting period, permitted reporting framework, company-size/tier conclusion, audit requirement, statutory directors' approvals and filing regime/deadline verified from the local regulator. A group IFRS reporting package does not settle whether a UK subsidiary uses FRS 102 or whether an Australian entity is Tier 1/2. US entity reporting also depends on its regulator and entity type; SEC rules do not govern every corporation's statutory statements. Filing law is a jurisdiction-specific legal overlay requiring current confirmation, not a stable cross-framework accounting paragraph.

**Illustrative bridge.** Management TB before statutory adjustments has assets 1,000, liabilities 600 and equity 400. For an illustrative UK FRS 102 subsidiary, a documented lease GAAP adjustment adds right-of-use asset 90 and lease liability 95, with retained earnings reduced by 5; a separately assessed presentation reclassification moves 20 from other payables to current borrowings with no net change. Final assets 1,090, liabilities 695 and equity 395: both sides equal 1,090. The 90/95/5 journal is an assumed **output** of a detailed lease/transition calculation, not a universal FRS 102 transition formula. Revised FRS 102 Section 20 from Periodic Review 2024 is principally effective periods beginning 1 January 2026; verify the entity's transition method, expedients, opening balances and comparative presentation via the Leases topic. The reclassification must have old/new line mapping and lender-term evidence, not be used to force a balance.

**Lineage and approval.** Every item has adjustment ID, originating ledger/package, GAAP basis, period, entry, reviewer, reversal, local tax interaction, version and statement/note line. Bridge management result to statutory profit and opening/closing equity, not just the closing balance sheet. Reconcile opening equity to prior signed statutory accounts, current-year movements to approved TB and closing numbers to drafted statements. Use signed-to-filed binary/version comparison and retain the filing acknowledgment. Trigger a rerun if a group top-side journal, lease schedule or local ledger changes after certification.

**Adverse checks.** A December 2025 UK period does not automatically apply the 2026 revised Section 20; a 2027 UK adapted-format choice has additional 2027 rules. An Australian Tier 2 note list cannot be copied from a full IFRS checklist. If management assets 1,000 and liability/equity sum 1,000 but the statutory bridge ends with a five-unit imbalance, block filing even if management EBITDA reconciliation passes. Source: [FRC FRS 102 current register](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/), [FRC presentation factsheets](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-factsheets/), [AASB standards portal](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/). Entity-specific legal filing requirements remain a case-level gate.
