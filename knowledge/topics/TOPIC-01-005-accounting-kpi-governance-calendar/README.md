# TOPIC-01-005 — Accounting KPI, Service Levels & Governance Calendar

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**
Capabilities: CAO-01-011, CAO-01-012
Knowledge type: PRINCIPLES / PRACTICE

## CAO execution logic
Start from controllership outcomes: complete and accurate books, timely reporting, controlled processes, resolved exceptions and defensible judgments. Define a balanced set of leading and lagging indicators with formula, owner, source, target, threshold and action. Pair speed with quality. Map governance forums to actual decisions and escalation thresholds.

## Required artifacts
KPI dictionary and lineage; SLA catalogue; governance calendar; escalation matrix; monthly controllership scorecard and action log.

## Controls and evidence
Definitions are version-controlled and reproducible. Material unresolved exceptions prevent green status. Governance records decisions, owners and dates. Dependency failures are distinguished from accounting execution failures.

## Scenario QA
PASS — day-5 close with material day-6 entries is not green. PASS — 98% reconciliation completion is red if the missing 2% is material. PASS — overdue audit issue without owner/date fails governance evidence.

## Framework/source routing
This is primarily controllership practice. Do not manufacture four-framework differences. Route recognition/measurement questions to the relevant technical topic. Statutory, listing, ICFR, audit, privacy and other jurisdiction-specific obligations require explicit jurisdiction and effective-period gating.

## Completion evidence
Mapped capabilities, decision logic, outputs, controls and scenarios are covered. REVIEWED is an internal factory status, not external approval.
