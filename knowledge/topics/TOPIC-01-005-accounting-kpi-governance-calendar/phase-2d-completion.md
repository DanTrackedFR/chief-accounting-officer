## KPI dictionary and governance cadence

Define each indicator before presenting it: close days (start and lock event), on-time reconciliations (approved by deadline / due population), first-time-right journals (approved without correction / submitted), aged exceptions (open > policy threshold / total), late adjustment value and count, reopened periods, evidence defects and automation exception rate. Store numerator/denominator, exclusions, entity, timezone, data source and query version. Disaggregate by process and risk; an average can conceal a material failing entity. Set thresholds from observed history and risk appetite; do not present the illustrative values here as industry benchmarks.

A weekly operational meeting owns exception aging, workload and upcoming close risks. The monthly controller certification reviews metrics with financial results, incomplete controls and audit issues. A quarterly CAO steering review changes policy, risk ownership, capacity and automation priorities. The governance calendar needs dated owners, dependencies, evidence due dates, escalation paths and minutes/decision IDs. Link repeated KPI misses to root cause and a funded remediation owner.

**Control failure probe:** 95% of reconciliations are on time, but the remaining 5% includes all material cash accounts. Risk-weighted reporting must flag an exception, not a green dashboard.
