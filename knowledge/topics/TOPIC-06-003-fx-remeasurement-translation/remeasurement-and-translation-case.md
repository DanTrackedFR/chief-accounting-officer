# Currency remeasurement versus translation — executed bridge

Capabilities CAO-06-006/007. Existing duplicate path retained. Entity's EUR functional currency has USD 1,200 receivable, originally recognized at EUR/USD 1.20 (EUR 1,000). At closing EUR/USD 1.50, receivable is EUR 800 and transaction FX loss is 200 in functional-currency earnings, absent special exceptions. This is remeasurement of a monetary item, not translation of a foreign operation.

Separately, a subsidiary with USD functional currency has USD 2,000 net assets. Translating to a EUR presentation currency at closing EUR/USD 1.25 gives EUR 1,600 net assets. If opening translated net assets were EUR 1,500 and current-period net income/contributions/distributions translated by appropriate rates net to EUR 150, a EUR −50 translation difference balances equity: 1,500+150−50=1,600. Do not call it the receivable's EUR −200 transaction FX. Actual OCI/equity presentation and recycling depend on framework and disposal facts.

**Executed:** 1,200/1.20=1,000; 1,200/1.50=800; 1,600−1,500−150=−50. PASS arithmetic. Source [IAS 21 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates/) and [2025 lack-of-exchangeability amendment](https://www.ifrs.org/news-and-events/news/2023/08/iasb-sets-out-accounting-requirements-for-when-currency-not-exchangeable/) checked 2026-09-27. If currency not exchangeable, do not select an arbitrary spot rate; apply amended assessment and disclosure. ASC 830, FRS 102 Section 30, AASB 121 period-specific conclusions remain PARTIAL.
