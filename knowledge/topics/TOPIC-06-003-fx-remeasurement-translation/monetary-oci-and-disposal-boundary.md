# Monetary FX, translation reserve and disposal boundary

The retained case separates EUR −200 monetary remeasurement loss from EUR −50 subsidiary translation difference. To extend it, assume parent lends USD 100 to a USD-functional foreign operation with settlement neither planned nor likely in foreseeable future. This might form part of the net investment under IAS 21, but the legal loan label alone is insufficient: document cash-flow plans, terms, ability and intent to settle, currency, entity and group reporting context. The exchange difference can have different locations in separate financial statements and consolidated financial statements, and eventual disposal can trigger reserve treatment. Do not credit OCI on every intercompany receivable or eliminate real monetary FX merely because principal cancels. Assess impairment/expected credit loss separately; long-term funding is not exemption from credit risk.

**Trace.** At each close, produce entity functional-currency monetary-item schedule, transaction-date and closing rates, settlement P&L and balance-sheet carrying amount; then translate foreign-operation results/assets/liabilities with supported average/spot rates and CTA/equity bridge. For a 60% subsidiary with a translated group net-asset opening of 1,500, profit/transfers of 150 and closing 1,600, CTA is −50. Attribution of comprehensive income between owners and NCI follows ownership/economic rights: a simple 40% NCI share of −50 would be −20 only if rights are ordinary and no other ownership movement. On partial disposal, distinguish loss of control from retained control and route recycling/reallocation under operative standard; do not automatically recycle all −50 to P&L.

**QA.** Fail mixing the −200 transaction FX with −50 CTA, treating USD loan as net investment with no documented settlement expectation, or a disposal journal lacking historical CTA by entity. Evidence includes debt/settlement plan, intercompany agreement, rate source, subsidiary TB, ownership rollforward, CTA by entity and disposal terms. Official [IAS 21](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates/) and [IFRS 10](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-10-consolidated-financial-statements/). AASB 121, ASC 830 and FRS 102 Section 30 require independent operative version/paragraph review. IAS 21 lack-of-exchangeability amendment from 2025 and issued 2027 presentation-currency amendment have distinct scopes.
