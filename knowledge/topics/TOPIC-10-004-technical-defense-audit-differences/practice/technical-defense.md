# Technical position and difference disposition

## Engagement route and decision
Each contested accounting position has facts, period, applicable framework version, independently authored analysis, credible alternatives, calculation, decision maker and signed conclusion. Keep auditor's proposed entry separate from management's view; auditors assess under their engagement standard, management decides its records and statements. PCAOB AS 2810 addresses evaluation of audit results where applicable; ISA/local analogues govern otherwise.

## Control, evidence and data
Difference register fields: proposed debit/credit, affected assertions/entities/periods, factual versus judgmental versus projected classification as the auditor specifies, management decision, aggregation, qualitative factors, disclosure and control impacts, governance communication and final signed TB version. Reconcile posted and unposted differences to final statements.

## Adverse case and escalation
Auditor proposes $0.4m reserve increase; CFO disputes it based only on materiality. Rebuild sensitivity with source population and framework support, consider related uncorrected differences and qualitative factors, record resolution and escalate if disagreement remains.

## Handoff and authority
Link the evidence packet to the request register and final statement version; route accounting errors to Domain 02, control failures to Domain 09 and systems/data gaps to Domain 11. Reassess scope and effective dates at each new engagement. References: [PCAOB auditing standards](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards), [IAASB standards and pronouncements](https://www.iaasb.org/standards-pronouncements), [IAASB 2025 Handbook](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/2025-handbook-international-quality-management-auditing-review-other-assurance-and-related-services). These references describe auditor responsibilities; management owns books, support and correction decisions.
