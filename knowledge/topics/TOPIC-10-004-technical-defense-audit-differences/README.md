# TOPIC-10-004 — Technical Accounting Audit Defense / Audit Adjustment Assessment / Unadjusted Difference Tracking

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Capabilities: CAO-10-010, CAO-10-011, CAO-10-012
Sensitivity: M

## Purpose
Equip the CAO to defend well-supported accounting positions, evaluate proposed audit differences independently and maintain a complete difference register through conclusion.

## Technical accounting defense
A defensible position contains facts, contract/evidence set, applicable framework and effective period, authoritative references, accounting question, alternatives considered, judgment and assumptions, calculations, conclusion, entries/disclosures, controls and explicit unresolved uncertainty. The CAO should challenge its own position before responding to auditor challenge.

## Proposed-adjustment workflow
1. Obtain exact proposed entry and auditor rationale.
2. Reconstruct factual basis and affected periods/entities/accounts/disclosures.
3. Determine whether management agrees an error exists under the applicable accounting framework.
4. Quantify current-period, prior-period, tax-interface and disclosure effects as relevant.
5. Assess qualitative as well as quantitative materiality through Domain 15.
6. Decide record / do not record / further analysis, with management approval under governance policy.
7. Update all linked schedules, statements and disclosures if recorded.
8. If unrecorded, retain rationale, cumulative/rollover effects and governance communication status.

Do not call a difference 'immaterial' solely because it is below a numerical threshold. Do not accept or reject an auditor proposal merely to reach an audit outcome; management retains responsibility for its financial statements.

## Difference register minimum fields
ID; origin; description; factual/accounting classification; entity; accounts; current/prior period; debit/credit; framework reference; quantitative amount; qualitative considerations; control implication; recorded status; cumulative effect; disclosure effect; owner; approver; auditor status; resolution.

## Cross-topic routing
Errors/policy/estimate classification → Domain 15. Control failures → Domain 09. Technical research/memo → Domain 14. FS/disclosure changes → Domain 08. Fraud indicators → governance/fraud-control route. Tax consequences are interfaced to tax specialists, not expanded into tax operations.

## Controls
No adjustment posted without controlled entry/review; difference register reconciled to posted entries; all unadjusted items rolled into final evaluation; prior-period effects tracked; related control deficiency assessment triggered; audit committee/governance communication routed according to applicable regime.

## Scenario tests
- Auditor proposes revenue cut-off entry based on new evidence: CAO independently validates facts/framework, quantifies and records if correct — PASS.
- Small recurring error below threshold: cumulative and qualitative effects assessed; no automatic dismissal — PASS.
- Auditor disagrees with a supported estimate range: CAO documents evidence, uncertainty and framework basis rather than changing solely for convergence — PASS.
- Posted audit adjustment not reflected in disclosure workbook: tie-out control catches it — PASS.

## Assurance anchor
PCAOB AS 1301 requires auditor communications with the audit committee about specified significant matters, disagreements and difficulties; this is an auditor obligation. CAO governance support must route by actual jurisdiction/engagement. Official PCAOB source checked 2026-09-23.
