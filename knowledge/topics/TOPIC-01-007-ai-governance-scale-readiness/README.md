# TOPIC-01-007 — Accounting AI Governance & Growth/Scale Readiness

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**
Capabilities: CAO-01-015, CAO-01-016
Knowledge type: PRINCIPLES / PRACTICE

## CAO execution logic
Govern AI by accounting consequence and evidence risk. Inventory use cases, data, model/tool, user, output and downstream decision. Tier use cases by materiality, autonomy and reversibility. Require human review for accounting judgments and material postings. Test accuracy, completeness, hallucination/unsupported-citation risk and drift. Separately assess whether people, process, systems, data and controls can absorb forecast growth.

## Required artifacts
AI use-case register and risk tiers; approved-use controls; evaluation set; human-review matrix; scale-readiness heatmap; capacity/control trigger plan.

## Controls and evidence
No unsupported AI output becomes accounting authority. Sensitive data access follows approved controls. Material decisions retain traceable inputs/reviewer. Scale triggers are leading indicators, not crisis thresholds.

## Scenario QA
PASS — AI draft memo requires authority verification and reviewer. PASS — automated low-risk classification can run within thresholds plus sampling. PASS — 3x growth with manual reconciliations triggers redesign before volume arrives.

## Framework/source routing
This is primarily controllership practice. Do not manufacture four-framework differences. Route recognition/measurement questions to the relevant technical topic. Statutory, listing, ICFR, audit, privacy and other jurisdiction-specific obligations require explicit jurisdiction and effective-period gating.

## Completion evidence
Mapped capabilities, decision logic, outputs, controls and scenarios are covered. REVIEWED is an internal factory status, not external approval.
