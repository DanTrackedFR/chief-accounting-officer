# TOPIC-08-007 — Earnings per Share / Alternative Performance Measure Governance

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Primary capabilities: CAO-08-013, CAO-08-014
Sensitivity: H
Source check: 2026-09-23

## Principles and routing
EPS is a controlled calculation, not a presentation-only disclosure. Resolve framework, listing/public scope, reporting period, capital structure, numerator, weighted-average shares and potential ordinary shares before calculation. Maintain a share-movement bridge and instrument inventory.

IFRS: IAS 33 governs basic and diluted EPS. Official IFRS material confirms scope for publicly traded ordinary/potential ordinary shares, equal-prominence basic/diluted EPS, weighted-average denominator logic and dilution from convertibles/options/warrants/contingent shares. IFRS 18 consequential amendments affect permissible numerators for additional per-share measures when effective.

US GAAP: route EPS to ASC 260 and SEC registrant overlays. Exact Codification paragraph claims remain PARTIAL where public primary body text is unavailable.

UK GAAP: determine applicable UK accounting/listing/legal requirements rather than importing IAS 33 into FRS 102 entities by default. UK listed entities using UK-adopted international standards route accordingly.

AASB: route to AASB 133 for entities in scope, with Australian entity/reporting requirements checked independently.

Alternative/non-GAAP performance measures are jurisdiction-sensitive. For SEC registrants, apply Regulation G, Item 10(e) of Regulation S-K and current SEC staff interpretations. SEC material states that a non-GAAP measure can be misleading despite detailed disclosure, requires appropriate labels and, where applicable, reconciliation to the most comparable GAAP measure. For IFRS reporters, distinguish IFRS-defined/subtotal measures, IFRS 18 management-defined performance measures when effective, and measures governed by securities/regulator requirements.

## CAO workflow
1. Resolve scope and period.
2. Obtain cap table, share register, treasury-share movements, equity issuances/buybacks, SBC data, convertibles/options/warrants and contingent-share contracts.
3. Reconcile ordinary shares to legal/equity records; calculate weighted-average denominator.
4. Reconcile numerator to attributable earnings and assess participating instruments/preferences.
5. Test each potential ordinary share for dilution/anti-dilution under applicable rules; calculate basic and diluted EPS; validate discontinued-operation and comparative effects.
6. Inventory every externally used APM/non-GAAP measure, define owner, formula, source data, comparable GAAP/framework measure and reconciliation.
7. Test labels, prominence, recurring adjustments, tax effects, consistency, comparatives and change rationale under the applicable regulator.
8. Tie filings, earnings releases, board materials and investor-facing outputs to the governed measure definitions.
9. Retain calculation/review evidence and promote durable policies/definitions to Company Accounting Memory.

## Controls and evidence
Minimum evidence: share bridge; instrument inventory; numerator reconciliation; dilution workbook; APM dictionary; reconciliation schedules; disclosure tie-out; change log; reviewer sign-off. Controls: legal-to-accounting capital-change trigger, equity/SBC completeness reconciliation, formula lock/versioning, independent review, external-publication consistency check.

## Scenarios
- Convertible instrument is anti-dilutive -> exclude from diluted EPS under applicable mechanics but retain evidence.
- Stock split after reporting period -> assess required retrospective EPS effect and disclosure.
- SEC registrant excludes a normal recurring cash operating cost from adjusted profit -> escalate misleading-measure risk; disclosure alone does not cure every misleading measure.
- IFRS 18-effective period uses an additional per-share measure -> route numerator through amended IAS 33/IFRS 18 requirements.
