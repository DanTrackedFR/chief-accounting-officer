# TOPIC-08-007 — claim-linked reporting/transaction case

Claims: [TOPIC-08-007-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-08-007-US-001], [TOPIC-08-007-UK-001], [TOPIC-08-007-AASB-001]. Existing workpaper preserved.

## Calculation and counterexample

Profit 120 less preference dividends 20 gives ordinary profit 100; weighted shares 50 gives basic EPS 2.00. Potential 10 shares cannot simply be added: apply instrument-specific treasury/if-converted method and exclude anti-dilutive instruments. An APM of adjusted profit 150 from statutory 120 plus 30 adjustment needs an itemised 30 reconciliation and consistent period definition; it does not replace statutory EPS. US ASC 260 and SEC non-GAAP rules, UK regulator practice and Australian ASIC/ASX overlays differ.

## Controls and jurisdiction gate

Retain source population, contract/legal terms, computation, journal/version, reconciliation, disclosure tie-out and reviewer. Recompute under US ASC/SEC where relevant, UK entity regime and Australian tier/period rather than asserting IFRS equivalence. All model-derived claims need direct authoritative audit; IFRS core claims are limited to the specified 2026 issued routes.
