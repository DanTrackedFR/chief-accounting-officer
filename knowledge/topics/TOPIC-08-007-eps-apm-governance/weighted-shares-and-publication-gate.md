# EPS denominator and alternative measure publication gate

**IAS 33 scope and example.** For a publicly traded IFRS issuer, profit attributable to ordinary shareholders is 120 million. There were 10 million ordinary shares from 1 January, and 2 million new shares issued for cash on 1 April in a non-leap year. Weighted-average ordinary shares are 10+2×9/12=11.5 million on an illustrative monthly approximation, and basic EPS ≈10.4348 currency units per share. A daily-weighted schedule is required for actual dates; month weights here are pedagogical. Obtain legal issue/consideration date, treasury-share and buyback movements, preference dividends and restatements. A 1-for-2 bonus issue or share split has special retrospective denominator treatment; never weight it as an ordinary cash issue.

**Dilution gate.** A convertible with interest savings after tax 6 million and 1 million incremental ordinary shares yields illustrative if-converted EPS (120+6)/(11.5+1)=10.08, below basic 10.4348 and thus dilutive in this isolated fact set. If instead after-tax interest savings are 20, (120+20)/12.5=11.2, anti-dilutive; exclude from diluted denominator/numerator while retaining the test. Options and contingently issuable shares require their own methods; do not add all potential shares to one denominator. Reconcile every instrument to TOPIC-13-007 and awards to 05-007/008; review discontinued-operations EPS and comparatives as applicable. ASC 260 and AASB 133 are separately scoped; FRS 102 Section 1 contains specified IAS 33 application requirements for certain entities, so “all FRS 102 private entities exempt” is also unsafe.

**APM release.** An external measure “adjusted operating profit” begins with a defined GAAP/IFRS subtotal, each add-back mapped to a governed journal population, and tax/NCI effect separately explained where relevant. Require same definition, comparable prior period, clear reconciliation, rationale and owner. SEC registrants test Regulation G, Item 10(e) and current SEC staff interpretations, including misleading-measure and prominence concerns; this is not a mechanical reconciliation-only control. IFRS 18 introduces management-defined performance measure requirements for applicable annual periods from 1 January 2027, with earlier application permitted, and consequential IAS 33 changes. Do not backdate these into a 2026 non-early-adopter. UK and Australian market overlay depends on issuer/jurisdiction.

**Evidence/tests.** Recalculate 11.5, 10.4348 and both convertible cases; fail 12 million denominator from ignoring timing, 11.2 included as diluted EPS, a split weighted as ordinary issuance, an unexplained recurring add-back and mismatched earnings-release/annual-report definitions. Retain cap table dated events, earnings bridge, instruments, APM dictionary, draft and approved release, reviewer sign-off. Official [IAS 33](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-33-earnings-per-share/), [IFRS 18](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). ASC 260 detailed paragraph assertions remain gated.
