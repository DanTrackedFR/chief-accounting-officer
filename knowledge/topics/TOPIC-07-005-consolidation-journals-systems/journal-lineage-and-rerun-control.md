# Consolidation system lineage and late-TB rerun

**Data contract.** Every entity TB extract has entity code, local account, group account, functional currency, period, status, submission timestamp, source system/run ID and controller certificate. Group mapping has version/effective date and approval. Translation records local balance, rate type, source/date, presentation amount and FX reserve bridge. Each consolidation journal has stable ID, input version, debit/credit, currency, entity pair, transaction ID, accounting rationale, reversal rule, preparer/reviewer and impacted output lines. Acquisition-date basis, ownership/NCI, intercompany eliminations, currency and policy alignment are distinct journal classes; a balanced entry can still be substantively wrong.

**Late change example.** Group intake originally includes Entity B AR 100 and intercompany AP 100. After consolidation freeze, B corrects a customer AR misclassification of 12 into other receivable, with no change to total assets. Replace B's certified TB version; rerun line mapping, intercompany pairing, statement/note lines and affected analytics. An elimination previously tied to the old AR account must be evaluated rather than blindly carried forward. If B's correction instead changes profit by 12, also rerun tax, retained earnings/NCI, EPS and disclosures. In the unchanged-total case, group assets still tie at the same total, so a control limited to aggregate balance does not detect wrong line presentation.

**Release controls.** Prevent direct edits to a locked source TB without a new version and recertification. Reconcile sum of entity TBs plus approved journals to consolidated TB by account, entity and currency, then to statements and NCI/equity rollforwards. Review recurring reversal and unpaired items; compare journal populations to prior period. Make automation deterministic and maintain a human exception queue for control/policy judgments. Fail a balanced but unmapped 12 reclass, a JE with no source-version ID, or an elimination of a new third-party AR. This is operational practice; technical authority for each entry remains IFRS 10/IAS 21/IFRS 3 or the applicable US, UK and AASB counterparts, never “the system did it.”
