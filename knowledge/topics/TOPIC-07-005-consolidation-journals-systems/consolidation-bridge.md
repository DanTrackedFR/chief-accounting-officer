# Consolidation journal control bridge

Capabilities CAO-07-009/010. Existing factory retained. Entity trial balances aggregate assets 1,000, liabilities 600 and equity 400. A 100 intercompany receivable/payable exists within assets and liabilities, and 20 unrealized inventory profit requires elimination. Ignoring tax and NCI solely for demonstration, consolidated assets 880 (1,000−100−20), liabilities 500 (600−100), equity 380 (400−20). **Executed:** 880=500+380. PASS bridge; omission of tax/NCI is an explicit simplification, not valid for real accounts.

Every consolidation JE has entity pair, source transaction, reporting period, GAAP basis, debit/credit, reversal schedule and owner/reviewer. Distinguish recurring eliminations from acquisition-date basis, FX translation, accounting-policy alignment and disposal journals. Lock source TB version; compare consolidation journals to prior period and tie retained earnings and NCI rollforwards. A balancing plug to equity destroys lineage. Maintain stage gates: entity intake → local TB tie → mapping → FX translation → group eliminations → acquisition/NCI/associate entries → financial statements. Official [IFRS 10 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-10-consolidated-financial-statements/) checked 2026-09-27; framework-specific journal effects route to 07-001/002/004/006/009.
