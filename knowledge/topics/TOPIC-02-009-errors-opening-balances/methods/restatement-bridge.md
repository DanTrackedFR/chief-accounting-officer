# Prior-period errors and opening balances — controlled bridge

Extends existing framework comparison and factory; preserve the longer alternate `prior-period-errors-opening-balances` paper. Capabilities CAO-02-019–020. PRINCIPLES/STANDARDS/PRACTICE/DIFFERENCES; prepared 2026-09-27.

## Facts before classification

Build a chronology of original transaction, reporting date, statement authorization/issuance, evidence then available or reasonably obtainable, discovery and new information. Distinguish: (1) error in an issued comparative, (2) estimate revision based on genuinely new information, (3) policy change, (4) reclassification and (5) current-period mistake. Assess materiality including qualitative/aggregate factors under the entity framework and securities regime. Do not apply hindsight to call every later outcome a past error. For a material error, construct each affected statement and opening balance, tax, EPS, covenant and disclosure consequence before choosing correction entries and filing actions.

## Worked bridge

Assume an 80,000 prior-year expense obligation existed and reliable evidence was available before the prior statements were authorized, but no accrual was recorded. For illustration only, assume deductible tax effect 20,000 and no other effect. Prior-year pre-tax profit was overstated 80,000, tax expense overstated 20,000 if the deduction should have been recognized then, and closing retained earnings overstated net 60,000. At earliest comparative opening/affected-period bridge, debit retained earnings 60,000 and debit deferred/current tax balance 20,000 (classification requires tax analysis), credit accrued liability 80,000; exact ledger mechanics depend on statement chronology and tax recognition. If the error arose **within** the comparative year rather than before the earliest period, restate comparative expense/tax and closing balances instead of assigning the entire amount to earliest opening equity. Reconcile original → adjustment → corrected for each line and period. Never use 80,000 as an unexplained current-year expense to avoid comparative analysis.

## Control and adversarial test

Archive originally issued statements and TB, original evidence, dated chronology, correction calculations, tax/EPS/covenant analysis, comparative tie-outs, legal/regulatory consultation, approval and revised statement version. Root-cause the omission; test whether other uninvoiced transactions are missing. The migration opening balance must equal prior closing balance plus **approved** restatement/mapping/acquisition adjustments, not a plug.
