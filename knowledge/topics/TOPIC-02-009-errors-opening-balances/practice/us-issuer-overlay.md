# US issuer correction overlay and verification limit

For a US GAAP issuer, document ASC 250 accounting conclusion separately from SEC registrant materiality and filing decisions. SEC Staff Accounting Bulletin 99 cautions against a mechanical percentage-only materiality test; evaluate qualitative effects and aggregate misstatements. SAB 108 addresses both income-statement and balance-sheet quantification of prior-year effects. SEC staff's 2022 statement distinguishes a prior period material correction from a current-period comparative revision in its discussion of restatements. These are issuer overlays, not replacements for authoritative GAAP or legal advice on filing deadlines.

**Decision case.** A 2025 expense omission of 80,000 was discovered in 2026. Build original and corrected 2025 income statement and 2025/2026 balance-sheet bridges; compute 2026 rollover effect if the prior accrual reverses, and cumulative balance-sheet error if it persists. Include tax, EPS, debt covenants, trends, executive compensation and the effect of any other errors. Document management and governance determination of materiality for each issued period and current year, and the correction/filing route. A current-year debit to expense alone is not adequate analysis.

**Evidence.** Dated chronology, original issued statements, source proof available then, ASC conclusion, separate SAB 99/108 worksheet, auditor/governance communications, legal/securities consultation, approved corrected TB and disclosure, and links from each revised figure to the journal. A new fact arising only in 2026 may call for prospective estimate analysis instead; test chronology before classifying.

**Source status (checked 2026-09-28).** [SEC SAB 99](https://www.sec.gov/interps/account/sab99.htm), [SEC SAB 108](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/staff-accounting-bulletins/staff-accounting-bulletin-no-108), [SEC staff materiality statement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/munter-statement-assessing-materiality-030922), [Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/files/form8-k.pdf) and [Commission clawback rule](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2022/33-11126.pdf) verified as official issuer sources. [FASB Codification](https://asc.fasb.org/) is authoritative for US GAAP; exact current ASC 250 error-correction paragraphs could not be verified in Basic View. Keep that paragraph/effective-version gate open; actual registrant facts and filing decisions are case inputs. The alternate same-ID paper is retained for comparison, not counted separately.

## Registrant decision workpaper (2026-09-28)

Freeze original filings, ledger versions, discovery and issuance dates, available evidence, transaction population and annual/interim effects. Separate an original error from a reasonable estimate changed by new information. Quantify each affected statement, tax, EPS and disclosure, plus other uncorrected errors. [SAB 108 Topic 1.N](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/staff-accounting-bulletins/staff-accounting-bulletin-no-108) requires analysis under both rollover (period earnings effect) and iron curtain (cumulative balance-sheet effect); [SAB 99 Topic 1.M](https://www.sec.gov/interps/account/sab99.htm) requires qualitative as well as quantitative assessment. Test each interim and annual period and both correcting and leaving the error in current statements.

| Conclusion | Correction route | Filing and governance route |
|---|---|---|
| Prior issued statements materially misstated | Reissuance restatement (Big R) of affected periods. | Authorized board, committee or officers determine non-reliance. [Form 8-K Item 4.02(a)](https://www.sec.gov/files/form8-k.pdf) is due within four business days of that conclusion and identifies periods, known facts and auditor discussion. Plan prompt amended 10-K/10-Q statements and assess later and incorporated filings with securities counsel. |
| Prior statements reliable, but current correction **or** uncorrected carryover would materially misstate current statements | Revise prior comparative amounts when next presented, with transparent error disclosure (little r); do not bury the correction in current expense. | Item 4.02(a) generally does not apply because the original statements remain reliable. Confirm exact next filing, pending offerings, and whether an independent auditor notice under Item 4.02(b) changes the route. |
| Both prior and current effects immaterial individually and in aggregate | Document a current out-of-period correction, or comparative correction where appropriate. | Maintain misstatement register and revisit as new periods or errors emerge; no automatic Item 4.02(a). |

The [SEC staff materiality statement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/munter-statement-assessing-materiality-030922) describes both restatement routes. The [Commission's 2022 clawback rule, pp. 27–28 and 32–33](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2022/33-11126.pdf) distinguishes their filing timing and includes both in its compensation-recovery scope, subject to issuer/listing applicability. If the auditor independently concludes a report or review should not be relied on, test Item 4.02(b)/(c): auditor notice date, affected statements, discussion, copy of issuer disclosure, auditor letter, and amendment deadline. Also reassess ICFR, disclosure controls, certifications, audit report, covenants and exchange rules. A discovery date alone does not start the Item 4.02(a) clock; document the authorized conclusion date. Foreign private issuer and non-US form routes need separate jurisdiction analysis.

**Adverse worked case, ignoring tax only for arithmetic.** A December 2025 $80,000 obligation was omitted despite evidence available before the 2025 Form 10-K and remains unpaid at December 2026. The 2025 expense/liability were understated $80,000 and profit/equity overstated $80,000. Leaving it uncorrected gives a December 2026 liability understatement of $80,000 (iron curtain), with no new 2026 expense error (rollover). A 2026 expense debit to correct the liability makes 2026 expense $80,000 too high and clears the 2026 balance-sheet error; it does not cure 2025. Test 2025 quarterly amounts, tax and EPS in the real case. If 2025 is material, use Big R; if 2025 is immaterial but the 2026 correction or carryover is material, use little r; only if both are immaterial, consider a documented out-of-period adjustment. No percentage or $80,000 amount alone chooses a route.

**Sign-off record.** Controller prepares a period-by-period original/adjustment/revised bridge and complete error register; CAO approves classification/materiality; authorized governance documents any non-reliance conclusion; disclosure committee and counsel own filing calendar; auditor discussions and disagreements are logged. Retain locked issued versions, source chronology, both SAB 108 measures, qualitative memo, journal-to-statement lineage, controls/remediation, tax/EPS/covenant analysis and compensation-recovery assessment. Reopen the conclusion if new facts or auditor notice change it.
