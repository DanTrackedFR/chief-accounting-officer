## A. Material 2025 accrual omitted; first discovered after issue

Facts: a December 2025 service was accepted and an $80,000 obligation supported by a then-available contract/invoice. The 2025 report was issued. The amount is stipulated material to 2025. Tax deduction/benefit of $20,000 is assumed recognizable in 2025; no cash settlement, other error, OCI or EPS in this test. Original 2025 revenue 1,000,000, expense 800,000, pretax profit 200,000, tax expense 50,000, net profit 150,000.

| 2025 line | Original | Correction | Corrected |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Expense | 800,000 | +80,000 | 880,000 |
| Pretax profit | 200,000 | -80,000 | 120,000 |
| Tax expense | 50,000 | -20,000 | 30,000 |
| Net profit / closing retained earnings effect | 150,000 | -60,000 | 90,000 |
| Accrued liability | 0 | +80,000 | 80,000 |
| Tax asset or reduction of tax payable (classification must be proven) | 0 | +20,000 | 20,000 |

Balance proof: asset +20,000 minus liability +80,000 = equity -60,000. Corrected 2025 close becomes 2026 opening before other approved movements. A controlled adjustment representation is debit 2025 comparative expense 80,000, credit liability 80,000; debit tax asset/payable 20,000, credit 2025 tax expense 20,000. Ledger mechanics depend on reporting layer and tax facts. **Expected:** IFRS/AASB/FRS 102 material-error comparative restatement, US ASC error route; if a US domestic registrant, Big R and Item 4.02(a) upon authorized non-reliance conclusion, amended filing plan. **Failure injection:** debit 2026 expense only. **Result PASS:** 2026-only posting leaves issued 2025 wrong and shifts earnings into the wrong year. [IFRS-02, UK-02, AU-02, US-02, SEC-03]

## B. Both prior and current effects immaterial

Facts: an omitted 2025 cost of 2,000 is found in 2026. 2025 pretax profit 500,000, 2026 profit before correction 600,000. The complete misstatement log and qualitative review establish that 2,000 is immaterial to each year's statements and to 2026 whether corrected or uncorrected. Assume no tax solely for this arithmetic. Current debit expense 2,000 / credit payable 2,000 gives 2026 profit 598,000; prior 2025 remains overstated by 2,000. Reperform: 600,000 - 2,000 = 598,000; 2,000 / 500,000 = 0.4%, 2,000 / 600,000 ≈ 0.33%, but neither ratio proves immateriality. **Expected:** documented out-of-period decision may be accepted for the stipulated US issuer; evaluate local IFRS/AASB/UK presentation/materiality and intentionality independently. **Failure injection:** aggregate another 100,000 uncorrected error or earnings-covenant threshold; classification must reopen. **Result PASS conditional** on stipulated facts. [SEC-01, SEC-02, SEC-04, AU-02]

## C. Prior immaterial; current effect material: little r boundary

Facts: omitted 2025 expense/payable 80,000. 2025 profit 5,000,000 and materiality analysis concludes prior statements reliable; 2026 otherwise earns 100,000 and the 80,000 current charge or an $80,000 uncorrected liability would materially misstate 2026 on stipulated facts. No tax or payment. Current-only charge would report 20,000 profit instead of 100,000 for 2026; 100,000 - 80,000 = 20,000. If left uncorrected, 2026 liability is understated 80,000. **Expected:** revise 2025 comparative when next filed and disclose the correction; do not put the whole cost in 2026 or leave the liability in error. Generally no Item 4.02(a) because 2025 was reliable; inspect auditor notice and pending filings. Evaluate listed-issuer clawback. **Result PASS:** both correction and carryover alternatives tested; an 80% current earnings impact is an illustration, not automatic materiality. [US-02, SEC-02, SEC-04, SEC-05]

## D. Interim periods offset in full year

Facts: 2025 Q1 expense 60,000 was wrongly deferred to Q2. Correct Q1 profit 100,000, but issued Q1 showed 160,000; Q2 correct profit 200,000, but issued Q2 showed 140,000. Full-year profit is unchanged. The Q1 report is stipulated materially misstated; Q2 and year-to-date impacts require independent tests. Q1 -60,000 and Q2 +60,000 sum to zero, yet Q1's income trend was wrong. **Expected:** do not use zero annual net effect as clearance. Analyze each issued interim and full-year trend; if Q1 is non-reliable for a US domestic registrant, Item 4.02 route and corrected quarter/EPS disclosure as applicable. A 2026 estimate revision cannot retroactively cure the 2025 timing error. **Result PASS:** the route catches the offsetting quarterly error. [US-04, SEC-01, SEC-03]

## E. Evidence boundary and earliest opening

Facts: $30,000 cost originated in 2023, before earliest displayed 2024 comparative in 2025 financial statements; source was available at 2023 authorization. No tax assumed. At 2024 opening, debit retained earnings 30,000 and credit accrued liability 30,000; do not charge 2025 P&L. If records are missing, reconstruct from bank/vendor and contract evidence before claiming impracticability. Separate case: a 2026 customer default follows a genuinely new 2026 shock, with a well-supported 2025 estimate. Treat as prospective estimate reassessment absent contrary evidence. **Expected:** opening asset/liability/equity restatement for material pre-comparative error; no hindsight on estimate. **Result PASS:** period of origin changes equity destination; evidence chronology governs classification. [IFRS-01, IFRS-02, AU-01, AU-02, UK-01, UK-02, US-01]
