# TOPIC-02-009 — Prior-Period Errors / Opening Balance & Roll-Forward Integrity

Status: REVIEWED
Capabilities: CAO-02-019, CAO-02-020
Knowledge mix: STANDARDS + PRINCIPLES + PRACTICE.

## Framework principle
Prior-period error accounting is standards-sensitive. IFRS/AASB route through IAS 8/AASB 108; US GAAP through ASC 250; UK GAAP through FRS 102 Section 10. Material prior-period errors generally require retrospective correction/restatement subject to framework-specific practicability rules and presentation/disclosure. Distinguish errors from changes in estimates and accounting policies before deciding treatment.

## Error-vs-estimate decision
Error: information available, or reasonably obtainable, when prior statements were authorized/issued was omitted or misused, including mathematical mistakes, policy misapplication, oversight or misinterpretation. Estimate change arises from new information/developments and is generally prospective. A later outcome differing from a reasonable prior estimate is not automatically an error.

## CAO workflow
1. Document issue, discovery date and affected periods/accounts/entities.
2. Identify applicable framework and reporting status of affected periods.
3. Establish facts/information available at original reporting date.
4. Classify error vs estimate vs policy change using TOPIC-15-006 when needed.
5. Quantify current-period and cumulative effects, including tax/accounting interactions as relevant.
6. Assess quantitative and qualitative materiality for each affected period and aggregate effects.
7. Determine retrospective correction/reissuance/revision/out-of-period treatment under framework and jurisdiction.
8. Prepare opening-equity and comparative adjustments where required.
9. Update reconciliations, rollforwards, disclosures and audit/regulatory communication.
10. Remediate root cause/control deficiency.

## Opening balance integrity
Opening TB must equal prior approved closing balances after explicitly controlled entries for consolidation changes, currency translation, retrospective policy/error adjustments, acquisitions/disposals, system migration and statutory-to-management mapping. Never overwrite opening balances without bridge.

Minimum bridge: prior close; approved retrospective adjustments; entity/perimeter changes; mapping changes; FX mechanics where applicable; opening current period. Each difference carries source, accounting basis and approval.

## Migration
For ERP migration, reconcile legacy closing TB to transformed/mapped opening TB at account/entity/dimension level. Separately validate open subledger items, historical attributes needed for future accounting, and retained earnings/equity. A balanced migrated TB can still be wrong if mapping offsets errors.

## Controls
Issue log; materiality review; technical accounting approval; opening-balance bridge; locked prior-period baseline; migration control totals; comparative tie-out; disclosure checklist; root-cause remediation.

## Artifacts
Error paper; materiality analysis; correction entry pack; comparative/restatement bridge; opening-balance reconciliation; migration validation; disclosure support; control-remediation plan.

## Scenarios
Prior depreciation used wrong useful life because new operational evidence emerged this year: evaluate estimate change, not automatic error. Revenue contract was misread despite terms being available: potential prior-period error. ERP opening TB balances but AR customer detail differs from legacy: migration not validated until subledger/detail is reconciled.

## QA
PASS: framework routing explicit; error separated from estimate/policy; opening balances require traceable bridge and subledger validation.
