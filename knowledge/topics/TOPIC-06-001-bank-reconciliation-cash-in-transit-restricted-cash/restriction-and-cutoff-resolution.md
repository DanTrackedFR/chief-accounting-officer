# Restriction, cash transfer and bank reconciliation release

**Resolve retained 20 difference.** Prior worked reconciliation has adjusted bank 900−70+40=870 and adjusted books 890−10+10=890, leaving 20 unexplained. Suppose a bank statement reveals a 20 direct debit on 31 December omitted from GL, with a valid supplier invoice and bank transaction ID. Book payable or expense reduction/settlement as dictated by the invoice history, credit cash 20, and adjusted books become 870. If no bank evidence supports this item, the reconciliation remains **FAIL**; inventing a 20 debit merely to close it is not an acceptable control. Investigator records search of fees, deposits, outstanding items, duplicate posting, uncleared checks and account ownership, plus dated independent review.

**Restricted deposit.** Bank balance includes a 100 demand deposit subject to contractual restrictions on use for a collateral obligation, withdrawable on demand. Under the IFRIC's IAS 7 agenda decision, restriction on use alone does not necessarily change the deposit's nature as cash; assess deposit terms and availability before classifying and disclose restrictions when relevant. US ASC 230 restricted cash cash-flow reconciliation architecture differs. UK FRS 102 Section 7 and AASB 107 require operative-period review. Do not infer balance-sheet presentation or cash-equivalent status from one “restricted” flag. Tie the 100 to the bank agreement, account statement, cash-flow reconciliation and liquidity disclosures. If the agreement prevents demand withdrawal, reassess from the facts; the illustrative demand-deposit conclusion is not transferable.

**Internal transfer.** Debit in Bank A 100 dated 31 December and credit in Bank B 2 January: match both sides by transfer ID, bank-clearing terms and year-end title/control. No double count, no fictitious operating cash flow; where recognition timing differs, record the correct in-transit position and reconcile both accounts. Fail aging outstanding items beyond policy without investigation. Preserve bank confirmations, signatory/access review, reconciliation populations, posting references and reviewer disposition. Official [IAS 7 demand deposits decision](https://www.ifrs.org/projects/completed-projects/2022/demand-deposits-with-restrictions-on-use-ias-7/), [IAS 7](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). US ASU 2016-18 is amendment history; verify current ASC text before paragraph claims.
