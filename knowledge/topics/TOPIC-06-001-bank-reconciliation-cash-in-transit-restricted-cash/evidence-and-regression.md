Claims: [TOPIC-06-001-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-06-001-US-001], [TOPIC-06-001-UK-001], [TOPIC-06-001-AASB-001]. Existing methods and duplicate materials remain intact.

## Workpaper and boundary

Bank statement 1,000 + evidenced deposit in transit 100 − outstanding cheques 80 = adjusted bank 1,020. Book 1,040 less an unposted bank fee 20 = adjusted book 1,020, requiring Dr bank fee 20 / Cr cash 20. Without bank fee evidence, the 20 remains an unresolved exception, not a plug. A restricted demand deposit may still be cash under IFRS if restriction does not change its nature, but disclose material amounts unavailable for group use. US ASC 230 restricted-cash cash-flow reconciliation is separately governed; UK §7 and AASB 107 presentation need own edition checks.

## Framework challenge and control

The IFRS proposition is tied to the identified issued edition. US public/private choices and operative ASC, UK FRS 102 edition, and AASB commencement/tier must each be checked for the same fact pattern. Reperform numerical entries and opposite branches. Retain source population, legal/contract terms, date-stamped valuation or bank/rate feeds, journal lineage, disclosure support and reviewer. The detailed non-IFRS claims remain in the audit queue; they do not prevent use of the substantive method with a disclosed source gate.
