# Customer credit origin, application and expiry review

Existing receipt bridge applies 950 of 1,050 cash to two invoices and parks 100 supported as customer-credit/unapplied liability pending identification. At next close, sales proposes moving 100 to revenue because no customer contacted finance for six months. Elapsed time alone does not prove obligation extinguishment. Investigate remittance, contract, refund rights, customer balance, errors/duplicate payments and jurisdiction-specific unclaimed-property or escheat requirements using current local legal advice. A newly signed order may allow application as deposit/contract liability or invoice settlement under agreed terms; link original 100 cash ID to new contract and obtain customer authorization. Do not net it against a different customer's AR to reduce bad debt.

**Dispute fork.** Customer disputes a 60 invoice due to nonconforming service. Evaluate whether service/control/revenue recognition was appropriate, whether a price concession/refund exists and whether credit loss also applies. A 60 short payment arising solely from insolvency instead routes to ECL, not revenue reversal. If dispute resolution approves a 25 credit and customer pays 35, reconcile invoice 60=credit 25+cash 35; document whether 25 adjusts price in current period or corrects a prior error based on dated facts. Ensure bank cash 35 is not double applied to another invoice. Supplier and customer credits are different legal relationships.

**QA.** Fail automatic 100 revenue on age, 100 offset against unrelated AR, or all 60 bad debt before resolving service concession. Retain aged unapplied cash by receipt/owner, contract, customer correspondence, legal conclusion, credit approval, bank, invoice/revenue memo, ECL and GL cross-check. IFRS 15/AASB 15, ASC 606 and FRS 102 Section 23 revenue route with relevant impairment model; local unclaimed property is a jurisdiction overlay, not invented here. [IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/).
