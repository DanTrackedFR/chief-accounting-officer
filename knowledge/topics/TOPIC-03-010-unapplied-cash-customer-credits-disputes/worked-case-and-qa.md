# Unapplied cash and dispute resolution case

Capabilities CAO-03-020/021. Existing practice records remain primary. This case shows why cash, customer credits, concessions and credit losses need distinct accounting owners.

## Receipt bridge

The bank receives 1,050 from Customer X. Supported remittance settles invoice 701 for 600 and invoice 702 for 350. The remaining 100 cannot yet be tied to an enforceable future supply, another invoice or a refund instruction. Dr cash 1,050; Cr AR 950; Cr customer-credit/unapplied-cash liability 100, assuming entity and currency alignment and no bank fees. A later signed order and permitted application may reclassify the 100 to a contract liability or settle an invoice; do not book revenue on receipt alone. **Executed:** 600+350=950 and 1,050−950=100. PASS arithmetic, account classification subject to contract/legal evidence.

## Dispute routing tree

Invoice 703 for 200 is short-paid by 30. Customer asserts a contractually specified service-level credit. The accountant obtains agreement, service logs, correspondence and approval. If valid and attributable to transaction price, process a 30 commercial credit/refund under the revenue model (TOPIC-03-007); if invoice was duplicated, correct the error; if service was properly delivered and customer cannot pay, evaluate credit loss (TOPIC-03-008); if it is a separate damages claim, assess applicable provision/legal guidance. The cash-application team may hold 30 open pending investigation, not create an automatic allowance or price concession. **Executed:** 200−170=30 dispute, no automatic P&L entry. PASS decision routing.

## Operational control and evidence

Daily reconcile bank file count and value to receipts ledger, then receipts ledger to AR applications and remaining customer credits. Preserve original bank ID, payer/entity/currency, remittance, invoice and contract IDs, application sequence, reason, reviewer, age and eventual settlement. Report 30/60/90-day aged credits separately from aged debit AR. Never offset different customers or entities merely to clear reconciliation. If an old credit has potential unclaimed-property obligations, route to jurisdictional counsel; do not credit revenue by aging policy.
