# TOPIC-02-004 — Trial Balance Review & Balance Sheet Reconciliations

Status: REVIEWED
Capabilities: CAO-02-009, CAO-02-010
Knowledge types: PRINCIPLES, PRACTICE
Framework sensitivity: LOW

## Objective
Prove material balance-sheet balances and identify TB anomalies before financial reporting.

## Reconciliation standard
A reconciliation is not a screenshot of the GL. It must compare the ledger balance to independent underlying support or a valid rollforward, explain reconciling items, establish ownership and age, and evidence review. The method must fit the account assertion: existence, completeness, valuation, rights/obligations, cut-off and classification.

## Reconciliation types
Subledger-to-GL; bank-to-GL; external statement; rollforward; schedule-to-GL; intercompany; system-to-system; zero-balance/clearing; reasonableness only when direct substantiation is impracticable and methodology is defensible.

## CAO workflow
1. Start from final/current TB and account ownership map.
2. Risk-tier accounts by materiality, complexity, judgment, volume, fraud/error exposure and history.
3. Select the strongest feasible independent support and assertion-specific method.
4. Reconcile opening balance + activity to closing balance where useful.
5. Identify differences rather than netting them away.
6. Age, classify and assign every reconciling item; distinguish timing from error.
7. Propose correcting entries and root-cause actions.
8. Reviewer challenges source independence, stale items, unexplained plugs and completeness.
9. Tie reconciled balances back to TB/reporting and certify.

## TB review
Scan unusual debit/credit signs; unexpected zeros; large/new movements; dormant accounts with activity; control accounts; suspense/clearing; intercompany; retained earnings/equity continuity; opening-balance continuity; new accounts; mapping anomalies; material manual journals.

## Controls
Account inventory completeness; frequency by risk; due dates; preparer/reviewer segregation; support standards; aging thresholds; escalation; no self-reconciliation of unsupported plugs; certification; stale-item governance; post-close exception reporting.

## Artifacts
Account inventory; reconciliation template; risk-tier matrix; reconciling-item log; TB review pack; aging dashboard; correction/root-cause tracker.

## Tests
A GL export compared to the same GL fails independence. A bank rec with an old unexplained difference is not complete because the total reconciles after a plug. A low-value account can still be high risk due to fraud or clearing activity. Zero balance does not eliminate reconciliation need where gross activity is significant.

## TrackedFR fit
Primary fit: recurring cross-system reconciliation, exception identification and data manipulation using ERP/subledger/bank/warehouse data with Excel as the working layer.
