# TOPIC-15-008 — Critical accounting estimate and judgment disclosure support

Status: DRAFT for independent QA. Capabilities: CAO-15-017, CAO-15-018. Prepared 2026-09-27.

## Principles, authority and applicability

Financial statement disclosures explain material judgments and sources of estimation uncertainty, with entity-specific assumptions and carrying amounts. SEC critical accounting estimate discussion is a separate jurisdictional narrative requirement, not a synonym for IFRS note wording. Resolve entity, framework, period, transaction and significance. Accounting policy/estimate/error classification is addressed in TOPIC-15-001, 15-002 and 15-006. The accounting standard governing a particular asset, liability or transaction controls its measurement.

Official source pointers, verified 2026-09-27: [IFRS IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/), [IFRS Practice Statement 2](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/materiality-practice-statement/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) (Periodic Review 2024 generally effective periods beginning 1 January 2026), [AASB 108](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-108-mar-2021) (compilation applicable from 1 January 2023 to before 1 January 2027), and [FASB Codification portal](https://asc.fasb.org/). IFRS 18 is effective periods beginning 1 January 2027 (early application possible); verify corresponding IAS 8 and presentation/disclosure amendments for that period. US ASC 250 covers changes/errors; ASC 275 and topic-specific guidance affect disclosures. Current paragraph-level FASB text is not verified in public access; do not imply precise US paragraph verification.

## Four-framework route and differences

- IFRS: IAS 8 policy/estimate/error architecture, with IAS 1 or IFRS 18 presentation/disclosure period gate and transaction-specific Standard.
- US GAAP: ASC 250 and relevant topic-specific requirements; SEC registrants require separate SEC filing/critical-estimate consideration. Audit performance materiality is set under auditing standards, not ASC 250.
- UK GAAP: FRS 102 Section 10 and topic-specific sections; check operative edition, SORP and UK company-law reporting separately.
- AASB: AASB 108 aligned core reasoning with independent Australian compilation, entity/tier and reduced-disclosure assessment.

Do not assume identical thresholds, disclosures, permitted elections or transition methods across frameworks. When precise divergence matters, verify the controlling provision for each reporting period; the public FASB portal may not expose paragraph bodies.

## CAO decision and workpaper

Build a disclosure inventory from estimate/judgment registers. For each item link the affected line, carrying amount, assumption, uncertainty range or reasonably possible change, time horizon, sensitivity and changed methodology. Match narrative to approved model and board forecasts; omit boilerplate that does not illuminate an entity-specific risk. Check IAS 1 or IFRS 18 effective-period presentation transition; ASC 275 and SEC MD&A where relevant; FRS 102 Section 8/other topic disclosure; AASB 101/18 and Tier 2 disclosures where applicable.

Required evidence: fact and learned-date chronology; authority/period/source register; original and updated calculation; assumptions and alternatives; GL and note cross-reference; reviewer challenge; approval; population and systems mapping. Core controls: complete inventory, periodic reassessment, review of significant changes, model/version access, data reconciliation, exception escalation, disclosure tie-out and audit evidence preservation. Record accounting entries only after classification and measurement, with debit/credit and period supported in the signed workpaper.

## Worked case

A 12m goodwill balance has 0.5m impairment headroom. A 0.2 percentage-point discount-rate increase would exhaust it. Disclose the relevant assumption/headroom sensitivity if required by the applicable impairment disclosure model, with consistent model values and affected CGU/segment.
