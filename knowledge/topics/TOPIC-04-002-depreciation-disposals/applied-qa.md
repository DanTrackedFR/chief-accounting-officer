# TOPIC-04-002 — depreciation and disposal calculation test

Retains the existing guide and the alternative `TOPIC-04-002-depreciation-useful-life-disposals/factory.md`. Capabilities CAO-04-004–006. Period-route [IAS 16](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-16-property-plant-and-equipment/), [AASB 116](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/), [FRS 102 Section 17](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) and [ASC 360](https://asc.fasb.org/), without assuming identical component or estimate rules. UK Periodic Review 2024 generally applies from 2026; current US paragraph bodies require authorized verification.

The [AASB 116 compilation](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-116-dec-2022) is period selected, and the [FRC September 2024 FRS 102 edition](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) is applied with its 2026 commencement/transition gate. Record whether the entity reports full Tier 1 or Tier 2 Australian disclosures; do not presume IAS 16 note completeness establishes AASB 1060 compliance.

## Reperformance example

A machine costs 600,000, residual value 60,000, initial useful life six years and is available at the beginning of year 1. Straight-line depreciation is (600,000 − 60,000)/6 = 90,000 annually. After years 1–2 the carrying amount is 420,000. At the beginning of year 3, credible engineering evidence changes remaining life to seven years, residual unchanged. Prospective annual charge is (420,000 − 60,000)/7 = 51,428.57, subject to rounding; do not restate the first two years simply because the estimate changed. At the end of year 3, carrying amount is 368,571.43. If sold then for 400,000 with no selling costs, gain is 31,428.57; Dr cash 400,000 and accumulated depreciation 231,428.57; Cr gross PPE 600,000 and gain 31,428.57. If it instead remains held for sale or abandonment is planned, route through the relevant classification and impairment rules before booking an ordinary disposal.

## Evidence and test outcomes

Tie acquisition and component costs to 04-001; prove availability date with commissioning acceptance, useful-life revision with engineering records and forecast utilization, residual value with market evidence, and proceeds with sale agreement/bank. The register must retain gross cost and accumulated depreciation separately rather than netting transfers through expense. A second reviewer independently recalculates the schedule and disposal entry, compares GL postings, and ties class movements to the note.

- **Idle machine:** continue depreciation under an applicable time-based method; reassess impairment and consumption pattern, not automatic suspension.
- **Significant short-lived component:** identify a separate component under IAS 16/AASB 116 and relevant UK rules; test the US model independently.
- **Revised useful life after damage:** change the estimate prospectively and separately test impairment under 04-006; estimate change does not repair a previously unsupported original life or an error.
- **Sale-and-leaseback:** route gain through 04-011, not the ordinary 31,428.57 disposal formula.

Desk arithmetic and journal balance pass on stated facts. US method-change classification and transaction-specific disposal require current Codification access before case approval.
