# Depreciation, estimate revision and disposal — completion method

Companion to the existing guide and worked 600,000 machine. CAO-04-004–006. Claim IDs refer to `standards-claims.json`.

## Framework and period routes

IFRS IAS 16 and AASB 116 depreciate significant components separately, review life/residual annually and start when available for use; idleness alone does not suspend depreciation. Method reflects consumption, with a PPE revenue-based method excluded. Revaluation and impairment are separate models. AASB 1060 determines Tier 2 disclosures [04-002-IFRS-01; 04-002-AASB-01]. FRS 102 Section 17 uses systematic depreciation, material component analysis and reassessment on significant-change indicators; do not import IAS 16's annual-review wording automatically. Section 27 is the impairment route. Select pre/post-2026 FRS 102 edition and transition [04-002-UK-01]. US cost allocation is systematic over supported useful life; componentization is not the blanket IAS 16 requirement. A new estimate of remaining life is prospective. A change in depreciation method can be a change in estimate effected by a change in principle under ASC 250; document the reason, prospective calculation and applicable disclosures rather than restating prior periods automatically [04-002-US-01]. Held-for-sale and abandonment classification precedes ordinary disposal entries [04-002-US-02].

The existing example recomputes: 540,000 depreciable amount / 6 = 90,000 per year. After two years carrying value 420,000. A new seven-year **remaining** life and unchanged 60,000 residual gives 360,000/7 = 51,428.57 year-3 charge; carrying value 368,571.43. Cash proceeds 400,000 yield gain 31,428.57; Dr cash 400,000 / Dr accumulated depreciation 231,428.57 / Cr PPE cost 600,000 / Cr gain 31,428.57. Both sides 631,428.57. If the revision arose from prior information that was available and omitted, route to error analysis rather than calling it a new estimate [04-002-IFRS-01; 04-002-US-01].

Alternative consumption boundary: if the machine is placed in service six months into a calendar year, first-year time-based charge is 45,000 on the same initial assumptions, not 90,000. If only the *method* changes from straight-line to units of production at year 3, document expected remaining units and period output; 420,000 carrying less 60,000 residual spread over 70,000 expected units gives 5.142857 per unit, or 51,428.57 for 10,000 units. The equal numerical result is coincidental and must not be used to infer identical consumption evidence [04-002-US-01; 04-002-IFRS-01].

## Execution and challenge

Register needs gross cost, accumulated depreciation, component ID, readiness date, life, residual, method, estimate history, transfer/held-for-sale status and disposal proceeds. Reconcile beginning gross + additions/transfers − removals to ending gross, and beginning accumulated + charge − removed accumulation to ending accumulation; match gain to GL and note. Reviewer inspects engineering update, actual usage, residual market data, commissioning and sale/bank evidence. Master-data edit rights and override log must be separate from journal approval. Annual IAS/AASB review and UK/US trigger review are separately evidenced [04-002-IFRS-01; 04-002-UK-01; 04-002-US-01].

Fail cases: idle asset depreciation stopped without a consumption-pattern change; cash received but gross cost not removed; impairment indicator ignored after life revision; sale-and-leaseback gain booked as ordinary disposal; held-for-sale asset continues ordinary depreciation after classification when the applicable model requires cessation. Presentation/disclosure must show class rollforward, methods/rates or lives, depreciation and impairment, with held-for-sale separately routed [04-002-US-02; 04-002-IFRS-02].
