# TOPIC-05-008 — evidence and adverse regression

Claims: [TOPIC-05-008-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-05-008-US-001], [TOPIC-05-008-UK-001], [TOPIC-05-008-AASB-001]. Existing methods and duplicate materials remain intact.

## Workpaper and boundary

Original equity award grant value 1,000 over four years, two years served: cumulative expense 500. A beneficial modification at year two adds modification-date incremental fair value 200 for the remaining two years. If all vest, recognize remaining original 500 plus incremental 200 over the remaining service, or 350 per year. A cancellation is not mechanically a forfeiture: evaluate acceleration/replacement separately. A cash settlement choice controlled by employee can imply liability/compound treatment; who controls the choice and past practice must be documented. US ASC 718 modification conditions, UK §26 and AASB 2 must not be assumed identical.

## Framework challenge and control

The IFRS proposition is tied to the identified issued edition. US public/private choices and operative ASC, UK FRS 102 edition, and AASB commencement/tier must each be checked for the same fact pattern. Reperform numerical entries and opposite branches. Retain source population, legal/contract terms, date-stamped valuation or bank/rate feeds, journal lineage, disclosure support and reviewer. The detailed non-IFRS claims remain in the audit queue; they do not prevent use of the substantive method with a disclosed source gate.
