# Cancellation, replacement and value-increasing modification

Retained IFRS 2 case: original 1,000 equity options × grant-date fair value 10 = 10,000, two-year service, year-one cost 5,000. At year-one modification, old terms fair value 8 and new terms 11: incremental modification-date value 3,000; remaining service cost 5,000 original +3,000 incremental =8,000, subject to service/vesting facts. **Different event:** entity cancels old unvested awards at year one and pays cash 7,000 with no replacement. Assess acceleration of remaining original grant-date expense and settlement treatment under IFRS 2; do not continue an 8,000 year-two modification schedule, because no revised award exists. A new award designated as replacement requires analysis of linkage and terms, not merely booking a fresh grant on top of cancellation.

**Classification change.** An existing cash-settled award liability is 40,000 on modification date and revised terms become equity-settled. IFRS 2 has specific classification-change requirements; do not automatically debit liability 40,000 and credit equity 40,000 with zero profit effect without measuring the equity-settled replacement and recognizing any difference as required. Conversely, an equity award switched to cash may create liability/remeasurement exposure. Preserve original/modified valuations, employee communications, modification date, settlement choices and whether award holders receive incremental benefit. US ASC 718 modification threshold, FRS 102 Section 26, AASB 2 and tax withholding differ; compute framework-specific result before entries.
