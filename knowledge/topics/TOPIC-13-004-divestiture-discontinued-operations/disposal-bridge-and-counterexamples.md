# Disposal measurement and discontinued-operation fork

**IFRS illustration.** A board approves sale of a component on 30 September X1. Management demonstrates the disposal group is available for immediate sale in its present condition and sale is highly probable under IFRS 5; obtain the committed plan, buyer process, regulatory conditions and expected completion date. Immediately before classification, remeasure constituent assets and liabilities under their own standards. The resulting group carrying amount is 330, including allocated goodwill 25. Expected sale cash is 400; directly attributable disposal costs 10; fair value less costs to sell is 390. The IFRS 5 group-level lower-of test does **not** create a 60 gain merely because 390 exceeds 330. If completed for those amounts, illustrative disposal gain is 400−10−330=60, after independently assessing pre-closing changes, taxes and retained interests. Reconcile 330 to the disposal-perimeter TB, leases, intercompany elimination, goodwill allocation and working-capital/debt definitions in the SPA. The transaction price is not automatically group fair value or net cash proceeds.

**Negative measurement case.** If revised fair value less costs to sell falls to 310 at the classification date, the indicated group write-down is 20 before considering IFRS 5 allocation restrictions and pre-test measurements. Do not allocate indiscriminately to excluded assets or liabilities; preserve the asset-level premeasurement and allocation waterfall. A later reversal cannot exceed relevant cumulative loss ceilings. Tie the 20 to valuation and the final 310 carrying amount before calculating an eventual disposal result.

**Presentation fork.** A profitable small product group can qualify as held for sale but fail the IFRS discontinued-operation definition; keep its operating results in continuing operations with applicable held-for-sale presentation. A separate major geographical line disposed of or qualifying as held for sale may trigger discontinued results and comparative re-presentation. Assessment follows the component and strategic significance, not whether a press release calls it “non-core.” Retained transitional services and supply arrangements need separate recognition and disclosure; document continuing involvement. Under US GAAP, assess ASC 205-20's strategic-shift threshold and applicable ASC 360 measurement independently; under FRS 102 assess the relevant presentation and impairment rules rather than imposing IFRS 5 measurement by analogy; AASB 5 uses the operative Australian compilation. This example is IFRS-only arithmetic.

**Release control.** Reviewer tests date-specific criteria, journal population, valuation sensitivity, gain bridge, cash-versus-accounting consideration, comparative income/cash-flow presentation and note tie-out. A planned sale signed after year-end does not backdate classification.
