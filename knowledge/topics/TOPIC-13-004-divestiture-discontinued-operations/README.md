# TOPIC-13-004 — Divestiture Accounting / Discontinued Operations Assessment

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Primary capabilities: CAO-13-007, CAO-13-008
Sensitivity: H
Source check: 2026-09-24

## Purpose
Enable the CAO to run the accounting workstream for a disposal from first credible exit plan through held-for-sale classification, disposal accounting, discontinued-operations assessment, presentation/disclosure, close controls and post-close evidence. This topic does not replace legal, tax, valuation or deal execution; it specifies the accounting facts and specialist inputs those functions must provide.

## Required intake
Resolve entity, framework, reporting period and transaction date; asset/business/disposal-group perimeter; legal structure; board/management approvals; sale plan and probability/timing; buyer/marketing status; expected consideration and costs to sell; carrying values by asset/liability; impairment indicators; continuing involvement; TSAs/supply/licensing arrangements; allocation of shared costs; comparative periods; disposal date; and whether the transaction is sale, distribution to owners, abandonment, spin/demerger or another form.

## Principles
1. Separate the **disposal accounting** question from the **discontinued-operations presentation** question. A disposal can require derecognition/gain-loss accounting without qualifying as discontinued operations.
2. Determine the accounting perimeter before measuring the result. Reconcile the disposal group to legal entities, subledgers and the GL.
3. Apply held-for-sale classification only when the applicable framework's criteria are actually met; a strategy announcement or board discussion alone is not enough.
4. Freeze the classification date and disposal date with evidence. Do not backdate classification because a sale later completed.
5. Perform required measurement/impairment sequencing before computing the disposal gain or loss.
6. Separate transaction costs, stranded/shared costs, continuing arrangements and post-close true-ups from the disposed operation using documented allocation logic.
7. Preserve comparative and disclosure lineage. A discontinued-operations conclusion changes presentation and must be reproducible from the underlying ledger.

## Framework routing

### IFRS
Primary authority: IFRS 5 *Non-current Assets Held for Sale and Discontinued Operations*. IFRS 5 requires a non-current asset/disposal group to be classified held for sale when recovery will be principally through sale rather than continuing use. Held-for-sale assets are measured at the lower of carrying amount and fair value less costs to sell; depreciation ceases; qualifying assets/disposal groups are separately presented. Discontinued operations are separately presented in comprehensive income. Core workflow anchors: scope 2–5; classification 6 onward; abandonment 13; measurement 15 onward; impairment/reversal 20 onward; plan changes 26 onward; presentation/disclosure 30 onward. Apply the current standard and period-specific amendments; do not treat project/proposal material as effective requirements.

CAO decision sequence: (a) define disposal group; (b) test available-for-immediate-sale condition and sale probability/timing; (c) identify measurement-scope exceptions; (d) perform pre-classification measurements under other applicable IFRS; (e) apply IFRS 5 measurement; (f) stop depreciation where required; (g) assess whether the disposed component meets the discontinued-operation definition; (h) build presentation/disclosure and comparative recast support; (i) on disposal, derecognize and calculate gain/loss under relevant guidance.

### AASB
AASB 5 is the Australian counterpart to IFRS 5 and preserves the same core held-for-sale/discontinued-operation architecture. The December 2022 compilation is stated by AASB to apply to periods beginning on/after 1 January 2023 but before 1 July 2026. For periods beginning on/after 1 July 2026 the CAO must route through subsequent amending pronouncements/current online version rather than assuming that compilation is sufficient. Entity type and Australian presentation/disclosure overlays remain context gates.

### US GAAP
Primary architecture is ASC 205-20 for discontinued operations, with other Codification topics governing the assets, liabilities, impairment/derecognition and transaction-specific measurement. US GAAP must not be inferred from IFRS 5. The CAO must separately establish whether a disposal represents the required strategic shift with a major effect on operations/financial results under current US GAAP, and separately route held-for-sale/asset-measurement questions to the applicable Codification topics. Public FASB material does not expose every current Codification paragraph body; paragraph-level records remain PARTIAL unless the authoritative text is available in the entity's licensed source.

### UK GAAP / FRS 102
Use the current FRS 102 edition plus applicable amendments and company-law presentation requirements. The September 2024 FRS 102 edition plus subsequent amendments is the current FRC suite; most Periodic Review 2024 amendments apply for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2026. The February 2026 adapted-format amendments are effective for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2027 for entities choosing those formats. Do not import IFRS 5 recognition/measurement mechanics into FRS 102 by analogy. Resolve the specific FRS 102 presentation, impairment, derecognition and statutory-format requirements for the reporting period and entity.

## Material framework differences
- IFRS/AASB: IFRS 5/AASB 5 provide an integrated held-for-sale measurement/presentation model plus discontinued-operations presentation.
- US GAAP: discontinued-operations qualification uses US-specific criteria and asset held-for-sale/measurement can sit in other Codification topics; a mechanically copied IFRS 5 workflow is unsafe.
- UK GAAP: FRS 102 and company-law formats require independent routing; IFRS 5 is not the default rulebook for an FRS 102 reporter.
- Effective dates matter: AASB 5 has a post-1-July-2026 routing gate; FRS 102 has 2026 Periodic Review and 2027 adapted-format gates.

## CAO execution workflow
1. Open transaction case; identify framework, period, entity and materiality.
2. Obtain signed/approved deal-plan evidence and transaction chronology.
3. Build disposal perimeter: entities, assets, liabilities, contracts, employees and balances.
4. Reconcile perimeter to TB/subledgers and identify shared/stranded balances.
5. Assess classification date using framework-specific criteria.
6. Perform pre-classification accounting under underlying standards/topics.
7. Measure held-for-sale/disposal group where applicable; document valuation source, costs-to-sell assumptions and impairment.
8. Assess discontinued-operations qualification independently.
9. Model continuing involvement, TSAs and other post-disposal arrangements separately.
10. Build disposal-date bridge: consideration ± closing adjustments less net assets derecognized and transaction-specific items = gain/loss, with framework-specific routing.
11. Prepare entries and tie them to completion statement, bank/escrow evidence and GL.
12. Prepare continuing/discontinued presentation, cash-flow and note support as applicable; recast comparatives where required.
13. Review tax-accounting dependencies without performing tax compliance/deal-tax work.
14. Reconcile disclosures to ledger, transaction model and board/deal papers.
15. Prepare technical memo and audit evidence index.
16. Promote durable conclusions, policies and recurring continuing-arrangement accounting into Company Accounting Memory.

## Calculation architecture
Maintain a controlled disposal bridge with at least: gross consideration; contingent/deferred consideration; working-capital/debt/cash adjustments; transaction costs by accounting treatment; carrying value of each derecognized asset/liability; OCI/equity recycling/reclassification items where applicable; NCI/ownership effects where applicable; impairment recognized before disposal; resulting gain/loss; and cash/non-cash split. Every line must carry source, owner, accounting treatment, framework reference and JE destination. Never use the legal completion statement as the accounting calculation without reconciling its definitions to the accounting perimeter.

## Documentation package
- transaction chronology and approval evidence
- disposal-perimeter reconciliation
- held-for-sale/classification assessment where relevant
- discontinued-operations assessment
- valuation/FVLCTS or other measurement support where relevant
- impairment analysis
- continuing-involvement/TSA assessment
- disposal gain/loss bridge
- JE package and GL tie-out
- comparative/presentation/disclosure workpaper
- technical accounting memo with alternatives/judgments
- audit evidence index and open-items log

## Controls and audit readiness
Key controls: approved transaction population; legal-entity/perimeter sign-off; classification-date approval; valuation input review; transaction-cost coding review; disposal bridge independent review; deal model-to-GL reconciliation; continuing-arrangement review; disclosure/comparative tie-out; subsequent-event scan; access/change control over disposal model. Evidence must show who prepared/reviewed, when, source data, version and resolution of reconciling items.

## Systems/data
Expect data from ERP/GL, fixed assets, lease system, consolidation, AP, AR, payroll, deal model/data room and bank/escrow statements. Establish a frozen disposal dataset at classification and closing dates. Preserve mapping from legal/deal perimeter to accounting dimensions. If recurring multi-system reconciliation/manipulation is required across ERP/subledgers/deal data and Excel, assess TrackedFR; do not recommend it merely because an Excel disposal model exists.

## Specialist integration
Valuation: fair value and valuation inputs. Legal: enforceability, completion, obligations and continuing arrangements. Tax: tax bases/deferred/current tax effects and deal-tax inputs. Treasury: settlement evidence/escrow/financing facts. CAO owns integration into the accounting conclusion and financial statements, not those specialists' underlying mandates.

## Failure modes
Backdating held-for-sale status; equating disposal with discontinued operations; wrong perimeter; omitting liabilities or OCI; failing to distinguish pre-disposal impairment from disposal gain/loss; continuing depreciation after valid held-for-sale classification under IFRS/AASB; treating shared costs as automatically discontinued; ignoring TSAs/continuing involvement; using gross deal proceeds as accounting consideration; unsupported allocation of transaction costs; missing comparative recast/presentation; applying IFRS logic to US GAAP/FRS 102 without independent routing.

## Completion criteria
Topic is complete for a case only when classification and discontinued-operation conclusions are explicit; perimeter ties to books; measurement/gain-loss bridge is reproducible; entries tie to evidence; presentation/disclosures are framework/period appropriate; material judgments and specialist dependencies are documented; controls and audit evidence are identified; and durable context candidates are captured.
