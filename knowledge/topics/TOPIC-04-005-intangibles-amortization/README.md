# TOPIC-04-005 — Intangible Asset Recognition & Amortization

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Built: 2026-09-23
Capabilities: CAO-04-013, CAO-04-014

## Source map
IFRS IAS 38; AASB 138; FRS 102 Section 18; US GAAP ASC 350 and specialized intangible guidance. Official IFRS, AASB and FRC source families checked 2026-09-23.

## Recognition
Start by identifying the resource and accounting scope. An intangible is non-monetary and without physical substance; identifiability normally arises from separability or contractual/legal rights. Establish control, future benefits and reliable measurement under the applicable framework. Purchased assets, business-combination intangibles and internally generated items have different recognition routes. Internally generated goodwill/brands/customer-list-type items are restricted/prohibited under IFRS/AASB and require framework-specific treatment elsewhere.

## IFRS / AASB
IAS 38/AASB 138 recognize qualifying intangibles at cost and distinguish finite from indefinite useful lives. Finite-lived assets are amortized systematically from when available for use and tested for impairment when indicated. Indefinite-lived intangibles are not amortized but require annual impairment testing and annual reconsideration of indefinite status. Revaluation is permitted only where the required active-market conditions exist, which is uncommon. Research/development routes to TOPIC-04-004.

AASB 138 contains Australian overlays, including NFP considerations; reporting tier affects disclosures.

## UK GAAP
FRS 102 Section 18 differs materially from full IFRS because all intangible assets are treated as finite-lived. If useful life cannot be reliably estimated, the life must not exceed 10 years under the current model. Amortization begins when available for use; method reflects consumption and straight-line is used when pattern cannot be determined reliably. Section 18.27 requires class-level useful-life/rate, method, gross/accumulated balances and rollforward disclosures. Do not import IFRS indefinite-life treatment into FRS 102.

## US GAAP
ASC 350 distinguishes finite-lived and indefinite-lived intangible assets. Finite-lived assets are amortized over estimated useful life; indefinite-lived assets are not amortized and follow the applicable impairment model. Useful life considers legal, regulatory, contractual, competitive, economic and other factors. Specialized software/goodwill guidance can override/generalize the route; software routes to TOPIC-04-004 and goodwill to TOPIC-04-006/business-combination topics.

## CAO workflow
1. Resolve framework, period, acquisition origin and asset nature.
2. Establish identifiability/control/rights and correct specialized guidance.
3. Determine initial cost/fair-value route.
4. For internally generated assets invoke R&D/software criteria.
5. Determine finite/indefinite status where framework permits.
6. For finite life: determine legal/contractual cap, expected use, obsolescence, renewals, dependencies, residual value and amortization method.
7. Establish available-for-use date and amortization schedule.
8. Review useful life/method/status and impairment triggers each reporting period as required.
9. Reconcile intangible register to GL/disclosures.

## Key differences
- IFRS/AASB and US GAAP permit indefinite-life classification when criteria are met; FRS 102 does not.
- IFRS/AASB revaluation requires active-market conditions and is rare; US GAAP does not provide a general upward revaluation model for ordinary intangibles.
- Internally generated development/software recognition differs sharply, handled in TOPIC-04-004.

## Controls/evidence
Contract/legal-right evidence; purchase/PPA documentation; capitalization memo; available-for-use approval; useful-life support; renewal assumptions; amortization-run control; indefinite-life reassessment; impairment trigger review; register-to-GL reconciliation; disclosure rollforward.

## Scenarios
1. Perpetually renewable licence under IFRS: assess indefinite life, renewal economics and impairment; do not assume no amortization without support.
2. Same fact pattern under FRS 102: indefinite life is unavailable; determine finite useful life.
3. Purchased patent with 8 years legal life but 5 years expected economic use: use shorter supported economic life.
4. Internally generated brand: do not capitalize under IAS 38/AASB 138 merely because valuation exists.

## QA
PASS. R&D/software, impairment and business-combination valuation are linked dependencies rather than duplicated.
