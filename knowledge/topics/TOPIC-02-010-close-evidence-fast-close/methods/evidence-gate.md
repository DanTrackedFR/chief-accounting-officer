# Close evidence, sign-off and fast close — gate

Extends existing README/factory. Capabilities CAO-02-021–022. PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE; 2026-09-27.

## Evidence hierarchy

Each task has a preparer assertion, source population, calculation/bridge, exceptions, reviewer action and immutable time. Subledger owner certifies completeness and reconciliation; entity controller certifies material GL accounts, journals, estimates and unresolved items; group controller certifies consolidation and reporting tie-out; CAO signs the risk/quality conclusion with disclosed exceptions. A late signature does not retroactively prove timely operation. Control evidence must show what was compared, at what precision and how anomalies were resolved.

Fast-close assessment measures both elapsed time and rework. Build a Pareto from three closes: upstream arrival, processing, waiting for review, system outage and accounting judgment. Move stable work earlier, automate deterministic checks and establish pre-close estimate plus controlled true-up only where appropriate. Never truncate input populations or remove independent review solely to gain a day.

**Illustration:** 200 due reconciliations, 190 approved by lock (95%). The ten late include cash and payroll liabilities of 1.2m with 80,000 unresolved gross items. Lock cannot be declared clean on a 95% count; escalate affected balances, source evidence, quantitative/qualitative exposure, sign-off authority and reopen/late-adjustment route. Conversely, a low-risk immaterial recon delay with documented compensating evidence may be accepted under approved policy; decision and reviewer must be explicit.

Controls and systems: task/dependency ID, source extract as-of time and parameters, journal/subledger totals, reviewer challenge, exception log, versioned snapshots, period lock/reopen permissions and read-only archive. Audit packet links source → workpaper → adjustment/decision → GL/reporting output. Retain failed runs and superseded versions. **Failure injection:** scorecard shows day-4 close but backdated sign-offs and day-6 material entries. Expected FAIL and root-cause/deficiency assessment. Result PASS for route. Accounting standards govern underlying transactions, not a universal fast-close target. Dependencies: 02-001/004/005/008, 09-005, 10-001, 11-003.
