# IPO and public-company accounting readiness — gated diagnostic

Knowledge type: PRINCIPLES / PRACTICE with JURISDICTION overlay. Capabilities: CAO-01-017, CAO-01-018. Extends existing README/completion note; prepared 2026-09-27.

## Scope before checklist

Record target exchange and filing path, issuer domicile and classification, framework(s), fiscal year, historical audited periods, group perimeter, acquisitions, expected timetable and advisers. Confirm current filing and control obligations against the actual facts with qualified securities/legal and audit advisers. US SEC examples do not automatically apply to an Australian, UK or other listing; a US foreign private issuer may have a different route from a US domestic issuer. Reassess when classification or timetable changes.

Build the gap register by workstream: historical statements and audit evidence; close/subledger/GL quality; GAAP conversion and policies; estimates and judgments; consolidation and equity; disclosures and non-GAAP metrics; controls and system access; corporate governance; staffing and backup; tax and legal entity data. For each gap record source requirement or internal readiness criterion, applicability, owner, predecessor, evidence of completion, risk/critical path, due date and reviewer. Do not turn unverified filing dates or reliefs into hardcoded requirements.

## Worked critical path and benchmark

Suppose registration is targeted in 18 months. Two historical audit periods need reconstruction (estimated 6 months), then auditor testing (4 months), while monthly cash reconciliation has not operated. Even if the nominal tasks sum to 10 months, a missing control history may require repeated operating periods and unexpected evidence reconstruction. Mark timetable conditional until the cash process has documented source-to-GL reconciliations, independent reviews and exception resolution for a sustained period agreed with advisers. Maintain contingency for acquisition accounting and disclosure revisions.

Peer A reports a four-day close with one entity and no post-close adjustments; target company closes in eight days across 12 entities and has 15 late journals. Normalize group complexity, transaction volume, GAAP, acquisition history, reporting frequency, staffing scope and adjustment definition before comparing. Even a normalized benchmark is diagnostic, not proof of readiness. Report quality and audit findings beside speed.

## Authority/effective-period gate

For a US issuer, [SEC Release 33-8810](https://www.sec.gov/rule-release/33-8810) provides management ICFR evaluation guidance and [PCAOB AS 2201](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201) addresses integrated audit work. Both checked 2026-09-27; exact reporting/attestation applicability and phase-in depend on classification, filing path and current rules and remain **case-specific**. For IFRS reporting, [IFRS 18](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/) becomes effective for annual periods beginning on/after 1 January 2027 unless early applied; evaluate presentation project timing. UK FRS 102 Periodic Review 2024 has principal effective date 1 January 2026 per [FRC](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). Do not substitute these general references for target-jurisdiction filing advice.

**Failure injection:** benchmark says ready because close is day 5, but quarterly financial package cannot tie to legal-entity GL. Decision **FAIL**; repair data lineage and certification before readiness claim. Dependencies: Domains 02, 08, 09, 10, 11 and 16; source-to-artifact lineage and management accountability stay with the CAO.
