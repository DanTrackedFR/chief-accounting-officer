## Authority and framework boundary

This is principally CAO practice, not a stand-alone IFRS/ASC/FRS/AASB recognition rule. Resolve the underlying accounting item in its technical topic and jurisdiction/period before any posting or external assertion. For a US issuer, the [SEC management guidance](https://www.sec.gov/files/info/smallbus/404guide/intro.shtml) and PCAOB [AS 2201](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201) are relevant to ICFR assessment and audit context; general control and assurance references are context, not an automatic obligation for all entities. Source checks: SEC management ICFR guidance (2007; [official page](https://www.sec.gov/files/info/smallbus/404guide/intro.shtml)) where US issuer status applies; PCAOB [AS 1105](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS1105) for PCAOB audit evidence context. Neither substitutes for applicable accounting standards. Exact local public-company requirements, filing deadlines and paragraph-level citations remain case-specific and require verification.

## Readiness gates and comparative benchmark discipline

Build a gap register by entity and intended filing regime: audited historical periods, framework conversion, consolidation and purchase accounting, recurring close, disclosure process, control design and operation, auditor capacity, governance, systems access, equity records, tax and legal interfaces. Record a due date, evidence criterion and dependency for each item. Confirm first filing/registration path and reliefs with counsel/auditors; an IPO target date is a planning assumption, not an authoritative filing requirement. If issuer classification changes, rerun the requirement matrix.

Benchmark only comparable companies with a defined revenue model, entity count, transaction volume, reporting complexity, framework and stage. Pair close speed and headcount with error rates, post-close adjustments, audit findings, control coverage and reporting quality. Do not use a low finance cost ratio to justify removing necessary review capacity. Model hiring against the future workload with a transition buffer and failure-contingency plan.

**Control failure probe:** a peer closes in four days while the company closes in eight; reducing the timetable without proving billing-to-GL completeness does not meet readiness. Gate advance on reconciled, reviewed monthly evidence over repeated periods.
