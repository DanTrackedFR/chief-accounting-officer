# TOPIC-01-008 — IPO / Public-Company Accounting Readiness & Benchmarking

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**
Capabilities: CAO-01-017, CAO-01-018
Knowledge type: PRINCIPLES / PRACTICE / JURISDICTION GATE

## CAO execution logic
Assess readiness against the target market and reporting path rather than a generic checklist. Establish the reporting basis, historical financial statements, close and reporting cadence, audit status, policies, estimates, controls, systems and data, governance, technical-accounting capacity and disclosure ownership. Benchmark only after normalizing scope and definitions. Convert gaps into sequenced remediation with owners, evidence and dependencies.

## Required artifacts
Readiness diagnostic; jurisdiction requirement map; gap/risk register; reporting and controls roadmap; staffing/adviser plan; benchmark pack with normalized definitions.

## Controls and evidence
Jurisdiction and effective-date gates are explicit. Readiness claims require evidence. Benchmark data must be comparable before conclusions. Management retains accounting conclusions when advisers assist.

## Scenario QA
1. Cross-border listing: route filing, internal-control and auditor requirements by actual jurisdiction rather than treating them as GAAP requirements. **PASS**
2. Peer closes in four days: reject comparison until close scope and post-close adjustments are normalized. **PASS**
3. Acquisition history lacks required audit evidence: make historical reporting an early dependency in the readiness roadmap. **PASS**

## Framework/source routing
Accounting recognition and measurement questions route to the applicable technical topic. Listing, filing, internal-control and audit requirements are jurisdiction-specific and require current authoritative-source verification before use.

## Completion evidence
Both mapped capabilities, workflow, outputs, controls and executable scenarios are covered. REVIEWED is internal factory status, not external approval.
