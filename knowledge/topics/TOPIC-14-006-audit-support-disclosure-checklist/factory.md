# Audit support papers and disclosure checklist — applied factory

Retains original README. CAO-14-016/017. Source check 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for execution method; actual framework/filing requirements still entity-specific.

## Audit paper as a reperformable result

Identify assertion and conclusion, entity/framework/period, complete source population and extraction parameters, procedure, sample or full-population basis, calculation, exceptions, alternative judgments, GL/FS tie and reviewer. An attachment without a reconciliation or conclusion is not an audit paper. Differentiate management assertion from auditor's sampling and performance materiality; the auditor designs the latter. For each material estimate, attach original-period data snapshot and change chronology to protect against hindsight. The source hierarchy routes IFRS through [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/) and relevant topic, US through [FASB Codification](https://asc.fasb.org/) and SEC overlay if applicable, UK through operative [FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/), Australian through operative [AASB](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/) and tier. A secondary checklist can prompt completeness but cannot replace the official rule.

## Disclosure checklist schema and worked tie

Each line carries source requirement/version, applicability facts, reason for N/A, owner, evidence, draft statement/note location, quantitative source, reviewer and disposition. Start with legal filer and period, then identify effective Standard amendments, required notes and regulator form. Reconcile every note total back to signed schedules and financial statements; compare disclosed narrative to technical papers and controls. If receivables note reports gross AR 5.5m, allowance 215k and net 5.285m, arithmetic ties (5.5m−0.215m=5.285m), but completeness still requires the 5.5m to reconcile to subledger and GL. If net BS presents 5.3m due rounding, explain rounding policy and detect any category mismatch. Do not mark a Tier 2 AASB disclosure N/A merely because full IFRS differs; check [AASB 1060](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-1060-aug-2025) for 2026 periods.

## Controls and challenge tests

An auditor's prepared-by-client request should be logged with requester, question, agreed population, original due date, source owner, response version and closure; a later changed request is a new version, not an overwrite. Where sampling is requested, preserve a complete frozen population with unique transaction IDs and reconciliation to the recorded total before handing over individual invoices. For confidential customer, employee or legal documents, use authorized access and redaction that does not remove the facts required for reperformance. A late adjusted journal requires the same disclosure checklist line and audit-support workpaper to be refreshed. Never represent a preliminary draft as an issued financial statement.

Current-checklist version control; completeness mapping to new transactions and amendments; IPE reliability; reviewer sample reperformance; numeric and textual tie-outs; final freeze and late-change impact. S1: current-year new share award but prior-year checklist reused → FAIL scope. S2: no applicable party transaction note and N/A has documented facts/source → conditional PASS. S3: 5.5m−0.215m=5.285m → arithmetic PASS, population still tested. S4: auditor sample support from another entity's GL → FAIL identity. S5: disclosure note conflicts with approved impairment memo → escalation. Evidence: source register, populated checklist, source-to-note mapping, audit workpapers, reviewer comments and approved final draft.
