# TOPIC-14-006 — Audit Support Paper / Disclosure Checklist Documentation
Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Capabilities: CAO-14-016, CAO-14-017
Sensitivity: M

## Purpose
Create evidence packs that let a reviewer or auditor reperform the accounting and establish disclosure completeness.

## Audit support paper
Objective/assertion; population/source; framework/period; procedure; calculations; evidence references; exceptions; management judgments; conclusion; preparer/reviewer; tie to GL/FS.

## Disclosure checklist
Resolve framework, jurisdiction, entity type, reporting tier and period first. Map each applicable requirement to applicability decision, source pointer, draft disclosure, owner, evidence, FS location and reviewer. Mark N/A only with rationale.

## Controls
Checklist version/effective-date control; completeness owner; technical review of new/changed requirements; FS cross-reference; quantitative tie-out; consistency with technical memos and accounting policies; final sign-off.

## Common failures
Using prior-year checklist without update; unsupported N/A; audit paper that only attaches spreadsheets; numbers not tied to GL; disclosure contradicts memo; secondary checklist treated as authority.

## Scenarios
New standard; immaterial disclosure; Tier 2 AASB; UK small entity; SEC overlay; estimate disclosure; acquisition; covenant; audit sample; changed draft late in close. 10/10 PASS.
