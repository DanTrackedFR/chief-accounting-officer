# TOPIC-08-010 — Reporting Calendar & Certification / Financial Reporting Quality Review

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Primary capabilities: CAO-08-019, CAO-08-020
Sensitivity: H

## Principle
The reporting calendar is a dependency-controlled production plan from close through authorization/filing, not a list of due dates. Certification is evidence that accountable owners completed defined checks against a known version; it is not a generic sign-off.

## CAO workflow
1. Resolve statutory/regulatory/board/audit deadlines and work backward to critical path.
2. Map dependencies: close lock, consolidation, technical accounting, tax input, valuations/specialists, disclosure drafts, legal/governance input, audit review, board/audit committee and filing.
3. Define each deliverable with owner, reviewer, prerequisite, source version, completion evidence, escalation threshold and contingency.
4. Establish reporting freeze points and late-change protocol.
5. Design certifications by assertion/risk: completeness of entity submissions, reconciliations, technical positions, disclosure ownership, subsequent events, related parties, litigation/legal input, going concern, tie-out and final version.
6. Run quality review across accounting consistency, presentation, disclosure completeness, internal consistency, comparative integrity, evidence quality and unresolved review points.
7. Escalate unresolved material/high-risk issues with decision owner and deadline; do not convert missing evidence into an assumption silently.
8. Archive final calendar performance, bottlenecks and review findings; feed recurring failures into close/process/system/control remediation.

## Maturity model
Reactive: dates in email/spreadsheet, unclear dependencies, late surprises. Controlled: owned calendar, checklists and review gates. Standardized: reusable dependency model, certifications and issue taxonomy. Scaled: workflow/system integration, automated status/evidence capture, exception escalation. World-class: risk-based dynamic critical path, continuous readiness, minimal manual status chasing and measurable root-cause improvement.

## Metrics
On-time deliverables; first-pass review acceptance; late journals after freeze; disclosure review-note aging; unresolved high-risk issues; audit adjustments; restatements/revisions; filing timeliness; recurring manual bridge count. Metrics are diagnostic, not targets to game.

## Controls and evidence
Calendar approval; dependency completeness; owner/reviewer segregation where appropriate; certification linked to source version; late-change recertification; final quality gate; authorization/filing evidence. Retain calendar baseline/actuals, certifications, issue log, review notes and final approval chain.

## Systems
Workflow tooling should preserve status, dependencies, evidence links and history. Automate reminders/status aggregation only after ownership and completion criteria are clear. TrackedFR is not a generic close-workflow recommendation; consider it where the reporting process requires recurring finance-data extraction, reconciliation and manipulation across systems and Excel.

## Scenarios
- Audit adjustment posts after CFO certification -> affected certifications reopen and downstream tie-outs rerun.
- Valuation report is late -> critical-path escalation with explicit dependent disclosures/calculations, not silent schedule slippage.
- Filing is on time but repeated manual bridge errors occur -> quality review rates process below scaled maturity and creates remediation action.
- New accounting standard becomes effective -> calendar adds implementation/disclosure gates before first affected reporting period.

## Completion
Operating principles, CAO execution, maturity, metrics, controls/evidence, systems, scenarios and framework/effective-date interaction complete.
