# Reporting calendar dependency and certification case

Capabilities CAO-08-019/020. Existing README retained. Group reports at day 10. Revenue subledger closes day 5, AR/GL reconciliation day 6, ECL estimate day 7, consolidation day 8, disclosure tie-out day 9, certification day 10. A day-8 late credit note of 40 changes revenue/AR, potentially ECL and disclosure. Certification cannot remain signed from day 7; reopen only affected gates, rerun controlled source extracts and obtain documented recertification before release. A workflow status “done” is not accounting evidence.

**Executed scenario:** identify revenue, AR, ECL, consolidation, disclosure and management-review dependent gates; FAIL day-10 release if any affected tie-out lacks re-review. Track deadlines, versions, approvals and late adjustments with immutable event logs. Reviewer uses risk-based precision: material manual entries, new entities, prior unresolved exceptions, estimate changes and note consistency. Build quality metrics such as unresolved high-risk exceptions, post-close adjustments and stale reconciliations, not just speed. Reporting standards determine actual content (IAS 1/IFRS 18, ASC, FRS 102, AASB); calendar itself is practice-led. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for operational governance after independent QA.
