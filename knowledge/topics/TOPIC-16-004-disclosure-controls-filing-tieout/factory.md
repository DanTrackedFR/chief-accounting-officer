# Disclosure controls and filing tie-out — substantive factory

Companion to existing README. Capabilities CAO-16-008/009. Source checked 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for cross-filing execution and evidence; a registrant's actual certification obligations must be checked.

## Jurisdiction, accounting and control boundary

Disclosure controls are broader than the accounting balance control: they capture, process, summarize and report information required in a filing on time. For a US registrant, review [SEC staff guidance on Exchange Act Rules 13a-15/15d-15](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/corporation-finance-interpretations/exchange-act-rules), applicable certification requirements and form instructions, distinguishing disclosure controls from ICFR. For UK statutory accounts, Companies House submission and directors' approval have separate company-law requirements. For an EU regulated issuer, ESEF tagging is part of filing integrity. For Australian entities, the directors' declaration, audit and lodgement route follows the relevant Corporations Act/ASIC obligations. None of those replaces GAAP recognition and disclosure assessment.

## Control architecture

Apply the regulator's current delivery and format guidance as another control input: the [SEC Inline XBRL overview](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/structured-data/inline-xbrl) addresses US machine-readable reporting, while [ESMA electronic reporting](https://www.esma.europa.eu/issuer-disclosure/electronic-reporting) describes ESEF for EU issuers in scope. Confirm the actual filer and year before selecting either.

Build a disclosure index with every statutory/form section and significant accounting note; map each assertion to management source, owner, preparer, reviewer, independent challenge, version, materiality conclusion and final location. Test completeness against current requirements and new-business change log, not merely the prior-year document. Reperform arithmetic, note-to-face and narrative-to-statement tie-outs; reconcile printed and tagged versions. For qualitative assertions (legal disputes, cyber events, climate, liquidity), obtain accountable specialist sign-off and test consistency with board papers, technical memos and financial model. Escalate contradictions rather than forcing a superficial wording match.

Set tolerances by assertion and risk. A zero-tolerance control for statement tie-out differs from a sensitivity review where estimates legitimately use different horizons. A management review control documents the population, threshold, actual items challenged and corrections; a signature without evidence of precision is insufficient. Assess information produced by the entity (IPE): report logic, parameters, extraction date, population, interface completeness and access/change controls. After final freeze, a material late change invalidates affected review evidence and requires targeted rerun, tagging, certification and board/audit-team communication as appropriate.

## Reperformance case

A 10-K draft reports 5.2m outstanding shares on the cover and an EPS note denominator of 5.0m. Both can be correct: 5.2m may be period-end shares while 5.0m is weighted average. The control verifies labels, dates, method, equity register and EPS schedule. In contrast, a 10.0m debt principal in the covenant section versus 9.6m in the debt note needs a quantified 0.4m bridge (for example unamortized discount or reporting date); an unexplained difference is a failed control. Log original value, revision, reviewer and all affected text/tag locations.
