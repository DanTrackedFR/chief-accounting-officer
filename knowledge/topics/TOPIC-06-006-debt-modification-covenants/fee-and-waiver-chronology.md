# Modification fee population and covenant date chronology

**IFRS quantitative test with fee isolation.** Old liability carrying amount and PV of old remaining cash flows at original effective interest rate are assumed 1,000 for this illustration. Revised cash flows PV excluding fees is 1,085. Qualifying fee paid by borrower to lender 20, and separate legal adviser fee 10. Assess the direction of lender fee in cash-flow comparison under IFRS 9 application guidance; for this illustration assume lender payment increases borrower modified cash outflow PV to 1,105, giving 105/1,000=10.5%. The 10 third-party fee is excluded from that numerical test, though it can have accounting consequences after classification. If facts instead show a 20 fee **received** from lender, sign and result differ. Do not use 1,115 by aggregating all fees or claim a <10% numerical result settles qualitative changes. Determine whether lender remains same, currency changes, embedded features, extinguishment rights and original EIR before journal. US ASC 470-50 and FRS 102 Section 11 calculations have distinct rules; route separately.

**Covenant chronology.** Debt covenant ratio tested 31 December X1, certificate due 28 February X2, actual X1 ratio 1.1 below required 1.2; lender waiver signed 15 January X2. The certificate due date does not shift the measurement date. Resolve whether a substantive right to defer settlement existed at 31 December under effective IAS 1 classification rules, whether waiver was already enforceable at that date, classification and IAS 10 disclosure. A covenant first required on 31 March X2 may have a different current/noncurrent outcome, but risk disclosure can still be necessary. Going concern uses cash forecasts and lender intentions separately; do not infer non-going-concern solely from current classification.

**Release.** Reviewer recalculates 10.5%, matches fee counterparties/invoices and waiver signature, checks original and modified effective dates, logs board/treasury correspondence and assesses covenants across all facilities. Fail counting adviser fee in lender test, treating January waiver as December right without contractual evidence, or borrowing a FASB proposed debt exchange model as current GAAP.
