# Debt modification and covenant decision case

Capabilities CAO-06-012/013. Existing factory retained. A lender and borrower change a debt's remaining cash flows. Before comparing, build original and revised schedules in the same currency and use the original effective interest rate where the applicable framework requires it. Illustrative IFRS 9/AASB 9 present value of remaining original lender cash flows is 1,000 and revised cash flows plus qualifying lender fees is 1,110: 110/1,000=11%, a potentially substantial change under the applicable test. If revised PV is 1,080, the 8% result does **not** automatically mean no derecognition: qualitative changes and framework-specific rules still matter. Fee paid to a third-party adviser is not automatically a lender fee in the 10% comparison. US ASC 470-50 and UK FRS 102 Section 11 have their own mechanics.

At 31 December a leverage covenant required compliance on that date, but certificate delivery is in February. Assess compliance at reporting date, waiver terms and rights existing at that date for current/noncurrent classification and disclosure; a February waiver cannot simply rewrite a breach at 31 December. A future covenant tested only after reporting date has different classification implications but may require risk disclosure.
