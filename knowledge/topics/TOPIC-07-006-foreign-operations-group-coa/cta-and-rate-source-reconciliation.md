# CTA and group account mapping verification

**Full currency bridge.** Foreign subsidiary USD functional; EUR presentation. Opening net assets USD 100 at opening EUR/USD 0.90 = EUR 90. Assume USD profit 20 recognized evenly (supported average 0.85 → EUR 17), no dividends/contributions/OCI and closing net assets USD 120 at closing 0.80 → EUR 96. Translation reserve movement is EUR 96−90−17=EUR −11, assuming no other FX/reclassification effects. Do not present −11 as a blind plug: trace source rate, opening net assets, local profit, capital flows and CTA rollforward, and reconcile translated individual assets and liabilities to EUR 96. If a dividend USD 10 occurred on a date with spot 0.82, this calculation is invalid and must include that movement at its appropriate rate. Verify income recognition pattern before using an average; uneven transactions require transaction-date rates.

**Mapping exception.** Local account 710 “other income” contains a 15 disposal gain and 5 customer contract revenue. Group COA cannot map all 20 to revenue or disposal based on the account label; obtain transaction-level source and split 15/5 under the framework/period. A remapped account must propagate to cash-flow, segment and note outputs, comparative mapping and approval history. Validate unmapped account count zero but also challenge ambiguous/many-to-one mappings; a zero count does not prove substantive accuracy. Controls: locked rate feed, local TB version, mapping version/effective period, dual review of unusual entries, FX reasonableness and final translated equity tie.

**Differences and QA.** IAS 21/AASB 121, ASC 830 and FRS 102 Section 30 must be applied on their own terms; lack of exchangeability effective IFRS periods from 2025 may affect rate selection, while the 2027 IAS 21 amendment on translation *into* a hyperinflationary presentation currency has separate scope. Test opening 90, profit 17, closing 96 and CTA −11; fail use of closing 0.80 for all income or mapping the full 20 to revenue. Evidence: local TB, rate source, legal entity/functional currency memo, dated transaction list, group chart, mapping approval, CTA and consolidated equity.
