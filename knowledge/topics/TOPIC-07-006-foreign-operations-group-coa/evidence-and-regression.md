## Calculation and adverse route

EUR functional subsidiary net assets EUR 600 at year-end USD/EUR 1.10 translate to USD 660. If opening net assets EUR 500 translated at 1.00 and current EUR 100 profit translates at weighted 1.05 to USD 105, CTA movement is 660−500−105=55 absent other movements. Use transaction-date rates for unusually large items; do not use average mechanically. Reconcile every account mapping, opening reserve, capital/dividends, NCI and disposal reserve, and exclude entity-level USD monetary FX already in subsidiary P&L.

## Evidence and framework regression

Prepare signed rights/ownership memo, consolidated journal with entity and counterparty IDs, source rate or valuation, reserve/NCI rollforward, tax bridge and disclosure tie-out. Test the opposite classification under US ASC 810/323/805, UK FRS 102 and AASB before asserting convergence. Select effective period and reporting tier separately. These four claim IDs remain the source-audit queue; IFRS core route uses 2026 issued text.
