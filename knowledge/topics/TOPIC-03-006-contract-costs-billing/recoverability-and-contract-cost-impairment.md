# Capitalized commission: recoverability and lost renewal

**Initial case.** Existing workpaper capitalizes incremental commission 600 for a three-year 9,000 service contract with 5,400 direct service cost, amortizing 200 each year when recoverability and period of benefit support it. After year one, customer signals it will terminate after year two; recoverable remaining consideration and directly related future costs must be updated and the contract-cost asset assessed for impairment under applicable IFRS 15/ASC 340-40/AASB 15/FRS 102 route. Illustrative carrying amount at end of year one is 400. Assume supported remaining consideration from related services is 900 and expected related costs excluding the asset are 650: headroom 250. Subject to the exact impairment test and relevant asset-scope order, a 400 asset would face a 150 shortfall. Do not automatically book 150 from these inputs without confirming the standard's impairment sequence, expected renewals and remaining scope; preserve downside and reversal policy. A management renewal forecast unsupported by customer history cannot justify keeping 400.

**Billing completeness.** Independent usage population 1,000 units; invoices 920; 40 free allowance, 10 dispute, residual 30 units worth 120 at four each. The 120 is a **billing exception**, not automatically revenue or AR. Verify control transfer, contract pricing, discounts, revenue constraint, entitlement and invoice-job failure by event. If the 10 disputed units reflect an enforceable price concession, update transaction price; if customer credit deterioration, route to 03-008. Sales commission capitalization and billing reconciliation are separate schedules joined by contract ID.

**QA/evidence.** Reperform 600−200=400 and illustrative 900−650=250, gap 150, while keeping recognition conditional; fail recovery justified solely by gross contract 9,000 after cancellation evidence or automated 120 revenue from usage count. Retain commission plan, contract/amendments, renewal evidence, cost forecast, impairment model, usage events, approved freebies/disputes, invoice run and reviewer. [IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/), [FRC revised Section 23](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) generally from 2026, ASC/AASB own operative details.
