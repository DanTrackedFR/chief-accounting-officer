# Contract cost and billing control: executed illustrations

Capabilities: CAO-03-012/013. This supplement preserves the parent topic's framework routing. Amounts are illustrative and require contract-specific judgment.

## Incremental commission

A sales employee earns 600 only when a three-year service contract is signed. Expected consideration is 9,000, direct service costs are 5,400 and no contrary recovery evidence exists. Assuming the award is incremental, recoverable and relates evenly to the three-year service, record Dr contract-cost asset 600 / Cr commission payable 600. Amortize 200 each year. The one-year practical expedient is unavailable on the assumed three-year amortization period. Reassess if a renewal expectation changes the period of benefit.

**Executed check:** 600 − 200 − 200 − 200 = 0 closing carrying amount; annual expense 200. PASS under stated assumptions. The 3,600 gross margin before commission supports, but alone does not prove, recovery.

## Failed capitalization gate

An 80 salary cost would be paid whether or not the contract was signed. It fails the incremental acquisition test in the parent README. An implementation cost of 120 relates solely to performance already transferred and cannot become a future-resource asset merely to smooth cost. Record expense when the governing standard requires it. If another asset standard applies, assess that standard first.

**Executed check:** proposed contract-cost asset 200 rejected; investigate any existing 200 asset and correct it with period/error assessment. PASS for routing; posting depends on actual books.

## Billing completeness reconciliation

The independent March usage ledger has 1,000 billable units at 4 each. Invoice lines total 920 units at 4. A 40-unit approved free allowance and a 10-unit pending dispute are supported by contract and case IDs. Residual unbilled units are 30, value 120. Do not infer revenue of 120 from this arithmetic: test transfer, variable consideration and contractual entitlement separately. Confirm that the 40 free units were actually excluded from the contractual billable population before treating them as a reconciling item.

**Executed check:** 1,000 − 920 − 40 − 10 = 30; 30 × 4 = 120 exception. PASS detection, unresolved billing exception. Investigate failed invoice jobs and cut-off; keep owner and due date.
