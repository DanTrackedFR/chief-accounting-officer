# TOPIC-03-006 — Contract Costs / Sales Commissions and Billing Completeness

Status: REVIEWED / US SOURCE-DEPTH PARTIAL
Capabilities: CAO-03-012, CAO-03-013
Knowledge: PRINCIPLES + STANDARDS + PRACTICE + DIFFERENCES
Source check: 2026-09-22

## Objective
Determine when customer-contract acquisition or fulfilment costs are assets versus current expense, amortize/test those assets appropriately, and prove that billable revenue activity is completely and accurately converted into billing without using billing as a substitute for revenue recognition.

## IFRS / AASB
IFRS 15 and AASB 15 contain a contract-cost model. Incremental costs of obtaining a customer contract are capitalized when expected to be recovered; costs that would have been incurred regardless of obtaining the contract are generally expensed unless explicitly chargeable regardless of outcome. A practical expedient permits expense when the otherwise-recognized asset's amortization period is one year or less. Fulfilment costs outside another Standard are capitalized only when they relate directly to an identified/anticipated contract, generate or enhance resources used for future performance, and are expected to be recovered. Existing asset Standards are considered first. Assets are amortized consistently with transfer of related goods/services and tested for impairment under the applicable model.

AASB 15 paragraphs 91–104 contain the core cost model; paragraphs 127–129 contain related disclosure architecture. Australian entity-type overlays remain applicable.

The IFRIC June 2019 agenda decision confirms that costs relating to past or partially satisfied performance are not turned into a fulfilment-cost asset merely to align cost recognition with revenue. IFRIC's training-cost decision also demonstrates that another Standard can require expense before IFRS 15 fulfilment-cost criteria are considered.

## US GAAP
FASB's official IFRS 15/Topic 606 comparison maps IFRS 15 contract-cost paragraphs to ASC 340-40, including 340-40-25-1 onward for incremental acquisition costs and 340-40-25-5 onward for fulfilment costs, with subsequent amortization/impairment in 340-40-35. Topic 606 governs related revenue/presentation. Current paragraph-body verification beyond official comparison material remains PARTIAL.

## UK GAAP
For periods principally beginning on/after 1 January 2026, use revised FRS 102 Section 23 and its transition requirements. The revised model is IFRS-15-based but proportionately simplified; do not assume every IFRS contract-cost detail is identical. Pre-2026 periods require old-Section-23 routing.

## CAO contract-cost workflow
1. Identify the specific cost and why it was incurred.
2. Check whether another accounting topic governs it first.
3. For acquisition costs, test whether the cost is incremental — would it have been incurred if the contract had not been obtained?
4. Test expected recovery and any practical-expedient election.
5. For fulfilment costs, establish direct relationship to identified/anticipated contract, future resource and expected recovery.
6. Reject capitalization of costs relating to past performance merely to smooth margins.
7. Determine amortization period/pattern including expected renewals where required by framework facts.
8. Test impairment/recoverability and reversal mechanics under framework.
9. Reconcile asset rollforward to payroll/AP/commission systems and GL.

## Billing completeness workflow
Define the billable-event population independently of invoices: contracts/orders, deliveries, usage, milestones, subscriptions, approved change orders and other billing triggers. Reconcile eligible events to generated invoices/credits, investigate unmatched items, verify price/quantity/tax/customer/entity/currency and cut-off, then reconcile billing subledger to AR/GL. Billing completeness is a control/process conclusion; revenue timing remains governed by the revenue model.

## Journal consequences
Capitalized commission: Dr contract-cost asset / Cr cash-payroll-payable, followed by amortization expense over applicable pattern. Nonqualifying selling cost: expense as incurred. Billing: Dr AR / Cr contract liability or revenue/other account according to performance status; never hard-code billing directly to revenue without revenue-model routing.

## Controls
Commission-plan accounting review; successful-contract linkage; duplicate employee/vendor cost check; practical-expedient policy; amortization/recoverability review; contract-cost rollforward; billable-event-to-invoice reconciliation; invoice sequence/completeness; failed-billing queue; price master/change control; credit-note monitoring; subledger-to-GL reconciliation.

## Scenarios
Sales commission paid only on signed three-year contract: test incremental/recovery and amortization. Salaried salesperson cost paid regardless of success: generally fails incremental test. Implementation labor relates to already-delivered performance: do not capitalize solely for matching. Usage events exist but no invoices due to interface failure: billing completeness exception, not automatic revenue conclusion. One-year practical expedient requires framework/policy applicability rather than ad hoc materiality shortcut.
