# Contract cost capitalization versus expense — adverse test

For every framework prefix `TOPIC-03-006-IFRS/AASB/UK/US`, suffix 001 acquisition, 002 fulfilment, 003 expedient, 004 amortization, 005 impairment and 006 disclosure/period. Retained README and worked scenarios map to these issue suffixes; billing-completeness steps are operational controls, not a revenue-recognition assertion. Historical REVIEWED is not a promotion.

A three-year customer contract generates a 90 commission payable only on successful signature; assume recovery and a supported three-year benefit period. A 30 salaried salesperson payment would occur even if the deal failed. Under the applicable capitalization route, Dr contract-cost asset 90 / Cr payroll payable 90; Dr selling expense 30 / Cr payroll payable 30. With even benefit, yearly Dr amortization expense 30 / Cr asset 30. At year one, carrying 60; if the supported remaining consideration less directly related unrecognized costs yields a recoverable amount of 45 after other relevant asset impairments, Dr impairment expense 15 / Cr contract-cost asset 15. Do not capitalize the 30 merely to match margins or expense the 90 under a one-year expedient when its actual amortization period is three years. Check 90−30−15=45.

Control compares signed-contract commissions to payroll/AP and flags failed billing-interface events independently.
