# Returns, price concession and credit deterioration

**Three customer events.** Entity sells goods for 1,000; reliable support indicates 10% expected returns at sale under an applicable IFRS 15 model. Illustrative expected revenue is 900 and refund liability 100, with a separate right to recover returned goods measured using the relevant cost/recovery facts; do not simply credit inventory for the entire 1,000 selling price. Later customer complains about a quality issue and entity grants a 40 price concession on goods not expected to return. Assess transaction-price reduction/refund or credit note based on enforceable terms and when conditions existed. Independently, customer cannot pay 60 of an otherwise unconditional 900 receivable: credit deterioration belongs to IFRS 9 expected-credit-loss assessment, not automatic further reduction of IFRS 15 revenue. A negotiated discount driven partly by quality and partly by ability to pay needs evidence to separate the elements; do not double count 40+60 against revenue and ECL indiscriminately.

**Journal/population.** Reconcile orders 1,000, expected returns 100, actual returns and approved credits to gross invoice, refund liability and receivable subledger. If actual returns are 80, revise remaining estimate using all relevant reporting-date evidence, return window and subsequent-event timing; do not book 20 “gain” before determining whether other customer returns are expected. Track return asset by cost of goods and impairment/recovery. Cash receipt 900 is not proof of no refund obligation. US ASC 606/326, revised FRS 102 Section 23/Section 11, AASB 15/9 have their own period and disclosure details.

**QA.** Fail revenue 1,000 with expected returns, failure to record any return-asset assessment, 60 bad debt hidden as goodwill/discount, or cross-customer netting of credit memos. Retain returns history, contract, credit approvals, customer correspondence, receivable aging, ECL model and note tie. [IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/), [IFRS 9](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/).
