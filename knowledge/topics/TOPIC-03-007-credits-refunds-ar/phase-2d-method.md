## Framework, election, transition and notes

IFRS/AASB return measurement uses the customer-contract model plus the respective financial-instrument standard for the valid receivable. [TOPIC-03-007-IFRS-R011] [TOPIC-03-007-AASB-R011]
US uses Topic606 return measurement and Topic326 for eligible amortized-cost rights; public/nonpublic disclosure relief does not change the refund/recovery entries. [TOPIC-03-007-US-R012]
UK FRS102 periods before revised adoption use old Section23 retained-benefit/risk/reward and reliable-estimate requirements; revised Section23 supplies the return/refund model from the valid 2026 or early-adoption date. [TOPIC-03-007-UK-R013]
UK financial-instrument measurement follows the elected Sections11/12, retained eligible IAS39 or IFRS9 policy, with Section11/12 disclosures; do not infer CECL from an IFRS9 election. [TOPIC-03-007-UK-R014]
UK FRS101 follows adopted-IFRS measurement and eligible note exemptions; an FRS105 reporter uses its own Section18/9 and micro disclosures, not the FRS102 financial-instrument elections. [TOPIC-03-007-UK-R015]
UK FRS102 revised adoption uses its full or cumulative uncompleted-contract transition; FRS105 uses prospective new-contract transition, preserving in-progress contracts. [TOPIC-03-007-UK-R016]
Australian entity scope independently tests enforceable sufficiently specific NFP arrangements versus AASB1058, and Tier2 uses AASB1060 notes rather than automatically all AASB15 notes. [TOPIC-03-007-AASB-R017]
IFRS/AASB historical revenue adoption and later local amendments must be selected separately from the current-period loss-model adoption; the same receipt may cross two adoption workpapers. [TOPIC-03-007-IFRS-R018] [TOPIC-03-007-AASB-R018]
US historical revenue/CECL adoption and ASU2025-05 current-balance expedients have distinct effective periods and eligibility; an ordinary refund estimate is not a CECL expedient. [TOPIC-03-007-US-R019]
Revenue notes describe material return/credit terms, disaggregation, price-estimate judgments and affected balances under the selected regime. [TOPIC-03-007-IFRS-R020] [TOPIC-03-007-US-R020] [TOPIC-03-007-UK-R020] [TOPIC-03-007-AASB-R020]
Financial-instrument notes separately explain the valid receivable, impairment method and credit risk under the applicable disclosure tier. [TOPIC-03-007-IFRS-R021] [TOPIC-03-007-US-R021] [TOPIC-03-007-UK-R021] [TOPIC-03-007-AASB-R021]
