# Regulatory applicability and filing calendar — substantive factory

This supplements, rather than replaces, the initial README. Capabilities: CAO-16-001, CAO-16-002, CAO-16-003. Source check: 2026-09-27. Status proposed for independent REVIEWED assessment **for the applicability method**, not for any unverified sector return.

## Decision model and authoritative layers

Start with a legal-entity register, not a consolidated reporting calendar. For each entity record incorporation jurisdiction, legal form, year end, public/private status, securities registration and trading venue, group relationships, regulatory licences, size thresholds, audit status and accounting framework. Determine whether each obligation belongs to the entity, issuer, parent group or license holder. File forms and deadlines are jurisdictional requirements; IFRS/US GAAP/FRS 102/AASB set accounting, not a universal filing date. Record the legal or regulator page, last-check date, underlying period, filer-classification evidence and effective/transition rule alongside the conclusion. A securities offering, merger, size-threshold crossing or change in public float triggers reclassification before the next close.

The [SEC Financial Reporting Manual, Topic 1](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-corporation-finance/financial-reporting-manual/frm-topic-1) distinguishes large accelerated (60-day 10-K, 40-day 10-Q), accelerated (75-day 10-K, 40-day 10-Q) and other classifications; the exact classification and current form instructions control, and 10-Q is generally for the first three quarters. [Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/life-of-a-company-annual-requirements/life-of-a-company-part-1-accounts) generally gives subsequent private-company accounts nine months and public-company accounts six months after the reference date, with distinct first-accounts and shortened-period rules. [ASIC lodgement guidance](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/financial-reporting-and-audit/preparers-of-financial-reports/lodgement-of-financial-reports/) distinguishes categories including listed/disclosing entities and other entities; do not assign one Australian deadline to the whole group. An EU regulated-market issuer may also face the [ESMA ESEF](https://www.esma.europa.eu/issuer-disclosure/electronic-reporting) format. These official sources establish routing, not legal advice for a named entity.

## Calendar construction and workpaper

For each obligation compute statutory date, internal readiness date, auditor delivery, board approval, signatory availability, translation/tagging, submission cut-off, confirmation of acceptance and contingency window. A rule such as “nine months” is not a hard-coded date if first accounts or a changed accounting reference period applies. Flag the earliest dependency and owner. Maintain a rules table with `entity_id`, `obligation_id`, `law_version`, `trigger`, `period_start/end`, `due_date_formula`, `calculated_due_date`, `extension`, `evidence_url`, `approver`, `filing_receipt`. Calculate dates automatically only after the governing formula is verified, with a reviewer comparing sampled dates to the official regulator calendar. Approval of a financial statement is not proof that it was accepted by a registry.

Public-company readiness is a different output: gap-assess close speed, audit committee timetable, disclosure controls, MD&A/narrative ownership, material-event escalation, board certifications, signed audit support, XBRL tooling, entity dimensions and access separation. Grade gaps by filing-critical path, not by a generic maturity score. Do not represent SOX/ICFR as universally applicable merely because a company has a public investor.

## Worked case and test

UK private company, normal subsequent 31 December 2026 accounting reference date: an initial planning date of 30 September 2027 follows the nine-month rule. A UK public company on the same ordinary reference date has an initial 30 June 2027 planning date. Change the period to first accounts or shorten it and both dates must be recomputed under Companies House rules. A US registrant with a 31 December year end cannot take the UK date: first determine filer status and exchange/SEC obligations. Test input: a group consolidation file lists one 30 September deadline for both UK entities. Expected: exception and two entity rows. Observed by tracing the rules above: exception; PASS. Test input: a new listing changes SEC filer classification and no rule-version review exists. Expected: hold calendar approval; observed: hold; PASS.

## Controls, evidence, boundaries

Quarterly entity/registration completeness certification, annual source refresh, automated deadline alerts with manual authoritative review, backup filer, submission/acceptance receipt control, and escalation of missed or changed deadlines. Evidence includes registers, size/public-float tests, legal advice if ambiguous, calculations, board/audit committee agenda, final signed report and acknowledgement. Dependencies: 08-008 statutory accounts, 08-010 certification, 09-006 disclosure controls, 16-002 annual/interim filing. Residual case blocker: exact legal status, industry licence and period-specific exemptions must be furnished before a filing conclusion; that is an input dependency, not an absence of factory method.
