Claims: [TOPIC-06-002-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-06-002-US-001], [TOPIC-06-002-UK-001], [TOPIC-06-002-AASB-001]. Existing methods and duplicate materials remain intact.

## Workpaper and boundary

EUR functional entity invoices USD 100 when USD/EUR rate is 0.90: Dr AR 90 / Cr revenue 90. At closing 0.95, Dr AR 5 / Cr FX gain 5; on settlement at 0.92, Dr cash 92 / Dr FX loss 3 / Cr AR 95. A prepayment for goods may be nonmonetary and not retranslated like a USD receivable; check refund rights. A 100-day deposit is not automatically a cash equivalent merely because its remaining maturity is 30 days. US ASC 830, FRS 102 §30 and AASB 121 require their own spot-rate and advance-payment rules.

US public/private choices and operative ASC, UK FRS 102 edition, and AASB commencement/tier must each be checked for the same fact pattern. Reperform numerical entries and opposite branches. Retain source population, legal/contract terms, date-stamped valuation or bank/rate feeds, journal lineage, disclosure support and reviewer. The detailed non-IFRS claims remain in the audit queue; they do not prevent use of the substantive method with a disclosed source gate.
