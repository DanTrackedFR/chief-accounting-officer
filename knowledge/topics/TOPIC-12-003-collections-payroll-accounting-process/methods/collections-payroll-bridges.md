# Collections and payroll-to-GL — distinct bridges

Extends substantive README. Capabilities CAO-12-007–009. PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE; 2026-09-27.

Collections handoff: collector records dispute/settlement and customer contact with invoice/credit ID; AR accountant decides credit, impairment, write-off and cash application under relevant policy and approval. An aging list is not an allowance calculation by itself. Separate promises to pay from bank receipts and claims from approved credit notes. Reconcile AR opening + invoices − credits − applied cash − authorized write-offs = closing, with FX/other items separately identified.

Payroll handoff: approved HR roster/rates and time/bonus inputs → payroll gross/net and employer costs → bank file and tax/social remittances → subledger/GL. Illustrative register gross pay 600,000, employee deductions 30,000, net bank pay 570,000; employer expense may differ from 600,000 because employer charges and accruals are separate. If GL expense is 605,000, bridge the extra 5,000 to supported employer charges or timing; do not compare net bank pay directly with expense. Reconcile payroll liabilities and benefit/bonus accruals separately by entity/period.

**Negative test:** collections team closes a 20,000 dispute by flagging invoice paid without bank receipt or authorized credit; AR and GL unchanged. Expected FAIL. Second negative: payroll bank 570,000 is called a 30,000 expense variance against gross 600,000. Expected FAIL; deductions are liability/cash bridge, not expense adjustment. Evidence: HR approvals, payroll run version, register, remittance, bank, AR/customer records, GL and reviewer. Technical impairment/benefit recognition routes Domains 03/05. Result PASS for arithmetic and accounting boundary.
