# Period-specific disclosure and comparative gate

**Requirement register.** Freeze entity, framework, consolidation/reporting tier, year beginning date, elections and filing regulator. For each relevant authoritative requirement store standard/section, effective date, transition method, whether the entity is in scope, evidence owner, affected primary lines and note paragraphs, and reviewer conclusion. This is a maintained register, not a copied checklist where every box is “N/A.” Link dynamic data populations for new acquisitions, leases, supplier-finance, segments, related parties and covenants. AASB 1060 Tier 2 relief, FRS 102 Section 1A UK small-entity rules and SEC registrant disclosures are distinct overlays; never treat a full IFRS list as all three.

**Comparative example.** Prior signed accounts show revenue 900 and trade receivables 120. Current source review discovers that 30 of prior-period revenue lacked delivery at prior year-end, with receivable 30 still outstanding. Assume this is a material prior-period error rather than a later estimate change; after determining the framework-specific tax and cost consequences, an illustrative pre-tax corrected comparative revenue is 870 and receivable 90. Do not simply subtract 30 from this year's revenue to hide the error. Reconcile opening equity, tax, related cash flows, note disaggregation and EPS if relevant, document nature/date/cause and apply the applicable retrospective/error disclosure requirements. If evidence instead shows a current-period return after proper prior delivery, do not automatically restate the prior comparative. Reviewer needs dated delivery evidence and decision memo.

**Effective-period forks.** For IFRS, use IAS 8 error/estimate distinction and IAS 1 presentation for 2026 non-early adoption; IFRS 18 generally applies to annual periods from 1 January 2027, with earlier adoption possible, and requires a transition/comparative plan. FRS 102 Periodic Review 2024 has principal 1 January 2026 date, including revised revenue and lease rules; assess its specific transition provisions before comparing pre/post-adoption figures. AASB 18 and local compilation require Australian commencement check. US ASC 250 and SEC change/revision guidance are independently sourced and not assumed identical.

**Test evidence.** Register includes standard version and dated approval; comparative mapping preserves original 900/120 and corrected 870/90 with a separate journal and restatement reason; check note and narrative references, not only two primary cells. Fail a checklist that marks all new requirements N/A with no scope evidence, a 2026 IFRS 18 requirement applied as mandatory to a non-early-adopter, or an error booked as this year's estimate. Official [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-accounting-policies-changes-in-accounting-estimates-and-errors/), [IFRS 18](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/). US detailed paragraph validation remains outstanding.
