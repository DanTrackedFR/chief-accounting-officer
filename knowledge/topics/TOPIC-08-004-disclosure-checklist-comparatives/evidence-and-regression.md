# TOPIC-08-004 — reporting evidence and adverse route

Claims: [TOPIC-08-004-IFRS-001], [TOPIC-08-004-US-001], [TOPIC-08-004-UK-001], [TOPIC-08-004-AASB-001]. Retain existing reporting pack.

## Worked case

A prior-year revenue overstatement of 20 discovered in 2026 is assessed for materiality to the prior year and current presentation. If material under IFRS, restate comparative revenue/profit and opening retained earnings as required; do not book a 2026 revenue reversal that conceals the prior-year error. Contrast a new estimate driven by new 2026 facts (prospective) and a policy change with its transition provisions. Reconcile every affected note and EPS/comparative bridge; keep source of discovery and approval.

## Cross-framework and control gate

Select reporting period, entity filing tier and applicable edition before conclusion. US public/private disclosures and SEC route, UK small entity/FRS 102 period, and AASB Tier 1/Tier 2 and NFP must be tested independently. Reconcile source GL, consolidation, note tables, comparatives and approval version with named reviewer. An inaccessible IAS 1 operative paragraph is model-derived, not source-verified. Every unverified claim remains in the audit queue.
