# TOPIC-08-004 — Disclosure Checklist Management / Comparative Financial Information

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**  
Primary capabilities: CAO-08-007, CAO-08-008  
Sensitivity: H

## Objective
Turn disclosure compliance and comparative information into controlled accounting processes rather than year-end checklist exercises.

## Inputs
Framework/period/tier/entity type; current authoritative disclosure sources/register; prior issued statements; current draft; new/changed standards; accounting cases; acquisitions/disposals/restructures; policy/estimate/error changes; materiality; regulator/statutory overlays.

## Disclosure-checklist method
1. Establish exact framework, reporting period, entity type/tier and regulatory overlays.
2. Start from a version-controlled requirements inventory appropriate to that period—not last year's completed checklist.
3. Determine applicability using company facts and accounting cases. Record YES/NO/N/A with rationale and source pointer.
4. For applicable requirements, assign note/statement location, data source, preparer, reviewer and evidence.
5. Reconcile quantitative disclosures to primary statements and controlled schedules.
6. Challenge boilerplate and disclosures that no longer match Company Accounting Memory.
7. Track new/changed requirements separately and test transition/effective dates.
8. Certify only after open disclosure exceptions are resolved or explicitly approved.

## Comparative-information method
1. Tie prior-year comparative numbers to issued statements before processing reclassifications/restatements.
2. Identify changes from policy, estimate, error, presentation/reclassification, acquisition/disposal or standard transition.
3. Determine whether retrospective application/restatement is required and whether an additional opening statement/balance sheet is triggered under the applicable framework.
4. Maintain a bridge from previously issued to current comparative presentation, with rationale and note cross-reference.
5. Check narrative comparatives and note tables, not only primary statements.

## Framework routing
IFRS: apply the reporting-period gate between IAS 1-era requirements and IFRS 18, mandatory for annual periods beginning on/after 1 January 2027 unless early applied. IFRS 18 requires comparative amounts and retrospective transition; implementation materials state comparative profit-or-loss line items are reconciled on first application. US GAAP: use applicable ASC and SEC/regulatory overlays where relevant; do not infer IFRS comparative mechanics. UK GAAP: FRS 102 plus statutory requirements, including 2026 Periodic Review changes and 2027 adapted-format amendments where applicable. AASB: framework plus reporting tier/entity-type gate; AASB 18 operative timing differs between relevant for-profit entities and NFP/specified superannuation entities.

## Controls
- version/effective-date control over checklist source;
- completeness reconciliation between applicable topics and disclosure inventory;
- independent review of N/A conclusions for high-risk topics;
- quantitative note tie-out;
- comparative bridge control;
- new-standard/change log;
- final open-item certification.

## Systems
Checklist tooling should retain requirement ID, framework/version/effective period, applicability, rationale, note, owner, evidence, status and reviewer. A spreadsheet is acceptable when governed; repeated multi-source tie-out and reconciliation can justify automation. TrackedFR is relevant only where recurring cross-system extraction/reconciliation/manipulation is the actual pain point.

## Artifacts
Disclosure checklist; applicability memo; comparative bridge; transition/reclassification schedule; new-requirements register; tie-out report; certification log.

## Scenario tests
1. Checklist copied from prior year misses newly effective standard: control fails.
2. Material prior-period error: CAO routes to error/policy/estimate topic and determines comparative treatment before editing numbers.
3. Presentation-only reclassification: bridge retains issued amount and shows destination/rationale.
4. IFRS 18 first-adoption period: transition/comparative requirements explicitly triggered.
5. 'N/A' financial-instrument disclosure despite material debt/derivatives: exception raised through topic-to-checklist completeness control.

## Completion assessment
PASS at REVIEWED / production-candidate level. This topic establishes period-aware checklist governance, comparative mechanics, controls, artifacts and CAO escalation logic without pretending a static public-repo checklist can remain authoritative indefinitely.
