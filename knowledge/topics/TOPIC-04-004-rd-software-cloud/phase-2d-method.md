# Research, software development and cloud implementation

Companion to the existing development-gate and cloud workpapers. CAO-04-010–012. Claims are ringfenced in `standards-claims.json`.

## Scope first

Under IAS 38/AASB 138, research expenditure is expensed. Development expenditure is recognized only from the date all recognition conditions are demonstrable: feasibility, intention and ability to complete/use/sell, probable benefits, resources and reliable cost measurement. An internally generated brand/customer list is outside the asset route. Expenses incurred before that dated gate cannot be reinstated later; qualifying costs stop at availability for use. Test impairment while not ready [04-004-IFRS-01; 04-004-AASB-01]. UK FRS 102 Section 18 has its own research/development accounting-policy choice and recognition conditions; record the elected treatment and apply it consistently, including disclosure, using the operative 2026 edition and transition. Do not carry the IFRS 250,000 result into UK books without that test [04-004-UK-01].

US routing starts with intended use and contractual control. Internal-use software is generally ASC 350-40; software to sell/license/market is ASC 985-20; R&D may fall under ASC 730; a hosted service can create a deferred implementation-cost asset without conveying software control under ASU 2018-15. Before adopting ASU 2025-06, apply the existing internal-use project-stage and application-development gate. The ASU changes the start criterion and related guidance for annual periods beginning after 15 December 2027, including interims, with early adoption and specified transition alternatives. Product purpose and adoption period are recorded per project; no US capitalization amount is inherited from IAS 38 [04-004-US-01; 04-004-US-02; 04-004-US-03].

## Worked cases and entries

The existing IFRS/AASB project has 100,000 discovery, 250,000 eligible engineering after its dated gate, 50,000 training and 100,000 maintenance. Capitalize 250,000; expense 250,000; Dr intangible 250,000 / Dr expense 250,000 / Cr payable/payroll 500,000. If gate approval and supporting conditions are established halfway through the 250,000 engineering period, only the demonstrably qualifying post-gate portion is eligible; an even 125,000 split is an **illustration**, not a default allocation. Reconcile work-item timesheets and payroll rates to both populations [04-004-IFRS-01].

For a US customer-hosted service with 120,000 qualifying implementation costs and 36 months of noncancelable hosting access, stipulate that the service contract, activity eligibility and term have been established. Illustratively Dr deferred implementation cost 120,000 / Cr cash 120,000; release 3,333.33 monthly over the hosting term with the expense classification consistent with hosting fees, rather than record customer-owned software and ordinary software amortization. Separate the hosting fee and training/data conversion. If 30,000 of the 120,000 is training, only 90,000 is eligible on these assumptions and illustrative monthly release becomes 2,500; expense training 30,000. This route is conditional on operative ASC 350-40/ASU 2018-15 and contract facts, not an IFRS cloud conclusion [04-004-US-02]. IFRS cloud configuration requires its own controlled-resource or service/prepayment analysis; supplier-controlled configuration is not automatically a customer software intangible [04-004-IFRS-02].

## Workpaper, controls and boundary tests

Freeze contract architecture, source-code rights, access and termination provisions, project-purpose approval, stage/gate dates, sprint/time records, release acceptance, upgrade versus maintenance and service period. Finance reconciles source payroll/AP to project ledger, eligible by phase and asset register/GL, then tests disclosure: policy, amount, useful life, expense and impairment or hosted-service deferred cost. Independent reviewer challenges retrospective gate dating and the cost classification. System controls block projects from capitalization before approved gate, tag 350-40/985-20/hosted separately and version the 2025-06 adoption choice [04-004-US-01; 04-004-UK-01].

Adverse tests: no demonstrable benefits/resources at engineering start; functionality sold to external customers rather than internal use; hosting supplier retains control but customer records a perpetual licence; a major upgrade has no incremental functionality; cancelled module remains capitalized; 2028 period ignores adoption election. Each fails the simplistic 250,000 capitalization result and requires rerouting. AASB 138 version and Tier 2 disclosure are selected for the exact period/entity, including the July 2026 public-sector insurer amendment gate [04-004-AASB-01].
