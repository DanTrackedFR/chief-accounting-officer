# TOPIC-04-004 — development gate and cloud cost case

Retains the substantive README. Capabilities CAO-04-010–012. Source gate: [IAS 38](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-38-intangible-assets/) and [2021 IFRIC cloud configuration/customisation decision](https://www.ifrs.org/projects/work-plan/configuration-or-customisation-costs-in-a-cloud-computing-arrangement-ias-38/); [AASB 138](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/); [FRS 102 Section 18](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/); [FASB ASC 350-40](https://asc.fasb.org/). Check software model, entity, reporting period and early-adoption status. ASU 2025-06 changes US internal-use guidance for annual periods beginning after 15 December 2027, including interims, with early adoption permitted; no unverified Codification paragraph is asserted here.

The published [AASB 138 December 2022 compilation](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-138-dec-2022) applies to periods beginning before 1 July 2026, so a later commencement cannot rely on that compilation alone. For the UK 2026 period select the FRC Periodic Review version of Section 18 and its allowed development treatment before carrying the IFRS 250,000 into UK books. Research-stage spending is not automatically a US software asset under ASC 350-40; the product purpose and adoption period control the routing.

The [AASB 138 version history](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/superseded-versions?pronouncement=274&standard_no=71) names the subsequent AASB 2022-9 public-sector insurance amendment effective 1 July 2026. Record whether the entity is a public-sector insurer and select the subsequent effective edition; do not imply that every commercial project's IAS 38-style development gate changed on that date. The [FRC edition list](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) confirms the September 2024 full edition plus later amendments as the current set and the principal 2026 Periodic Review effective date; an actual UK year-end still needs entity tier and early-application evidence.

The official [FASB ASU 2025-06](https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202025-06.pdf) amends ASC 350-40-25-12's development/capitalization start and relocates maintenance, training and upgrade instructions into the revised guidance, including 350-40-25-17A–17E. Effective annual periods begin after 15 December 2027, including interims, with early adoption permitted at the start of an annual period. The 2026 decision must therefore prove whether the pre-amendment rules apply; a 2028 decision must record transition approach and probable-to-complete assessment. A software product to sell/licence externally and an access-only hosting service must each be routed to their separate US guidance; this ASU does not collapse those categories into internal-use software.

## Gate-dated cost population

An internal product project incurs 100,000 discovery, 250,000 engineering after a dated approval showing technical feasibility, completion intent and ability, probable benefit, resources and reliable cost tracking, 50,000 training and 100,000 maintenance. Assuming all IAS 38 development criteria truly became demonstrable before the 250,000 spend, IFRS/AASB capitalize 250,000 and expense 250,000. Dr software development 250,000; Dr research/training/maintenance expense 250,000; Cr payroll/AP 500,000. An undocumented gate or mixed engineering time is not rescued by a later launch: remove unsupported costs from the asset. Demonstrate employee time by work item, pay rate, stage and service date; reconcile 500,000 source costs to project and GL. Amortize only when the asset becomes available for use; assess impairment if abandoned.

The same 500,000 does not establish a US result: distinguish internal use, external-sale software, R&D and hosted service; obtain the applicable ASC model and period before calculating a US capitalization amount. UK Section 18 requires its own development policy/recognition and life assessment, not the IFRS 250,000 by default. Australian tier affects disclosures even where recognition aligns.

## Cloud adverse case

An entity prepays 120,000 for implementation of supplier-hosted software it cannot control. Do not record a 120,000 software intangible merely because the vendor calls it implementation. Identify whether configuration creates a separate controlled resource; if not, assess whether the service is distinct from access and who performs it. If payment precedes the relevant service, record a prepayment and release it as service is received using supported dates; immediate expense may instead be required for a service already received. The contract, statement of work, architecture, service acceptance and allocation of consideration are required. Separate data conversion and staff training, and do not assume identical IFRS, UK and US timing.

Desk tests: pre-gate expenditure excluded; post-gate evidence complete; allocation independently recomputed; cloud contract correctly classified; change in 2028 US adoption status re-routes the model. First three pass on the stipulated facts; cloud and US conclusions are conditional on contract and authorized guidance, not fabricated precision.
