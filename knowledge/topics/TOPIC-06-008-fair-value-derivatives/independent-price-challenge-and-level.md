# Derivative fair value: model challenge and hierarchy evidence

**Snapshot.** Treasury's EUR/USD forward was an asset 40 at prior close and internal model values it at 65 now. Independent counterparty valuation is 59. Before booking a gain 25, reconcile 6 gap by checking notional/currency direction, maturity, forward curve date, discount factors, collateral and counterparty/own credit treatment, and model inputs. If reviewer accepts 59 after documented challenge, illustrative movement is 19 (59−40) before cash settlements and other adjustments; if 65 is supported, movement 25. Do not average 59 and 65 mechanically or sign either number merely to hit profit forecast. Investigate whether prior 40 was prepared under consistent methodology; material methodology change warrants separate explanation.

**Hierarchy.** A broker quote for a bespoke OTC forward is not automatically Level 1. Determine lowest significant input's observability within the measurement as a whole under IFRS 13/AASB 13 or ASC 820, including any credit/volatility assumptions. Record valuation technique, input-level rationale, transfers if applicable, Level 3 sensitivity/disclosure where relevant, day-one inputs and subsequent change. Derivative recognition and hedge accounting are separate IFRS 9/ASC 815/FRS 102 Section 12 questions; even a reliable fair value 59 does not establish hedge designation. Under UK FRS 102 revised Section 2A, select edition and principal 2026 effective date. AASB compilation and US access gate apply.

**Control/evidence.** Trade ticket, confirmation, master netting/collateral, independent market curves timestamp, model version, counterparty quote, reconciliation of 6, GL 40 opening and journal, hierarchy assessment and disclosure tie-out. Negative tests: gain 25 booked without investigating 6, broker quote labeled Level 1 by source alone, derivative omitted because settlement is future. Official [IFRS 13](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-13-fair-value-measurement/), [IFRS 9](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/), [FRC FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/); precise US and UK exceptions require current text.
