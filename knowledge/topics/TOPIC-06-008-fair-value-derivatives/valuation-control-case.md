# Derivative valuation and hierarchy control case

Capabilities CAO-06-016/017. Existing topic supplies four-framework workflow; this case tests valuation, journal and source-data distinction. A forward contract to buy USD 1,000 for EUR 900 in six months has value at reporting date based on market forward rates, discounting, credit and terms—not merely the difference between today's spot EUR value and EUR 900. Suppose a validated independent valuation gives positive fair value 40 at prior close and 65 at current close. Absent hedge designation and other exceptions, illustrative remeasurement is Dr derivative asset 25 / Cr fair-value gain 25; cash settlement remains a later event. **Executed:** 65−40=25. PASS arithmetic, not an independent valuation of 65.

Challenge valuation with counterparty confirmation, observable forward curve, valuation date and model, collateral/credit assumptions, independent price verification and sensitivity. A broker quote does not itself determine Level 1 classification; hierarchy reflects the *inputs* and unit of account. Separate recognition/measurement requirement (IFRS 9/AASB 9, ASC 815, FRS 102 Section 12) from measurement method (IFRS 13/AASB 13, ASC 820, FRS 102 revised Section 2A when effective). [IFRS 13 standard overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-13-fair-value-measurement/) and [FRC FRS 102 register](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) checked 2026-09-27; UK new Section 2A principal 2026 effective period. Exact current US/UK/AASB hierarchy, derivative exemption and disclosure paragraph mapping remains PARTIAL. Preserve trade confirmation, market input snapshot, model version, fair-value bridge, journal and reviewer challenge.
