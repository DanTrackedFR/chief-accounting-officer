## Executed case

Dealer quotes 102 and 98 for the same derivative. Do not average blindly to 100: reconcile bid/ask convention, valuation adjustments, credit exposure and observable inputs. If a significant unobservable volatility input changes value by 15, challenge Level 3 classification and sensitivity disclosure. Dr derivative asset / Cr fair-value gain follows the approved current valuation where appropriate, not a quote selected to manage earnings. A forward embedded in a host contract requires separate scope assessment before valuation.

## Review controls and period gates

Record input/source data, alternative decision branches, calculation, journals, disclosure tie-out and reviewer. The IFRS claim uses the 2026 issued route. US ASC, UK FRS 102 and Australian AASB need separate operative-period and entity-type audits; do not infer identical treatment. The four claims are tested by the cases above.
