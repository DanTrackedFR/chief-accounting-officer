# Acquisition costs and a provisional valuation correction

**Population.** An IFRS acquirer records adviser fees 12 for the business acquisition, 3 of directly attributable debt issuance costs, 2 of directly attributable equity issuance costs, and a 9 employee retention award. Classify each invoice and contractual term: acquisition fees are expensed in the relevant period; debt/equity transaction costs follow the financial-instrument/equity rules rather than purchase-price accounting; retention follows employee compensation. An accounts-payable label “deal fees 26” does not establish any one journal. Reconcile 12+3+2+9=26 to invoices, signed mandates, bank payments and GL lines, including a duplicate check against the closing statement. FRS 102 Section 19 requires an independent directly-attributable combination-cost analysis; copying the IFRS expense answer would misstate the purchase method.

**Provisional PPA.** At a 1 March X1 acquisition, identifiable net assets were provisionally 200 and goodwill 100 on an illustrative 300 consideration. On 30 June X1, a pre-existing, enforceable customer contract omitted at 1 March is valued at 30. Assume, solely for the illustration, a 25% deferred-tax effect of 7.5 is required on its tax basis difference. Incremental net identifiable assets are 22.5; adjusted net identifiable assets 222.5 and goodwill 77.5. Reperform opening tax, useful-life/amortization and any reporting-period catch-up effects. This is not a free-standing journal to reduce goodwill 30: the liability and consequential effects matter. An X1 June customer win created after acquisition cannot enter this adjustment. Document why information describes acquisition-date facts, date learned, whether initial accounting was provisional and the maximum measurement-period deadline. If a material omission instead reflects a mistake discovered after the allowed period, route to the error policy, not indefinite true-up.

**QA and audit trail.** Assert 200+30−7.5=222.5; 300−222.5=77.5; cost invoices reconcile to 26 with no retention in consideration. Recompute under FRS 102 purchase-method cost inclusion and its edition-specific provisional-accounting mechanics. Route US GAAP independently to current ASC 805, including the US current-period recognition of measurement-period adjustment effects; ASU 2015-16 is amendment history, not a full replacement for the current Codification. IFRS 3 paragraphs 45–50 and 53, AASB 3 matching compilation and FRS 102 Section 19 are authoritative anchors. Store the original and corrected valuation version, dated contract, tax workpaper, journal approvals, comparative disclosure and GL-to-PPA bridge. Public US paragraph text has not been validated.
