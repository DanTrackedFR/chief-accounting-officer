# TOPIC-14-007 — Executive / Audit Committee Accounting Summary / Conclusion Challenge
Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Capabilities: CAO-14-018, CAO-14-019
Sensitivity: M

## Purpose
Translate technical accounting into decision-useful governance communication while independently challenging the underlying conclusion.

## Challenge protocol
Reconstruct conclusion from facts and authority; identify strongest contrary treatment; test scope/definitions; challenge material assumptions and specialist inputs; rerun sensitivities; check framework/effective date; reconcile memo to JE/disclosure; assess consistency with prior decisions; identify management-bias incentives; grade residual uncertainty.

## Executive output
Decision required; conclusion; financial impact; key judgment; alternatives; risk/confidence; audit/regulator sensitivity; controls/process implications; unresolved items; owner/date. Technical detail remains linked in evidence pack.

## Escalation
Escalate unresolved material authority conflict, missing critical fact, unsupported valuation/legal assumption, inconsistent group policy, or conclusion whose accounting effect exceeds approved materiality/risk tolerance.

## Scenarios
Aggressive revenue conclusion; impairment sensitivity; acquisition PPA; covenant classification; prior-year inconsistency; auditor disagreement; regulator question; immaterial issue; specialist report; pipeline amendment. 10/10 PASS.
