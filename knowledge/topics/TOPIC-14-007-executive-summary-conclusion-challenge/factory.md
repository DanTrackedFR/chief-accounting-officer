## Challenge before compression

Independently reconstruct issue, facts, framework/period, applicable authority, strongest contrary view and amount rather than editing the memo's prose. Ask: could a missing contract clause, scope exception, company election, jurisdictional filing rule or subsequent event reverse the answer? Is the calculation from source data and is the model assumption independently challenged? Are booked JE, statements, note, tax/covenant and process controls consistent? Grade uncertainty: resolved authority and facts; conditional on a critical fact; or unresolved material authority conflict. Escalate unresolved material conclusion or management-bias signal to CFO, audit committee, auditor or specialist as appropriate. Board communication does not confer standards authority or cure absent evidence.

## Decision-card template and worked example

Card: decision sought; recommended treatment; framework/period; transaction; key facts; material financial effect and sensitivity; strongest alternative; source/evidence confidence; controls/audit/filing implications; open facts, owner/date, and a link to detailed memo. For an illustrative goodwill impairment, CGU carrying amount 30m, recoverable amount 30.5m, base-case headroom 0.5m; supportable downside recoverable amount 29.8m implies 0.2m shortfall in stress. The executive summary should say **no booked base-case impairment only if approved 30.5m is valid**, but 0.5m headroom and 0.2m downside exposure may require sensitive disclosure. Do not state “not material” based on the small headroom alone. Link model, assumption owner, discount rate and required disclosure; audit committee may ask what decision changes next quarter.

## Scenarios and QA

Set escalation thresholds before drafting: a potential material restatement, violation of a lender or regulatory capital condition, authority conflict, critical missing contract evidence, or auditor/regulator dispute merits a named owner and decision date. The summary should show base-case and downside effects in the same units and reporting period, and distinguish what management can decide (accounting treatment, disclosure, control remediation) from what needs third-party confirmation (legal rights, valuation input, waiver). After a committee decision, capture conditions and any dissent; revise the underlying memo, journal and note rather than allowing the executive deck to become a separate unsupported accounting record.

S1: committee receives a conclusion with no contrary treatment → fail challenge. S2: 0.5m headroom but an earlier committee deck says 2m → reconciliation/updated version required. S3: 30−29.8=0.2 scenario effect → PASS arithmetic, not an accounting entry. S4: regulator inquiry unresolved at filing deadline → escalate status and contingency, not a silent green indicator. S5: auditor disagreement omitted because management expects resolution → fail balanced summary. Evidence: underlying signed memo, source register, model, challenge notes, board pack version and recorded decision/conditions.
