# Write-off, partial recovery and invoice-specific cash allocation

Retained case removes 100 of a 120 receivable after reliable evidence supports only 20 recovery, using allowance 90 and incremental loss 10. Suppose customer later pays 12 of the written-off portion plus the surviving 20, bank receipt 32. Confirm remittance/customer/invoice identity. Under the entity's applicable accounting policy, record recovery of previously written-off amount 12 through the appropriate impairment/recovery presentation and clear the surviving 20 receivable; do not recognize 32 new revenue or revive the entire 100 write-off. Reconcile gross AR, allowance and impairment income/expense rollforwards with the 12 cash application. If subsequent collection before original write-off showed recovery reasonably expected, revisit original estimate/error, not reflexively book a fortunate gain.

**Control split.** Credit team authorizes write-off based on collection/legal evidence; cash operations apply remittances; GL owner reconciles recovery and allowance. A 12 unmatched bank credit should remain an unapplied-cash exception until customer identity is supported, even if aged write-offs total 12. Write-off does not legally forgive the debt unless separately agreed; preserve collection rights/records and any external debt sale. FX settlement, tax deductibility and bad-debt policy are separate considerations.
