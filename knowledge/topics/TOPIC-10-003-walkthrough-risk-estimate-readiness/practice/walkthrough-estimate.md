# Walkthrough and estimate challenge file

## Engagement route and decision
Begin at transaction initiation and follow authorization, processing, exception handling, posting and disclosure. Record control objective, owner, frequency, IPE and access dependencies, dates and retained evidence; an interview alone does not prove operation. For an estimate, separate accounting-framework measurement from audit response; route PCAOB AS 2501 or ISA 540 as the engagement requires.

## Control, evidence and data
Estimate paper identifies population, source lineage, model version, assumptions, sensitivity, retrospective comparison and contradictory evidence, with a conclusion tied to the reporting framework/effective period. Walkthrough sample traces initiation to final statement and tests what happens for rejected entries and manual overrides.

## Adverse case and escalation
A forecast reserve uses a new model and excludes recent adverse collections. The paper fails challenge until exclusion is explained, sensitivity and outcome back-test are documented, and control change is assessed. Escalate possible misstatement and deficiency.

## Handoff and authority
Link the evidence packet to the request register and final statement version; route accounting errors to Domain 02, control failures to Domain 09 and systems/data gaps to Domain 11. Reassess scope and effective dates at each new engagement. References: [PCAOB auditing standards](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards), [IAASB standards and pronouncements](https://www.iaasb.org/standards-pronouncements), [IAASB 2025 Handbook](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/2025-handbook-international-quality-management-auditing-review-other-assurance-and-related-services). These references describe auditor responsibilities; management owns books, support and correction decisions.
