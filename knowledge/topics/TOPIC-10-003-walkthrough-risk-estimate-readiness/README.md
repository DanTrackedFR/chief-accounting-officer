# TOPIC-10-003 — Walkthrough Readiness / Significant Risk Readiness / Accounting Estimate Audit Readiness

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Capabilities: CAO-10-007, CAO-10-008, CAO-10-009
Sensitivity: H

## Objective
Prepare the accounting organization to demonstrate how material transactions flow, how risks are addressed and how significant estimates are governed, calculated and evidenced.

## Walkthrough readiness
For each material process maintain: trigger/source; systems and interfaces; key master data; transaction stages; accounting event and posting logic; manual interventions; approvals; exception routes; relevant controls; reports/IPE; GL/subledger reconciliation; disclosure output; owners and evidence. The walkthrough narrative must match actual system behavior and current practice, not an obsolete SOP.

## Significant-risk readiness
Maintain a risk-to-accounting map: risk statement, affected accounts/disclosures/assertions, fraud considerations where relevant, accounting policy, key controls, substantive evidence available to management, judgment papers and unresolved issues. Audit risk designation belongs to the auditor; the CAO prepares management's accounting and control evidence and anticipates areas likely to receive heightened scrutiny.

## Estimate readiness
For each material estimate document:
- applicable accounting requirements and unit of account;
- method/model and why appropriate;
- data sources and completeness/reliability controls;
- significant assumptions and management rationale;
- specialist inputs;
- historical accuracy/back-testing where meaningful;
- sensitivity/range and estimation uncertainty;
- management bias challenge and contradictory evidence;
- approvals and governance;
- JE, presentation and disclosure consequences;
- subsequent events/information considered under the applicable accounting framework.

The CAO must distinguish a change in estimate, correction of error and accounting-policy change by routing to Domain 15 rather than using audit readiness to decide classification informally.

## Framework routing
The underlying estimate accounting follows the entity framework: e.g. impairment, ECL, provisions, fair value, useful lives, variable consideration or employee benefits route to their substantive topic. Audit-readiness requirements depend on engagement jurisdiction. Do not import PCAOB terminology into non-PCAOB engagements without resolving applicability.

## Controls
Annual/current process-owner certification; walkthrough change trigger after system/process change; risk register-to-workpaper linkage; estimate model version control; source-data validation; assumption approval; independent review; sensitivity review; disclosure tie-out; issue escalation.

## CAO decision logic
If process narrative differs from actual flow → update narrative and assess control implications. If significant assumption lacks evidence → obtain evidence or explicitly document uncertainty; do not fabricate precision. If contradictory evidence exists → evaluate it and document resolution. If specialist output is used → establish scope, inputs, competence/relevance information and management review rather than treating the report as a black box.

## Scenario tests
1. Revenue walkthrough reveals manual spreadsheet allocation omitted from SOP: update process map, identify IPE/control dependency and assess accounting impact — PASS.
2. Impairment model has unchanged assumptions despite market deterioration: CAO challenges assumptions and requires contrary evidence assessment — PASS.
3. Auditor labels a risk significant: CAO maps accounting/control/evidence response but does not override auditor risk assessment — PASS.
4. Valuation specialist report arrives: CAO validates management inputs, assumptions, scope and accounting integration — PASS.

## Assurance anchor
PCAOB AS 1105 requires sufficient appropriate evidence; AS 1215 links documentation depth to risk and judgment. These provide a useful current US-public-company readiness overlay, not universal company requirements. Official sources checked 2026-09-23.
