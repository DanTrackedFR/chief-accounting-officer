# Walkthrough and estimate readiness — challenge paper

Extends substantive README. Capabilities CAO-10-007–009. PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE with technical-topic handoff; 2026-09-27.

Walk one actual transaction forward and one reported amount backward: source trigger, system/users, master data, approval, transformation, exceptions, journal, reconciliation, disclosure and controls. Compare narrative to current practice and system version. If a manual spreadsheet step is missing, update control/IPE scope and assess periods affected before telling auditors the process is unchanged. The auditor identifies its own significant risks; management prepares evidence for its accounting assertions and controls.

Example impairment forecast assumes revenue growth 15% while current signed bookings support 4% and demand weakened. Management model must explain unit of account, applicable standard, forecast source, assumption owner, alternatives, sensitivity, historical back-test, specialist scope, contradictory evidence and disclosure. If 15% yields headroom 2m and 4% yields shortfall 1m, the 3m swing is a sensitivity requiring technical reassessment, not automatic recognition of either amount. Preserve source versions and mathematical checks; valuation and accounting conclusion route to asset impairment topic.

**Negative test:** specialist report includes 15% assumption and finance accepts it without challenging inputs. Expected FAIL. [PCAOB AS 2501](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2501) is auditor-side estimates guidance, checked 2026-09-27; no claim that this paper itself satisfies the auditor's standard. Result PASS for management evidence and challenge route, conditional on technical framework review.
