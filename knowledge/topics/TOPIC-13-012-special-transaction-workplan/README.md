# TOPIC-13-012 — Special Transaction Accounting Workplan

Status: REVIEWED / production-candidate
Primary capability: CAO-13-022
Sensitivity: M

## Purpose
Provide a reusable CAO workplan for transactions that cut across multiple accounting topics and cannot safely be handled as a single technical question.

## Intake
Transaction objective and signed documents; chronology; entities/jurisdictions; framework and periods; consideration/funding; counterparties/related parties; employee arrangements; valuations; tax/legal/specialist inputs; board approvals; system/ledger impacts; reporting deadlines.

## Workplan
1. Translate legal steps into an accounting-event chronology.
2. Identify accounting units of account and transaction boundaries.
3. Build issue tree: recognition, measurement, classification, presentation, disclosure, consolidation, FX, tax-accounting interface, controls and systems.
4. Route each issue to canonical CAO topics; assign owner/reviewer/evidence and dependencies.
5. Separate current requirements from pipeline proposals and entity policy choices.
6. Maintain decision register for significant judgments and alternatives.
7. Build pre-close models/JEs and post-close reconciliations.
8. Validate source-to-GL-to-financial-statements lineage.
9. Prepare technical papers and executive/audit summaries.
10. Promote durable policies/elections/history to Company Accounting Memory.

## Mandatory gates
No final conclusion while transaction perimeter, framework/effective period, executed legal terms, material consideration, accounting acquirer/control consequences where relevant, or material specialist assumptions are unresolved.

## Specialist integration
Valuation, actuarial, legal and tax specialists provide inputs where genuinely outside accounting remit. The CAO owns integration of those inputs into the accounting conclusion rather than outsourcing the accounting analysis.

## Controls
Document completeness; version-controlled deal chronology; issue register; evidence links; model/JEs review; closing statement tie-out; disclosure tie-out; post-close true-up queue; memory promotion.

## Completion criteria
Every material issue has disposition, authority/method, evidence, owner/reviewer, accounting entry/presentation consequence, disclosure/control consequence and open-item status.
