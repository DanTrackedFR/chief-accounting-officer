# Integrated transaction workplan: acquisition financed with debt

**Facts and owners.** Parent signs to acquire 80% of Target on 1 October X1 for 300 cash, plus a cash earn-out initially estimated at 60, financed with new debt face 250 issued for 245 and share proceeds 120. Legal documents include a 20 retention payment contingent on seller employment; adviser fees 12, debt issue costs 3 and share issue costs 2. Sources 245+120=365; announced cash consideration 300 and other fees 17 mean 48 remains for other uses or closing cash movements in this simplified funding bridge. Do not mistake face debt 250 for cash proceeds 245 or force unused 48 into goodwill. Owner: acquisition accounting lead; dependent owners: treasury, valuation, payroll, consolidation, tax and reporting; reviewer: group controller/CAO.

| Issue | Mandatory disposition before close | Evidence and reconciliation |
|---|---|---|
| Scope/control/acquisition date | Test business, acquirer, 80% control and actual closing date; route to 13-001 and 07-001/002 | Signed SPA, closing certificate, voting rights, NCI policy and consolidated TB |
| Earn-out 60 | Separate contingent consideration from employee retention 20; apply 13-002 and 05 compensation | Valuation probability/discount inputs, service-forfeiture clauses and liability rollforward |
| Adviser/debt/share fees 12/3/2 | Apply 13-003, 06-005 and 13-006 respectively; no undifferentiated 17 goodwill debit | Five cost invoices, capitalization/expense memo and GL bridge |
| Debt 250 face, cash 245 | Identify original issue discount 5, applicable issuance costs and EIR schedule | Bank proceeds 245, executed note, amortization and debt subledger |
| Financial reporting | PPA/NCI and goodwill, EPS/cash flow/disclosure implications | 07/08 workpapers, acquired net-assets valuation and note tie-out |

**Decision chronology.** Draft term sheet produces issue tree, not posting. At signing, confirm alternatives and specialist instructions. On 1 October closing, rerun legal terms, acquisition-date FV, classification and dates. Prepare provisional acquisition accounting only with transparent open-item register and measurement-period deadline; book financing separately. At first close, reconcile source 365 to uses, cash/bank, 20 employee-benefit accrual over the relevant service period, 60 contingent liability and all intercompany entries. At each later close, analyze whether new facts are acquisition-date evidence or post-acquisition performance. Complete disclosures and audit evidence index; promote durable elections and judgments to Company Accounting Memory.

**Adversarial tests.** If an amended side letter changes employment forfeiture before closing, invalidate the prior earn-out paper. If acquired net assets and NCI valuations are missing, do not invent goodwill; proceed with debt and cost workstreams and record PPA blocker with owner and due date. If bank evidence only shows 245 and cap table 120, 365 sources PASS; a 370 sources schedule that substitutes 250 face FAILS. If a share issue includes warrants, suspend the simple share-cost route pending 13-007. This operational orchestration has no universal single accounting standard; each row's technical conclusion must cite its own operative framework. The reviewer signs issue ownership, source-version register, journal approval, funds-flow bridge, independent valuation challenge and open-item clearance.
