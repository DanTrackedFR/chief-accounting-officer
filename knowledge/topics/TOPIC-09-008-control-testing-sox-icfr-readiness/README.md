# TOPIC-09-008 — Control Testing Readiness & SOX / ICFR Readiness

Status: **REVIEWED / production-candidate**  
Primary capabilities: CAO-09-019, CAO-09-020  
Sensitivity: J — jurisdiction/entity-status led.  
Knowledge type: PRACTICE + JURISDICTION OVERLAY

## Purpose
Prepare an accounting organization for defensible control testing and, where applicable, US SOX/ICFR requirements without turning SOX into a universal requirement or designing controls merely to satisfy an auditor.

## Jurisdiction gate
First establish issuer/listing/entity status, filer obligations, management assessment requirements, auditor-attestation requirements/exemptions, reporting period and control framework adopted. US SEC/PCAOB material applies only when applicable. Other jurisdictions may require different governance/control statements or no equivalent statutory ICFR attestation.

## Authoritative US anchors
SEC Release 33-8810 provides management with a top-down, risk-based approach to ICFR evaluation and emphasizes tailoring to facts/circumstances. PCAOB AS 2201 governs integrated audits and uses significant accounts/disclosures, relevant assertions, risk-based control selection, design/operating effectiveness and deficiency evaluation. SEC guidance explicitly avoids requiring management to identify/test every control.

## Readiness workplan
### 1. Scope
Determine material entities/processes/accounts/disclosures, relevant assertions, locations/service organizations and period-end reporting processes. Document rationale for inclusions/exclusions.

### 2. Risk-control linkage
Each key control must address an identified financial-reporting risk. Rationalize redundant controls. Include entity-level controls where they directly or indirectly affect financial-reporting risks, but do not rely on vague tone-at-the-top controls as substitutes for precise transaction/account controls.

### 3. Design readiness
For each control establish objective, owner, frequency, population, procedure, precision, thresholds, evidence, system/IPE dependencies, reviewer competence and exception handling. Perform walkthroughs from initiation to reporting.

### 4. Operating evidence
Ensure retained evidence proves what happened: exact population/version, performer/reviewer, date, review attributes, exceptions and resolution. Electronic approval alone may not evidence substantive review.

### 5. Testing readiness
Create test attributes based on control design. Determine population completeness and sample universe. Preserve evidence in a repeatable repository. Testing strategy and sample sizes ultimately follow the applicable assessor/auditor methodology; CAO should not invent universal sample counts.

### 6. Deficiency governance
Centralize failures, evaluate severity/aggregation, implement interim mitigation, remediate and retest before conclusions where possible.

### 7. Change management
New ERP, acquisition, reorganization, process outsourcing, key-person turnover or report logic changes trigger scope/design reassessment.

## Service organizations
Identify controls performed externally, reports/assurance available, complementary user-entity controls and gaps. Outsourcing does not outsource management's responsibility for relevant financial-reporting controls. If necessary evidence is unavailable, assess compensating controls and reporting consequences.

## Maturity path
**Controlled:** RCM, owners, evidence, periodic self-assessment.  
**SOX-ready:** scoped key controls, walkthrough-ready narratives, IPE/system dependencies, testing repository, deficiency governance, change process.  
**World-class:** continuous population/evidence monitoring, automated evidence capture, control rationalization, issue analytics and traceability from financial-statement risk to control to evidence.

## CAO execution
1. Resolve legal/regulatory applicability.
2. Build scoping memo and risk universe.
3. Map key controls and rationalize.
4. Walk through design and evidence.
5. Validate IPE/system dependencies.
6. Prepare testing attributes/populations.
7. Dry-run selected controls.
8. Log deficiencies and remediation.
9. Produce readiness dashboard and governance cadence.

## Artifacts
Applicability/scoping memo; RCM; narratives/flow maps; walkthrough pack; key-control inventory; IPE/system dependency register; testing-ready evidence index; deficiency/remediation log; management certification support.

## Scenario tests
1. Private UK company asks for SOX readiness: **CLARIFY OBJECTIVE**; build scalable ICFR if desired but do not label SOX mandatory.
2. US issuer RCM contains 450 controls with weak risk linkage: **RATIONALIZE** to controls needed for material reporting risks.
3. Outsourced payroll has assurance report but company ignores complementary user controls: **GAP**; map and operate required user controls.
4. Reviewer signs 70 reconciliations in ten minutes: **CHALLENGE OPERATING EVIDENCE/PRECISION**, not accept timestamps as proof.

## Completion criteria
PASS when CAO correctly gates SOX applicability, builds risk-based ICFR readiness, creates testable controls/evidence, handles service-organization dependencies and avoids universal sample-size or jurisdiction assumptions.
