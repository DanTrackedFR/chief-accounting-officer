# Control testing and ICFR readiness — applicability and sample plan

Extends substantive README. Capabilities CAO-09-019–020. PRINCIPLES/PRACTICE/JURISDICTION; 2026-09-27.

Start with actual issuer/entity, jurisdiction, filer classification, reporting date and adopted framework. US SOX 404 management assessment/auditor attestation are not universal; resolve current SEC rules before a legal conclusion. For an applicable US issuer, map significant accounts/disclosures and assertions to risks and controls using management's risk-based evaluation; [SEC Release 33-8810](https://www.sec.gov/rule-release/33-8810) checked 2026-09-27. [PCAOB AS 2201](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201) governs the auditor's integrated audit, not management's entire internal testing program.

Testing specification per control: objective, population, frequency, operator, selection rationale, design attributes, operating evidence, IPE reliability, exceptions, deficiency escalation and retest. There is no universal sample count independent of risk/frequency/control nature and audit plan. If a monthly control was absent in March and April, selecting only May–December clean examples cannot prove operation for the full year. Test historical exposure, compensating controls and reporting date effectiveness separately. Service-organization reports may support but must cover relevant controls, complementary user controls, period and exceptions; do not copy a certificate into evidence uncritically.

Worked case: 12 monthly reconciliations, 10 timely performed, two missing. Operating rate 10/12 does not by itself classify severity; failed months, exposed balances, compensating controls and risk determine escalation. A tester samples one clean month and declares “SOX ready” — expected FAIL. Preserve complete occurrence inventory, test attributes, workpapers, owner challenge and remediation. Non-US private entity requesting readiness gets scalable controls, not false mandatory SOX certification. Result PASS for applicability gate, test design and fail-stop; precise jurisdictional filing/exemption rules remain case-specific.

**Negative test:** test plan omits the two missing months and extrapolates from one clean sample. Expected FAIL; evaluate historical gap, material exposure and compensating controls.
