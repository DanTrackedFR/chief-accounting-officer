# CAO control specification — reconciliation and close

Supplements substantive README and acceptance case for CAO-09-013–014.

**Control population.** The account inventory, risk tier and reconciliation due list must tie to the complete GL entity/book/account inventory, including active zero balances and manual top-side postings. For each account capture preparer/reviewer independence, due date, source schedule, GL snapshot, opening-to-closing rollforward, gross/aged differences, risk-based threshold, correction IDs and escalation. Close sign-off consumes but does not replace these controls. Reviewer verifies evidence and challenged exceptions, not just that a task has a green icon.

**Illustration.** Bank 300,000 versus cash GL 315,000 contains 15,000 old transit. Obtain bank statement independently, stable deposit ID and subsequent clearing; if still outstanding at 120 days, evaluate error/fraud and block unqualified certification. A net −15,000 payment item does not clear the two gross items. If system auto-closes them, test matching rule and account completeness under TOPIC-11-010; classify a missed control under 09-007. A late reviewer signature remains a late signature; archive the original status/timestamp.

**Evidence and boundary.** Source statements/queries, GL version, recon workpaper and review challenge, exception aging, journal approval and final close certificate form the audit trail. Underlying cash recognition/classification routes Domain 06; error correction TOPIC-02-009. No accounting framework mandates a universal reconciliation cadence, while entity-specific law/ICFR may require controls. SEC 33-8810 and PCAOB AS 2201 are US-context references only, checked 2026-09-27. Output: risk-tier close control matrix and signed exception register.
