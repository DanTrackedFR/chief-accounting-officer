# Framework comparison, guidance-to-facts and alternatives — applied factory

Retains initial README. CAO-14-004–006. Source check 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for comparison method; transaction-specific authority still needed.

## Same facts, different permitted treatments

Lock legal transaction, rights, entity type, year, framework and comparison purpose. Prepare a matrix with rows scope, recognition, initial measurement, subsequent measurement, classification, presentation, disclosure, transition/election, source/version, and an explicit “not yet verified” state. Do not compare two frameworks using different fact sets or assume an Australian Standard is identical merely because IFRS-derived. [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/) governs IFRS policy hierarchy; [AASB 108](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-108-mar-2021) and Australian tier/entity overlays independently govern AASB; [FRS 102](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) requires a 2025/2026 version gate; [FASB Codification](https://asc.fasb.org/) requires current US source and SEC overlay where applicable. A general comparison protocol is PRACTICE, not a standards file.

For alternatives, record whether the framework permits an accounting policy election, practical expedient, estimation technique or transaction-specific exception, its required population level and change/transition rules. Evaluate consequences rather than choose the treatment with favorable EBITDA. A good difference record identifies actual divergence and impact on journal, system mapping and note, or explicitly says no material divergence on the defined issue after review. Report uncertainties with owner and stop final recommendation if the unresolved authority could reverse the answer.

## Worked comparative bridge

Five-year premises lease, fixed annual payment 100k, no purchase option, commenced 1 January, illustrative present value 400k and no initial direct cost/incentive. Ignore real lease-rate verification for the comparison. Under IFRS 16 a lessee normally recognizes ROU asset and liability and subsequently interest plus ROU depreciation; under ASC 842 a US lessee must separately classify finance versus operating lease, affecting expense and ROU mechanics. Under FRS 102 for periods beginning 2026, revised Section 20 has its own choices/effective transition; a 2025 commencement period cannot simply inherit the 2026 model. AASB 16 requires independent entity/tier/NFP overlay. Even if initial 400k liability matches, the subsequent P&L, cash-flow, note and schedule routes cannot be collapsed. Link full technical calculation and official sources to TOPIC-04-010 rather than duplicate or invent an interest rate.
