# TOPIC-02-007 — Accrual Accounting / Prepaid Expense Accounting

Status: REVIEWED
Capabilities: CAO-02-015, CAO-02-016
Knowledge mix: PRINCIPLES + STANDARDS + PRACTICE.

## Accounting principle
Recognition follows the applicable asset/liability and expense requirements rather than cash timing. An accrual records an existing period-end obligation/expense when the underlying accounting criteria are met and the amount is not yet recorded through normal processing. A prepaid balance is an asset only while a future economic resource/right remains; an advance payment is not automatically a prepaid asset.

Framework routing: determine the underlying transaction first. Accruals can fall under ordinary payables/expenses, employee benefits, provisions, leases, revenue/contract costs or other specific guidance. Prepayments can interact with inventory, PPE/intangibles, contract costs, leases or ordinary services. Do not apply one generic rule over more specific guidance.

## Accrual workflow
1. Define population of unrecorded obligations: PO/receipt data, vendor statements, contracts, recurring vendors, service confirmations, subsequent invoices, payroll/bonus data and prior accruals.
2. Establish service/goods received through period end.
3. Determine whether liability/expense recognition is appropriate under relevant topic.
4. Estimate amount using best available evidence and document methodology/uncertainty.
5. Separate routine accrual from provision where uncertainty/nature invokes specific guidance.
6. Post and define reversal/true-up logic.
7. Compare subsequent invoice/actual to estimate; analyze bias and methodology accuracy.

## Prepaid workflow
1. Trace payment to contract/invoice and identify remaining future right/service.
2. Determine whether another asset model applies instead.
3. Establish service coverage/consumption pattern.
4. Capitalize only qualifying unconsumed portion.
5. Expense systematically as benefit/service is consumed; reassess recoverability/expiry.
6. Reconcile schedule to GL and inspect old/negative/expired items.

## Estimate governance
Method should specify population, source data, estimation technique, assumptions, thresholds, evidence hierarchy, reviewer, backtesting and change triggers. Repeated large true-ups can indicate bias or weak source completeness.

## Controls
Uninvoiced-receipt/service completeness; recurring-vendor scan; subsequent-invoice review; accrual-to-actual analysis; prepaid addition approval; contract-period validation; automated amortization review; expired-prepaid review; schedule-to-GL reconciliation.

## Entries
Accrual: Dr expense/asset; Cr accrued liability, with reversal/settlement according to process. Prepayment: Dr prepaid/appropriate asset; Cr cash/AP, then Dr expense; Cr prepaid as consumed. Exact accounts and timing depend on underlying topic.

## TrackedFR fit
Strong when recurring accrual/prepaid calculations require combining ERP/AP/PO/contracts/warehouse data and reconciling schedules to GL in Excel.

## Artifacts
Accrual methodology; accrual register; subsequent-invoice test; accrual-to-actual dashboard; prepaid schedule; amortization logic; reconciliation; estimate judgment paper for material uncertain balances.

## Scenarios
Annual insurance paid upfront: recognize remaining service right and amortize over coverage, subject to contract facts. December consulting completed but January invoice: accrue based on service received. PO raised but no goods/service received: PO alone is not an accrual. Old prepaid for cancelled service: assess recoverability/refund right rather than continue amortizing mechanically.

## QA
PASS: avoids cash/invoice-date shortcuts, routes to specific standards when applicable, includes completeness, estimation and backtesting.
