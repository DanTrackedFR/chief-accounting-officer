# TOPIC-02-007 — Accrual Accounting & Prepaid Expenses

Status: REVIEWED
Capabilities: CAO-02-015, CAO-02-016
Knowledge: PRINCIPLES + STANDARDS + PRACTICE
Framework sensitivity: MEDIUM
Source check: 2026-09-22

## Objective
Recognize expenses and assets in the correct periods using evidence-based estimates and systematic release. Distinguish a genuine prepaid asset from an amount that no longer represents a future resource.

## Framework routing
An accrual is not a standalone accounting category. Identify the underlying obligation and route it to the applicable framework topic. IFRS and AASB use accrual accounting and IAS 8/AASB 108 estimate/error concepts. UK GAAP uses the relevant FRS 102 section plus Section 2 pervasive principles. US GAAP conclusions trace to the relevant FASB Codification topic.

## Accrual method
1. Define the underlying goods, service, employee or contract event and recognition period.
2. Establish whether an obligation and expense exist under the applicable topic.
3. Determine measurement from contract, PO, receipt, milestone, usage or other support.
4. Separate known amounts from estimates and identify uncertainty.
5. Avoid duplication with AP postings and existing accruals.
6. Record accrual with appropriate reversal or true-up logic.
7. Compare estimate with subsequent invoice or settlement and assess methodology quality.
8. Update methodology when new information improves estimation. Distinguish estimate change from error.

## Prepaid method
1. Identify payment and underlying future goods, services or right.
2. Establish whether an asset remains under applicable requirements.
3. Determine service or coverage period and allocation basis reflecting consumption.
4. Record initial prepayment and systematic expense release.
5. Reassess cancellation, recoverability, contract changes and classification.
6. Reconcile schedule to GL and source invoices/contracts.

## Estimate discipline
IAS 8 and AASB 108 distinguish estimates updated for new information from prior-period errors. Estimate changes are prospective; material prior-period errors are generally retrospectively corrected when practicable. Document the distinction for significant true-ups.

## Controls
Uninvoiced-liability completeness review; duplicate AP/accrual check; methodology approval; estimate-versus-actual back-test; stale accrual review; prepaid schedule-to-GL reconciliation; contract/service-period validation; additions/amortization review; aged prepaid recoverability review.

## Artifacts
Accrual methodology paper; accrual rollforward; estimate back-test; prepaid schedule; journal pack; reconciliation; exception log.

## Tests
Annual insurance paid upfront: establish coverage period and asset/release schedule. December services invoiced in February: accrue based on service evidence. A recurring materially inaccurate accrual triggers methodology review. A prepaid with no remaining service or right triggers asset-recognition challenge.

## TrackedFR fit
High when recurring accrual/prepaid schedules must reconcile contracts, invoices, ERP and operational data in Excel.
