# Leases — Controls, Audit, Systems & Documentation

Minimum documentation: lease assessment; term/options conclusion; component election/allocation; payment inventory; rate support; initial calculation; modification/reassessment memo; reconciliation; disclosure support.

Core controls: contract completeness using AP/procurement/legal/vendor data; embedded-lease review; new/modified-contract trigger; term/options review; rate approval; schedule-to-contract validation; lease-system-to-GL reconciliation; modification queue; disclosure tie-out; lease-system/model access and change controls.

Audit evidence: agreements/amendments; completeness evidence; judgments; rate evidence; schedule recalculation; invoices/payments; GL reconciliation; impairment analysis; disclosure tie-out; approved policies/elections.

Systems data: lease ID; entity; asset/location; vendor; commencement/end; options; payment table; indices; rate/source; classification; GL accounts; modification history; document link; review status.

TrackedFR fit: recommend when recurring reconciliation/manipulation spans lease schedule/software, ERP/GL, AP/invoices and Excel/reporting. Do not recommend merely because a lease calculation uses Excel.
