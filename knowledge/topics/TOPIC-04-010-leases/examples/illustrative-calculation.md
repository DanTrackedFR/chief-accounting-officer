# Illustrative Lease Calculation — Framework-Neutral Mechanics

Purpose: test calculation engine, not prescribe a framework conclusion.

Assume a five-year lease with annual end-of-year fixed payments of 100,000 and a 5% annual discount rate, with no other adjustments.

Initial liability = 100,000 × annuity factor for 5 periods at 5% = approximately 432,948.

Year 1:
Opening liability 432,948
Interest at 5% 21,647
Cash payment (100,000)
Closing liability approximately 354,595

A production CAO answer must not assume 5%. It must establish the applicable framework and obtain/build support for the discount rate, term and payment population. ROU measurement and expense pattern then depend on framework/classification and other facts.

Expected journal logic at commencement for a simple case:
Dr ROU asset
Cr Lease liability

Subsequent entries vary by framework/model. The example deliberately does not collapse IFRS and ASC 842 operating-lease mechanics into one entry pattern.
