# Lease Calculation Model

Inputs: commencement date; payment dates/amounts/types; term/options; rate; incentives; initial direct costs; prepayments; restoration obligation; ownership/purchase conclusion; framework/classification.

Initial liability = present value of included unpaid lease payments at commencement. For end-period payments: PV = sum(Payment_t / (1+r)^t).

Initial ROU asset starts with initial liability and adjusts for applicable commencement/prepayment amounts, incentives, eligible direct costs and restoration-type obligations.

Subsequent liability = opening liability + interest/accretion - cash payments +/- required remeasurement.

IFRS/AASB ordinary lessee: ROU depreciation/amortization and liability interest are separately determined.

ASC 842 finance: separate interest and ROU amortization. ASC 842 operating: apply single lease-cost mechanics; ROU reduction is determined under Topic 842, subject to impairment/other guidance.

Controls: payment population to contract; dates to term memo; rate to approved support; schedule rollforward closes; liability/ROU to GL; presentation/disclosure totals trace to schedule.
