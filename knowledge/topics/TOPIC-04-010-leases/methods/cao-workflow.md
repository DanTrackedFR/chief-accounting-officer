# CAO Lease Case Workflow

1. Resolve entity, framework, reporting period, currency, materiality and lessee/lessor role.
2. Obtain executed contract, amendments and side letters.
3. Test scope and exemptions/elections.
4. Identify specified asset and substantive substitution rights.
5. Test economic-benefit rights and right to direct use.
6. Separate components; document expedients/elections.
7. Establish commencement date.
8. Determine enforceable period and options; document economic incentives.
9. Build payment population: fixed/in-substance fixed, index/rate, residual guarantee, purchase, termination, variable/excluded.
10. Determine discount rate with evidence matched to relevant framework factors.
11. Determine framework-specific classification/model.
12. Calculate initial liability and ROU asset.
13. Build amortization/expense schedule and journal logic.
14. Assess reassessment/modification triggers.
15. Assess impairment interaction.
16. Determine presentation/disclosures.
17. Reconcile lease system/schedule to GL and disclosures.
18. Produce conclusion memo, calculation, reconciliation and control evidence.
19. Promote durable policy/elections/rate methodology to Company Accounting Memory.

Do not finalize when framework/period, enforceability, material payment terms, commencement date or rate basis is unresolved and material. Surface the exact missing input.
