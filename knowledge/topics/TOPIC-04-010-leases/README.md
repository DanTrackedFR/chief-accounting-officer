# TOPIC-04-010 — Leases

Status: BUILDING — Phase 2C vertical slice  
Primary domain: 04 Assets & Capitalization  
Primary capabilities: CAO-04-017 through CAO-04-024  
Related: CAO-04-025/026, reporting, controls, audit, systems, technical accounting, estimates/judgments.

## Scope
Contract identification; scope/exemptions; components; lease term; payments; discount rate; lessee classification/model; initial/subsequent measurement; remeasurement/modifications; impairment interaction; lessor classification/model; subleases; presentation/disclosure; transition/effective-period handling.

Sale-and-leaseback is linked to TOPIC-04-011 but included in Phase 2C differences/testing because it is integral to a complete lease case.

## Frameworks
IFRS 16; ASC 842; FRS 102 Section 20 as amended by Periodic Review 2024 (generally effective periods beginning on/after 1 Jan 2026); AASB 16 current compilation applicable to 2026 reporting periods.
