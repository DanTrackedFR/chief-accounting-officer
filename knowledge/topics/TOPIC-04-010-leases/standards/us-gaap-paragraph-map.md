## Classification criteria — CAO interpretation
Finance classification is triggered by one or more criteria addressing ownership transfer, reasonably-certain purchase option, major part of remaining economic life, substantially-all fair-value economics, or specialized nature/no expected alternative use. If none applies, lessee classification is operating.

Do not convert “major part” or “substantially all” into mandatory bright-line percentages unless the applicable authoritative/implementation guidance and entity policy support doing so.

## Discount rate
Use the rate implicit when readily determinable; otherwise IBR. For an eligible nonpublic entity, the risk-free-rate election is by class of underlying asset rather than necessarily entity-wide. Record election, class, comparable term and disclosure.

## Lessor
ASC 842 retains sales-type, direct-financing and operating models. FASB's Topic 842 basis and post-issuance material confirm this architecture. Variable-payment lessor guidance was amended by ASU 2021-05 for specified selling-loss fact patterns.
