# US GAAP — ASC 842 Leases

Sources: SRC-FASB-ASC842; SRC-FASB-PIR842  
Status: REVIEW CANDIDATE  
Checked: 2026-09-22

## Authority
ASC 842 in the FASB Codification is authoritative US GAAP. FASB states ASUs communicate changes to the Codification but are not themselves authoritative.

## Model
Topic 842 recognizes ROU assets and lease liabilities for lessees for leases generally beyond the short-term exception, but unlike IFRS retains two lessee classifications: finance and operating. FASB's current PIR materials confirm this dual model.

Lease identification is control-based: identified PP&E plus the customer's ability to obtain substantially all economic benefits and direct use.

## Lessee workflow
1. Identify lease and components under ASC 842-10.
2. Determine lease term/options and payments.
3. Determine discount rate: rate implicit when appropriate/readily determinable under the guidance; otherwise incremental borrowing rate. Eligible nonpublic entities have a risk-free-rate accounting policy election by class of underlying asset.
4. Classify as finance when Topic 842 finance criteria are met; otherwise operating.
5. Recognize ROU asset and liability at commencement (subject to permitted short-term treatment).
6. Finance lease: interest on liability plus amortization of ROU asset, generally producing a front-loaded total lease cost.
7. Operating lease: subsequent ROU measurement is designed to produce a single lease cost generally recognized straight-line over the lease term.
8. Remeasure/modification when specified events occur.
9. Apply Topic 842 presentation/disclosure requirements.

## Classification
Finance classification considers transfer of ownership, purchase option reasonably certain to be exercised, lease term representing a major part of remaining economic life, PV/fair-value relationship, and specialized nature. The model deliberately retained a classification approach substantially similar to legacy US GAAP.

## Variable payments
The initial liability generally includes variable payments based on an index/rate using the commencement-date index/rate. Other variable payments are generally excluded from the liability until incurred unless they are in-substance fixed.

## Impairment
ROU asset impairment interacts differently with the operating-lease single-expense model than IFRS's ROU asset model; this is a required differences/test area.

## Lessor
Topic 842 retains sales-type, direct-financing and operating lease models, with specific variable-payment guidance and Topic 606 interactions.

## CAO evidence
Same core contract/payment/rate evidence as IFRS plus documented classification analysis, any risk-free-rate election and entity eligibility, operating-v-finance expense mechanics, and US-GAAP-specific disclosure/support.
