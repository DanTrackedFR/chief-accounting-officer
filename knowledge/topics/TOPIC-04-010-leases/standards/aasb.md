# AASB — AASB 16 Leases

Source: SRC-AASB-AASB16  
Status: REVIEW CANDIDATE  
Checked: 2026-09-22

## Effective version
For 2026 reporting periods, the AASB standards portal identifies the November 2022 compilation as current for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2024 and before 1 January 2027. Future AASB 18 consequential amendments require period-sensitive handling.

## Core model
For for-profit entities, AASB 16 is closely aligned with IFRS 16: control-based lease identification; lessee ROU asset and lease liability model; short-term/low-value recognition exemptions; lease-term, payment, discount-rate, remeasurement, lessor and sale-and-leaseback mechanics corresponding closely to IFRS 16.

## Australian overlay
AASB 16 contains Australian-specific material, particularly for not-for-profit/public-sector contexts and Tier 2 disclosure interactions. The CAO must therefore never infer AASB compliance solely from an IFRS record.

## Workflow
Use the IFRS-style lease workflow but independently verify AASB paragraph applicability, Australian paragraphs, entity type, reporting tier and effective compilation.

## Sale and leaseback
AASB incorporated the Lease Liability in a Sale and Leaseback amendment for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2024.

## Evidence
In addition to ordinary lease evidence: entity type (for-profit/NFP/public sector), reporting tier, applicable AASB compilation and any Australian-specific elections/guidance.
