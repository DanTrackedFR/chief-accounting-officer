# AASB 16 — Verified Paragraph / Australian Overlay Map

Status: REVIEWED FOR CORE ROUTING
Checked: 2026-09-22

Primary source: current AASB 16 compilation operative for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2024 but before 1 January 2027.

## Version routing
AASB portal confirms the November 2022 compilation is current for that period. AASB 18 consequential amendments affect later periods, so reporting period remains a mandatory routing input.

## Core paragraph architecture
AASB 16 retains the IFRS 16 paragraph architecture for core lease accounting. Key core references used by the CAO include:
- 9 and B9 onward — lease identification/control model.
- 22–28 — initial recognition/measurement.
- 36 onward — subsequent lease-liability mechanics.
- 44 onward — modifications.
- 51 onward — lessee disclosure objective/requirements.
- 61 onward — lessor classification/accounting.
- 98–103 — sale and leaseback.

## Australian-specific overlays
- Aus59.1 adds disclosure for a not-for-profit lessee that elects cost initial measurement for classes of ROU assets arising from leases with significantly below-market terms principally to further its objectives.
- Tier 2 entities apply AASB 1060 disclosure requirements rather than assuming full AASB 16 disclosure package.
- Entity type and reporting tier are therefore mandatory Company Context for Australian disclosure conclusions.
- Public-sector/NFP transactions can interact with non-exchange / below-market arrangements; do not infer the answer from IFRS alone.

## Sale and leaseback
AASB 2022-5 incorporated Lease Liability in a Sale and Leaseback, effective for periods beginning on/after 1 January 2024 and included in the current compilation.

## Completion caution
Core recognition/measurement alignment can inherit independently verified IFRS reasoning, but an AASB record is never complete until current AASB version, Australian paragraphs, entity type and tier are checked.
