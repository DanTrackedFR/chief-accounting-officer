# IFRS 16 — Current Status / Update Watch

Checked: 2026-09-22

IFRS 16 became mandatory for annual periods beginning on/after 1 January 2019. The IFRS Foundation maintains implementation support including agenda decisions, educational materials and webinars on definition, exemptions, lease term, modifications and disclosures.

The 2022 Lease Liability in a Sale and Leaseback amendment is part of the current-period lease knowledge and applies from 1 January 2024.

## 2026 post-implementation review
In July 2026 the IASB concluded its IFRS 16 post-implementation review discussion and said the Standard is overall working as intended. It decided to add future work on reducing ongoing application costs relating particularly to remeasurement and discount rates, plus a narrow-scope project on specified rent concessions.

These are pipeline projects, not current amendments. The CAO MUST NOT alter current IFRS 16 accounting based on tentative/research pipeline work. Update monitoring should flag future exposure drafts/final amendments and effective dates.
