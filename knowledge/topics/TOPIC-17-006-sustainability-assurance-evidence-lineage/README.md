# TOPIC-17-006 — Sustainability assurance readiness, evidence and lineage

Status: DRAFT for independent Phase 2D QA; canonical capabilities: CAO-17-012, CAO-17-013. Prepared 2026-09-27. This topic contains independently authored decision and execution guidance; restricted standards and regulations are reference-only.

## Principles and scope

Assurance readiness is reproducibility: an independent practitioner must trace a disclosure back through method, calculation and source without relying on management recollection. Identify the legal entity, jurisdiction, report date, group perimeter and applicable framework before evaluating requirements. This is a regulatory overlay, not a replacement for financial-statement recognition and measurement under IFRS, US GAAP, FRS 102 or AASB.

## Authoritative source and effective-period gates

- ISSB: [IFRS S1](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/ifrs-sustainability-standards-navigator/ifrs-s1-general-requirements/) and [IFRS S2](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/ifrs-sustainability-standards-navigator/ifrs-s2-climate-related-disclosures/) effective from periods beginning 1 January 2024, subject to jurisdiction adoption and transitions.
- Australia: [AASB S2](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-s2-sep-2024) effective from periods beginning 1 January 2025; [ASIC cohort test](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/sustainability-reporting/for-preparers-of-sustainability-reports/who-must-prepare-a-sustainability-report/) distinguishes statutory start dates and entity eligibility. AASB S1 is voluntary unless separately adopted.
- UK: [government UK SRS status](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/uk-sustainability-reporting-standards): S1/S2 issued February 2026 for voluntary use; specific mandates require a separate current-rule check.
- EU: [Commission CSRD and amendments timeline](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/financial-markets/company-reporting-and-auditing/company-reporting/corporate-sustainability-reporting_en), ESRS and local transposition; scope and phased dates must be rechecked after Omnibus changes.
- US: [SEC climate rule defense decision](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2025-58) and [2026 proposed rescission](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/05/s7-2026-19). A proposal is not a final rule; check current litigation/stay and other disclosure obligations.
Source check: 2026-09-27, official publisher pages. Exact paragraph and form instructions require verification for the specific entity and period; FASB Codification paragraph bodies were not relied upon. Treat amended laws, pending rules, adoption elections and transition relief separately. Do not infer a mandatory obligation solely from a standard's publication date.

## CAO decision and execution

For each material claim define criteria/standard, assertion, source system, data owner, transformation, calculation, evidence ID, reviewer and reporting version. Assess control design and operation over organizational boundary, meter/vendor data, factors, estimates, overrides and disclosure. Prepare sample-ready workpapers and resolve exceptions before sign-off. Determine the legally required assurance level/provider and independence rules for the actual jurisdiction and period; do not infer it from IFRS S1/S2 issuance alone.

Workpaper minimum: entity and period; governing rule and URL/version; applicability facts; scope/boundary; inputs and provenance; calculation/mapping; alternatives and judgments; review/sign-off; reporting output; exceptions, remediation and change log. Control owner reconciles source population to accounting or operational systems, and a second reviewer checks the conclusion against the effective rule. Preserve submission/assurance evidence and the signed version.

## Worked application and boundaries

Auditor selects 12 MWh from a warehouse month. Evidence chain: meter read → invoice → site mapping → unit conversion → factor/version → consolidation → Scope 2 total → report cell, with timestamps and approvals.

For each workpaper consider materiality, disclosure, data quality, presentation, applicable assurance and whether a discovered difference changes an accounting entry. Route underlying accounting recognition/measurement to the governing financial topic. Do not post a journal solely to make a regulatory return or sustainability metric reconcile; document the rule-specific bridge.

## Executed desk scenarios (decision-route QA)

Provider has only a PDF with totals and no source lineage: FAIL; restated emissions lack old/new bridge: FAIL; sampled meter is absent from site register: FAIL. These checks exercise decision routing only; they do not certify a live entity's filing or a paragraph-level legal conclusion. A production case must collect actual evidence and rerun current-rule verification.
