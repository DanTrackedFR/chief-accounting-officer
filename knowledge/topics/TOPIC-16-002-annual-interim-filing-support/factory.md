# Annual and interim filing support — substantive factory

Supplements the retained README. Capabilities: CAO-16-004, CAO-16-005. Source check: 2026-09-27. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for the execution method; filing-specific conclusions require actual form instructions.

## Annual versus interim decision map

Lock legal filer, accounting framework, regulator, period, form, comparative requirements, audit/review status and reporting currency. A US domestic registrant normally uses [Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/files/form10-k.pdf) for annual and [Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/files/form10-q.pdf) for quarterly reports, with Part I financial information and narrative/disclosure sections whose instructions must be checked. An Australian company lodging with [ASIC](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/financial-reporting-and-audit/preparers-of-financial-reports/lodgement-of-financial-reports/) or a UK company filing with [Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/life-of-a-company-annual-requirements/life-of-a-company-part-1-accounts) follows different legal packages and deadlines. IFRS IAS 34, US ASC 270, UK FRS 102 Section 6/other applicable interim guidance and Australian AASB 134 may govern accounting/presentation when an interim report is prepared; they do not by themselves impose all filing obligations. Resolve availability of comparative and year-to-date columns before populating a template.

Annual workplan: signed closing trial balance; consolidation; cash flows; statements/notes; disclosure checklist; subsequent-events and going-concern review; MD&A/strategic report and other narrative as required; audit report; board/committee approval; XBRL; certifications/exhibits; release. Interim workplan is not merely the annual document divided by four: update seasonality, significant transactions since year end, changed estimates, material events, comparative presentation, interim tax and going concern, and review status as required. Track each item to an assertion, owner, workpaper, governing instruction and sign-off. A regulatory form's presentation may differ from management reporting; maintain a quantified bridge instead of silently remapping the ledger.

## Quantitative bridge and example

Illustrative Q2 reporting pack: H1 signed revenue is 61.2m; Q1 previously filed revenue was 29.4m; Q2 stand-alone revenue should be 31.8m (61.2 − 29.4). A 0.3m late credit posted to the wrong period changes H1 to 60.9m, Q2 to 31.5m and may require an assessment of Q1 depending on the underlying cut-off date. Reconcile GL → consolidation → financial statement → note → narrative → structured tag for both H1 and Q2. Do not clear the difference by altering only the face statement. For the annual package, if the signed TB shows 125.1m while narrative says 125.4m, investigate source/version rather than assume immateriality; aggregate this with all differences and test qualitative effects.

## Process and controls

Create a filing map with disclosure ID, authoritative form/standard, data provider, ledger/source workpaper, exact statement/note/narrative location, comparative, auditor/reviewer, drafting version and final tagged value. Version-lock inputs before final tie-out; journal after freeze requires assessed reopening, re-tie, re-tag and reapproval. Separate preparer from reviewer and retain review evidence that identifies actual challenges and resolution. Check post-balance-sheet events through authorization/filing cut-off appropriate to the framework, including amendments to filed forms if necessary. Preserve final rendered and machine-readable packages plus submission receipt.

## Executed case tests

S1: Q2 standalone value derived from H1 and Q1: 61.2 − 29.4 = 31.8; PASS. S2: late 0.3m credit entered only in annual narrative: the source-to-filing bridge fails; require period analysis and all dependent outputs refreshed; PASS. S3: UK private company's annual report submitted as a US 10-K because parent is SEC registrant: reject entity/form mapping; PASS. S4: material interim covenant event has no disclosure assessment: reject sign-off until topic-specific rule and case evaluated; PASS. These are reproducible routing tests, not a claim that a real filing is complete.
