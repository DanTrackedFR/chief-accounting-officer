# Onerous contract: asset impairment before provision

Retained scenario has contract consideration 500, direct fulfilment costs 530 and termination penalty 40, giving provisional lower loss 30. Before recognizing that 30 as IAS 37 onerous-contract provision, identify assets used in fulfilling contract and assess impairment under applicable standards. Suppose dedicated equipment carrying amount 100 is impaired by 12 from its own required impairment analysis. Determine which forecast cash flows and asset values the impairment test already captured, then recompute remaining contract unavoidable loss; do not automatically book 12 impairment **plus** unchanged 30 provision without examining overlap. Directly related contract depreciation allocation of 80 belongs in the 2022-effective IFRS IAS 37 cost-of-fulfilling assessment; future unrelated overhead and payroll administrative postings are not inserted indiscriminately. Contractual ability to cancel for 40 and enforceable minimum future receipts matter.

**Payroll control boundary.** Staff labor 150 is a forecast cost input; payroll GL actually incurred in December is a separate service-period expense/accrual. A forecast 150 cannot be booked twice as wage liability and contract provision without obligation reconciliation. Link contract ID to resource plan, time sheets, asset register, provision model and actual payroll journal; unwind provision against future losses according to approved policy, and reassess estimate as terms change. US GAAP has contract-specific loss guidance rather than one universal IAS 37 analogue. FRS 102 Section 21 and AASB 137 have own version gates.

**QA.** Confirm 530−500=30 versus exit 40, but classify full accounting conclusion conditional on asset impairment and forecast overlap. Fail booking 30 with no asset test, excluding allocated direct depreciation solely because not incremental, or treating a payroll upload as settlement of every provision. Keep signed contract, termination terms, impairment model, forecasts, payroll subledger, approval and sensitivity.
