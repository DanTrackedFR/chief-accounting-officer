# AP subledger, three-way match and aged exception

**Book-to-subledger bridge.** AP control GL credit 510; supplier subledger open credit items total 525. The 15 difference is an unmatched manual GL debit. It may be a valid supplier credit or settlement, but do not accept it without supplier invoice/credit/payment evidence and matching account. Reconcile by vendor, legal entity, currency, document ID, posting period and intercompany status. If the debit is an erroneous duplicate cash settlement, reverse it only after bank/remittance evidence and approval, yielding GL 525; if subledger contains a duplicate 15 invoice, correct that instead, yielding subledger 510. Both arithmetic paths balance but have opposite financial-statement consequences. Invoices received after period end for services received before period end also require cutoff/accrual review, not just AP posting-date comparison.

**Procurement control.** A purchase order 100, receiving log for 80 delivered by period end and invoice for 100 creates a 20 quantity/acceptance exception. Confirm contract terms, returns/disputes and whether a recognized liability exists for undelivered or unaccepted portion before posting 100 cost. Supplier credits and goods returned after period end require conditions-at-period-end analysis. An invoice coded “inventory” can belong to PPE, lease, prepaid service or expense under its underlying standard; this operational control does not make every AP amount an expense. Payment scheduling, duplicate-payment keys, vendor master changes and manual journals need segregation and bank confirmation.

**Framework/QA.** Recognition routes to the acquired goods/services and financial-liability standards, with IAS 37 for qualifying provisions, FRS 102 applicable sections, US ASC and AASB versions; there is no universal AP three-way-match paragraph. Fail a 15 balancing plug or auto-booking full invoice 100 despite delivery only 80 without contract assessment. Retain PO, receipt, invoice, approval, vendor statement, credit note, bank, GL/subledger extracts, cutoff search and exception owner aging. Reperform 525−510=15 and 100−80=20 before reviewer sign-off. Cross-topic 05-002/003, 03 AR and 01 close controls.
