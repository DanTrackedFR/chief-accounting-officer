# AP accounting — cut-off and three-way bridge

Capabilities CAO-05-001/002. The existing factory and README give the comprehensive operating method. This example deliberately tests both an AP-to-GL difference and a liability missing from *both* systems.

At 31 December the AP subledger open-item control total is 1,240 and the GL trade AP control is 1,260. Investigation finds a direct 20 GL credit journal with no vendor/invoice ID. It cannot be called a valid liability merely because it closes the difference. Trace source and reviewer; reclassify, reverse or create a proper subledger item based on evidence. Separately, goods received under a 75 purchase order on 29 December were not invoiced until 8 January and never entered AP or GL. Assess control/receipt terms and debit classification (inventory, PPE or expense); if a 75 present obligation existed at 31 December, record a receipt accrual/GRNI of 75, not an arbitrary trade-AP vendor invoice. Thus a zero AP/GL difference after dealing with the 20 would *still* not prove completeness.

**Executed:** GL−subledger=1,260−1,240=20; missing received-not-invoiced population=75 independently. PASS detection, posting conditional on receipt/underlying standard evidence.

For one invoice of 120 in foreign currency, preserve transaction and reporting dates, currency and original exchange rate. Period-end unpaid monetary AP routes to TOPIC-06-003; do not post a plug to expense simply to align translated balances. Supplier financing may require distinct classification and disclosure (TOPIC-06-005/006, reporting topics). This AP record does not invent four separate accounting standards for control-account mechanics.

Evidence pack: receipt and title terms, PO, contract, supplier confirmation, post-close invoice, AP aging extract/count/filter, GL control detail, manual JE population, interface logs, accrual journal and subsequent clearing. Review control tests both balance and outside-system population; owner/date/expected clearing documented for each exception. Relevant source gate is the underlying asset/expense/liability standard by framework and period; [FRC FRS 102 register](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) confirms principal 2026 effective period for UK amendments. Proposed REVIEWED candidate for operational method, with transaction-specific accounting delegated to owning topic and independent QA of bridge pending.
