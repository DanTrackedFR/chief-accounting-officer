# Current authority research and retained audit limits

Research date: 4October2026. Source standards are reference-only; no standard/PDF text is copied into this package.

IFRS Foundation official IFRS17 page was inspected: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-17-insurance-contracts/ . It identifies operative January2023 commencement, scope, separate service/finance results and the optional simplification. Clicking the Standard tab yielded the overview, not an accessible operative provision population. Atomic operative propositions remain model-derived with no fabricated paragraph locators and an open direct-source audit. Public overview does not certify all IFRS17 requirements. The current IFRS route excludes early IFRS18 and IFRS19 adoption.

AASB17 official December2022 compilations were inspected at https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-17-dec-2022 and https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-17-dec-2022-0 . Select by annual reporting-period beginning: January2026–June2026 uses the compilation operative January2023 before July2026; July2026–December2026 uses the July2026-before-January2027 compilation. The latter includes public-sector modifications, including aggregation, recognition and optional PAA differences. These cannot be generalized to ordinary IFRS. This package restricts executable AASB to Tier1 for-profit, excluding public sector, not-for-profit and Tier2 disclosure regimes. Four narrow claims have operative provisions directly checked across both compilations. Other independently authored propositions retain the unresolved individual operative audit despite accessible source material; inspection of a portal must not be recorded as individual verification.

FASB Codification access was attempted at https://asc.fasb.org/ and the official codification page https://www.fasb.org/page/PageContent?pageId=/standards/accounting-standards-codification.html . Retrieval yielded no operative text. The official ASU2018-12 document URL was attempted and errored. Official short-duration disclosure and acquisition-cost announcements were found, but these are not complete current ASC944 authority. US propositions are model-derived audit-required. Revised long-duration guidance, product-specific benefit liabilities, DAC and market-risk benefits are investigated as boundaries, not imported into supported short-duration accounting. No Codification paragraph is invented.

FRC official FRS103 page and September2024 PDF were inspected: https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-103/ and https://www.frc.org.uk/documents/7669/FRS_103_September_2024_rSi5poe.pdf . FRC expressly distinguishes FRS103 from IFRS17. Base standard requirements date from2015; PeriodicReview2024 amendments generally apply to periods starting January2026, with the specified transition provision January2024. FRS103 continues actual existing insurance policies subject to its requirements. Five narrow adequacy, reinsurance and extinguishment safeguards are directly checked. A generic FRS102-to-IFRS17 measurement route is invalid and remains blocked.

Insurer investment accounting belongs to Financial Instruments; IFRS9/AASB9 apply to the relevant separated instruments and ordinary insurer investments. Derivatives owns separated derivative and hedge accounting. FX consumes IAS21/AASB121 functional currency and translation. Insurance measurement does not include the issuer's own nonperformance risk in ordinary fulfilment cash flows; reinsurance-held counterparty nonperformance is distinct. IFRS13/AASB13 valuation is consumed only where actually required, including specialized acquisition/transition/underlying-item routes, which this engine blocks. IFRS7 financial-instrument disclosures cannot replace insurance disclosures.
