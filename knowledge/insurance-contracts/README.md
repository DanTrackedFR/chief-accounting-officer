# Supplemental Insurance Contracts knowledge

This package owns separately governed Insurance accounting propositions for roadmap Skill39. It does not create a canonical topic or change the 157 topics, 347 mappings or 1,598 canonical claims. Its 168 atomic claims are separately counted: IFRS65, AASB65, US24 and UK14. Approval is independent of direct source assurance. The author supplied pending claims; independent review has now approved all168 claims, with eight substantive findings remediated and independently retested. The independent reviewer owns approvals and challenge evidence.

The supported accounting engine consumes current qualified actuarial/model results. It cannot replace an actuary. Source records must identify report, entity, product, group, valuation date, model version, assumption register, unique contracts/claims/costs, qualified reviewer and reconciliations. No mortality, morbidity, lapse, catastrophe, ultimate-loss, IBNR, yield curve, risk adjustment or coverage forecast is fabricated. An assertion of signoff without a controlled source artifact does not meet this contract.

`standards-claims.json` contains internal provenance and individual approval hashes. `retrieval.py` requires explicit framework, model, entity and 2026 period and validates the entire register before returning allowlisted approved propositions. `validate_supplement.py` rejects fabricated canonical identifiers, wrong populations, self approvals, stale hashes and evidence inflation. Public retrieval includes accounting limitations while excluding evidence labels, source notes and reviewer metadata. Live application enforcement remains an integration dependency; package tests verify this boundary only.

Exact accounting scope differs by framework. IFRS/AASB can support bounded current-contract GMM and PAA issued accounting plus separately eligible PAA reinsurance held. US support is short-duration premium earning, gross claims, eligible DAC and supported ceded reinsurance. UK FRS103 knowledge supports framework classification and adequacy/nonoffset safeguards, but a generic UK measurement route is blocked because actual existing policy/legal scope is required. Knowledge approval does not promise every normative route is implemented. VFA, complex guarantees/DPF, long-duration US, MRB, complex modifications/acquisitions, multi-currency/OCI and transition need separately certified execution.

Run `python knowledge/insurance-contracts/validate_supplement.py` and `python knowledge/insurance-contracts/test_supplement.py`. Independent claim QA lives separately and must challenge every claim, retest findings and bind approval to content hash before retrieval opens.
