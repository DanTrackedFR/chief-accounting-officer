# Framework methods, workpapers and accounting boundaries

## Decision order

1. Bind actual framework, jurisdiction, entity type, annual-period start/end, operative edition and accounting policy. Reject ambiguous UK framework and unsupported AASB public-sector/Tier2 routes.
2. Inspect actual enforceable contract terms, insured event, adverse effect, significant risk scenarios, scope exceptions and separated components. Labels and insurer identity are insufficient.
3. Reconcile unique economic contract/claim/treaty/cost identities to source controls. Classify issued versus held, identify portfolios/groups/cohorts where required, and freeze recognized group membership.
4. Determine recognition independently from cash receipt. Bind qualified initial/current actuarial inputs, assumptions, boundary, model version and valuation date. Reconcile actual premiums, claims and acquisition costs against controlled sources.
5. Select the framework/product model using actual eligibility evidence. Calculate only supported measurement effects. Unsupported VFA, long-duration US, transition, modifications and acquisition routes require exact specialist handoff rather than an approximate answer.
6. Roll forward remaining coverage, incurred claims, CSM, risk adjustment, loss component and reinsurance separately as applicable. Every amount must reconcile opening plus named movements to closing without plugs.
7. Produce balanced journals and GL ties, insurance-specific financial-statement inputs and disclosure workpapers. Mark missing source populations PARTIAL/BLOCKED; do not invent actual reserves or complete final statements/checklists.

## IFRS/AASB current nonparticipating GMM

Take net present value of boundary cash flows and qualified RA as the source measurement. At first recognition, CSM makes initial nononerous measurement free of unearned profit after contemporaneous premiums and acquisition-asset inclusion. An initial net loss is immediately recognized and held in a loss component. No arbitrary CSM balances.

Example: premium1,000 paid at inception, future claim/expense PV800 and qualified RA50. Before premium receipt the net FCF is minus150 and CSM150, giving initial net balance zero. Cash receipt debits cash1,000 and credits the insurance liability1,000. Component liabilities are PV800, RA50 and CSM150. At a qualified locked annual rate4%, CSM accretes6. If current coverage units25 and remaining75, release is156×25/100=39 and closingCSM117 before further changes. The finance journal for CSM accretion debits insurance finance6 and credits CSM6; release debitsCSM39 and credits insurance revenue39. Full revenue additionally requires actual expected-service/cost and RA release schedules. Those schedules cannot be inferred from CSM alone.

Onerous example: premium1,000, future PV1,050 and RA50 gives net FCF100, CSMzero, initial loss100. Debit insurance service loss100 and credit insurance liability100; premium cash adds liability1,000, yielding liability1,100. Subsequent loss-component allocations must come from a qualified systematic method, cover applicable service, RA and financial changes, and reach zero by coverage end. A universal invented ratio is not supported. Future-service favorable changes first reverse tracked loss where required; losses cannot be hidden in negative CSM.

Changes must distinguish current/past versus future service and financial versus nonfinancial effects. GMM future-service CSM measurement uses the applicable locked-rate basis; current measurement and finance differences require separate reviewed source outputs. Coverage release is based on actual quantity/duration of services and qualified forecasts. No smooth-revenue coverage forecast is generated here.

## IFRS/AASB PAA issued

Eligibility is assessed at inception. A contract's full boundary coverage must satisfy the one-year criterion or supported material-equivalence analysis; product marketing labels do not suffice. On a simple short-term capitalized acquisition example, premium1,200 less acquisition cash120 plus amortization30 less revenue300 gives closing remaining coverage810. Initial cash receipt debits cash1,200 and credits remaining coverage1,200. Acquisition payment debits remaining coverage120 and credits cash120. Amortization debits service expense30 and credits remaining coverage30. Revenue debits remaining coverage300 and credits insurance revenue300. These entries balance and exactly reproduce810.

Expense election requires every contract coverage of one year or less; it changes the acquisition entry to debit service expense/credit cash and removes later amortization. Incurred claims are measured separately using qualified FCF and RA, with discount exemption only when payment is expected within one year of incurrence. OpeningLIC200 plus incurred150 plus estimate/finance changes10 less claim payments100 equals closingLIC260. Claim payments debitLIC and credit cash; they do not generate a second service expense. Remaining coverage financing exemption is a separate service-to-premium-due test. PAA does not exempt onerous assessment.

## Reinsurance held

Treaty risk, counterparties, covered source policies/claims, inception and recognition must reconcile separately. Proportionate held recognition can be later than treaty coverage start when underlying contracts are not yet initially recognized. PAA eligibility belongs to the treaty population; issued eligibility is insufficient. Maintain remaining-coverage asset, incurred recovery asset, ceded expense, recovered service and finance bridges. Account for reinsurer nonperformance in qualified estimates. Recovery cash debits cash/credits recoverable; do not record the same recovery twice through held claims and direct issued claims.

Gross issued liability500 and held recoverable150 remain two statement populations. Presenting a net liability350 would falsely hide both populations. A held loss-recovery component requires its own timing, underlying-loss and expected-recovery evidence; it is not simply every direct loss times treaty percentage. GMM held CSM represents purchasing cost/gain and is distinct from issued unearned profit. Complex GMM held, retroactive treaties, disputed/nonproportionate structures and loss-recovery execution can remain blocked even with normative knowledge.

## US short duration

Earn premium with actual insurance protection, maintain UPR, qualified unpaid losses/IBNR and claim-adjustment expenses, and capitalize only eligible successful-acquisition DAC. US does not use IFRS grouping, RA, CSM or PAA labels. The bounded route uses undiscounted claims and excludes exceptional discounting. DAC amortizes under the supported premium/protection pattern and is assessed under the premium-deficiency policy.

Example: UPR600, expected claims650, maintenance50 and unamortized eligibleDAC80, with no investment-income consideration under the documented policy. Deficiency is650+50+80−600=180. Debit deficiency expense180, creditDAC80 and credit additional premium-deficiency liability100. This writes offDAC before increasing the liability and avoids double counting. A documented investment-income policy and actual US aggregation are required to alter that calculation. Reinsurance US risk transfer is independently assessed; failed risk transfer cannot be silently treated as insurance. Ceded premiums and recoverables remain separately supported; special commissions and retroactive treaties require review.

## UK FRS103

Actual FRS102+FRS103 existing accounting policy and legal formats are required. IFRS17 adoption is not an ordinary FRS103 policy choice. Safeguards include liability adequacy, no reinsurance offset, reinsurance impairment and extinguishment-only derecognition. For liability1,000 lessDAC100 and intangibles50, tested carrying amount850 against current contractual cash flows920 gives deficiency70. Do not deductDAC a second time to reduce the deficiency. The remedy depends on actual FRS103 policy and eligible asset reductions/liability additions. A full generic UK measurement route remains blocked absent that actual policy and legal evidence.

## Disclosure and handoffs

Insurance supplies gross issued/held asset and liability totals, service revenue/expense, finance and applicable OCI, plus applicable remaining-coverage/loss/LIC/FCF/RA/CSM reconciliations, methods/inputs and risk populations. Disclosure Management owns the final applicable checklist and workflow. Financial Statements owns the final statement compilation. Systems/Data owns interface and policy/claims system controls. Controls owns control design/testing. Accounting Changes governs method/policy changes and error correction; an accounting error must not be relabeled actuarial experience. Insurer investment, derivative/hedge, entity FX, qualified valuation and acquisition basis stay with their respective owners. No ERP posting or full CAO orchestration occurs here.
