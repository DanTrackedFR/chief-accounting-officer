# Manufacturing and inventory accounting method

This is an original controlled accounting workflow. Framework conclusions are retrieved by decision from the supplemental register; company facts and accounting architecture are supplied inputs. The formulas below decompose supplied quantities and prices. They do not manufacture standards, capacity, selling prices or an allocation policy.

## Determine population and ownership

Obtain the controlled item/location/entity population, reporting period, functional currency and units. Classify materials, WIP, finished goods, resale goods and production supplies by actual use. Separately list consigned stock, goods in transit, third-party custody, returned goods and assets belonging to another owner. Consume reviewed ownership/control evidence and contract dates. The same physical item cannot become two assets because two warehouses or SKU aliases describe it.

Use the transaction owner's completed initial basis for acquired inventory and Revenue's reviewed expected-return recovery asset. Long-lived machinery and qualifying spares belong to Fixed Assets; financial instruments and intangibles retain their own owners. Extraordinary commodity measurement, noncommercial distribution, industry exceptions and specialist initial-basis routes must pass their own applicability gates. Accounting Policy Inventory is unrelated to goods inventory.

## Build source-linked purchased and production cost

For purchased stock, retain purchase-price, discount/rebate, freight, duty, recoverable/nonrecoverable tax and landed-charge identities. Every qualifying amount must have an economic source and allocation evidence. A supplier liability is an AP result; this workflow does not create a second liability simply because its cost enters inventory.

Manufacturing starts with supplied controlled BOM and routing versions, production orders/batches/jobs, net component issues and actual good output. Actual issues less component returns establish consumption. Expected usage establishes a comparison baseline; it does not overwrite actual consumption. Approved substitutions, scrap, rejected production, rework and remaining component quantities must explain differences. Operations and cost centres establish accounting lineage, not an execution system.

Direct labour uses attributable manufacturing Payroll cost with actual hours and reviewed production mapping. Completed Fixed Assets depreciation, factory leases, utilities/AP accruals and FX amounts may enter eligible overhead pools. Check entity, currency, period, certification and duplicate cost identities before inclusion. Inventory owns eligibility and recovery; it does not recalculate those source owners.

## Reconcile RM, WIP and FG

Maintain quantity and monetary bridges independently:

| Class | Required bridge |
|---|---|
| RM | Opening + purchases/receipts + signed transfers/adjustments − net production issues = closing |
| WIP | Opening + eligible material + direct labour + eligible manufacturing overhead + signed reviewed variance adjustments − completed production − separately traced losses = closing |
| FG | Opening + production completion + purchased FG + signed adjustments − sale relief/usage/disposals = closing |

Internal movements eliminate between source/destination locations. Transfers do not create entity-level cost. Completion removes WIP and adds FG once; sales relieve supported carrying cost once. The consolidated cost bridge includes external additions, eligible conversion additions, losses and dispositions, without double-counting internal RM→WIP→FG movement. Require complete location populations and reject unexplained negative stock or balancing plugs.

Illustration in currency units: opening RM/WIP/FG 10,000/2,000/4,000; purchases 5,000; RM issue 9,000; labour 3,000; eligible overhead 1,500; completion 14,000; sale relief 11,000. Closing RM/WIP/FG is 6,000/1,500/7,000. Total opening 16,000 + purchases 5,000 + conversion 4,500 − relief 11,000 = closing 14,500. The material issue and completion disappear on consolidation because they are internal movements.

## Recover overhead without hiding capacity losses

Separate fixed and variable production pools. Require actual source inputs, exclusions, normal-capacity evidence, actual driver usage, rates and approved allocation policy. A recovery calculation that reconciles using the wrong denominator is still invalid.

For an applicable normal-capacity fixed pool method, rate = eligible fixed pool / controlled normal driver capacity. Low output must not increase the per-unit rate. High output requires preventing recovery above eligible actual pool cost. Variable recovery follows supported actual usage. An entity's standard architecture may instead use a budgeted standard rate: separately reconcile actual pool, standard absorption, spending and volume effects, then apply the framework restrictions to disposition.

The signed difference actual overhead − absorbed overhead is under-recovery if positive and over-recovery if negative. For 1.20m actual and 1.05m absorbed, under-recovery is 0.15m. Suppose reviewed cause analysis identifies 0.10m unallocated capacity loss and 0.05m normal rate differences. The capacity loss is not buried in stock. If a supplied appropriate disposition allocates the normal difference 10% WIP, 30% FG and 60% sold cost, adjustments are 0.005m/0.015m/0.030m. Stock rises 0.020m, rather than the full 0.15m. This allocation is illustrative company evidence, not a universal accounting policy.

For over-recovery, investigate duplicate recovery, high-volume absorption or lower actual spending. A negative difference cannot create fictitious inventory. Apply reviewed disposition to reverse excessive stock/COGS absorption and preserve the actual eligible cost ceiling. Neither blanket capitalization nor blanket expensing of material normal variances is a valid substitute for analysis.

## Standard cost and variance architecture

Obtain current approved material quantities/prices, labour hours/rates, overhead drivers/rates and the effective standard version. Check the reviewed approximation to actual cost and whether material differences require unsold-stock/sold-cost adjustment. A standard update requires a traced cost revaluation method; it is not permission for unsupported uplift.

Use only the company's actual decomposition. Positive values below mean adverse cost:

| Effect | Controlled formula |
|---|---|
| Material price on consumption | Actual quantity × (actual price − standard price) |
| Material usage | (Actual net quantity − standard quantity allowed for actual good output) × standard price |
| Labour rate | Actual hours × (actual rate − standard rate) |
| Labour efficiency | (Actual hours − standard hours allowed) × standard rate |
| Variable overhead spending | Actual variable pool − actual driver × standard variable rate |
| Variable overhead efficiency | (Actual driver − standard allowed driver) × standard variable rate |
| Fixed overhead spending | Actual eligible fixed pool − budgeted fixed pool |
| Fixed overhead volume | Budgeted fixed pool − standard absorbed fixed overhead |

Receipt PPV and consumed-material price effects require an exact-once architecture. Yield/mix cannot be added again if already captured in usage. Normal/abnormal scrap and rework classifications require production evidence; material loss cannot disappear in an unexplained quantity bridge. Separate abnormal loss from cost adjustment before any journal.

Actual costing uses the same source identity and eligibility rules without invented standards. Job, order and batch cost are supported collection dimensions. Process costing needs controlled equivalent-unit completion stages and an explicit opening-WIP policy; no percentage is inferred. Complex joint/co-product allocation, material by-products and retail/dollar-value LIFO methods require a reviewed method beyond this ordinary route and fail closed when absent.

## Valuation, counts and reporting

Apply the chosen reviewed formula consistently. FIFO layer relief and periodic/moving weighted average use actual eligible quantities, costs and dates. Specific identification follows genuine segregated/noninterchangeable items. US LIFO must pass its applicable supplied-method gate; the other frameworks do not inherit that option. The framework lower-cost route is separate from the formula calculation: ordinary US non-LIFO/non-retail stock and US LIFO/retail must not share one unconditional NRV engine.

For value recovery, require current controlled price, completion and selling/disposal cost evidence. A zero completion or selling amount is an explicit supported fact, not a default. Review expiry, damage, discontinuation and excess quantities using supplied demand evidence. No future forecast or arbitrary reserve percentage is generated. Raw-material exceptions and inventory grouping require their framework-specific route; portfolio profit must not conceal expired stock. Prior allowance and proposed write-down must reconcile once. A supported IFRS-family reversal cannot be transplanted into US accounting.

Counts establish signed adjustments only after complete population, custody and cutoff checks. Receipts, shipments, transfers and production completion must be tied to actual economic dates. Reconcile subledger→GL→statements and relief→COGS, separately showing non-sale production expense. Supply inventory-specific disclosure data to the Disclosure owner using the correct framework/entity route. Maintain balanced journals as proposed workpaper implications; never post them.

## Owner handoffs and unresolved methods

Agriculture is one upstream owner: consume completed harvest basis exactly once and preserve its dimensions. Do not repeat harvest gain, remeasure biology or reassign post-harvest adjustments. Borrowing Costs remains nonproduction; a qualifying-inventory capitalization dependency blocks that component and does not authorize this workflow to implement it.

Systems/Data owns BOM/routing/item/interface integrity. Controls owns control design and readiness. Close/Reconciliations consume these bridges; Statements and Disclosures own final presentation. Revenue owns revenue, Payroll owns payroll, Fixed Assets owns depreciation, Leases owns lease accounting, FX owns translation and AP owns liabilities. The Inventory result exposes inputs, methods, reconciliations, exceptions, journals and owner dependencies so a future orchestrator can compose manufacturing review without rebuilding these owners.
