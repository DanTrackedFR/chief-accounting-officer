# Framework and Overlay Rules

## Principle

A topic is framework-neutral at the topic level. Framework-specific accounting lives in knowledge records beneath it.

## Active frameworks

IFRS, US GAAP, UK GAAP and AASB.

## AASB inheritance

AASB may reuse a verified IFRS-derived conceptual explanation only where the Australian requirements have been independently checked and no Australian-specific difference affects the issue. The AASB record MUST still:
- identify the AASB authoritative source;
- state whether it inherits common reasoning;
- record Australian additions, exclusions, effective dates or disclosure differences;
- avoid silently assuming equivalence.

## Jurisdiction

Framework and jurisdiction are separate overlays. A US GAAP conclusion does not automatically imply SEC applicability. UK GAAP does not automatically imply a particular Companies Act filing requirement.

## Industry

Industry overlays alter facts, risks, data, controls, regulatory interfaces and execution. They do not replace the applicable accounting framework.

## N/A

A framework may be N/A for a topic only with a documented reason. Operational topics must not be forced into artificial four-framework analysis.
