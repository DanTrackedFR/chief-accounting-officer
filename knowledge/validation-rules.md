# Knowledge Validation Rules

A topic cannot be COMPLETE until applicable validation passes.

## Structural

- Valid stable IDs.
- Valid topic and framework.
- Source IDs resolve.
- Related skill IDs resolve.
- Effective-period fields are considered.
- Review status is present.
- No orphan difference records.

## Authority and provenance

- Standards claims trace to primary authority.
- Interpretive claims identify their source level.
- Source-check date is present.
- Professional commentary is not presented as authoritative literature.
- Superseded records remain traceable.

## Copyright and licensing

- No prohibited source text stored.
- No close reconstruction of restricted standards text.
- Restricted source files are not committed.
- Links and paragraph references comply with recorded source-use classification.
- Unknown rights default to restrictive handling.

## Accounting quality

- Applicability is explicit.
- Required facts and decision points are explicit.
- Exceptions and alternatives are considered.
- Significant judgments and estimates are surfaced.
- Accounting, journal, presentation and disclosure consequences are considered.
- Documentation, control, audit and systems/data implications are considered where relevant.

## Cross-framework

- All active applicable frameworks are complete or explicitly N/A.
- Differences analysis completed.
- Same-fact-pattern tests produce expected framework distinctions.

## Completion gate

Only after validation, GitHub commit and Master Build Map update may status become COMPLETE.
