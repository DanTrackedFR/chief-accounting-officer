# Standards Rights and Licensing Posture

Last reviewed: 2026-09-22

This file governs conservative repository handling. It is not legal advice and does not grant rights beyond publisher terms.

## IFRS Foundation
Status: REFERENCE_ONLY pending separate licensing.
Repository may identify standards, cite paragraph references, link to official sources and contain independently authored CAO analysis. Do not reproduce or ingest standards text without applicable rights.

## FASB / US GAAP Codification
Status: REFERENCE_ONLY.
FASB's Codification user materials state Codification content is copyrighted and direct users to terms/copyright permission for reproduction. Store ASC references and original CAO analysis; do not reproduce Codification text absent permission.

## FRC / UK GAAP
Status: REFERENCE_ONLY for the open-source product.
FRC terms permit reproduction for defined Professional Use/private study and education, while uses outside Professional Use require a separate licence and distribution is restricted. An open-source CAO knowledge product is therefore treated conservatively as outside the safe repository reproduction case unless rights are confirmed. Cite FRS sections/paragraphs and official pages; author original analysis.

## AASB
Status: REFERENCE_ONLY for the open-source product.
AASB standards containing IFRS Foundation material state that unaltered reproduction is permitted for personal/non-commercial use subject to conditions, while commercial reproduction requires permission; rights outside Australia are also restricted. The repository will therefore cite AASB paragraph references and official portal pages and use independently authored analysis.

## Default
Public availability does not equal republication permission. UNKNOWN rights default to REFERENCE_ONLY.
