## Official entrypoints and version control

- IFRS Foundation [issued standards](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/) and corresponding individual standard pages. For 2026 non-early-adopters, IAS 1 still applies before IFRS 18 annual periods beginning 1 January 2027. IAS 21 lack-of-exchangeability from 2025; IAS 21 hyperinflationary-presentation amendment issued November 2025 effective 2027. IFRS 9/7 classification amendment effective annual periods from 2026.
- FASB [Codification](https://asc.fasb.org/) is authoritative for current US GAAP. ASUs/official project pages verify particular amendments and dates but are not substitutes for the full current paragraph body. Public access here has not exposed all current paragraphs. Paragraph-level US claims remain source-depth PARTIAL pending authorized access.
- FRC [FRS 102 current register](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-102/) and [official factsheets](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/frs-factsheets/). Periodic Review 2024 principal effective date 1 January 2026; particular transition and relief must be checked in the applicable edition. Separate adapted-format changes effective 2027 for entities electing them.
- AASB [standards portal](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/accounting-standards). Select the compilation operative for the beginning of the reporting period and entity/tier. For example, [AASB 3 December 2022](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-3-dec-2022) applies before 1 July 2026; [AASB 9 February 2025](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-9-feb-2025) applies to specified 2026 periods with subsequent amendments; [AASB 10 November 2024](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-10-nov-2024) and [AASB 128 November 2024](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-128-nov-2024) provide versioned entrypoints. AASB 8 online compilation is [March 2020](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-8-mar-2020) with later amendment register. Avoid treating a URL date as a universal operative period.

## Verified focused differences and limitations

- IFRS 3/AASB 3 acquisition method differs from FRS 102 Section 19 purchase method: the [FRC official significant-differences page](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/accounting-and-reporting/uk-accounting-standards/uk-accounting-standards-overview/significant-differences-between-frs-102-and-the-ifrs-for-smes-accounting-standard/) identifies directly attributable combination cost inclusion at FRS 102 19.11(b), merger accounting for qualifying group reconstructions at 19.29–19.33 and different contingent consideration and intangible asset treatment. Do not transpose modern IFRS 3 entries to UK GAAP.
- IAS 29 IFRS Foundation overview identifies general-price-index restatement, net-monetary gain/loss in profit or loss, and disclosures. The IASB issued the IAS 21 translation-to-hyperinflationary-presentation-currency amendment in November 2025, generally effective 2027, correcting older pipeline descriptions.
- IFRS 18, FRS 102 Periodic Review 2024 and AASB 18 have separate commencement/transition gates. SEC/UK company law/Australian tier overlays require entity and filing facts. An operational topic can use its underlying standards as a handoff, without inventing a new four-framework recognition rule.
- The crosswalk is an entrypoint for QA, not evidence that every applicable paragraph and transition was verified. For each production case, record the operative source version, exact paragraph where accessible, entity scope, date and reviewer. The per-topic residuals are in `qa-remediation-self-review.md`.
