# Agriculture accounting method and owner boundaries

## Framework selection precedes arithmetic

IFRS and AASB full for-profit Tier1: managed agricultural living assets use fair value less costs to sell (FVCTS). Bearer plants satisfying the repeated-produce/remote-sale conditions belong to PPE; their growing produce stays Agriculture. Dairy and breeding animals remain Agriculture: do not infer a bearer-animal PPE exemption from bearer plants. Annual crops and lumber trees are consumable; fruit/lumber dual-purpose plants need careful sale-probability review. Livestock, managed crops, forestry plantations and aquaculture can qualify. Unmanaged ocean fishing or unmanaged forest harvesting is outside the managed-biological-transformation scope. Apply AGR-IFRS/AASB scope, bearer, nonbearer and land-rights decisions.

UK full FRS102: document elected policy for each biological asset class and related produce. Fair value and cost are alternatives. Under fair value, use FVCTS and P&L; under cost, use cost less depreciation/impairment, with distinct harvest measurement options. Do not import IAS41 bearer-plant carve-out: Section34 preharvest produce is not separately distinguished from its biological asset. Plant production classes need Section34 classification and policy rather than automatic PPE reclassification. Ordinary farm equipment and land remain their respective owners. Apply AGR-UK_GAAP election, preharvest, fv, exception, cost and harvest decisions.

US: agricultural growing crops and developing sale animals are inventory cost routes. Ready-for-sale animals and harvested crops can have a bounded NRV industry alternative, conditional on the actual prerequisites. Productive animals and productive orchards require industry PPE treatment. No blanket IAS41 fair value gain and no automatic FVCTS deemed inventory cost at harvest. Inventory monetary execution is blocked because Skill24 remains reserved; PPE work goes to the existing fixed-asset owner, with specialist scope review. Cooperatives and specialized industry cases are excluded. Apply AGR-US_GAAP relevant category decision.

## Evidence and control

Identify entity/framework/jurisdiction, period start/end, biological asset class, documented policy, actual source/subledger and GL populations, currency and units. Require actual rights/control documentation, probable-benefit evidence and identity/quantity records at each accounting measurement date. Physical presence or a livestock tag by itself does not establish recognition. Inventory acquired from third parties is not the entity's produce growing on an agricultural biological asset. Land, equipment, contractual rights and produce after harvest are different owner scopes.

Reconcile individually identified opening units plus verified additions/births/plantings minus verified whole-unit harvest/deaths/sales to actual closing units. Weight or grade changes are not extra animal identities. Prevent source aliases, duplicate transactions, missing records, and sold/dead/terminally harvested units remaining at closing. Each class/unit must use compatible quantity units; hectares, number of animals, kg and litres must not be combined into one quantity equation. Monetary source reconciliation uses one currency and controlled opening/closing GL amounts. Foreign-currency cases consume FX-owner results and otherwise block aggregation.

## Qualified valuation and measurement dates

Agriculture owns the accounting basis and deduction of eligible incremental selling costs. Fair Value owns method, market participants, hierarchy and model governance. A qualified external agriculture valuation may supply asset, currency, quantity/grade, measurement-date fair value and report/accountable specialist evidence. A stale report cannot establish a different date's value unless a qualified reviewed update supports that date. Selling costs must exclude financing/income taxes and not repeat a transport/location adjustment already included in the valuation. Contract prices and transaction costs are not automatically fair value or costs to sell. No made-up yield, quantity, price, or autonomous discounted cash-flow engine.

Measure at initial recognition, reporting date and actual harvest date as applicable. IFRS/AASB exception is initial recognition only, absent quoted prices and clearly unreliable alternatives. Prior FVCTS cannot revert to cost. UK elected FV asset has its own inability-to-measure rule until reliable FV returns; do not falsely apply the IAS41 initial-only restriction. Cost exception, return-to-FV transitions and elected UK cost require qualified specialist results; bounded implementation may block these with the reason and required owner data rather than fabricate completeness.

## Rollforward and journals

In a single currency, closing biological carrying amount = opening + purchases + recognized birth/other biological additions + remeasurement − biological carrying removed for harvest − death/disposal carrying removed, plus any separately supported combination/FX/other movements. If birth values are separately shown in the bridge, exclude them from the residual remeasurement amount; total P&L includes both birth recognition and residual remeasurement. A purchase's invoice value and initial FVCTS may differ: record initial loss/gain explicitly, never silently treat invoice as FVCTS.

For a whole-unit terminal harvest, establish the supported preharvest biological carrying amount and separately obtain qualified harvested-produce fair value less costs to sell at the actual harvest date on the actual harvested-produce quantity and unit. A living animal measured per head and its carcass measured in kilograms are different valuation populations; do not default their amounts or units to equality. Support any biological remeasurement separately. Derecognize the biological carrying amount once, recognize inventory at the independently supported harvested-produce FVCTS, and recognize the conversion difference in profit or loss. For the supported IFRS/AASB terminal route, if biological carrying is70 and harvested-produce FVCTS65, debit harvest-boundary inventory65 and Agriculture loss5, and credit biological assets70. Missing produce valuation or quantity/unit evidence blocks the harvest accounting result. UK harvest FVCTS measurement remains governed by Section34.5, but unequal terminal conversion profit/loss execution requires separately governed UK conversion support; until supplied, block that monetary route rather than label a deduction from Section34.4/34.5 as direct verification. This limitation does not impose equal biological/produce values: separately qualified equal-value UK cases may proceed within their supported contract. For a surviving parent producing milk, fruit or wool, do not derecognize the parent; produce recognition and parent measurement must reflect the actual framework and avoid duplication. Bounded execution can require separately identified produce-growing units and decline unsupported parent-yield allocation. Hand inventory only its quantity, currency, harvest date and harvest accounting amount; no downstream costing, NRV, reserve, absorption or manufacturing computation.

Synthetic arithmetic: biological opening100,000 + purchases20,000 + biological remeasurement33,000 − preharvest biological carrying derecognized28,000 − deaths5,000 = closing120,000. Units100 + purchases10 − terminal harvest20 − deaths5 =85. Separate qualified harvested-produce valuation35,000 less eligible selling costs5,000 gives30,000 inventory entry on the actual produce quantity/unit. The journal debits inventory30,000, credits biological assets28,000 and credits Agriculture conversion gain2,000; total Agriculture gains are35,000, comprising biological remeasurement33,000 and harvest conversion2,000. Do not include the conversion gain a second time in biological remeasurement. Journal pairs balance; inventory amount is counted once. Gain credits P&L and loss debits P&L; no OCI/equity plug. Death loss cannot be hidden by inventing growth gains: movement evidence and bridge decomposition must be reconciled.

Sales with proceeds, grants, acquisitions, FX and cost-model calculations require explicit qualified owners. A bounded implementation can block such cases; recording a limitation alone does not authorize false COMPLETE.

## Reporting and disclosure support

IFRS/AASB: group descriptions, aggregate Agriculture gain/loss, activity descriptions, actual closing physical quantities and produce output if not otherwise disclosed; restricted title/pledged assets, development/acquisition commitments and financial risk strategy; carrying reconciliation with separately controlled movements. Consumable/bearer and mature/immature quantified splits and price/physical change separation are encouraged, rather than universal mandatory disclosure. Do not fabricate such splits from a residual gain. Exception assets need the additional cost/valuation-reliability information. Valuation-method/hierarchy details belong to the qualified FV/reporting owner, with actual applicable general-standard disclosure review. Overall statement presentation is consumed through Financial Statements/Disclosure Management; this package is support, not a standalone financial statement renderer.

UK FV: biological class description, measurement methods/significant assumptions, current carrying reconciliation and harvested-produce FV methods/assumptions. Cost: class description, depreciation methods/lives, reconciliation including impairment movements; FV harvest assumptions where elected. Do not copy all IAS41 quantity/pledge disclosure mandates to UK. Section1A and company-law relief/overlays are outside full FRS102 support.

US: no IAS41 disclosure mandate is presumed; needs ASC905/general Topic and entity reporting-owner review, with the direct-source limitations retained. No full US Agriculture monetary pack is represented as COMPLETE.

All agriculture grants cases route/block. IAS41/AASB141 have specific FV-biological grants rules, cost/bearer grants involve the general grant standard, UK uses its own Section24 and AASB NFP differs. Government Grants remains NONPRODUCTION; knowledge does not silently rebuild it.
