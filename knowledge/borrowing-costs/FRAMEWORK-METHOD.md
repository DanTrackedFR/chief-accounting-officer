5. Apply the framework's method independently. IFRS/AASB specific financing takes actual qualifying-period eligible interest less related temporary investment income (005). General financing applies its actual weighted borrowing cost rate to actual eligible expenditure; eligible expenditure generally includes previously capitalized borrowing cost where relevant. Cap total capitalization at actual incurred eligible cost (006–009). A specifically financed completed asset's debt may become general only after separately evidenced completion. Allocation among concurrent assets is unsupported.
6. US calculates avoidable interest on weighted accumulated qualifying expenditure: specific construction rates first up to supported specific debt capacity, other-debt weighted rates on the excess. Actual incurred interest is a second ceiling; it is not specific actual interest less investment income. Ordinary investment returns remain separate income; tax-exempt financing exceptions are blocked (005–007,023). No single blended IFRS algorithm qualifies a US case.
7. UK first applies explicit current expense or capitalization policy consistently to the asset class (023). Expense policy produces zero capitalization. Capitalization-specific financing deducts supported temporary investment income. The general route applies weighted general rates to average carrying amount INCLUDING previously capitalized borrowing costs (006); a cash-only opening basis is insufficient. All continuing asset and financing disclosures remain relevant (019).
8. Reconcile eligible cost incurred = capitalization + recognized expense + separately evidenced adjustments; investment-income offset is a separate support line. Do not net an unexplained residual. Reconcile capitalized addition to asset/CIP GL, residual expense to finance-cost GL and incurred source to financing accounting. If interest already exists as expense, proposed journal is debit CIP, credit finance expense for the capitalized reclassification, without a second lender liability. No ERP posting. No journals on incomplete or uncertified work (016,018,021).
9. Disclose capitalized amount and applicable rate for IFRS/AASB/UK capitalization; US reports total incurred interest and capitalized amount (019). Record significant judgments and unsupported populations. Public accounting output is allowlisted; private reviews and assurance notes remain internal.
