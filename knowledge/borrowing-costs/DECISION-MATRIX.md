# Qualification and decisions

| Fact / objective | IFRS | AASB Tier1 for-profit | US GAAP ordinary | FRS102 full commercial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary own-use tangible construction with substantial preparation | Qualify actual asset then capitalize direct borrowing cost | Separate Australian applicability, then direct-cost method | Qualify ASC835-20 asset then avoidable-interest method | Explicit consistent asset-class expense/capitalization policy |
| Loan specifically identified with project | Eligible actual cost in active period less related investment income | Same measurement through independently qualified AASB claims | Apply specific rate to weighted qualifying expenditure and capacity | Eligible actual cost less related investment income under capitalization policy |
| General loans | Weighted eligible borrowing cost rate on qualified expenditure | Exclude incomplete-asset specific loans from pool | Other debt rate on weighted expenditure excess after specific capacity | Weighted rates on average carrying amount including prior capitalization |
| Ordinary temporary investment return | Deduct related specific-funding return in eligible amount | Same independently sourced rule | Separate income; no ordinary offset | Deduct related specific-funding return for capitalization method |
| Necessary short process delay | Normally continue if requirements hold | Normally continue | Qualify US-specific temporary-delay rule | Independently qualified application judgment; not blanket IAS23 inference |
| Extended intentional inactive development | Suspend | Suspend | Suspend based on US conditions | Suspend under capitalization policy |
| Asset ready or independently usable part complete | Stop attributable allocation | Stop attributable allocation | Stop actual qualifying portion | Evidence-supported completion, with component specialist judgments reviewed |
| Budget-only or unsupported accounting dates | Block | Block | Block | Block |
| FX, leases, software, inventory, public-sector funding, equity-method, tax-exempt or group interest | Specialist block | Specialist block | Specialist block | Specialist block |
| Historical year / early new presentation / future year | Applicability block | Applicability block | Applicability block | Applicability block |

Every included economic cost appears exactly once in a financing population and one capitalization/expense allocation. Imported results must be exactly requalified; simple journal claims cannot replace source-owner evidence. Reviewer identity records validate supplied approvals, not authenticated people.
