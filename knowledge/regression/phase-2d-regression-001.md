## Result

## Architecture findings
1. Operational topics should remain compact knowledge packages rather than manufacture framework-specific records where no meaningful GAAP divergence exists.
2. Controls, artifacts, systems/data and scenario tests remain mandatory even when STANDARDS records are not.
3. Reconciliation knowledge requires a strict distinction between accounting substantiation and workflow completion.
4. Recurring dependency: close architecture → journal integrity → GL integrity → account reconciliation → certification.
5. Evidence lineage, risk/materiality tiering, exception management and source completeness are recurring primitives and should become shared methods.
6. Automation never removes accounting ownership: source completeness, configuration/change control, exception routing and output reconciliation remain required.

## Temporal/source finding
Presentation and basis-of-preparation knowledge needs reporting-period routing beyond leases. IFRS 18 replaces IAS 1 for annual periods beginning on/after 1 January 2027 and consequentially moved some preparation concepts to IAS 8. FRS 102 Periodic Review 2024 has a principal 1 January 2026 effective date, while 2026 adapted-format amendments are effective 1 January 2027. AASB 18 also creates consequential version changes. Future reporting/preparation topics must therefore resolve reporting period before selecting records.
