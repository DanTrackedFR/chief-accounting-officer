# Knowledge Update and Supersession

Accounting knowledge is temporal.

## Change triggers

Review may be triggered by:
- new or amended standards;
- effective-date transitions;
- official interpretations or agenda decisions;
- regulator publications;
- authoritative corrections;
- jurisdiction adoption changes;
- identified errors or contradictions;
- licensing/copyright changes.

## Workflow

1. Identify source change.
2. Register or update source metadata.
3. Identify affected knowledge records and skills.
4. Determine effective period.
5. Draft replacement record/version.
6. Run framework differences review.
7. Run affected scenario/regression tests.
8. Mark old record superseded; never erase historical meaning.
9. Commit.
10. Update Master Build Map and decision/change log.

## Dates

Keep separate:
- source publication date;
- accounting effective date;
- source verification date;
- CAO knowledge-record preparation/review date.

A later discovery of an earlier effective date updates applicability evidence; it must not pretend the CAO learned the information earlier.
