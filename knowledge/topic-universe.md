# Phase 2B — Complete Accounting Topic Universe v1

Status: MAPPED — QA pending  
Date: 2026-09-22

This is the framework-neutral knowledge map between the 347 Phase 1 CAO capabilities and Phase 2 knowledge records. Topic IDs are stable. This first-pass denominator deliberately groups adjacent capabilities into coherent build units; Phase 2C may add subtopics beneath these IDs without silently changing the top-level denominator.

## Rules

- Build topic-by-topic across applicable frameworks, not framework-by-framework.
- Sensitivity: H = standards-heavy; M = some framework dependency; L = primarily principles/practice; J = jurisdiction/regulatory overlay.
- H topics evaluate IFRS, US GAAP, UK GAAP and AASB unless explicitly inapplicable.
- Operational topics must not manufacture four-framework content.
- Industry remains a cross-cutting overlay.

6. Sustainability, jurisdiction and industry overlays layered onto relevant core topics.
