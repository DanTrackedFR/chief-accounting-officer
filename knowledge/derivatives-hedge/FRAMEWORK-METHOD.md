# Bounded accounting method and owner handoffs

Signed instrument values are asset-positive/liability-negative. Values and designated-risk changes are independently qualified, currency/entity/date/quantity matched inputs. Change equals closing minus opening plus settlement where settlement removes an instrument balance; never treat a settled closing zero as zero period gain. Produce balanced journals, reserve movements, GL ties and unresolved items; do not price a derivative or post to ERP.

For IFRS9/AASB9 cash-flow hedges, cumulative effective reserve entitlement is the instrument sign times the lesser of cumulative instrument gain/loss and absolute cumulative offsetting designated-risk change. Require opposite risk movements before using this method. Current OCI is the change in cumulative entitlement; current earnings residual is current instrument movement less current OCI. Separate prior reclassification/basis adjustment ledgers from cumulative entitlement, so prior reserve releases cannot be reintroduced as current OCI. A qualifying nonfinancial forecast purchase removes the associated reserve into asset basis exactly once: positive gain reserve reduces asset cost; negative loss increases asset cost. Forecast-sale reserve releases when actual designated revenue affects earnings, without recomputing revenue.

The ordinary US qualifying cash-flow route with ASU2017-12 adopted puts the full change included in assessment into OCI and releases to earnings with the actual underlying transaction. It does not use the IFRS lower-of split and does not directly adjust inventory purchase basis. Probability qualification and probable nonoccurrence reserve release are distinct. US documented assessment methods and eligible components must independently qualify; quantitative numbers alone do not prove highly effective status. Unsupported early adoption of ASU2025-07 or2025-09 is blocked rather than mislabelled proposed.

Native FRS102 Section12 cash-flow mechanics use lower-of amounts from the qualification date, separate earnings residual and nonfinancial basis adjustment. Hedge start is the first date all12.18 conditions are satisfied, which may differ from original instrument inception. For an ordinary continuing entity no pre-qualification retroactive accounting is allowed; first-time adoption/transition relief is outside scope. A documented voluntary discontinuation is permitted. Effective net-investment reserve does not recycle on disposal under12.24; IFRS/AASB disposal routing cannot be copied here.

A basic fair-value hedge puts instrument change through earnings and independently measured designated-risk item change through earnings with the appropriate signed asset/liability carrying adjustment. Debt owner must acknowledge the exact carrying amount and basis adjustment without reperforming its amortised-cost schedule. Financial asset amortised-cost hedge basis amortisation requires the owner's recalculated effective-interest schedule; missing schedule leaves that output unresolved. Unrecognized firm-commitment routes need separate recognised commitment basis and fulfilment evidence.

Net-investment routes consume current foreign-operation/net-investment and reporting-perimeter evidence. They do not rebuild CTA. IFRS/AASB effective amounts enter translation reserve with ineffectiveness earnings and actual disposal-owner handoffs; ordinary US included change follows US translation adjustment method. FRS102 no-recycle applies. Effective exposure quantities cannot exceed supported net investment.

Rebalancing under IFRS9/AASB9 is prospective and keeps documented history and pre-change ineffectiveness. A changed objective is discontinuation, not cosmetic ratio repair. US and native FRS102 have distinct continuation/dedesignation controls. Terminated instruments cannot keep generating current hedge OCI. Discontinued cash-flow reserves may remain for expected transactions; failure of ongoing probability threshold alone does not automatically release OCI.

Fair Value owns prices/curves and valuation hierarchy. FX/Consolidation owns currency/CTA/net investment. Debt owns principal/amortised cost. Inventory consumes the exact basis handoff once. Financial Instruments keeps classification/ECL. Revenue keeps sales recognition. Reporting owns final statements/disclosures. This package supplies derivative, earnings, OCI and reserve/basis movement evidence, including missing narrative evidence explicitly; it does not create the orchestrator.
