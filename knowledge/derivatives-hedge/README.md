# Supplemental Derivatives / Hedge knowledge

Namespace `SUPPLEMENTAL_DERIVATIVES_HEDGE` contains 100 separately counted decision-specific claims (25 each framework). It does not alter any canonical denominator. Canonical TOPIC-06-008/009 supply derivative/hedge ownership and baseline gates; this namespace supplies missing atomic mechanics necessary to turn those gates into a controlled accounting workflow. It excludes broader investment accounting CAO-06-019.

Status is REVIEWED until a separate knowledge reviewer signs each material claim. Author cannot approve their own claims. Claims retain actual evidence, confidence, direct-audit obligations, entity/period limits and approval provenance separately from approval status.

Supported bounded models are IFRS9_CH6, AASB9_CH6, ASC815_BASELINE and FRS102_SECTION12. Alternative retained IAS39/AASB139 and UK recognition/measurement elections block. US baseline assumes adopted ASU2017-12 and does not implement early adoption of ASU2025-07/-09. ASU2025-09 is issued current amendment rather than a mere proposal: PBE effective annual starts after15December2026, other entities after15December2027; early adoption permitted. ASU2025-07 is effective annual starts after15December2026 for all entities, early adoption permitted. No IASB PIR, draft risk mitigation or future amendment is treated as current normative mechanics.

Retrieval requires explicit framework, model, supported entity scope, dates, approved claim hashes and optional exact decision set. Public projection excludes provenance, reviewer controls and source notes. Direct-source audit remains open on model/primary-corroborated claims even after authorized training-data approval.

The bound is ordinary, individually documented full-instrument derivative and hedge accounting. Valuation and underlying accounting remain external owners. Options/excluded components, commodity/location basis, macro hedging, portfolio layers, shortcut/critical terms, advanced partial-term or benchmark components, nature-dependent electricity and specialized industry methods block pending further governed work.

Run `python knowledge/derivatives-hedge/validate_supplement.py` and unittest discovery in this directory. Independent reviewer owns their review report/tests; implementation reviewer is a separate context.
