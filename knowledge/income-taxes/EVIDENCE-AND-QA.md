# Income-tax evidence, period and QA record

## Evidence policy
Architecture approval in PR #15 authorizes a supplemental namespace and the repository two-track approval policy. Approval and source assurance are separate. SOURCE_VERIFIED requires inspection of operative authority and a locator. TRAINING_DATA_CHECKED may support project approval after genuinely independent claim-level challenge while retaining a lower evidence rating and audit flag.

## Authoritative research performed 2026-10-02

### IFRS — IAS 12 / IFRIC 23
Official IFRS Foundation IAS 12 materials were inspected at https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-12-income-taxes/. The official page confirms the 2021 single-transaction amendment and the May 2023 Pillar Two amendment, including the temporary deferred-tax exception and targeted disclosures. The publicly inspected material did not expose the complete operative IAS 12/IFRIC 23 paragraph set needed to directly verify every proposition. No blanket SOURCE_VERIFIED upgrade is justified; propositions not tied to inspected operative text remain model-derived and require the authorized training-data review track.

Period gate: determine the entity adopted IFRS edition and amendment effective dates, including the single-transaction and Pillar Two amendments, before applying a conclusion. IFRS 18 consequential changes must be considered for periods to which IFRS 18 applies.

### US GAAP — ASC 740 / SEC
Official FASB ASU 2023-09 was inspected at https://fasb.org/page/document?pdf=ASU%202023-09.pdf&title=ACCOUNTING%20STANDARDS%20UPDATE%202023-09. It applies to Topic 740 entities and enhances rate-reconciliation and income-taxes-paid disclosures. Public-business-entity annual periods beginning after 15 December 2024 are within its mandatory effective-date population; other entity effective dates and early-adoption facts must be checked against the ASU for the reporting entity. SEC filings and XBRL references corroborate current practice but are not substituted for operative Codification text.

The operative FASB Codification text for the complete population was not legitimately inspected in this workstream. Core ASC 740 recognition, valuation allowance, uncertain-tax-position, allocation and presentation propositions therefore must use the training-data checked route unless an exact operative Codification provision is independently inspected. ASU or SEC corroboration alone does not justify SOURCE_VERIFIED for an unchanged Codification proposition.

### UK GAAP — FRS 102 Section 29
The FRC September 2024 edition was directly inspected at https://media.frc.org.uk/documents/FRS_102_September_2024_Redacted_edition_UkckFQ0.pdf. The FRC identifies September 2024 as the latest full edition and Periodic Review 2024 principal effective date as 1 January 2026. Section 29 paragraphs 29.2–29.17C were inspected, including current tax, enacted/substantively enacted measurement, timing-differences-plus, recoverability, business combinations, no discounting and uncertain tax treatments. The 2024 edition contains a Pillar Two exception. Later amendments must be screened for the entity period; FRC records include amendments effective in 2025 and 2027.

This supports direct-source treatment only for propositions whose locator and period match the inspected paragraphs. It does not make IFRS temporary-difference conclusions interchangeable with FRS 102 timing-differences-plus model.

### Australia — AASB 112
The AASB September 2023 compilation was directly inspected at https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-112-sep-2023. The AASB identifies it as operative for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2023 but before 1 January 2027 that end on or after 30 September 2023. Inspected provisions include definitions/tax bases, paragraphs 12–14 current tax, 15–45 deferred-tax recognition, 46–48 measurement, 57–68C allocation/share-based payments, 71–76 offset and 88A–88D Pillar Two disclosures. AASB version history identifies later AASB 18 consequential amendments for future periods.

AASB claims may be SOURCE_VERIFIED only when mapped to the inspected operative provision and entity/tier/period. Tier 1 for-profit AASB 112 compliance also complies with IAS 12; Tier 2 uses Australian Simplified Disclosures and does not assert IFRS compliance.

## Effective-date workpaper requirement
Every retrieval request must identify framework, reporting period and entity scope. Before using a claim, document the operative edition, amendments effective for the period, early adoption, jurisdiction-specific tax-law enactment status and reporting tier/SEC status where applicable. Tax rates are not hard-coded in this knowledge package.

## Independent QA
The implementing context performed source research and authored the package. It is not an independent reviewer and cannot sign the independent-review field. A separate reviewer must challenge every material proposition and examples, record exact claim dispositions, and rerun the gate. Until that happens the namespace remains INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_PENDING and retrieval fails closed. This is an assurance boundary, not a source-access blocker.
