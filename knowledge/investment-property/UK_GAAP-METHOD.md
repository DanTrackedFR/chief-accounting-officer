# UK_GAAP — owned investment-property accounting

2026 full commercial FRS102; direct assurance remains claim-specific.

## Definition

Investment property is land, a building or an identified portion held for rentals, capital appreciation or both; classification depends on actual economic use rather than the GL name.

## Owner Use

Property used for production, services or administration is owner occupied and follows the PPE owner; substantive ancillary services can make a rental property owner occupied.

## Mixed Use

FRS102 16.4 separates mixed-use portions if separately saleable or separately finance-leasable. This runtime supports only separately identified rental portions with reconciled source costs; inseparable mixed use is blocked rather than importing the IAS40 insignificant-use rule. The inspected text also addresses unreliable separate fair value; this is not an ordinary-property cost election under the deleted 16.8.

## Ancillary Services

Ordinary security and maintenance may be incidental; significant hotel-style services require owner-occupied classification and a documented significance judgment.

## Construction

Property under construction for future investment use is considered in Section16 scope; the 2026 requirement cannot be avoided merely by construction status. This runtime purchase/period adapter blocks construction and specialist valuation routes.

## Initial Recognition

FRS102 Section16 includes owned property and, in the 2026 lease model, qualifying right-of-use interests; recognition requires actual rights and reliable cost, with ROU measurement reserved to the Lease owner.

## Initial Cost

Initial owned-property cost includes purchase price and directly attributable costs; self-construction follows Section17 cost rules. Deferred payments use present value; financing and construction routes are outside this bounded purchase adapter.

## Subsequent Policy

For ordinary investment property FRS102 16.7 in the September2024 edition requires fair value at every reporting date. The old undue-cost-or-effort exception and 16.8 were deleted for 2026. No general voluntary cost election is supported.

## Fair Value

Fair value is a reporting-date market-participant exit price under the valuation standard; management budgets, asking prices and valuation certificates without methodology or current scope are insufficient.

## Fv Movement

Under the fair-value model recognize the period change in profit or loss; do not charge depreciation separately or deduct disposal transaction costs from the property fair value.

## Cost Model

FRS102 16.4A permits a group-lessor to elect either fair value or transfer qualifying property rented to another group entity to PPE/ROU with the Section17 cost model. The election applies consistently to the group-rented class; it is not available for ordinary external rental property.

## Valuation Exception

For operative 2026 FRS102 ordinary investment property, inability or undue cost of valuation does not recreate the deleted cost exception. Unsupported valuation prevents completion.

## Change Use

FRS102 16.9 requires an actual change in use and meeting or ceasing to meet the investment-property definition; a statement of intent alone is insufficient.

## Cost Transfer

Group-rented cost property is presented as PPE/ROU; Section17 accounting and disclosures apply, not a generic IAS40 cost-model investment-property balance.

## Fv Transfer Out

FRS102 16.9A uses fair value at the change-of-use date as deemed cost when investment property becomes PPE; avoid counting a previous valuation movement again.

## Fv Transfer In

FRS102 16.9B treats an owner-occupied property becoming fair-value investment property as a Section17 revaluation at the transfer date; do not put the entire difference automatically into profit or loss.

## Disposal

Derecognize on disposal or permanent withdrawal when future benefits are no longer expected. Disposal profit or loss equals net disposal proceeds less carrying value; revenue/lease sale criteria and contingent consideration require their owners.

## Leases

The September2024 Periodic Review lease changes are effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January2026. This owned-property adapter blocks ROU and finance-lessor interests pending separately owned Lease accounting.

## Disclosures

Full commercial Section16 disclosures include valuation methods and assumptions, qualified valuer involvement, restrictions, obligations and a carrying-value reconciliation. Section1A and charity/entity exceptions are outside full-framework scope.

## Fv Disclosure

Section16 reconciliation distinguishes additions including combinations, fair-value profit/loss, disposals, transfers and other movements; comparative reconciliation exemption must not be mistaken for exemption from all comparative disclosures.

## Cost Disclosure

Group-rented property elected to Section17 cost requires applicable PPE disclosure, including the closing carrying value of property rented to group entities under 17.31A. There is no general Section16 cost election.

## Owner Boundaries

PPE depreciation/revaluation, impairment, leases, borrowing costs, debt, tax, consolidation and financial statement approval remain separately owned; supplied evidence never authorizes duplicate journals.

## Period Scope

FRS102 September2024 Periodic Review amendments apply from annual periods beginning on or after 1 January2026. This runtime supports full commercial FRS102 calendar2026 without early adoption; FRS101/105, small-entity/charity overlays and other periods are excluded.

## Lineage

Each source, legal entity, property interest, currency, event date and accounting owner must reconcile to the asset rollforward and GL, with current independent certification; a balanced journal alone is insufficient.
