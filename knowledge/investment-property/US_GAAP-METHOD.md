# US_GAAP — owned investment-property accounting

2026 full-framework bounded method. Independently authored; source ratings remain claim-specific.

## Definition

Ordinary US GAAP has no universal IAS40-equivalent investment-property accounting category. Describing an owned rental property as investment property does not authorize recurring fair-value recognition.

## Owner Use

Ordinary owned rental real estate and owner-occupied real estate follow applicable historical-cost property guidance; use does not create an IAS40 fair-value election.

## Mixed Use

Maintain separately evidenced legal and component identities for mixed-use real estate. This adapter accepts a separately identified rental component at cost; it does not import the IFRS insignificant-owner-use exception as US authority.

## Ancillary Services

Rental and substantive service activities affect revenue and lease classification under their respective owners; incidental-service terminology does not enable a US fair-value property model.

## Construction

ASC835-20 may govern eligible capitalized interest in constructed real estate, with ASC360 property guidance; project/development, inventory and sale routes require their owners and are blocked here.

## Initial Recognition

An ordinary owned asset purchase follows applicable historical-cost asset acquisition guidance; business-combination scope and consolidation conclusions cannot be inferred from the property label.

## Initial Cost

Record the asset acquisition at cost including eligible directly attributable acquisition costs; business combinations, contingent/deferred price and financing need separately governed scope.

## Subsequent Policy

For ordinary held-and-used real estate this adapter supports historical cost less depreciation and impairment. IAS40 cost-or-fair-value elections and generic upward valuation gains are blocked.

## Fair Value

ASC820 explains measurement when another applicable Topic requires or permits it; an appraisal or ASC820 reference alone does not authorize recognizing fair-value gains on ordinary real estate.

## Fv Movement

No recurring fair-value profit is supported for ordinary US held-and-used rental property. Investment-company ASC946 and specialized entity/industry models require separate scope and are blocked.

## Cost Model

Depreciable building components use systematic cost allocation; land is not depreciated. ASC360 held-and-used recoverability and loss measurement remain with the impairment owner.

## Valuation Exception

US historical-cost accounting is not an IAS40 exceptional valuation fallback. ASC946, pension plan and other specialized fair-value scopes are not enabled by a reviewer assertion.

## Change Use

Changes between owner use and ordinary rental use do not alone justify US revaluation. Held-for-sale, inventories or other disposal classification changes require their existing owners.

## Cost Transfer

An internal presentation reclassification between ordinary owned rental real estate and PPE preserves historical carrying value; it does not create a distinct US investment-property category.

## Fv Transfer Out

An IAS40 fair-value deemed-cost transfer is unsupported for ordinary US real estate; separately governed industry/entity methods cannot be presumed.

## Fv Transfer In

An IFRS-style PPE-to-investment-property fair-value transfer or OCI uplift is unsupported for ordinary US real estate.

## Disposal

Derecognition uses applicable sale/transfer-of-nonfinancial-asset criteria, with net consideration and carrying basis. Installments, contingent price, sale-leaseback, held-for-sale and business disposal require specialist owners.

## Leases

ASC842 governs lessor/lessee treatment. This route supports legal ownership and an independently evidenced operating-lessor rental arrangement; ROU, finance/sales-type leases and sale-leaseback are blocked.

## Disclosures

Provide applicable property cost/depreciation policies and balances, disposals and impairment disclosures through the financial statements owner; rental revenue and lease commitments remain with their owners.

## Fv Disclosure

An independently supplied appraisal can be additional evidence but is not an authorization to record a recurring property fair-value movement under ordinary US GAAP.

## Cost Disclosure

Reconcile gross cost, accumulated depreciation/impairment and carrying amount, with methods/lives and required material movements; do not label notes as mandatory IAS40 investment-property disclosures.

## Owner Boundaries

US property/depreciation, ASC360 impairment, ASC842 leases, ASC835 borrowing costs, ASC740 taxes and consolidation retain owners. Investment-company/plan/insurance exceptions remain unsupported.

## Period Scope

The supported ordinary for-profit US GAAP full reporting scope is calendar2026 held-and-used owned real estate; specialized entity/industry guidance and alternative measurement elections are blocked.

## Lineage

Each source, legal entity, property interest, currency, event date and accounting owner must reconcile to the asset rollforward and GL, with current independent certification; a balanced journal alone is insufficient.
