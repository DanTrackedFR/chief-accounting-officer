# IFRS — owned investment-property accounting

2026 full-framework bounded method. Independently authored; source ratings remain claim-specific.

## Definition

Investment property is land, a building or an identified portion held for rentals, capital appreciation or both; classification depends on actual economic use rather than the GL name.

## Owner Use

Property used for production, services or administration is owner occupied and follows the PPE owner; substantive ancillary services can make a rental property owner occupied.

## Mixed Use

Separately saleable portions, or portions separately finance-leasable, are separated. An inseparable property qualifies only when owner use is insignificant; substantial mixed owner use fails the investment-property classification.

## Ancillary Services

Ordinary security and maintenance may be incidental; significant hotel-style services require owner-occupied classification and a documented significance judgment.

## Construction

Construction or development for future investment-property use is in investment-property scope, but construction evidence, measurement reliability and borrowing-cost eligibility remain distinct requirements.

## Initial Recognition

Recognize an owned property when economic benefits are probable and cost is reliably measurable, with independently evidenced ownership and asset identity.

## Initial Cost

Purchased property initially includes purchase price and directly attributable transaction costs. Startup, abnormal waste and losses before planned occupancy are excluded; business combinations and deferred-financing prices require their owners.

## Subsequent Policy

Choose cost or fair value consistently for the supported ordinary owned investment-property class; changing a model is an accounting-policy change, not a period-by-period gain election.

## Fair Value

Fair value is a reporting-date market-participant exit price under the valuation standard; management budgets, asking prices and valuation certificates without methodology or current scope are insufficient.

## Fv Movement

Under the fair-value model recognize the period change in profit or loss; do not charge depreciation separately or deduct disposal transaction costs from the property fair value.

## Cost Model

Cost-model carrying value is historical cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment; land and depreciable buildings are distinguished, and the impairment owner governs recoverability.

## Valuation Exception

Exceptional inability to measure fair value reliably is tightly constrained and is not a free cost-policy election within a fair-value portfolio; construction exceptions require separately approved scope.

## Change Use

Transfers require evidence of an actual change of use and satisfaction or cessation of the definition; management intention alone is insufficient.

## Cost Transfer

Under cost-model transfers between PPE and investment property preserve carrying value and accumulated depreciation/impairment; a transfer cannot create a gain.

## Fv Transfer Out

For fair-value investment property transferred to owner-occupied PPE or inventory, fair value at actual change of use becomes deemed cost; remeasurement must be recognized only once.

## Fv Transfer In

For owner-occupied property transferred to fair-value investment property, account under PPE through the change date; the difference is treated as PPE revaluation, including prior impairment reversal and OCI distinctions.

## Disposal

Derecognize on disposal or permanent withdrawal when future benefits are no longer expected. Disposal profit or loss equals net disposal proceeds less carrying value; revenue/lease sale criteria and contingent consideration require their owners.

## Leases

Owned property leased to others requires correct operating/finance-lessor classification. Lessee right-of-use assets use the lease standard for initial measurement and require the Lease owner; property fair value cannot remove a lease liability.

## Disclosures

Review model, classification judgments, valuation basis and valuer involvement, rental income and direct operating expenses, restrictions and contractual obligations, together with movement reconciliation.

## Fv Disclosure

Fair-value-model reconciliation distinguishes additions, disposals, valuation movements, transfers and other changes; exceptions and valuation-standard disclosures require specific review.

## Cost Disclosure

Cost-model notes include methods, lives/rates, gross cost, accumulated depreciation/impairment and movements, plus property fair value or exceptional inability explanations.

## Owner Boundaries

PPE depreciation/revaluation, impairment, leases, borrowing costs, debt, tax, consolidation and financial statement approval remain separately owned; supplied evidence never authorizes duplicate journals.

## Period Scope

This implementation reviews ordinary full-framework for-profit annual 2026 periods without early presentation adoption; transitions, other editions and specialist entity overlays require separate approval.

## Lineage

Each source, legal entity, property interest, currency, event date and accounting owner must reconcile to the asset rollforward and GL, with current independent certification; a balanced journal alone is insufficient.
