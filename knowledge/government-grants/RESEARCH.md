## Current US position

The historical statement that US GAAP lacks a business-entity grant recognition standard is incomplete as of the research date. FASB issued ASU2025-10 in December2025. Its new Topic832 recognition is mandatory for public business annual periods beginning after15December2028 and other business entities after15December2029. Early adoption is allowed while statements are unissued/not made available; interim adoption applies from the annual-period beginning. This release supports only evidenced adoption in the reviewed2026 reporting window, with a separately qualified transition conclusion. It does not force the new method on nonadopters or automatically select IAS20/ASC958 analogy.

Important differences independently checked: US transfer scope includes money and tangible nonmonetary assets, excludes intangible/services and government liability abatements, excludes below-market-loan benefits and guarantees, permits qualifying monetary refundable credits outside740, requires probable rather than an imported reasonable-assurance label, requires costs incurred for asset recognition, and measures nonmonetary grants differently by asset method. Proposals and historical explanations were not used as operative authority.

## Effective and entity boundaries
