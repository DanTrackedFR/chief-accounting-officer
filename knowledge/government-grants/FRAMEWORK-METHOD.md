# Government Grants accounting method

## Ownership and classification

Start with an actual agreement and legal entity/reporting window. Identify grantor, delegated administering body, resource transferred, consideration and government capacity. The contract label does not establish a grant. A procurement/customer exchange goes to Revenue; government acting as shareholder goes to Equity; an ordinary loan goes to Debt. A benefit tied to taxable profit or income-tax liability goes to Income Tax. A refundable credit paid through a tax authority needs substance analysis; payment department alone does not decide accounting.

IFRS/AASB define an asset grant principally by long-term-asset acquisition conditions. US adopted Topic832 can classify inventory acquisition subsidies as asset grants. UK accrual classifies each part as revenue or asset grant. Mixed packages need a supported allocation with distinct grant parts, not arbitrary smoothing. Unmeasurable assistance, guarantees and regulatory concessions do not create invented monetary income; supply appropriate assistance-disclosure facts and specialist handoffs.

## Recognition and conditions

Capture precedent, ongoing, milestone, eligible-cost, employment, production, geographic, asset-use, retention and clawback terms separately. Keep current award/entitlement and legal evidence, eligible expenditure records and condition compliance. For IFRS/AASB and both UK models reasonable assurance addresses receipt and compliance. The test can assess supported future compliance; it does not require falsely saying every future condition is already satisfied. Missing evidence blocks recognition. Cash is evidence of payment, never proof of future compliance.

US adopted Topic832 instead requires probable receipt/compliance plus the route-specific requirements. Asset recognition follows incurred compensated costs. A future-cost reimbursement grant cannot create a receivable merely from a probable project plan. Eligible expenses and a supported reimbursement right remain necessary. This release requires documented probable conclusions, not a percentage invented by the engine.

UK additionally requires the actual class-level performance/accrual election. Under performance model income waits for specified future performance conditions to be met; advance cash remains a liability. With no such conditions, income arises when received/receivable after assurance. Under accrual model income follows compensated cost periods. Performance is not a general expense-matching method and cannot be silently replaced with IAS20.

## Income and past costs

Related cost owner supplies expense amounts and their periods. Payroll supplies eligible wages once; Intangibles supplies whether R&D is expense or a capitalized development asset; Inventory supplies expense versus held RM/WIP/FG. The grant never decides capitalization or manufactures eligible expenditure. Match future compensated expenses systematically under IFRS/AASB, US adopted Topic832 and UK accrual. A grant100 for supported total eligible cost500, of which200 is an expense in the current period, yields current release40 under an evidenced proportional policy, not cash-based income100. Other patterns require an actual supported basis.

Past-cost/loss compensation without future costs enters the supported current receivable/recognition period; it is not spread into future periods to smooth income. UK performance follows its actual conditions. Grants exceeding eligible costs need a supportable fixed-support/past-loss explanation or remain blocked; the engine does not silently cap or invent a reimbursement rate.

## Asset grants

The asset owner supplies identity, cost, date available for use, useful life, residual value, depreciation/amortization, disposal and impairment. IFRS/AASB permit deferred income or carrying-value deduction with a consistent documented election. Deferred income follows the relevant expense proportions; carrying-value reduction appears through reduced depreciation and has no additional grant-income stream. The grant deduction is applied once and passed back to the asset owner to perform revised depreciation.

UK accrual requires deferred income for the deferred asset component. Netting off the asset is explicitly prohibited. UK performance asset grants follow performance conditions rather than automatically waiting across asset useful life. US adopted Topic832 offers deferred income or cost accumulation; costs must be incurred before recognizing the asset grant. Cost accumulation revises basis without separate later grant earnings. Inventory subsidies under this US route need Inventory to use the grant-adjusted basis exactly once and determine COGS.

Nondepreciable land is not assigned an invented depreciation schedule. Actual cost-bearing obligations can define a systematic release, such as building-related costs. Disposal, impairment, changed lives and missing cost patterns require the asset owner's current reassessment; an engine cannot reuse a stale straight-line plan after the underlying asset is sold.

## Nonmonetary grants

IFRS/AASB allow fair value or nominal amount as a supported policy. The actual fair value comes from Fair Value Measurement and the asset owner, with matched valuation date and asset identity. Nominal is an actual documented election/amount, not an invented substitute for missing valuation. UK requires fair value and has no IAS20 nominal alternative. US tangible nonmonetary grants use fair value under deferred income, but cost to the entity under cost accumulation; intangible transfers and free services are outside its new grant scope. US fair-value disclosure still applies under cost accumulation. A free asset with zero cost does not remove the requirement to obtain the required disclosure valuation.

## Government loans

Ordinary debt recognition and interest remain with Financial Instruments/Debt. IFRS/AASB below-market proceeds100 and supplied initial liability80 create a separate grant benefit20, with the benefit recognition pattern determined by the agreement. The liability is not recreated by Grants; future interest accretion is Debt's job. US Topic832 expressly excludes below-market loan benefits: do not import the IFRS20 calculation. UK concessional loans require an independently governed financial-instrument/policy route; this release does not invent a Section24 below-market benefit method.

For IFRS/AASB forgivable loans, reasonable assurance supports satisfying forgiveness terms; US adopted Topic832 uses probable plus full grant-route requirements. This can assess evidenced future compliance, but management optimism without terms/evidence is insufficient. Do not credit all loan cash to current income merely because forgiveness is contemplated. A preexisting debt requires exact debt-owner derecognition/output and grant component identity. Unsupported legal debt-release and UK forgiveness routes remain blocked pending qualified owner conclusions.

## Repayments

Use actual repayment terms, triggering evidence, amount and obligation assessment. Distinguish a new clawback event from an earlier error. IFRS/AASB treat repayability as an estimate-change event, without automatically restating prior years. Income repayment consumes the remaining deferral and charges excess to current P&L. Deferred asset repayment similarly consumes remaining grant balance, with released amounts requiring appropriate current-period expense rather than a negative deferral plug.

For an asset basis deduction repayment, restore the basis by the amount payable and consume the asset owner's calculated cumulative additional depreciation in current income. The owner also assesses restored-basis impairment. US cost accumulation has equivalent route-specific catch-up including prior disposal effects; do not substitute a historical grant-income reversal. Example: original deduction100, remaining related asset benefit80 and repayable100 requires basis restoration100, additional accumulated depreciation20 and liability100. Grants journals do not independently depreciate the whole asset.

UK Section24 expressly recognizes the liability once its definition is met. Its repayment details depend on actual model and circumstances; this release supports documented simple unamortized deferred liability repayment and current-income clawback only where the qualified conclusion supports them. Complex policy interpretation, prior-error correction, disposal and disputed clawbacks remain explicit owner-review boundaries.

## Agriculture

Agriculture owner determines whether the biological asset is fair-value-less-costs-to-sell or the permitted cost model. IAS41/AASB141 special-grant scope is not ordinary IAS20/AASB120: unconditional FVLCTS grants become income when receivable and conditional ones when their conditions are met, including retention terms. Cost-model biological asset grants fall back to ordinary grant accounting. A government grant associated with an agricultural entity's ordinary equipment is not automatically a biological-asset grant. Consume Agriculture conclusions; never remeasure the biological asset or duplicate its grant gain.

## Reconciliation and journals

Bind agreement, payment, receivable, qualifying cost, asset, recognition, deferral and repayment economic identities. Reject alias duplication, duplicate cash loads, repeated payroll/R&D claims, grant/revenue double recognition and grant/tax-credit double recognition. One grant part uses one asset/deferred policy; both policies cannot post the same benefit.

Receivable/deferred initial recognition may debit receivable and credit deferred grant. Cash subsequently debits cash and credits receivable. Periodic income release debits deferred grant and credits grant income or the permitted expense offset. Immediate receivable income debits receivable and credits the appropriate grant-income line. Basis-deduction journals adjust the asset/grant contra-basis once. A loan handoff pack shows proceeds, existing supplied liability and separated supported benefit, without reissuing Debt entries. Repayment debits unamortized deferral and supported current expense/basis effects and credits payable/cash. All draft journals balance and are never posted to ERP.

Opening deferred grant + recognized additions − current release − repayments ± identified corrections = closing deferred grant. Receivable has its own recognition/cash/adjustment rollforward. Reconcile source population, schedule, draft journal, GL and exact Financial Statements lines; no unexplained plugs. Current/noncurrent classification requires the evidenced realization/release schedule. Cash/noncash data and supported transaction classification go to Cash Flow; final statement construction remains there.

## Disclosure and operations

IFRS/AASB need policy/presentation, nature/extent, other assistance and unfulfilled conditions/contingencies. UK needs the elected model and Section24 grant/assistance information. US adopted Topic832 adds route-specific statement effects, nonmonetary fair value even under cost accumulation, related asset life and significant duration/commitment/recapture terms. Disclosure owner constructs and reviews the final disclosures; this skill supplies exact conclusions and reconciled values.

Systems/Data supplies controlled populations and Controls supplies actual approvals. No fabricated compliance/legal/control certification is allowed. Accounting Changes owns policy changes and earlier errors. Keep unsupported entity, period, program, valuation, transition or repayment scope explicit in ordinary public limitations. Internal source notes and approval metadata are excluded by allowlisted retrieval and tested public boundary.
