# Artifact Architecture

Artifacts are first-class accounting outputs and evidence, not decorative attachments.

## Artifact classes

- accounting memo / position paper;
- accounting policy;
- judgment or estimate paper;
- calculation/workpaper;
- reconciliation;
- journal-entry support;
- process narrative;
- SOP;
- control design / RCM;
- close checklist;
- audit support paper / PBC response;
- disclosure checklist;
- financial-reporting schedule;
- regulatory filing support;
- executive or Audit Committee summary;
- system/accounting requirements document;
- decision record.

## Artifact metadata

Every governed artifact SHOULD support identity, type, status, version, owner, preparer, reviewer, approval, entities, periods, framework, effective date, source case, related decisions, source evidence, and supersession.

Statuses SHOULD distinguish at least DRAFT, REVIEWED, APPROVED, SUPERSEDED, and ARCHIVED.

## Lineage

A material conclusion SHOULD be traceable from source evidence → case analysis → decision/judgment → artifact → accounting/reporting/control consequence. Artifacts MUST NOT silently lose their source lineage when updated.
