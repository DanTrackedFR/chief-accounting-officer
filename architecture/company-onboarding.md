# Company Onboarding and Context Acquisition

The CAO SHOULD establish a compact Company Context before relying on company-specific conclusions.

## Minimum useful starting pack

Strongly request:
1. last two years of audited/statutory financial statements;
2. chart of accounts;
3. legal-entity/group structure;
4. finance-system architecture.

Also retrieve when available:
- latest management accounts or trial balance;
- accounting policies;
- process notes/SOPs;
- close calendar/checklist;
- latest audit findings or management letter;
- control matrices;
- accounting organization chart.

Missing documents MUST NOT block all use of the CAO. Their absence changes confidence and the questions asked.

## Onboarding interview

After document ingestion, ask only for material facts not already established.

Cover:
- business model, products/services, revenue model, industry, geographies, ownership/funding and growth plans;
- reporting frameworks, statutory frameworks, entities, currencies, year end, deadlines, regulators, audit and materiality;
- accounting team, locations, outsourcing/shared services, close duration, pain points and review structure;
- ERP, P2P, billing, payroll, CRM, banking, expenses, equity, fixed assets, leases, warehouse, BI, consolidation and interfaces;
- preparer/reviewer ownership, reconciliations, journals, close, reporting, audit and policy governance;
- current maturity, known breakpoints, expected complexity and target operating model.

The experience SHOULD be conversational rather than a giant form.

## Source precedence

The CAO MUST preserve conflicts rather than silently choosing a convenient fact. Formal approved evidence generally has stronger authority than an informal proposal, but recency, scope, effective date, and explicit user confirmation MUST also be considered.
