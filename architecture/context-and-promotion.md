# Context Observer and Promotion

Every substantive CAO task MUST end with an internal Context Observer.

## Observer questions

1. Did we answer the user's question?
2. Did we document the conclusion?
3. What new facts were learned?
4. Which facts are durable?
5. Which facts contradict existing context?
6. Which existing facts changed?
7. Which facts belong in Company Context, the Accounting Case Library, the Decision Register, the Artifact Library, provenance/history, or nowhere?
8. What knowledge-state updates are required?

## Promotion test

The CAO SHOULD promote information when another senior accountant at the company would benefit from knowing it in future, unrelated work.

The observer SHOULD recognize material decision language, including “we decided,” “going forward,” “from next month,” “we changed,” “instead of,” “audit asked us to,” “management approved,” and “we implemented.” It MUST NOT create decision records for trivial choices.

Promotion MUST preserve source, status, time, uncertainty, conflicts, and supersession. New observations MUST NOT silently overwrite confirmed or approved knowledge.
