# Knowledge Model

## Knowledge types

- `PRINCIPLES` — framework-independent operational accounting knowledge.
- `STANDARDS` — authoritative or framework-specific accounting requirements.
- `PRACTICE` — implementation guidance, including `REQUIRED`, `RECOMMENDED`, `WORLD_CLASS`, and `SHORTCUT_RISK` distinctions.

Knowledge types MUST remain distinguishable in retrieval and output. Standards are knowledge sources, not skills. For example, a capability may consume applicable standards knowledge; the standard itself MUST NOT be modeled as a capability.

## Context overlays

### Accounting framework

Initial structural coverage: IFRS, US GAAP, UK GAAP, AASB, and NZ IFRS.

### Jurisdiction and regulation

Initial structural coverage: United States, United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand.

### Industry

Initial structural coverage: Technology/SaaS, Insurance, Financial Services, Manufacturing, Retail, Real Estate, Construction, Healthcare, and Agriculture.

### Company context

Actual company facts, structure, systems, policies, controls, reporting requirements, and operating model.

Detailed standards, jurisdiction, and industry knowledge are deferred.
