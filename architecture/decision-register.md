# Decision Register

The Decision Register preserves the rationale for material accounting, process, system, and control decisions.

It SHOULD support questions such as:

- Why do we do this?
- When did we start?
- What did we do before?
- Who approved it?
- What accounting issue drove it?
- Which policy, process, system, or control changed?
- What breaks if we stop?
- Was it required by accounting guidance or chosen by management?

## Rationale categories

- Accounting Standard
- Accounting Policy
- Accounting Judgment
- Control
- Process Design
- Management Choice
- Legacy / No Documented Rationale

Records MUST distinguish effective date, decision date, and learned date where applicable. Changes MUST preserve prior state and provenance. The implementation schema is deferred.
