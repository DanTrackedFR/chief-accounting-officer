# Management Reporting & Accounting Analytics — bounded methods

Version 1.1.0 extends the existing accounting analytics contract with bounded diagnostics; it preserves accounting authority and all source/release/case approval gates. Supplied comparators are allowed. Autonomous FP&A and accounting treatment remain excluded.

## Governed source and execution

Read every actual document and claim register in ../GOVERNANCE-KNOWLEDGE-MAP.json. The map freezes live main5a011a7 and selected actual capability IDs before implementation. Existing canonical historical labels yield to the APPROVED manifest and individual Phase2E reviews. Empty registers provide approved practice; OTHER audit claims retain their auditor-side meaning and are not automatically applied as universal company obligations. No evidence rating, audit flag, limitation or paragraph is upgraded.

Use the existing production envelope, case certification and public adapter. Require current independently reviewed entity/framework/jurisdiction/period scope, actual reporting editions/adoptions and externally qualified local requirements. Unsupported entity/tier/industry overlays fail closed. The software validates supplied review records; it does not authenticate people or verify a stored URL against actual law.

## Population and version discipline

An independently frozen source population must reconcile IDs and immutable semantic fields to the tracker; gross and signed amounts, source identities and periods cannot be replaced by equal totals. Source documents retain system/query/parameters/snapshot, period and currency. Content digests bind actual supplied source bytes. Every current version has separate preparer/reviewer approval. Release review additionally hashes the source/artifact/dependency payload; fresh outer approval cannot hide a late change. Supplied data and approvals remain independently examined case evidence, not manufactured source verification.

## Ownership and output

Import only actual complete, current, unaltered owner cases/results with exact entity/framework/jurisdiction/period. Each semantic numeric assertion resolves to the actual completed owner result; duplicate conclusions and reposting block. Unresolved material evidence or accounting issues remain partial/blocked. Only numeric/count workpapers, generated guidance and material limitations cross all seven public routes. Raw inputs, memos, reviewers, internal claims and digests remain private. All candidate synthetic approvals/examples are explicitly tests, never real sign-off. No journals or external actions are created.

## Individual supported method

The roadmap title is explicitly mapped to management/statutory bridge, close flux, accounting KPI and reporting-layer lineage capabilities, not an FP&A title in the canonical map. Current and prior-year calendar account-balance populations retain posted state, currency and signed convention; every source ledger record/count/gross total and report row reconciles. Nonzero movements require a source-backed factual driver decomposition; interpretations are retained as qualified private memos. Nonzero bridge adjustments must bind exact numeric completed-owner results, signed and used once. Only on-time reconciliation KPI is executable, using actual independently complete due/completion event population; material incomplete reconciliations prevent clean quality completion. No invented threshold, budget/forecast generation, unexplained plug, generic dashboard or invented business explanation is supported.


## Diagnostic methods and comparator contract (1.1.0)

`diagnostics.py` is practice mathematics, mapped to the same approved practice topics; no new accounting-standard namespace. Every `diagnostic` field is in the native release-review payload and the module bytes enter implementation fingerprints. `None` retains the original flux/KPI behavior.

Current actuals reconcile posted records to signed completed native owner components. For ordinary factory gross profit the Revenue period metric is positive, Inventory COGS negative, and unallocated manufacturing expense negative only under the approved inclusive presentation basis. A supplied comparator has exact kind/version/entity/unit/currency/target period, explicit supplied/approved state and ISO approval/freeze dates. Actual historical comparators precede current scope; budget/forecast/standard/target comparators target the current period. Changing either edition or accounting presentation blocks comparability. The software checks reviewed evidence; it does not independently authenticate management's budget approval or assume forecast accuracy.

Driver group populations reconcile internally and attach exact owner component ties. Different groups must use disjoint original economic components; owner-flux metrics and component-tie metrics additionally prohibit semantic alias duplication. Unexplained or omitted components create a visible residual. Identified subcomponents are source-supported, not recalculated recognition/valuation. FX inside material prices cannot be also a separate FX contribution unless the controlled component identities are disjoint. Unsold imported stock in the flagship has no cost-of-sales FX contribution.

For rate/quantity: quantity=(q1-q0)*p0; rate=q1*(p1-p0). Quantity runs first; the cross term belongs entirely to rate. Sign comes from the actual signed metric component. For PVM: total volume=(Q1-Q0)*sum(q0*p0)/Q0; mix=sum((q1-Q1*q0/Q0)*p0); price=sum(q1*(p1-p0)). This is one explicit convention, not a universal business formula. Baseline quantity must be positive; quantities/prices are supplied nonnegative values in matching units and complete product populations. Rates and quantities must reproduce the source amounts. Alternative conventions need separately reviewed methods.

For flux: contribution=sign*(current-baseline). Every bridge satisfies starting+drivers+residual=ending. Residual is actual change less explained change, never allocated back to drivers or hidden as timing/other. Absolute tolerance is deliberately supplied in [0,.01] units; materiality must be supplied or remain unknown. An immaterial residual stays visible; unknown/material unexplained residual makes the work partial. Component baseline differences are themselves residual, not silently changed to fit the bridge.

Margin percentage points use GP driver/current revenue plus a separate denominator effect baseline_GP*(1/current_revenue-1/baseline_revenue)*100. Residual points=residual/current revenue*100. Prior revenue is bound to the frozen comparator; current revenue to Revenue's actual output. Both denominators must be positive. This reconciles the percentage movement without confusing percentage points and percent change.

Hypotheses retain observation, hypothesis, controlled tests, evidence classes, confidence and calculated disposition. All tests true yields SUPPORTED; some PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED; all false REJECTED; no tests or association/management/model evidence UNRESOLVED. Direct evidence status must exist in the tested source, not only the hypothesis proposal. Supported means the supplied evidence tests hold within scope, not proof of unobserved causality. Bridge attribution is mathematical attribution, not an invented operational story. Threshold signals create observations/questions; unusual journal counts do not assert accounting errors.

Accounting inquiries bind an actual numeric owner metric and source group, with issue/reason/amount/dimensions/materiality/evidence. They cannot contain treatment, journal or capitalization instructions. Runtime adds bounded follow-up nodes and reexecutes the existing production owner; actual original owner result and certification must remain unchanged and current. It records the owner conclusion internally and publishes a curated check status. Material unresolved diagnostics prevent a clean follow-up/Case close. No entries are reposted. Future intake adapters feed these structured source contracts; model-proposed intent/hypotheses never bypass them.
