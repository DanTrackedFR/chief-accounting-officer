# Accounting Estimates, Policy Changes & Error Corrections: executable methods and boundaries

Classify by evidence chronology, not management label. A prior error uses information reasonably available at original authorization; an estimate uses genuinely new information afterwards and prospective recognition. Voluntary permitted/preferable policy changes are retrospective; mandatory changes use the specific standard transition first, including supported modified-retrospective/prospective transitions. US depreciation-method change effected by a principle uses the reviewed ASC250 prospective hybrid route. IAS8/FRS102 Section10/AASB108 impracticability requires technical evidence and earliest practicable date; do not import this relief into US prior-error correction.

Inventory original issued annual/interim TBs and adjusted TBs separately. Journals must balance, accounts must exist in the full original population, and corrected lines sum to zero. Comparative-year error originates in that year's expense/revenue; only effects before earliest presented period enter opening equity. Carry forward corrected closing stocks; do not sum the same cumulative error across every annual/interim restatement. Current opening-equity bridge uses latest corrected prior close. Layer and date must match prospective/current versus comparative/earliest-opening/specific transition. Basic EPS uses supplied supported weighted shares; diluted EPS and tax effects require reviewed Tax and EPS sources, including a current completed Income Taxes workpaper where supported; no supplemental tax knowledge is re-approved here.

Materiality requires qualitative factors, aggregation, annual and interim evaluation. SEC domestic US-GAAP errors require separately selected verified SEC claims and counsel/auditor/governance evidence. Prior material = Big R reissuance; previously reliable but current correction or uncorrected effect material = little r revision; both current effects immaterial = reviewed out-of-period correction. Calculate net rollover, net iron-curtain ending balance and gross errors without automatic percentage thresholds. Audit committee/counsel decide non-reliance, Item4.02 timing/filing, preferability, ICFR and clawbacks; engine produces routing, never files or makes a legal decision. Foreign-private issuer filing routes block. Interim consequences require separately approved interim memo/population and cannot be inferred from annual immateriality.

Complete synthetic example: prior cash100/revenue-100 originally profit100; omitted expense20/payable20 corrected by Dr expense20 Cr payable20. Corrected profit80, EPS10→8 with shares10, next opening equity100→80. For a carried correction in another prior close, use its cumulative-20 once, not -40. No invented tax rate: Tax and EPS specialist explicitly reviews nil/actual adjustments. Partial example: missing original information evidence, provisional normative reference presented as verified or unresolved counsel route blocks; absent exact-case certification is partial. SEC-specific references are selected only for actual registrant cases.

Inputs and identifiers are illustrated in `examples/*.case.json`; these are wholly synthetic and contain no real approvals. Corresponding partial and complete public outputs demonstrate both certification states. Regression reissues synthetic certification against the current exact implementation; archived cases intentionally remain unsigned. Every successful route still requires all contract controls and specialist handoffs. Unsupported optional accounting routes cannot obtain a clean result merely by excluding a source item.
