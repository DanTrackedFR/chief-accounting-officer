## Manufacturing

RM net issues are selected by actual governed formula and original issues support returns only up to remaining quantity at original cost. BOM versions, substitutions and independently controlled maximum good output and net component usage constrain actual yield. Routing hours and driver units tie to actual original labour/OH sources. Production output/closing homogeneous equivalent units are supplied, not estimated. Normal capacity uses actual reviewed multi-period evidence. Fixed absorption is actual fixed cost × actual production driver / max(normal capacity,actual driver); the unallocated portion is period expense. Variable OH follows actual controlled facility usage. Normal scrap/rework is supported as actual eligible cost and reviewed source yield/loss; abnormal source costs are expensed.

Actual eligible order cost = opening WIP + net actual material + actual eligible labour + variable OH + absorbed fixed OH. Homogeneous completion share determines actual WIP and FG cost; completed units and closing equivalents cannot exceed controlled source yield. Jobs, batches, orders and homogeneous process collection share this method. No WIP or quantity plugs are allowed. Completion occurs exactly once before sales relief.

Standard costing consumes actual effective standards from BOM/routing and regular approximation review. Material price = actual material cost − actual quantity × standard price. Usage = (actual quantity − allowed quantity) × standard price. Labour rate/efficiency and variable spending/efficiency use actual hours/driver against supplied allowed/rate. Fixed spending = actual fixed pool − budgeted pool; fixed volume = budgeted pool − standard absorbed fixed. Gross signed components reconcile to actual total manufacturing cost less standard. Separate journal bridge books the standard basis, gross variance accounts and component-level disposition of normal actual approximation to WIP versus idle expense; favorable amounts remain signed. Completion subsequently allocates eligible normal actual cost to FG/WIP and sales relieve the resulting FG cost. Standard recovery can be negative; it is not capitalized automatically. The company may report those underlying components individually or in controlled groups; every measured component belongs to exactly one supplied group, gross favorable/adverse exposure is retained and idle/abnormal cause is separately allocated. No generic materiality threshold or standards are generated.

## Measurement and reporting

Purchased cost includes qualifying net price/duty/freight/handling/insurance/nonrecoverable tax from actual components; recoverable tax/selling/unrelated admin/abnormal cost are rejected. FIFO takes earliest eligible supplied layers; moving WA recalculates after receipts; specific ID requires noninterchangeability and specified layers. Separately controlled resale purchase standard-price variances book the supplied standard cost then gross PPV and eligible normal inventory adjustment; manufacturing acquisition-PPV versus consumed-price allocation remains fail-closed to avoid duplication. US unit LIFO takes latest layers and uses LCM replacement cost constrained by NRV ceiling and NRV-minus-normal-profit floor. Other frameworks reject LIFO.

Every item requires a current independently supported measurement-indicator/ageing/expiry/demand review. Indicator items need original carrying cost, previously recognized write-down, original cost ceiling and actual selling/completion/selling-cost evidence. Controlled recovery is capped at original write-down for IFRS/FRS102/AASB; US basis never increases on recovery. Raw-material indicator routes require current linked actual FG unit cost and controlled net-sales evidence. IFRS/AASB preserve cost when those FG remain recoverable; a stand-alone RM price decline cannot create a write-down. For nonrecoverable FG, replacement cost requires supplied appropriateness review. US/UK use their actual reviewed cost-formula LCNRV/LCM route and never receive the IAS2 exception automatically. Future-production raw-material contexts without an actual FG cost basis remain separately gated. Item grouping is the bounded route. Forecast use requires current independent review; no demand estimate is manufactured. Expired items require supported salvage for nonzero value.

Subledger opening/closing RM/WIP/FG/merchandise sums tie to GL and supplied statement amounts; every journal offset ties to its GL movement. Inventory relief ties to COGS, excluding non-sale disposals/abnormal/idle expense. Framework disclosure source requirements differ: FRS102 does not universally require inventory-expense disclosure; US does not receive IAS2 reversal requirements. Reconciled outputs identify FS, Revenue/COGS, Disclosure, Systems/Data and Controls owner handoffs; they are accounting workpaper data, not a full orchestration engine.
