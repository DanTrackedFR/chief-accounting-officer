# Intangible Assets & Amortization — bounded governed methods

## Executed method

Recognition/control/identifiability, rights and directly attributable purchase eligibility are independently evidenced. Cost model only; no appraisal creates active-market revaluation authority. Purchased intangible costs and qualifying IFRS/AASB development costs use separate populations. For development, all six evidence-backed conditions (feasibility, intention, ability, benefits, resources and reliable cost) must exist at the dated gate. Research/training/maintenance and pre-gate expenditure are expenses, never reinstated. Eligible development costs stop at availability. UKFRS102 uses its independently elected capitalize/expense model, not mandatory IAS38 capitalization. US internal-use/external-sale/hosted software/R&D remain distinct specialist methods, including ASU2025-06 actual adoption; this bounded workflow cannot generalize them.

Finite-life straight line: (cost − opening accumulated − supported residual)/supported remaining years × actual available service year fraction. The explicit actual/actual policy derives each calendar-year day interval independently; a quarter is not a full year. Unsupported consumption methods/month conventions require a distinct approved method. Availability before reporting cutoff is mandatory for expense; a mixed new/existing component must be split. FRS102 life is finite; if unreliable, it cannot exceed ten years. IFRS/AASB/US indefinite life has no amortization but needs actual annual/triggered impairment and life reassessment. Complex estimate changes route to Accounting Changes and Impairment; no retroactive life rewrite is inferred.

Purchased control/recognition date must reconcile to the acquisition cost date and precede availability; opening stock cannot first be recognized later. New additions on an existing asset require a separate dated component rather than amortizing late purchases from an old ready date. Amortization is capped at remaining depreciable carrying value, preserving the residual floor even when remaining life ends during the period.

Gross/accumulated source opening and closing tie independently to GL and every expense/cash/disposal offset. Full disposal has an explicit evidenced in-period date and amortization stops there; it removes cost and accumulated amortization and reports proceeds minus net book as gain/loss. Partial disposals, impairment allocations, PPA and complex new-component changes require originating specialist engines. Complete current acquisition/impairment/accounting-change results are evidence-only, not reposted as local cost.
