## Final harvest-unit challenge — AGR-IQA-07

A biological animal immediately before harvest and the resulting carcass at harvest are distinct measurement units. The operative harvested-produce requirement measures produce at its own harvest-date FV less costs to sell; it does not universally equate that value to the living asset's immediately preceding value. Existing execution validated only the biological asset valuation and transferred that number into inventory. A fresh source explicitly supplies biological net70 and independently qualified produce net65; original execution ignored the produce valuation and returned COMPLETE with inventory70. This is a material false COMPLETE, also relevant to crop grades/yields.

Required correction: require a separate qualified produce valuation tied to actual produce quantity, unit, identity/category, harvest date, entity, framework and currency. Reject a contradictory/missing produce measurement. A bounded equal-value route may use independently established equality; unequal harvest conversion requires a governed P&L/biological/produce bridge and GL, or must fail closed. No downstream inventory measurement is authorized. Regressions: `test_iqa07_harvest_requires_distinct_produce_measurement`, `test_iqa07_actual_produce_value_cannot_be_ignored`.

AGR-IQA-07 remediation independently verified: separate produce measurement is mandatory and binds produce identity/category, actual harvest quantity/unit, original biological asset, harvest point/date and qualified valuation/cost evidence.
