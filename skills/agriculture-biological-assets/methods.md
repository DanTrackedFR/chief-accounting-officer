# Bounded Agriculture execution

Supported candidate: 2026 for-profit IFRS, AASB Tier 1, and FRS 102 elected fair value less costs to sell accounting for individually identified biological units. Agricultural activity, control, classification, population and quantities require independent current source snapshots. Mature/immature, consumable/bearer and growing-produce classifications are source judgments. Money uses one currency and quantities one biological unit. No yields or prices are inferred.

An opening unit may remain living, be entirely harvested or die with zero salvage. A purchased or born unit uses qualified dated initial FV less costs to sell. Surviving units use qualified reporting-date FV less costs to sell. Harvested units use a harvest-date qualified measurement and transfer that amount into an inventory entry clearing account, then leave the biological population. Partial harvest and continuing bearer-asset extraction need separately governed methods. A bearer plant under IFRS/AASB belongs to Fixed Assets; separately identified growing produce may use Agriculture. FRS 102 does not inherit that carve-out. US ASC 905 routes are asset-specific; no IAS 41 model is copied to US GAAP.

Qualified independent reports supply fair value, applicable costs to sell, net amount, date, quantity, identity and category. Agriculture checks that arithmetic and accounting bridge; it does not choose valuation methodology, hierarchy, transport, market assumptions or models. The existing Fair Value Measurement production adapter is equity-specific and cannot be relabelled as biological-asset authority. Actual supplied owner results are reexecuted and blocked if a valid Agriculture binding is unavailable. External qualified Agriculture valuation reports are the supported evidence alternative.

Every original register and movement agrees with its separately inventoried snapshot. Physical opening and closing populations reconcile by identity and quantity. A terminal movement removes the entire unit exactly once; terminal units cannot remain closing. Separate original movement source identities catch aliases. All balanced journals reconcile each offset to the GL and statement source. Initial recognition differences and subsequent remeasurement affect profit or loss, with mortality separately identified. The bridge is opening + purchase consideration + measurement gain − harvest entry − mortality = closing. Birth recognition enters measurement gain. Price/physical split is excluded unless a separate qualified method is implemented; the total remains reconciled.

Births additionally require independently dated physical-event source evidence. Selling costs use reconciled eligible component populations and explicit exclusion declarations for financing, income tax and duplicate transport. Output currency and quantities use curated unit codes. Each biological class has a source-supported model consistent with the documented policy. An independent operative-edition source binds the actual framework, period, reporting basis and adoption status; superseded editions block. Disclosure support binds each current requirement to separate source evidence, and reconciles the quantitative bridge, gain, harvest entry, quantities, class population, policy and valuation-report inventory.

Disclosure support must use actual current framework-specific requirement populations and amount proofs. General financial statements and final note authoring belong to their existing owners. Reporting completion means a bounded reviewed workpaper, not statutory compliance certification. Missing exact-case independent certification returns PARTIAL; contradictory or unsupported facts return BLOCKED.

Explicitly excluded: postharvest costing/NRV and inventory reserves (#24 unavailable); derivatives (#29); insurance (#39); agricultural grant recognition; borrowing costs; investment property; land/intangible rights/PPE accounting; US agricultural monetary execution; UK cost model; FV reliability exception and transition; Tier 2/NFP overlays; pre/post-2026 editions; multi-currency; changes in unit quantities without separately identified birth/purchase/death/harvest records; sales/compensated deaths; partial harvest; autonomous legal and valuation conclusions. Route these to the named existing or specialist owner. Classification outside Agriculture is a fail-closed handoff, never a substitute journal.

Public schedules contain generated monetary/quantity amounts, fixed captions and material limitations. Raw source documents, arbitrary asset identifiers, memos, review identities, provenance, fingerprints and internal claim fields never enter public routes. Synthetic examples do not authenticate real people or fabricate real source evidence.
