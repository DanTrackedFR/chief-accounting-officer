## Exact supported and excluded execution

Supported: controlled, individually identified biological units in a single currency and compatible quantity unit, for 2026 for-profit full IFRS, AASB Tier 1 and full FRS 102 class-elected FV less costs to sell. Opening biological assets, purchases and independently witnessed births can remain living, be wholly harvested or die with zero salvage. Qualified dated external valuation reports supply initial and reporting-date values plus separate preharvest biological and harvest-point produce values, with reconciled incremental selling costs. Outputs include classification support, asset/quantity/monetary schedules, positive or negative P&L gains, mortality losses, balanced journals, GL/statement ties, disclosure support and harvest inventory-entry amounts. IFRS/AASB bearer animals and separately identified growing produce remain agricultural; qualifying bearer plants route PPE. UK class policy retains its distinct preharvest/bearer treatment. A classification-only case performs no monetary recognition.

Excluded and fail-closed: all postharvest costing/NRV/reserves/absorption; US monetary execution (ASC 905 inventory/PPE industry routes); UK unequal biological-to-produce conversion, UK cost classes and FV reliability exceptions; exception transition; partial harvest/continuing parent yields; sales and compensated deaths; land/PPE/intangible accounting; autonomous valuation or legal conclusions; grants; FX/multicurrency; entity/tier/adoption periods outside the bounded scope; generic financial statements; unsupported price/physical decomposition; unbound owner-result imports. Existing Fair Value Measurement has an equity-specific integration adapter, so it cannot be relabelled as Agriculture authority; qualified Agriculture reports are the supported alternative. Supplied actual owner imports reexecute and block if no exact Agriculture binding exists. Final general reporting belongs to Financial Statements and Disclosure Management. No excluded package was repaired or weakened.
