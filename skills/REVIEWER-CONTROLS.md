# Four accounting workflows — reviewer controls

Use `production.assess_case(package, case)` as the CAO-facing governed entrypoint. `execute` is its internal certification engine and raises `ReviewRequired` for unresolved inputs. Legacy `engine.run` functions are internal bounded arithmetic prototypes and cannot certify a production case. `run_skill.py` renders the result through the same public allowlist used by all seven registered output routes.

Before execution, prepare the complete source population, contract/legal evidence, policy election, reporting-period start/end, entity type, jurisdiction, standards edition and applicable amendments. Do not represent a management assertion as external evidence. Each workflow deliberately distinguishes arithmetic from accounting judgments; a calculator cannot establish legal rights, a credit forecast or control of another entity.

Call `canonical_knowledge` to retrieve the approved claim population and the hash manifest of canonical documents, including the claim registers. Read the actual documents identified in that manifest; IDs alone are not a review. Record the reviewer, reviewed claim IDs and exact document hashes in `knowledge_review`. Identify the actual applied claim subset, selection memo and curated accounting/period/authority caveats; unrelated claims are not emitted as citations. Resolve operative period and entity exceptions in `applicability_review`; historical and early-adoption branches require their own assessment. A full population review does not mean that every claim applies to every transaction. Select and document the material rules actually used in the accounting judgment memo.

The independent reviewer must reperform the numerical schedule, challenge the adverse route, inspect supporting evidence and reconcile journal effects to the ledger and quantitative disclosures. `reviewer_signoff.case_fingerprint` must match `case_fingerprint(case)`. Certification changes when case inputs, claim-register bytes, canonical knowledge documents or implementation bytes change. The reviewer must differ from the preparer. This mechanism validates an external approval record; it does not authenticate a human identity or manufacture approval.

Every entry must have valid Dr/Cr sides, nonnegative amounts and exactly balanced cent-rounded totals. Every source TB must balance; an aggregate balanced TB does not excuse a failed equity, NCI, cash-flow or CTA bridge. Unknown/missing facts, unresolved specialized features, invalid model scope and unbalanced entries raise `ReviewRequired`. Missing/stale independent signoff returns `partial`.

Do not post these entries to an ERP. Map clearing/illustrative account labels to approved entity-specific accounts, attach source lineage and obtain posting approval through the company close controls. Route tax, legal and valuation opinions to specialists. Standards evidence ratings and the future direct-source assurance queue remain unchanged.

Public output contains generated conclusion, numeric workpapers, balanced journals, disclosure review requirements and curated caveats. Internal evidence objects, document hashes, reviewer records and source notes remain internal. Contaminated public content fails closed. Never expose raw `execute` output in a public API or export.

## Additional accounting workflows

The six additional package contracts use the same entrypoint and certification controls. Independently check acquisition-date versus opening-PPA scope, cost/NCI model and tax/valuation exceptions; impairment test sequencing, ASC360/350 scope, allocation floors and reversal caps; currency opening balances, functional/presentation layers and accumulated CTA; award opening cost, current-period service attribution, condition/forfeiture eligibility and event chronology; and statement/comparative/equity/cash/disclosure population ties including IFRS18 performance categories and MDP reconciliation. An approved-looking boolean is only a recorded judgment: inspect its actual source memo and challenge alternatives.

Income taxes remains a fail-closed review package until a canonical or separately governed tax knowledge extension receives independent standards approval. Do not count this blocked route as a production tax method. Existing archived cases require fresh independent certification when implementation or knowledge changes; never automatically copy old real approvals to a new fingerprint. Synthetic fixtures may regenerate clearly labeled test certifications for regressions only.

## Operational accounting workflows

The six operational packages use the same retrieval, envelope, fingerprints and public adapter. Empty operational registers provide approved practice, not invented normative authority. IFRS18-only close and auditor-only journal claims remain reviewed document evidence and are not automatically cited as recognition guidance. Operational completion requires independently controlled source counts, absolute amounts, period-end snapshots, complete inventories and supported current-version approvals. Reporting cutoff and execution date are separate; normal post-period close approval must never be backdated. Validate actual task/calendar/lock chronology, gross reconciliation exposure, every correcting journal offset, invoiced rights versus cash, unbilled receipt evidence, asset commissioning and component/estimate histories, and bilateral source/rate/settlement bridges. External specialist memos are reviewed evidence; supplied downstream skill results must be complete and match entity/framework/period. No scalar resolved flag substitutes for an evidence memo. Public workpapers exclude raw specialist memos, ownership/approval records and claim metadata.
