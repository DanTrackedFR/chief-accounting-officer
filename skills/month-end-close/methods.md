# Month-End Close & Journal Governance — governed method

Dependency critical path, due-day checks, complete/rejected populations, immutable approvals, recurring rule version and exact-once source keys; economic cut-off and net accrual after already posted invoices; independent account certification before lock and authorized reopen/reclose.

Calendar durations are evidenced business-day inputs, with local timezone and calendar maintained by the close owner. No holiday calendar is inferred. Journals are already approved and posted records for review; this workflow cannot submit to an ERP.

Two chains of four business days followed by consolidation and reporting yield day six. Services received 75,000 less posted 50,000 produce accrual 25,000, linked once to debit expense / credit accrued liabilities.

Transaction recognition, prior-period correction, materiality, subsequent-event assessment and presentation transition go to the relevant technical accounting or Financial Statements skill. Operational PCAOB auditor guidance is not a universal company obligation.

Read the exact case schema in workflow.py and synthetic examples. Decimal strings only; positive rates/lives, nonnegative amounts and balanced cent-rounded journals. Source and GL gross populations must agree independently of net totals. Use case fingerprint certification only after source, period, framework and specialist review.

The company supplies an independently reviewed chronological local working-date calendar, timezone and holiday assessment. Actual integer business-day starts/finishes map to those dates; tasks cannot complete after approval or execution. Lock certification follows every task approval and actual journal posting. Planned critical-path days and actual completion proof are separate. Duplicate source and destination posting IDs block; journal source IDs may not be repeated across this population. Complex one-to-many journal source allocation requires a separately reviewed source-grain extension.
