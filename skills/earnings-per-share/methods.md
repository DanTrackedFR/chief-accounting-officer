# EPS methods and limitations

Read TOPIC-08-007's complete canonical method tree and mapped claims. Broad attribution, daily weighting and instrument-specific anti-dilution are approved; detailed options, contingent shares, two-class and rights mechanics are not an autonomous model here.

Ordinary shares outstanding = whole issued shares minus treasury shares. Require contiguous dated intervals covering every reporting day, complete independent inventory, opening/closing legal register and each dated movement. Daily weighted denominator sums outstanding × retrospective split/bonus factor × actual interval days / actual reporting days. Split/bonus boundaries separately adjust issued and treasury counts; post-period actions before authorization adjust historical denominators but not legal year-end counts. Ordinary cash issues/repurchases never receive a retrospective factor. Rights issues need separate method. Before-period comparatives use independently reviewed original numerator/weighted shares and current split factors; complex diluted-comparative reconstruction remains qualified.

Basic attributable numerator = supplied profit − NCI − preferred dividends + other supported adjustments. Reconcile total, continuing and discontinued earnings to actual statements. Preferred rights and amounts are qualified inputs, not assumed from declared cash distributions. Positive whole-share or supported weighted denominators are mandatory.

Qualified instrument methods separately supply weighted incremental shares and total/continuing numerator effects, including actual dates, tax and contractual terms. Supported controlled routes: if-converted, treasury-stock, contingent and award, with no guessed exercise prices, conversion rates, contingencies or tax rates. Treasury/award numerator effects cannot be supplied as arbitrary expense add-backs. Multiple/two-class or participating securities block. Sort by continuing numerator adjustment per incremental share, test incremental continuing EPS sequentially without cent-rounding, and exclude anti-dilution. Continuing-operation loss excludes dilution even if discontinued gain makes total income positive. Apply the same accepted instruments to total/discontinued EPS. Reconcile each final EPS to statements and retain excluded instruments.

IFRS IAS33, US ASC260 and AASB133 require separate actual entity/period scope. UK explicitly uses evidenced specified IAS33 application or a reviewed voluntary method, not universal exemption/obligation. Additional/IFRS18 per-share measures fail closed to a separate effective numerator route. Comparative, preference, instrument and disclosure methods bind actual framework/entity/both dates and current approvals. EPS creates no accounting journal; existing Equity, Debt, SBC and earnings owners retain entries.
