# Revenue recognition method and execution boundary

Canonical authority is the approved claim register for TOPIC-03-001–006/012 and the canonical `phase-2d-method.md` documents, not this implementation note. Use each framework's record and reporting-period branch. Representative rule links: contract criteria TOPIC-03-001-IFRS-R002; variable constraint TOPIC-03-002-IFRS-R003 / US-R003 / UK-R004; specific allocation IFRS-R007 / UK-R008; timing TOPIC-03-003-IFRS-R001–006; modifications R009–012. Cite the actual applied framework claim and only an established paragraph locator. Evidence ratings remain unchanged.

1. Reconcile the contract population to CLM, order forms, billing and GL. Review approval/commitment, rights, payment terms, commercial substance and framework-specific collectibility. Distinguish price concessions from credit losses. Contracts outside scope, failed criteria, nonrefundable receipts before recognition eligibility and mixed leases/insurance/instrument arrangements need their own accounting route; the ordinary customer workflow must not force revenue. Record combination analysis and all express/implied promises.
2. Determine distinct goods/services, integrated bundles, series, warranties, options/material rights, setup activities, licences and shipping. `obligations` contains the reviewed accounting units, not necessarily invoice lines. Support inception SSP with observable sales or a documented estimation method. `role` is the conclusion of control-of-specified-output analysis, with its supporting memo; agency price and SSP must represent the entity's fee rather than supplier gross consideration. US fulfilment elections must not become IFRS/UK elections.
3. Build `price_components`: fixed, noncash, consideration payable to customers, financing adjustment and variable elements. Expected-value distributions and most-likely distributions both require probabilities totaling one. `included_amount` is the independently reviewed constrained entitlement, not automatically the statistical estimate. IFRS/AASB nonreversal, ASC probable nonreversal and UK entitlement constraints require separate memos. Royalty exceptions require sales/usage and related satisfaction evidence. Noncash measurement dates, customer payments and significant financing must be calculated in approved supporting schedules; the workflow applies their reconciled price amounts, not invented rates or valuation dates.
4. Allocate by inception relative SSP with deterministic largest-remainder cent rounding. A supported `specific_allocation` must address all obligations and total the price; the memo must establish the actual framework's exception/objective. The revised UK alternative allocation is evaluated independently. Price reassessment uses inception weights rather than a refreshed SSP that silently reallocates prior performance.
5. Point-in-time recognition requires reviewed control transfer. Over-time recognition separately tests simultaneous receipt, customer control of the created asset, or no alternative use plus enforceable payment for work to date including margin. Deposits/cost-only cancellation reimbursement are not that payment right. Use a faithful, supported progress measure; exclude waste and non-performance inputs in the evidence schedule. If progress is unreliable, supply the absolute permitted recoverable-cost revenue, never a percentage implying full contract revenue. Licences, consignment, bill-and-hold, acceptance and royalties require the canonical specialist analysis before the ordinary pattern is entered.
6. Approved additions at adjusted SSP that are distinct route to a **separate new contract case**, preserving the original contract. Distinct remaining performance uses unrecognized old price plus new price prospectively. Integrated performance uses revised price/progress for cumulative catch-up. Mixed modifications require separate reviewed component schedules. Legacy FRS 102 uses its sale/service and identifiable-component fair-value model; `legacy_component_consideration` replaces relative-SSP allocation, and sale recognition uses risks/rewards. Do not label the legacy route IFRS 15. Early adoption must be an evidenced boolean and match the selected edition/election.
7. Reconcile cumulative earned amounts to opening revenue, then calculate current-period revenue. Bridge opening contract net plus current revenue minus principal billings to closing contract asset/liability. Reconcile receivable opening plus billings minus cash. Contract clearing is a computational bridge; approve presentation/account mapping per contract and do not offset unrelated contracts. Credit notes, refunds, overpayments, financing interest and non-revenue tax/supplier collections require separate approved event schedules.
8. For contract costs, document incremental/fulfilment asset eligibility, recoverability, useful service pattern, practical expedients and impairment. Add only qualifying recoverable costs; expense other additions. Amortization and impairment of opening assets remain required even when new costs do not qualify. Tie closing asset to GL and the disclosure table; UK, IFRS and US reversal/expedient differences remain separately reviewed.

## Worked case

Synthetic two-promise price1,000, SSP600/400: delivered product earns600; six of twelve service months earn200. Cumulative revenue800 minus opening recognized300 gives period500. Opening contract liability200 (signed net−200) plus period500 minus billings400 gives closing liability100. Opening AR200 + bills400 − receipts300 gives AR300. Opening cost asset50 + qualifying commission100 − amortization25 − impairment5 gives closing120.

Period entries: Dr contract clearing500 / Cr revenue500; Dr AR400 / Cr clearing400; Dr cash300 / Cr AR300. Cost additions Dr contract cost asset100 / Cr cash/AP100; Dr amortization25 / Cr asset25; Dr impairment5 / Cr asset5. Map clearing to the actual contract-balance accounts using the controlled bridge. The example is executable in `examples/ifrs-case.json`; the four-framework variants and legacy UK/adverse timing/constraint/modification routes are tested.

## Reporting and review

Prepare a memo with facts, assumptions, five-step/legacy route, alternative route and actual applied claim IDs. Reconcile disaggregated revenue, contract balances, remaining obligations, judgment disclosures and contract-cost disclosures to the controlled schedule. Apply US nonpublic, UK Section1A and AASB1060 relief only on documented eligibility. Australian NFP/public-sector scope and transition/complex events route to the indicated specialist rather than inheriting the for-profit calculation. Reviewer controls and public-output integration are in `../REVIEWER-CONTROLS.md`.
