# Commitments, Guarantees & Contingencies: executable methods and boundaries

Inventory signed contracts, procurement/capital orders, guarantees/letters of credit, legal claims/litigation and loss/gain contingencies. Search contracts, legal letters, board minutes and ledgers; no empty register certification without independent completeness proof. Every contract/claim has approved legal rights/probability, accounting route and measurement evidence. Contract exposure rollforward opening + new - fulfilled - cancelled + FX = close. Recognized carrying value opening + charge + unwind - settlement + FX = close = GL. Maximum exposure and carrying amount are distinct, never additive liabilities by default.

Ordinary purchase/capital commitments are unrecognized until applicable underlying recognition; onerous/loss obligations route existing Provisions & Contingencies. Litigation/loss recognition uses that engine's IFRS/UK/AASB probability/best-estimate and US ASC450 probable/range differences. Potential gains remain unrecognized until underlying realization is independently supported. Financial guarantees and letters of credit always require qualified financial-instrument/legal valuation evidence, including a conclusion that the recognized amount is zero. This package intentionally has no ASC460/IFRS9 guarantee valuation model: canonical approved material supports inventory/routing, not autonomous guarantee measurement. Route/type consistency cannot be bypassed by relabeling a guarantee as purchase.

Import balanced specialist journals tied to exact source case and liability/asset GL movement; do not independently recompute provision or ECL recognition. Complete disclosure disposition covers every included/excluded contract, legal reasoning, exposure and recognized amount; reconcile note population and aggregate GL. Preserve framework/entity/period-specific specialist conclusions and disclosure checklist; unsupported measurement remains blocked until qualified external evidence exists. Legal probability is never generated from narrative sentiment.

Complete synthetic example: purchase opening100 + new50 - fulfilled20 - cancelled10 =120 exposure; recognized0, no journal. A reviewed provision charge45 produces Dr expense45 Cr provision45, recognized45 (US reviewed source may be40 for the same range). Guarantee maximum50 is not payable50 or fair value50; external valuation supplies its actual carrying amount and required journals. Partial example: missing valuation/legal evidence blocks the affected case; reviewer omission yields partial for otherwise valid inventory. No independent legal probability or guarantee measurement claims are added.

Inputs and identifiers are illustrated in `examples/*.case.json`; these are wholly synthetic and contain no real approvals. Corresponding partial and complete public outputs demonstrate both certification states. Regression reissues synthetic certification against the current exact implementation; archived cases intentionally remain unsigned. Every successful route still requires all contract controls and specialist handoffs. Unsupported optional accounting routes cannot obtain a clean result merely by excluding a source item.
