# Related Parties & Related-Party Transactions: executable methods and boundaries

Populate documented control/parent/subsidiary/associate/JV/KMP/close-family/KMP-controlled relationships and effective dates; reconcile independent ownership/director declarations and counterparty master/GL searches. Never infer relationship from names. Each declaration must enumerate all identified relationships; negative declaration cannot silently hide a positive documented relationship. Transactions use relation at transaction date; closing balances/commitments use relation at reporting date. July KMP appointment does not turn January transaction into a related transaction.

Reconcile gross sales/purchases, receivables/payables and commitments separately to source GL and note; no netting unrelated assertions. Approval evidence covers terms, authorization, collateral/security and governance. Arm-length assertion requires specific independent pricing evidence and approved transfer-pricing conclusion, not absence of discounts. Consolidated intragroup amounts need group elimination handoff; separate-entity statements cannot eliminate them. Exemptions require actual approved jurisdiction/entity/period conclusion. FRS102 periods beginning2026 require explicit revised small-entity related-party disclosure review; no wholesale small-company exemption. Applicable IFRS IAS24, US ASC850, FRS102 Section33 and AASB124 scope is separately reviewed; KMP disclosure differences follow actual approved scope memo rather than transplanting IAS24 into US.

Where KMP pay is applicable, inventory approved payroll/award sources, allocate service to actual KMP period, tie GL and review all short-term, post-employment, other-long-term, termination and share-based categories including nil. Statement totals and disclosure category review independently reconcile; omitted or excluded applicable rows block. Underlying compensation recognition stays SBC/payroll; no new journal for merely related-party disclosure. Ultimate parent/control and nonnumerical terms/comparative disclosures need independent checklist review; segments in canonical08-006 are a companion Financial Statements specialist scope, not automatically built here.

Complete synthetic example: separate-entity parent sales100 and closing receivable20 produce distinct note totals100 and20, with evidenced relation. Consolidated case eliminates supported intragroup sources and note totals0; opening related/KMP timeline independently controls inclusion. Partial example: unsupported arm-length pricing or incomplete KMP category population blocks; exact-case reviewer absent yields partial. Pricing, company-law governance and legal relationship conclusions remain specialists.

Inputs and identifiers are illustrated in `examples/*.case.json`; these are wholly synthetic and contain no real approvals. Corresponding partial and complete public outputs demonstrate both certification states. Regression reissues synthetic certification against the current exact implementation; archived cases intentionally remain unsigned. Every successful route still requires all contract controls and specialist handoffs. Unsupported optional accounting routes cannot obtain a clean result merely by excluding a source item.
