# Audit Support & PBC Management — bounded methods

Management PBC population, source reconciliation, query and auditor-selected sample support only; no opinion, sufficiency/independence determination, confirmations, adjustments or accounting alteration.

## Governed source and execution

Read every actual document and claim register in ../GOVERNANCE-KNOWLEDGE-MAP.json. The map freezes live main5a011a7 and selected actual capability IDs before implementation. Existing canonical historical labels yield to the APPROVED manifest and individual Phase2E reviews. Empty registers provide approved practice; OTHER audit claims retain their auditor-side meaning and are not automatically applied as universal company obligations. No evidence rating, audit flag, limitation or paragraph is upgraded.

Use the existing production envelope, case certification and public adapter. Require current independently reviewed entity/framework/jurisdiction/period scope, actual reporting editions/adoptions and externally qualified local requirements. Unsupported entity/tier/industry overlays fail closed. The software validates supplied review records; it does not authenticate people or verify a stored URL against actual law.

## Population and version discipline

An independently frozen source population must reconcile IDs and immutable semantic fields to the tracker; gross and signed amounts, source identities and periods cannot be replaced by equal totals. Source documents retain system/query/parameters/snapshot, period and currency. Content digests bind actual supplied source bytes. Every current version has separate preparer/reviewer approval. Release review additionally hashes the source/artifact/dependency payload; fresh outer approval cannot hide a late change. Supplied data and approvals remain independently examined case evidence, not manufactured source verification.

## Ownership and output

Import only actual complete, current, unaltered owner cases/results with exact entity/framework/jurisdiction/period. Each semantic numeric assertion resolves to the actual completed owner result; duplicate conclusions and reposting block. Unresolved material evidence or accounting issues remain partial/blocked. Only numeric/count workpapers, generated guidance and material limitations cross all seven public routes. Raw inputs, memos, reviewers, internal claims and digests remain private. All candidate synthetic approvals/examples are explicitly tests, never real sign-off. No journals or external actions are created.

## Individual supported method

Actual request population binds purpose/account/assertion/date/source/ledger. Source records tie exactly by identity and contents to independent actual books, including gross amounts; files alone do not prove completeness. Management retains selected auditor sample IDs and actual evidence; missing evidence stays an exception, never a substitute selection. Accounting query conclusions must equal actual completed owner conclusions. Auditor procedures, confirmation control, independence, evidence sufficiency and opinion remain outside scope. Audit adjustments and unadjusted-difference accounting are not implemented by this bounded package.
