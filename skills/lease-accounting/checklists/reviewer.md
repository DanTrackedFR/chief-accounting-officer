# Lease skill reviewer gate

A reviewer must sign off before the skill result is treated as production accounting.

- Confirm entity, jurisdiction, framework, annual-period start, adoption status and role.
- Confirm the contract contains a lease; inspect specified asset, substitution, economic benefits and decision rights.
- Confirm commencement, enforceable term, options and exemptions/elections.
- Reconcile every included payment to the executed contract and explain excluded/variable consideration.
- Confirm discount-rate basis, periodic convention and supporting evidence.
- For US GAAP, approve finance/operating classification and any eligible risk-free-rate election.
- For UK GAAP, confirm revised Section 20 is effective/early adopted; do not route pre-2026 cases through the revised model.
- For AASB, confirm entity type, reporting tier and applicable compilation.
- Reperform PV and rollforward; investigate residuals beyond rounding tolerance.
- Review ROU bridge, expense pattern, impairment indicators, modifications/reassessments and sale-and-leaseback routing.
- Tie liability, ROU, cash and expense to GL/subledger; verify journal debits/credits and bridge accounts.
- Verify each public pinpoint citation is established in governed knowledge for the applicable period. Do not upgrade a provisional locator.
- Complete disclosure checklist and retain contract, rate, schedule, reconciliation and review evidence.
