# Government Grants production methods

The existing 172-claim supplement is independently rechecked, separately governed and hash frozen. Canonical 157 topics, 347 mappings and 1,598 claims remain untouched. Read knowledge/government-grants/FRAMEWORK-METHOD.md for full framework reasoning. This implementation is a bounded subset, described in FRAMEWORK-SUPPORT.md.

Use production.assess_case("government-grants", case). Actual agreements, identified government grantors, condition populations, independently reviewed receipt/compliance conclusions, original cost-owner records, actual payments, opening cumulative history and reconciled GL/disclosures precede recognition. Immutable original source capture binds all grant rows, ledger, currency and ownership population. Review records are supplied evidence assertions, never authenticated human identity.

IFRS/AASB require reasonable assurance. UK uses independently elected accrual or performance model; performance income follows actual satisfaction of its performance conditions. US requires current evidenced ASU2025-10 early adoption for an eligible business entity and probable receipt/compliance plus incurred cost conditions. Mandatory future dates do not permit automatic 2026 adoption. AASB compilation, FRS102 edition, grant method, entity and jurisdiction must match the supported actual reporting window.

For income accrual grant G and supported original eligible cost T with current expense E, current income is cent-rounded G*E/T. This release reconciles T to incurred original cost records and blocks unsupported future budgets. Asset deferral uses the asset owner's supported expense pattern, available-for-use date, useful life and residual/depreciable basis, never invents depreciation. Already-due unmet conditions contradict an assurance conclusion; independently evidenced future compliance remains distinguishable. Condition evidence must be current at the reporting cutoff. US recognized grant dates cannot precede the compensated incurred cost. Deferred closing = opening + additions - current release - repayment consumed from deferral. Receivable closing = opening + monetary additions - actual grant receipt. Cumulative recognized and cash totals cannot exceed the agreement. Opening receivable = prior recognized - prior paid; opening deferral = prior recognized - prior income - prior basis reduction. Prior repayment/advance history requires a separately qualified continuation route.

Basis deduction (IFRS/AASB) and cost accumulation (adopted US) credit only a grant contra-basis account. The asset owner consumes this adjustment exactly once, retains original recognition and computes subsequent adjusted depreciation/COGS. The grant workflow never posts the asset owner's acquisition or normal depreciation. Nonmonetary grants consume separately qualified matched valuation; the grant entry debits the independently evidenced owner clearing balance, rather than recreating the asset acquisition. IFRS/AASB fair-value or actual nominal election, UK fair value and adopted-US tangible FV/deferred methods remain distinct. US nonmonetary cost accumulation is blocked pending a dedicated original-cost-to-entity owner adapter: cost to the entity cannot be treated as an additional grant basis deduction. The underlying owner retains cost-to-entity recognition and required fair-value disclosure.

New unrecognized advance cash becomes Grant advance without income. Past-cost compensation without future costs is current income. A new simple monetary clawback consumes unamortized deferral then charges excess to current expense; opening30/repayment70 yields expense40. Complex asset-basis restoration/catch-up, prior errors, disputed liabilities, loans and loan forgiveness retain their qualified owners and remain blocked here.

Independent source count/absolute award amount, grant economic IDs, eligible-cost IDs, bank IDs and journal owners are unique. Already posted grant components are blocked, rather than proposed a second time. Current-period grant income/repayment expense, basis adjustments, payable and nonmonetary clearing also tie to their source amounts; unsupported preexisting current-period postings require a separate reconciliation route. Every eligible cost stays with Payroll, AP, Inventory, Intangibles or Fixed Assets. Revenue, Tax, Equity, Debt, Agriculture, Leases, Borrowing Costs, Consolidation and statement owners are never reperformed. Grant-related stock balances, all journal offsets, source GL and final statement amounts reconcile without plugs. Each per-grant quantitative disclosure and future deferral schedule ties exactly. Future release amounts are calculated from the actual supplied owner expense bases; total supported future expenses reconcile the closing grant. Only the final future row allocates a deterministic cent residual. Repayment retaining a future deferral requires a separately revised pattern and remains blocked in this release. The original case, reviewed knowledge bytes and owner implementation closure bind the supplied independent signoff. Missing/stale certification returns partial with zero executable journals; unresolved/unsupported evidence returns blocked with zero journals.

All response/export routes use the existing public allowlist. Private source population, reviewer records, approval provenance and fingerprints stay internal. Public output retains substantive limits. Native orchestration, persistence, memory and clients remain the existing authorities; no new engine or posting system is introduced.
