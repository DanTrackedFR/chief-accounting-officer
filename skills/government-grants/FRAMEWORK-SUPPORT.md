# Government Grants support matrix

All supported routes require for-profit entity, reviewed reporting periods wholly in calendar2026, exact source context, elected class policy and independent certification. Jurisdictions: IFRS NL/GB/AU/US subject to no unresolved overlays; AASB AU Tier1; UK GB FRS102 September2024; US US business entities with evidenced adoption. No early presentation-regime adoption.

| Route | IFRS IAS20 | AASB120 | UK FRS102 Section24 | US adopted Topic832 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Income / expense matching | Supported actual incurred cost basis | Supported Tier1 | Elected accrual | Supported probable + incurred costs |
| Performance conditions | Assurance assesses evidence | Assurance assesses evidence | Elected performance; conditions must be met | Probable threshold + applicable incurred costs |
| Asset deferred income | Owner expense pattern | Same bounded route | Accrual deferred; no basis netting | Deferred-income approach |
| Asset basis adjustment | Deduction election | Deduction election | BLOCKED | Cost accumulation election |
| Nonmonetary | Qualified FV or actual nominal | Qualified FV or actual nominal | FV only | Tangible FV/deferred only; cost-to-entity accumulation BLOCKED pending owner adapter; FV disclosure required |
| Past-cost compensation | No future costs | No future costs | Actual model and terms | Actual qualified past incurred expense |
| New advance | Actual monetary cash; no income | Same | Performance/accrual conditions independently reviewed | Probable conditions absent; no income |
| Simple monetary repayment | Fully consumed deferral then current expense | Same | Qualified simple model conclusion | Fully consumed deferral then current expense |
| Nonadopter policy analogy | N/A | N/A | N/A | BLOCKED; no qualified nonadopter method implemented |

Blocked in every applicable route: unsupported entity/jurisdiction/period; missing valuation/cost/compliance/policy; Agriculture FVLCTS grant accounting; grants acting as exchange/shareholder/income-tax/ordinary financing; below-market loans and forgiveness without qualified debt owner; complex repayment/basis catch-up/prior errors; changed/disposed/impaired assets without separately qualified owner route; mixed packages without allocation; prior advances/repayments and their conversion; unsupported budgets/forecast release bases; repayment retaining future deferral without revised owner pattern; already posted current-period grant components; imported specialist journals without economic adapters. These boundaries are limitations, not claims that the standards prohibit the underlying transactions.

Disclosures supply actual model/presentation, grant nature/extent, quantitative effects, assistance and unfulfilled conditions/contingencies. Future release schedules tie deferrals. Final statement/note construction remains with its existing owner. Standard source assurance and future direct-source audit flags remain unchanged.
