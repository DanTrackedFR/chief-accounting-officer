# Subsequent-event methods and limitations

Read TOPIC-08-005's entire approved canonical method tree and claims. Authorization differs from shareholder approval; dates and authorizer/issuance status are supplied legal/governance evidence. IFRS/AASB/UK use actual authorization; US public uses issuance and nonpublic available-to-issue in this bounded method. Other specialized filer/issuance patterns need their actual qualified method. Require cutoff between reporting date and execution, complete searches through that cutoff, and actual operative edition/tier; do not transplant future presentation rules.

Independently search board, legal, treasury, commercial, tax, asset, post-close and forecast feeds; their union equals the event register. Feed completeness evidence includes actual search and date; empty event lists still require source-owner certification. Every event's occurrence/discovery falls after report date and through cutoff, with discovery no earlier than occurrence. The relevant condition date may predate reporting; classification must agree with evidence of conditions existing at reporting date. Confirming customer insolvency/litigation/impairment evidence differs from later causation. New financings/acquisitions/disposals/dividends do not rewrite year-end merely because they matter to liquidity.

Adjusting/recognized measurement belongs to an existing production owner: ECL, Provisions, Impairment, Business Combinations, Fixed/Intangible Assets, Debt, Equity, Accounting Changes or Taxes, controlled by event type. Compare actual fresh COMPLETE original and revised results of the same owner/case, exact framework/entity/both dates, current knowledge/implementation and independent certification. Derive all Dr-positive journal differences, validate event measured-account effect, and apply once to a complete balanced original/revised statement population. Owner entries are NOT reposted here. No adjusted valuation of a reserved specialist area is implemented.

Nonadjusting/nonrecognized events have zero period-end measurement movement. Material disclosure requires nature, quantified effect or reviewed inability to estimate, and aggregate materiality review. Separately inspect going-concern developments even for nonadjusting events; require an actual complete Going Concern import and basis review. Inappropriate going-concern basis fails closed to separate basis accounting, not a clean nonadjusting conclusion. Post-issuance discovery/reissuance, SEC/legal filing, dividend legal determination and unsupported measurement remain specialists.

A later condition labelled adjusting, incomplete event-feed search, arbitrary scalar specialist approval, repeated source update or post-authorization date blocks.

Public output includes controlled event types, numerical effects and cutoff, not event source notes/reviewer records. Retain material uncertainty and measurement boundaries; no filing or communications.
