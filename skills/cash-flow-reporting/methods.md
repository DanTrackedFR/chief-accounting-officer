# Cash Flow Statement & Cash Reporting: executable methods and boundaries

Cash accounts have opening/closing bank and GL balances, definition/restriction evidence, cash-flow and balance-sheet inclusion flags and FX amounts. Independently inventory every cash account; equivalents use original maturity from acquisition, known amount, insignificant risk and cash-management purpose. Three calendar months is a supported screening ceiling, not proof that every shorter instrument qualifies. US ASC230 cash-flow totals include restricted cash; balance-sheet mapping remains separate. IFRS/UK/AASB restricted demand deposits require withdrawability analysis, and qualifying integral repayable-on-demand overdrafts may be in the cash-flow population. Account-level FX is supplied from Foreign Currency.

Classify signed bank movements by underlying nature: receipts/payments operating, capital expenditure and net business acquisition/disposal investing, debt/equity financing. Internal included-cash transfers must net to zero and are excluded. Review tax tracing; US tax cash remains operating, other frameworks allow specifically identified investing/financing taxes. New lease liabilities are noncash; cash lease principal/interest versus US operating-lease payments follow reviewed Lease Accounting conclusions. Acquisitions/disposals need Consolidation net-cash handoff; never recalculate PPA.

US interest paid/received and dividends received are operating; dividends paid financing. Pre-2027 IFRS/AASB and FRS102 support reviewed consistent permitted interest/dividend elections. IFRS18/AASB18 ordinary-entity annual periods beginning 2027 (or evidenced early adoption) require operating-profit indirect start; interest paid/dividends paid financing, interest/dividends received investing. Main-business investing/financing and Australian Tier2 adoption overlays fail closed to specialists. Supplier-finance classification needs underlying recognition and disclosure review, not an inferred financing designation.

Indirect cash = reviewed starting subtotal + supported noncash/other adjustments + working-capital changes stripped of acquisition, FX and noncash changes; assets reverse sign, liabilities retain sign. Direct bank-derived CFO must equal indirect CFO. Opening cash + operating + investing + financing + FX = closing cash; opening/closing cash definitions independently tie to GL/balance sheet. Disclose population differences/restrictions, FX, noncash, financing changes and elected classifications. Output is a statement workpaper, not automatic filing.

Noncash lease20 is excluded. Four-framework cases exercise this bridge.
