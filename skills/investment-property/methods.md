# Investment Property — governed owned-property methods

The original PPE classification-only boundary remains in the unchanged canonical
157-topic library. Recognition authority is the separately approved 96-claim
SUPPLEMENTAL_INVESTMENT_PROPERTY package, not a new canonical topic. Inspect the
framework METHOD documents, exact source assurance and independent review ledger.
All claims retain model-derived assurance and an open operative-authority audit.

fair value cannot be rescued with a cost flag or certificate. US ASC820 measurement
knowledge never itself authorizes recurring fair-value recognition.

## Actual input contract and accounting

Use production.assess_case("investment-property",case). Each case owns one exact
legal property interest, one documented annual-cutoff event, one entity and one
functional currency. The independent inventory must equal actual source identities,
count and gross cost. Original source captures bind property records, prior-model
and carrying history, policy, valuation, owner measurements, event, GL, ownership
and disclosure population. Current-version evidence approvals require separate
recorded owner/reviewer, operative edition and actual period; these are supplied
documentary controls, not authentication or generated accounting approvals.

Initial recognition adds purchase price and eligible direct transaction costs and
proposes Dr property/Cr cash. Only a cash purchase at the reporting cutoff is
supported; acquisition rights, cash date, cost dates and asset identity must agree.
Excluded financing, AP, combinations and abnormal costs remain with their owners.

Fair-value holding remeasures the original carrying basis to the independently
supplied reporting-date market-participant value; the difference is P&L, not OCI.
Qualified methodology, report hash, actual rights, valuation standard, valuer and
separate qualified reviewer are required. Material uncertainty, stale dates,
asking-price or transaction-cost-deducted values block. The runtime does not price
property. IFRS/AASB cost-model FV evidence is required for every closing property,
including reporting-cutoff purchases and transfers in. The qualified reporting-date
amount is output as `fair_value_disclosure`, never a gain journal. Stale, unreliable
or missing note valuation evidence blocks; zero closing IP stock has a zero note.

Cost holding separately reperforms unchanged straight-line building depreciation
from actual land/building/residual/life/service evidence and annual days. Depreciation
and any current impairment effects have already been recognized by their original
owners and form the pre-adapter GL; IP emits no second owner journals. Prior
impairment, estimate changes, advanced components and reversals remain blocked.

Cost-to-cost IFRS/AASB transfers preserve **current** carrying amount. Transfer-in
requires the existing PPE depreciation and impairment owners' reporting-date
measurements, independently reperformed from the original prior-period opening.
Pre-transfer charges are disclosed separately and never deducted again in the IP
class: zero opening IP plus current net transfer equals closing IP. The pre-event
PPE GL must equal that reconciled current carrying basis; prior-year value alone is
insufficient. FV-to-PPE transfers use
actual change-of-use date fair value as deemed cost, recognizing the preceding
movement once. Into-FV PPE transfers remain unsupported: original-owner revaluation
and previous impairment/OCI allocation cannot be replaced with a generic IP gain.
Intent alone is insufficient. Intra-year events are blocked until remaining-period
ownership, valuation and depreciation can be independently qualified.

Cash disposal remeasures an FV asset through the disposal date, then derecognizes
carrying amount against actual net cash proceeds. Net proceeds less carrying amount
is disposal P&L. Exact source/GL bridges tie the FV movement, net proceeds, disposal
gain and zero closing asset. Continuing involvement, sale-leaseback and contingent
consideration remain with separately governed owners.

Mixed-use separation needs actual separate disposal/finance-lease ability and
component costs reconciling to whole-property cost. Inseparable insignificant owner
use is bounded to IFRS/AASB with a documented judgment; UK/US inseparable routes
are blocked. Significant hotel-like ancillary services fail IP classification.

## Owner, disclosure and release gates

Exact economic movement ownership must match initial/FV/transfer/disposal and
existing depreciation/impairment populations. Other owners cannot recognize the
same movement and balanced netting cannot hide duplicates. Underlying rental
income, financing, tax, leases, construction, consolidation and statements remain
with their owners. Native upstream result imports are blocked pending a separately
asset-bound adapter; reviewed source workpapers are not native engine certificates.

Model-specific disclosure requirements include policy/classification, restriction
and commitment review, rental/direct expense source amounts, valuation or cost
method/life/gross balances, group-rental PPE amount where applicable, and a complete
movement-to-statement bridge. Numerical disclosure rows exactly equal calculations.
Missing material evidence fails closed; unknown materiality does not waive a gap.

All proposed entries use Decimal and exact cents, balance individually and reconcile
every source/GL/statement offset. Missing/stale final signoff returns PARTIAL and
suppresses released journals. The seven public routes expose curated conclusions,
numeric workpapers and limitations, never source captures, reviewer identities,
hashes or provenance ratings. No ERP posting or authenticated certification occurs.

# Cash-purchase qualification

Initial cases must explicitly provide `events[0].acquisition_nature="cash_purchase"`, `properties[0].gifted=false`, and positive source-supported purchase-price consideration separate from direct transaction fees. Paid fees alone never qualify a gifted or zero-price property as a cash purchase. Nonexchange, nominal/zero-price and other unsupported acquisition methods require separately governed original-owner accounting; no gift measurement basis is inferred.
