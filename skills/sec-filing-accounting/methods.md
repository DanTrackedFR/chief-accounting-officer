# SEC / Public Company Reporting & Filing Accounting — bounded methods

US registrant accounting-support controls only: actual qualified filer/form/calendar, statement-to-filing tie-out, structured tags, late changes and query readiness. No legal opinion, filing, officer certification or compliance assertion.

Actual canonical sources: TOPIC-16-001, TOPIC-16-002, TOPIC-16-003, TOPIC-16-004, TOPIC-16-005. The immutable map preserves original claims and effective-period/entity/jurisdiction qualifications.

## Actual filer, form and calendar

Only US registrant accounting support. Ordinary domestic US GAAP annual10-K or
Q1–Q3 interim10-Q, and qualified FPI annual20-F/interim6-K cases are supported.
FPI IFRS and US GAAP accounting bases remain separate from securities-law status.
Actual registration/filer facts and exact qualified form/period instructions are
required; the software does not determine legal status. Other forms, amendments,
reissuance, certifications, audit opinions and submission requests block.

The independently verified actual-case SEC rule record has jurisdiction, issuer,
form, reporting kind, effective period, version, execution-date check and authority
provenance. Supplied verified due dates tie to calendar readiness/audit/board
workpapers. No statutory deadline is calculated. A 6-K does not inherit10-Q timing.
Current authoritative verification is a case input, not an assertion that a stored
URL or the accounting framework is a complete SEC rulebook.

## Statement and tag population

Every approved full-entity current/comparative typed source metric has actual GL
line lineage and source span. Prior-year calendar comparability is mandatory.
Financial Statements imports bind exact full-entity values. The independently
inventoried filing-line population covers each source exactly once, matching
metric/unit/period and human-rendered amount. Profit/revenue/expense/OCI sources
are durations; assets/liabilities/cash/closing equity are instants. Caller-owned
expected tag fields cannot override those temporal meanings.

Taxonomy/entry point, approved concept, dimensions, unit, context/entity/period,
reviewed scale/sign and extension rationale remain actual current method inputs.
Machine raw ×10^scale ×sign ties exactly to the human/source amount; scaling occurs
once. Required tag populations cover every supplied filing line. This is accounting
consistency review, not an XBRL schema/EDGAR conformance engine or regulator-valid
certificate. Actual technical validation logs, warning resolution and current
instructions remain mandatory supplied control evidence.

## Release and issue governance

Annual/interim checklist, disclosure controls, source/narrative consistency and
legal/IR/auditor review must be closed. An APM in the filing requires the actual
completed current APM owner and numeric cross-check. Other accounting imports
remain evidence only, current/complete/unaltered and dimension-matched. They do
not authorize a filing or replace the statement/tie-out population.

Issue and query inventories bind accounting owners, original-period evidence,
reproducible response workpapers and separate correction assessment. No query
is submitted. Late changes reopen source, rendering, tags and approvals.
Independent release review binds a digest of all sources/filing/tags/rules/issues/
changes/imports; mutation invalidates it even if a fresh outer certificate is
supplied. Exact case certification and public allowlisting remain separate gates.
