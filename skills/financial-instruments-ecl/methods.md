# Financial instruments and credit-loss method

Retrieve TOPIC-06-007–010 and TOPIC-03-008/009, including framework-specific claims, scope exceptions and operative editions. The canonical classification and impairment records distinguish IFRS/AASB business-model/SPPI and ECL from US instrument-specific categories and CECL/AFS and UK policy elections. Do not substitute an IFRS claim for ASC326 or FRS102 authority. Exact pinpoints appear only when established by the applied claim; source assurance is independent of topic approval.

1. Reconcile the instrument inventory to GL, loan/AR subledgers, treasury confirmations and off-balance commitments. Separate financial assets, liabilities, equity instruments, guarantees, derivatives and lease receivables. Resolve contractual cash flows, currency, inception/date, maturity, embedded features and legal terms before measurement. IFRS/AASB debt assets require business-model and SPPI analysis, including applicable 2026 contingent-feature amendments; collect-only alone does not establish amortized cost. Resolve US ASC320/321/825 and CECL/AFS categories separately. UK Sections11/12, permitted IFRS9 or legacy IAS39 election and applicable edition must be evidenced.
2. The ordinary workflow supports debt/trade/contract assets and reviewed lease net investments; amortized-cost debt liabilities have their own EIR expense schedule, and equity investments have their own fair-value schedule. IFRS-style equity FVOCI needs eligible non-trading instrument and irrevocable election, with no gain recycling. US equity cannot use that IFRS election. Complex liability own-credit, compound instruments, measurement alternatives, derivatives and hedge designation/effectiveness route to specialist schedules. A derivative fair-value computation does not establish hedge accounting.
3. Apply impairment only within scope. FVTPL/equity effects are in their measurement route, not a separate ECL allowance. IFRS/AASB general ECL selects stage1 for no SICR, stage2 for SICR and stage3 for credit impairment. Stage1 uses default events possible in the next12months but cash shortfalls over their recovery life; it is not “losses occurring in12months.” Simplified lifetime routes require eligible receivables/contract assets/lease election. POCI needs credit-adjusted EIR and cumulative lifetime-loss-change analysis; purchased US financial assets require their own PCD/purchased-asset effective-date branch. Route these specialized models explicitly rather than forcing ordinary initial allowances.
4. Review origination/current risk, default definition, SICR indicators and rebuttals, term, segmentation, collateral/enforceable guarantees, expected recovery costs and timing, reasonable/supportable forecasts and mean reversion where applicable. Terms contain marginal default probabilities, not repeated unconditional cumulative PDs. Sum of marginal PDs may not exceed1. Scenario weights sum1; PD/LGD and loss rates are between0 and1; discount factors are positive and use the supported original EIR/effective method. Do not invent PD/LGD or forecasts from ageing labels.
5. Supported methods include scenario-weighted term PD×LGD×EAD, discounted contractual-minus-expected cash shortfalls and supported lifetime loss rates. UK own-model incurred impairment requires objective evidence and recoverable-cash-flow measurement, not unconditional IFRS lifetime staging. US CECL contractual-life, extension/cancellation and forecast-reversion assumptions require the US memo. AFS intent/requirement to sell before recovery routes to cost write-down; ordinary AFS credit allowance is capped by fair-value shortfall. OCI captures only the noncredit fair-value component so credit loss is not counted twice.
6. Build gross carrying, interest and allowance bridges. Ordinary EIR uses opening gross; IFRS stage3 interest uses net carrying with the gross-versus-net difference reconciled in the allowance. Model output and management overlay are separate, evidence-supported and checked for double counting. Opening allowance + expense + recoveries + FX + net-interest adjustment − writeoffs = closing allowance. Tie gross and allowance writeoffs. FX movements require balanced supported journals that reconcile both bridges, never an unexplained amount.
7. Track the **opening** fair-value adjustment and closing required adjustment, then post their period difference. For AFS, allowance plus noncredit valuation adjustment must leave the security at fair value. IFRS FVOCI allowance is in OCI and does not reduce the fair-value asset carrying amount. Credit-impaired FVOCI net-interest mechanics are a separate specialist schedule. Equity fair-value changes follow the elected P&L/OCI route; liability interest debits finance cost and credits liability, never interest revenue.

## Disclosure and specialist gates

Reconcile class/category carrying values, allowances by stage/model, gross changes, writeoffs/recoveries, collateral, credit concentrations, modified/credit-impaired exposures, model/forecast/overlay judgments and sensitivity. Add liquidity maturities, market risk and fair-value hierarchy where applicable. UK elected-model disclosures and AASB tier/sector overlays remain distinct. Guarantees/commitments, POCI/PCD, IAS39, derivative/hedge, modification/derecognition, negative-yield and complex FVOCI routes require specialist evidence and are blocked until their reviewed event-specific schedules are completed. This is a bounded governed expert skill, not an autonomous valuation or credit-underwriting model.
