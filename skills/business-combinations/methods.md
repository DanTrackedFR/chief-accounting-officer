# Acquisition method and framework decision workpaper

## Scope and decisions

Read canonical TOPIC-13-001/002/003 and their claim registers. Confirm business definition (including applicable concentration-test election), accounting acquirer, control date, contractual consideration and identifiable population. Ordinary business acquisitions only. Asset deals, common control, reverse/VIE acquisitions, step acquisitions, pre-existing relationships, employee-contingent payments, complex awards and private-company alternatives return a specialist handoff, not a false ordinary PPA. A shareholding percentage never proves control. Consider ASU 2025-03 prospective/early adoption for qualifying VIE acquirer scope; that specialized case is not calculated here.

Freeze acquisition-date valuations, tax bases and recognition exceptions (contingent liabilities, employee benefits, indemnification, reacquired rights and tax). Set tax_review_complete and exceptions_resolved only against attached specialist workpapers. Fair value exceptions require a reviewed_exception basis, not a blanket fair-value assumption. Tax is an external dependency because no canonical tax topic exists.

| Framework | Costs | NCI | Goodwill/subsequent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IFRS | Expense acquisition services; issuance separate | Qualifying present ownership: reviewed FV or proportionate election | Consideration + NCI - identifiable net assets; no amortization |
| US GAAP | Expense services; separate issuance model | FV; no IFRS proportionate election | Ordinary ASC 805/350 goodwill, no amortization; private alternatives routed |
| FRS 102 | Include directly attributable costs in purchase cost | Proportionate net assets | Section 19 amortization over reviewed useful life, then impairment |
| AASB | Independently confirm operative AASB 3/amendments, generally service expense | Reviewed permitted election and tier | Applicable AASB 3/136, not assumed inherited authority |

For UK legacy/adopted editions record specific Section 19 applicability and life support; do not infer a life of ten years. Australian compilations after July 2026 need actual amendments selected. Negative goodwill under UK requires its own recognition/release schedule, not immediate IFRS bargain gain.

## Inputs, arithmetic and events

Assets/liabilities are complete, uniquely identified, nonnegative cent-rounded amounts with account, recognition memo and valuation evidence. Consideration is cash + equity + other + qualifying contingent amount (+ UK direct costs). Prior interest is explicitly zero; otherwise route step-acquisition FV/OCI treatment. Net identifiable assets cannot omit tax effects merely because the calculator balances.

Goodwill = consideration + NCI - net assets. IFRS/AASB/US negative residual becomes a bargain gain only after documented reassessment. Journal debits identifiable assets/goodwill and credits liabilities, consideration/NCI/bargain gain. This is an acquisition consolidation worksheet, not a parent standalone investment journal.

A measurement-period asset adjustment must concern an identified provisional item and acquisition-date facts, within the one-year maximum. Supported tax/NCI effects must already be incorporated in a separate reviewed PPA; changes affecting those layers require specialist routing. Ordinary no-tax/no-NCI-change asset delta adjusts goodwill, with independently reconciled remaining goodwill and catch-up depreciation. IFRS/AASB presentation is retrospective; US current-period catch-up is separate. UK adjustments are routed. Contingent liability remeasurement after acquisition goes to P&L under the ordinary non-UK model; equity consideration is not remeasured. UK purchase-cost contingent adjustments need separate goodwill/amortization support.

## Worked examples and controls

Synthetic assets 900, liabilities 200 => net 700. Cash price 800, 80% ownership: FV NCI 180 gives goodwill 280; qualifying IFRS proportionate NCI 140 gives goodwill 240. UK direct costs 20 increase price to 820 and goodwill to 260 (NCI 140); supported ten-year full-year amortization 26 leaves 234. IFRS service costs 20 expense separately and leave goodwill 280. Cash 500/NCI 140/net 700 gives bargain gain 60 after reassessment, not UK immediate income.

A +40 provisional asset correction in an IFRS full-FV NCI case with independently reviewed unchanged NCI/tax reduces goodwill 280 to 240; catch-up depreciation 4 is separately booked. Unsupported NCI/tax changes block this slice.

Challenge missing intangible/tax, remuneration disguised as consideration, late estimates called acquisition-date facts, imported UK NCI election and double-counted costs. Review acquisition notes, provisional items, valuation assumptions, consideration, bargain reassessment and subsequent goodwill. Governing locators are selected from approved claims; IFRS 3 45/49–50/53 and FRS 102 19.11–19.13B are established only where their register claims support the actual issue; other references remain qualified.
