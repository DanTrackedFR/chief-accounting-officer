# Impairment method and valuation boundaries

Read canonical TOPIC-04-006 README, phase-2d-method.md, applied-qa.md and claim register. Choose unit perimeter based on independent cash inflows/reporting-unit economics, not management preference. Test individual assets and other applicable impairment methods before goodwill. Annual tests for goodwill/indefinite-lived items and indicator tests for finite assets are separate required judgments.

| Framework/model | Trigger and loss | Allocation/reversal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IAS 36/AASB 136 | Recoverable = max(VIU, FV less disposal costs); loss = carrying - recoverable if positive | Goodwill first, other assets pro rata subject to floors; never reverse goodwill |
| FRS 102 Sections 19/27 | Reviewed recoverable amount and unit, after goodwill amortization | Reviewed Section 27 allocation and reversal ceilings; no IFRS unamortized opening goodwill |
| ASC 360 held-and-used | Carrying exceeds undiscounted use/disposal cash => loss = carrying - FV | Long-lived asset group excluding goodwill, allocation with supported FV floors; no reversal |
| ASC 350 goodwill | Reporting-unit loss = min(goodwill, carrying - FV), after prior tests | Goodwill only; no reversal |
| ASC 350 indefinite | Separately tested intangible carrying - FV | Separate asset; no reversal |

Private-company alternatives, held-for-sale, revaluation-reserve/OCI allocation, tax feedback loops, complex corporate-asset allocation, financial instruments and specialized asset scope route to specialists. The unit model is not a declaration that all balance-sheet assets fall under IAS 36.

## Deterministic inputs

Supply unit/model, indicator/annual test, sequencing/perimeter memos and complete asset list. Asset carrying, allocation floor and no-impairment ceiling must be independently supported and cent-rounded. Floor means applicable individual recoverable/FV/zero threshold; do not set it arbitrarily. UK amortization-complete gate requires an attached amortization workpaper.

DCF uses reviewed signed cash flows at unique positive integer end-of-year times, rate > -1 and supplied terminal value discounted at final forecast year. No growth, risk, tax or valuation input is inferred. The reviewer aligns currency, nominal/real, pre-/post-tax, budget exclusions, forecast period, disposal costs and market-participant assumptions. Fair value and FVLCD are independent supported inputs; discounted VIU never substitutes for US undiscounted screen. Loss allocation repeatedly redistributes when floors bind and explicitly blocks an unallocated residual.

Non-goodwill reversal is a separately reviewed event, limited to recoverable headroom and the depreciated no-impairment carrying ceiling. Never combine simultaneous same-unit loss and reversal. Cost-model journal is Dr impairment expense/Cr asset; reversal Dr asset/Cr reversal income. Map actual presentation accounts before posting.

## Worked and adverse cases

Synthetic IFRS unit carrying 12m (goodwill 1.5m), VIU10.8m/FVLCD11m => loss1m, remaining goodwill0.5m. An increase to13m does not reverse goodwill. Different US ASC360 group12m, undiscounted11.5m/FV10.8m => loss1.2m; undiscounted12.1m => zero, despite FV10.8m. ASC350 unit8m includingGW2m/FV7.4m => loss0.6m, capped atGW.

Allocation example GW100 + asset A600 floor580 + B300 floor0; recoverable700 => loss300, GW100, A20 and B180. Floors exhaust => block instead of invent a liability. Asset carrying70, no-impairment ceiling90, recoverable100 => allowable supported reversal20, not30.
