## Isolated workpaper, not full statements

Require actual functional-currency/economy facts, qualitative and quantitative
indicators, group-consistency and onset/cessation review. A country label or a
three-year inflation threshold alone cannot authorize the route. No economic
status or index is invented. IFRS/AASB actual edition and entity overlay are
independently reviewed; US ASC830 and UK detailed models block.

A single approved general-index series/revision has exact dated records and a
reporting-date closing index. Historical nonmonetary, dated equity and in-period
income/expense rows use amount × closing index / exact recognition-date index.
Report-date monetary and current-value nonmonetary stocks stay unindexed. Earlier
current-value measures need a separate schedule. Rows reconcile to independent
source IDs/count/absolute amounts. Values are Decimal inputs; isolated results
are not rounded into a fabricated monetary balancing gain.

The output explicitly declares full_statement_result=false. It does not calculate
net monetary gain/loss, first-year/cessation equity, tax, complete restated income,
full comparatives, translation or consolidation. Qualified workpaper handoffs
identify those dependencies without treating reviewer assertions as approved
calculation engines. No journals or postable statement package are emitted.
Existing Foreign Currency and Consolidation hyperinflation exclusions are intact;
imports cannot bypass them. This completed result certifies the bounded isolated
schedule only, and cannot be used as a completed restated financial statement.
