# Going Concern & Liquidity Assessment: executable methods and boundaries

Assessment uses approved current management forecasts and a complete debt/covenant register. Never manufacture receipts, payment timing, commitments or intent. IFRS/AASB minimum horizon is at least12 months from reporting date, UK FRS102 at least12 months from authorization, US ASC205-40 one year from issuance/available-to-issue date. Evidence cutoff must cover actual issue/authorization. Extension beyond minimum and forward indicators are management judgments; minimum is not a safe harbor. Liquidation/non-going-concern basis and unsupported NFP/public-sector overlays block for separate method.

Require one base and at least one downside scenario; complete consecutive month ends cover horizon, with independently reviewed intra-period minimum cash to expose midmonth deficits. Available cash excludes evidenced inaccessible amounts and ties total GL; cash specialist provides actual available population. Before-plan and after-plan cash = opening + receipts - operating payments - debt principal + eligible plan funding only after available date. Minimum headroom includes opening reserve and intra-period minima; runway identifies first exhausted forecast period, not an asserted exact date without daily source. Debt principal cannot exceed confirmed register; maturity/on-demand cash must be included. Contract covenant tests are inventoried by scenario; ratios use approved numerator/positive denominator and actual minimum/maximum thresholds. Later waiver affects liquidity separately from reporting-date debt classification.

Plans require actual management intent, independent feasibility and financing evidence, timing/amount within support, commitment/within-control and for US probable implementation and mitigation. Uncommitted financing cannot be inserted as cash. Calculate sensitivities from evidenced receipt reductions/incremental costs. Evaluate US substantial doubt before plans then whether supported plans alleviate; IFRS/UK/AASB assess material uncertainty/significant judgment with reviewed conclusions and downside funding. Negative forecast cannot receive unsupported no-uncertainty certification. Management conclusions may require disclosure despite positive numerical cash.

Complete synthetic example: available1000, monthly base receipts100/payments100 leaves1000; downside80/100 over12months leaves760. UK/US15months throughMarch2028 leaves700 and meets March2027 authorization/issuance horizon. Reduction100×20%+cost5=25. Opening cash100/reserve150 yields initial headroom-50 even if later cash increases. Partial example: an unsigned forecast or uncommitted funding plan blocks; complete numbers without exact-case certification remain partial. Debt, subsequent-event and liquidity disclosures route Financial Statements; qualified financing/legal evidence is mandatory.

Inputs and identifiers are illustrated in `examples/*.case.json`; these are wholly synthetic and contain no real approvals. Corresponding partial and complete public outputs demonstrate both certification states. Regression reissues synthetic certification against the current exact implementation; archived cases intentionally remain unsigned. Every successful route still requires all contract controls and specialist handoffs. Unsupported optional accounting routes cannot obtain a clean result merely by excluding a source item.
