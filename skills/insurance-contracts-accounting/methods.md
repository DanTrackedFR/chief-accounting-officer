# Bounded methods and actuarial boundary

The approved supplement is independently governed across four frameworks. Its
approval does not claim direct paragraph verification for unavailable operative
text. Preserve evidence ratings, period/entity restrictions and audit-required flags.
Execution retrieves only approved applicable claims and binds the frozen knowledge
map and current implementation to case certification.

## Supported routes

- IFRS/AASB GMM: first-year issued nonparticipating groups, all boundary premiums
  received at initial recognition, qualified initial present-value outflows and RA,
  initial CSM or immediate onerous loss, supplied finance changes and locked-rate
  CSM interest, simple nonfinancial future-service changes where current/locked
  discount bases coincide, qualified current/remaining coverage-unit release,
  systematic report-supported initial-loss allocation, qualified incurred-claim
  estimates and cash settlement. Changes exhausting CSM or reversing initial loss
  remain blocked. No CSM plug or actuarial rate/RA/coverage forecast is constructed.
- IFRS/AASB PAA issued: inception one-year coverage or evidenced material equivalence,
  qualified time/risk expected-premium allocation, separate remaining-coverage and
  incurred-claims bridge, one-year premium/claims financing exemption evidence,
  attributable acquisition cash flows expensed under an eligible election or
  capitalized in remaining coverage with supplied amortization, qualified onerous
  excess where indicated. Unreceived premium/receivable routes remain blocked.
- IFRS/AASB held PAA: prospective proportionate one-year/equivalent treaties with
  native held recognition, separate treaty/counterparty and exact underlying claims,
  qualified proportional recoveries/nonperformance, ceded-premium service expense,
  separate held remaining coverage/incurred recovery and cash. Issued onerous
  underlying/loss recovery, commissions, disputed/retroactive/nonproportionate and
  GMM held routes remain blocked.
- US short duration: independently qualified insurance-entity and contract scope;
  fully paid new protection begins at the actual premium due/recognition date, with
  the complete premium cash population received at that inception. Native
  successful-acquisition DAC, protection-pattern earned
  premium, UPR, undiscounted qualified claim/claim-adjustment estimates, premium
  deficiency under actual native aggregation with investment-income exclusion
  policy, DAC writeoff before additional deficiency liability. Public workpapers
  use premium-deficiency and DAC terminology, with no IFRS CSM/RA/loss-component
  placeholders. No RA, CSM, PAA,
  IFRS group model, LDTI, long-duration, universal life, annuity, MRB or ceded route.

## Input and evidence contract

Every policy, group, claim, cash event, report, GL and review record names an exact
independently frozen document. Content hashes and source context/date must agree.
Independent actuarial reviewer differs from preparer and report preparer; qualified
report identifies model/assumption versions, actual valuation and signoff dates,
entity/group/product/portfolio/currency/framework, boundaries and original policy,
claim and cash population hashes. Duplicate economic aliases fail even when row
IDs differ. Original recognized group membership is locked. Actual terms, risk
scenarios and scope exceptions drive classification; product labels never suffice.

Claim payments reduce incurred liability/recoverable and do not incur a second
expense. Incurred amounts explicitly tie to qualified PV plus RA (RA prohibited in
US). Report totals cannot override incomplete source populations. Distinguish actual
experience, nonfinancial assumptions, current/future service and financial changes.
Errors/model/policy changes require Accounting Changes owner review.

## Reconciliation and output

Named movements reproduce every remaining-coverage, incurred-claims, CSM, RA and
loss-component closing balance. Balanced journals reconcile each signed debit GL
movement; source stock values and statement balances tie exactly. Issued and held
sources remain separate. Public outputs contain generated workpapers, journal
amounts and curated caveats, excluding internal source notes/provenance and hashes.
Missing/stale certification is PARTIAL; unsupported models or sources are BLOCKED.

Investments stay with Financial Instruments. Derivative scope/designation stays
with Derivatives/Hedge. Independent valuation stays with Fair Value. Entity FX
stays with Foreign Currency; foreign-currency insurance execution is blocked here.
Non-insurance services stay with Revenue; insurance revenue is never also ordinary
Revenue. Insurance liabilities are not duplicated in generic Provisions. Acquired
basis stays with Business Combinations. Final statements and disclosure checklist
remain with Financial Statements and Disclosure Management. Systems/Data owns the
source-system/interface controls; Controls owns design/testing readiness.

## Explicit unsupported accounting

VFA/direct participation, risk mitigation elections, retained transition, acquired
contracts/portfolio transfers, separated or nondistinct investment components,
financial guarantees/fixed-fee elections/seller warranties, contract modifications
and specialist derecognition, multi-currency FX, OCI disaggregation, first-year
negative remaining-coverage asset balances, GMM acquisition-asset allocation,
CSM-exhaustion/reversal, US long-duration/reinsurance and generic UK FRS103
measurement require governed extensions and actual qualified owner inputs.
No ERP posting, actuarial certification, legal opinion or final filing is generated.
