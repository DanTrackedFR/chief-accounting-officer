# Provisions, contingencies and recovery method

Use approved TOPIC-05-003 and TOPIC-05-004, not equity-note topics as recognition authority. TOPIC-13-005 provides restructuring boundary and TOPIC-15-004 judgment/uncertainty documentation. IAS37/AASB137 provision and capped separate recovery claims are TOPIC-05-003-IFRS-D001 and their framework counterparts; onerous fulfilment/impairment waterfall is TOPIC-05-004-IFRS-D001/E005. ASC450 qualitative likelihood/range measurement and FRS102 Section21 must be reviewed independently. Apply the operative reporting-period edition, not a historical topic heading.

1. Reconcile claims/obligations from counsel, warranties, contracts, restructuring plans and environmental/restoration populations to existing accruals and subsequent settlements. Separate payables, vendor debit assets, provisions, contingent liabilities and contingent gains. Confirm obligating past event and present legal/constructive obligation under the actual framework. Future operating losses and mere management intentions are not present obligations. A merely possible obligation cannot simultaneously be asserted as established.
2. IAS37/AASB/FRS102 ordinary provision recognition uses probable/more-likely outflow and reliable estimate; ASC450 uses its qualitative probable plus reasonable-estimation assessment. These are not one universal numeric probability. Possible/reasonably-possible exposures follow the disclosure route; remote exposures follow framework-specific exceptions. Unestimable exposure may require disclosure instead of an arbitrary zero measurement. A release of an opening provision needs explicit evidence/memo.
3. Support the estimate and measurement method. Portfolio expected values require valid outcome distributions; a single obligation uses a best-estimate/most-likely judgment informed by other outcomes. A continuous equally likely range uses its midpoint where applicable. ASC450 accrues the better estimate in a range; absent a better estimate, use its minimum, not the IFRS midpoint. The US estimate and residual reasonably possible exposure must be documented. Validate every probability and amount; no negative probabilities or accidental averaging across unrelated claims.
4. Discount material long-duration cash flows under the applicable model using supported risk/timing inputs without double counting risk. The deterministic PV is amount/(1+rate)^years. ASC450 is not discounted by default; exceptions require a specific reviewed authority memo. Unwinding is finance cost, settlement reduces provision, and estimate changes differ from a simple closing-minus-opening delta. FX/other changes require explicit balanced event journals tying to the provision movement.
5. Onerous contracts: apply another scoped standard first; assess relevant asset impairment, then recompute directly related fulfilment costs versus receipts and the supported exit alternative without double counting. IFRS 2022 cost-of-fulfilling change includes directly related allocations. General IAS37 onerous accounting is not a universal US model. Restructuring: detailed formal plan, valid expectation/communication and qualifying direct expenditure require evidence; future business operations do not belong in the provision. US exit/termination obligations route to ASC420/712 specialist timing rather than generic ASC450.
6. Decommissioning: US ARO routes to ASC410. IFRS/AASB/UK restoration uses the approved asset/provision interaction. Cost-model asset changes need carrying amount, remaining life and impairment review. Decreases cannot create a negative asset: reduce the asset only to its carrying floor and recognize excess gain. After useful life ends, changes go to P&L rather than reviving an exhausted asset. Revaluation-model reserve mechanics route to an independently reviewed specialist schedule.
7. Reimbursement recognition is a separate assessment; under IAS37 its virtually-certain condition and cap require evidence. US recovery criteria are separately supported, not imported from IAS37. The liability remains gross; the receivable does not automatically permit balance-sheet netting. Bridge opening reimbursement plus recognition/impairment minus cash to closing. Supplier debit balances require their own refund/credit and recoverability analysis.
8. Contingent assets/gains: IFRS-style virtually-certain inflow may become an asset; probable inflow supports disclosure without recognition. ASC450 gain realization is independently evaluated and ordinarily is not accelerated solely by expected success. Never enter a contingent gain as a negative provision. The gain branch returns a separate asset/gain/cash bridge. `gain_amount` means the closing outstanding recognized receivable after cash collections, not the total award including cash already received.

## Disclosure and review

Produce each class's obligation nature, expected timing, estimation/risk assumptions, movements and reimbursements. Contingencies need financial-effect estimates or supported impracticability explanation; ASC450 includes reasonably possible exposure above the accrual. Apply prejudicial-information exceptions only with reviewed scope and required alternative information. Tie quantitative notes to GL and the calculation bridge; document subsequent-event evidence against reporting-date conditions. Counsel/actuary/legal opinions and tax consequences remain specialist inputs. `../REVIEWER-CONTROLS.md` sets certification and output controls.
