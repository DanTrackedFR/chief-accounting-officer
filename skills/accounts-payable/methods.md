# Accounts Payable & Accrued Expenses — governed method

Supplier/invoice duplicate screening, PO quantity/price and receipt match, economic cut-off, independent received-not-invoiced calculation, prior accrual clearing, planned reversal and backtest; segregated payment approval and bank master evidence; supplier-to-GL proof.

Ordinary invoiced operating costs and supported receipt accruals in one functional currency. Recoverable taxes, discounts, currency remeasurement, supplier finance, provisions and specialized inventory/PPE recognition route to separate approved accounting conclusions.

12 December service days x800 =9,600 unbilled. January invoice10,400 comprising January800 clears December9,600 rather than repeating expense. Ordinary invoice120 and bank payment100 leave AP20 independently confirmed by supplier.

Procurement confirms goods/services; technical specialist determines asset versus expense or provision. Reversal without a linked replacement/clearing plan blocks. Subsequent invoice errors require period/materiality analysis.

Read the exact case schema in workflow.py and synthetic examples. Decimal strings only; positive rates/lives, nonnegative amounts and balanced cent-rounded journals. Source and GL gross populations must agree independently of net totals. Use case fingerprint certification only after source, period, framework and specialist review.

Accrual opening balances are cleared once against linked supplier invoices; the closing received-but-uninvoiced estimate is then compared with the remaining opening accrual. Future dated reversal journal plans are separate from current-period journal effects and require monitored invoice replacement. Subsequent invoices produce a numerical backtest and period/error memo, not an automatic late expense. Supplier credit notes, advance payments, FX and supplier-financing classifications route to separate review.
