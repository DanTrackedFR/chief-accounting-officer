Accounting-function gap/evidence/dependency roadmap only; no IPO score, arbitrary benchmark, legal obligation, success/timing prediction or filing mechanics.

## Governed source and execution

Read every actual document and claim register in ../GOVERNANCE-KNOWLEDGE-MAP.json. The map freezes live main5a011a7 and selected actual capability IDs before implementation. Existing canonical historical labels yield to the APPROVED manifest and individual Phase2E reviews. Use the existing production envelope, case certification and public adapter. Require current independently reviewed entity/framework/jurisdiction/period scope, actual reporting editions/adoptions and externally qualified local requirements. Unsupported entity/tier/industry overlays fail closed. The software validates supplied review records; it does not authenticate people or verify a stored URL against actual law.

## Population and version discipline

An independently frozen source population must reconcile IDs and immutable semantic fields to the tracker; gross and signed amounts, source identities and periods cannot be replaced by equal totals. Source documents retain system/query/parameters/snapshot, period and currency. Content digests bind actual supplied source bytes. Every current version has separate preparer/reviewer approval. Release review additionally hashes the source/artifact/dependency payload; fresh outer approval cannot hide a late change. Supplied data and approvals remain independently examined case evidence, not manufactured source verification.

## Ownership and output

Import only actual complete, current, unaltered owner cases/results with exact entity/framework/jurisdiction/period. Each semantic numeric assertion resolves to the actual completed owner result; duplicate conclusions and reposting block. Unresolved material evidence or accounting issues remain partial/blocked. Only numeric/count workpapers, generated guidance and material limitations cross all seven public routes. Raw inputs, memos, reviewers, internal claims and digests remain private. All candidate synthetic approvals/examples are explicitly tests, never real sign-off. No journals or external actions are created.

## Individual supported method

Nine accounting diagnostic areas cover historical reporting, close, policies, technical accounting, controls, audit, systems/data, calendar and ownership/resources. Each source observation binds actual criterion/state and evidenced remediation. Gap/dependency tracking never yields an IPO-ready score, peer benchmark, staffing threshold, prediction or filer/legal determination. A completed owner supports its own dependency only; qualified external control/adviser evidence remains separate. Unresolved gaps/dependencies prevent clean completion.
