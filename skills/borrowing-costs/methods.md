# Borrowing Costs — independently governed bounded allocation

The production entrypoint is `production.assess_case("borrowing-costs", case)`. TOPIC-04-003 remains a limited CIP classification/routing pointer. It cannot qualify this method: the workflow requires only independently approved `SUPPLEMENTAL_BORROWING_COSTS` claims. Approval and source assurance remain separate.

## Supported object and period

One separately identified, conventional tangible construction asset for own use, for-profit entity, annual period 1 January–31 December 2026. IFRS IAS23_2026; AASB123_MAR2020 Tier 1; ordinary US ASC835_20_2026; full commercial FRS102_SEP2024_2026. The operative applicability review and policy edition must match exactly. This does not support FRS101/105, AASB Tier2/NFP/public sector, cross-border currency, assets constructed for sale, non-PPE assets, multiple projects/shared components, project abandonment, tax-exempt borrowing, fees/coupon–effective-interest differences, or complex instruments. A single independently usable component can be its own separately inventoried object; a shared multi-component project blocks.

Qualifying status is independently reviewed against the framework. Account labels, budgets and purchase orders do not prove eligibility. Actual expenditure is dated paid cash, with the bank payment date matching the expenditure; negative expenditure reversals reduce the base without duplicating economic identity. Beginning expenditure is separately reconciled. Non-UK routes currently require no previous capitalized borrowing cost and explicitly select actual cash expenditure. A previously capitalized-cost accumulated-expenditure route needs separate qualification. UK requires the carrying-cost base, including prior capitalization and the previous day's current-year capitalization.

## Distinct measurements

IFRS/AASB and elected UK capitalization use actual specific borrowing costs during the capitalization window, less specifically reviewed temporary investment income allocated evenly within the provided contract-interest segment. General debt excludes every designated specific loan. The general capitalization rate is actual general cost divided by time-weighted principal, using actual calendar-year weights, then applied to dated residual qualifying expenditure after specific financing. An income schedule that is not uniformly earned within its supplied segment must be split into accurate segments. Only one specific loan is supported; multiple specific loan ordering blocks.

US uses avoidable interest: specific financing rate applies to accumulated actual qualifying expenditure up to the specific principal; the weighted other-debt rate applies to the excess. It does not deduct investment income. Investment income and tax-exempt financing are blocked pending separately owned accounting evidence. US methodology does not reuse the IFRS actual-specific-interest amount.

The period capitalized total is limited to total actual supported borrowing cost incurred for the reporting period. There is no daily incurred-cost ceiling. Each contract accrual is calculated with exact Decimal arithmetic using the evidenced actual/actual, actual/365 or actual/360 convention. The general weighted capitalization rate is normalized using actual-year time-weighted principal. Draws and repayments occur at the start of their recorded date. Rate changes use complete contiguous dated contract segments. Source loan costs and totals independently reconcile to the calculated accrual. No assumed rates or universal day-count convention exist. Final amounts round to cents; daily unrounded calculation precision and final rounding explain the period bridge.

## Timing

Full reporting-period timeline requires contiguous reviewed predevelopment, active, temporary interruption, suspended and completed states. Project authorization precedes physical development. Capitalization requires expenditure, borrowing cost and activity together. Extended ceased development can suspend; necessary delays or substantial technical activity cannot be marked suspended. Temporary continuation needs actual necessary-delay or technical-activity evidence. Ready date ceases capitalization from that date. An open CIP balance never overrides readiness. Unsupported abandonment returns BLOCKED for expense/impairment owner review.
