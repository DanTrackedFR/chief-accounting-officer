# Non-GAAP / Alternative Performance Measures — bounded methods

Source-backed APM dictionary, adjustment reconciliation, comparisons and actual jurisdictional publication-review controls. No invented adjustments or automatic regulatory compliance.

Actual canonical sources: TOPIC-08-007. The immutable map preserves original claims and effective-period/entity/jurisdiction qualifications.

## Controlled reconciliation and dictionary

Exactly current and comparable prior-year calendar statements supply the same
metric, units and independently approved GL line population. Full-entity imports
bind the actual completed owner metric where supplied. Controlled source statements
may be used instead; approvals are supplied records, not authenticated identities.
Every expense addback binds its complete journal IDs and expense account to the
same-period GAAP source ledger; balanced journal expense sums reconcile to source
GL amount. A boolean claiming inclusion cannot replace this lineage. Journals
stay with the recognition owner and are never posted by the APM skill.

Each adjustment has a rationale, eligibility/classification review, tax/NCI effect
and recurrence description. Definitions, labels and recurrence semantics must
match across periods; changed definitions, per-share and invented adjustment
routes block. A recurring expense cannot be called nonrecurring. GAAP subtotal +
source-backed adjustments = published measure exactly, with separate reviewed
tax/NCI totals. Publication dictionary and units match each communication version.

## Jurisdiction and MPM gates

Require actual jurisdiction, issuer type, rule regime, current-authority check on
the execution date, exact case/period, version and independently reviewed draft,
misleading-measure and prominence workpapers. SEC RegulationG/Item10(e), EU,
UK and Australian overlays are distinct. An authoritative URL is supplied
provenance, not proof this software performed external verification. These
qualified evidence records are mandatory, case-specific application inputs;
the skill does not independently issue legal/regulatory clearance.

Effective IFRS18/AASB18 MPM scope is a separate reviewed question from external
APM regulatory scope. Non-early 2026 does not acquire 2027 MPM requirements.
When effective and actually in scope, the separate disclosure review must be
complete; applicable accounting owners retain tax/NCI recognition and reporting.
No adjustments or management measures are invented. Public outputs contain
controlled totals and limitations, never raw evidence/reviewer/source metadata.
