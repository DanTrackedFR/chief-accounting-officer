## Executable scope

All executable routes are first-year new groups recognized in 2026, one functional currency, with exact entity/framework/product/population/report/version bindings and qualified source review. This is not general support for all IFRS 17 or ASC 944 products.

* IFRS full-standard for-profit / AASB Tier 1 for-profit: issued nonparticipating GMM, fully collected initial premiums, qualified cash flows/RA/coverage units, profitable and initial onerous groups, actual dated simple CSM interest, supported future-service nonfinancial changes when current and locked-in discount bases agree, coverage release, supplied contract finance in profit or loss. CSM exhaustion, later loss reversals and more complex changes are blocked.
* IFRS/AASB eligible issued PAA: qualified actual service fraction/time or risk allocation, financing/claims-settlement exemption horizons, attributable acquisition capitalization/release or supported expense election, onerous assessment, actual incurred claims, estimate changes, settlement cash and qualified LIC PV/RA.
* IFRS/AASB prospective proportionate eligible PAA reinsurance held: separately recognized/populated, exact underlying policies/claims and qualified recoverability/nonperformance, separate asset, expense/recovery and rollforwards. No automatic netting with issued business.
* Native US ASC 944 short-duration issued insurance-entity route: fully paid coverage inception with recognition, premium due and cash aligned to inception, earned premium/protection pattern, unearned premium, undiscounted claims/LAE, successful attributable DAC and protection-pattern amortization, reviewed premium-deficiency aggregation excluding investment income, DAC writeoff before additional deficiency liability. No IFRS CSM/RA/loss-component output labels or schedules.
* UK GAAP FRS 103: supported framework/scope and existing-policy boundary assessment; ordinary native measurement is fail-closed without a governed actual entity policy/legal-format route. No invented UK IFRS 17 equivalence.

Balanced journals reconcile to exact GL, named opening-plus-signed-movements stock schedules, statement support and group-specific disclosure amounts. Acquisition cash flows are not double counted; US DAC is separate. Claims come from controlled populations and reviewed reports, not inferred IBNR or guessed reserves. Finance is supplied, bound and kept distinct; OCI elections/disaggregation are blocked. No ERP posting occurs.

## Actuarial boundary and blocked scope

Actual actuarial report, valuation date, model version, assumption register, population/reconciliation, qualified separate review and all material inputs are required. The skill neither projects nor certifies mortality, morbidity, lapse, ultimate losses/IBNR, catastrophe rates, curves, RA/confidence levels or coverage forecasts. Synthetic examples are explicitly synthetic and do not authenticate real professional approval. Unsupported or unbound actuarial/specialist inputs fail closed.

Blocked executable scope: VFA/direct participation/underlying items; OCI; transition; prior-period or multi-year opening books; acquired contracts/portfolio transfers; modifications and derecognition beyond supported claim settlements; GMM reinsurance/loss recovery; retroactive/nonproportionate/disputed held arrangements and reinsurance commissions; advanced CSM/discount or model/method changes; investment components, embedded derivatives, fixed-fee services, seller warranties and financial guarantees; foreign-currency measurement/translation; US long-duration/LDTI, participating/universal-life/annuities/MRB and US reinsurance; unqualified UK policy routes; AASB NFP/public-sector/Tier 2 and wrong compilation/period. See SKILL.md and methods.md for exact per-field restrictions and specialist handoffs.
