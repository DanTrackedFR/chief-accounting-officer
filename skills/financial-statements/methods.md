## Populations, deterministic tie-outs and judgments

TB rows have unique IDs, signed decimal balances (debits positive), category, statement line, source version, classification memo and cash flag. Current and comparative TBs independently balance. Current profit = -(revenue+expense signed totals), OCI = -OCI signed totals, closing equity = opening-equity credit balance + profit + OCI. Assets = liabilities + closing equity. Categories do not decide current/noncurrent or legal offset rights: those require separate complete review of maturity/covenants/rights and classification evidence. Do not offset balances merely to make a statement tie.

Modern IFRS18/AASB18 revenue/expense rows additionally require an approved performance_category: operating, investing, financing, income_tax or discontinued. Compute operating profit, profit before financing/tax, profit before tax and net profit for current and retrospectively mapped comparative periods. The cash starting subtotal must equal computed operating profit, not merely a supplied number. Each in-scope management performance measure reconciles from a computed subtotal through disclosed adjustments to the reported measure, with tax/NCI effects as reviewed specialist inputs; definition/public-communication scope and population completeness require memos. Empty MDP populations still require scope evidence. Numeric tax/NCI reconciliation is not a tax-law conclusion.

Equity component bridges include owners/NCI, opening, profit, OCI, owner transactions, retrospective adjustments, other and closing. Both total and component rolls reconcile to P&L/OCI/BS; restated comparative equity matches adjusted opening equity. Preserve prior issued/restated versions and source journals/tax effects. Current-period estimate changes are not retrospectively relabeled errors.

Cash flow begins with supported net/pre-tax/operating subtotal, with explicit net-profit reconciliation for non-net-income starts. Adjustments use signed noncash/working-capital inputs with acquisition/FX exclusion review. Classify a complete unique cash population; its operating/investing/financing totals must match the bridge. Interest/dividends are independently gated (US interest/receipts operating, dividends paid financing; ordinary IFRS18 interest/dividends paid financing and receipts investing). Pre-IFRS18/FRS102 classification needs reviewed consistent policy. Closing cash = opening + operating + investing + financing + FX; opening/closing BS cash bridges reconcile restricted cash/overdraft conventions without arbitrary plugs.

Six sets are required: balance sheet, income, comprehensive income, cash flow, equity, notes. A reviewed requirement-population/checklist version covers every set; no open items. Each requirement has effective version, scope decision/evidence, owner and memo. Numerical notes cross-tie to primary totals but do not prove completeness. Independent narrative/specialist scope covers going concern, subsequent events, related parties, segments, EPS, tax, leases, acquisitions, instruments, contingencies and awards. Not-applicable requires evidence, not empty checkboxes.

## Separate opening stock and reporting comparative

A supplied `opening_tb` is a separately reviewed adjacent prior closing stock,
with `opening.period_end` exactly one day before current execution starts.
Equity and cash opening ties use that stock. The independently balanced
comparative TB remains presentation history and is not required to equal the
current opening when the comparison spans a different interval. Absence of this
optional contract preserves the bounded legacy consecutive-period tie; it does
not supply missing intervening history. A governed OPENING execution represents
the exact reporting-boundary stock, not an extra accounting movement.

An empty cash-classification population is accepted only with a complete,
nonempty-evidence zero-movement review, integer movement count zero and zero
actual operating/investing/financing/FX totals. No zero-valued fictitious capital
transaction is inserted. This is controlled supplied review, not authenticated
external approval.

Complete governed Group reporting additionally consumes exact native opening and
comparative versions through `orchestration/reporting_temporal.py`. Opening and
comparative declarations remain distinct. Scope, Case, calendar, Period,
framework, currency, source fingerprint, TB population and recursive currentness
are qualified against Stage2 contracts. An as-of opening version binds its exact
adjacent closing and then feeds current reporting through an explicit alignment;
no relabeling or arbitrary framework/currency conversion is authorized.
