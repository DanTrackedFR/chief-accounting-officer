# Bounded method

Sources are independently qualified frozen records with exact content hashes,
entity/framework/currency/measurement-date checks. The original contract register
and exact notional sum constrain derivative completeness. Identify underlying,
payment provision, initial investment relative to a comparable response and future
settlement; ASC815 additionally requires net settlement. Physical own-use and US
normal purchase/sale routes require actual delivery/usage/practice evidence. Other
exceptions and separated embedded valuation are blocked. IFRS financial-asset hosts
route to whole-instrument classification with the current Financial Instruments
owner. Nonfinancial/liability closely-related features require actual conclusions.

Signed valuation assets are positive, liabilities negative; opening plus current
change minus settlement equals closing. No hedge means all current change in
ordinary earnings. Failed timing means standalone derivative accounting. No
retrospective designation. FRS102 native Section12 may qualify prospectively from
the conditions-met date; measurement splits within the period are unsupported.

IFRS9/AASB9 Chapter6, ASC815 with ASU2017-12, and 2026 FRS102 native Section12 are
separate models. Retained IAS39/AASB139 and alternate FRS financial-instrument
policy routes are blocked. Early ASU2025-07/09 adoption is unsupported; mandatory
2025-07 annual periods beginning after December15 2026 are also blocked.

Fair-value hedge books instrument change and independently measured designated-risk
item change in earnings; the latter adjusts the item basis. Debt schedules remain
with Debt. Post-discontinuation basis amortization needs a recomputed Debt schedule
and is blocked in this isolated workpaper.

Cash-flow IFRS/AASB/FRS effective accumulation is lower absolute instrument versus
opposing hypothetical designated-risk cumulative changes; period OCI is change in
that effective accumulation, with remainder earnings. ASC815 supported included
whole-instrument changes go to OCI; do not apply IFRS lower-of. Require actual
framework probability, supported quantity and designated timing. Pending forecast
reserves remain even on discontinuation. A forecast no longer expected requires
immediate earnings release. Occurred nonfinancial acquisition removes reserve to
basis under IFRS/AASB/FRS; US reserve remains until underlying earnings occur, then
reclassifies to that period. Sales release uses actual earnings timing, not revenue
recomputation.

Net-investment effective portion goes to equity and remainder earnings using actual
Foreign Currency/Consolidation sources, without reconstructing CTA. Qualifying
IFRS/AASB/US disposal release is separate from foreign-operation CTA. FRS102 net
investment hedge reserves do not recycle on disposal.

End-period prospective IFRS/AASB rebalancing preserves historical ineffectiveness,
actual quantity ratios and continuing objective. Midperiod changes require qualified
split measurements. IFRS/AASB qualifying continuing objectives cannot be voluntarily
dedesignated. All reserves satisfy opening + OCI - recycling - basis adjustment =
closing. Every journal ties to source balances, GL movements and statements.

Advanced option/time-value, forward-point/basis, partial designation, macro,
portfolio-layer, dynamic, shortcut, benchmark-component and industry routes fail
closed. No framework symmetry is asserted for these advanced routes.

Monetary execution requires zero initial investment. Nonzero premiums/comparison
cases need a governed inception payment/valuation recognition method and are
blocked rather than treated as a free derivative. Current native FRS102 eligibility
uses external FVTPL instruments, non-written options in this bounded route, and
its economic-relationship/documented-ineffectiveness-causes requirements; no old
pre-2014 prescribed maturity rules or ASC815 effectiveness test are imported.

Debt numeric assertions bind the exact original debt row's closing schedule,
principal, maturity and supplied fixed/variable contractual terms. Exact equal
instrument/debt maturity is an intentional adapter boundary, not a universal
accounting rule. FX transaction assertions bind the same actual transaction ID,
foreign quantity and functional currency; an unrelated receivable cannot authorize
a forecast exposure. Net investment binds the same operation's original TB/rates,
asset/liability population and ownership across current Consolidation/FX owners.
Disposal reserve release requires actual qualifying full-disposal FX evidence and
a same-operation nonempty Consolidation derecognition event. Without those current
owner outputs it remains blocked. No arbitrary group equity/cash is a net investment.

Sales earnings release consumes the actual Revenue owner's current contract,
quantity, currency and period revenue output. It does not compute revenue. Every
imported numeric fact must be consumed exactly once by a semantic accounting
assertion; unrelated Fair Value quoted-equity output cannot qualify a derivative.
