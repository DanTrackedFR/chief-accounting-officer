## Frameworks, periods and specialist boundaries

All five supported contracts have IFRS, US GAAP, UK GAAP/FRS102 and applicable for-profit AASB worked routes. Framework/entity/jurisdiction/both reporting dates, operative edition/adoption and policy evidence gate every case. FRS101/105, nonprofit/public-sector AASB and unresolved overlays fail closed. Fair Value's UK pre-2026 method is explicitly unsupported.

Tax uses enacted rates for US and evidenced enacted/substantively enacted status where applicable; UK timing-difference-plus is not generalized to IAS12 tax bases. US DTA gross/allowance differs from supported recoverability recognition elsewhere. Legal rates, taxable adjustments, credits/expiry, probability assessments, uncertain positions, outside-basis/recognition exceptions, acquisition/SBC allocation, PillarTwo and disclosure-effective requirements require qualified current evidence. Refundable/negative current tax needs a separate adapter. No tax returns or legal determinations.

Benefits require actual entitlements/service and employer burdens; no actuarial or employment-law inference. Defined benefit/other long-term benefits and capitalized labor route to qualified owners. IFRS/AASB termination's executed route requires evidenced inability to withdraw without future service; linked-only restructuring fails closed pending a plan/obligation linkage adapter. US/UK termination requires its own qualified framework method. Preservice or excess remittance advances need separate asset accounting.

Debt requires a supplied independently supported yield, complete contractual cash flows, explicit actual/actual compound convention and reviewed principal-pro-rata maturity allocation. It does not solve arbitrary yields or substitute coupon interest. Intra-interval cash flows require split intervals. Modification/extinguishment, covenant-waiver legality, convertible/embedded derivatives, revolvers, negative yields and specialized restructuring are specialist boundaries; an old schedule cannot silently survive a changed instrument.

Intangibles do not infer technical feasibility, rights, benefits, useful lives or management intent. US software/development, specialized cloud/website/crypto, revaluation, partial disposals and complex component/life changes require dedicated methods. UK finite-life and supported unreliable-life ceiling differ from permitted indefinite-life routes elsewhere. Actual impairment and acquisition conclusions remain with their existing owners.

Fair Value is measurement governance, not autonomous appraisal. Level2/3 models, rates and valuations require qualified evidence. Liability/non-equity adapters, disposal/FX/transport integration and unsupported specialized valuations fail closed. Recognition owner determines P&L/OCI; no invented markets or inputs.

## Architecture, outputs and controls

Registry/routing, canonical retrieval, result envelopes and public allowlist are reused. Supplemental tax retrieval validates the existing approved package; no claim is recreated, reapproved or upgraded. APPROVED is not SOURCE_VERIFIED: all 64 supplemental claims remain TRAINING_DATA_CHECKED / MODEL_DERIVED_AUDIT_REQUIRED with qualified references.

Guarded Decimal calculations produce balanced journals, independently owned opening/closing source stocks and complete all-offset GL/statement bridges. Source populations, IDs/counts/gross amounts and current approvals must reconcile. Cross-skill imports require fresh completed results with identical case, framework, entity, period, knowledge/implementation fingerprints and exact-once ownership. General imports are evidence-only rather than duplicated journals; FV regenerates an exact controlled mapping from its actual recognition owner's result. No ERP postings, filing, returns or counterparty contact.

Independent preparer/reviewer separation and exact case/knowledge/implementation certification remain mandatory. Missing or stale certification produces partial; unsupported or unresolved accounting produces blocked without accounting journals. Supplied approval records are assertions, not authentication of human identity. All seven public routes exclude source notes, evidence ratings, internal reviewer information and hashes. This batch also minimally patches two demonstrated existing defects: Cash Flow public noncash-type input validation and Accounting Changes annual versus interim stock/flow selection (patch versions1.0.1), preserving their established methods.
