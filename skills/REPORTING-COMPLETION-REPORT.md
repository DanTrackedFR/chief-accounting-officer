## Framework and reporting-period coverage

Every package has complete governed IFRS, US GAAP, FRS102 and applicable for-profit AASB ordinary cases. Resolve actual operative edition, jurisdiction/entity, US entity/filer, FRS102 edition/elections, AASB compilation/tier and period before execution. Unsupported FRS101/105, public-sector/NFP, unresolved overlays and applicability exceptions fail closed. Mechanical calculations do not substitute for framework-specific recognition conclusions.

Cash classification includes US ASC230 interest/dividend/restricted-cash rules, reviewed pre-2027 IFRS/AASB and FRS102 elections, and ordinary-entity IFRS18/AASB18 2027 or evidenced early-adoption start subtotal/classification. Main-business investment/finance and AASB Tier2 adoption overlays require specialists. Use-only restrictions cannot remove an otherwise qualifying withdrawable demand deposit from cash. Original acquisition-to-maturity and risk/purpose evidence are required for equivalents; included overdrafts need demand and integral-cash-management evidence. Lease Accounting supplies the distinction between financed principal/interest and operating/exempt rental cash; acquisitions/disposals are net-cash inputs from Consolidation.

Changes apply IAS8, ASC250, FRS102 Section10 and AASB108 with chronology, specific mandatory transition and supported practicability gates. SEC domestic-issuer materiality and filing claims are selected only for actual registrant cases; qualitative/aggregate/interim conclusions and legal filing ownership remain explicit. Mandatory modified opening effects and cumulative comparative carryforwards are handled separately. No tax rate or missing original information is generated.

Going-concern minimum horizons anchor IFRS/AASB at reporting date, UK at authorization and US at issuance/available-to-issue. Complete monthly scenarios include independently evidenced intraperiod minima; horizon minima are not a safe harbor. Baseline before plans is distinct from supported funding afterwards. FRS102 related-party disclosure scope includes explicit 2026-period review rather than an assumed small-entity exemption.
