# Equity & Capital Accounting: executable methods and boundaries

Maintain credit-positive share capital, premium/APIC, retained earnings, OCI and other reserves, with negative treasury contra-equity. Each component has parent/NCI owner and capital/profit/OCI role, opening/closing GL and classification evidence. Cap table opening/closing issued and own shares reconcile; own shares cannot exceed issued shares. Ordinary issued share count times issue price = gross proceeds; count times par = capital; balance less supported incremental directly attributable cost = premium. Nonqualifying issue cost is expense; imported period profit must already include it. Tax effects come from externally reviewed source entries, never this blocked-tax workflow.

Treasury purchases debit contra-equity/credit cash. Reissuance reverses evidenced carrying cost; gains stay premium/equity, losses have an explicitly approved premium/retained-earnings allocation and never P&L. US cost-method versus applicable IFRS/UK/AASB own-share legal allocations require actual policy/legal evidence; par-value, redeemable, compound/warrant, conversion and complex equity allocation are specialist boundaries. Distributions require actual legal authorization, debit retained earnings/credit payable, and payment cannot exceed the declared balance. Supported capital reductions require qualified legal/court approval and authorized component mapping; no company-law conclusion is generated.

Import profit, OCI, SBC, consolidation/NCI and retrospective corrections through dimension-matched specialist handoffs. Recognition stays in Financial Statements, Share-Based Compensation, Consolidation and Accounting Changes. NCI profit/OCI uses separately owned components and reviewed group attribution. Journals are imported only when source accounting journals exist; closing profit/OCI presentation is not invented as a transaction. Every component opening + signed changes = closing = GL; component totals tie statement of equity, dividends payable and share register.

Complete synthetic example: opening equity150; issue20 shares at10/par1 gives capital20/premium180; incremental cost8 debits premium; dividend10 debits RE. Closing capital120+premium172+RE40=332; Dr cash200 / Cr capital20 / Cr premium180, Dr premium8 / Cr cash8, Dr RE10 / Cr payable10. Partial example: valid bridge without legal capital evidence fails closed; valid evidence without exact reviewer certification is partial. Other reserves, acquisitions, legal reductions and complex tax are imported specialist conclusions, not invented legal facts.

US treasury reissuance has a material approved-knowledge boundary: the canonical topics do not supply the full ASC505 cost/par-value and permissible APIC loss-allocation subrules. Autonomous US reissuance fails closed. A qualified Treasury Shares Accounting specialist must supply a current framework/entity/period cost-method policy, a separately substantiated treasury_apic component and usable loss capacity, authorized allocation and exact-once balanced source journals. Ordinary issuance premium is never assumed to be usable treasury APIC. The workflow checks these supplied conclusions and their complete numerical/journal consequences; it does not create or verify new standards authority. Par-value/other unsupported treasury methods remain blocked. This boundary does not prevent ordinary issuances, treasury purchases, equity bridges or the supported reviewed non-US cost routes.

Inputs and identifiers are illustrated in `examples/*.case.json`; these are wholly synthetic and contain no real approvals. Corresponding partial and complete public outputs demonstrate both certification states. Regression reissues synthetic certification against the current exact implementation; archived cases intentionally remain unsigned. Every successful route still requires all contract controls and specialist handoffs. Unsupported optional accounting routes cannot obtain a clean result merely by excluding a source item.
