# Debt, Borrowings & Financing Accounting — bounded governed methods

## Executed method

Plain amortized-cost term loans only. Independent executed terms and source cost population determine opening legal principal/carrying value, draws, eligible directly attributable costs and excluded service costs. Draw less eligible cost is the initial carrying increase; service cost is expense, not part of yield. Undrawn/revolver/FVTPL/compound/derivative costs require a distinct specialist route. Current framework and period/election review covers IFRS/AASB amendments, current US instrument-specific presentation and FRS1022026.

Starting carrying = opening + draws − eligible costs. The explicit independently reviewed actual/actual compound convention derives periodic yield from the supplied annual EIR and dated service fraction. A complete remaining contractual payment population backtests discounted amounts to starting carrying; coupon/principal amounts tie to each current interval, and future principal dates tie exactly to maturity disclosures. No numerical solver manufactures a yield. Each interval uses its current opening stock, not nominal coupon or an initial stock reused in every interval. Closing = opening + effective interest − contractual cash interest − principal repayment. Intervals cover the cutoff without gaps, overlaps or future events. Negative yields, intra-interval draws/fees/resets, other compounding conventions and redemption residuals need a separate supported schedule. Legal principal is independently rolled forward and tied to lender.

Current/noncurrent cannot be an unchecked label: the bounded term-loan method requires a supplied independently approved principal-pro-rata carrying allocation policy; each maturity allocation is reperformed with a deterministic final cent residual, not arbitrary future carrying labels. Sum principal equals lender; sum carrying equals closing; carrying due within twelve months equals current presentation. This is the supported supplied-method convention, not a universal framework election. Distinct tranche allocation/accrued-interest/other methods require a qualified specialist schedule. Rights/covenants/waiver/refinancing conclusions require actual reporting-date framework/legal review. Overdue/demand/default cases fail closed, and a later waiver cannot silently establish earlier rights. Modification/extinguishment/qualitative/borrower-lender fee conclusions are specialist boundaries rather than continuing the original yield automatically; current IASB/FASB proposals are not operative rules.

Financial Instruments, FX and Accounting Changes results are dimensioned current completed evidence-only dependencies; their journals remain with their originating owners. Outputs are reusable workpapers for Going Concern, Cash Reporting, Equity and Financial Statements. No complete result alone proves lender intent or legal release.

Every contractual cash date within the reporting period must terminate an inventoried interest interval, not merely be present in the PV source. A mid-interval payment cannot be ignored because the end coupon agrees; split the independently reviewed schedule at that date or fail closed. Complete future source PV is not a substitute for current bank/payment completeness.
