# Debt, Borrowings & Financing Accounting — bounded governed methods

## Executed method

Plain amortized-cost term loans only. Independent executed terms and source cost population determine opening legal principal/carrying value, draws, eligible directly attributable costs and excluded service costs. Draw less eligible cost is the initial carrying increase; service cost is expense, not part of yield. Undrawn/revolver/FVTPL/compound/derivative costs require a distinct specialist route. Current framework and period/election review covers IFRS/AASB amendments, current US instrument-specific presentation and FRS1022026.

Starting carrying = opening + draws − eligible costs. Each dated, independently inventoried full interval uses its current opening stock × supplied actual effective-period yield, not nominal coupon; closing = opening + effective interest − contractual cash interest − principal repayment. Intervals must cover the actual cutoff without gaps, overlaps or future events. Actual periodic yields require independent PV/contract validation; no numerical solver manufactures a yield. Negative yields, intra-interval draws/fees/resets and final-redemption residuals need a separately supported schedule. Legal principal is independently rolled forward and tied to lender.

Current/noncurrent cannot be an unchecked label: complete maturity rows carry independently evidenced legal principal and accounting carrying allocations. Sum principal equals lender; sum carrying equals closing; carrying due within twelve months equals current presentation. Rights/covenants/waiver/refinancing conclusions require actual reporting-date framework/legal review. Overdue/demand/default cases fail closed, and a later waiver cannot silently establish earlier rights. Modification/extinguishment/qualitative/borrower-lender fee conclusions are specialist boundaries rather than continuing the original yield automatically; current IASB/FASB proposals are not operative rules.

Financial Instruments, FX and Accounting Changes results are dimensioned current completed evidence-only dependencies; their journals remain with their originating owners. Outputs are reusable workpapers for Going Concern, Cash Reporting, Equity and Financial Statements. No complete result alone proves lender intent or legal release.
