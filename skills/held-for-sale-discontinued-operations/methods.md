# Assets Held for Sale & Discontinued Operations — bounded methods

IFRS/AASB classification and discontinued-presentation workpaper only. It does not measure disposal groups or prepare complete disposal accounting.

Actual canonical sources: TOPIC-13-004. The immutable map preserves original claims and effective-period/entity/jurisdiction qualifications.

## Executable classification/presentation scope

IFRS/AASB for-profit cases only. The frozen classification date must be in the
reporting period and equal the evidenced date all criteria were met. Independent
plan approval precedes it; availability, highly probable sale, marketing and
within-year completion are separately evidenced. Timing exceptions, distributions,
completed disposals, allocation/reversal and measurement requests block. This
workpaper does not conclude that an entire asset population can use one IFRS5
measurement model.

Supply independent legal and book inventories for every asset/liability row,
stock kind, approved preclassification carrying amount, underlying measurement
owner and premeasurement evidence. Net carrying population subtracts liabilities;
it is a population reconciliation, not a lower-of test. Actual depreciation
source rows and inventory must demonstrate cessation from classification. Missing
measurements/scope exceptions remain specialist dependencies. No generic reviewer
record can unlock group allocation, ceiling/reversal or excluded-asset models.

The discontinued fork requires a separable component and supported major line,
geography or coordinated major plan. HFS alone does not qualify. Separate current
component/continuing typed source ledgers reconcile to the full entity subtotal;
each comparative component reconciles to its actual prior source. Prior balance
sheets are not automatically reclassified. Cash-flow categories tie to independent
bank-source amounts. Same prior-year calendar spans are required; 52/53-week,
transition and other comparative bases need a separate method.

Completed Financial Statements imports reconcile full-entity totals, never a
component subset to the whole entity. A period-end Asset Impairment import can
substantiate exact matching classification stocks only at the same date through
its current closing-stock adapter. Other supplied asset/acquisition/group imports
block absent a specific stock/perimeter adapter; no amount is silently ignored.
All imports are complete/current/unaltered, exact dimensions and evidence only.
No source journal is reposted. Ordinary existing Fixed Assets/Impairment boundaries
remain unchanged. Full disposal/OCI/NCI/retained-interest results are not produced.

Component and continuing profit ledgers must partition the independently approved full-entity profit-after-tax source lines exactly once, without duplicate statement or ledger identities. Comparative component lines must be an exact subset of the comparable prior profit ledger. All profit sources use the same units. Component cash flows reconcile exactly once to a separately inventoried bank transaction population; repeat use and omissions block.
