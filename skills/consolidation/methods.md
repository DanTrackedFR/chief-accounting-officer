# Consolidation and group reporting method

Retrieve approved TOPIC-07-001–009 and business-combination TOPIC-13-001. Framework control, investment exceptions, NCI, foreign operations, acquisitions/disposals and ownership changes are separate accounting decisions. IFRS10/AASB10 power/returns/link, ASC810 voting/VIE and FRS102 Section9 records are independently selected; percentage ownership alone is not control. Evidence ratings and provisional paragraph limitations remain unchanged.

1. Establish group structure, legal rights, substantive/protective/participating rights, contractual arrangements, relevant activities, variable returns and ability to affect returns. US scope/VIE analysis precedes voting-control route; primary beneficiary needs both power over significant activities and potentially significant losses/benefits. Document investment-entity exemptions and alternative associate/joint arrangement routes rather than forcing full consolidation. Consolidate from actual control date, not signing/payment date, and stop at loss of control. The reviewed entity reporting package must cover only in-scope results.
2. Import certified entity TBs, map to approved group COA, align policies and reporting dates and resolve account/dimension exceptions. Exactly one reporting parent and unique entity IDs are required. Each source TB balances; common-currency entities explicitly mark translation not applicable. Unknown/unmapped material accounts block certification rather than default to suspense.
3. Distinguish functional currency, transaction remeasurement and foreign-operation translation. The translation workpaper contains every account's approved rate, closing versus transaction/representative-average/historical policy, source evidence and CTA account. Assets/liabilities, income/expense and historical equity follow the actual framework. Hyperinflation, changing functional currency and rate volatility require specialist sequence rather than generic multiplication. Rate completeness and positivity are checked; translated TB difference is CTA only after the rate-policy review, not an unexplained group balancing plug. Bridge opening/current/disposal/other CTA to TB reserve.
4. Investment/equity elimination reconciles each subsidiary's acquisition equity, reviewed FV adjustments, consideration, goodwill and acquisition NCI. Validate supported PPA, deferred tax, NCI election and acquisition-date data through the acquisition specialist. Duplicate subsidiary schedules are prohibited; allocated investments reconcile to the full parent source account. Do not calculate goodwill from arbitrary missing fair values or book a bargain purchase without its separate remeasurement review. UK goodwill and other post-acquisition measurement differences remain part of the approved group accounting package.
5. Reciprocal elimination has source entity/counterparty/account/transaction ID and matches actual balances. Eliminate matched receivable/payable and revenue/expense families in full. Entity IDs must be within perimeter; cumulative eliminations cannot exceed the entity/account source population. Material differences return to source accounting, never a plug. Map all consolidation journals to approved group accounts.
6. Inventory profit elimination uses supported seller profit and fraction still held within the group. Asset transfers eliminate gain and reverse excess depreciation over remaining group life. Review intragroup losses for external impairment evidence, seller-specific NCI attribution and income-tax effects; tax journals need their own supported memo. Dividends, loans/interest and other transaction families use their documented reciprocal schedules. Maintain additions/releases and original group basis, not just current closing profit amounts.
7. NCI reconciles opening rights-specific balance plus adjusted profit/OCI share minus distributions plus supported other ownership effects. Adjusted profit must reflect relevant upstream eliminations and PPA expense; do not substitute unadjusted legal-entity income. Losses may create a deficit as the actual framework requires. The group NCI account is reconciled to the calculated closing bridge by a separate equity attribution entry, not additional group expense. Wholly owned entities cannot retain unexplained nonzero NCI.
8. Ownership changes retaining control are equity transactions: cash paid versus carrying NCI acquired changes parent equity, not new goodwill. Loss of control uses reviewed derecognition/proceeds/retained-interest/FV and component-specific OCI schedules. FRS102 and US exceptions, common-control and partial disposals need their framework-specific specialist memo. The workflow applies the balanced approved event journal; it does not invent retained-interest FV or blanket OCI recycling.
9. Reconcile entity aggregate + translation + elimination/event journals = consolidated TB. Map every group account to assets/liabilities/equity/income/expenses. Check group profit against equity bridge; opening equity + profit + OCI + owner/other movements = closing equity tied to TB plus unclosed income/expense. Cash-flow opening + operating/investing/financing + FX = closing cash tied to specified TB cash accounts. CTA and NCI bridges must also tie. A balanced TB alone cannot certify the group.

## Disclosure, controls and routing

Review group composition, control judgments, NCI and restrictions, summarized relevant financial information, structured entities, associates/JVs, acquisitions/disposals and reserves under IFRS12/ASC810/FRS102/AASB and applicable tier. Tie primary statements, equity, cash flows and note tables to the controlled group TB. Perimeter, PPA/valuation, tax, hyperinflation and equity-method/joint-arrangement exceptions have explicit specialist handoffs; unresolved evidence produces a blocked envelope with no postable journals. Methods are bounded governed workflows, not autonomous legal-control or valuation opinions.
