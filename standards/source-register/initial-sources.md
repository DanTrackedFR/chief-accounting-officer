## IFRS Foundation

- Publisher: IFRS Foundation / IASB
- Authority: PRIMARY for IFRS Accounting Standards.
- Repository treatment pending licensing outcome: REFERENCE_ONLY.
- Allowed current design: standard/title identification, paragraph references, official links, independently authored CAO analysis.
- Restricted current design: reproduction, close reconstruction, storage or automated ingestion of Standards text.
- Official source family: IFRS Accounting Standards Navigator and IFRS Foundation legal/licensing pages.
