tax-supported identifiable balances, acquisition costs and bounded subsequent
events. Consolidation supports rights-based control, balanced source TBs,
investment/equity eliminations, bilateral eliminations, NCI attribution and
equity/CTA/cash bridges. FX supports independently supplied historical/profit/
closing rates and a separate foreign-operation rollforward. IC reconciles bilateral
principal and local books; its journals are legal-entity implications, never group
eliminations. Tax requires reviewed bases/rates and allocation evidence. Impairment
requires supplied valuation inputs and reviewed unit/perimeter. FS consumes a
qualified balanced population; Analytics explains factual movements.

Integration deficiencies to address narrowly: missing semantic families; explicit
reviewed entity/currency/period scopes; acquisition-date segmentation; exact typed
specialist-result bindings; no repeated translation in Consolidation; legal/group
journal destinations and source identity. Any accounting-method change requires
specialist versioning and independent QA. No general arbitrary entity graph,
comparative runtime, persistence or automatic memory approval is authorized here.
