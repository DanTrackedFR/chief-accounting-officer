## Stable substrate

Explicit stable Scope IDs, separate legal identifiers/display names, parent metadata, framework/jurisdiction, functional/presentation currencies, calendar reference, status/effective metadata and provenance are serializable. Missing values are not invented. Parent structure is validated; no parent edge establishes control, VIE status, consolidation method/perimeter, acquisition accounting or an execution dependency.

execution_identity canonically hashes owner, Scope ID/type, framework/jurisdiction, functional/presentation currencies and bounded start/end dates. Workplans, native inputs and result envelopes use exact node keys. Unique package/issue aliases preserve ordinary callers; repeated package lookup is ambiguous and fails. Diagnostic rechecks have a deterministic role suffix and reuse actual qualified owner evidence. Do not regress to package-keyed dictionaries or execution-order IDs.

Scope-aware facts preserve actual source ID/location/fingerprint, dimensions and transformations. Semantic proposals can select registered Scopes but cannot approve new entities. Unknown/ambiguous entities remain unresolved candidates/questions. Context observations remain unpromoted and scope-specific. Repeated native owner executions require separate reviewed packs, exact per-Scope bindings and scoped source manifests checked against sealed inventory. Owner results retain native certification, with dimensional producing-node/currentness/result envelopes.

Typed receipts qualify source and target nodes/Scopes, actual producer result, semantic metric, framework/currency dimensions, currentness, exact-case/result fingerprints and economic identity. Explicit local observations preserve conversion boundaries and create no accounting authority. Existing Group accounting semantic receipts remain owned by their production specialists. Parent hierarchy alone cannot authorize consumption.

Legal journals remain in one LEGAL_ENTITY, Group/Subgroup journals in their reporting Scope. The scoped wrapper qualifies owner/node/Scope identity and currency/period before the inherited gross-line allocator. Economic identity includes posting Scope and bounded period; legal-A/legal-B/Group events related to one commercial relationship remain distinct. Whole populations and actual accounting implications must be accounted for; relabelled lines/events do not waive exact-once gates. Existing Group source assembly retains account-by-account replay and evidence-only embedded specialist implications.
