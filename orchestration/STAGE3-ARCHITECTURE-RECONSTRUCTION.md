## Authority and composition

The business network must be a separate relationship registry. Only explicit producer/consumer Dependency contracts create execution edges. Relationship cycles are therefore permitted; Graph rejects dependency cycles. No Stage 1/2 identity is replaced.

Intercompany supports ordinary reciprocal balances, reviewed service allocations, settlements and local FX mechanics. It retains separate legal-book journals and delegates Group elimination to Consolidation. Unresolved mismatches cannot receive clean native IC certification. FX supports reviewed functional-TB translation and independently reconciled CTA, ordinary exchangeable operations only. Consolidation requires qualified policy/date alignment, mapped balanced source TBs, rights-based control and actual transaction/account populations. Financial Statements consumes qualified statement populations and independently reviewed presentation contracts.

There is no generic framework converter. A supported conversion must use an existing native production owner with a separately reviewed target-framework case and an explicit exact-source mapping; equal values do not waive conversion. Unsupported general conversion remains unresolved. Framework reassessment and functional-to-presentation translation must remain separate receipts and explicit dependency order. Native owner results are not orchestration approvals.

## Preserved boundaries

Legal postings, reporting-basis adjustments, translation workpapers and Group eliminations are separate objects. Scope/Case parents do not establish control or dependency. Existing gross-line economic allocation remains the posting gate. Source notes and reviewer metadata remain internal under the current public adapter. Government Grants, Borrowing Costs, Investment Property and canonical/supplemental knowledge are excluded. No Stage 4 flagship, persistence or authenticated approval is built here.
